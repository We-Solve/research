Let \(S^n\) denote the sphere with its standard smooth structure, and give \(S^1\times S^3\) the product smooth structure. An exotic smooth structure on a specified smooth manifold \(M\) means a smooth manifold \(X\) homeomorphic to \(M\) but not diffeomorphic to \(M\). The supplied record does not specify whether the homeomorphisms and diffeomorphisms must preserve orientation.

X\not\cong_{\mathrm{diff}}S^4?
