Let $\alpha_1(G)$ be the largest size of an edge set containing at most one edge from each triangle of an $n$-vertex graph $G$, and let $\tau_1(G)$ be the fewest edges meeting every triangle. We prove
\[
 \alpha_1(G)+\tau_1(G)\le n^2/4.
\]
The proof reconstructs Norin--Sun's stronger cut inequality. Fix a triangle-independent edge set $S$, put $C=E(G)\setminus S$, and construct a vertex bipartition with at most $n^2/4-|S|$ internal edges. Deleting those edges makes the graph bipartite, and therefore triangle-free.
