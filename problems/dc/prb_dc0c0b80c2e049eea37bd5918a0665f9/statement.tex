Let $g(n)$ be the largest $|A|$ for $A\subseteq[1,n]$ such that its set of nonempty subset sums is primitive: no two distinct values in that sumset divide one another. We prove
\[
 \log_2 n-O(1)\le g(n)\le\log_2 n+\tfrac12\log_2\log_2 n+O(1),
\]
so in particular $g(n)\sim\log_2 n$. The construction $\{2^m-2^j:0\le j<m\}$ and this order of estimate are recorded in Erd\H{o}s--Lev--Rauzy--S\'andor--S\'ark\"ozy, \emph{Greedy algorithm, arithmetic progressions, subset sums and divisibility}, Discrete Mathematics (1999), and the current statement \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/882}. We supply elementary proofs of both bounds.
