\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a closed smooth manifold \(M\), meaning a compact smooth manifold without boundary, define its rational Betti numbers by
\[
 b_i(M;\Q)=\dim_{\Q}H_i(M;\Q).
\]
The coefficient field is not specified in the source; the following is an explicit rational-coefficient interpretation requiring confirmation.

For each fixed positive integer \(k\), characterize all tuples
\[
 (b_0,\ldots,b_{4k})\in\Z_{\geq 0}^{\,4k+1},
 \qquad b_0=b_{4k}=1,\qquad b_{2k}\equiv 1\pmod 2,
\]
for which there exists a closed smooth manifold \(M\) of dimension \(4k\) satisfying
\[
 \dim_{\Q}H_i(M;\Q)=b_i
 \qquad\text{for every }i\in\{0,\ldots,4k\}.
\]
The task is to give necessary and sufficient conditions for this existence. Connectedness and orientability need not be imposed separately in this rational interpretation: they follow from the prescribed zeroth and top Betti numbers. The source does not specify the range of \(k\); the formulation above treats positive dimensions, and whether \(k=0\) is intended remains unresolved.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine which finite sequences of nonnegative integers, with first and last entries equal to one and odd middle entry, are realized as the Betti numbers of a closed smooth manifold of dimension \(4k\).
