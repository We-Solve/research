\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 20000528}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(u:\R\to\R\) be measurable. For a test function \(f\in C_c^\infty(\R^2)\), define
\[
 M_uf(x,y)=\sup_{\epsilon>0}\left|
 \frac{1}{2\epsilon}\int_{-\epsilon}^{\epsilon}f(x-t,y-u(x)t)\,\dd t\right|.
\]
The slope is evaluated at the output coordinate \(x\), not at \(x-t\), and the absolute value encloses the signed average. Thiele's source question asks whether this operator has an \(L^p(\R^2)\) bound for some finite \(p\). The uniform formulation addressed by the aid is whether, for some \(1<p<\infty\), there is \(C_p<\infty\) such that
\[
 \|M_uf\|_{L^p(\R^2)}\leq C_p\|f\|_{L^p(\R^2)}
 \quad\text{for every measurable }u\text{ and every }f\in C_c^\infty(\R^2).
\]
For \(1<p<\infty\), define the extended-valued optimal constant
\[
 \mathcal C_p=\sup_{u:\R\to\R\,\mathrm{measurable}}
 \sup_{0\neq f\in C_c^\infty(\R^2)}\frac{\|M_uf\|_p}{\|f\|_p}.
\]
The supplied reduction states that \(\mathcal C_p\) is unchanged if \(u\) is restricted to measurable functions with a finite image contained in \(\Q\). In particular the original uniform bound is equivalent to bounds for these fields with a constant independent of the number of slope values and their measurable partition; this equality does not assert that either supremum is finite.

The separate partial theorem states that, for every \(1<p<\infty\), there is \(C_p<\infty\) such that for every integer \(N\geq1\), every measurable partition \(\R=\coprod_{j=1}^N E_j\), every \(a_j,b_j\in\R\), and
\[
 u(x)=a_jx+b_j\quad(x\in E_j),
\]
one has the full-scale estimate
\[
 \|M_uf\|_p\leq C_p N^{1/p}\|f\|_p.
\]
The constant is independent of the partition and of the affine coefficients. For one branch \(u(x)=ax+b\), the measure-preserving shear
\[
 S(x,y)=(x,y-ax^2/2-bx),\qquad g=f\circ S^{-1},
\]
gives the exact identity
\[
 (M_uf)(S^{-1}(x,v))=
 \sup_{\epsilon>0}\left|\frac{1}{2\epsilon}
 \int_{-\epsilon}^{\epsilon}g(x-t,v-at^2/2)\,\dd t\right|,
\]
which is the aid's reduction to polynomial parabolic maximal averages. Finally, for every \(0<p\leq1\), no finite strong \(L^p\) bound holds even for \(u=0\); here \(\|\cdot\|_p\) denotes the usual quasi-norm when \(p<1\). No endpoint weak-type assertion or bound uniform in arbitrary slope complexity is supplied.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Thiele asks for finite-\(p\) bounds for maximal averages along a merely measurable slope field depending on one coordinate. The supplied reduction makes finite rational-valued fields sufficient, provided their bounds are uniform in complexity. A separate theorem gives a bound growing as \(N^{1/p}\) for measurable patchings of \(N\) affine slopes; this does not settle the uniform problem.
