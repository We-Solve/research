In the usual terminology, a mock modular form is the holomorphic part of a harmonic Maass form with a modular nonholomorphic completion; its shadow is obtained from the completion by the appropriate \(\xi_k\)-operator. A precise setting for the aid's cone identity is: let \(r\geq1\), let \(A\) be a symmetric positive-definite rational \(r\)-by-\(r\) matrix, let \(\epsilon_i\) be unit-modulus twists, and let \(q=e^{2\pi i\tau}\), \(\tau\in\mathbb H\). With fractional powers defined using \(\tau\), put
\[
 U(x_1,\ldots,x_r)=\sum_{\mathbf n\in\Z_{\geq0}^r}
 \frac{q^{\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}A\mathbf n/2}\prod_jx_j^{n_j}}
 {\prod_j(\epsilon_jq;q)_{n_j}},\qquad
 (a;q)_n=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(1-aq^j).
\]
These hypotheses ensure nonzero denominators and compact convergence in the \(x_j\); the source summary does not supply its full admissible parameter class. Let \(s_ix\) multiply \(x_i\) by \(q\), let \((r_ix)_j=q^{A_{ij}}x_j\), and let \(U_{\mathrm{face},i}\) be the same sum restricted to \(n_i=0\). The reported exact identity is
\[
 U(x)-\epsilon_iU(s_ix)
 =(1-\epsilon_i)U_{\mathrm{face},i}(x)
 +q^{A_{ii}/2}x_iU(r_ix)\qquad(1\leq i\leq r).
\]
It isolates an affine source on a lower-dimensional face; iterating over coordinate faces gives a finite face-source filtration. It does not by itself prove that a given sum is mock modular or determine its shadow.

In rank one with real \(A>1\), a precise negative-cone correction is

 V(x)=\sum_{m\geq1}q^{Am^2/2}x^{-m}(\epsilon q^{1-m};q)_m\qquad(x\neq0).

\[

For \(A=2\), \(\epsilon=-1\), the unilateral sums at \(x=1,q\) are

 f_0(q)=\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{q^{n^2}}{(-q;q)_n},\qquad

\]

\[
