\section{Functional equations and an Euler-product obstruction for mixed mock L-series}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and notation.}A mixed mock modular form combines mock-modular and modular factors under a specified convention; its nonholomorphic completion encodes the shadow. For a Fourier expansion $f(\tau)=\sum a_nq^n$, an L-series is a Dirichlet series built from its coefficients, typically $\sum_{n\ge1}a_n n^{-s}$ in a region of convergence. Growth, cusps and completion may affect analytic continuation and functional equations.
