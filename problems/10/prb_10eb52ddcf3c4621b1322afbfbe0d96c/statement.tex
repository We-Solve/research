\section*{Almost half-maximal diameter and threshold realizability}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix an integer $n\geq2$.
For a closed connected Riemannian $n$-manifold $(M,g)$, write $\sec_g\geq1$ when all tangent two-planes have sectional curvature at least one, and let $\operatorname{diam}_g(M)$ be the supremum of geodesic distances between pairs of points.
A homeomorphism means a continuous bijection with continuous inverse; no differentiability requirement is imposed on it.

Does there exist a number $\varepsilon(n)>0$ such that every $(M,g)$ satisfying
\[
 \sec_g\geq1,\qquad
 \operatorname{diam}_g(M)\geq\frac\pi2-\varepsilon(n)
\]
is homeomorphic to some closed smooth $n$-manifold $M'$ admitting a Riemannian metric $g'$ with
\[
 \sec_{g'}\geq1,\qquad
 \operatorname{diam}_{g'}(M')\geq\frac\pi2?
\]
The replacement manifold and metric may depend on $M,g$, while the allowed diameter loss depends only on dimension.
The source also asks for the weaker version with a prescribed positive volume lower bound: for every $V>0$, may one choose $\varepsilon(n,V)>0$ when additionally $\operatorname{Vol}_g(M)\geq V$?
The volume condition is imposed on the original metric; the question does not demand that the replacement retain that volume.
Neither formulation requires a small metric deformation or a bound on the distance between the two metrics.
The target is a topological stability statement near the diameter threshold, permitting a new realization of the same homeomorphism type exactly at or above that threshold.
\subsection{Short English statement}
In each fixed dimension, are manifolds just below the diameter threshold $\pi/2$ topologically realizable at the threshold, either in general or with an additional volume bound?
