\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 800904}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((X,\omega)\) be a compact K\"ahler manifold, let \(Y\subset X\) be a complex submanifold, and write \(\omega_Y=\omega|_Y\). For a complex manifold \(M\) with a smooth real closed \((1,1)\)-form \(\theta\), the local condition defining a \(\theta\)-plurisubharmonic function \(u:M\to[-\infty,\infty)\) is that \(u\) is upper semicontinuous and \(u+\rho\) is plurisubharmonic whenever \(\rho\) is a local smooth potential satisfying \(\theta=i\partial\bar\partial\rho\). Here plurisubharmonicity means that restriction to each complex line in local coordinates is subharmonic or identically \(-\infty\). Denote the resulting class by \(\operatorname{PSH}(M,\theta)\), subject to the convention about identically \(-\infty\) functions discussed below.

The supplied question, attributed there to Coman--Guedj--Zeriahi, asks whether for every such \((X,\omega,Y)\), every \(u\in\operatorname{PSH}(Y,\omega_Y)\) admits an extension \(U\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)\) satisfying \(U|_Y=u\). The source expresses this as
\[
\operatorname{PSH}(Y,\omega|_Y)
\stackrel{?}{=}
\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)|_Y,
\qquad
\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)|_Y
:=\{U|_Y:U\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)\}.
\]

The source does not specify whether \(Y\) must be closed in \(X\), nor its connectedness or that of \(X\); no such hypotheses are added here. It also does not specify whether identically \(-\infty\) functions are admitted, globally or on individual connected components. Restrictions of ambient plurisubharmonic functions can be identically \(-\infty\) on components of \(Y\), so this convention matters for the literal set equality. The extension assertion and the displayed equality are retained as the supplied target, with these qualifications requiring clarification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The question asks whether every \(\omega|_Y\)-plurisubharmonic function on a complex submanifold \(Y\) of a compact K\"ahler manifold \((X,\omega)\) extends to an \(\omega\)-plurisubharmonic function on all of \(X\). The source leaves closedness of \(Y\) and conventions concerning identically \(-\infty\) functions unspecified.
