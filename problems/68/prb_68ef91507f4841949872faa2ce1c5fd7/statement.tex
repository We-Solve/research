\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701503}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
All groups are abstract groups. The class of elementary amenable groups is the smallest class containing all finite groups and all abelian groups and closed under isomorphisms, subgroups, quotient groups, group extensions, and directed unions. Closure under extensions means that a group belongs to the class whenever it has a normal subgroup such that both that subgroup and the corresponding quotient belong to the class.

For a group \(K\), write \(K'=[K,K]\) for its commutator subgroup. The group \(K\) is metabelian if \(K''=[K',K']=1\). Define its lower central series by \(\gamma_1(K)=K\) and \(\gamma_{i+1}(K)=[\gamma_i(K),K]\) for \(i\geq 1\). The group \(K\) is nilpotent if \(\gamma_{c+1}(K)=1\) for some integer \(c\geq 0\), and virtually nilpotent if it has a nilpotent subgroup of finite index. A section of \(G\) means a quotient \(H/N\), where \(H\leq G\) and \(N\trianglelefteq H\).

\textbf{Question (A. Yu. Olshanskii, Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.74).} For every finitely generated elementary amenable group \(G\) that is not virtually nilpotent, do there exist \(H\leq G\) and \(N\trianglelefteq H\) such that \(H/N\) is finitely generated, metabelian, and not virtually nilpotent? The finite generation requirement concerns the section \(H/N\); no separate finite generation hypothesis on \(H\) is imposed in this formulation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every finitely generated elementary amenable group that is not virtually nilpotent have a finitely generated metabelian quotient of a subgroup that is also not virtually nilpotent?
