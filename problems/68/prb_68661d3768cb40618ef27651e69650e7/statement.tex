\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source asks to study collapse of Riemannian manifolds with boundary, with $\sec\geq K$ in the interior and controlled boundary concavity. It does not specify dimension, compactness, diameter, concavity convention or a desired structural theorem.

The aid gives an explicit compact model. Let $(N^m,h)$ be a closed connected Riemannian manifold with $m\geq1$ and $\sec_h\geq0$, and fix $\lambda\geq0$. For $\eps>0$ put
\[
M_\eps=N\times[0,\eps],\qquad
g_\eps=\dd t^2+a_\eps(t)^2h,\qquad
a_\eps(t)=1+\frac\lambda\eps t(\eps-t).
\]
Use the inward unit normal and the convention $\mathrm{II}(X,Y)=-g(\nabla_X\nu,Y)$, so convex Euclidean boundaries have positive form. Since $a(0)=a(\eps)=1$, $a'(0)=\lambda$ and $a'(\eps)=-\lambda$, both boundary faces satisfy
\[
\mathrm{II}=-\lambda\,g_{\partial M_\eps}.
\]
Thus concavity is fixed rather than deteriorating with collapse.

The warped-product sectional curvatures are
\[
\sec(\partial_t,X)=-\frac{a''}{a}=\frac{2\lambda}{\eps a}\geq0,
\qquad
\sec(X,Y)=\frac{\sec_h(X,Y)-(a')^2}{a^2}\geq-\lambda^2
\]
for the corresponding mixed and tangential planes; the second formula is used when such tangential planes exist. The aid reports the exact inradius and quantitative collapse
\[
\operatorname{inrad}(M_\eps)=\eps/2,\qquad
d_{\mathrm{GH}}(M_\eps,N)\leq\eps/2,
\]
and volume
\[
\operatorname{Vol}(M_\eps)=\operatorname{Vol}(N)\int_0^\eps a_\eps(t)^m\,\dd t
\sim\eps\operatorname{Vol}(N).
\]
These are the intrinsic length metrics; projection to $N$ supplies the stated correspondence.

Let $D M_\eps$ be the raw metric double, gluing two copies by the identity on both boundary faces, without smoothing. The aid asserts
\[
d_{\mathrm{GH}}(D M_\eps,N)\leq\eps/2,
\]
but for $\lambda>0$ each individual double has no finite Alexandrov lower curvature bound. It attributes this to Kosovskii's necessary gluing condition: the sum of the two face forms is $-2\lambda g$, which is negative in every nonzero tangent direction. Positive base dimension is therefore retained in this obstruction. At $\lambda=0$ the product double has no such negative-form obstruction.

The model shows that controlled concavity and an interior lower curvature bound do not justify applying ordinary lower-curvature gluing directly to raw doubles, even when both original spaces and doubles collapse to a smooth nonnegatively curved base. It does not classify all boundary collapse or exclude other smoothing, extension or structural approaches to the original question.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The supplied collar family collapses with fixed boundary concavity and a uniform interior curvature lower bound. Its raw doubles still have no finite Alexandrov lower bound when the concavity is negative, so direct doubling is not automatically a valid reduction.
