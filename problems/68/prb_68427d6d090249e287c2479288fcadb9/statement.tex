\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30000750}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For $R(x)=\sum_{j=0}^r r_jx^j\in\R[x]$, define its length and reduced length by
\[
 L(R)=\sum_{j=0}^r|r_j|,\qquad
 \ell(R)=\inf_{\substack{Q\in\R[x]\\Q\text{ monic}}}L(RQ).
\]
The infimum ranges over all degrees of monic real polynomials, including the constant polynomial $1$; no minimizing $Q$ is assumed to exist.

For a nonzero polynomial $P(x)=a\prod_{j=1}^m(x-\alpha_j)$ with roots counted with multiplicity, its multiplicative Mahler measure is
\[
 M(P)=|a|\prod_{j=1}^m\max\{1,|\alpha_j|\}.
\]
For a nonzero constant this is its absolute value. The zero polynomial can be included by the usual extensions $M(0)=0$ and $\ell(0)=0$; otherwise it may be treated separately.

The conjectural inequality is
\[
 \forall t\in[-2,2]\ \forall P\in\R[x]:\qquad
 \ell\bigl((x^2+tx+1)P(x)\bigr)\ge2M(P).
\]
Equivalently, with the same $t,P$ quantifiers, every monic $Q\in\R[x]$ should satisfy
\[
 L\bigl((x^2+tx+1)P(x)Q(x)\bigr)\ge2M(P).
\]
The Mahler measure on the right is that of $P$, not of a different product and not its logarithm. There is no monicity or integrality requirement on $P$. Both endpoints $t=-2,2$ are included.

The supplied literature assessment describes the question as posed for all real $t$, whereas both original and restored statements explicitly restrict $t$ to $[-2,2]$. This discrepancy is retained: the draft's mathematical target is the supplied interval statement and is not silently broadened to every real parameter.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every real polynomial and every $t$ in the closed interval $[-2,2]$, is the reduced coefficient length of $(x^2+tx+1)P$ at least twice the multiplicative Mahler measure of $P$? The multiplier infimum includes monic polynomials of arbitrary degree.
