\textbf{Question.} Which additional conditions on a finite group \(G\) yield a free action by homeomorphisms or diffeomorphisms on \((S^n)^k\) with \(k>1\)? A free topological action is a homomorphism \(\rho:G\to\operatorname{Homeo}(X_{n,k})\) such that \(\rho(g)x\ne x\) for every \(x\in X_{n,k}\) and every \(g\ne e\); a free smooth action requires \(\rho(g)\in\operatorname{Diff}(X_{n,k})\).
