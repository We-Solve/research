\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F_n$ be a free group of rank $n\geq2$, and let $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n)$. Following the supplied clarification, ``irreducible'' means \emph{fully irreducible} (iwip): no positive power preserves the conjugacy class of a proper nontrivial free factor of $F_n$. A subgroup $H$ is purely fully irreducible if every $h\in H\setminus\{1\}$ is fully irreducible.

Use the supplied modern interpretation of Schottky: a free purely fully irreducible subgroup with convex-cocompact geometry, namely a quasi-isometrically embedded orbit in the standard free-factor graph $\mathcal{FF}_n$. For a finitely generated $H$, the latter means that an orbit map $h\mapsto h\cdot x$ obeys, for some $L\geq1$, $A\geq0$,
\[
L^{-1}d_H(h,k)-A\leq d_{\mathcal{FF}_n}(h\cdot x,k\cdot x)
\leq Ld_H(h,k)+A
\quad(h,k\in H).
\]
Here $d_H$ is a word metric and the rank-dependent standard convention for $\mathcal{FF}_n$ must be fixed, especially at rank $2$.

The two requested analogues for $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Does there exist a finitely generated free subgroup $H\leq\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ that is purely fully irreducible and is not Schottky, equivalently not convex cocompact under the stated interpretation?
\item Does there exist a nonfree subgroup $H\leq\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ that is purely fully irreducible?
\end{enumerate}
