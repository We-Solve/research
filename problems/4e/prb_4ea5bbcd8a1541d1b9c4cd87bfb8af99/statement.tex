\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Write $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ with its fixed free generators. Given a probability space $(A,\nu)$, its Bernoulli, or i.i.d., action is the probability-preserving action $T$ of $F_2$ on $(X,\mu)=(A^{F_2},\nu^{F_2})$ defined by
\[
 (T_gx)(h)=x(g^{-1}h)\qquad(g,h\in F_2).
\]
A finite action means a homomorphism $T^{(m)}:F_2\to\operatorname{Sym}(X_m)$ on a nonempty finite set, equipped with uniform probability measure. No transitivity assumption is added.

One standard action version of local-global convergence is the following. Let $B_r$ be the word ball of radius $r$ in $F_2$ and let $[k]=\{1,\ldots,k\}$. For a probability-preserving action $U$ on $(Y,\rho)$, define $C_{r,k}(U)$ to be the closure, in the finite-dimensional probability simplex on $[k]^{B_r}$, of the distributions
\[
 \operatorname{Law}_{y\sim\rho}\bigl((c(U_{g^{-1}}y))_{g\in B_r}\bigr),
 \qquad c:Y\to[k]\text{ measurable}.
\]
The local-global convergence condition is convergence of these compact sets in Hausdorff distance, using the $\ell^1$ metric on probability vectors:
\[
 d_H\bigl(C_{r,k}(T^{(m)}),C_{r,k}(T)\bigr)\longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{for every }r\geq0\text{ and }k\geq1.
\]
This requires approximation of all finite-color local statistics in both directions; merely approximating the uncolored local neighborhoods is insufficient.
