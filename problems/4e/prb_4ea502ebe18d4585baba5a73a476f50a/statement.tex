\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $d\geq1$ and let $P\subset\R^d$ be a Delone set: it is uniformly discrete and relatively dense. Finite local complexity means that for each radius there are finitely many point-centered patches up to translation. Repetitivity means that each finite patch occurs relatively densely. Let
\[
 \Omega_P=\overline{\{P-t:t\in\R^d\}}
\]
be its translation hull in the usual local-matching topology, and assume the translation action has a unique invariant Borel probability $\mu$, hence an ergodic one.

The Ruelle--Sullivan averaging map in degree one sends real cohomology to covectors on $\R^d$. Using the Euclidean identification $(\R^d)^*\simeq\R^d$, write it in the source's notation as $\tau_\mu^*$. On a suitable closed leafwise representative $\omega=\sum_{j=1}^d f_j\,dx_j$, it is represented by
\[
 \tau_\mu^*([\omega])=
       \left(\int_{\Omega_P}f_1\,d\mu,\ldots,
             \int_{\Omega_P}f_d\,d\mu\right).
\]
For integral \v{C}ech classes, use the coefficient map into real cohomology before this averaging. Thus
\[
 L_P=\tau_\mu^*(\check H^1(\Omega_P,\Z))\subset\R^d
\]
is an additive subgroup determined by \emph{integral} classes; replacing them by arbitrary real classes changes the problem.

The Kellendonk--Putnam conjecture identified in the supplied assessment is
\[
 \bigl(\forall t\in\R^d\setminus\{0\}:P-t\ne P\bigr)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \overline{L_P}^{\,\R^d}=\R^d.
\]
The left side uses the no-nonzero-translational-period meaning of aperiodic. In dimension greater than one this is stronger than merely lacking a full-rank period lattice, so the source convention should be confirmed. Density on the right is Euclidean topological density, not merely full real linear span. The statement concerns every repetitive, uniquely ergodic FLC Delone set under the given hypotheses; no Meyer, substitution or special-model restriction is added.

The supplied literature triage checked 21 August 2026 still presents this general density characterization as conjectural. Its status is retained as a dated supplied finding, not an independent literature determination.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For a repetitive uniquely ergodic finite-local-complexity Delone set, is having no translational period equivalent to density of the averaged integral first cohomology of its hull in $\R^d$? Integral coefficients and actual density, rather than full span, are essential.
