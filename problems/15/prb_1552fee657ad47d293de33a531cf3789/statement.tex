\section{Volume of the Boolean Quadric Polytope}\label{30002965}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 30002965 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


$x_i\in\{0,1\}$ and $y_{ij}=x_ix_j$ describe the vertices before taking their convex hull. Volume is $d$-dimensional Lebesgue measure in the displayed coordinate space.

A polyhedron is an intersection of finitely many closed affine half-spaces; a polytope is a bounded polyhedron. Its faces are its intersections with supporting hyperplanes.

A Boolean function maps $\{0,1\}^n$ to $\{0,1\}$. A Boolean circuit is an acyclic network of logical gates; a formula is a circuit with no sharing of intermediate gate outputs. Size and depth measure its number of gates and longest gate path.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $n\geq2$, let $P_n$ be the Boolean quadric polytope, the convex hull in dimension $d=n(n+1)/2$ of the $0/1$ points satisfying $y_{ij}=x_ix_j$ for $i<j$. Find a formula or nontrivial upper and lower bounds for the $d$-dimensional volume of $P_n$. \sref{30002965}{2}
