\section{Finite certificate interfaces for finite-field zeta functions}\label{20001265}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 20001265 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


For a variety $V$ over $\mathbb F_q$, its zeta function is $Z(V,T)=\exp(\sum_{r\ge1}\#V(\mathbb F_{q^r})T^r/r)$. The decision task is whether a supplied function equals this zeta function. Membership in NP requires a finite certificate verifiable in time polynomial in the encoded input length.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Question 15. Given a variety over a finite field, can you verify that a given function is its zeta function (i.e., is the question in NP)? (Lenstra, Edixhoven) \sref{20001265}{2}
