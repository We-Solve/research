\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
An alternating link admits a diagram in which successive crossings encountered along each component alternate over and under. Let $\sigma(L)$ be its signature, $P_L(l,m)$ its HOMFLYPT polynomial, and $F_L(a,z)$ its Kauffman polynomial, all with the source's intended orientation and normalization conventions.

\[
\sigma(L)\geq\min\deg_l P_L(l,m)

\]
