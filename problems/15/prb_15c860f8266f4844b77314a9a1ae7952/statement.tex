\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a polynomial automorphism \(f\colon\C^2\to\C^2\), define its forward filled Julia set and forward Julia set by
\[
K^+(f)=\left\{z\in\C^2:\sup_{n\in\Z_{\geq 0}}\|f^n(z)\|<\infty\right\},\qquad J^+(f)=\partial_{\C^2}K^+(f),
\]
where \(f^0\) is the identity and the norm is the Euclidean norm. A nonsingular one-dimensional holomorphic foliation \(\mathcal F\) on an open set \(U\subseteq\C^2\) is a foliation locally represented in holomorphic coordinates by the sets on which one coordinate is constant; its leaves are connected complex curves. A subset of \(U\) is saturated by \(\mathcal F\) if it is a union of whole leaves.

The supplied Question 29 asks whether there exist a generalized complex H\'enon map \(f\), an open set \(U\subseteq\C^2\), and a holomorphic foliation \(\mathcal F\) of \(U\) such that
\[
U\cap J^+(f)\neq\varnothing,
\qquad
\forall L\in\operatorname{Leaves}(\mathcal F),\quad
L\cap J^+(f)\neq\varnothing\ \Longrightarrow\ L\subseteq J^+(f).
\]
Thus \(J^+(f)\cap U\) must be saturated by \(\mathcal F\). No invariance of \(U\) or of the foliation under \(f\) is requested in the supplied statement.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does some generalized complex H\'enon map have a forward Julia set that, on an open set meeting it, is a union of entire leaves of a holomorphic foliation? The intended map class and foliation conventions require confirmation from the source.
