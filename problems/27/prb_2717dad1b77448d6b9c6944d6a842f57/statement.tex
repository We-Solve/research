Every sufficiently large positive integer $n$ can be written
\[
 n=x^2+y^2-z^2,\qquad x,y,z\in\mathbb Z,\quad |x|,|y|,|z|\le\sqrt n.
\]
We reconstruct the open-patch and parity argument of Chojecki and GPT-5.4 Pro, \url{https://www.ulam.ai/research/erdos1148-full.pdf}. The deep input is equidistribution of indefinite integral binary quadratic forms, in the version for \emph{all nonsquare discriminants}, from Einsiedler--Lindenstrauss--Michel--Venkatesh, \emph{The distribution of closed geodesics on the modular surface, and Duke's theorem}, Theorem 2.3 and the duality following it, \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.0413}. Its proof is an external dependency, not supplied here.
