\section*{Rational injectivity of maximal coarse assembly}\label{statement}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every uniformly discrete bounded-geometry metric space X, is maximal coarse assembly $KX_{1}(X)\otimes Q\to K_{1}(C*max(X))\otimes Q$ injective? In particular this asks about coarse disjoint unions of finite connected graphs of uniformly bounded degree.
