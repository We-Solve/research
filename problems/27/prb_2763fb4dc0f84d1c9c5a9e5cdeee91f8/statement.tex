\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a Banach space over its scalar field \(\mathbb K\). The supplied record does not specify whether \(\mathbb K=\R\) or \(\mathbb K=\C\). Recall that \(X\) is reflexive if its canonical embedding into \(X^{**}\) is surjective. A sequence \((x_n)_{n\geq1}\) in \(X\) is normalized and weakly null if \(\|x_n\|=1\) for every \(n\) and \(f(x_n)\to0\) for every \(f\in X^*\).

For this draft, interpret the spreading-model notation in the following standard sense; the supplied excerpt does not itself define it. A normalized basic sequence \((e_j)_{j\geq1}\) in a Banach space is a spreading model generated by \((x_n)\) if, for every \(k\geq1\), every \((a_1,\ldots,a_k)\in\mathbb K^k\), and every \(\varepsilon>0\), there is \(N\geq1\) such that
\[
\left|\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_jx_{n_j}\right\|_X-\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_je_j\right\|\right|<\varepsilon
\quad\text{whenever }N\leq n_1<\cdots<n_k.
\]
Write \(\mathrm{SP}_w(X)\) for the spreading models generated by normalized weakly null sequences in \(X\). Two basic sequences \((e_j)\) and \((v_j)\) are equivalent, written \((e_j)\sim(v_j)\), if there are constants \(0<A\leq B<\infty\) such that, for every \(k\geq1\) and every \((a_1,\ldots,a_k)\in\mathbb K^k\),
\[
A\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_jv_j\right\|
\leq\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_je_j\right\|
\leq B\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_jv_j\right\|.
\]
Thus \(\mathrm{SP}_w(X)/{\sim}\) denotes equivalence classes of these basic sequences.

Let \(c_0(\mathbb K)\) be the space of scalar sequences tending to zero, with the supremum norm, and, for \(1\leq p<\infty\), let \(\ell_p(\mathbb K)\) be the space of scalar sequences \((a_j)\) with \(\sum_j|a_j|^p<\infty\), with norm \((\sum_j|a_j|^p)^{1/p}\). Their standard unit vectors are denoted by \((u_j)\).
