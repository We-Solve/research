\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30003296}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathcal M_4\) denote the moduli space of smooth genus-\(4\) complex curves, understood in the usual sense of smooth, connected, projective curves over \(\C\). In the standard coarse algebraic moduli space interpretation, a closed complex algebraic subvariety \(S\subseteq\mathcal M_4\) is called complete if its structure morphism \(S\to\operatorname{Spec}\C\) is proper.

Does there exist a closed complex algebraic subvariety \(S\subseteq\mathcal M_4\) such that
\[
\dim_{\C}S=2
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
S\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}\C\ \text{is proper}?
\]
The source imposes no smoothness condition on \(S\). Completeness is required inside the moduli space of smooth curves, so a surface lying only in a compactification does not satisfy the question. The supplied record does not explicitly distinguish the coarse moduli space from the moduli stack or specify whether ``complex subvariety'' is intended algebraically or analytically; the formulation above makes the standard algebraic interpretation explicit.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the moduli space of smooth genus-\(4\) complex curves contain a complete complex surface? In the algebraic interpretation stated above, the surface must be proper over \(\C\), must have complex dimension \(2\), and need not be smooth.
