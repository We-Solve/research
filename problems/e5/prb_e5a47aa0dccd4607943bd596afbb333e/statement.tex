Use the usual spectral (Harrison) topology, generated by the sets on which finitely many specified elements are strictly positive. Define the separating ideal by \[I(\alpha,\beta)=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle=\big\langle a\in A:a(\alpha)\geq0,\ a(\beta)\leq0\big\rangle_A,\] and put $Z(g)=\{\delta\in\operatorname{Sper}A:g(\delta)=0\}$. Ask whether two real-spectrum points can remain in one connected component after removing finitely many zero sets of elements outside their separating ideal. The topology is the usual real-spectrum topology; the intended hypotheses on the ring are missing from the supplied record.
