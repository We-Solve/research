\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 11000192}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a closed connected oriented surface \(\Sigma_g\), let
\[
 X_g=\operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1\Sigma_g,\operatorname{SU}(2))/\operatorname{SU}(2)
\]
be the character quotient by conjugation. In the usual genus-\(g\geq2\) setting, give it the finite Goldman symplectic measure from its smooth irreducible locus, normalized to total mass \(1\), with the lower-dimensional singular locus treated by the intended convention. An orientation-preserving mapping class \(f\) acts by
\(F_f([\rho])=[\rho\circ(f_*)^{-1}]\), independently of choices up to conjugation, and preserves that measure.

The task is to construct a pseudo-Anosov \(f\) on a closed surface for which this transformation is not ergodic. A precise witness is a measurable \(A\subset X_g\) such that
\[
 \mu(F_f^{-1}(A)\mathbin{\triangle}A)=0,
 \qquad0<\mu(A)<1.
\]
It is not enough merely to exhibit a periodic character or a measure-zero invariant subset. The source does not explicitly fix the genus, measure, singular-locus or surface-orientation conventions; the displayed statement specifies the standard symplectic-measure interpretation, subject to checking the preceding context.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct a closed-surface pseudo-Anosov whose action on the \(\operatorname{SU}(2)\)-character variety has a proper invariant measurable subset of positive symplectic measure.
