\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be an atoroidal three-manifold and $\mathscr F$ a foliation arising from the source's slithering over $S^1$. Let $X$ be a pseudo-Anosov flow transverse to $\mathscr F$, and suppose its time-one map $Z$ preserves the foliation. Lift the data to $\widetilde M$. For a leaf $\lambda$ and its images $Z^n(\lambda)$, use the hyperbolic uniformizations by $\mathbb H^2$ supplied in the statement via Candel's theorem. Thus an iterate restricts to a map between two hyperbolic leaves, after choices of identifications.

The approximation question asks for integers $n_i$, compact hyperbolic surfaces $\Sigma_i$, and Teichm\"uller representatives $\phi_i:\Sigma_i\to\Sigma_i$ of their mapping classes, with compatible lifts/markings, such that
\[
F_i=\widetilde{\phi_i^{-1}}\circ Z^{n_i}:\mathbb H^2\longrightarrow\mathbb H^2
\]
is a $k_i$-quasi-isometry with $k_i\to1$. A Teichm\"uller representative refers to the extremal quasiconformal representative of an isotopy class, not an arbitrary homeomorphism or an isometry.

Several data needed for precision are absent. The source does not require $n_i$ positive, nonzero, or tending to infinity; permitting $n_i=0$ and identity mapping classes makes the displayed existence condition trivial. A nontrivial iterated-approximation requirement therefore needs an explicit restriction, not a silently inserted $n_i\to\infty$.

The one-constant quasi-isometry convention is also unspecified. Usually two constants appear in inequalities $K^{-1}d-A\leq d(Fx,Fy)\leq Kd+A$, together with coarse surjectivity. Requiring $K\to1$ alone, requiring additive error also to vanish, and a bi-Lipschitz bound are different assertions. The source does not select among them.

Finally, the slithering/flow conventions and global category of $M$, the surface boundary convention, and the identifications relating leaf uniformizations to universal covers of $\Sigma_i$ must be supplied. Compact closed hyperbolic surfaces have universal cover $\mathbb H^2$; compact surfaces with geodesic boundary require a different covering description. No stronger Teichm\"uller-distance or convergence conclusion is inferred from the printed composition.
