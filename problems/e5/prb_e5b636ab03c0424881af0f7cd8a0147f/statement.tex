The supplied record asks about the explicit normal forms in a complete classification of two-dimensional $\mathrm{gl}$-regular Nijenhuis operators. Neither the normal forms nor the definitions of conservation law and regular conservation law are included in the record. The following is a conditional local formulation using standard differential-geometric terminology.

Let $M$ be a smooth real manifold of dimension $2$, and let $L\colon TM\to TM$ be a smooth field of endomorphisms. Its Nijenhuis torsion is
\[
N_L(X,Y)=[LX,LY]-L[LX,Y]-L[X,LY]+L^2[X,Y]
\]
for smooth vector fields $X,Y$. The operator $L$ is Nijenhuis if $N_L=0$. It is pointwise $\mathrm{gl}$-regular if, at every point $p$, the minimal polynomial of $L_p$ equals its characteristic polynomial and therefore has degree $2$.

For a one-form $\alpha$, define $(L^*\alpha)(X)=\alpha(LX)$. Under the interpretation that a scalar conservation law is a function $f$ satisfying
\[
\dd(L^*\dd f)=0,
\]
the requested classification is: for every normal form $L$ in the classification referenced by the source, determine all local functions $f$ satisfying this equation. More explicitly, on a neighborhood $U$ where that normal form is defined, determine
\[
\mathcal C_L(U)=\{f\in C^\infty(U,\R):\dd(L^*\dd f)=0\}.
