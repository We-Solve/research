\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be an integer and let \(\Omega\subset\C^n\) be a bounded domain with smooth strictly pseudoconvex boundary. Here strict pseudoconvexity means that a smooth local defining function \(\rho\), with \(\Omega=\{\rho<0\}\) and \(\dd\rho\ne0\) on the boundary, has positive Levi form on every nonzero complex tangent vector to \(\partial\Omega\). Let \(\phi\in\mathcal C^{1,1}(\partial\Omega;\R)\), meaning that in smooth boundary coordinates its first derivatives are locally Lipschitz.

A function is plurisubharmonic if it is upper semicontinuous and its restriction to each complex line is subharmonic wherever defined. Use the convention \(d^c=\frac{i}{2}(\bar\partial-\partial)\), so that \(dd^c=i\partial\bar\partial\). For a locally bounded plurisubharmonic function, interpret \((dd^c u)^n\) as its Bedford--Taylor complex Monge--Amp\`ere measure. The normalization of \(d^c\) does not affect the equation that this measure vanishes.

Let \(u\in\operatorname{PSH}(\Omega)\cap\mathcal C(\overline\Omega;\R)\) be the unique solution specified in the source of
\[
\begin{cases}
(dd^c u)^n=0 & \text{in }\Omega,\\
u=\phi & \text{on }\partial\Omega.
\end{cases}
\]
\textbf{Requested statement (attributed to Demailly).} For every such \(\Omega\) and \(\phi\), prove, using complex analytic methods, that \(u\) is \(\mathcal C^{1,1}\)-smooth in \(\Omega\).

The interior regularity conclusion is interpreted here as \(u\in\mathcal C^{1,1}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)\): in the real coordinates of \(\C^n\cong\R^{2n}\), the function is continuously differentiable and its first derivatives are locally Lipschitz. The supplied source writes \(\mathcal C^{1,1}\) in \(\Omega\) without specifying whether a uniform bound throughout \(\Omega\) is intended. That stronger interpretation remains unresolved; no regularity on \(\overline\Omega\) is asserted here. The requirement to use complex analytic methods is part of the problem, but the source does not define the permitted class of methods.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For the continuous plurisubharmonic solution of the homogeneous complex Monge--Amp\`ere Dirichlet problem on a smooth bounded strictly pseudoconvex domain, with \(\mathcal C^{1,1}\) boundary data, give a proof by complex analytic methods of interior \(\mathcal C^{1,1}\) regularity. The intended strength of the interior bounds and the permitted methods require clarification.
