\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Does positive topological entropy for the Hamiltonian flow on a fiberwise star-shaped hypersurface extend to every hypersurface of restricted contact type?

In a standard exact symplectic setting $(W,\omega=\dd\lambda)$, a hypersurface $\Sigma$ is of restricted contact type when a global primitive of $\omega$ restricts to a contact form on $\Sigma$; if a fixed primitive is required, that must be specified. For $W=T^*Q$ with its canonical Liouville structure, fiberwise star-shaped ordinarily means transversality to the radial Liouville vector field in the cotangent fibers, with the relevant radial-side convention. These definitions explain the usual setting but the source does not actually identify $W$, $Q$, or the allowed primitives.
