\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n,q\in\N\) satisfy \(3\leq q\leq n+1\), and let \(\gamma_n\) denote the standard Gaussian probability measure on \(\R^n\). A measurable partition \(\mathcal A=(A_1,\ldots,A_q)\) consists of pairwise disjoint measurable sets whose union is \(\R^n\). It has equal Gaussian measures if \(\gamma_n(A_i)=1/q\) for every \(i\).

We use the usual Gaussian same-part definition of noise stability, which the supplied statement does not explicitly define. For \(\rho\in[-1,1]\), let \(X,Z\) be independent standard Gaussian vectors in \(\R^n\), set \(Y_\rho=\rho X+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,Z\), and define
\[
\operatorname{Stab}_\rho(\mathcal A)=\sum_{i=1}^q\mathbb P(X\in A_i,\ Y_\rho\in A_i).
\]
This definition includes the possibly degenerate joint distributions at \(\rho=\pm1\).

Choose unit vectors \(a_1,\ldots,a_q\in\R^n\) satisfying
\[
\langle a_i,a_j\rangle=-\frac{1}{q-1}\qquad(i\ne j).
\]
A standard simplex partition \(\mathcal S=(S_1,\ldots,S_q)\) is a measurable partition satisfying
\[
S_i\supseteq\{x\in\R^n:\langle a_i,x\rangle>\langle a_j,x\rangle\text{ for every }j\ne i\}.
\]
The remaining points lie in the finite union of comparison hyperplanes \(\langle a_i-a_j,x\rangle=0\); assign them measurably among the parts. These hyperplanes have Gaussian measure zero. Thus the assignment does not affect noise stability, including at \(\rho=\pm1\), and each \(S_i\) has Gaussian measure \(1/q\).

\textbf{Standard Simplex Conjecture, as supplied.} For every pair of integers \(n,q\) satisfying \(3\leq q\leq n+1\), every measurable equal-Gaussian-measure partition \(\mathcal A\) of \(\R^n\) into \(q\) parts, and every standard simplex partition \(\mathcal S\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Stab}_\rho(\mathcal A)&\leq\operatorname{Stab}_\rho(\mathcal S)&&\text{for every }\rho\in[0,1],\\
\operatorname{Stab}_\rho(\mathcal A)&\geq\operatorname{Stab}_\rho(\mathcal S)&&\text{for every }\rho\in[-1,0].
\end{aligned}
\]
No uniqueness assertion is included.

The supplied background additionally states that this conjecture implies the Plurality Is Stablest Conjecture attributed to Khot, Kindler, Mossel, and O'Donnell [KKMO04], and that the latter implies this conjecture for \(\rho\geq-1/(q-1)\). These implications are retained as source-reported qualifications; the record does not supply a formulation of the Plurality Is Stablest Conjecture or detailed hypotheses for these implications.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every \(3\leq q\leq n+1\), partition standard Gaussian space \(\R^n\) into \(q\) sets of equal measure. The supplied conjecture says that a regular-simplex partition maximizes the probability that two \(\rho\)-correlated Gaussian points fall in the same part when \(0\leq\rho\leq1\), and minimizes that probability when \(-1\leq\rho\leq0\). The registry labels the problem open; this is a supplied status, not an independently verified literature assessment.
