For every fixed $C>0$ and $0<\varepsilon<1/2$, we prove that infinitely many triples satisfy
\[
 \varepsilon n\leq a,b\leq(1-\varepsilon)n,\qquad
 a+b>n+C\log n,\qquad a!b!\mid n!(a+b-n)!.
\]
Thus the proof addresses the intended balanced version, as well as the printed version that permits trivial choices $a=b=n$. We reconstruct the carry-versus-large-valuation method from the cited contemporary proofs, with simpler nonoptimal constants.
