\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a graph $H$ with a linear vertex ordering $\prec$, let $r_<((H,\prec))$ be the least $N$ for which every red/blue coloring of the ordered complete graph on $[N]$ contains an order-preserving monochromatic copy of $H$. The copy is not required to be induced.

\textbf{Primary ordered-Ramsey question.} Is there $c>0$ such that, for every sufficiently large $n$, some $100$-regular graph $H$ on $n$ vertices satisfies
\[
 r_<((H,\prec))\geq n^{1+c}\qquad\text{for every ordering }\prec?
\]
The graph must be chosen before the universal quantifier over its orderings.

\textbf{Supplied affirmative answer.} The aid credits Balko--Jelinek--Valtr with proving that almost every fixed-degree $d$-regular graph obeys, simultaneously for all orderings,
\[
 r_<((H,\prec))\geq\frac{n^{3/2-1/d}}{4\log n\log\log n}.
\]
This is interpreted for sufficiently large admissible $n$ with $dn$ even; the theorem's precise fixed-degree range and citation are not supplied. At $d=100$, the exponent is $149/100$, and every fixed $0<c<49/100$ works after absorbing the logarithmic denominator. In particular $c=12/25$ answers the source question; even degree $100$ imposes no parity restriction on $n$.

\textbf{Reported regular completion.} If integers $0\leq d\leq D$ satisfy $D-d$ even and
\[
 n\geq\max\{D+2,2d+2\},
\]
the aid proves that every $d$-regular $n$-vertex graph $G$ has a spanning $D$-regular supergraph $H$. Edge containment gives the order-robust monotonicity
\[
 \min_\prec r_<((H,\prec))\geq\min_\prec r_<((G,\prec)).
\]
Thus robust degree-four examples lift to degree $100$ on the same vertex set for $n\geq102$. This is a transfer lemma, separate from the supplied literature proof of existence.
