\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For this draft, use the standard unweighted graph convention described below. Let \((G_n)_{n\in\N}\), where \(G_n=(V_n,E_n)\), be a sequence of finite connected simple undirected graphs with \(|V_n|\ge 2\) and \(|V_n|\to\infty\). Each edge in \(E_n\) is an unordered pair of vertices. Call this sequence an \emph{expander sequence} if there exist constants \(D<\infty\) and \(h>0\) such that, for every \(n\), every vertex of \(G_n\) has degree at most \(D\), and
\[
 |\partial_{E_n}S|\ge h|S|
 \qquad\text{whenever }S\subseteq V_n,\quad 0<|S|\le |V_n|/2,
\]
where
\[
 \partial_{E_n}S=\{\{u,v\}\in E_n:u\in S,\ v\in V_n\setminus S\}.
\]
A Banach space \(X\) is \emph{superreflexive} if it admits an equivalent uniformly convex norm. A norm \(\lVert\cdot\rVert_*\) is uniformly convex if, for each \(\varepsilon\in(0,2]\), there is \(\delta>0\) such that
\[
 \lVert x\rVert_*=\lVert y\rVert_*=1,
 \quad \lVert x-y\rVert_*\ge\varepsilon
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \left\lVert\frac{x+y}{2}\right\rVert_*\le 1-\delta.
\]
Using the quadratic Poincar\'e convention, call \((G_n)\) a \emph{superexpander sequence} if, for every superreflexive Banach space \((X,\lVert\cdot\rVert_X)\), there is a constant \(C_X\in(0,\infty)\) such that, for every \(n\in\N\) and every map \(f:V_n\to X\),
\[
 \frac{1}{|V_n|^2}\sum_{u,v\in V_n}
 \lVert f(u)-f(v)\rVert_X^2
 \le
 \frac{C_X}{|E_n|}\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E_n}
 \lVert f(u)-f(v)\rVert_X^2.
\]
Here \(C_X\) may depend on \(X\) and on the graph sequence, but must be independent of \(n\) and \(f\).

\textbf{Question.} Is every expander sequence a superexpander sequence?
