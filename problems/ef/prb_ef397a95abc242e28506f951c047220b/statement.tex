\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Question 1.18 conjectures that a smooth complex quadric contains no two disjoint Lagrangian spheres and calls the even-dimensional case trivial. Its displayed coordinates \(z_0,\ldots,z_{n+1}\) conflict with the printed ambient \(\C\PP^n\). For the aid's dimensionally consistent interpretation use
\[
 Q^n=\{[z_0:\cdots:z_{n+1}]\in\C\PP^{n+1}:
 z_0^2+\cdots+z_{n+1}^2=0\},
\]
of complex dimension \(n\), equipped with the standard restricted Fubini--Study symplectic form. Here \(\C\PP^j\) denotes complex projective space. The ambient correction and symplectic convention are explicit working assumptions, not a verified correction of the canonical typography. A Lagrangian sphere means a smoothly embedded \(S^n\subset Q^n\) on which the symplectic form restricts to zero. The intended question in dimensions \(n\ge2\) is
\[
 \forall L_0,L_1\subset Q^n\text{ Lagrangian spheres},\qquad L_0\cap L_1\ne\varnothing.
\]
The source does not state this lower bound; in the literal \(n=1\) case the conic is a symplectic two-sphere and two disjoint embedded latitude circles are Lagrangian, so that version is false.

For every even \(n\ge2\), the aid reports a stronger integral intersection proposition. Let \(h\in H^2(Q^n;\Z)\) be the restricted hyperplane class. Then \(H_n(Q^n;\Z)\) has rank two and
\[
 K=\ker\bigl(H_n(Q^n;\Z)\longrightarrow\Z,
 \quad a\longmapsto\langle h^{n/2},a\rangle\bigr)
\]
is a primitive rank-one subgroup. If \(\delta\) is a primitive generator of \(K\), every oriented Lagrangian sphere has
\[
 [L]=\delta\ \text{or}\ -\delta,
 \qquad |[L]\cdot[L]|=|\delta\cdot\delta|=\chi(S^n)=2.
\]
Consequently any two such spheres satisfy
\[
 |[L_0]\cdot[L_1]|=2,
\]
so they intersect; if their intersection is transverse, it contains at least two points. The absolute-value statement avoids choices of orientation and intersection-sign convention. The Lagrangian condition is used to place the class in \(K\), not merely to assert that a sphere is topologically embedded.

For odd \(n\), the aid discusses Entov--Polterovich and Biran--Cornea arguments but explicitly disclaims verification of the characteristic-zero/integral orientation extension on which those arguments depend. Thus it records that route only conditionally; no unconditional odd-dimensional theorem is asserted in this draft. The needed extension and its exact hypotheses are not reproduced.
