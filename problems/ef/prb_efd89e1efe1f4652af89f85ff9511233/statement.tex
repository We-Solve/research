\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A profinite group is a topological group isomorphic to an inverse limit of finite discrete groups. Here isomorphism is interpreted as topological group isomorphism; the supplied record does not explicitly specify this convention or the universe and coding of profinite groups.

For equivalence relations \(E\) on a standard Borel space \(X\) and \(F\) on a standard Borel space \(Y\), write \(E\leq_B F\) if there is a Borel map \(f:X\to Y\) such that
\[
\forall x,x'\in X,\qquad x\,E\,x'\iff f(x)\,F\,f(x').
\]
Write \(E<_B F\) when \(E\leq_B F\) and \(F\not\leq_B E\). Let \(\Delta_{\R}\) denote equality on \(\R\). An equivalence relation is smooth if it is Borel reducible to \(\Delta_{\R}\). Let \(S_\infty\) be the group of permutations of \(\N\), with the topology of pointwise convergence, and let \(E_{\mathrm{gr}}\) be isomorphism of simple graphs on vertex set \(\N\), coded in \(2^{[\N]^2}\). This is a standard representative of the \(S_\infty\)-complete degree: it is an orbit equivalence relation of a Borel \(S_\infty\)-action to which every such orbit equivalence relation is Borel reducible.

Subject to specifying a standard Borel space \(\mathcal P\) of codes for the intended profinite groups, the question is whether there exists a Borel subset \(\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal P\) such that, for

\Delta_{\R}<_B E_{\mathcal C}<_B E_{\mathrm{gr}}.
\]
The source asks in particular whether such a \(\mathcal C\) can consist entirely of abelian profinite groups.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does some Borel family of profinite groups have topological isomorphism strictly above equality of real numbers and strictly below an \(S_\infty\)-complete equivalence relation under Borel reducibility? Can the family consist of abelian groups? The source reports an affirmative answer by Gao and Nies, while the registry labels the problem open; the scope of that answer and the intended coding require clarification.
