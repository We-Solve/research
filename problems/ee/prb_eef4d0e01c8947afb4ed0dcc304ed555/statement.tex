Under the usual complex-coefficient convention, an ordinary Dirichlet series is an expression
\[
F(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n n^{-s},\qquad a_n\in\C,\quad s\in\C,\quad n^{-s}=\exp(-s\log n).
\]
For a real number \(\sigma\), write
\[
H_\sigma=\{s\in\C:\operatorname{Re}s>\sigma\}.
\]
Convergence in this half-plane means that the partial sums converge at every point of \(H_\sigma\); their limit defines a holomorphic function there.
