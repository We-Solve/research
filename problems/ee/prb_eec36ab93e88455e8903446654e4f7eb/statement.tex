\section{Capacity and Reliability of Mismatched Decoding}\label{30005062}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 30005062 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


For a memoryless channel $W(y\mid x)$, a mismatched decoder ranks codewords by a prescribed metric $q$ instead of the channel likelihood; in the product-metric formulation it maximizes $\prod_{t=1}^n q(x_t,y_t)$. Mismatched capacity is the supremum of rates achievable with vanishing decoding error for this fixed metric. At rate $R$, the reliability function measures the best attainable exponent $\liminf_{n\to\infty}-n^{-1}\log P_e^{(n)}$. A single-letter characterization uses distributions on one-use variables rather than a limit over block lengths.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find a single-letter characterization of mismatched decoding capacity, and determine the exact reliability function for mismatched decoding. \sref{30005062}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
Tie breaking, input constraints, alphabet and average/maximum error conventions are those of the cited problem. The metric is fixed, so optimizing it into maximum-likelihood decoding would change the question.
