\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#34; reconstruction, 2026-09-23.}
For a permutation $\pi$ of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, let $S(\pi)$ count the
distinct sums of nonempty intervals of positions. Must $S(\pi)=o(n^2)$
uniformly over permutations? We construct permutations of every even
size $n=2m$ having at least $m(m+1)$ distinct interval sums. In particular
the proposed assertion is false.
