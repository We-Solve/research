\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S^1=\R/\Z\), and let \(\operatorname{Diff}^{\infty}_{+}(S^1)\) be the group of smooth orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms of the circle. An action of \(\Z^2\) is a homomorphism
\[
\rho:\Z^2\longrightarrow\operatorname{Diff}^{\infty}_{+}(S^1).
\]
Writing \(f=\rho(1,0)\) and \(g=\rho(0,1)\), this means \(fg=gf\). The action is faithful if
\[
 f^m g^n=\operatorname{id}_{S^1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(m,n)=(0,0)
 \qquad\text{for all }(m,n)\in\Z^2.
\]
The supplied question, attributed to Navas (2017), asks whether there exists such a faithful action that is \(C^{\infty}\) structurally stable.

The supplied record does not define structural stability. One possible precise interpretation uses the space
\[
\mathcal A=\{(f,g)\in\operatorname{Diff}^{\infty}_{+}(S^1)^2:fg=gf\}
\]
with the subspace topology induced by the product \(C^{\infty}\) topology. Under the interpretation that stability means topological conjugacy among all nearby actions with the generators fixed, the question becomes: does there exist a faithful \((f,g)\in\mathcal A\) and a neighborhood \(U\) of \((f,g)\) in \(\mathcal A\) such that, for every \((\widetilde f,\widetilde g)\in U\), there exists a homeomorphism \(h:S^1\to S^1\) satisfying
\[
 h\circ f=\widetilde f\circ h,
 \qquad
 h\circ g=\widetilde g\circ h?
\]
This interpretation requires source confirmation: the record does not specify the regularity or orientation of the conjugacy, whether perturbations are restricted to faithful actions, or whether the conjugacy must be close to the identity. These unresolved choices are part of the formulation, and the displayed interpretation is not asserted to be the source's intended definition.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Navas asks whether two commuting smooth orientation-preserving circle diffeomorphisms can generate a faithful \(\Z^2\)-action that is \(C^{\infty}\) structurally stable. The supplied record leaves the precise meaning of structural stability unresolved. Its registry status is supplied as open.
