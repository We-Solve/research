\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a complex manifold \(X\), let \(\mathcal O(X)\) denote its holomorphic functions. Recall that \(X\) is Stein if global holomorphic functions separate distinct points, provide local holomorphic coordinates at every point, and make \(X\) holomorphically convex: for every nonempty compact \(K\subseteq X\), the set
\[
\widehat K_X=\{x\in X: |f(x)|\leq \sup_{y\in K}|f(y)|\text{ for every }f\in\mathcal O(X)\}
\]
is compact.

The source does not define ``locally Stein.'' A conventional boundary formulation for an open subset \(U\subseteq M\) is
\[
\forall p\in\partial_M U\quad \exists\text{ an open neighborhood }V_p\subseteq M\text{ of }p
\quad U\cap V_p\text{ is Stein}.
\]
This interpretation is provisional and requires review against the source's intended convention.

\textbf{Problem (N. Sibony).} Find appropriate conditions \(\mathcal C\) on a compact complex manifold \(M\) ensuring that
\[
\mathcal C(M)\ \Longrightarrow\
\bigl[\forall U\subseteq M\text{ open},\quad
U\text{ locally Stein}\ \Longrightarrow\ U\text{ Stein}\bigr].
\]
This is a request for sufficient conditions, with no specified class of admissible conditions or optimality requirement. The source does not require \(U\) to be proper, connected, or to have nonempty boundary. In particular, its quantifier includes \(U=M\), for which the displayed boundary condition is vacuous. The intended treatment of this case is unresolved; no additional restriction is imposed here.
