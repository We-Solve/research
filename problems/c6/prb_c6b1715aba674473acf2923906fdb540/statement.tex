\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathbb D=\{z\in\C:|z|<1\}\), and let \(\Gamma\le\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb D)\) be a discontinuous group of M\"obius transformations, in the usual discrete disk-automorphism sense. A meromorphic map \(f:\mathbb D\to\widehat\C\) is automorphic with respect to \(\Gamma\) when
\[
f(\gamma z)=f(z)\qquad(\gamma\in\Gamma,\ z\in\mathbb D).
\]
Its spherical derivative is \(f^\#(z)=|f'(z)|/(1+|f(z)|^2)\), extended across poles using the coordinate \(1/f\). The required normality condition is
\[
\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}(1-|z|^2)f^\#(z)<\infty.
\]
