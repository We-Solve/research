\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 700962}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a group. For subsets \(A,F\subseteq G\), write
\[
A^{-1}A=\{a^{-1}b:a,b\in A\},\qquad A^{-1}AF=\{a^{-1}bf:a,b\in A,\ f\in F\}.
\]
The question is whether, for every group \(G\), every integer \(n\geq 1\), and every partition
\[
G=A_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup A_n,
\]
there exist an index \(i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\) and a finite subset \(F\subseteq G\) such that
\[
G=A_i^{-1}A_iF\qquad\text{and}\qquad |F|\leq n.
\]
The supplied source states that such an index and a finite set \(F\) always exist without the bound \(|F|\leq n\), citing I. V. Protasov (1993), and that the bound holds for amenable groups. Here amenability means the existence of a finitely additive probability measure on all subsets of \(G\) that is invariant under left translation. These background assertions are retained as supplied source claims; they have not been independently verified.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every partition of any group into \(n\) subsets, must some cell \(A_i\) satisfy \(G=A_i^{-1}A_iF\) for a set \(F\) of at most \(n\) group elements? The source attributes the problem to I. V. Protasov and states that the answer is affirmative for amenable groups.
