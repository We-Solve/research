\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The supplied record does not explicitly specify the graph category or the edge-density convention. The following precise formulation adopts finite simple undirected graphs and edge density \(p(G)=e(G)/\binom{v(G)}{2}\); these conventions require confirmation from the primary source. Here \(v(H)=|V(H)|\) and \(e(H)=|E(H)|\). For graphs \(F,G\), let \(\operatorname{hom}(F,G)\) denote the number of maps \(\phi:V(F)\to V(G)\) such that \(\{\phi(u),\phi(v)\}\in E(G)\) whenever \(\{u,v\}\in E(F)\). For \(v(G)\geq2\) and \(p=p(G)>0\), define
\[
t_p(F,G)=p^{-e(F)}v(G)^{-v(F)}\operatorname{hom}(F,G).
\]
Write \(C_4\) for the cycle on four vertices and \(K_3\) for the complete graph on three vertices.

Does there exist a sequence \((G_n)_{n\geq1}\) of such graphs, with \(N_n=v(G_n)\geq2\) and \(p_n=p(G_n)>0\), satisfying all of the following conditions?
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(N_n\to\infty\) and \(p_n=N_n^{-o(1)}\), meaning
\[
\frac{\log(1/p_n)}{\log N_n}\longrightarrow0.
\]
\item For every finite simple undirected graph \(F\), including graphs with isolated vertices, the finite real limit
\[
a_F=\lim_{n\to\infty}t_{p_n}(F,G_n)
\]
exists.
\item \(a_{C_4}=a_{K_3}=1\).
\item The limits satisfy the uniform bound
\[
\sup_{\substack{F\text{ finite simple undirected}\ e(F)\geq1}}a_F^{1/e(F)}<\infty.
\]
\item There is at least one finite simple undirected graph \(F\) for which \(a_F\neq1\).
\end{enumerate}
The restriction \(e(F)\geq1\) in the supremum makes the exponent meaningful; the supplied statement writes \(\sup_F\) without this restriction. Edgeless graphs still occur in the limit condition and have normalized homomorphism count \(1\). No connectedness restriction on \(F\), and no additional requirement \(p_n\to0\), is stated in the supplied record.
