\section{Exact period-quotient reduction and unary lift cost for hidden shift on Z\textasciicircum{}d}\label{20001332}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 20001332 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A hidden-shift instance consists of functions $f_0,f_1$ on a group $G$ related by $f_1(x)=f_0(xs)$ for an unknown shift $s$, with the additive version $f_1(x)=f_0(x+s)$ for abelian groups. The task is to recover a compatible shift from the stipulated function access. For $G=\mathbb Z^d$, periodicity under a sublattice means $f(x+\lambda)=f(x)$ for every period $\lambda$. Shifts differing by a period are indistinguishable. Unary query cost counts the size of an integer argument in unary, rather than only its binary length.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Hidden shift on $\mathbb Z^d$ where $f_0$ and $f_1$ are periodic under unary oracle cost. \sref{20001332}{2}
