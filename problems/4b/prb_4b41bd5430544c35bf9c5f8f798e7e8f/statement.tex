\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a rational map, the Julia set \(J\) is the complement of the normality locus of its iterates in the Riemann sphere. For a polynomial \(P\), its filled Julia set is \(K(P)=\{z\in\C:(P^m(z))_{m\geq0}\text{ is bounded}\}\). A Siegel disk is a maximal rotation domain conformally conjugate to an irrational rigid disk rotation. For an irrational rotation number \(\theta\) with continued-fraction convergent denominators \(q_j\), the Brjuno condition is \(\sum_j(\log q_{j+1})/q_j<\infty\); bounded type means bounded continued-fraction coefficients. A Jordan arc is a space homeomorphic to a closed interval, a Jordan curve one homeomorphic to a circle, and a quasi-arc is a quasisymmetric image of an interval in the intended ambient metric.

\item[6.] Find a combinatorial description of Branner--Hubbard slices of cubic polynomials. The slices and the intended combinatorial equivalence are not defined here.
\item[7.] Must every Cantor Julia set of a rational map have zero spherical area? Also, is the Brjuno condition optimal for rational maps, for the intended property not specified by the extraction?
\item[8.] Is the Brjuno condition optimal for entire transcendental maps?
\item[9.] Is it optimal for relatively compact maximal Siegel disks? In addition, for \(P_\lambda(z)=\lambda z+z^2\) and \(K_\lambda=K(P_\lambda)\), is there a single \(C>0\) such that
\[
\operatorname{area}(K_\lambda)\geq C\qquad\text{for every }\lambda\in\C\text{ with }|\lambda|\leq1?
\]
The area convention for this separate question is not explicit.
\item[10.] Is every boundary of a Siegel disk of a polynomial or rational map a Jordan curve?
\item[11.] Is the Zakeri set always a Jordan arc in the moduli space of cubic polynomials?
\item[12.] Does the corresponding Jordan-arc assertion hold for quadratic rational maps and for more general bicritical maps?
\item[13.] For bounded-type rotation numbers, is the Zakeri arc a quasi-arc? Also, can generalized tuning replace the closure of a basin of a rational map \(R\), modeled by \(z\mapsto z^d\), by the filled Julia set of any degree-\(d\) polynomial \(P\); and, if so, does local connectivity transfer? \item[16.] Is the closure of the rigidity locus of cubic polynomials the Misiurewicz locus? Both named loci and the closure topology need to be specified.
