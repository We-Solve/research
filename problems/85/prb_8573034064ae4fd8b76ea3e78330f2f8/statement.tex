\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a higher-rank simple Lie group, as in the supplied source. Fix a Haar measure on \(G\). A lattice \(M\leq G\) is a discrete subgroup for which the quotient \(G/M\) has finite invariant volume; write its covolume as \(\operatorname{vol}(G/M)\). For a finitely generated group \(M\), let
\[
d(M)=\min\{\lvert S\rvert:S\subseteq M,\ \langle S\rangle=M\}
\]
be its minimal number of generators. The displayed question presupposes that these generator numbers are finite.

The supplied question asks whether, for every such \(G\) and every sequence of lattices \((M_i)_{i\geq1}\) in \(G\) with unbounded covolumes, one has
\[
\lim_{i\to\infty}\frac{d(M_i)-1}{\operatorname{vol}(G/M_i)}=0.
\]
No torsion-free hypothesis is imposed in this question.

The record also does not specify the rank convention or the connectedness and centre conventions for a simple Lie group. Under the real-rank convention, higher rank means \(\operatorname{rank}_{\R}(G)\geq2\); this interpretation requires confirmation against the source.
