There are no absolute constants \(D\) and \(\varepsilon>0\)
forcing every sufficiently large graph with \(N\log N\)
edges to have a \(D\)-balanced \(m\)-vertex subgraph with
\(\varepsilon m\log m\) edges, for every prescribed \(m\).
We reconstruct Alon's layered random counterexample:
\emph{Problems and results in extremal combinatorics, II},
Proposition 2.1,
\url{https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~nogaa/PDFS/extremalII1.pdf}.
See \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/803}.
All logarithms in this proof are base two.
