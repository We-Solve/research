\section*{Hahn–Wilson thick generation at fp-type two}\label{30007503}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each prime p, must every connective p-complete spectrum of exact fp-type two lie in the thick subcategory generated by $BP\langle 2\rangle _p^\wedge $? The claimed counterexamples occur at every sufficiently large prime and also have $L_{2}^fX\simeq L_{2}X$ and $L_{T(2)}X\simeq L_{K(2)}X$.
