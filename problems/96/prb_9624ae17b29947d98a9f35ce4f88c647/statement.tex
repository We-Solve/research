\section*{Cartan determinant conjecture}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $A$ be an Artin algebra: an associative unital algebra, finite as a module
over a commutative Artinian ring acting centrally. Choose representatives
$S_1,\ldots,S_n$ of the simple left $A$-modules and their projective covers
$P_j\twoheadrightarrow S_j$. Its integral Cartan matrix is
\[
 C(A)_{ij}=[P_j:S_i],
\]
where the right-hand side counts composition factors. Use the same ordering
for the simple modules and their corresponding projective covers.
The global dimension of $A$ is the supremum of the projective dimensions of
left $A$-modules.

Does finite global dimension imply $\det C(A)=1$ for every Artin algebra $A$?
No algebraically closed coefficient-field hypothesis is imposed.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Must an Artin algebra of finite global dimension have Cartan determinant one?
