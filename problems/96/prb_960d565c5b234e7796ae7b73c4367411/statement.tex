\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(T^2=\R^2/\Z^2\), and let \(F\) be a Finsler metric on \(T^2\). Let \(\phi_F^t\) denote its unit-speed geodesic flow. The entropy hypothesis is
\[
 h_{\mathrm{top}}(\phi_F^1)=0.
\]
Write
\[
 S^1_{\mathrm{rat}}=\left\{\frac{v}{\lVert v\rVert_2}:v\in\Z^2\setminus\{0\}\right\}\subset S^1.
\]
Thus rational directions are represented by nonzero integer vectors; opposite directions are retained separately.

The source does not specify the precise conventions for ``nonminimal'' or ``asymptotic direction.'' The following makes explicit a possible interpretation, whose agreement with the source requires review. Let \(\widetilde F\) be the lifted Finsler metric on \(\R^2\), and let \(d_{\widetilde F}(x,y)\) be its forward distance, defined by the infimum of \(\widetilde F\)-lengths of curves from \(x\) to \(y\). A complete unit-speed geodesic \(c:\R\to T^2\) is called globally minimizing in the universal cover if a lift \(\widetilde c:\R\to\R^2\) satisfies
\[
 L_{\widetilde F}\bigl(\widetilde c|_{[a,b]}\bigr)
 =d_{\widetilde F}\bigl(\widetilde c(a),\widetilde c(b)\bigr)
 \qquad\text{for every }a<b.
\]
Under this interpretation, it is nonminimal if this equality fails for some \(a<b\). Define its forward asymptotic direction, when the displacement tends to infinity and the limit exists, by
\[
 \rho_+(c)=\lim_{t\to+\infty}
 \frac{\widetilde c(t)-\widetilde c(0)}
 {\lVert\widetilde c(t)-\widetilde c(0)\rVert_2}.
\]
These definitions are independent of the choice of lift. With these explicit conventions, put
\[
 E_\xi(F)=\left\{c:\R\to T^2:
 \begin{array}{l}
 c\text{ is a complete unit-speed }F\text{-geodesic},\\
 c\text{ is nonminimal in the universal cover},\\
 \rho_+(c)\text{ exists and equals }\xi
 \end{array}\right\},\qquad \xi\in S^1_{\mathrm{rat}}.
\]
The supplied problem asks whether, for every Finsler metric \(F\) satisfying the entropy hypothesis, each of the following assertions holds:
\begin{enumerate}
\item There exists \(\xi\in S^1_{\mathrm{rat}}\) such that \(E_\xi(F)=\varnothing\).
\item The set
\[
 \left\{\xi\in S^1_{\mathrm{rat}}:E_\xi(F)\ne\varnothing\right\}
\]
is finite.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Short English statement}
For every Finsler metric on the two-torus whose geodesic flow has zero topological entropy, must some rational direction contain no nonminimal geodesic? Must only finitely many rational directions contain such geodesics?
