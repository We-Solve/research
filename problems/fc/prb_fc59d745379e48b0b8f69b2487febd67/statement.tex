\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(F(a,b)\) be the free group on the two generators \(a,b\). Write
\[
\operatorname{GL}_3(\C)=\{M\in M_3(\C):\det M\ne 0\},\qquad
\operatorname{SL}_3(\C)=\{M\in M_3(\C):\det M=1\}.
\]
For \(H\in\{\operatorname{GL}_3(\C),\operatorname{SL}_3(\C)\}\) and \(A,B\in H\), let \(\rho_{A,B}:F(a,b)\to H\) be the unique group homomorphism satisfying \(\rho_{A,B}(a)=A\) and \(\rho_{A,B}(b)=B\). For \(w\in F(a,b)\), set \(w(A,B)=\rho_{A,B}(w)\); thus inverse letters are evaluated using matrix inverses. For \(M=(m_{ij})\in M_3(\C)\), let \(\operatorname{tr}(M)=\sum_{i=1}^{3}m_{ii}\). Two elements \(u,v\in F(a,b)\) are conjugate if \(v=gug^{-1}\) for some \(g\in F(a,b)\).

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Do there exist \(u,v\in F(a,b)\) such that
\[
\bigl(\forall g\in F(a,b),\ v\ne gug^{-1}\bigr)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\bigl(\forall A,B\in\operatorname{GL}_3(\C),\
\operatorname{tr}(u(A,B))=\operatorname{tr}(v(A,B))\bigr)?
\]
\item[(b)] Do there exist \(u,v\in F(a,b)\) such that
\[
\bigl(\forall g\in F(a,b),\ v\ne gug^{-1}\bigr)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\bigl(\forall A,B\in\operatorname{SL}_3(\C),\
\operatorname{tr}(u(A,B))=\operatorname{tr}(v(A,B))\bigr)?
\]
\end{enumerate}
The existence questions in (a) and (b) are posed separately; their witnesses are not required to coincide. The supplied record imposes no additional restrictions on the words or matrix pairs. Its designation of the problem as open is supplied source metadata, not an independently verified mathematical or literature assessment.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can two nonconjugate elements of the free group on two generators have equal matrix traces under every substitution of invertible complex \(3\times3\) matrices? Ask the same question when both substituted matrices must have determinant \(1\).
