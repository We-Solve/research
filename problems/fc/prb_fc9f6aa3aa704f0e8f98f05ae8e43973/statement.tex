\section{Gauge Fixing for Quantum Vertex Algebra Deformations}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} A vertex algebra packages fields and their operator products; a quantum vertex algebra has a deformed version of these algebraic structures. Its deformation complex controls infinitesimal changes and their compatibility equations. In ordinary Calabi--Yau Kodaira--Spencer theory, a $(0,1)$-form $A$ valued in the holomorphic tangent bundle satisfies $\bar\partial A+\tfrac12[A,A]=0$. Tian gauge is the gauge choice enabling the corresponding perturbative action treatment. The report seeks an analogue for its coupled quantum-vertex-algebra deformation equations.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualifications.} The required gauge and physical interpretation are two separate targets. The report's proposed fivebrane interpretation is suggestive context, not an established identification or a definition that may be assumed.

\paragraph{Catalogue mathematical target.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For the deformation theory of quantum vertex algebras, find an analogue of the Tian gauge enabling perturbation theory, and identify the physical degrees of freedom described by the resulting action principle.

\paragraph{Saved literature context.} The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: Heterotic Kodaira--Spencer theories and one-loop partition functions have since been constructed, with anomaly-cancellation results, but a general Tian-gauge analogue and complete physical degree-of-freedom interpretation remain open. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find a workable gauge for perturbing quantum vertex algebras and identify the physical fields represented by its action.
