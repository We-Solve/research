\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $P$ be a lattice polytope in a lattice $M$ and use the induced lattice in its affine span. Write
\[
 E_P(m)=\#(mP\cap M),\qquad mP=\{mx:x\in P\},
\]
for its Ehrhart counting function. A smooth full-dimensional lattice polytope is conventionally one whose primitive edge directions at every vertex form a lattice basis. Normality, or the integer-decomposition property in the usual full-lattice convention, means that for every $m\ge1$, each lattice point of $mP$ is a sum of $m$ lattice points of $P$.

The source requests three exploratory directions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Find examples with $E_P(1)$ substantially smaller than $E_P(2)$, preferably with $P$ smooth. The source gives no ratio, difference, dimension range, or extremal target defining substantially smaller. Smoothness is a preference in this clause, not an unconditional hypothesis for every example.
\item Test smooth polytopes arising from smooth Fano toric varieties and from small projective embeddings in the intended search framework. No dimension bound, list of varieties, line bundles, or measure of smallness is supplied. A smooth Fano variety has ample anticanonical bundle, but the record does not restrict all the associated polytopes to the anticanonical embedding.
\item Determine whether useful invariants, including Ehrhart functions, detect nonnormality. The source does not demand that a chosen invariant give a complete necessary-and-sufficient criterion or specify whether detecting means a sufficient certificate, an effective algorithm, or distinguishing examples.
\end{enumerate}
