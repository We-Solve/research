\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Conjecture 3.2 has two assertions concerning the source's map $U$ and parameter $k$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $k=1$, then $U$ is surjective.
\item In general, every $\operatorname{PSL}_2(\R)$ representation with dense image lies in $\operatorname{Image}(U)$.
\end{enumerate}
Here $\operatorname{PSL}_2(\R)=\operatorname{SL}_2(\R)/\{\pm I\}$, and dense image means density in its ordinary Lie-group topology: for an intended source group $\Pi$, $\overline{\rho(\Pi)}=\operatorname{PSL}_2(\R)$. Zariski density is not substituted.

These are only logical restatements of the two claims; the missing construction and admissibility conditions prevent a complete standalone conjecture.
