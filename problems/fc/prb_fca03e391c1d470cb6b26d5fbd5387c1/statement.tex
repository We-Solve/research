\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\) be a number field, let \(G\) be a finite group, and let \(S\) be a finite set of primes of \(k\). For each \(\mathfrak p\in S\), write \(k_{\mathfrak p}\) for the completion of \(k\) at \(\mathfrak p\), and prescribe a finite Galois extension \(F_{\mathfrak p}/k_{\mathfrak p}\) such that there exists an injective homomorphism
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(F_{\mathfrak p}/k_{\mathfrak p})\hookrightarrow G.
\]
The injections are existence conditions; the record does not prescribe particular embeddings into \(G\).

\textbf{Question as supplied.} For every such choice of \(k,G,S\), and \((F_{\mathfrak p}/k_{\mathfrak p})_{\mathfrak p\in S}\), does there exist a finite Galois extension \(F/k\) satisfying
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(F/k)\cong G
\]
and, for every \(\mathfrak p\in S\), a prime \(\mathfrak P\) of \(F\) above \(\mathfrak p\) such that
\[
F_{\mathfrak P}\cong_{k_{\mathfrak p}} F_{\mathfrak p}?
\]
Here \(F_{\mathfrak P}\) is the completion of \(F\) at \(\mathfrak P\), and the displayed isomorphism is an isomorphism of extensions of \(k_{\mathfrak p}\). Since \(F/k\) is Galois, its completions above a fixed \(\mathfrak p\) are mutually isomorphic over \(k_{\mathfrak p}\). No ramification conditions outside \(S\) are imposed in the supplied question.
