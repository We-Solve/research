\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(p\) be a prime, and let \(P\) be a finite group of order \(p^n\) for some integer \(n\geq 0\). Let \(A,B\leq P\) satisfy
\[
P=AB=\{ab:a\in A,\ b\in B\},
\]
where \(A\) is abelian and \(B\) is nilpotent of class two. Write
\[
[B,B]=\langle x^{-1}y^{-1}xy:x,y\in B\rangle,
\qquad
Z(B)=\{z\in B:zb=bz\text{ for every }b\in B\}.
\]
The class-two condition gives \([B,B]\leq Z(B)\). If ``class two'' means class exactly two, additionally require \([B,B]\neq\{1\}\); the supplied record does not specify whether class at most two is permitted.

The question is whether, for every such choice of \(p,P,A,B\), there exists a subgroup \(B_0\leq B\) such that
\[
[B_0,B_0]=\{1\}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\langle A,[B,B]\rangle=AB_0,
\]
where \(\langle A,[B,B]\rangle\) is the subgroup generated by \(A\) and \([B,B]\), and \(AB_0=\{ab_0:a\in A,\ b_0\in B_0\}\) denotes a set product.

The source adds ``Suppose, if necessary, \(A\cap B=1\).'' Thus it also permits considering the same universal assertion restricted to factorizations satisfying \(A\cap B=\{1\}\). \subsection{Short English statement}
For a finite \(p\)-group that is the product of an abelian subgroup \(A\) and a class-two subgroup \(B\), must the subgroup generated by \(A\) and the commutator subgroup of \(B\) equal \(AB_0\) for some abelian subgroup \(B_0\leq B\)? The source permits adding \(A\cap B=\{1\}\) if necessary, leaving the intended scope unresolved.
