\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each integer \(n\geq 1\), let \(B_n=(b_{ij})_{1\leq i,j\leq n}\) be a random matrix whose entries are mutually independent and satisfy
\[
\mathbb P(b_{ij}=1)=\mathbb P(b_{ij}=-1)=\frac12.
\]
Regard \(B_n\) as a matrix over \(\R\), and write
\[
p_n:=\mathbb P(\det B_n=0).
\]
The supplied historical problem asks for the sharp asymptotic behavior of \(p_n\) as \(n\to\infty\), with the conjectural exponential scale
\[
p_n=\left(\frac12+o_n(1)\right)^n

\]

p_n=2^{-n}\exp(o(n)).
