\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 6000001}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A statistical manifold $(M,g,\nabla,\nabla^*)$ has a Riemannian metric $g$ and torsion-free affine connections dual with respect to $g$:
\[
X(g(Y,Z))=g(\nabla_XY,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_X^*Z).
\]
A dually flat manifold $(N,G,\overline\nabla,\overline\nabla^*)$ has both affine connections flat. In the usual statistical-submanifold sense, a realization of $M$ in $N$ is an isometric immersion $i:M\to N$ for which the tangent projections of the ambient dual connections induce the given ones:
\[
i^*G=g,\qquad
(\overline\nabla_{i_*X}i_*Y)^\top=i_*(\nabla_XY),\qquad
(\overline\nabla^*_{i_*X}i_*Y)^\top=i_*(\nabla_X^*Y).
\]
Here $\top$ means orthogonal projection onto the tangent space of the immersed submanifold.

For an $n$-dimensional statistical manifold, find conditions for such a realization in an $m$-dimensional dually flat manifold with $m>n$. Determine whether some finite $m$ always suffices; if not, identify the additional quantity or obstruction governing finite-dimensional realization. The supplied item does not define what is meant by this additional quantity, nor distinguish local from global realization, immersion from embedding, or a prescribed $m$ from an adjustable ambient dimension. These distinctions remain part of the required formulation, rather than being silently settled by the immersion convention above.
\subsection{Short English statement}
When can a statistical manifold be realized inside a higher-dimensional dually flat manifold with its metric and both connections induced from the ambient space? Determine the conditions or additional invariant controlling finite-dimensional realization.
