\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $K^{\theta}=(K^{\theta}_{s,t})_{s\le t}$ be the stochastic flow of probability kernels obtained from the source's $\theta$-joining of the Arratia flow, and let $L=(L_{s,t})_{s\le t}$ be the Le Jan--Raimond sticky flow to which the source compares it. Their construction and the matching of their parameters must be recovered from the report. Write $S$ for their common state space. For a flow $K$, its $n$-point transition kernel is
\[
 P^{K,n}_{s,t}(x_1,\ldots,x_n; A)
 =\E\!\left[\left(\bigotimes_{j=1}^n K_{s,t}(x_j,\cdot)\right)(A)\right],
 \qquad A\subseteq S^n\ \text{measurable}.
\]
This definition uses the same random kernel in all coordinates and averages over the flow randomness. Agreement of $n$-point motions means equality of the resulting transition families for all relevant times and initial configurations.

The conjecture is that, for the two flows with the parameters intended in the report,
\[
 \forall n\in\N,\ n\ge4:\qquad
 P^{K^{\theta},n}\ne P^{L,n},
\]
and consequently their laws as stochastic flows of kernels are different. Here inequality means that, for each such $n$, some admissible $s<t$, initial vector $x\in S^n$, and measurable $A\subseteq S^n$ distinguish the transition probabilities. It does not assert inequality for every initial vector or every test set.

According to the supplied literature assessment, the report establishes agreement of the two-point motion, whereas disagreement from four points onward is conjectural. No conclusion about the three-point motion is supplied. The record does not define the joining, the sticky-flow parameters, the state-space conventions, or the admissible range of $\theta$; it also does not state whether the claim is to hold for every admissible $\theta$ or only specified nondegenerate parameters. These qualifications remain unresolved and prevent an unconditional parameter-uniform formulation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The two constructions agree at the level of two-point motion according to the source. The conjecture asks whether every motion involving at least four sampled particles distinguishes their transition laws, and hence distinguishes the flows themselves. The joining and parameter matching still need explicit definitions.
