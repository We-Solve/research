\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a totally disconnected locally compact Hausdorff topological group, with identity \(1\), and let \(\alpha:G\to G\) be a topological automorphism. Its contraction group is
\[
U_\alpha=\{x\in G:\alpha^n(x)\longrightarrow1\text{ as }n\to\infty\}.
\]
Explicitly, \(x\in U_\alpha\) means that for every neighborhood \(V\) of \(1\), there exists \(N\geq0\) such that \(\alpha^n(x)\in V\) for every integer \(n\geq N\).

The question is to identify conditions on \((G,\alpha)\) under which \(U_\alpha\) is closed in the given topology of \(G\), that is,
\[
\overline{U_\alpha}^{\,G}=U_\alpha.
\]
It is a request for criteria or a characterization, not an assertion that every contraction group is closed. No second-countability, metrizability, linearity, compact-generation, or building-action hypothesis is stated. Accordingly closedness is understood topologically, rather than being reduced without justification to a criterion using only sequences. The extraction gives no particular proposed necessary-and-sufficient condition or distinguished subclass whose case is the intended remaining problem.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which totally disconnected locally compact groups and automorphisms have a contraction subgroup that is closed in the original group topology?
