\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite simple graph $H$ and integer $r\geq2$, write $G\to_r H$ if every coloring of $E(G)$ with $r$ colors contains a monochromatic copy of $H$. A graph $G$ is $r$-Ramsey-minimal for $H$ if $G\to_r H$ but no proper subgraph has this property. Define
\[
 s_r(H)=\min\{\delta(G):G\text{ is $r$-Ramsey-minimal for }H\}.
\]
For integers $2\leq a<b$ and $N\geq1$, let $\alpha_a(G)$ be the maximum cardinality of a vertex subset inducing no $K_a$, and define the Erd\H{o}s--Rogers function
\[
 f_{a,b}(N)=\min\{\alpha_a(G): |V(G)|=N,\ K_b\not\subseteq G\}.
\]
All graph copies here are ordinary, not necessarily induced, copies.

The source prints the Fox--Grinshpun--Liebenau--Person--Szab\'o conjecture as $s_r(k)=\Theta(r f_{k,k+1}(r)^2)$, without defining the clique index of $s_r(k)$. The aid flags a one-step mismatch under the convention $s_r(k)=s_r(K_k)$ and identifies the primary conjecture instead as, for every fixed $k\geq3$,
\[
 s_r(K_k)=\Theta_k\!\left(r f_{k-1,k}(r)^2\right)\quad(r\to\infty),
\]
or equivalently, for every fixed $q\geq2$,
\[
 s_r(K_{q+1})=\Theta_q\!\left(r f_{q,q+1}(r)^2\right).
\]
The constants and any lower threshold for $r$ may depend on the fixed clique parameter, but not on $r$. The original printed formula is retained as an indexing issue, not silently identified with the corrected one.

The supplied aid attributes to Morris--Sahasrabudhe--Verstra\"ete a July 2026 theorem $f_{q,q+1}(N)=\Theta_q(\sqrt{N\log N})$ for fixed $q\geq2$. It reports that, combined with the FGLPS recurrence for $P_r(q)=s_r(K_{q+1})$, this yields

\]
It leaves the matching upper bound for $k\geq4$ open and identifies a simultaneous packing obstruction: obtaining $r$ suitable pairwise edge-disjoint $K_{q+1}$-free graphs on a common vertex set of size $N=\Theta_q(r^2\log r)$, rather than obtaining just one Erd\H{o}s--Rogers graph.

Then every vertex-coloring with colors $1,\ldots,r$ has some color class containing a $K_q$ in its matching $G_i$. This states the relevant simultaneous condition; existence of one graph does not provide disjoint copies of all $r$.
