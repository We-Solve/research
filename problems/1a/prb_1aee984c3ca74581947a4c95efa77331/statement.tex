\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix \(d\geq1\) and \(\mathcal H=L^2(\R^d,\C)\), with inner product linear in the first variable. Let \(U=\widetilde\mu_e\) denote the metaplectic--Heisenberg representation of the ambient group \(G\) meant in the report. The supplied record does not specify \(G\), its central extension or covering convention, or the formula for \(U\); these are part of the data still requiring source recovery.

For a locally compact subgroup \(H\leq G\), equipped with the intended Haar measure \(\dd h\), a reproducing or admissible window is a vector \(0\ne\varphi\in\mathcal H\) such that the same window works for all \(f\in\mathcal H\):
\[
 f=\int_H\langle f,U(h)\varphi\rangle U(h)\varphi\,\dd h.
\]
A precise weak interpretation is
\[
 \forall f,g\in\mathcal H,\qquad
 \langle f,g\rangle
 =\int_H\langle f,U(h)\varphi\rangle
              \langle U(h)\varphi,g\rangle\,\dd h.
\]
Equivalently, under this interpretation the coefficient map
\[
 C_\varphi:\mathcal H\longrightarrow L^2(H,\dd h),\qquad
 (C_\varphi f)(h)=\langle f,U(h)\varphi\rangle
\]
is an isometry, or
\[
 \int_H|\langle f,U(h)\varphi\rangle|^2\,\dd h=\|f\|_2^2
 \quad\text{for every }f\in\mathcal H.
\]
The cross integral then exists by the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality and is recovered from the norm identity by polarization. A strong or Bochner interpretation of the vector-valued integral would require additional justification.

The problem is to classify the subgroups \(H\) for which such a window exists, for the intended metaplectic--Heisenberg ambient representation in general dimension. This asks for a single-window reproducing identity with constant one on all of \(L^2(\R^d)\), not merely a frame inequality, reconstruction on an invariant proper subspace, or a formula using a separate dual window. The source leaves the subgroup class and equivalence relation for classification unspecified.

The supplied assessment checked 2026-08-21 reports classifications in dimension one and important two-dimensional classes in the metaplectic setting, but no complete general-dimensional classification. Its scope must not be expanded to all subgroups of an unspecified metaplectic--Heisenberg extension.
