\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 700997}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each integer \(n\geq 3\), let \(B_n\) be the Artin braid group on \(n\) strands, with presentation
\[
B_n=\left\langle \sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\ \middle|\ 
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_i\sigma_j&=\sigma_j\sigma_i &&(1\leq i,j\leq n-1,\ |i-j|\geq 2),\\
\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i&=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1} &&(1\leq i\leq n-2)
\end{aligned}
\right\rangle.
\]
Two elements \(x,y\) of a group \(G\) are conjugate if \(y=gxg^{-1}\) for some \(g\in G\). The group \(G\) is \emph{conjugacy separable} if, for every pair of nonconjugate elements \(x,y\in G\), there exist a finite group \(Q\) and a surjective homomorphism \(\pi:G\to Q\) such that \(\pi(x)\) and \(\pi(y)\) are not conjugate in \(Q\).

The question is whether \(B_n\) is conjugacy separable for every integer \(n\geq 3\). Explicitly, is it true that
\[
\begin{gathered}
\forall n\in\Z,\ n\geq 3,\quad \forall x,y\in B_n,\\
\bigl(\nexists g\in B_n:\ y=gxg^{-1}\bigr)
\ \Longrightarrow\ 
\left[\begin{gathered}
\exists\text{ a finite group }Q\text{ and a surjective homomorphism }\pi:B_n\to Q\\
\text{such that }\nexists q\in Q:\ \pi(y)=q\pi(x)q^{-1}
\end{gathered}\right]?
\end{gathered}
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
For every integer \(n\geq 3\), can every pair of nonconjugate braids in \(B_n\) be distinguished as nonconjugate in some finite quotient? The finite quotient may depend on the pair. The supplied registry labels the question open.
