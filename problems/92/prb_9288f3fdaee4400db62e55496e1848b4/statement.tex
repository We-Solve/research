\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Under the usual real-valued interpretation of the source, let \(n\) be a positive integer and let \(F_1,\ldots,F_n\) be distribution functions on \(\R\). A coupling of these margins is a random vector \((X_1,\ldots,X_n)\) with \(X_i\sim F_i\) for every \(i\). It is a joint mix if, for some \(k\in\R\),
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_i=k\qquad\text{almost surely}.
\]
Write
\[
\mathcal S(F_1,\ldots,F_n)=\left\{k\in\R:\text{there exists such a coupling with }\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_i=k\text{ almost surely}\right\}.
\]
