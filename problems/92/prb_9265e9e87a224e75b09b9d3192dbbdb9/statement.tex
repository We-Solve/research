\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Use the standard convention that the smooth knot concordance group \(\mathcal C\) consists of smooth concordance classes of oriented knots in \(S^3\), with connected sum as addition and the unknot class as zero. Two knots are smoothly concordant if they cobound a smoothly embedded annulus in \(S^3\times[0,1]\). A knot \(K\) represents torsion if
\[
\exists n\in\Z_{\geq 1}\quad n[K]=0\quad\text{in }\mathcal C.
\]
This includes the zero class. Equivalently, some positive connected sum of copies of \(K\) bounds a smoothly embedded disk in \(B^4\).

The Arf invariant \(\operatorname{Arf}(K)\in\Z/2\Z\) is the Arf invariant of the quadratic refinement on \(H_1(F;\Z/2\Z)\) associated to a Seifert surface \(F\) for \(K\).
