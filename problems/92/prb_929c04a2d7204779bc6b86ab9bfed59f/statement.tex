\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S$ be a closed marked oriented surface of genus $g\geq2$, and fix a Hitchin component $\operatorname{Hit}_n$ of the $\operatorname{SL}(n,\R)$ character variety, with the necessary lift/section data fixed. For a complex structure $J$, let $K_J$ be its canonical bundle. In Hitchin-section coordinates,
\[
s_J:\bigoplus_{k=2}^nH^0(S,K_J^k)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hit}_n,
\]
Higgs-field scaling by an $n$th root of unity acts on $q_k$ by multiplication by that root to the $k$th power. Fixedness means equality up to Higgs-bundle isomorphism, not literal equality of a nonzero field with its scalar multiple. The fixed Betti locus is
\[
\operatorname{Cyc}_n(J)=s_J(0,\ldots,0,H^0(S,K_J^n)).
\]
Oscar Garcia-Prada asks whether this cyclotomic space is independent of the complex structure. The aid interprets independence as equality of subsets in the same marked Betti component, rather than noncanonical abstract isomorphism of equal-dimensional vector spaces.

It reports that for $n=2$ this locus is the whole Hitchin component, hence is independent of $J$. For every $n\geq3$, let $\operatorname{Fuch}_n$ denote the principal Fuchsian locus in the fixed component and $\rho_J$ the principal Fuchsian point uniformizing $J$, with the chosen lift convention. The aid's distinguishing identity is
\[
\operatorname{Cyc}_n(J)\cap\operatorname{Fuch}_n=\{\rho_J\}.
\]
Consequently $\operatorname{Cyc}_n(J)=\operatorname{Cyc}_n(J')$ forces $J=J'$ in marked Teichm\"uller space. This establishes dependence for that interpretation; it does not say all different fibers are disjoint in every rank.

For $n=3$, the aid invokes the Labourie--Loftin correspondence to state more: the cubic-differential fibers over Teichm\"uller space form a pairwise-disjoint partition of $\operatorname{Hit}_3$. For $n\geq4$, consider instead the smooth universal cyclic map from the bundle with fibers $H^0(K_J^n)$ over Teichm\"uller space. Its real domain and target dimensions are
\[
(4n+4)(g-1),\qquad2(n^2-1)(g-1),
\]
with positive gap
\[
2(n-3)(n+1)(g-1).
\]
The aid therefore asserts that its image has measure zero in smooth local coordinates and empty interior. This does not imply that the image is nondense or prove disjointness of different higher-rank fibers.

The family is mapping-class covariant: transporting $J$, representation marking and any lift/component data together transports $\operatorname{Cyc}_n(J)$ to the corresponding locus for the transported $J$. Covariance is not constancy of the marked subset. Exact Hitchin-section and nonabelian-Hodge normalization conventions require source review.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Under the marked-Betti interpretation, the supplied aid says the cyclotomic locus is independent of complex structure only in rank two. In higher rank its unique principal Fuchsian point recovers the complex structure; rank three has a partition theorem, while higher ranks have a dimension obstruction to filling the component.
