\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701718}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite simple graph \(\Gamma\), the associated right-angled Artin group is
\[
A_\Gamma=\left\langle v\in V(\Gamma)\ \middle|\ [v,w]=1\text{ whenever }\{v,w\}\in E(\Gamma)\right\rangle,
\]
where \([v,w]=v^{-1}w^{-1}vw\). Its Salvetti cube complex has one vertex, one circle for each generator, and a coordinate torus for each clique of \(\Gamma\). A compact special cube complex is understood in the sense of Haglund and Wise cited below: it is a compact connected nonpositively curved cube complex admitting a cubical local isometry to a Salvetti complex.

A group \(G\) is \emph{virtually compact special} if it has a subgroup \(G_0\leq G\) with \([G:G_0]<\infty\) such that \(G_0\cong\pi_1(X)\) for a compact special cube complex \(X\).

A group \(G\) is a \emph{virtual retract} of a group \(A\) if there exist an injective homomorphism \(\iota:G\hookrightarrow A\), a subgroup \(A_0\leq A\) of finite index containing \(\iota(G)\), and a homomorphism \(r:A_0\to\iota(G)\) satisfying \(r(h)=h\) for every \(h\in\iota(G)\).

\textbf{Problem (A. Minasyan, 2022; Kourovka Notebook 20.60).} For every finite simple graph \(\Gamma\) and every group \(G\) that is a virtual retract of \(A_\Gamma\), must there exist a finite-index subgroup \(G_0\leq G\) and a compact special cube complex \(X\) such that
\[
G_0\cong\pi_1(X)?
\]

The supplied source states that an affirmative answer would give an algebraic characterization of virtually compact special groups as precisely the groups having finite-index subgroups that are virtual retracts of right-angled Artin groups. This is retained as the source's stated consequence, rather than as an independently verified result.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every virtual retract of a finitely generated right-angled Artin group have a finite-index subgroup isomorphic to the fundamental group of a compact special cube complex? This question was proposed by A. Minasyan in 2022 and is labeled open in the supplied registry.
