\section{Weak Verlinde Formula beyond Rational Conformal Field Theory}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
The fusion ring has a basis of sectors or modules and product given by fusion multiplicities. A one-dimensional representation of this ring is a multiplicative scalar assignment to those basis elements. An asymptotic quantum dimension is obtained from an appropriate limiting ratio of characters, when that limit exists. The weak Verlinde question asks whether these asymptotic dimensions respect fusion even outside semisimple rational conformal theories.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Does the weak Verlinde formula hold beyond rational conformal field theory; specifically, do asymptotic quantum dimensions define a one-dimensional representation of the fusion ring for nonrational or logarithmic conformal field theories?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
Existence and normalization of the character limits and the chosen fusion ring are part of the problem. Logarithmic and nonrational theories must not be replaced by the rational case in a proposed answer.

\paragraph{A necessary nilpotent test for scalar fusion characters.} Any unital ring homomorphism $d:R\to\mathbb C$ annihilates nilpotents: if $x^n=0$, then $d(x)^n=0$ and hence $d(x)=0$. Thus whenever the chosen complexified fusion ring has a nilpotent element, a proposed asymptotic dimension must vanish on it. For the simple algebra $R=\mathbb C[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$, every scalar character sends $a+b\epsilon$ to $a$; assigning a nonzero value to $\epsilon$ is immediately incompatible with multiplicativity.

\paragraph{Ordinary traces can miss logarithmic structure.} Let a two-dimensional generalized energy block be $L=hI+N$, where $N\ne0$ and $N^2=0$. Then $e^{-tL}=e^{-th}(I-tN)$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tL})=2e^{-th}$, exactly the same as for the semisimple block $hI$. Thus ordinary character traces may ignore nilpotent Jordan data. Ratios of such traces can exist while failing to distinguish extensions; their existence alone supplies no fusion-product identity.

\paragraph{Original-target gap.} The toy dual-number ring is not asserted to be a particular logarithmic CFT fusion ring, and the Jordan calculation does not disprove any correctly regularized weak Verlinde formula. It identifies two checks: choose the actual fusion ring and prove that the character limits respect its relations, including nilpotent ones if present. The first missing step is an analytic fusion/character asymptotic relation in the nonrational or logarithmic theory with controlled normalization and regularization. A rational semisimple theorem would not settle that step.
