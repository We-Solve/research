The aid supplies a background blow-up diagnostic. Let a group act smoothly on a smooth $d$-manifold $M$, $d\geq2$, and let $F$ be a finite invariant orbit. The real projective blow-up $\widetilde M=\operatorname{Bl}_F(M)$ has blow-down $b:\widetilde M\to M$ and exceptional set
\[
 E=\bigsqcup_{p\in F}\PP(T_pM),
\]
a closed invariant embedded hypersurface. This is projective blow-up, not an oriented blow-up introducing a sphere boundary. Every action diffeomorphism lifts functorially; on the exceptional fiber over $p$ its action is induced by the derivative at $p$. The lifted maps satisfy the group relations and $b$ is equivariant. Its restriction
\[
 b:\widetilde M\setminus E\longrightarrow M\setminus F
\]
is a smooth conjugacy on invariant open dense complements.

For every invariant probability measure $\mu$ on $M$ with $\mu(F)=0$, the inverse map on these complements gives a unique probability lift $\widetilde\mu$ with $b_*\widetilde\mu=\mu$. It is invariant and $\widetilde\mu(E)=0$, and $b$ gives an isomorphism of the probability actions modulo null sets. Uniqueness here is uniqueness of the lift through this blow-down and its specified complement identification, not uniqueness of every possible abstract measurable conjugacy. The lifted measure need not be a smooth everywhere-positive invariant volume form, so this construction does not automatically preserve all smooth volume-rigidity hypotheses.

If the original action has no closed invariant topologically embedded hypersurface, the lifted action cannot be globally topologically conjugate to it: a conjugacy would send $E$ to such an invariant hypersurface in $M$. This is a sufficient conditional obstruction, not a claim that every finite-orbit blow-up changes the global conjugacy class.
