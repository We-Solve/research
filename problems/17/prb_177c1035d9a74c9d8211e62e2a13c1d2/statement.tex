A statistic is sufficient for a model parameter if the conditional law of the data given that statistic does not depend on the parameter. A margin sums a joint table over selected coordinates; a conditional distribution divides joint probabilities by the relevant marginal when positive. Gibbs updates sample specified full conditional laws, and stationarity requires their compatibility with a joint law. Hidden-variable models can impose polynomial constraints beyond conditional independence.
