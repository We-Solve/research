\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a unital tracial von Neumann algebra $(M,\tau)$, an element $a\in M$ is centered if $\tau(a)=0$. Following the supplied record, unital von Neumann subalgebras $A,B\subseteq M$ are $k$-independent if
\[
\tau(a_1\cdots a_k)=0
\]
for every word whose factors are centered elements taken alternately from $A$ and $B$, with either algebra allowed to supply the first factor. Here the condition concerns words of length exactly $k$. The term independent means $2$-independent. Thus independence is equivalent to
\[
\tau(ab)=\tau(a)\tau(b)\qquad(a\in A,\ b\in B).
\]
Unitaries $u,v\in\mathcal U(M)$ are independent when the unital von Neumann algebras $W^*(u)$ and $W^*(v)$ are independent.

\mathcal I_{\mathcal U}=\{(x_n)\in\ell^\infty(\N,N):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau_N}=0\}.

\textbf{Lifting question.} If $u,v\in\mathcal U(N^{\mathcal U})$ satisfy

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can two independent unitaries in a tracial ultrapower be represented by unitary sequences whose corresponding terms are exactly independent in the original algebra? Independence here means trace factorization between their generated von Neumann algebras. The assumptions on the original algebra and ultrafilter, and the meaning of pairwise independence across sequences, require clarification.
