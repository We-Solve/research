\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701564}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a positive integer \(n\), let \(F_n\) denote the free group on \(n\) generators. Given an automorphism \(\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\), define the free-by-cyclic group
\[
G_\alpha=F_n\rtimes_\alpha\mathbb Z,
\qquad
(x,k)(y,\ell)=(x\alpha^k(y),k+\ell).
\]
A group \(G\) is linear over a field \(K\) if there exist a positive integer \(d\) and an injective homomorphism \(G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_d(K)\).

Under the usual finite-rank interpretation of \(F_n\), and interpreting linearity as linearity over some field, the question is:
\[
\forall n\geq 1\;\forall\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\;
\exists\text{ a field }K\;\exists d\geq 1\;\exists\text{ an injective homomorphism }
F_n\rtimes_\alpha\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_d(K)?
\]
The field and dimension may depend on \(n\) and \(\alpha\). The supplied statement does not explicitly specify the range of \(n\), the coefficient field, or its characteristic; the displayed formulation makes these interpretations explicit and requires review if a more restrictive meaning of linearity was intended.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every semidirect product of a finite-rank free group with \(\mathbb Z\), for every automorphism defining the action, admit a faithful finite-dimensional representation over some field? The rank convention and allowable fields are not explicitly specified in the supplied source.
