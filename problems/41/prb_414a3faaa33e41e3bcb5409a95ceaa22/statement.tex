For a Banach space \(X\), let \(\mathcal B(X)\) denote the bounded linear operators on \(X\). The strong operator topology on \(\mathcal B(X)\) is the topology of pointwise norm convergence: a net \((T_i)\) converges strongly to \(T\) if \(\|T_i\xi-T\xi\|_X\to0\) for every \(\xi\in X\).

\lambda_p(f)\in\overline{\lambda_p(\C\Gamma)}^{\mathrm{SOT}}.

\bigl\|\lambda_p(f)\xi_j-\lambda_p(a)\xi_j\bigr\|_{L^p(G)}<\varepsilon.

The approximating element \(a\) may depend on all the test vectors and on \(\varepsilon\).
