For this draft, interpret a primitive positive-definite binary quadratic form in the usual integral sense:
\[
Q(x,y)=ax^2+bxy+cy^2,\qquad a,b,c\in\Z,\quad \gcd(a,b,c)=1,\quad a>0,\quad b^2-4ac<0.
\]
Keep \(Q\) fixed. Under the convention that representation allows every integer pair, define
\[
\mathcal R_Q=\{Q(x,y):(x,y)\in\Z^2\}\cap\Z_{>0}
             =\{s_1<s_2<s_3<\cdots\}.
\]
Thus distinct represented positive integers are listed once, irrespective of their numbers of representations. Set
\[
g_n=s_{n+1}-s_n\qquad(n\geq1).
\]
To make the largest-gap question precise, adopt the explicit cutoff convention
\[
G_Q(X)=\max\bigl(\{g_n:n\geq1,\ s_{n+1}\leq X\}\cup\{0\}\bigr),\qquad X\in\R_{>0}.
\]
\textbf{Problem.} For a fixed primitive positive-definite integral binary quadratic form \(Q\), determine the optimal asymptotic order of \(G_Q(X)\) as \(X\to\infty\), specifying any dependence of bounds and constants on \(Q\).
