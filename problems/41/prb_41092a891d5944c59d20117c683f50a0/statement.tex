In the standard projective syzygy setting, take a smooth projective toric variety $X$ over an algebraically closed field and an ample line bundle $L$ with its complete-linear-series embedding. Put
\[
 V=H^0(X,L),\qquad S=\operatorname{Sym}V,\qquad
 R(X,L)=\bigoplus_{m\geq0}H^0(X,L^{\otimes m}).
\]
Under the usual convention, $(N_0)$ requires the natural graded map $S\to R(X,L)$ to be surjective, and $(N_1)$ further requires its homogeneous kernel to be generated in degree two.  I_P=\ker\bigl(k[z_u:u\in P\cap M]\to k[M][t],
