\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\operatorname{Ham}$ be the Hamiltonian diffeomorphism group of the symplectic manifold intended by the report, with Hofer distance $\rho$. In the standard setting the Hofer length of a Hamiltonian path generated by $H_s$ is
\[
 \ell_H=\int_0^1(\max H_s-\min H_s)\,\dd s,
\]
and the distance is the infimum of such lengths for paths joining the prescribed endpoints. Compact-support or normalization conventions depend on the unspecified ambient manifold.

For $t>0$, retain the closed annulus
\[
 A_t=\{f\in\operatorname{Ham}:t\le\rho(\operatorname{id},f)\le2t\}.
\]
Its intrinsic length distance is conventionally
\[
 \rho_t(f,g)=\inf\{\operatorname{Length}_\rho(\gamma):
 \gamma\text{ joins }f\text{ to }g\text{ within }A_t\},
\]
where path length is the supremum of sums of $\rho$-distances over finite parameter partitions, and an empty path class gives distance $+\infty$.

The source asks:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is $A_t$ path connected for all sufficiently large $t$?
\item What is the large-$t$ geometry of $(A_t,\rho_t)$? In particular, is
\[
 \operatorname{diam}(A_t,\rho_t)
       =\sup_{f,g\in A_t}\rho_t(f,g)<\infty
\]
for the intended large values of $t$?
\end{enumerate}
