\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.51}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $D\subset\mathbb C$ be a connected open set containing $0$, of finite connectivity $n\ge1$, meaning that $\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus D$ has $n$ connected components. Let $S(D)$ consist of holomorphic injective maps with $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=1$. Define $E_D(f)=\int_D|f'(z)|^2\,dA(z)\in[0,\infty]$, equal to the area of $f(D)$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each such $D$, determine $\inf_{f\in S(D)}E_D(f)$ and characterize all functions attaining the infimum, including cases where the infimum is not finite or is not attained. \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find the normalized conformal maps of a finitely connected plane domain having smallest image area.
