\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\geq 1\) be an integer; the supplied source gives no further restriction on \(k\). Let
\[
Z=\sum_{j=1}^{k} Z_j(z)\frac{\partial}{\partial z_j}

Let \(L\) be a nonsingular one-dimensional leaf of the holomorphic foliation induced by \(Z\), and let \(\phi:U\to L\subset\C^k\) be a holomorphic universal covering map, where either \(U=\C\) or \(U=\mathbb D:=\{\zeta\in\C:|\zeta|<1\}\), as stipulated in the source.

The following is therefore an explicit conventional interpretation of its question, subject to those unresolved source qualifications.

Fix a Fubini--Study K\"ahler form \(\omega\) on \(\PP^k(\C)\). Put \(R=\infty\) when \(U=\C\), and \(R=1\) when \(U=\mathbb D\). For \(0<r<R\), define

T_\omega^\phi(r)

\langle\tau_r^\phi,\eta\rangle
