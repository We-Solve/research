\section*{Simple combinatorial bidding}
\textbf{Problem ID:} \texttt{D44-108}\quad \textbf{JEL:} \texttt{D44}\par
% BEGIN ECONOMICS STATEMENT
\label{problem:OP-0294}
{\sffamily\small\color{muted}Original subject group: Mechanism design and market design\par}
\label{definitions:problem:30007576}
\textbf{Audit classification:} Published research agenda. Roth 2010 §1 and the 2024 auction survey §4.3 supply simplicity agendas. The combined cognitive/communication frontier is editorial; unrestricted real-valued messages make a message-count bound meaningless.
\subsection*{Definitions and precise research target}
\textbf{Model and research target.} The editorial target is a family of frontiers indexed by a complete specification \(\sigma\), rather than a single numerical frontier with unspecified primitives. Fix any such specification before optimizing over interfaces and mechanisms. It includes positive integers \(m,n\), a set \(G\) of \(m\) indivisible goods, and a nonempty, explicitly defined class \(\mathcal V_\sigma\) of profiles of nonnegative monotone values \(v_i:2^G\to[0,1]\). Feasible allocations are disjoint bundles \(A_i\subseteq G\), possibly leaving goods unallocated, and
\[
 \operatorname{OPT}(v)=\max_{A\text{ feasible}}\sum_{i=1}^n v_i(A_i)>0
 \qquad(v\in\mathcal V_\sigma).
\]
The specification states precise substitutes/complements predicates and their quantifiers: whether they concern types within the domain, bidders within a profile, or interactions within one valuation. The phrase ``admitting both substitutes and complements'' alone is not a membership rule. The supplied values are the cardinal quantities summed in welfare; no separate bidder rescaling is implicit. Values at the empty bundle need not be zero and remain in both welfare terms. Zero-baseline restrictions or baseline subtraction would change the specified domain or benchmark.

\emph{Complete specifications (editorial unless a source definition is named).} Each \(\sigma\) supplies the following objects and rules; leaving a field as an instruction to choose it does not define an instance of the family.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A protocol class and communication-cost convention. These specify senders, recipients, observations, feedback, message alphabets, scheduling and stopping rules, and whether \(k\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\) counts messages in total or separately for each bidder. Rounds are distinguished from messages. In the bit-message branch, fix a positive integer \(b\), and every charged message has at most \(b\) bits. State how bundle identifiers, sender identities, feedback, query requests and answers, timing and termination are encoded and charged, or fixed independently of private information. State every uncharged channel and its permitted information. In a value-query branch, specify permitted queries, precision, an explicit query-count bound as a function of \(k\), and costs in each direction. A real-valued message or an uncontrolled information channel cannot support a finite-bit claim. At most \(kb\) bits follows only for the messages covered by a total bound of \(k\), not automatically for an entire interactive transcript.
\item A population \(P\), the joint assignment or sampling law of the \(n\) bidders' characteristics and experience conditional on each fixed profile, their information and any beliefs, and a behavioral rule for every candidate \((I,M,v)\). This rule gives a nonempty specified set \(\mathcal Q_\sigma(I,M,v)\) of compatible joint probability laws on characteristics, interaction histories, decision times \(C=(C_1,\ldots,C_n)\), random seeds, allocations and payments. Each law respects the protocol, the mechanism and its announced randomization. A calibrated or measured kernel, or a declared equilibrium selection, gives a singleton set; robust equilibrium evaluation uses the specified set of equilibrium-induced laws. These alternatives are different specifications, not interchangeable interpretations of one expectation.
\item A time-measurement rule and a nonnegative, possibly infinite time-cost functional \(\Theta_\sigma(Q)\) of each joint law. State units, measured intervals, treatment of training, valuation elicitation, waiting and repeated decisions, and the recorded cost of noncompletion or timeouts. State the aggregation explicitly: for example, \(\max_i\mathbb E_Q C_i\), \(n^{-1}\sum_i\mathbb E_Q C_i\), \(\sum_i\mathbb E_Q C_i\), and \(\mathbb E_Q\max_i C_i\) are distinct choices. No one of them is selected here.
\item An exact participation and incentive predicate. It specifies utility as a function of allocations, payments and any cognitive costs, payment restrictions, outside options and participation timing. An equilibrium specification states the game, information, beliefs where needed, equilibrium concept, existence requirement and selection or robustness convention. Candidates lacking the required equilibria at any admitted profile are inadmissible. Participation follows the declared criterion, rather than an undefined exclusion of nonparticipation. A dominant-strategy specification includes a designated representable reporting strategy and the deviations against which it is optimal.
\item An input/access model, representation and operation budgets, and an admissible implementation class. State whether inputs are rational encodings with a specified bit-length parameter or oracle-accessible values; in the latter case specify query precision and cost. State which computations and preprocessing are charged and which parameter bounds apply. Allocation and payment rules use only the permitted transcript, declared public primitives and permitted randomness; direct access to private valuations is not implicit. The behavioral and cost rules are fixed across candidate designs and are not fitted after observing their performance.
\end{enumerate}
Let \(\mathscr A_\sigma(k)\) be exactly the pairs \((I,M)\) satisfying these communication, implementation, participation and incentive requirements, including nonemptiness of \(\mathcal Q_\sigma(I,M,v)\) for every \(v\in\mathcal V_\sigma\). For a finite budget \(c\geq0\), put
\[
 \mathscr A_\sigma(k,c)=\left\{(I,M)\in\mathscr A_\sigma(k):
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal V_\sigma}\sup_{Q\in\mathcal Q_\sigma(I,M,v)}\Theta_\sigma(Q)\leq c\right\}.
\]
When this class is nonempty, define
\[
 F_\sigma(k,c)=\sup_{(I,M)\in\mathscr A_\sigma(k,c)}
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal V_\sigma}\inf_{Q\in\mathcal Q_\sigma(I,M,v)}
 \frac{\mathbb E_Q[\sum_{i=1}^n v_i(A_i)]}{\operatorname{OPT}(v)}.
\]
Report an empty feasible class separately; do not interpret it as a welfare guarantee. Both welfare and time are evaluated using the same compatible conditional laws, including mechanism randomness. With a selected law the inner infimum over \(Q\) disappears. All primitives other than \(k,c\) are held fixed in \(\sigma\); a result varying \(m,n,b\), input precision or other primitives must state its family of specifications and uniformity quantifiers.

Characterize attainable welfare guarantees and communication, cognitive and implementation costs across declared specifications, and design interfaces approaching their frontiers. For \(\varepsilon>0\), approaching a nonempty frontier means supplying an admissible design whose worst-case ratio in the display is at least \(F_\sigma(k,c)-\varepsilon\), with its resource dependence stated. This is an existence/design objective; the supremum alone supplies neither an algorithm for computing it nor an efficient construction. The empirical research objective remains validation under unfamiliar valuations and bidder experience. A particular empirical guarantee additionally requires declared evaluation distributions, a sampling law, an estimand, a benchmark and an uncertainty or accuracy criterion. No particular population, measurement protocol or success threshold is selected here. Finite held-out evidence alone does not establish the displayed uniform guarantees.
\subsection*{Variants and assumption changes}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Strategic bit budget (editorial).} In a separately completed specification, require dominant-strategy truthfulness and ex-post IR on an explicitly defined substitutes or complements domain. Specify utilities, outside options, admissible payments and a truthful reporting strategy representable in the bidding language. Specify whether guarantees concern that strategy or every permitted best response. Impose at most \(kb\) bits under an explicit transcript-cost convention, identifying any uncharged communication. Seek worst-case welfare guarantees and information lower bounds under those same conventions; this strategic variant does not impose its assumptions on every \(F_\sigma\).
\item \textbf{Human interfaces (editorial).} Fix a finite set of implementable interfaces and estimate joint bidding/time kernels experimentally. Compare held-out welfare under identical declared valuation, population and experience distributions. An empirical study must specify whether its welfare estimand is mean welfare, a ratio of means or a mean of profilewise ratios, along with its benchmark, time criterion, sampling law and uncertainty criterion. These are different estimands and require explicit choices before a quantitative empirical guarantee is stated. This empirical comparison cannot automatically inherit rational-equilibrium guarantees or establish the worst-profile frontier over all admissible interfaces.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Common conventions: Source mathematical conventions}

These conventions apply to each entry unless explicitly replaced.

\textbf{Models and identification.} A primitive model \(m\) specifies all probability laws, feasible choices, information, equilibrium and measurement rules used by its target. The admissible class \(\mathcal M\) is fixed independently of outcomes. If \(P_O(m)\) is its observable probability law and \(\tau(m)\) the target, its identified set at observable law \(P\) is
\[
 \mathcal I_\tau(P)=\{\tau(m):m\in\mathcal M,\ P_O(m)=P\}.
\]
Point identification means that this set is a singleton. An empty set signals incompatibility of data and assumptions. Coordinate bounds need not describe the joint set. Bounds are sharp only if every retained target is attainable or arbitrarily approachable by compatible models. Finite bounds require explicit restrictions on unobserved quantities; unrestricted confounding, hidden wealth, extrapolation or arbitrary equilibrium selection can yield uninformative sets.

\textbf{Causal interventions.} A potential outcome \(Y_i(p)\) is the outcome under a complete policy regime \(p\), including timing, financing, information and market spillovers. The target population and units are fixed before treatment. With interference, use the complete assignment vector or a justified exposure mapping; individual no-interference notation alone cannot identify an economy-wide intervention. A difference of mean potential outcomes does not require the cross-world joint distribution. Conditioning on post-treatment migration, survival, enrollment or employment changes the estimand and needs separate justification.

\textbf{Statistical statements.} An estimator, confidence set, forecast or test specifies a sampling law, model class and loss/coverage criterion. Uniform \((1-\alpha)\)-coverage means \(\inf_{m\in\mathcal M}\Pr_m\{\tau(m)\in C_n\}\geq1-\alpha\), or an explicitly asymptotic version. The whole parameter space trivially has coverage, so informativeness requires a stated width, risk or power objective. A forecasting improvement is relative to a named benchmark, information set and evaluation population.

\textbf{Equilibrium and optimization.} An equilibrium comprises feasible plans, optimality, beliefs where needed, budget constraints and all market/resource clearing. The primitives or a stated benchmark define each correspondence \(\mathcal E(p;m)\). Nonemptiness is assumed or proved, never inferred just from compact policy sets. A selected-equilibrium target uses a declared selection rule; a robust target quantifies over all permitted equilibria. Use suprema or infima unless attainment is established. An \(\varepsilon\)-optimal policy is feasible and within \(\varepsilon\) of the finite optimum value. Report an empty feasible set separately.

\textbf{Welfare and accounting.} Normative utility, interpersonal normalization, social weights, numeraire, discounting and the use of public receipts are primitives. Behavioral utility need not coincide with the welfare criterion. Household welfare includes ownership income and rents once; adding profits again to compensating variation generally double-counts them. A monetary equivalent is defined by an explicit transfer and price convention, with monotonicity and range conditions. Average effects, mechanism contributions, transfers and welfare losses are different targets. Infinite sums require convergence and dynamic feasible plans require terminal or no-Ponzi conditions.

\textbf{Source versions.} A working-paper date, revision date and journal publication year are distinguished. A DOI for a journal version is not automatically the DOI of an earlier manuscript. Locators refer to the stated version and printed pages where specified. A metadata-only check does not verify a claim about a full-text conclusion. Every entry's source subsection narrows the provenance claim accordingly.
