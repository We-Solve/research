\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Smale space \((X,\varphi)\) consists of a compact metric space \(X\) and a homeomorphism \(\varphi\colon X\to X\) satisfying the Smale local product axioms: nearby points have a compatible local stable--unstable product structure, with uniform contraction along local stable sets under forward iteration and along local unstable sets under backward iteration. It is mixing if, for every pair of nonempty open sets \(U,V\subseteq X\), there exists \(N\in\N\) such that
\[
\varphi^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothing\qquad\text{for every integer }n\ge N.
\]
For such a space, define homoclinic equivalence by
\[
x\sim_h y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\lim_{n\to\infty}d(\varphi^n x,\varphi^n y)=0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\lim_{n\to\infty}d(\varphi^{-n}x,\varphi^{-n}y)=0.
\]
Write \(H(X,\varphi)\) for the homoclinic \(C^*\)-algebra: the reduced groupoid \(C^*\)-algebra of this equivalence relation equipped with its standard Smale-space étale groupoid topology.

\bigl(K_0(A),K_0(A)^+,[1_A],K_1(A),T(A),r_A\bigr).
\]
