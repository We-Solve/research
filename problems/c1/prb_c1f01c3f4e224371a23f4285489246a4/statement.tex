\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S$ be an integral algebraic surface over a field $k$, let $K=k(S)$, and let $X$ be a variety over $K$. Its geometric generic fiber is $X_{\overline K}=X\times_K\overline K$, or equivalently the geometric generic fiber of a model over $S$. The source asks for geometric hypotheses on this fiber which ensure that the Brauer obstruction is the only obstruction to $X(K)\ne\varnothing$. It does not specify $k$, characteristic, properness, smoothness, the model, or the meaning of ``Brauer obstruction''.

One necessary Brauer-theoretic condition can be stated without choosing local data. For the structural map $\pi:X\to\operatorname{Spec}K$, a rational point $x$ gives
\[
 x^*\circ\pi^*=\operatorname{id}_{\operatorname{Br}(K)},\qquad
 \operatorname{Br}(X)=H^2_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(X,\mathbb G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}}.
\]
Consequently $\ker(\operatorname{Br}(K)\to\operatorname{Br}(X))\ne0$ obstructs a rational point. This kernel obstruction is not automatically the source's intended complete Brauer obstruction. A Brauer--Manin formulation would require specified local fields, a space of local points, invariant maps and a reciprocity pairing; these are not determined merely by saying ``function field of a surface''. Elementary or descent obstructions are also distinct notions and must not be substituted silently.

After the intended Brauer obstruction $\mathcal O_{\mathrm{Br}}(X)$ and geometric class $\mathcal C$ have been fixed, the requested sufficient statement has the form
\[
 X_{\overline K}\in\mathcal C\quad\text{and}\quad
 \mathcal O_{\mathrm{Br}}(X)=0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X(K)\ne\varnothing,
\]
with all field and model hypotheses quantified as part of the theorem. This is a formulation of the research target, not a claimed theorem for every variety with vanishing Brauer kernel.

The supplied assessment checked 21 August 2026 records substantial rational-point results for rationally simply connected varieties and relevant period--index and Serre-conjecture cases, while calling the broad characterization for rationally $1$-connected fibers open. In this context rational simple connectedness concerns rational curves and their moduli, not just triviality of an ordinary topological fundamental group; precise variants and hypotheses are absent. The linked global-function-field results must not be treated without further hypotheses as an unrestricted theorem over every surface function field.
