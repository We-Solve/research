The source asks for connections between ``random sparse polygons'' and Newton polytopes, extended to random Viro diagrams. In the supplied report's interpretation, a finite exponent set \(A\subset\Z^d\) has a Bernoulli-selected support \(S\); the Newton polytope of a polynomial supported on \(S\) is \(P=\operatorname{conv}S\). Independent lift heights induce a regular subdivision, and independent fair signs decorate selected vertices. This model is additional to the literal source, not its unique recovered interpretation.

Condition on a pure \(d\)-dimensional regular triangulation \(T\), with \(N\) top-dimensional simplices. For each top simplex \(\sigma\), its local Viro chart is active when its \(d+1\) vertex signs are not all equal. Put \(Y=\sum_\sigma I_\sigma\), and let \(n_r(T)\) count unordered pairs of distinct top simplices sharing exactly \(r\) vertices. The supplied candidate moment formulas are
\[
 \mathbb E[Y\mid T]=(1-2^{-d})N,
\]
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(Y\mid T)=N(1-2^{-d})2^{-d}
 +2^{1-2d}\sum_{r=2}^dn_r(T)(2^{r-1}-1).
\]
Distinct charts sharing zero or one vertex have zero covariance; for \(r\ge2\) the reported covariance is \(2^{-2d}(2^{r-1}-1)\). The sum is empty for \(d=1\). These count active local charts, not connected components or Betti numbers of a global real zero set.

\[
 \mathbb P(|Y-\mathbb E[Y\mid T]|\ge t\mid T)

\[
 \operatorname{Var}(Y)=\mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid T)]

This uses a distribution supported on the specified pure full-dimensional triangulations; empty or lower-dimensional support outcomes require separate conventions.
