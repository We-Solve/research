\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $E\subset\C$ be compact. For $n\geq2$, define its $n$-diameter by
\[
 d_n(E)=\left(\max_{w_1,\ldots,w_n\in E}
             \prod_{1\leq\mu<\nu\leq n}|w_\mu-w_\nu|\right)^{2/(n(n-1))}.
\]
Its transfinite diameter, equal to its logarithmic capacity, is $d(E)=\lim_{n\to\infty}d_n(E)$. Assume $d(E)=1$. The source reports that $d_n$ decreases to one, hence $d_n(E)\geq1$.

The first question, literally as printed, asks whether
\[
 d_n(E)\geq n^{2/(n-1)}\qquad(n\geq2)
\]
holds for all such compact sets, and attributes this lower bound for connected $E$ to Pommerenke. There is a normalization inconsistency: at $n=2$ the displayed definition gives $d_2(E)=\operatorname{diam}(E)$, while the claimed connected lower bound would require diameter at least four for capacity one. The closed unit disk has capacity one and diameter two. Thus the quoted lower-bound formula cannot be accepted together with the supplied normalization. No exponent or normalization is silently repaired, and the intended general lower-bound question requires source verification.

The second question is independently well formed: is there an absolute constant $K>0$ such that
\[
 \forall E\subset\C\text{ compact and connected with }d(E)=1\ \forall n\geq2,
 \qquad d_n(E)^{(n-1)/2}\leq Kn?
\]
The source reports the weaker estimate
\[
 d_n(E)^{(n-1)/2}\leq\left(\frac4e\log n+4\right)n
\]
for connected $E$. The desired $K$ must be independent of both $E$ and $n$; connectedness is not replaced by a stronger domain or smoothness assumption.
