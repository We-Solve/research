\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

A quadratic form on a finite-dimensional \(F\)-vector space \(V\) is a map \(r:V\to F\) such that \(r(cv)=c^2r(v)\) and \(r(u+v)-r(u)-r(v)\) is bilinear in \(u,v\). Write \(\dim(r)=\dim_F(V)\). The form is anisotropic if \(r(v)=0\) implies \(v=0\). For a field extension \(L/F\), write \(r_L\) for scalar extension and interpret its isotropy index as
\[
i_0(r_L)=\max\{\dim_L U: U\subseteq V\otimes_F L\text{ is an }L\text{-linear subspace and }r_L|_U=0\}.
\]
