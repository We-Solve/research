The proposed assertion is that, for every fixed $r\ge3$ and all sufficiently large $k$, every $r$-uniform hypergraph with chromatic number $k$ has at least $\binom{(r-1)(k-1)+1}{r}$ edges, with the specified complete hypergraph as the only equality case. Alon disproved this. We reconstruct a disproof with the single fixed uniformity $r=100$ and arbitrarily large $k$, using a precisely stated Tur\'an covering estimate. This is enough to refute the universal assertion; it makes no claim to settle the separate large-$k$ case $r=3$.
