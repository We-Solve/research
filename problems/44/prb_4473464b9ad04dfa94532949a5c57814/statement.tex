\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\operatorname{Mod}_g\) be the mapping class group of the source's genus-\(g\) surface. For a finite symmetric generating set \(S\), let \(d_S\) be the word metric and \(B_S(n)=\{x:d_S(1,x)\le n\}\). In the standard Cannon convention, \((\operatorname{Mod}_g,S)\) is almost convex if there is a constant \(K\) such that for every integer \(n\ge0\) and every \(x,y\) with
\[
 d_S(1,x)=d_S(1,y)=n,\qquad d_S(x,y)\le2,
\]
there is an edge path from \(x\) to \(y\) of length at most \(K\) entirely contained in \(B_S(n)\). The constant is independent of \(n,x,y\).
