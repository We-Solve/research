\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(Y\) be a variety and let \(E\) be a finite-rank algebraic vector bundle on \(Y\). The base field and the precise convention for ``variety'' are not specified in the supplied record. All varieties, bundles, and morphisms below are to be taken over the same base field.

For a toric variety \(X_\Sigma\) with acting algebraic torus \(T\), a \emph{toric vector bundle} \(F\) is an algebraic vector bundle equipped with an algebraic \(T\)-action lifting the action on \(X_\Sigma\), such that the induced maps between fibres are linear. Write \(i^*F\) for the pullback vector bundle along a morphism \(i:Y\to X_\Sigma\).

The problem is to characterize precisely those pairs \((Y,E)\) for which
\[
\exists\,X_\Sigma\;\exists\,i:Y\hookrightarrow X_\Sigma\;\exists\,F
\quad\text{such that}\quad
\begin{cases}
X_\Sigma\text{ is a toric variety},\\
i\text{ is an embedding},\\
F\text{ is a toric vector bundle on }X_\Sigma,\\
E\cong i^*F\text{ as algebraic vector bundles on }Y.
\end{cases}
\]
The ambient toric variety, embedding, and toric vector bundle may depend on both \(Y\) and \(E\). No torus action on \(Y\) or equivariant structure on \(E\) is specified or required by the supplied formulation.
