\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Use the standard finite-graph and tame (for example piecewise-linear) spatial-embedding conventions. A graph $G$ admits a knotless embedding in $\R^3$ if there is an embedding $\iota:G\hookrightarrow\R^3$ such that the image of every cycle is an unknot, that is, ambient isotopic to a round circle. Knotlessness does not require distinct cycles to form an unlink.

A graph is intrinsically knotted if every spatial embedding contains at least one cycle whose image is a nontrivial knot. The cycle may depend on the embedding. Graph minors are obtained by edge and vertex deletions and edge contractions, with the report's loop/multiple-edge conventions; the usual finite simple convention suppresses these when appropriate.

Find additional minor-minimal intrinsically knotted graphs, with the stated goal of determining the complete obstruction set
\[
 \mathcal O=\{H:H\text{ is intrinsically knotted and every proper minor of }H
                                  \text{ admits a knotless embedding}\}.
\]
Completeness would mean that a graph $G$ admits a knotless embedding exactly when it has no minor isomorphic to a member of $\mathcal O$. An additional obstruction must be proved both intrinsically knotted and minor minimal; simply exhibiting an intrinsically knotted graph does not identify a new forbidden minor.

The original question asks whether more forbidden minors can be found, and the restored statement makes the eventual complete-classification goal explicit. No baseline list of known obstructions is supplied, so ``additional'' cannot be interpreted as a precise novelty test from this record alone. The graph category and tame-embedding convention should be confirmed, and no linked-cycle prohibition or stronger embedding condition is inserted.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find further graphs that force a knotted cycle in every spatial embedding but lose that property in every proper minor, ultimately characterizing all knotlessly embeddable graphs by excluded minors. The known-obstruction baseline is not supplied.
