\section{Robustness of Climate Models at Unresolved Scales}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A reduced geophysical-flow model omits unresolved spatial or temporal scales. Grid-scale perturbations test numerical sensitivity at the mesh length. White noise is distribution-valued forcing and Brownian motion its time integral in a basic model. Rough paths, regularity structures and paracontrolled methods provide ways to define dynamics with rough inputs.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Determine whether reduced geophysical-flow models admit sufficiently rough solution spaces, whether their numerical approximations are robust under grid-scale perturbations, and which stochastic structures correctly represent unresolved processes. In particular, decide whether white-noise and Brownian-motion models suffice or whether rough paths, regularity structures, or paracontrolled distributions are required.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}Selecting a stochastic framework depends on the particular equation and covariance; the source asks whether simpler noise models suffice, not whether every model needs all three advanced theories.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine suitable rough solution spaces and robust approximations for reduced climate models, and identify the stochastic structures needed at unresolved scales.
