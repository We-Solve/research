\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let
\[
F(x,y,z,w)=x^3+2xyz+xw^2-y^2w+wz^2,
\qquad
S=\{[x:y:z:w]\in\PP^3_{\Q}:F(x,y,z,w)=0\}.
\]
The supplied source describes \(S\) as a twisted form of the Cayley cubic surface with four singularities. For a real number \(B>1\), define
\[
N(B)=\#\left\{(x,y,z,w)\in\Z^4:
\begin{gathered}
\max\{|x|,|y|,|z|,|w|\}\le B,\qquad x\ne0,\\
\gcd(|x|,|y|,|z|,|w|)=1,\qquad F(x,y,z,w)=0
\end{gathered}
\right\}.
\]
Thus the boundary of the box is included, and primitive integer vectors are counted individually: a vector and its negative are distinct solutions.

\textbf{Requested asymptotic.} Determine explicit real constants \(c_1,c_2>0\) and prove that

\]
where \(\log\) denotes the natural logarithm. The source requires \(c_1\) to be the product of local densities with the value predicted by Peyre, and attributes \(c_2\) to a Zariski-dense thin set of rational points on \(S\).
