Its automorphism obstruction is explicit. Let \(k\) be a finitely generated field, for example \(\mathbb Q\) or a finite field, and let \(t,u\) be algebraically independent. In

 K=k(t,u),\qquad
 \mathcal O_{v_t}=k[t,u]_{(t)},
\]
the divisor valuation has \(v_t(t)=1\), \(v_t(u)=0\). The field automorphism
\[
 \sigma(t)=t,\qquad\sigma(u)=tu,\qquad\sigma|_k=\operatorname{id}

\[

\]

Therefore the aid concludes that \(\mathcal O_{v_t}\) is not definable over \(k(t)\), even with a finite tuple from that subfield; in particular no scalar \(a\in k(t)\), including the uniformizer \(t\), can be its sole parameter.
