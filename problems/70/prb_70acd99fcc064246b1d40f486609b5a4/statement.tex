\subsection{Short English statement}
Does a Radon measure with positive finite lower \(n\)-dimensional density and finite square integral of its density differences between radii \(r\) and \(2r\), almost everywhere, have to be \(n\)-rectifiable? The same question is asked for \(\mathcal H^n\!\restriction E\). The parameter range, the square-function quantifier, and the set measurability convention require source confirmation.
