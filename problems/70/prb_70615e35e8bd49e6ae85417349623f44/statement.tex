Given $\alpha\ge1$, find positive integers $n_j,m_j$ with $\sigma(n_j)=\sigma(m_j)$ and $n_j/m_j\to\alpha$. We reconstruct Pollack's affirmative proof. Its explicitly external deep input is the bounded-prime-clusters theorem for affine forms: there is an absolute integer $K\ge2$ such that every admissible collection of $K$ distinct, pairwise nonproportional integral affine forms with positive leading coefficients has two forms simultaneously prime for infinitely many positive integer arguments. This is the Maynard form of the Zhang--Maynard theorem, stated as Proposition 1 in Pollack's paper. Admissible means that no prime divides the product of the forms for every integer argument.
The divergence of the sum of reciprocals of the primes is the only other standard input. All fiber constructions and the passage to arbitrary ratios are proved below.
