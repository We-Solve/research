\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each positive integer \(n\), let \(A^{(n)}\in\R^{n\times n}\) be a random matrix with \(A^{(n)}_{ii}=0\) and, for every \(i\ne j\),
\[
\Pr\bigl(A^{(n)}_{ij}=1\bigr)=\frac12+\delta_n,
\qquad
\Pr\bigl(A^{(n)}_{ij}=-1\bigr)=\frac12-\delta_n.
\]
The probabilities require \(-\tfrac12\leq\delta_n\leq\tfrac12\). The conjectured regime is, for a fixed \(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
(1+\varepsilon)\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{2n}}
\leq\delta_n\leq\frac12
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\). Here \(\log\) denotes the natural logarithm, and the subscript on \(\delta_n\) permits dependence on \(n\).
