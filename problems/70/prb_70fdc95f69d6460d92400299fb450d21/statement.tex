\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite simplicial complex \(S\), let \(f_i(S)\) denote the number of its \(i\)-dimensional simplices, and let \(\dim S=\max\{i:f_i(S)>0\}\). Write \(|S|\) for its geometric realization. A topological embedding into \(\R^{2d}\) is a continuous map \(e:|S|\to\R^{2d}\) that is a homeomorphism onto its image.
