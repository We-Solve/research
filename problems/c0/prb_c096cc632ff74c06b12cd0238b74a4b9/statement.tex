\section{Fermionic Character of Low-Lying Excitations}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


Fermionic quasiparticles are effective low-energy excitations carrying fermionic statistics and described by an appropriate dispersion relation and effective creation operators. Weak interaction refers to the source perturbative regime of the many-fermion Hamiltonian. Low lying concerns energies near its many-body ground energy.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Do the low-lying excitations of a weakly interacting fermionic system have the character of fermionic quasiparticles?

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}A mathematical answer must state a spectral or dynamical comparison and its limiting regime; the source does not reduce the question to comparing ground-state energies.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine whether low-energy excitations of the weakly interacting fermionic system admit a fermionic quasiparticle description.
