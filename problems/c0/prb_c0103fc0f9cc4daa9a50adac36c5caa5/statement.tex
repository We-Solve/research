\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
In the conventional setting of finite simple undirected graphs, a class \(\mathcal{G}\) is \emph{bridge-addable} if, for every \(G\in\mathcal{G}\) and every pair of vertices \(u,v\in V(G)\) in distinct connected components, the graph \(G+uv\) obtained by adding the edge \(uv\) also belongs to \(\mathcal{G}\). The added edge is a bridge: deleting it increases the number of connected components.
