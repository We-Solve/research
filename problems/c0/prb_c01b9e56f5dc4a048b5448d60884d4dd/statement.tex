\section{Orthogonal Symmetry Conjecture for Rigid Higher Categories}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


An $(\infty,n)$-category has morphisms at all levels, with those above level $n$ invertible. Rigidity supplies the duals or adjoints in the report's enriched setting. Taking the opposite at each of $n$ tiers gives commuting reflections, hence an action of $O(1)^n$. An extension to $O(n)$ must restrict to that action and retain the required homotopy-coherent compatibility.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Let $\operatorname{Cat}^{\mathrm{rigid}}_{(\infty,n)}(\mathcal{V})$ be the $\infty$-category of rigid $(\infty,n)$-categories enriched over a presentably symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category $\mathcal{V}$. Taking opposites in each categorical tier gives an action of $O(1)^n$. Prove that this action extends to an action of the orthogonal group $O(n)$.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Extend the independent categorical opposite symmetries to the full orthogonal group action on rigid enriched higher categories.
