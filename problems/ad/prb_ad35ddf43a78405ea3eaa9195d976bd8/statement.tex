\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#127; reconstruction, 2026-09-23.}
Let $b(m)$ be the minimum, over graphs with $m$ edges, of the size of
a maximum cut. Put
\[
 f(m)=b(m)-\left(\frac m2+\frac{\sqrt{8m+1}-1}{8}\right).
\]
Is $f(m)$ unbounded along some sequence? We reconstruct Alon's proof
that $f(n^2/2)\gg\sqrt n$ for large even $n$.
