\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\mathcal A_g$ be the moduli space of principally polarized complex abelian varieties of dimension $g$, and let $j:\mathcal M_g\to\mathcal A_g$ be the Jacobian (Torelli) map. The Schottky or Jacobian locus is customarily $\mathcal J_g=j(\mathcal M_g)$; some conventions instead use an appropriate closure, which must be distinguished.

Problem 4.12 asks to compute the distortion of the Schottky locus in $\mathcal A_g$. The excerpt says only that a naive guess might be exponential; it does not state exponential distortion as an established fact or a fully specified conjecture.

To make distortion precise one must fix a metric on $\mathcal A_g$, a metric on $\mathcal J_g$ (for example an intrinsic path metric, or one transferred from a metric on curve moduli), and a comparison convention. Under one possible all-pairs convention the distortion function is
\[
 \Delta(R)=\sup\{d_{\mathcal J}(x,y):x,y\in\mathcal J_g,
 d_{\mathcal A}(x,y)\leq R\}.
\]
The problem would then ask for its growth, allowing that the supremum might be infinite. This formula is illustrative only: the excerpt supplies no metrics, basepoint/uniformity choice, or coarse equivalence relation for growth functions. In particular, the Siegel metric on $\mathcal A_g$ and either Teichm\"uller or intrinsic induced metrics on the source cannot be selected silently. The genus range and image-versus-closure convention also matter.
