\section{Extremal Top-$m$ Representability of Output Projection Matrices}\label{30006668}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


For $z\in\mathbb R^N$, $\operatorname{softmax}(z)_i=e^{z_i}/\sum_j e^{z_j}$. Its probability ranking agrees with the ranking of logits. An output projection sends an embedding $x$ to the token-score vector $Ax$.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a vocabulary of $N$ tokens and embedding dimension $d$, what is the largest $m$ for which there exists an output projection matrix $A\in\mathbb R^{N\times d}$ such that, for every set of $m$ tokens, some embedding $x\in\mathbb R^d$ makes those tokens the $m$ most probable under $\operatorname{softmax}(Ax)$? \sref{30006668}{2}
