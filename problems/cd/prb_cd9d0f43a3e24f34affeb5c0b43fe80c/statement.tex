|g_T(y)-g_T(x)|\leq K_TS(f)\,d(x,y)

near $x$, and there are normalized nonnegative local smoothing densities supported at scale $\eps\leq\eps_x$ with $L^2$ norm at most $B_x\eps^{-D/2}$. Then
\[
|A_Tf(x)-\mu(f)|\leq S(f)\bigl(K_T\eps+B_xE_T\eps^{-D/2}\bigr).

For $K_T>0$, if $\eps=(B_xE_T/K_T)^{2/(D+2)}$ is locally admissible, this gives
\[
|A_Tf(x)-\mu(f)|\leq
2S(f)(B_xE_T)^{2/(D+2)}K_T^{D/(D+2)}.
\]
