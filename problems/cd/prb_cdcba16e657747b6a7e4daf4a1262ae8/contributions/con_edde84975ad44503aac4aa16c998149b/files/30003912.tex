\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
Anti-de Sitter space solves the vacuum Einstein equations with negative cosmological constant, $\operatorname{Ric}(g)=\frac{2\Lambda}{D-2}g$ in spacetime dimension $D$, with $\Lambda<0$. Its conformal boundary is timelike; reflecting boundary conditions prevent energy from escaping there. Arbitrarily small perturbations means that for every positive size threshold in a specified initial-data norm one seeks smaller constraint-compatible data. A black-hole region is defined relative to causal communication with the conformal boundary.

\paragraph{Testing small stationary black holes as a shortcut.} In four dimensions write the Schwarzschild--AdS lapse as \(f_M(r)=1+r^2/L^2-2M/r\), with \(L>0\), \(M>0\). Its positive horizon radius \(r_h\) solves
\[
 2M=r_h(1+r_h^2/L^2).
\]
The right side is strictly increasing for \(r_h\ge0\), so there is a unique positive solution and \(r_h\le2M\). Dividing by \(2M\) and taking \(M\downarrow0\) gives \(r_h/(2M)\to1\), because \(r_h^2/L^2\to0\). At every fixed positive radius the lapse converges to the AdS lapse.
\paragraph{The central countercheck.} This convergence is not uniform down to the center: at \(r=2M\), the lapse difference \(2M/r\) equals one for every \(M\). The geometry also already has a horizon and cannot serve as a smooth global perturbation of a horizon-free AdS slice by merely citing small mass. Thus the existence of arbitrarily small stationary black-hole parameters does not prove formation from arbitrarily small regular initial data in the requested norm.
\paragraph{Remaining scope.} A proof needs constraint-compatible smooth vacuum data, reflecting boundary control and nonlinear evolution long enough to create a black-hole region. The calculation uses neither scalar matter nor a resonant approximation, and proves no instability in any initial-data topology. The first gap is the actual dynamical concentration mechanism with controlled small vacuum initial norm. The known lapse family here is only a concrete test of an invalid shortcut, not new progress on the source vacuum problem.
