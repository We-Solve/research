\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((X,g)\) be a compact K\"ahler manifold whose holomorphic sectional curvature is nonnegative in every nonzero holomorphic tangent direction. Write \(\pi_1(X,x)\) for its fundamental group at a base point \(x\). The source poses the following three questions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is \(\pi_1(X,x)\) an ``extension of a finite group by \(\mathbb Z^{\oplus 2m}\)'' for some integer \(m\geq 0\)? Under the usual convention for an extension of a group by another group, this asks for a finite group \(G\) and an exact sequence
\[
1\longrightarrow \mathbb Z^{\oplus 2m}\longrightarrow \pi_1(X,x)\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow 1.
\]
The source gives no exact sequence. If the quoted wording instead intends a finite kernel and free abelian quotient, the sequence would be
\[
1\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow \pi_1(X,x)\longrightarrow \mathbb Z^{\oplus 2m}\longrightarrow 1.
\]
These formulations must be distinguished; the intended ordering requires source review.
\item For an appropriate finite \'etale cover \(p:X^*\to X\), consider its Albanese map
\[
\alpha:X^*\longrightarrow \operatorname{Alb}(X^*).
\]
Here a finite \'etale cover is a finite unramified holomorphic covering, and \(\operatorname{Alb}(X^*)\) is the Albanese complex torus, with its map determined up to translation after the usual base-point choice. Does the fiber \(F\) admit a K\"ahler metric \(g_F\) such that
\[
n_{\mathrm{tf}}(F,g_F)=\dim F?
\]
The invariant \(n_{\mathrm{tf}}\) is not defined in the supplied record, so this equality is retained as a source condition whose precise meaning requires verification. The source does not explicitly require \(g_F\) to have semi-positive holomorphic sectional curvature.
\item For the fiber \(F\) of the Albanese map of an appropriate finite \'etale cover as above, is \(F\) projective and rationally connected? For a smooth projective variety, rational connectedness means that two general points can be joined by the image of a morphism from \(\mathbb P^1\).
\end{enumerate}
The expression ``appropriate finite \'etale cover'' has no stated selection criterion. The record also does not specify whether \(F\) denotes every fiber, a general fiber, or a selected fiber; whether the relevant fibers are smooth and connected; or whether one cover must serve both fiber questions. These qualifications remain unresolved, and no smoothness, connectedness, surjectivity, or additional curvature assertion about \(\alpha\) or \(F\) is assumed here.
