\section{Entrainment-Mediated Tropical-Cyclone Intensification}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


Entrainment is the incorporation of surrounding air into a rising cloud tower. The proposed asymptotic mechanism creates a radial mean flow and transports angular momentum toward the cyclone center. A three-dimensional resolved simulation can test these fluxes and the predicted change in cyclone strength.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Using three-dimensional high-resolution simulations, verify the asymptotically derived pathway in which cloud-tower entrainment produces a radial mean flow that transports angular momentum inward and intensifies a tropical cyclone.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The source requests physical-model validation, not a proof for an unspecified universal PDE. The asymptotic pathway and simulated observables must be compared in the source regime.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Test the proposed cloud-entrainment pathway to cyclone intensification using high-resolution three-dimensional simulations.
