\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A group \(G\) is LERF, or subgroup separable, if for every finitely generated subgroup \(H\le G\) and \(g\notin H\), there is a finite quotient \(q:G\to F\) with \(q(g)\notin q(H)\). This separates finite subgroup data by finite covers.

The source asks for a useful geometric analogue for foliations/laminations of three-manifolds: what properties of a three-manifold \(M\) guarantee that immersed essential laminations are virtually embedded? A two-dimensional lamination has local product charts with disk plaques and a closed transverse set. The precise immersion and essentialness conditions for the intended laminations are not supplied; leafwise immersion alone does not specify the global laminated object or the essentialness notion.
