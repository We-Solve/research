\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a locally compact topological group. Say that \(G\) is covered by compact subgroups if
\[
\forall g\in G\;\exists K\leq G\quad(K\text{ is compact and }g\in K).
\]
The classification problem in the supplied record is to describe all such \(G\) that are primary and locally soluble and satisfy
\[
\forall A\leq G,\qquad
\bigl(A\text{ is closed in }G\text{ and abelian}\bigr)
\Longrightarrow A\text{ has finite rank}.
\]
The finite rank condition is imposed separately on each closed abelian subgroup; no bound uniform over all such subgroups is stated.
