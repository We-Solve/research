\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The supplied question asks whether three-dimensional Leray--Hopf solutions on \(\R^3\) are globally regular and unique. It does not specify the initial-data class, forcing, or the meaning of regularity at the initial time. The following makes explicit a conventional unforced interpretation; these choices require confirmation against the source.

Fix a viscosity \(\nu>0\), and define
\[
L^2_\sigma(\R^3)=\overline{\{v\in C_c^\infty(\R^3;\R^3):\nabla\cdot v=0\}}^{\,L^2(\R^3)}.
\]
For initial velocity \(u_0\in L^2_\sigma(\R^3)\), consider the unforced incompressible Navier--Stokes equations
\[
\partial_t u+(u\cdot\nabla)u-\nu\Delta u+\nabla p=0,
\qquad \nabla\cdot u=0
\quad\text{on }(0,\infty)\times\R^3,
\qquad u(0)=u_0.
\]
Here \(u\) is the velocity and \(p\) is the pressure. Under this interpretation, a Leray--Hopf solution is a distributional solution for which, for every \(T>0\),
\[
u\in L^\infty(0,T;L^2_\sigma(\R^3))\cap L^2(0,T;H^1(\R^3;\R^3)),
\qquad u\in C_w([0,\infty);L^2_\sigma(\R^3)),
\]
where \(C_w\) denotes continuity for the weak \(L^2\) topology, and
\[
\lim_{t\downarrow0}\|u(t)-u_0\|_{L^2}=0.
\]
It also satisfies the energy inequality
\[
\frac12\|u(t)\|_{L^2}^2+\nu\int_s^t\|\nabla u(\tau)\|_{L^2}^2\,\dd\tau
\leq\frac12\|u(s)\|_{L^2}^2
\]
for \(s=0\), and for almost every \(s>0\), for every \(t\geq s\). The spatial domain is all of \(\R^3\); there is no spatial boundary, and the stated function spaces impose finite energy.

\textbf{Question under these explicit conventions.} For every \(\nu>0\) and every \(u_0\in L^2_\sigma(\R^3)\), is every Leray--Hopf solution globally regular in the sense that
\[
u\in C^\infty((0,\infty)\times\R^3;\R^3),
\]
and is its velocity unique among Leray--Hopf solutions with the same viscosity and initial velocity? Explicitly, must any two such solutions \(u,v\) satisfy \(u(t)=v(t)\) in \(L^2(\R^3)\) for every \(t\geq0\)? Pressure uniqueness is understood only up to addition of a function of time.
