\section{The Tate Conjecture}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $X$ be smooth and projective over a finite field, let $r\geq0$, and let $\ell$ differ from the field characteristic. Must every Frobenius-invariant class in $H^{2r}_{\mathrm{et}}(X_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q},\mathbb Q_\ell(r))$ be a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-linear combination of cycle classes of codimension-$r$ algebraic cycles defined over the finite field? Applying the assertion after finite field extensions gives the usual Tate-class formulation.
