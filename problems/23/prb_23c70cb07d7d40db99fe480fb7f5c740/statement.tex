\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Banach space \(X\) is \(K\)-convex if its Rademacher projections are uniformly bounded on \(L^2\): explicitly, let \(\Omega_n=\{-1,1\}^n\) with uniform probability measure, let \(\varepsilon_j\) be the coordinate functions, and set
\[
P_n f=\sum_{j=1}^n\varepsilon_j\,\E_{\Omega_n}[\varepsilon_j f],
\qquad f:\Omega_n\to X.
\]
Then \(K\)-convexity means that there is a finite constant \(K\) such that
\[
\|P_n f\|_{L^2(\Omega_n;X)}\le K\|f\|_{L^2(\Omega_n;X)}
\quad\text{for every }n\ge1\text{ and every }f:\Omega_n\to X.
\]
