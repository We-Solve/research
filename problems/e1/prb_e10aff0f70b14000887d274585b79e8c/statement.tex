\Gamma_0(N)=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\operatorname{SL}_2(\Z):N\mid c\right\}.
\]
Write \(S_k(N)\) for the source's space of cusp forms of weight \(k\) and level \(N\). The source does not explicitly specify the permitted weights or the character convention, so these remain qualifications requiring review. For \(f\in S_k(N)\), use the unnormalized Petersson norm and the normalized Fourier coefficients specified in the source:
\[
\|f\|^2=\int_{\Gamma_0(N)\backslash\mathbb H}|f(x+iy)|^2y^{k-2}\,\dd x\,\dd y,
\qquad
f(z)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_f(n)n^{(k-1)/2}e(nz),
\qquad e(t)=\exp(2\pi i t).
\]
The requested assertion is that, for each admissible fixed weight \(k\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\), there exist constants \(C=C(k,\varepsilon)>0\) and \(B=B(k,\varepsilon)>0\) such that, for every positive integer \(N\) and every \(f\in S_k(N)\),

\|f\|^2\leq B\sum_{\substack{n\in\Z_{\geq1}\\n\leq C N^{1+\varepsilon}}}|a_f(n)|^2.

\subsection{Short English statement}
For each fixed admissible weight and every \(\varepsilon>0\), ask whether the unnormalized Petersson norm of every level-\(N\) cusp form is bounded uniformly by the sum of squares of its normalized Fourier coefficients through \(C N^{1+\varepsilon}\).
