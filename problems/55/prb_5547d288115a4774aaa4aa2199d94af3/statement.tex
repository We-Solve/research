\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F_n$ be the free group on $n$ named generators and use the stabilization $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\to\operatorname{Aut}(F_{n+1})$ that fixes the new generator. Put
\[
 \operatorname{Aut}(F_\infty)=\varinjlim_n\operatorname{Aut}(F_n).
\]
Here the notation means this stable direct-limit group, not the full automorphism group of a countably generated free group. Let $BG$ denote a group classifying space and $BG^+$ its Quillen plus construction in the customary stable perfect-subgroup convention. Let
\[
 QS^0=\varinjlim_n\Omega^nS^n
\]
be the infinite loop space of the sphere spectrum, denoted $\Omega^\infty S^\infty$ in the source; it has components indexed by $\Z$.

\textbf{Question 3.4.} Is there a homotopy equivalence
\[
 \Z\times B\operatorname{Aut}(F_\infty)^+\ \simeq\ QS^0?
\]
The factor $\Z$ is discrete and records components. The connected version would compare $B\operatorname{Aut}(F_\infty)^+$ with $Q_0S^0$, the degree-zero component, not with all of $QS^0$.

The supplied research summary reports an affirmative solution by Galatius (2011), expressed through agreement of stable integral homology of free-group automorphism groups and symmetric groups. This is a supplied solved-in-literature report, not a new proof or independent verification. Its shorthand statement $B\operatorname{Aut}(F_\infty)^+\simeq QS^0$ requires the component correction just explained.

The appended text motivates infinite-loop-space methods through Segal's surface category and conformal field theories [18]. It reports morphism spaces modeled homotopically by disjoint unions of $B\Gamma_{g,b}$, where $\Gamma_{g,b}$ is a surface mapping class group with parametrized boundary. It invokes Harer stability, Tillmann's infinite-loop-space structure on $B\Gamma_\infty^+$ [19], and the infinite-loop-map property of a map $\alpha$ in the source's Theorem 2.1 [13], with an algebraic $K$-theory analogy [20]. The map $\alpha$ is not defined in this record.

It also describes the oriented cobordism category $\mathcal C_d$, $d\ge1$: objects are embedded closed oriented $(d-1)$-manifolds in $\R^\infty$, and morphisms are compact oriented $d$-dimensional cobordisms in $[0,a]\times\R^\infty$ for $a>0$, meeting the end walls transversely, with the appropriate boundary objects. For $d=2$, the source reports equivalence with Segal's category at the level of classifying-space homotopy types [6,13], rather than asserting equality of the categories.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the component-indexed plus construction of the stable free-group automorphism classifying space equivalent to the infinite loop space of the sphere spectrum? The registry reports Galatius's affirmative solution; the connected version uses $Q_0S^0$.
