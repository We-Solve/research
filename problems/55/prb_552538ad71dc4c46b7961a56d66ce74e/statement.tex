If every large integer is a sum of at most $h$ elements of $A$, must the sums of at most $h$ distinct elements have bounded gaps? The answer is yes for $h=2$ and no for every $h\ge3$. We reconstruct the block counterexample of Hegyv\'ari, Hennecart and Plagne, \emph{Answer to a question of Burr and Erd\H{o}s on restricted addition and related results}, \url{https://hegyvari.web.elte.hu/artEB4.pdf}, including the role of earlier blocks.
