\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$. A group $H$ has Serre's Property FA if every simplicial action of $H$ on a nonempty tree without edge inversions has a global fixed vertex. No properness or cocompactness of the action is assumed. This is a simplicial-tree property, distinct from the stronger fixed-point property for all real trees.

Question 2.8 asks whether
\[
 \forall n\geq4\quad\forall H\leq\operatorname{Aut}(F_n),\qquad
 [\operatorname{Aut}(F_n):H]<\infty
 \Longrightarrow H\text{ has Property FA}.
\]
Equivalently, for every such $H$ and every inversion-free action on a tree $T$, there should be $v\in V(T)$ fixed by all of $H$. The question concerns every finite-index subgroup, including nonnormal ones, rather than only the full automorphism group. No uniform bound on index is imposed.

\subsection{Short English statement}
For every free-group rank $n\geq4$, must every finite-index subgroup of $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$ fix a vertex in every inversion-free simplicial tree action?
