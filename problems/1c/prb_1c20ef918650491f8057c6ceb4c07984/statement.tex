The supplied source asks: ``Solve $\bar\partial$ with $L^p$ estimates, $1<p<\infty$, for smooth convex domains in $\C^n$.''

The usual estimate can be expressed by the following template. Fix a positive integer $n$, a convex domain $\Omega\subseteq\C^n$ with smooth boundary, an exponent $1<p<\infty$, and a form degree $q$ with $1\leq q\leq n$. Here a domain is a connected open set. Write $L^p_{0,q}(\Omega)$ for the measurable $(0,q)$-forms whose coefficients have finite $L^p$ norm with respect to Euclidean volume. For a form $v$, the operator $\bar\partial$ is interpreted distributionally as
\[
\bar\partial v=\sum_{j=1}^{n}d\bar z_j\wedge\frac{\partial v}{\partial\bar z_j}.

\forall f\in L^p_{0,q}(\Omega),\qquad

\bar\partial u=f,\qquad
\|u\|_{L^p(\Omega)}\leq C_{\Omega,p,q}\|f\|_{L^p(\Omega)}.
