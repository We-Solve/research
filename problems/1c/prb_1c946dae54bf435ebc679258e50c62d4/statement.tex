\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be an integer, and let \(\mu\) be a nonnegative Borel measure on \(\R^{n+1}\). Suppose that there is a finite constant \(c_0\geq 0\) such that
\[
\mu(B(x,r))\leq c_0r^n\qquad(x\in\R^{n+1},\ r>0),
\]
where \(B(x,r)=\{y\in\R^{n+1}:|y-x|<r\}\). Write \(r(B)\) for the radius of a ball \(B\). Define
\[
\Theta_\mu(x,r)=\frac{\mu(B(x,r))}{r^n},\qquad
\beta_{\mu,2}(x,r)^2
=\inf_L\frac{1}{r^n}\int_{B(x,r)}
\left(\frac{\operatorname{dist}(y,L)}{r}\right)^2\,d\mu(y),
\]
where the infimum is over all affine \(n\)-dimensional planes \(L\subset\R^{n+1}\).

For \(\varepsilon>0\), define the truncated codimension-one Riesz transform, initially for bounded compactly supported functions \(f\), by
\[
\mathcal R_{\mu,\varepsilon}f(x)
=\int_{|x-y|>\varepsilon}
\frac{x-y}{|x-y|^{n+1}}f(y)\,d\mu(y).
\]
Interpret boundedness of the Riesz transform on \(L^2(\mu)\) as the existence of a finite constant \(M\) such that
\[
\|\mathcal R_{\mu,\varepsilon}f\|_{L^2(\mu;\R^{n+1})}
\leq M\|f\|_{L^2(\mu)}
\qquad(\varepsilon>0)
\]
for every real-valued bounded compactly supported \(f\), with \(M\) independent of \(\varepsilon\) and \(f\).

The question is whether, for every such \(n\) and \(\mu\), this boundedness holds if and only if there exists a finite constant \(C\geq 0\), independent of the ball \(B\), such that
\[
\int_B\int_0^{r(B)}
\beta_{\mu,2}(x,r)^2\Theta_\mu(x,r)
\,\frac{dr}{r}\,d\mu(x)
\leq C\mu(B)
\qquad\text{for every ball }B\subset\R^{n+1}.
\]
The constants may depend on the fixed measure and dimension; no quantitative comparison between \(M\) and \(C\) is specified by the record.
