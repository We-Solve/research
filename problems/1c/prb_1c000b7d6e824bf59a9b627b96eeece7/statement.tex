\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\textbf{Research problem.} Develop adaptive error control and optimal mesh-selection theories that use a posteriori estimates allowing cancellation of elementwise errors while preserving relevant stability properties. Here an adaptive method repeatedly computes a discrete PDE solution on a mesh, estimates the error from computed data, and changes the mesh in response. Cancellation means that the error assessment can retain the interaction of signed local contributions, rather than necessarily replacing their global sum by a sum of absolute values. The PDE class, error quantity, approximation spaces, admissible meshes, stability conditions, and meaning of optimality are not specified in the record, so this is a research program rather than a single quantified theorem.\par A conditional formulation illustrating the required choices is as follows. Seek a mesh-selection procedure that achieves a prescribed error tolerance while retaining the fixed stability conditions, together with an optimality or complexity theorem relative to the best meshes in that same admissible stable class.
