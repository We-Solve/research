\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
In the usual number-field pro-$p$ formulation, let $p$ be a prime, let $K$ be a number field, let $S$ be a finite set of finite primes of $K$ with no prime above $p$, and write $G_{K,S}=\operatorname{Gal}(K_S(p)/K)$, where $K_S(p)$ is the maximal pro-$p$ extension unramified at finite primes outside $S$. These are explicit contextual conventions: the extract itself does not define $G_{K,S}$ or specify its treatment of infinite places.

Let $\gamma(G)$ denote the source's rank-growth exponent. The first question is the universal assertion, in the intended arithmetic class,
\[
G_{K,S}\text{ infinite}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\gamma(G_{K,S})\geq\frac12.
\tag{RG}
\]
For an open subgroup $H$ of a pro-$p$ group, its generator rank is $d(H)=\dim_{\F_p}H^1(H,\F_p)$. However, the source gives no formula for $\gamma$ in terms of these ranks: the choice of subgroups or filtration, limit operation and normalization are missing. Thus (RG) is retained with its exact threshold, without inventing a definition of $\gamma$.
