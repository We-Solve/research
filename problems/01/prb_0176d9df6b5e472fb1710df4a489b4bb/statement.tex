\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Identify the oriented circle with $S^1=\R/\Z$. Model the universal covering group of $\operatorname{Homeo}^+(S^1)$ by
\[
G=\{f:\R\to\R\mid f\text{ is an increasing homeomorphism and }f(x+1)=f(x)+1\text{ for every }x\in\R\},
\]
with composition as the group operation. For $f\in G$, its lifted rotation number, also called its translation number, is
\[
\operatorname{rot}_{\mathrm{lift}}(f)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f^n(x)-x}{n}\in\R,
\]
where $f^n$ denotes the $n$th iterate; the limit exists and is independent of $x$. This is a real number, rather than its class modulo $\Z$. Let $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ be the free group on two generators, with identity $e$. A representation into $G$ means a group homomorphism $\rho:F_2\to G$.

The first question is whether
\[
\exists w\in F_2\setminus\{e\}\quad\forall\rho\in\operatorname{Hom}(F_2,G),\qquad
\operatorname{rot}_{\mathrm{lift}}\bigl(\rho(w)\bigr)=0.
\]

The second question is reported as follows: for arbitrary $w\in F_2$, does there exist a real-analytic representation $\rho$ such that
