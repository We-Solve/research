Thus the notation in the supplied statement uses an infinite principal specialization of a symmetric function.

Define
\[
(a;q)_\infty=\prod_{r\geq0}(1-aq^r),\qquad
\theta(a_1,\ldots,a_k;q)=\prod_{s=1}^k(a_s;q)_\infty(q/a_s;q)_\infty.

For every pair of positive integers \(m,n\), put \(\kappa=2m+2n+1\). The requested problem is to find a combinatorial interpretation of the identity
\[
\sum_{\substack{\lambda\text{ a partition}\\\lambda_1\leq m}}

\frac{(q^\kappa;q^\kappa)_\infty^n}{(q;q)_\infty^n}
\prod_{i=1}^n\theta(q^{i+m};q^\kappa)

\]
Empty products are understood to equal \(1\).
