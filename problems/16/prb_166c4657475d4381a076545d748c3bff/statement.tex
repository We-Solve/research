\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A generic plane immersion is understood here as a smooth immersion \(\gamma:S^1\to\R^2\) whose only multiple points are finitely many transverse double points. A crossing assignment chooses, at each double point, which of the two branches passes over the other. This produces a knot diagram; it does not mean smoothing the crossing.

For a specified collection \(\mathscr K\) of knot types, let
\[
\mathcal D(\gamma)=\{K\in\mathscr K: \text{some crossing assignment on }\gamma\text{ gives a diagram of }K\}.
\]
The usual interpretation takes \(\mathscr K\) to be the ambient isotopy classes of tame, unoriented knots in \(\R^3\). This convention, as well as the smoothness convention above, is not expressly specified in the supplied record and requires confirmation.

Let \((\gamma_n)_{n\geq 1}\) be a sequence of generic plane immersions of \(S^1\). Under the eventual-tail interpretation of ``eventually carries every knot,'' the requested problem is to find necessary and sufficient conditions on this sequence for
\[
\forall K\in\mathscr K\;\exists N_K\geq 1\;\forall n\geq N_K,
\qquad K\in\mathcal D(\gamma_n).
\tag{1}
\]
The crossing assignment may depend on both \(K\) and \(n\).
