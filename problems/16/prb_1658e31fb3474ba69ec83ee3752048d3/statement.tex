\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\mathcal A$ be the finite alphabet of a one-sided subshift $S\subseteq\mathcal A^{\N_0}$, equipped with the product topology and the left shift $\sigma_S$. Thus $S$ is closed and shift invariant; whether $\sigma_S$ must be onto is not made explicit in the source. Let
\[
 T=\{1,\ldots,N\}^{\N_0}
\]
be the full one-sided shift with shift $\sigma_T$. Here $\N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and $N$ is a positive integer. Write $h(S)$ for topological entropy, equivalently the exponential growth rate of the numbers of allowed words.

Under the supplied hypotheses
\[
 h(S)<\log N,\qquad
 \forall x\in S,\quad
 |\{y\in S:\sigma_S(y)=x\}|\le N,
\]
must there exist a continuous injection $\iota:S\to T$ with
\[
 \iota\circ\sigma_S=\sigma_T\circ\iota\ ?
\]
Because $S$ is compact and $T$ is Hausdorff, such an injection is a homeomorphism onto its image, and that image is a subshift. This is the topological meaning of ``isomorphic to a subshift of $T$'' used here, rather than a merely measurable isomorphism.
