The exact graph, directed-versus-undirected convention, edge labels, and rooting/orientation equivalence used for the uniform law are not supplied.

In a usual geometric embedding, scaling lattice coordinates by $1/N$ produces curves on the unit flat torus. A continuum-limit statement would need interpolation, starting-point law, time normalization, curve topology, and a limit as $N\to\infty$.
