\section{Convex Relaxations for Planted Graph Matching}\label{30005063}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 30005063 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A permutation $\pi$ represents a planted vertex correspondence; its permutation matrix has one $1$ in each row and column. A doubly stochastic matrix is an entrywise nonnegative square matrix with each row and column sum equal to $1$. The Frobenius norm is $\lVert M\rVert_F^2=\sum_{i,j}|M_{ij}|^2$. The optimization relaxes a search over permutation matrices to doubly stochastic matrices. Rounding converts an optimizer back into a vertex permutation, by the source rounding rule. With high probability means probability tending to $1$ as matrix dimension grows.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $\pi$ be a uniformly random permutation, and let $A$ and $B$ be correlated symmetric random matrices under the planted correspondence $\pi$. For which correlations does the minimizer of
$$
\min_{X\ \mathrm{doubly\ stochastic}}\|AX-XB\|_F^2
$$
round to $\pi$ with high probability? \sref{30005063}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
The correlated random-matrix family, signal normalization and rounding map must be the ones specified by the source; arbitrary joint symmetric-matrix laws do not give the same recovery threshold.
