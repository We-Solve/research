\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701745}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite group \(G\) and \(g\in G\), let \(o(g)\) denote the order of \(g\), and define
\[
\pi(o(g))=\{p\in\N : p\text{ is prime and }p\mid o(g)\}.
\]
The condition in the question is
\[
\forall g\in G,\qquad |\pi(o(g))|\leq 2.
\]
Thus the bound concerns distinct prime divisors; their exponents are unrestricted, and the identity has no prime divisors in its order.

A composition series of \(G\) is a chain
\[
1=G_0\triangleleft G_1\triangleleft\cdots\triangleleft G_n=G
\]
with \(n\geq 0\), such that each quotient \(G_i/G_{i-1}\), for \(1\leq i\leq n\), is a nontrivial simple group. These quotients are the composition factors.

Determine, up to isomorphism, all nonabelian finite simple groups \(S\) for which there exist a finite group \(G\) satisfying the displayed element-order condition, a composition series of \(G\), and an index \(i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\) such that
\[
G_i/G_{i-1}\cong S.
\]
The supplied source points separately to a reference for the case in which every element order has at most one prime divisor; it supplies no theorem from that reference. The local record labels this classification problem open; that label is supplied source metadata, not an independently verified literature assessment.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify the nonabelian simple groups that occur as composition factors of finite groups in which every element order has at most two distinct prime divisors. The question is attributed to W. J. Shi (2022). The supplied record labels it open and gives a separate reference for the one-prime case.
