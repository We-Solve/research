Let $L$ be a Lie group, $G<L$ a closed subgroup, $\Lambda<L$ a lattice, $a\in G$ semisimple, and $x_0\in L/\Lambda$ with $\overline{Gx_0}=L/\Lambda$. Define the expanding horospherical subgroup
\[
 U=\{u\in G:a^{-n}ua^n\longrightarrow e\text{ as }n\to\infty\}.
\]
For a real-analytic curve $\gamma:[0,1]\to U$, let $\nu$ be the image of Lebesgue probability measure under $t\mapsto\gamma(t)x_0$, and let $\lambda$ be Haar probability on $L/\Lambda$. N. Shah's Question 17 asks for conditions on $\gamma$ ensuring
\[
 (a^n)_*\nu\overset{w^*}{\longrightarrow}\lambda,
 \qquad\text{that is,}\qquad
 \int_0^1 f\bigl(a^n\gamma(t)x_0\bigr)\,\dd t
 \longrightarrow\int_{L/\Lambda}f\,\dd\lambda
 \quad(f\in C_c(L/\Lambda)).
\]
The preceding source notes convergence for measures arising from probabilities on $U$ absolutely continuous with respect to its Haar measure, citing [177]; the curve question is a refinement for potentially singular measures. The current extract's trailing ``Recently'' is an incomplete continuation, not an extra condition. The aid reports that a following rank-one ambient-group conjecture has been proved, without claiming a general classification in this full setting.

An obstruction reported by the aid is containment of $\gamma([0,1])x_0$ in a closed set $F\subset L/\Lambda$ with $aF\subset F$ and $\lambda(F)<1$. Then all translated measures are supported on $F$ and cannot converge to $\lambda$.

The supplied exact model is separate from the homogeneous problem. Let $d\ge1$, $q>1$, $\eta:[0,1]\to\R^d$ be real analytic, $t_0\in[0,1]$, and $w\in L^1([0,1])$ satisfy $w\ge0$ and $\int_0^1w=1$. On $\mathbb T^d=\R^d/\Z^d$, put

The aid reports that $H_\eta$ is a connected subtorus and that the weak cluster measures of $\sigma_n$ are exactly normalized Haar measures on the cosets $z+H_\eta$ whose classes are cluster points of
