Let \(\Delta_n\) denote the standard \(n\)-dimensional simplex, with \(n+1\) vertices, and let \(\Delta_n^{(k)}\) be its simplicial subcomplex consisting of all faces of dimension at most \(k\). Write \(|\Delta_n^{(k)}|\) for its geometric realization. For a manifold \(M\), define its \(k\)th Betti number over \(\mathbb Z_2\) by

b_k(M;\mathbb Z_2)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}H_k(M;\mathbb F_2),
