\section{Liouville Theory from the Virasoro Conformal Net}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A conformal net assigns local von Neumann algebras to intervals, with locality and conformal covariance. The Virasoro net is generated by the corresponding stress-energy representation. The reported construction assigns algebras, bimodules and intertwiners to points, one-dimensional bordisms and conformal surfaces. Liouville theory is the proposed resulting nonrational conformal theory.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Prove that applying the fully extended two-dimensional conformal-field-theory construction to the Virasoro conformal net produces Liouville conformal field theory.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}For a nonrational net the source construction is defined on the subcategory whose connected surface components have nonempty incoming and outgoing boundaries. An extension to every conformal bordism is not silently assumed.

\paragraph{A continuous-spectrum annulus test.} On the auxiliary Hilbert space \(\mathcal H=L^2([0,\infty),dP)\), let \(K\) multiply by \(P^2+\kappa\), with \(\kappa\geq0\). The operators \(A_t=e^{-tK}\), \(t>0\), are bounded contractions and satisfy the exact sewing identity \(A_sA_t=A_{s+t}\). This models one analytic requirement of gluing annuli with nonempty incoming and outgoing boundaries, without identifying the actual Virasoro-net spectrum.
\paragraph{Why closing an annulus is additional work.} Despite exponential decay in \(P\), \(A_t\) is not trace class on this nonatomic space. Choose infinitely many disjoint positive-measure subsets \(E_j\subset[0,1]\) and normalized indicator functions \(f_j\). They are orthonormal and
\[
 \langle f_j,A_tf_j\rangle\geq e^{-t(1+\kappa)}.
\]
Thus the partial sums of diagonal matrix elements diverge, which a positive trace-class operator cannot do. Integrating the multiplier \(e^{-t(P^2+\kappa)}\) against \(dP\) is not its Hilbert-space operator trace. This explicitly rejects an attempted passage from open-boundary sewing to an automatically finite closed partition function.
\paragraph{Remaining identification.} The source nonrational construction is restricted to connected surfaces with nonempty incoming and outgoing boundaries, so the trace test is a warning about an unnecessary extension, not an obstruction to its stated functor. To identify that functor with Liouville theory one must match state spaces, spectral measures, annulus operators and interaction/sewing maps such as pair-of-pants amplitudes. Even a correct annulus spectrum would not determine all of those maps. The first gap is a source-specific unitary comparison compatible with these nontrivial sewing operations; none is supplied here.
