First, let \(X\) be a projective complex K3 surface, meaning a smooth simply connected projective surface with trivial canonical bundle, and let \(f\in\operatorname{Aut}(X)\) have positive topological entropy \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)>0\). Does there exist \(x\in X\) such that
\[
\overline{\{f^m(x):m\in\Z\}}^{\mathrm{an}}=X?
\]
The closure is in the usual complex-analytic, or Euclidean, topology, as specified by the supplied literature assessment; Zariski density would be weaker. The display uses a two-sided automorphism orbit. The source does not specify whether only forward iterates \(m\geq0\) are intended, so this convention needs source confirmation. The question asks for existence of a dense orbit for every such automorphism, not density of every point's orbit.

Second, in the usual irreducible holomorphic symplectic interpretation of hyperk\"ahler manifold, let \(M\) be a compact simply connected K\"ahler manifold with
\[
H^0(M,\Omega_M^2)=\C\sigma,

\[
a(M)=\operatorname{trdeg}_{\C}\mathcal M(M),

where \(\mathcal M(M)\) is its field of meromorphic functions. Is

a(M)\in\{0,m,2m\}?

Ask whether every positive-entropy automorphism of a projective K3 surface has an analytically dense orbit, and whether a compact irreducible hyperk\"ahler manifold has algebraic dimension zero, half its complex dimension, or its full dimension. Orbit and manifold conventions need confirmation.
