\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((G_i)_{i\in I}\) be the infinite family of finite groups in the source. Under the usual undirected Cayley-graph convention choose symmetric generating sets \(S_i=S_i^{-1}\subseteq G_i\setminus\{1\}\), and form \(\operatorname{Cay}(G_i,S_i)\) with edges between \(g\) and \(gs\). The task asks whether the given family admits constants \(D<\infty\), \(h>0\) and such sets \(S_i\) with
\[
 |S_i|\leq D\quad\text{for all }i,
\]
\[
 |\partial_E A|\geq h|A|
 \quad\text{for every }i\text{ and }\varnothing\ne A\subseteq G_i
 \text{ with }|A|\leq|G_i|/2.
\]
Here \(\partial_E A\) consists of graph edges with exactly one endpoint in \(A\). The constants are fixed for the family, while the generating set may depend on \(i\). \subsection{Short English statement}
For the given finite-group family, can one choose generators with one common size bound and one common positive expansion constant? The sets may vary by group. The source supplies no family hypotheses or growth convention, so this is not a universal affirmative claim about arbitrary finite groups.
