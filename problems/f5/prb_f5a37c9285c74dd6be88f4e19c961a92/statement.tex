\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an integer \(n\geq 1\), let
\[
S^{n-1}=\{v\in\R^n:\langle v,v\rangle=1\}.
\]
For \(0<\alpha<1\), define \(N_\alpha(n)\) to be the maximum cardinality of a family \(\mathcal L\) of distinct one-dimensional linear subspaces of \(\R^n\) such that
\[
|\langle u,v\rangle|=\alpha
\]
whenever \(u\) and \(v\) are unit representatives of distinct members of \(\mathcal L\). The absolute value makes this condition independent of the choices of representatives.
