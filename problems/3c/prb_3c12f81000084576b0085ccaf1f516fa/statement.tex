\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a metric space \((X,d)\), write
\[
B_X(x,R)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)\le R\}.
\]
The cardinality convention for \emph{bounded geometry} is
\[
\forall R>0\quad \sup_{x\in X}|B_X(x,R)|<\infty.
\]
Some conventions additionally require uniform discreteness, meaning that there exists \(\delta>0\) such that \(d(x,y)\ge\delta\) whenever \(x\ne y\). The supplied record does not specify whether this additional condition is intended.

For \(2<p<\infty\), let \(\ell_p\) be the space of scalar sequences \(a=(a_n)_{n\ge1}\) satisfying \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|^p<\infty\), equipped with the metric induced by
\[
\|a\|_p=\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|^p\right)^{1/p}.
\]
The scalar field may be \(\R\) or \(\C\); the source does not specify one. A \emph{coarse embedding} of a metric space \((X,d_X)\) into a metric space \((Y,d_Y)\) is a map \(f:X\to Y\) for which there exist nondecreasing functions \(\rho_-,\rho_+:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)\) such that
\[
\lim_{t\to\infty}\rho_-(t)=\infty,
\qquad
\rho_-(d_X(x,y))\le d_Y(f(x),f(y))\le\rho_+(d_X(x,y))
\quad(x,y\in X).
\]
