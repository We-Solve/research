\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A rack is a set with bijective right translations and right self-distributive operation. The free rack \(F_{\mathrm{Rack}}(S)\) on a set \(S\) has the property that every map from \(S\) to a rack extends uniquely to a rack homomorphism. Let \(X\) be the free rack intended by the source, and retain its notation \(\Gamma X\) for the associated space.

The question is whether \(\Gamma X\), with the intended metric, is CAT\((0)\). A CAT\((0)\) space is a geodesic metric space in which distances between points on sides of any geodesic triangle are at most the corresponding distances in its Euclidean comparison triangle. The source states that a positive answer would imply
\[
 \pi_n(\Gamma X,x)=0\qquad(n\geq2)
\]
for every relevant basepoint \(x\).

The construction \(\Gamma\), the number or set of free generators, and the metric are absent from the excerpt. It is therefore unresolved whether the question concerns a specified cubical metric, existence of some metric, a local nonpositive-curvature condition, or a universal cover. These are distinct targets; none is substituted for the printed global CAT\((0)\) question.
