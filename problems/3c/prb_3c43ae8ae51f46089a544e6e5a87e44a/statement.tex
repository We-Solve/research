\section{Computing Hecke Operators for Arithmetic-Group Cohomology}\label{30003266}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 30003266 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


Arithmetic-group cohomology is the cohomology of the specified arithmetic group or associated quotient. A Hecke operator is a correspondence-induced linear action on it; eigenvalues are its scalar actions on eigenvectors.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Can Hecke operators and their eigenvalues be computed for all arithmetic groups for which the cohomology can be calculated? \sref{30003266}{2}
