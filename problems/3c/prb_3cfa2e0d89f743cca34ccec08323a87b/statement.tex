The History of Women in Combinatorics

What is the history of women in combinatorics? Answering this question might involve researchers from many different areas, including historians, sociologists, mathematicians, and data scientists. As other possible guiding questions: What departments are producing the most women PhDs in math? What are they doing right? What is going wrong at other departments?
