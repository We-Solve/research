Let \(W=S^2\times S^2\setminus\operatorname{int}(D^4)\), where \(D^4\) is a smoothly embedded closed ball, and for each integer \(n\geq 1\) let

W_n=\mathop{\natural}^{n}W
\]
be the boundary connected sum of \(n\) copies of \(W\). Thus \(W_n\) is a compact smooth four-manifold with boundary diffeomorphic to \(S^3\). Use the conventional interpretation that \(\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_n)\) is the full group of smooth diffeomorphisms that are the identity on a neighborhood of the boundary, equipped with its \(C^\infty\) topology. Write \(BG\) for the classifying space of a topological group \(G\), and \(H_k(-;\Q)\) for singular homology with rational coefficients.

Under the usual boundary connected sum stabilization convention, attaching another copy of \(W\) and extending diffeomorphisms by the identity gives maps
\[
s_n:B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_n)\longrightarrow B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_{n+1}).
\]

H_k^{\mathrm{st}}=\varinjlim_{n\geq 1}\bigl(H_k(B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_n);\Q),(s_n)_*\bigr),
