\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $(X,d)$ be a proper metric space, meaning closed bounded subsets are compact, and choose $o\in X$. The normalized distance functions are
\[
 h_x(z)=d(z,x)-d(o,x).
\]
A usual horofunction compactification is obtained by taking the closure of these functions in the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets; limit functions not representing points of $X$ form the horofunction boundary. The source proposes an incidence geometry at infinity built from horofunctions, halfspaces, and limiting objects called stars.
