\paragraph{A round time-symmetric exterior calculation.} Restrict to \(k=0\) and a spherical exterior metric \(g=dr^2/f(r)+r^2d\Omega^2\), \(f>0\). Computing its scalar curvature yields \(R_g=2(1-f-rf')/r^2\). Thus the time-symmetric vacuum constraint gives \((rf)'=1\), or
\[
 f(r)=1-2m/r.
\]
At a round boundary of radius \(R\), the unit normal is \(\sqrt f\,\partial_r\) and the area derivative gives \(H=2\sqrt{f(R)}/R\). Accordingly the boundary pair \((R,H)\), with \(H>0\), determines
\[
 m=\frac R2\left(1-\frac{H^2R^2}{4}\right).
\]
The radial family is asymptotically flat and has \(f>0\) for \(r\ge R\). The momentum boundary component is zero since \(k=0\). This verifies uniqueness of the mass parameter within this restricted radial ansatz.
\paragraph{Limits of the reduction.} It proves neither uniqueness among nonspherical stationary exteriors nor an extension for arbitrary source boundary tensors. The source allows expansion that is merely not everywhere nonpositive, substantially weaker than the pointwise \(H>0\) used here with \(k=0\). Also regular dominant-energy interiors may constrain the allowed round data; none is constructed for every algebraic pair.
