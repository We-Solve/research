Does some constant $c>0$ guarantee a $K_4$ in every sufficiently large $n$-vertex graph with at least $(1/8-c)n^2$ edges and independence number less than $n/\log n$? The answer is no. We reconstruct the quantitative Bollob\'as--Erd\H{o}s construction as developed by Fox--Loh--Zhao. Two precise geometric existence/measure estimates are used as external inputs; the graph construction, forbidden-subgraph proof and parameter choice are supplied below.
