\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(E/\Q\) be an elliptic curve without complex multiplication, meaning that \(\operatorname{End}_{\overline{\Q}}(E)\cong\Z\), and let \(K\) be an imaginary quadratic field, that is, a degree-two extension of \(\Q\) with no real embedding. For each rational prime \(p\) of good reduction for \(E\), write \(E_p/\F_p\) for its reduction and define
\[
a_p(E)=p+1-\#E_p(\F_p).
\]
Let \(\pi_p(E)\in\C\) be either root of the Frobenius polynomial
\[
T^2-a_p(E)T+p.
\]
The field \(\Q(\pi_p(E))\) is independent of the choice of root, since the two roots sum to \(a_p(E)\). The reduction is called ordinary when \(p\nmid a_p(E)\). For real \(x>1\), define
\[
\Pi_E(K;x)=\#\bigl\{p\le x : p\text{ is a rational prime of good ordinary reduction for }E,\quad \Q(\pi_p(E))\cong_{\Q}K\bigr\}.
\]
The supplied Lang--Trotter conjecture for fixed Frobenius fields asks whether, for every such \(E\) and every such \(K\), there exists a real constant \(c(E,K)>0\), independent of \(x\), such that
\[
\Pi_E(K;x)\sim c(E,K)\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\log x}\qquad(x\to\infty),
\]
where \(\log\) denotes the natural logarithm. Equivalently, the ratio of the left-hand side to the displayed expression tends to \(1\). Strict positivity of \(c(E,K)\) is part of the supplied conjecture; no additional restrictions on \(E\) or \(K\) are supplied. The source record labels this problem open and its statement corrected\_verified; these are supplied curation labels, not independent mathematical verification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every elliptic curve over \(\Q\) without complex multiplication and every imaginary quadratic field \(K\), the conjecture predicts that the number of good ordinary primes up to \(x\) whose Frobenius field is isomorphic to \(K\) is asymptotic to a positive constant times \(\sqrt{x}/\log x\).
