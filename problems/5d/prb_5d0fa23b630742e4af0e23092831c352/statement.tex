\section{Higher-Dimensional Quantum Cellular Automata beyond Translations}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A quantum cellular automaton is an automorphism of a lattice observable algebra which increases operator support by only a uniformly bounded range. Finite-depth local circuits form the source's trivial subgroup; lattice shifts supply another allowed class. The quotient asks about locality-preserving automorphisms beyond these constructions.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Classify nontrivial elements in the quotient of higher-dimensional quantum cellular automata by finite-depth circuits and shifts. In particular, determine which elements occur beyond lattice translations and the currently known three-dimensional examples.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}Classification must keep the spatial dimension, stabilization and equivalence relation specified in the source; a one-dimensional index classification does not answer the higher-dimensional problem.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify higher-dimensional cellular automata beyond finite-depth circuits, lattice shifts and the currently known examples.
