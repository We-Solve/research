\subsection{Short English statement}
Find the smallest number of points in \(\R^d\) that always permits a partition into two nonempty parts such that every union of at most \(s\) convex sets containing the first part intersects every union of at most \(t\) convex sets containing the second. The source also asks for polynomial growth in the parameters.
