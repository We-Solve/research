\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Albert Fathi's original question asks whether, for generic $L\in C^\infty$, the corresponding Hamilton--Jacobi equation has only a one-parameter family of viscosity solutions. The source does not identify the configuration manifold, equation, topology of genericity, or cohomology class. The aid explicitly reconstructs the following autonomous Tonelli weak-KAM setting, in which the parameter is addition of a constant.

Let $M$ be a compact connected smooth manifold and $L:TM\to\R$ a smooth fiberwise strictly convex, superlinear Tonelli Lagrangian. Fix $c\in H^1(M;\R)$, represented by a smooth closed one-form $\eta_c$, let $H$ be the Legendre-dual Hamiltonian, and let $\alpha(c)$ be the critical value. Critical viscosity solutions solve
\[
 H(x,\eta_c+\dd u)=\alpha(c).
\]
Define the Peierls barrier by
\[
 h_c(x,y)=\liminf_{T\to\infty}\left\{
 \inf_{\substack{\gamma(0)=x,\ \gamma(T)=y\\\gamma\text{ absolutely continuous}}}
 \int_0^T(L(\gamma,\dot\gamma)-\eta_c(\dot\gamma))\,\dd t
 +T\alpha(c)\right\}.
\]
Let $\mathcal A_c=\{a:h_c(a,a)=0\}$ be the projected Aubry set. On it put $\rho_c(a,b)=h_c(a,b)+h_c(b,a)$; static classes are the equivalence classes for $\rho_c=0$.

The aid states that all critical viscosity solutions differ by constants if and only if $\mathcal A_c$ consists of one static class. More quantitatively, for $a,b\in\mathcal A_c$ let $u_a=h_c(a,\cdot)$, and equip continuous functions modulo constants with
\[
 d_\infty([u],[v])=\inf_{C\in\R}\|u-v-C\|_\infty.
\]
Its reported exact identity is
\[
 d_\infty([u_a],[u_b])=\tfrac12\rho_c(a,b).
\]
Thus the map $[a]\mapsto[h_c(a,\cdot)]$ embeds the quotient Aubry set isometrically when its metric is $\rho_c/2$. This statement concerns elementary solutions; uniqueness of the entire critical-solution space is the separate static-class criterion above.

For a fixed Tonelli $L$ and fixed $c$, the aid invokes Ma\~n\'e's generic uniqueness of the minimizing measure for smooth potential perturbations $L+V$, $V\in C^\infty(M)$, to obtain a residual set of potentials for which the critical solutions are precisely $\{u+C:C\in\R\}$. Here residual means containing a countable intersection of open dense sets in the specified smooth-potential topology. For every prescribed countable set $\{c_j\}$, intersection of the corresponding residual sets yields simultaneous uniqueness modulo constants at every $c_j$. This does not give one residual set working for all uncountably many cohomology classes, nor does it identify the original unrestricted meaning of ``generic $L$.'' The exact hypotheses of the invoked generic theorem still require source review.
\subsection{Short English statement}
In the explicitly reconstructed fixed-cohomology Tonelli problem, uniqueness up to addition of constants is equivalent to having one Aubry static class. The supplied generic-potential result applies at a fixed class, or simultaneously at any prescribed countable set of classes, rather than automatically at every class.
