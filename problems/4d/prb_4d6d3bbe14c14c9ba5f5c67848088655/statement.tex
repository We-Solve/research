\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{\Q}$ of $\Q$. The maximal abelian extension $\Q^{\mathrm{ab}}\subseteq\overline{\Q}$ is the compositum of all finite Galois extensions of $\Q$ in $\overline{\Q}$ with abelian Galois group. A nonzero abelian variety over $\Q$ means an abelian variety $A/\Q$ with $\dim A>0$. For an extension $K/\Q$, let $A(K)$ denote its abelian group of $K$-rational points, and define
\[
\operatorname{rank} A(K)=\dim_{\Q}\bigl(A(K)\otimes_{\Z}\Q\bigr).
\]
This definition does not require $A(K)$ to be finitely generated.

The supplied question is whether
\[
\text{for every abelian variety }A/\Q\text{ with }\dim A>0,\qquad
\operatorname{rank} A(\Q^{\mathrm{ab}})=\infty.
\]
Equivalently, for every such $A$ and every integer $n\geq 1$, there exist $P_1,\ldots,P_n\in A(\Q^{\mathrm{ab}})$ whose images in $A(\Q^{\mathrm{ab}})\otimes_{\Z}\Q$ are linearly independent over $\Q$.

The supplied record labels this question open. Its background and literature assessment instead discuss whether $\Q^{\mathrm{ab}}$ is ample. The record does not establish an equivalence between that field property and the infinite-rank assertion above; consequently, that assessment does not by itself verify the status of the stated question.
