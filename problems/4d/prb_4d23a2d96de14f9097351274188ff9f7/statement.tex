\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor is a diffuse von Neumann algebra \(N\) with center \(\C 1\) and a faithful normal tracial state \(\tau_N\), normalized by \(\tau_N(1)=1\). The supplied statement imposes no separability hypothesis.

Under the usual convention that \(\mathcal U\) is a nonprincipal ultrafilter on \(\N\), the tracial ultrapower is
\[
N^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(\N,N)\big/\mathcal I_{\mathcal U},\qquad
\mathcal I_{\mathcal U}=\left\{(x_n):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau_N}=0\right\},
\]
where \(\ell^\infty(\N,N)\) denotes the algebra of operator-norm-bounded sequences and \(\|x\|_{2,\tau_N}=\tau_N(x^*x)^{1/2}\).

For a type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(M\) and \(t>0\), its amplification is
\[
M^t=p\bigl(M\,\overline\otimes\,B(\ell^2(\N))\bigr)p,
\qquad (\tau_M\otimes\operatorname{Tr})(p)=t,
\]
with the normalized trace on the corner; here \(\operatorname{Tr}\) assigns trace one to a rank-one projection. The amplification is well-defined up to von Neumann algebra isomorphism. The fundamental group is
\[
\mathcal F(M)=\{t\in\R_{>0}:M^t\cong M\}.
\]
It is full when \(\mathcal F(M)=\R_{>0}\).
