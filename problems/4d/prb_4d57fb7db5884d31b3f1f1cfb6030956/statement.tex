The supplied record asks for a comparison between the isomorphism-class distribution of the \(p^e\)-Selmer group \(\operatorname{Sel}_{p^e}(E)\) of an elliptic curve \(E\) and a limiting distribution of intersections of random maximal isotropic direct summands. The base field of \(E\), the family of elliptic curves, its ordering and weighting, and the intended range of primes are not specified.

For clarity, if a number field \(K\) is intended, the usual Selmer group is
\[
\operatorname{Sel}_{p^e}(E/K)
=\ker\!\left(
H^1(K,E[p^e])\longrightarrow
\prod_v
\frac{H^1(K_v,E[p^e])}
{\operatorname{im}\bigl(E(K_v)/p^eE(K_v)\longrightarrow H^1(K_v,E[p^e])\bigr)}
\right),
\]
where \(v\) ranges over all places of \(K\), \(E[p^e]\) is the kernel of multiplication by \(p^e\), the displayed local maps are Kummer maps, and the cohomology is Galois cohomology. This definition is conditional on a number-field interpretation; the record does not identify \(K\).
