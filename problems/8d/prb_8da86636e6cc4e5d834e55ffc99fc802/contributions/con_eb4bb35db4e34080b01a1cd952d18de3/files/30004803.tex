\section{Asymptotics of Expanding Vacuum Spacetimes Near Singularities}\label{30004803}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30004803 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
A globally hyperbolic spacetime has Cauchy hypersurfaces crossed once by every inextendible causal curve. Vacuum means zero matter stress tensor. A constant-mean-curvature (CMC) foliation uses spatial slices on which the trace of their second fundamental form is spatially constant; its sign convention and monotone time parameter describe expansion. Compact Cauchy slices describe a spatially closed cosmological model. Past-singularity asymptotics and future expanding behavior are distinct temporal limits.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For a globally hyperbolic expanding vacuum spacetime with compact Cauchy hypersurfaces and a CMC foliation, what is the asymptotic behavior as the past singularity is approached, and what is the future behavior of the spacetime?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The report discusses the $3+1$-dimensional setting, with some results extending further. No particular rescaled limit, topology or genericity condition is fixed by the extraction; both temporal directions remain in the research target.

\paragraph{An exact compact Kasner benchmark.} On \((0,\infty)\times\mathbb T^3\), take \(g=-dt^2+\sum_{i=1}^3t^{2p_i}dx_i^2\) with \(\sum p_i=\sum p_i^2=1\). Direct Christoffel differentiation gives \(R_{00}=(\sum p_i-\sum p_i^2)/t^2=0\) and \(R_{ii}=p_i(\sum p_j-1)t^{2p_i-2}=0\), with off-diagonal Ricci entries zero. Thus the metric is vacuum. In the convention \(k=-\frac12\partial_tg_{\rm spatial}\), the mean curvature is \(-1/t\), and the compact slice volume is its unit-coordinate volume times \(t\).
Its orthonormal curvature components give
\[
 |\operatorname{Riem}|_{\rm Lorentz}^2
 =\frac4{t^4}\left[\sum_i p_i^2(p_i-1)^2+\sum_{i<j}p_i^2p_j^2\right],
\]
where the left side denotes the full curvature contraction in this diagonal metric. The bracket is positive except for permutations of \((1,0,0)\).
\paragraph{Two temporal limits.} Generic members of this family therefore have curvature blowup as \(t\downarrow0\) and curvature decay as \(t\to\infty\). Volume expansion does not imply expansion in every direction: the admissible triple \((-1/3,2/3,2/3)\) contracts its first circle as time increases. The exceptional flat triple also shows that the CMC parameter alone does not certify curvature blowup.
\paragraph{Remaining scope.} These homogeneous examples are familiar exact solutions. They do not classify inhomogeneous compact CMC vacuum cosmologies, establish future completeness for all of them, or provide a generic rescaled singular limit. The first gap is uniform control of spatial-curvature and anisotropy terms outside the homogeneous family, in both temporal directions.
