For a fixed base scheme \(S\), an algebraic stack \(\mathcal X\) over \(S\) is Deligne--Mumford if it admits an étale surjective atlas by a scheme. It is separated over \(S\) if its diagonal \(\Delta_{\mathcal X/S}\colon\mathcal X\to\mathcal X\times_S\mathcal X\) is proper.

Given an algebraic group scheme \(G\) over \(S\) acting on an \(S\)-scheme or algebraic space \(U\), the quotient stack \([U/G]\) assigns to an \(S\)-scheme \(T\) the groupoid of principal \(G_T\)-torsors \(P\to T\) for the fppf topology equipped with a \(G_T\)-equivariant morphism \(P\to U\times_S T\).
