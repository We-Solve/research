\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(W_n=C_2^n\rtimes S_n\) be the hyperoctahedral group, with \(S_n\) permuting the sign coordinates. A bipartition \(\lambda=(\lambda^+,\lambda^-)\) of \(n\) is an ordered pair of partitions with \(|\lambda^+|+|\lambda^-|=n\); write \(\operatorname{Bip}(n)\) for their set. Conjugacy classes are indexed by signed cycle type: the product of signs around a cycle is positive or negative and its length contributes to the corresponding partition. Let \(C_\lambda\) be the class with the source's indexing convention and choose \(w_\lambda\in C_\lambda\).

Let \((E_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\operatorname{Bip}(n)}\) be the stated family in the rational Mantaci--Reutenauer algebra, viewed as a subalgebra of \(\Q W_n\). Its hypotheses are
\[
 E_\lambda^2=E_\lambda,\qquad
 E_\lambda E_\mu=0\ (\lambda\ne\mu),\qquad
 \sum_\lambda E_\lambda=1,\qquad
 \dim_\Q(\Q W_nE_\lambda)=|C_\lambda|,
\]
and each \(E_\lambda\) is primitive in that subalgebra. Primitivity is not asserted in the full group algebra, nor is centrality imposed. The left ideal \(\Q W_nE_\lambda\) is a \(W_n\)-module under left multiplication.

The question is whether, for every bipartition \(\lambda\), there exists a linear character
\[
 \zeta_\lambda:C_{W_n}(w_\lambda)\longrightarrow\C^\times
\]
such that \(\Q W_nE_\lambda\) affords the induced character \(\operatorname{Ind}_{C_{W_n}(w_\lambda)}^{W_n}\zeta_\lambda\). In the ordinary complex-character interpretation this means
\[
 \C\otimes_\Q(\Q W_nE_\lambda)
 \cong\operatorname{Ind}_{C_{W_n}(w_\lambda)}^{W_n}\C_{\zeta_\lambda}
\]
as complex \(W_n\)-modules, where \(C_{W_n}(w)=\{g:gw=wg\}\). It does not require the one-dimensional character itself to take values in \(\Q\). The dimension condition agrees with \(|C_\lambda|=[W_n:C_{W_n}(w_\lambda)]\), but equality of dimensions alone is not the asserted character identity.
