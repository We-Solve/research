\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$ and let $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n)$. A CAT$(0)$ cube complex is a cube complex with its intrinsic Euclidean cubical metric satisfying the CAT$(0)$ inequality. It is finite-dimensional when the dimensions of its cubes have a finite upper bound. In the usual interpretation, a cubical action is a homomorphism into its cubical automorphism group.

\textbf{Question 2.11.} For $n\ge4$, can $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ act on a finite-dimensional CAT$(0)$ cube complex without a global fixed point? For each fixed such $n$, the existence question is
\[
 \exists X,\ \dim X<\infty,\quad
 \exists\rho:\operatorname{Out}(F_n)\to\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cub}}(X),
 \qquad\bigcap_{a\in\operatorname{Out}(F_n)}\operatorname{Fix}(\rho(a))=\varnothing.
\]
A global fixed point is a point of the geometric realization fixed by every group element, not necessarily a vertex. The alternative negative answer would state that every such action has a global fixed point.

The question requires neither a proper nor a cocompact action, and does not impose local finiteness or a prescribed dimension bound. Thus a result excluding proper cubulations alone would not settle the stated fixed-point problem. The action category is not explicitly specified in the excerpt; if arbitrary metric isometries beyond cubical automorphisms are intended, that variant must be stated.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For each rank at least four, can the outer automorphism group of a free group have a finite-dimensional CAT$(0)$ cubical action with no point fixed by the whole group? Properness and cocompactness are not part of the question.
