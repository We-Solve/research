Let \(\mathcal X\) be a specified nonempty finite collection of such characters, and let \(\mu\colon\mathcal X\times G\to[0,1]\) be a specified probability mass function, so that
\[
\sum_{\chi\in\mathcal X}\sum_{g\in G}\mu(\chi,g)=1.
\]
Define
\[
Z(G,\mathcal X)=\{(\chi,g)\in\mathcal X\times G:\chi(g)=0\}.
