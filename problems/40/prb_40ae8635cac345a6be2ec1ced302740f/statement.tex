\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathbb K\in\{\R,\C\}\). Dimensions and linear maps are taken over \(\mathbb K\). For two normed spaces \(X,Y\) of the same finite dimension over \(\mathbb K\), define their Banach--Mazur distance by
\[
 d_{\mathrm{BM}}(X,Y)=\inf\bigl\{\|T\|\,\|T^{-1}\|:T:X\longrightarrow Y\text{ is a bijective }\mathbb K\text{-linear map}\bigr\},
\]
where the norms of maps are operator norms. Write \(\ell_2^n(\mathbb K)\) for \(\mathbb K^n\) with norm \(\|(x_i)_{i=1}^n\|_2=(\sum_{i=1}^n|x_i|^2)^{1/2}\). Every subspace is equipped with the norm inherited from its ambient space.

\textbf{Requested stability statement.} For every pair of integers \(n,k\) with \(2\leq k<n\), there exists a function

such that, for every \(\mathbb K\in\{\R,\C\}\), every \(n\)-dimensional normed space \(V\) over \(\mathbb K\), and every \(\delta>0\), the implication

 \left[\ d_{\mathrm{BM}}(E,E')<1+\delta
 \quad\text{for every pair of }k\text{-dimensional subspaces }E,E'\subset V\ \right]
 \ \Longrightarrow\ 
 d_{\mathrm{BM}}\bigl(V,\ell_2^n(\mathbb K)\bigr)<1+\varepsilon_{n,k}(\delta)

holds. The function must be independent of \(V\). A common function for the two scalar fields expresses the supplied notation \(\varepsilon(n,k,\delta)\); allowing separate functions for the two fields gives an equivalent formulation by taking their maximum.
