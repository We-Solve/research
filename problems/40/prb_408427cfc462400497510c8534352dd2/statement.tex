\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a compact manifold with an oriented surface foliation $\mathcal F$ and a holonomy-invariant transverse measure $\mu$, giving a Ruelle--Sullivan current $C_\mu$ and real homology class $[C_\mu]\in H_2(M;\R)$. Let $X$ be a Hermitian symmetric target, $G=\operatorname{Isom}(X)^0$, and $\rho:\pi_1(M)\to G$. A suitably smooth $\rho$-equivariant map $f:\widetilde M\to X$ gives the leafwise class of $f^*\omega_X$. Marc Burger asks for bounds on its pairing with the current and a study of maximal representations. Leaf orientation, transverse-measure invariance, target type and K\"ahler normalization are not explicit in the canonical sentence.

The aid fixes $X$ of noncompact Hermitian type, normalized on each irreducible factor to minimal holomorphic sectional curvature $-1$, and writes $r=\operatorname{rank}_{\R}X$. Let $\|[C_\mu]\|_1$ denote the real singular $\ell^1$ homology seminorm, not the mass norm of an arbitrary representative current. In this setting the reported bounds are
\[
 |\langle f^*\omega_X,C_\mu\rangle|
 \leq\pi r\,\|[C_\mu]\|_1,
 \qquad
 |\operatorname{Tol}_\mu(\rho)|
 \leq\frac r2\|[C_\mu]\|_1,
\]
where $\operatorname{Tol}_\mu(\rho)=(2\pi)^{-1}\langle f^*\omega_X,C_\mu\rangle$. Scaling $\mu$ scales both sides, so no measure-independent fixed bound is implied.

The aid uses the bounded K\"ahler class $\kappa_G^b$ with seminorm $\pi r$. Equality with $\|[C_\mu]\|_1>0$ forces
\[
 \|\rho_b^*\kappa_G^b\|_\infty=\pi r,
\]
i.e. tightness of the pullback, and says the current class is norming for the relevant dual pairing. If its $\ell^1$ seminorm is zero, the bound instead forces the pairing to vanish, and equality $0=0$ gives no tightness conclusion. Full maximal-representation classification is not supplied.

A separate extension obstruction takes any map $i:\Sigma\to M$ from a closed oriented surface of genus at least two. If $\sigma=\rho\circ i_*$ is maximal in the surface Toledo convention $|\operatorname{Tol}(\sigma)|=r|\chi(\Sigma)|$, the aid reports
\[
 \|i_*[\Sigma]\|_1=2|\chi(\Sigma)|,
 \qquad\|\rho_b^*\kappa_G^b\|_\infty=\pi r.
\]
Thus strict simplicial-volume compression of this fundamental class obstructs extension of a maximal surface representation through $\pi_1(M)$. No embedding or incompressibility of $i$ is silently assumed merely from its being a map.

For an exact group extension $1\to\pi_1(\Sigma)\to E\to Q\to1$ and an extending representation, conjugation supplies a homomorphism from $Q$ to
\[
 N_G(\sigma(\pi_1\Sigma))/\sigma(\pi_1\Sigma).
\]
Identification of the resulting normalizer action with the full outer monodromy requires the appropriate faithfulness/identification hypotheses on $\sigma$, not stated in detail in the summary. The aid in particular excludes infinite outer monodromy for $G=PSL(2,\R)$ in the maximal Fuchsian case, where this normalizer quotient is finite. The exact extension hypotheses and bounded-cohomology references must be verified. These are partial bounds and obstructions, not a solution for every target interpretation or every maximal foliated representation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The reported bound uses the homology seminorm of the measured foliation current and a specified Kähler normalization. Nonzero equality forces tightness. A maximal surface representation can extend only without compressing its fundamental class, with a further normalizer restriction in the stated group-extension setting.
