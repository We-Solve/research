\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A group $G$ is periodic if every element of $G$ has finite order. A subgroup $U\leq G$ is a $2$-subgroup if every element of $U$ has order $2^n$ for some integer $n\geq 0$. A group is locally finite if each of its finitely generated subgroups is finite. The condition $U\trianglelefteq G$ means that $gUg^{-1}=U$ for every $g\in G$.

\bigl[G\text{ is locally finite}\ \lor\ U\trianglelefteq G\bigr].
