\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 800926}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Interpret the source in the standard smooth Riemannian setting. Let \(M\) be a nonempty closed surface, meaning a compact smooth manifold of dimension two without boundary, let \(g\) be a smooth Riemannian metric on \(M\), and let \(\omega\) be a smooth closed differential two-form on \(M\). The form \(\omega\) need not be exact. Let \(\pi:T^*M\to M\) denote the cotangent bundle projection, and define the tautological one-form by \(\theta_{(x,p)}(v)=p(\dd\pi_{(x,p)}v)\). Equip \(T^*M\) with the twisted symplectic form
\[
\Omega=\dd\theta+\pi^*\omega.
\]
In cotangent coordinates this is \(\sum_i\dd p_i\wedge\dd x^i+\pi^*\omega\). Define the kinetic Hamiltonian, with the normalization used in the source, by
\[
H(x,p)=g_x^{-1}(p,p)=\sum_{i,j=1}^{2}g^{ij}(x)p_ip_j.
\]
Let \(X_H\) be determined by \(\Omega(X_H,\cdot)=-\dd H\). A closed magnetic geodesic at energy \(E\) means the projection \(\gamma=\pi\circ z\) of a nonconstant periodic integral curve \(z\) of \(X_H\) lying in \(H^{-1}(E)\).

The question is whether, for every such \((M,g,\omega)\) and every \(E>0\), there exist \(T>0\) and a smooth nonconstant curve \(z:\R\to T^*M\) such that
\[
\dot z(t)=X_H(z(t)),\qquad H(z(t))=E,\qquad z(t+T)=z(t)
\quad\text{for every }t\in\R.
\]
The smooth Riemannian interpretation makes explicit conventions implicit in the supplied source; it is not an additional assertion about the source's intended generality.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every closed Riemannian surface and every closed magnetic two-form, must the magnetic Hamiltonian flow have a nonconstant periodic orbit on each positive energy level? The magnetic form is allowed to be nonexact.
