\section{Vertex Operator Algebras with Haagerup Symmetry}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A vertex operator algebra packages chiral fields and their operator products. Its representation category records modules and tensor or fusion structure where defined. The Haagerup fusion category has the exceptional fusion data of the corresponding subfactor construction. Containing this symmetry data must be specified as a suitable categorical realization, rather than only a numerical coincidence of dimensions.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Construct a vertex operator algebra arising in conformal field theory whose representation category contains the Haagerup fusion category or its symmetry data, or prove that no such vertex operator algebra exists.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The report leaves the precise realization framework part of the problem. A no-go result in one restricted VOA class would not settle all permitted realizations.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct a conformal vertex algebra realizing Haagerup symmetry data, or establish an obstruction to such a realization.
