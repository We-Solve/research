\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


For a facility set $X$, each site pays its minimum cost to a facility in $X$. The ordered-median objective sorts those site costs and weights their ranks by the given $\lambda_k$.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Given $n$ sites, a nonnegative cost matrix $C=(c_{j\ell})$, and $p<n$ facilities, choose $X\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$ with $|X|=p$ to minimize the ordered weighted cost $\sum_{k=1}^n\lambda_k c_{\sigma_X(k)}(X)$, where $c_j(X)=\min_{\ell\in X}c_{j\ell}$ and $\sigma_X$ sorts these costs. \sref{30003653}{2}
