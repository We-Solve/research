\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite group \(H\), let \(\operatorname{Irr}(H)\) denote the set of irreducible ordinary complex characters of \(H\). For a prime \(\ell\), define
\[
\operatorname{Irr}_{\ell'}(H)=\{\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(H):\ell\nmid\chi(1)\}.
\]
Let \(G\) be a finite group, let \(\ell\) be a prime, and let \(P\) be a Sylow \(\ell\)-subgroup of \(G\); thus \(|P|=\ell^a\), where \(\ell^a\) is the largest power of \(\ell\) dividing \(|G|\). Define its normalizer by
\[
N_G(P)=\{g\in G:gPg^{-1}=P\}.
\]
The equality referred to as the McKay conjecture in the supplied record is
\[
\bigl|\operatorname{Irr}_{\ell'}(G)\bigr|
=
\bigl|\operatorname{Irr}_{\ell'}(N_G(P))\bigr|
\qquad\text{for every finite group }G,\text{ every prime }\ell,\text{ and every Sylow }\ell\text{-subgroup }P.
\]
This includes \(\ell\nmid |G|\), when \(P=\{1\}\) and \(N_G(P)=G\).
