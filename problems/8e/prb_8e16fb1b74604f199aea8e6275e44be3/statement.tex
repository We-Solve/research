\textbf{Question (Cooper).} For which closed $3$-manifolds $M$ does $\pi_1(M)$ act on the affine building referred to in the source as ``the affine building for $\mathrm{SL}(4,\R)$,'' with quotient retracting to $M$?

If $X$ denotes the intended building and $G=\pi_1(M)$, the requested condition is an action
\[
 \rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(X)
\]
for which $Y=\rho(G)\backslash X$ contains a copy $i(M)$ of $M$ and admits a continuous retraction
\[
 r:Y\longrightarrow M,\qquad r\circ i=\operatorname{id}_M.
\]
This uses ``retracts'' in its ordinary topological sense. A deformation retraction is not asserted by the excerpt. Nor does the excerpt require the action to be faithful, free, proper, or cocompact; those additional conditions cannot be supplied silently. The notation $\mathrm{SL}(4,\R)$ alone does not identify such data, and its usual real symmetric space is not to be silently substituted for an affine building. Likewise, replacing $\R$ by a non-Archimedean local field would change the stated problem. The precise model and automorphism/action conventions therefore remain essential unresolved definitions. The source gives $M=\operatorname{Vol}3$ as an example; this notation is retained literally because the record supplies no further identification.
