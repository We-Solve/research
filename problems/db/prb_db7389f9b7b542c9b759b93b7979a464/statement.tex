\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a prime number \(p\), a finite \(p\)-group is a group \(G\) of order \(p^n\) for some integer \(n\geq 0\); it is nontrivial when \(n\geq 1\). Let \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\) denote the group of all group automorphisms of \(G\), with composition as its operation. Write
\[
D_8=\langle r,s\mid r^4=s^2=1,\ srs^{-1}=r^{-1}\rangle
\]
for the dihedral group of order \(8\). The supplied statement gives \(D_8\cong\operatorname{Aut}(D_8)\) as its motivating example.

Does there exist a prime \(p\), an integer \(n\geq 1\), and a finite group \(G\) such that
\[
|G|=p^n,\qquad G\not\cong D_8,\qquad G\cong\operatorname{Aut}(G)?
\]
Here \(p\) ranges over all primes, since the supplied question specifies no particular prime. The statement parenthetically names A. Mann, whereas the background and proposer field name Ya. G. Berkovich; the supplied record does not explain their respective roles.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there a nontrivial finite group of prime-power order, other than the dihedral group of order \(8\), that is isomorphic to its own automorphism group?
