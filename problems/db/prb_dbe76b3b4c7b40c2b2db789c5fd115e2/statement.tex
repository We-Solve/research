\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be an infinite set and let \(F(X)\) be the free group with free basis \(X\). Write \(G_X=\operatorname{Aut}(F(X))\) for its group of automorphisms. Interpret \emph{finite width} as the following property: for every generating subset \(S\subseteq G_X\), there is an integer \(n\geq 1\) such that every element of \(G_X\) is a product of at most \(n\) elements of \(S\cup S^{-1}\), where \(S^{-1}=\{s^{-1}:s\in S\}\). Equivalently,
\[
\forall S\subseteq G_X,\qquad
\langle S\rangle=G_X\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists n\geq 1:\quad
G_X=(S\cup S^{-1}\cup\{1\})^n.
\]
The bound may depend on \(X\) and \(S\).

\textbf{Question (catalogue entry).} Does \(\operatorname{Aut}(F(X))\) have finite width in this sense for every infinite set \(X\)?
