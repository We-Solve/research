Attempt dated 2026-09-23. For a simple graph $G$ with $\chi(G)=\aleph_1$, must every cardinal $\mu$ be the chromatic number of some graph $H_\mu$ such that each finite subgraph of $H_\mu$ is isomorphic to a subgraph of $G$? Subgraphs here need not be induced.

The live problem page [1] labels the question NOT PROVABLE. The original paper of Komj\'ath and Shelah [2] gives a consistency result with a graph $G$ of chromatic number $\aleph_1$ for which all graphs with the required finite-subgraph property have chromatic number at most $\aleph_2$. Consequently this attempt does not seek a ZFC proof of the universal assertion. Its objective is a positive sufficient condition and a precise accounting of the distinction between arbitrarily large chromatic numbers and every exact chromatic number.
