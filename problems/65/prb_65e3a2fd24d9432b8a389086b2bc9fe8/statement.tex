\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\zeta(s)\) denote the Riemann zeta function, defined for \(\operatorname{Re}s>1\) by
\[
\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}
\]
and elsewhere by its meromorphic continuation to \(\C\), with its sole pole at \(s=1\). The Lindel\"of hypothesis asserts that
\[
\forall\varepsilon>0\;\exists C_{\varepsilon}>0\;\forall t\in\R,
\qquad
\left|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac12+it\right)\right|
\leq C_{\varepsilon}(1+|t|)^{\varepsilon}.
\]
Here \(C_{\varepsilon}\) may depend on \(\varepsilon\), but not on \(t\).

BMO means bounded mean oscillation. For context, the standard space \(\operatorname{BMO}(\R)\) consists of locally integrable functions \(f\) for which
\[
\sup_I\frac{1}{|I|}\int_I|f(x)-f_I|\,\dd x<\infty,
\qquad
f_I=\frac{1}{|I|}\int_I f(x)\,\dd x,
\]
where the supremum is over bounded intervals \(I\subset\R\) of positive length. This definition supplies terminology only: the source does not identify the domain, measure, or particular BMO space intended for the proposed approach.
