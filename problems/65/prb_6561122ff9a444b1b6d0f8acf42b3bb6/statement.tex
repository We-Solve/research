\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a regular scheme that is projective and flat over \(\operatorname{Spec}\Z\). Write \(|X|\) for its set of closed points and \(\kappa(x)\) for the residue field at \(x\). Its zeta function is initially defined, in a right half-plane of absolute convergence, by
\[
\zeta(X,s)=\prod_{x\in|X|}\bigl(1-\#\kappa(x)^{-s}\bigr)^{-1}.
\]
Whenever a meromorphic continuation exists near a specified point \(s_0\), its leading term is defined by
\[
\zeta^{*}(X,s_0)=\lim_{s\to s_0}(s-s_0)^{-m}\zeta(X,s),
\qquad m=\operatorname{ord}_{s=s_0}\zeta(X,s).
\]
