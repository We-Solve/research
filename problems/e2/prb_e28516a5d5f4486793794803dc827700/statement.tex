\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The supplied record calls this Debarre's minimal cohomology-class conjecture. It does not specify a ground field or cohomology theory. The following complex formulation makes these choices explicit and requires confirmation against the source.

Let \(g,d\in\Z\) satisfy \(1\leq d\leq g-2\). Let \((A,\Theta)\) be a complex principally polarized abelian variety of dimension \(g\), indecomposable in the sense that it is not isomorphic, as a polarized abelian variety, to a product of two positive-dimensional principally polarized abelian varieties. Write \(\theta=c_1(\mathcal O_A(\Theta))\). Let \(X\subset A\) be a reduced closed subvariety all of whose irreducible components have dimension \(d\). Its cohomology class \([X]\) is the Poincar\'e dual of its fundamental cycle. Assume the minimal-class equality
\[
[X]=\frac{\theta^{g-d}}{(g-d)!}\qquad\text{in }H^{2(g-d)}(A,\Q).
\]
For a smooth connected projective complex curve \(C\) of genus \(g\), let \(J(C)=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)\), with its canonical principal polarization \(\Theta_C\). After choosing \(p_0\in C\), define
\[
W_d(C)=\bigl\{\mathcal O_C(D-dp_0):D\text{ is an effective divisor of degree }d\bigr\}\subset J(C).
\]
Changing \(p_0\) changes this subvariety by translation. For a smooth cubic threefold \(Y\subset\PP^4_{\C}\), let \(F(Y)\) be the surface parametrizing lines on \(Y\), let \(J(Y)\) be its intermediate Jacobian with canonical principal polarization \(\Theta_Y\), and let \(\iota_Y:F(Y)\to J(Y)\) denote an Abel--Jacobi embedding, whose choice must be specified when interpreting the conclusion.

\emph{Question as stated in the corrected registry formulation.} Must at least one of the following hold?
\begin{enumerate}
\item There exist such a curve \(C\), a polarized isomorphism \(\varphi:(A,\Theta)\to(J(C),\Theta_C)\), and \(a\in J(C)\) such that \(\varphi(X)=a+W_d(C)\).
\item One has \(g=5\) and \(d=2\), and there exist a smooth cubic threefold \(Y\), a polarized isomorphism \(\varphi:(A,\Theta)\to(J(Y),\Theta_Y)\), and \(a\in J(Y)\) such that \(\varphi(X)=a+\iota_Y(F(Y))\).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Short English statement}
The question asks whether every reduced, pure-dimensional subvariety of minimal cohomology class in an indecomposable principally polarized abelian variety must arise from effective divisors on a curve, or from the surface of lines on a smooth cubic threefold.
