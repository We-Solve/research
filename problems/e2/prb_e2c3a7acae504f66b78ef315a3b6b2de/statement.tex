\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a topological space. A countable Borel cover of \(X\) is a countable family \(\mathcal U\) of Borel subsets of \(X\) whose union is \(X\), with \(X\notin\mathcal U\). Such a family is an \(\omega\)-cover if every finite subset of \(X\) is contained in some member of \(\mathcal U\). It is a \(\gamma\)-cover if it is infinite and, for every \(x\in X\), the set \(\{U\in\mathcal U:x\notin U\}\) is finite. Write \(\mathcal B_\Omega(X)\) and \(\mathcal B_\Gamma(X)\) for the families of countable Borel \(\omega\)-covers and countable Borel \(\gamma\)-covers, respectively.

For families \(\mathcal A(X)\) and \(\mathcal D(X)\) of covers, the property \(\mathsf S_1(\mathcal A,\mathcal D)\) means that for every sequence \((\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq1}\) with \(\mathcal U_n\in\mathcal A(X)\), there are choices \(U_n\in\mathcal U_n\) such that \(\{U_n:n\geq1\}\in\mathcal D(X)\). The property \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal D)\) means that for every such sequence there are finite subfamilies \(\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n\), possibly empty, such that \(\bigcup_{n\geq1}\mathcal F_n\in\mathcal D(X)\). These selected collections are families of sets; in particular, a selected \(\gamma\)-cover must have infinitely many distinct members.

Consider the three properties
\[
P_1=\mathsf S_1(\mathcal B_\Omega,\mathcal B_\Gamma),\qquad
P_2=\mathsf S_1(\mathcal B_\Omega,\mathcal B_\Omega),\qquad
P_3=\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal B_\Omega,\mathcal B_\Omega).
\]
For a specified ambient class \(\mathscr C\) of spaces, the finite-power closure question for each \(i\in\{1,2,3\}\) is whether
\[
\forall X\in\mathscr C\;\forall m\in\Z_{\geq1},\qquad
P_i(X)\Longrightarrow P_i(X^m),
\]
where \(X^m\) has the product topology and its cover families are defined using Borel subsets of that product.
