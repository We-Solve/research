\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S\) be a connected normal Stein complex space of finite complex dimension \(n\), and let \(K=\mathcal M(S)\) be its field of global meromorphic functions. Here normality means that every local analytic ring is an integrally closed domain. Interpret cohomological dimension in the standard Galois-cohomological sense: choose a separable closure \(K^{\mathrm{sep}}\), put \(G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K)\), and define
\[
\operatorname{cd}(K)=\inf\bigl\{d\in\Z_{\geq 0}: H^q_{\mathrm{cont}}(G_K,A)=0\text{ for every integer }q>d\text{ and every discrete torsion }G_K\text{-module }A\bigr\},
\]
with value \(\infty\) if no such \(d\) exists. The action on each module is required to be continuous.

The question is whether, for every such \(S\),
\[
\operatorname{cd}\bigl(\mathcal M(S)\bigr)=\dim_{\C}S=n.
\]
The supplied dated assessment identifies \(n\geq 3\) as the remaining general range, and states that the question remains open already for \(S=\C^n\) in that range. It also states that the equality has been proved for connected normal Stein spaces of dimension two. These are supplied literature-status claims, not independently verified conclusions of this draft. The brief original excerpt does not define \(\operatorname{cd}\); the Galois-cohomological convention made explicit above requires confirmation against the report.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every connected normal Stein complex space have a meromorphic function field whose Galois cohomological dimension equals its complex dimension? The supplied assessment reports the dimension-two case as proved and the general case in dimensions at least three as open, including complex affine space.
