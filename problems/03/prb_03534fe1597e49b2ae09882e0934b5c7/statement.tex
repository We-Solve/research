\section{Extremal parameters for D(n)-quintuples}\label{8500005}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 8500005 · Graph Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For $n\in\mathbb Z$, a $D(n)$-$m$-tuple is a set $A=\{a_1,\ldots,a_m\}\subset\mathbb N$ of distinct positive integers such that $a_i a_j+n$ is the square of an integer for every $i<j$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine both \[ n_+=\min\{n\in\mathbb N:\text{a }D(n)\text{-quintuple exists}\},\qquad n_-=\max\{n\in\mathbb Z_{<0}:\text{a }D(n)\text{-quintuple exists}\}.\] \sref{8500005}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find the least positive and the negative parameter closest to zero for which a positive integer quintuple exists.
