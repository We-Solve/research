 \operatorname{Fav}(K)=\frac1\pi\int_0^\pi\mathcal L^1(\pi_\theta K)\,\dd\theta.

The intended problem is to estimate \(\operatorname{Fav}(E_\delta^{(2)})\) as \(\delta\downarrow0\).
