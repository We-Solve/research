\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Omega\subseteq\C^d\) be a domain, including the unit disk when \(d=1\), and let \((\varphi_j)_{j\geq1}\), \((\vartheta_j)_{j\geq1}\) be holomorphic self-map sequences of \(\Omega\). Use \(d\) for ambient dimension to distinguish it from the sequence index. Put
\[
 H^\infty(\Omega)=\{g\in H(\Omega):\|g\|_\infty<\infty\},\qquad
 \psi_j=\varphi_j\circ\vartheta_j.
\]
The source assumes that each original sequence admits a universal function ``relative to \(H^\infty\)'' and asks for natural, interesting sufficient conditions or a composition principle ensuring the same for \((\psi_j)\).

The universality data must include an admissible generating class \(\mathcal X\), a target class \(\mathcal Y\), and a topology. For example, under compact-open approximation the assertion for \((\psi_j)\) would mean
\[
 \exists f\in\mathcal X\ \forall g\in\mathcal Y\ \forall K\subseteq\Omega
 \text{ compact}\ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists j\geq1:\quad
 \sup_{z\in K}|f(\psi_j(z))-g(z)|<\varepsilon.
\]
This display is a precise schema, not a choice of the missing source data. The two original sequences may have different universal generators; neither a common generator nor a relation between them is supplied. The composition is synchronized at the same index \(j\), not the full family of independent pairs \(\varphi_j\circ\vartheta_k\). For \(C_\varphi f=f\circ\varphi\), the order is \(C_{\varphi_j\circ\vartheta_j}=C_{\vartheta_j}C_{\varphi_j}\).

A material boundedness issue remains. If \(f\in H^\infty(\Omega)\), then \(\|f\circ\psi_j\|_\infty\leq\|f\|_\infty\) for every \(j\). Its orbit therefore cannot be dense in all of \(H^\infty(\Omega)\), even for compact-open approximation, since constants of larger modulus cannot be approximated at one point. The intended formulation might use a bounded target ball, a different generator space, or another convention. The record does not specify which. Uniform norm density is also distinct from compact-open density. These choices cannot be inferred merely from the phrase relative to \(H^\infty\).

The question seeks conditions producing meaningful positive cases, not an explicit assertion that separate universality always passes to synchronized composition. \subsection{Short English statement}
When do two universal holomorphic self-map sequences remain universal after same-index composition? The target space, generator class, and topology must first be fixed; a bounded generator cannot approximate all bounded holomorphic functions of arbitrary norm. The record asks for useful composition conditions rather than an unconditional closure claim.
