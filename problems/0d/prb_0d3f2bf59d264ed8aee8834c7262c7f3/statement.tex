\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(p\) be a prime, and let \(\Z_p=\varprojlim_{n\geq 1}\Z/p^n\Z\) be the additive profinite group of \(p\)-adic integers. A pro-\(p\) group is an inverse limit of finite \(p\)-groups. Let \(F\) be a free pro-\(p\) group of finite rank, meaning that it has a finite free generating set in the category of pro-\(p\) groups. Write \(\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cont}}(F)\) for its group of continuous automorphisms, equipped with the natural profinite topology induced by the actions on finite characteristic quotients of \(F\).

Suppose that \(\Delta\leq\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cont}}(F)\), with its inherited topology, is isomorphic as a profinite group to \(\Z_p\). Define the closed fixed subgroup by
\[
F^\Delta=\{x\in F:\delta(x)=x\text{ for every }\delta\in\Delta\}.
\]
Is it true, for every such \(p,F,\Delta\), that \(F^\Delta\) is topologically finitely generated? Explicitly, must there exist a finite subset \(X\subseteq F^\Delta\) such that
\[
F^\Delta=\overline{\langle X\rangle},
\]
where the closure is taken in \(F\)?
