\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S\) be a closed surface, meaning a compact connected surface without boundary, and let \(g\) be a Riemannian metric on \(S\). Under the usual smooth-metric interpretation, define its unit tangent bundle by
\[
T^1_gS=\{(x,v)\in TS:g_x(v,v)=1\}.
\]
The geodesic flow \(\varphi_g^t:T^1_gS\to T^1_gS\) sends an initial unit tangent vector to the velocity at time \(t\) of its geodesic. Let \(\mu_g\) be the invariant Liouville measure normalized to have total mass one. More explicitly, if \(\pi:T^1_gS\to S\) is the bundle projection and \(\alpha_g\) is the canonical contact form
\[
(\alpha_g)_{(x,v)}(\xi)=g_x\bigl(v,\dd\pi_{(x,v)}\xi\bigr),
\]
then \(\mu_g\) is the probability measure obtained by normalizing the volume density \(\lvert\alpha_g\wedge\dd\alpha_g\rvert\). Define the Liouville entropy of the flow to be its measure-theoretic entropy per unit time,
\[
h_{\mathrm L}(g):=h_{\mu_g}(\varphi_g^1).
\]
The question attributed to Paternain is whether
\[
\exists\,(S,g)\quad \text{such that}\quad S\text{ is closed},\qquad \operatorname{genus}(S)\ge 2,\qquad h_{\mathrm L}(g)=0.
