\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(L\cong T^n\) be a smooth closed embedded Lagrangian torus in \((\C^n,\omega_0)\), \(n\geq1\). Its Maslov homomorphism is \(\mu_L:\pi_2(\C^n,L)\to\Z\); its minimal Maslov number is the positive generator of the image when nonzero (and may be called \(\infty\) for zero image). Audin's conjecture, discussed by Biran in the source, is
\[
N_L=2.
\]
Orientability implies the Maslov image is even, so a relative class of index two suffices. The original historical comment proves the monotone case using Oh's spectral sequence and says the general case was then unproved. The supplied aid reports the full conjecture solved by Cieliebak--Mohnke, without a detailed citation; this later status is not an independent verification.

The aid's structural proposition uses the contractibility of \(\C^n\) to identify \(\pi_2(\C^n,L)\cong\pi_1(L)\cong\Z^n\). Choose \(\beta_0\) with \(\mu_L(\beta_0)=2\), and let \(K=\ker\mu_L\cong\Z^{n-1}\). Then
\[
\mu_L^{-1}(2)=\beta_0+K.
\]
With \(\lambda_0=\tfrac12\sum_j(x_j\dd y_j-y_j\dd x_j)\), define the topological area homomorphism \(\mathcal A(\beta)=\int_\beta\omega_0=\int_{\partial\beta}\lambda_0\). Its entire index-two topological spectrum is
\[
\mathcal A(\mu_L^{-1}(2))=\mathcal A(\beta_0)+\mathcal A(K).
\]
The finitely generated additive subgroup \(\mathcal A(K)\subset\R\) is either zero, a discrete cyclic group \(a\Z\), \(a>0\), or dense in \(\R\). Thus the spectrum is respectively a singleton, a discrete arithmetic coset, or dense. These are areas of relative homotopy classes and can include values not represented by holomorphic disks.

The singleton condition is exactly \(\mathcal A|_K=0\), hence algebraically \(\mathcal A=\kappa\mu_L\) with \(\kappa=\mathcal A(\beta_0)/2\). Positive monotonicity additionally means \(\kappa>0\); the aid reports equivalence with monotonicity for embedded tori in this setting. The positive sign uses geometric positive-area input and does not follow from the affine-lattice calculation alone.

For a product torus \(L=\{|z_j|=r_j>0\}\), let \(a_j=\pi r_j^2\). Coordinate-disk classes have Maslov index two and area \(a_j\), and the topological spectrum is
\[
\left\{\sum_jm_ja_j:m_j\in\Z,\ \sum_jm_j=1\right\}
=a_n+\sum_{j<n}\Z(a_j-a_n).
\]
The aid reports that the standard-complex-structure holomorphic index-two area spectrum is only the finite set \(\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}\), while the topological set can be dense when its differences generate a dense subgroup. These two spectra must not be identified, and the topological trichotomy supplies no general holomorphic-disk count.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The source states Audin's minimal-Maslov-two conjecture for embedded Lagrangian tori in complex affine space. The supplied aid reports it solved and describes the affine lattice of index-two classes and its area-spectrum trichotomy. Topological areas can be dense even when standard holomorphic index-two disk areas form a finite set.
