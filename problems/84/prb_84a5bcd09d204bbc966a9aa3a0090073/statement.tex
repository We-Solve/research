\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite triangulation \(T\) of a surface, define its dual graph \(G_T\) to have one vertex for each triangle of \(T\), with two distinct vertices adjacent when their triangles share an edge. If \(G_T\) is connected, its dual diameter is
\[
\operatorname{diam}(G_T)=\max_{\sigma,\tau\in V(G_T)}d_{G_T}(\sigma,\tau),
\]
where \(d_{G_T}\) is shortest-path distance.

Let \(\mathcal S_n\) denote the intended class of triangulated surfaces with boundary having exactly \(n\) vertices. The supplied record describes \(\overline f(2,n)\) as the maximal dual diameter in this class. Thus, whenever the class and its maximum are well defined,
\[
\overline f(2,n)=\max_{T\in\mathcal S_n}\operatorname{diam}(G_T).
\]
The proposed question is whether, for every admissible integer \(n\),
\[
\overline f(2,n)\leq n-3.
\]
Equivalently, once the intended class is specified, the proposed bound is \(\operatorname{diam}(G_T)\leq |V(T)|-3\) for every \(T\) in that class.
