For every $k$-colouring of $[1,N]$, with $N$ sufficiently large depending on $k$, prove that at least $cN$ integers at most $N$ are sums of two distinct elements of the same colour, for an absolute $c>0$. We prove the stronger assertion that all but
\[O_k\bigl(N^{1-2^{-(k+1)}}\bigr)\]
of the even integers at most $N$ have such a representation. The requirement that the two summands differ is essential.
