For positive integers $m,n$, write $\mathrm{PHP}_n^m(f)$ for
\[
 \bigl(\forall i\le m\ (i\ge1\Rightarrow1\le f(i)\le n)\bigr)

 \exists i,j\ (1\le i<j\le m\ \text{and }f(i)=f(j)).
\]
