\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#384; literal counterexamples and the published non-strict theorem, 2026-09-24.}
The current page asks whether, for $2\leq k\leq n-2$, the coefficient $\binom nk$ has a prime divisor strictly less than $n/2$, except for $\binom73=35$. The strict inequality requires correction: Ecklund's published theorem gives a divisor \emph{at most} $n/2$ (after using symmetry), with the exceptional positions $k=3,4$ when $n=7$.
