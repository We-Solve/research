Write \(A_+=\bigoplus_{j>0}A_j\). In this framework, \(A\) is Artinian if it is finite dimensional over \(k\), and it is quadratic if the kernel of the natural surjection \(\operatorname{Sym}_k(A_1)\twoheadrightarrow A\) is generated by homogeneous elements of degree two. It is Gorenstein if its socle
\[
\operatorname{Soc}(A)=\{a\in A:A_+a=0\}
\]
is one dimensional over \(k\). If \(d=\max\{j:A_j\ne0\}\), this socle is \(A_d\), and a choice of isomorphism \(\deg:A_d\to k\) gives perfect multiplication pairings \(A_i\times A_{d-i}\to k\), \((a,b)\mapsto\deg(ab)\).

The algebra \(A\) is Koszul if the graded \(A\)-module \(k=A/A_+\) has a linear minimal graded free resolution, equivalently if
\[
\operatorname{Tor}^{A}_{p}(k,k)_q=0
\qquad\text{for all }p,q\geq0\text{ with }q\ne p.
\]
Here \(p\) is homological degree and \(q\) is internal degree.
