\section{SU(3) Braided Fusion Spin Systems and Haah Nets}\label{30006354}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30006354 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


The category $\mathrm{SU}(3)_1$ is the level-one category appearing in the source construction; reversing braiding reverses exchange data. A bounded-spread isomorphism maps an algebra localized in a region into an algebra in a uniformly enlarged region, with a comparable locality bound for its inverse. Clock and shift matrices satisfy the source's finite-dimensional Weyl relation.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Prove that the braided fusion spin system associated with $\mathrm{SU}(3)_1$, or with its reverse braiding, is bounded-spread isomorphic to Haah's proposed two-dimensional net of algebras with the specified local clock-and-shift generators.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The specified source generators, local relations and braiding choice must be matched; an abstract isomorphism without a uniform locality bound is insufficient.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Prove the stated local-net identification between the level-one fusion spin system and Haah's two-dimensional clock-and-shift net.
