Let \(\N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\). The supplied record asks about every group \(G\), without stating countability, torsion, or finiteness hypotheses. The following makes a conventional interpretation explicit; the source record does not itself specify all the conventions below.

Regard \(G\) as a discrete group. Define its action dimension by
\[
\operatorname{actdim}(G)=\inf\bigl\{n\in\N_0:\ G\text{ acts properly by homeomorphisms on a nonempty contractible }n\text{-manifold}\bigr\},
\]
with \(\inf\varnothing=+\infty\). Here manifolds are taken to be Hausdorff and second countable, without boundary, and properness means that for every compact subset \(K\) of the manifold, the set \(\{g\in G:gK\cap K\ne\varnothing\}\) is finite. This definition allows finite stabilizers.

For the interpretation of \(L^2\)-dimension through \(L^2\)-Betti numbers, let \(\mathcal N(G)\) be the group von Neumann algebra and set
\[
b_n^{(2)}(G)=\dim_{\mathcal N(G)}H_n(G;\mathcal N(G)),\qquad
\dim_{(2)}(G)=\sup\bigl(\{0\}\cup\{n\in\N_0:b_n^{(2)}(G)>0\}\bigr).
\]
Thus this convention gives dimension zero when all \(L^2\)-Betti numbers vanish, and allows dimension \(+\infty\).

\textbf{Action Dimension Conjecture, under these conventions.} Is it true that every discrete group \(G\) satisfies
\[
\operatorname{actdim}(G)\geq 2\dim_{(2)}(G),
\]
where the inequality is interpreted in the extended nonnegative real numbers?
