\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a positive integer \(n\), let \(A_n\) be the group of even permutations of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\), with multiplication given by composition. A subset \(A\subseteq A_n\) is \emph{product-free} if
\[
AA\cap A=\varnothing,\qquad AA=\{xy:x,y\in A\}.
\]
Thus there are no \(x,y,z\in A\) with \(xy=z\); the elements in this condition need not be distinct. Define
\[
m(n)=\max\{|B|:B\subseteq A_n,\ BB\cap B=\varnothing\},
\]
and the family of extremal sets by
\[
\mathcal E_n=\{A\subseteq A_n:AA\cap A=\varnothing,\ |A|=m(n)\}.
\]
