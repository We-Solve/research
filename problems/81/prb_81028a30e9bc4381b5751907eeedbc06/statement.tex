For a graded real vector space \(V\), let \(\wedge V\) denote the free unital graded-commutative algebra generated by \(V\). Thus homogeneous elements satisfy \(ab=(-1)^{|a||b|}ba\); the notation does not mean an ordinary exterior algebra on generators of every degree. A differential on this algebra is a linear map \(d\) of degree \(+1\) such that \(d^2=0\) and
\[
 d(ab)=d(a)b+(-1)^{|a|}a\,d(b).
