\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix a scalar field \(\mathbb K\in\{\R,\C\}\). The supplied record does not specify which scalar field is intended. Let \((X,\|\cdot\|)\) be an infinite-dimensional Banach space over \(\mathbb K\), and let \(\mathcal S_\infty(X)\) denote its infinite-dimensional closed linear subspaces. A norm \(p\) on \(X\) is equivalent to \(\|\cdot\|\), written \(p\sim\|\cdot\|\), if there exist constants \(0<m\le M<\infty\) such that
\[
 m\|x\|\le p(x)\le M\|x\|\qquad(x\in X).
\]
For such a norm and a subspace \(Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X)\), define
\[
 \Delta_Y(p)=\sup\left\{\frac{p(y)}{p(z)}:y,z\in Y,\ \|y\|=\|z\|=1\right\}.
\]
Use the following distortion terminology: \(X\) is distortable if
\[
 \exists a>1\ \exists p\sim\|\cdot\|\ \forall Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X),\qquad \Delta_Y(p)>a,
\]
and arbitrarily distortable if
\[
 \forall a>1\ \exists p\sim\|\cdot\|\ \forall Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X),\qquad \Delta_Y(p)>a.
\]
Interpret \emph{boundedly distortable} as distortable but not arbitrarily distortable. Thus the proposed statement of the question is: does there exist an infinite-dimensional Banach space \((X,\|\cdot\|)\) satisfying both
\[
 \exists a>1\ \exists p\sim\|\cdot\|\ \forall Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X),\qquad \Delta_Y(p)>a
\]
and
\[
 \exists C>1\ \forall q\sim\|\cdot\|\ \exists Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X),\qquad \Delta_Y(q)\le C?
\]
The bound \(C\) is independent of the equivalent norm \(q\); the subspace \(Y\) may depend on \(q\).
