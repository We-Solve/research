Can a three-uniform hypergraph on \(n\) vertices have maximal complete vertex sets of \(n-O(1)\) distinct sizes? Here a clique means an inclusion-maximal complete vertex set.
No. We reconstruct the three-uniform case of Gao's proof: for every fixed integer \(C\geq0\), there is a finite bound \(N(C)\) such that a hypergraph with at least \(n-C\) distinct maximal-clique sizes has \(n\leq N(C)\). Attempt status: solved.
