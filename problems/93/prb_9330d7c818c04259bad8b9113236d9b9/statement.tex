\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a compact polyhedron, regarded as a finite CW complex. A simple homotopy equivalence is a homotopy equivalence represented up to homotopy by elementary expansions and collapses. Let $\operatorname{hAut}_s(X)$ denote the group of homotopy classes of simple self-homotopy equivalences.

A natural homotopy-Nielsen-realization formulation of the source is: given a discrete group $G$ and a specified subgroup embedding $\alpha:G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{hAut}_s(X)$, does there exist a compact space $X'$, a homotopy equivalence $h:X'\to X$ with homotopy inverse $k$, and a homomorphism $\rho:G\to\operatorname{Homeo}(X')$ such that
\[
[h\circ\rho(g)\circ k]=\alpha(g)\qquad(g\in G)?
\]
The compatibility equality means the homeomorphism action realizes the specified homotopy action, not just an unrelated action of the abstract group. With injective $\alpha$, compatibility forces faithfulness of $\rho$. The source asks only that $X'$ be homotopy equivalent, not simply homotopy equivalent, to $X$.

The source says ``a group of simple homotopy equivalences $X\to X$'' without specifying whether these are actual maps, homotopy classes, or a homotopy-coherent action. If they are actual maps forming a group with literal compositional inverses, they are already homeomorphisms. The nontrivial classes/action interpretation in the display therefore requires checking. The source also does not explicitly state the compatibility marking or whether $X'$ must be Hausdorff, metrizable, finite dimensional, or a polyhedron; none of those stronger model restrictions is silently imposed.
