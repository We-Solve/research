\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $L:TM\to\R$ be a convex superlinear Lagrangian on a compact manifold, and let $\widetilde L:T\widetilde M\to\R$ be its lift to the universal cover. The source asks whether its universal Ma\~n\'e critical value equals
\[
 \inf\{h:\exists x\in\widetilde M\ \forall y\in M\
   \exists\text{ energy-}h\text{ trajectory joining }x\text{ to }y\}.
\]
As the supplied verification emphasizes, $y\in M$ is printed in both source versions: this is a genuine mixed-space ambiguity, not an OCR correction. One must either quantify $y\in\widetilde M$, choose a lift of each $y$, or ask for a projected endpoint in $M$. These are different formulations. The source's apparent item number 5 is verified to be 15.

The aid treats the off-diagonal universal-cover interpretation for a Tonelli Lagrangian: assume $M$ is connected, closed and of positive dimension, $L$ is $C^2$, its velocity Hessian is positive definite, and it is superlinear in velocity. Its energy is
\[
 E(q,v)=\partial_vL(q,v)\cdot v-L(q,v),\qquad
 e_0=\max_{q\in M}E(q,0).
\]
Use the loop-action convention
\[
 c_u=\inf\left\{a:\int_0^T(\widetilde L(\gamma,\dot\gamma)+a)\,\dd t\ge0
 \text{ for every closed absolutely continuous }\gamma\text{ and }T>0\right\}.
\]
Define
\[
\begin{split}
 R_{\exists}=\{h:\ &\exists x\in\widetilde M\ \forall y\in\widetilde M\setminus\{x\}\
 \exists T>0\ \exists\gamma:[0,T]\to\widetilde M:\\
 &\gamma\text{ solves Euler--Lagrange},\quad
 \gamma(0)=x,\ \gamma(T)=y,\ E(\gamma,\dot\gamma)\equiv h\}.
\end{split}
\]
Let $R_{\forall}$ replace the first existential quantifier by $\forall x\in\widetilde M$. Positive time and exclusion of $y=x$ are explicit conventions.

Under the additional gauge-closed hypothesis that the momentum one-form $\eta_q=\partial_vL(q,0)$ is closed, the aid reports
\[
 c_u=e_0,\qquad R_{\forall}=R_{\exists}=(c_u,\infty).
\]
On the simply connected cover the gauge form is exact. Subtracting it leaves the zero velocity as the fiberwise minimum, which identifies the critical value. Above $e_0$, the shifted energy hypersurfaces define a complete Finsler metric on the cover; its directed geodesics reparameterize to energy-$h$ trajectories between distinct points. At a maximizer of $E(q,0)$, no energy below $e_0$ is available. At energy $e_0$ the sole velocity there is zero and gives an equilibrium, so uniqueness prevents a nonconstant finite-time connection to or from that phase point. This excludes the critical endpoint without assuming the equilibrium is topologically isolated among all equilibria.

The diagonal convention is material. For $M=\mathbb T$, $L(v)=\tfrac12v^2$ and $\widetilde M=\R$, one has $c_u=0$. An energy-$h>0$ trajectory on the cover is a nonconstant straight line and cannot return to its starting point at positive time; energy zero cannot join distinct points. Thus the positive-time formulation requiring every $y\in\widetilde M$, including $y=x$, has empty admissible energy set. This example concerns the cover-endpoint reading, not the distinct projected-endpoint reading on the circle. The reported special case does not settle the source question for general Tonelli Lagrangians or resolve its mixed-space ambiguity.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Ask whether the universal critical value is the threshold for exact-energy connections from one point to all targets. The source mixes base and cover endpoints. Under a stated off-diagonal cover convention and a closed zero-velocity momentum form, the aid gives the exact open spectrum $(c_u,\infty)$; including positive-time self-connections changes the claim.
