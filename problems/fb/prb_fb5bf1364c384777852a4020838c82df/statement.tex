\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A closed compact orientable smooth four-manifold is a compact orientable smooth manifold of dimension four with empty boundary. The supplied statement does not specify connectedness. Say that an underlying topological four-manifold admits multiple smooth structures if it supports two smooth structures whose resulting smooth manifolds are homeomorphic but not diffeomorphic. Here diffeomorphisms are not required to preserve an orientation; the source does not specify an orientation-sensitive equivalence relation.

For an orientation of such a manifold \(M\), its real intersection form is
\[
Q_M\colon H^2(M;\R)\times H^2(M;\R)\longrightarrow\R,
\qquad Q_M(a,b)=\langle a\smile b,[M]\rangle.
\]
Call \(M\) definite if \(Q_M\) is positive definite or negative definite. Reversing all component orientations changes the sign of this form. The source does not specify whether manifolds with \(H^2(M;\R)=0\) are included; under the usual universal-inequality definition, definiteness on the zero vector space holds vacuously.

The supplied problem asks for the following.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Determine the simplest closed compact orientable smooth four-manifold admitting multiple smooth structures. No complexity invariant, ordering, or equivalence criterion for ``simplest'' is supplied, so this part does not yet specify a precise minimization problem.
\item Determine whether there exist closed compact orientable smooth four-manifolds \(M_0,M_1\) such that
\[
M_0\cong_{\mathrm{homeo}}M_1,
\qquad M_0\not\cong_{\mathrm{diff}}M_1,
\qquad Q_{M_0}\text{ is definite}.
\]
Orientations may be transported by the homeomorphism when comparing the forms. No restriction on the fundamental group is imposed in the supplied question.
\end{enumerate}
