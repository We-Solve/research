\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $n\geq0$ and $p\geq1$ be integers, and let $Z$ be a compact connected complex manifold of complex dimension $n+p$. Suppose that $Z$ contains a complex submanifold $Y$ of dimension $p-1$ with positive normal bundle
\[
 N_{Y/Z}=(T_Z|_Y)/T_Y,
\]
and that $Z$ has a covering compact family of $n$-dimensional analytic cycles. Here an effective $n$-cycle is a finite sum $\sum_i m_i[V_i]$ of compact irreducible analytic subsets of pure dimension $n$, with positive integer multiplicities. A compact family can be expressed by a compact complex parameter space $S$ and an analytic family of such cycles $C_s$ with
\[
 \bigcup_{s\in S}|C_s|=Z.
\]
This describes the covering condition; the source does not fix the precise cycle-space or family hypotheses.

Write $\mathcal M(Z)$ for the field of meromorphic functions on $Z$ and
\[
 a(Z)=\operatorname{trdeg}_{\C}\mathcal M(Z)
\]
for its algebraic dimension. The source asks whether these hypotheses imply
\[
 a(Z)\geq p.
\]
No projectivity or K\"ahler assumption on $Z$ is supplied.

The word ``positive'' is not defined in the record. Possible relevant conventions include ampleness of the tautological line bundle $\mathcal O_{\PP(N_{Y/Z})}(1)$, with the quotient-projectivization convention, or a specified positive Hermitian curvature condition. These must not be silently identified. The source also does not explicitly state whether $Y$ is closed (and hence compact), nor the exact irreducibility, incidence, or compactness conditions required of the cycle family.

The supplied assessment checked 21 August 2026 says that Barlet and Magnusson prove a positive answer under substantial compactness, positivity and cycle-family hypotheses. It warns that the extracted question is not a theorem for arbitrary compact complex manifolds. The full hypotheses of that result are not supplied, so the inequality above is retained as the qualified source question rather than asserted in a broader setting.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does a positive normal bundle on a $(p-1)$-dimensional submanifold, together with a compact family of $n$-cycles covering an $(n+p)$-dimensional compact complex manifold, force at least $p$ algebraically independent meromorphic functions?
