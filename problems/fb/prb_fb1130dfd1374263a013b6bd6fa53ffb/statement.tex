 \operatorname{vein}(K)=\inf\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{N}\|p_i\|_K:

\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
The conjecture asks whether the cheapest finite collection of points whose convex hull contains the Euclidean unit ball has total distance from the origin $2n^{3/2}$. Regular enclosing crosspolytopes attain that value. The supplied partial result proves optimality only among symmetric configurations with exactly $2n$ vertices; the unrestricted infimum allows all finite configurations.
