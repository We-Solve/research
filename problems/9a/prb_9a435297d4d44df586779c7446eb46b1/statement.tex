\textbf{Line-bundle tilting question.} Does every smooth compact toric variety admit a tilting bundle that is a finite direct sum of line bundles, equivalently a full strongly exceptional sequence of line bundles? In the usual algebraic interpretation, compact means complete, hence proper over the base field; completeness must not silently be replaced by projectivity or the Fano condition. A toric variety contains an algebraic torus as a dense open subset with the torus action extending to the variety. The source does not specify the base field; a conventional formulation fixes an algebraically closed field \(k\) and a smooth complete toric \(k\)-variety \(X\).\par A tilting bundle \(T\) has \(\operatorname{Ext}_X^q(T,T)=0\) for every \(q>0\) and generates \(D^b(\operatorname{Coh}X)\): the smallest triangulated subcategory containing \(T\) and closed under direct summands is the entire bounded derived category. The requested extra condition is \(T=\bigoplus_{i=1}^rL_i\) for line bundles \(L_i\), not just arbitrary higher-rank vector bundles.\par An ordered sequence \((L_1,\ldots,L_r)\) is strongly exceptional if each \(L_i\) has scalar endomorphisms and no positive self-extensions, \(\operatorname{Ext}_X^q(L_i,L_j)=0\) for all \(i,j\) and \(q>0\), and \(\operatorname{Hom}_X(L_j,L_i)=0\) whenever \(j>i\). It is full if the line bundles generate the same derived category. The source presents the two formulations as equivalent in its toric setting; ordering and duplicate removal in the direct-sum formulation use the standard proper toric conventions. An exceptional sequence without strongness or without fullness is not substituted. \subsection{Short English statement}
Ask for a derived-category generator with no higher self-extensions that splits entirely into line bundles, on every smooth complete toric variety.
