\section{Equality constraints do not characterize hidden-variable models}\label{20001228}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 20001228 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A graphical model specifies a family of joint distributions through a graph and its associated conditional-independence constraints. Hidden variables are summed or integrated out to obtain the joint distribution on observed variables. The observed model is the image of the full parameterization after eliminating the hidden coordinates. Its constraints can include polynomial equalities and inequalities enforcing nonnegativity. An algorithm describing that image must account for the chosen input and output representations.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Question 51 (Meek). Is there a polynomial time algorithm for describing the constraints on the joint probability distribution on the observed variables in a graphical model with hidden variables? \sref{20001228}{2}
