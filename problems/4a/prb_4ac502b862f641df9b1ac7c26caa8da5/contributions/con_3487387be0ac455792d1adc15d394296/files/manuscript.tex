\subsection{One elastic contrast does not identify a nonlinear response}
The primary source describes hierarchies of laminates trained on linear elastic responses. We test a precise necessary ingredient for transfer: whether elastic data at one constituent contrast identify even a scalar series/parallel architecture. This model is a restricted constitutive experiment, not a theorem about the full material-network class.
Take two scalar constituents with stresses $\sigma_1(u)=u+u^3$ and $\sigma_2(u)=4u$. Their tangent moduli at zero are 1 and 4, and their stored energies $u^2/2+u^4/4$ and $2u^2$ are strictly convex. Compare a parallel mixture with constituent-1 fraction $1/2$ to a series mixture with constituent-1 fraction $1/5$.
\begin{proposition}
Their effective linearized elastic moduli are both $5/2$, but at macroscopic strain $3/5$ their nonlinear stresses are respectively $201/125$ and $2$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
In parallel, both phases have the macroscopic strain $u$, so their volume-averaged stress is
\[
\sigma_P(u)=\tfrac12(u+u^3)+\tfrac12(4u)=\tfrac52u+\tfrac12u^3.
\]
Its tangent modulus is $5/2$, and substitution $u=3/5$ gives $201/125$.
In series, the stress $s$ is common to both phases and the macroscopic strain is the volume-weighted sum. In the linearized experiment, $u_1=s$, $u_2=s/4$, so $u=s/5+(4/5)(s/4)=2s/5$, again giving modulus $5/2$. For the nonlinear experiment, let $s=2$. The unique phase-1 strain is $u_1=1$ since $1+1^3=2$ and the stress function is strictly increasing. Phase2 has strain $u_2=1/2$. Their weighted strain is $1/5+(4/5)(1/2)=3/5$, proving the claimed series stress. Strict monotonicity also makes this effective stress at the given strain unique.
\end{proof}
Thus any predictor receiving only this linearized elastic response must return the same nonlinear-stress prediction for the two models. At strain $3/5$, at least one absolute error is at least half their difference, namely $49/250$, by the triangle inequality. Arbitrarily many load amplitudes in the same linearized elastic model do not distinguish them. Varying the constituent elastic moduli, or supplying known geometry or volume fractions, can distinguish these examples; the impossibility concerns the restricted data stated here.
