\section{Section Conjecture for Hyperbolic Curves}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $X/k$ be a smooth geometrically connected hyperbolic curve over a number field or a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$. Does the map sending a rational point to its decomposition-group section biject $X(k)$ with conjugacy classes of continuous sections of $\pi_1(X/k)\to\operatorname{Gal}(\overline k/k)$?
