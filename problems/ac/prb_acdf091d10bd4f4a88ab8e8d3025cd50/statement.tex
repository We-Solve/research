\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\operatorname{vol}_3\) denote three-dimensional Lebesgue measure and \(\operatorname{conv}\) denote convex hull. For \(\varepsilon>0\), define
\[
H=\{x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)\in\R^3:\|x\|_2\leq1,\ x_1\geq0\},\qquad
L_\varepsilon=\operatorname{conv}\bigl(H\cup\{(-\varepsilon,0,0)\}\bigr).
\]
For \(\delta>0\), define the truncated body
\[
K_{\varepsilon,\delta}=L_\varepsilon\cap\{x\in\R^3:x_1\geq-\varepsilon+\delta\}.
\]
Both bodies are compact and convex, and \(K_{\varepsilon,\delta}\subseteq L_\varepsilon\). To define a uniform distribution using three-dimensional volume on \(K_{\varepsilon,\delta}\), one needs \(\operatorname{vol}_3(K_{\varepsilon,\delta})>0\), which here is equivalent to \(0<\delta<1+\varepsilon\). At \(\delta=1+\varepsilon\) the intersection is a singleton, and for larger \(\delta\) it is empty.

For any compact convex body \(B\subset\R^3\) with positive volume, let \(X_0^B,\ldots,X_3^B\) be independent random points with common distribution
\[
\mathbb{P}(X_i^B\in A)=\frac{\operatorname{vol}_3(A\cap B)}{\operatorname{vol}_3(B)}
\quad\text{for every Borel set }A\subseteq\R^3.
\]
Define its expected random-simplex volume by
\[
M(B)=\E\!\left[\operatorname{vol}_3\!\left(\operatorname{conv}\{X_0^B,X_1^B,X_2^B,X_3^B\}\right)\right].
\]
Thus the simplex volume is ordinary Euclidean volume; the sampling probability is normalized separately for each body.

The supplied problem asks whether the half-ball-and-cone construction, after the indicated truncation, gives a counterexample to monotonicity under inclusion in dimension three. The precise inequality at an admissible parameter pair is
\[
M(K_{\varepsilon,\delta})>M(L_\varepsilon),
\qquad \varepsilon>0,\quad 0<\delta<1+\varepsilon.
\]
Any such pair would violate the proposed monotonicity implication \(B\subseteq C\Longrightarrow M(B)\leq M(C)\) for convex bodies of positive volume in \(\R^3\).

The source describes \(\varepsilon\) and \(\delta\) only as positive and small. It supplies neither thresholds nor a dependence of \(\delta\) on \(\varepsilon\), and does not specify whether the inequality is sought for some small pair, for every sufficiently small pair, or in an iterated limiting regime. These stronger quantified formulations therefore remain unresolved in this draft. The accompanying curation also mentions an unresolved exact integral evaluation in dimension three, but supplies no particular integral formula or evaluation target.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Form a convex body from a half unit ball in \(\R^3\) and a point just beyond its flat face, then truncate near that point. Does this truncation increase the expected volume of the tetrahedron formed by four independent uniform points? The source leaves the meaning and quantifiers of the small parameters unspecified.
