\section{Erdős–Moser problem}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For integers $m\ge2$ and $k\ge1$, the Erd\H{o}s--Moser equation is
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{m-1}j^k=m^k.
\]
The pair $(m,k)=(3,1)$ gives $1+2=3$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is $(m,k)=(3,1)$ its only solution with $m\ge2$ and $k\ge1$? Equivalently, does it have no solution with $k\ge2$? \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is one plus two equals three the only positive-integer power-sum equality of this form?
