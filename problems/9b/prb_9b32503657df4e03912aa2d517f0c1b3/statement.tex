\[
1\longrightarrow \pi_1(S)\xrightarrow{\iota}G\xrightarrow{p}Q\longrightarrow 1
\]
means that \(\iota\) is injective, \(p\) is surjective, and \(\ker p=\iota(\pi_1(S))\).

A \(\operatorname{CAT}(0)\) cube complex is a complex obtained by gluing Euclidean cubes along faces whose intrinsic metric is geodesic and satisfies the \(\operatorname{CAT}(0)\) comparison inequality. Here \emph{cubulable} means admitting an action by cubical automorphisms on such a complex \(X\) that is free, proper, and cocompact: no nonidentity element fixes a point; for every compact \(K\subseteq X\), the set \(\{g\in G:gK\cap K\neq\varnothing\}\) is finite; and \(G\backslash X\) is compact.
