\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source asks broadly which limit theorems or statistical properties can be obtained for slowly mixing systems, without defining a system class, observable regularity, mixing norm, normalization, or target law. The aid supplies one stationary regenerative example showing different limit types for bounded observables on the same system.

Fix \(1<\beta<2\). Take independent renewal blocks with integer length \(L\) satisfying
\[
 \Pr(L\ge\ell)=\ell^{-\beta}\quad(\ell\ge1),\qquad
 \mu=\E L=\sum_{\ell\ge1}\ell^{-\beta}<\infty,
\]
and independent fair signs \(Z\in\{-1,1\}\), constant on each block. Use the stationary two-sided renewal construction: the block containing time zero has the size-biased length distribution and the origin is uniformly positioned within it. Shift by one time unit. The aid reports this system to be stationary and mixing.

For \(0<\gamma\le1\), let \(f_\gamma\) at a time in a block be \(ZL^{\gamma-1}\). Then \(|f_\gamma|\le1\) and its stationary mean is zero. Writing \(C_\gamma(n)=\E[f_\gamma\,f_\gamma\circ T^n]\), its same-block covariance is
\[
 C_\gamma(n)=\frac1\mu\E[(L-n)_+L^{2\gamma-2}],
\]
and the aid reports
\[
 C_\gamma(n)\sim
 \frac{\beta\,n^{2\gamma-\beta-1}}
 {\mu(\beta-2\gamma+1)(\beta-2\gamma+2)}.
\]
In particular the nonnegative covariance series is summable exactly when \(\gamma<\beta/2\).

For \(S_nf_\gamma=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}f_\gamma\circ T^j\), the reported limit regimes are
\[
 \frac{S_nf_\gamma}{\sqrt n}\Longrightarrow
 \mathcal N\left(0,\frac{\E L^{2\gamma}}\mu\right)
 \quad(\gamma<\beta/2),
\]
\[
 \frac{S_nf_\gamma}{\sqrt{(n/\mu)\log n}}
 \Longrightarrow\text{ a nondegenerate centered Gaussian law}
 \quad(\gamma=\beta/2),
\]
and, for \(\gamma>\beta/2\), a nondegenerate symmetric stable law of index
\[
 \alpha=\beta/\gamma\in(1,2)
\]
under the corresponding \((n/\mu)^{\gamma/\beta}\) stable normalization, with the limiting scale convention to be fixed. The supplied summary does not state all limiting-law constants, so the borderline and stable laws are not assigned invented scales. The block reward is \(ZL^\gamma\); its dependence on the block duration means the renewal stopping index is not independent of rewards. The aid reports explicit control of this dependence and of the size-biased endpoint blocks, but its proof artifact is not included.

Thus the covariance-summability threshold and the Gaussian/borderline/stable transition coincide in this particular duration--amplitude family. Boundedness of an observable alone does not force a standard \(\sqrt n\) Gaussian limit here. This is not a theorem that correlation exponents determine limit laws for every slowly mixing system, nor a complete answer to the source's broad research direction.
