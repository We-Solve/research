\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Gamma_g\) denote the source's mapping class group and let \(H\leq\Gamma_g\) be convex cocompact. In the usual Teichm\"uller convention this means that an orbit of the finitely generated subgroup is quasiconvex for the Teichm\"uller metric. The exact surface, genus range, and convex-cocompact convention are not supplied.

A subgroup \(H\) is separable in \(\Gamma_g\) if for every \(x\in\Gamma_g\setminus H\) there exist a finite group \(F\) and homomorphism \(q:\Gamma_g\to F\) with \(q(x)\notin q(H)\). Equivalently, every such \(x\) is excluded by some finite-index subgroup containing \(H\), or \(H\) is closed in the profinite topology.

\emph{Question 3.5.} Is \(\Gamma_g\) \(H\)-separable for every convex-cocompact subgroup \(H\)? Explicitly,

The finite quotient may depend on \(x\) and \(H\); a single quotient distinguishing all outside elements is not required. Nor is this a claim that all finitely generated subgroups are separable.
