\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Omega=\{s\in\C:\operatorname{Re}s>\tfrac12\}\), and let \(\zeta\) denote the meromorphic continuation of the Riemann zeta function. Use the standard half-plane Hardy space
\[
H^2(\Omega)=\left\{f\text{ holomorphic on }\Omega:\sup_{\sigma>1/2}\int_{\R}|f(\sigma+it)|^2\,\dd t<\infty\right\}.
\]
Let \(E^2\) be the Hilbert space of Dirichlet series \(D\) such that \(D(s)/s\), initially considered where the series converges, extends to a function \(f_D\in H^2(\Omega)\). Equip it with the induced norm \(\|D\|_{E^2}=\|f_D\|_{H^2(\Omega)}\), and identify \(D\) on \(\Omega\) with its holomorphic extension \(s f_D(s)\).

For every positive integer \(n\) and coefficients \(c_1,\ldots,c_n\in\C\) satisfying
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{c_k}{k}=0,
\]
consider \(\zeta(s)\sum_{k=1}^{n}c_k k^{-s}\), with the singularity at \(s=1\) removed. Define
\[
V=\overline{\left\{\zeta(s)\sum_{k=1}^{n}c_k k^{-s}:n\geq1,\ c_1,\ldots,c_n\in\C,\ \sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{c_k}{k}=0\right\}}^{\,E^2}.
\]
The question is whether, unconditionally,
\[
V=\left\{D\in E^2:\frac{D(s)}{\zeta(s)}\text{ extends holomorphically throughout }\Omega\right\}.
\]
Here holomorphic extension of the quotient includes removability at zeros of \(\zeta\) and at its pole \(s=1\); no holomorphic extension across the boundary \(\operatorname{Re}s=\tfrac12\) is requested. The Riemann hypothesis is not an assumption of the question.
