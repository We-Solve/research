\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a countably infinite discrete amenable group. Here amenability means that for every finite \(K\subseteq G\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\), there is a nonempty finite \(F\subseteq G\) such that \(|kF\mathbin{\triangle}F|<\varepsilon|F|\) for every \(k\in K\). Let \(X\) be a Cantor space, meaning a nonempty compact metrizable totally disconnected space without isolated points. Consider an action \(G\curvearrowright X\) by homeomorphisms, written \((g,x)\mapsto gx\). The action is minimal if \(\overline{Gx}=X\) for every \(x\in X\). No freeness assumption is included in the supplied statement.

Write \(M_G(X)\) for the set of Borel probability measures \(\mu\) on \(X\) satisfying \(\mu(gE)=\mu(E)\) for every \(g\in G\) and every Borel set \(E\subseteq X\). A subset of \(X\) is clopen if it is both open and closed. The action has \emph{clopen strict comparison} if, for all clopen \(A,B\subseteq X\),
\[
\bigl(\forall\mu\in M_G(X),\ \mu(A)<\mu(B)\bigr)
\ \Longrightarrow\
\begin{gathered}
\exists n\in\Z_{\geq 0},\ \exists A_1,\ldots,A_n\subseteq X\text{ clopen},\ \exists g_1,\ldots,g_n\in G:\\
A=\bigsqcup_{i=1}^{n}A_i,\qquad g_iA_i\subseteq B\quad(1\leq i\leq n),\\
g_iA_i\cap g_jA_j=\varnothing\quad(1\leq i<j\leq n).
\end{gathered}
\]
The empty partition is allowed when \(A=\varnothing\). The measure inequality is required separately for every invariant probability measure; no numerical gap is specified in the hypothesis.

\textbf{Question.} Does every minimal action by homeomorphisms of every countably infinite discrete amenable group on a Cantor space have clopen strict comparison?

\subsection{Short English statement}
For every minimal action of a countably infinite amenable group on a Cantor space, must a clopen set that is smaller than another for every invariant probability measure split into finitely many clopen pieces whose group translates fit disjointly inside the other set?
