\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 4000012}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a metric space \((X,d)\) and a Markov kernel \(P\) with finite-first-moment transition laws, coarse Ricci curvature is
\[
\kappa(x,y)=1-\frac{W_1(P(x,\cdot),P(y,\cdot))}{d(x,y)}\quad(x\ne y),
\]
where \(W_1\) is the optimal transport cost for the distance \(d\). A positive uniform lower bound has the form \(\kappa(x,y)\ge K>0\) on the pairs required by the chosen framework. Volume or isoperimetry additionally requires a reference measure \(m\), not merely the metric and kernel.

Ollivier's Problem L asks for an analogue of Bishop--Gromov volume comparison or the isoperimetric Gromov--Lévy comparison under positive coarse Ricci curvature, including an appropriate model space and discrete examples. For instance, if \(V_x(r)=m(\{y:d(x,y)\le r\})\) and a comparison function \(V_{\mathrm{model}}\) has been selected, a volume-ratio formulation would ask for an appropriate monotonicity or comparison of
\[
\frac{V_x(r)}{V_{\mathrm{model}}(r)}.
\]
No such model function or monotonicity claim is specified in the source, and no particular inequality is asserted here.

Likewise, on a finite measured Markov space one possible boundary functional is
\[
Q(A,A^c)=\sum_{x\in A}m(x)P(x,A^c),\qquad
I(v)=\inf_{A:\,m(A)=v}Q(A,A^c)
\]
for attainable masses \(v\); geometric perimeter gives a different formulation in other settings. The source asks for a suitable isoperimetric comparison rather than selecting this boundary convention. A required motivating discrete example is the cube with vertex set \(\{0,1\}^n\) and Hamming distance. Its kernel, holding probability, reference measure, and the role of dimension must be specified in an actual theorem; they are not fixed by the extracted question.

The supplied research summary reports existing Lévy--Gromov-style isoperimetric, concentration, Laplacian-comparison, and diameter results, while saying that a general Bishop--Gromov volume-ratio comparison with a natural model remains unsettled in its assessment. These partial results are kept distinct from a complete answer to the programme.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find volume or isoperimetric comparison theorems for positive coarse Ricci curvature, with suitable model spaces and a formulation that includes discrete cubes. The model, measure, and precise comparison inequality remain to be chosen.
