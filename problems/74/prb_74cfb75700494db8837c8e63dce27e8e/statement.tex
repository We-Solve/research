\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each integer \(n\geq 0\), write its finite binary expansion as \(n=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_j(n)2^j\), where \(a_j(n)\in\{0,1\}\) and all but finitely many digits vanish. Define the base-\(2\) radical inverse by
\[
\phi_2(n)=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_j(n)2^{-j-1},
\]
so that \(\phi_2(0)=0\). For \(x\in\R\), let \(\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor\). The sequence in question is
\[
z_n=\bigl(\phi_2(n),\{n\sqrt{2}\}\bigr)\in[0,1)^2,\qquad n\geq 0.
\]
For a positive integer \(N\), consider its first \(N\) points, indexed by \(0\leq n<N\). One standard normalized discrepancy convention is
\[
D_N=\sup_{\substack{0\leq u_i\leq v_i\leq 1\\i=1,2}}
\left|\frac{1}{N}\#\left\{0\leq n<N:z_n\in[u_1,v_1)\times[u_2,v_2)\right\}-(v_1-u_1)(v_2-u_2)\right|.
\]
The corresponding star discrepancy is
\[
D_N^*=\sup_{0\leq t_1,t_2\leq 1}

\]
