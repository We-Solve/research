\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Landweber's Conjecture 5.2 says that Kirwan surjectivity holds ``under these conditions.'' Companion record 20001956 supplies the antecedent: a group action with a nondegenerate abstract moment map, without assuming a global symplectic form. For an equivariant map \(\mu:M\to\mathfrak g^*\), put \(Z=\mu^{-1}(0)\). With a coefficient field \(K\), the cohomological restriction is
\[
\kappa:H_G^*(M;K)\longrightarrow H_G^*(Z;K),
\]
where \(H_G^*(X;K)=H^*(EG\times_GX;K)\). If the action on a regular level is free, the target identifies with \(H^*(Z/G;K)\). Group, compactness/properness, regular-value, isotropy and coefficient hypotheses must be specified for a definitive version of the original conjecture.

For a compact torus \(T\), a usual abstract-moment-map condition says that for each subtorus \(H\), the projection of \(\mu\) to \(\mathfrak h^*\) is locally constant on \(M^H\). Componentwise nondegeneracy is read as the Morse--Bott property of \(\mu^\xi=\langle\mu,\xi\rangle\), with critical set \(M^{T_\xi}\), where \(T_\xi\) is the closure of the subgroup generated by \(\xi\). For a dense generator this is \(M^T\). The companion's rough description says \(M^G\) for every component and omits detailed subgroup axioms, so it cannot be treated as a complete precise definition without this qualification.

The supplied aid reports a sufficient rational-flag certificate. Choose a rational basis \(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_r\) of \(\mathfrak t\), let \(\mu_j=\langle\mu,\xi_j\rangle\), and set
\[
Z_0=M,\qquad Z_j=\{\mu_1=\cdots=\mu_j=0\},\qquad Z_r=Z.
\]
Assume all nested partial levels are regular smooth \(T\)-manifolds. At each stage require that \((\mu_j|_{Z_{j-1}})^2\) gives a finite equivariant Morse--Bott/Thom filtration with bottom \(Z_j\), with the compactness or properness needed to exhaust the stage; every negative normal bundle is equivariantly oriented over \(K\), and its Euler class is a non-zero-divisor in the \emph{full} \(T\)-equivariant cohomology of its critical manifold. Under these explicit outputs the aid reports surjections
\[
H_T^*(Z_{j-1};K)\twoheadrightarrow H_T^*(Z_j;K),
\]
whose composition is \(\kappa\). No torus factor is silently discarded in the induction. Componentwise nondegeneracy alone is not asserted to provide these square-function filtrations or Euler properties. The aid says a Baird--Lin invariant almost/stable-complex hypothesis supplies such a certificate under its theorem's hypotheses, but gives no full statement or citation. It describes the unrestricted compact-torus case as unresolved in the literature checked.

The original comments, attributed to Harada, Holm and Mare, report the compact \(S^1\) case in [GGK02] and an error in that source's torus-action proof. Landweber separately asks whether local symplectic forms can be found and used for the needed geometry. Harada cites [GGK02, Theorem G.22] as addressing local existence when the abstract map is nondegenerate and the symplectic slices admit invariant complex structures. This is explicitly a rough local-near-an-orbit report, not a claimed global symplectic form or a completed proof of general Kirwan surjectivity.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does abstract-moment-map nondegeneracy imply Kirwan surjectivity? The supplied aid gives a conditional flag certificate with explicit regularity, Morse-filtration, orientation and Euler-class assumptions. It preserves the historical torus-proof error and the separate local symplectic-form question.
