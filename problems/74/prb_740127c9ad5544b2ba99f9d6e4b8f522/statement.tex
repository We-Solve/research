\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a discrete group, with no countability assumption. Let \(\ell^2(G)\) be the Hilbert space of square-summable complex functions on \(G\). The left regular representation is given by
\[
 (\lambda(g)\xi)(h)=\xi(g^{-1}h)
 \qquad(g,h\in G,\ \xi\in\ell^2(G)).
\]
The reduced group \(C^*\)-algebra is the unital complex \(C^*\)-algebra
\[
 A_G=C^*_{\lambda}(G)
 :=\overline{\operatorname{span}_{\C}\{\lambda(g):g\in G\}}^{\|\cdot\|}
 \subseteq B(\ell^2(G)),
\]
where the closure is taken in operator norm. Its unit is \(\lambda(e)\), where \(e\) is the identity of \(G\). A nonzero \(C^*\)-algebra is simple if its only norm-closed two-sided ideals are \(\{0\}\) and the whole algebra. For a unital \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\), write
\[
 A^{\times}=\{b\in A:\text{there exists }c\in A\text{ with }bc=cb=1_A\}.
\]
Here stable rank one means topological stable rank one, equivalently that \(A^{\times}\) is norm dense in \(A\).

\textbf{Question.} Is it true that, for every discrete group \(G\),
\[
 C^*_{\lambda}(G)\text{ simple}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \overline{\bigl(C^*_{\lambda}(G)\bigr)^{\times}}^{\|\cdot\|}
 =C^*_{\lambda}(G)?
\]
Equivalently, whenever \(C^*_{\lambda}(G)\) is simple, must every \(a\in C^*_{\lambda}(G)\) and every real \(\varepsilon>0\) admit an invertible \(b\in C^*_{\lambda}(G)\) such that \(\|a-b\|<\varepsilon\)?

The supplied record labels this question open. Its dated literature assessment states that stable rank one is known for important families, including free products and many acylindrically hyperbolic groups, while the general question remains open. \subsection{Short English statement}
Does every discrete group whose reduced group \(C^*\)-algebra is simple have the property that every element of that algebra can be approximated arbitrarily closely in norm by invertible elements? No countability assumption on the group is imposed. The supplied open status requires independent review.
