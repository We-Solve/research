\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
This canonical record merges source problems \(1\)--\(17\), rather than one problem. Items \(18\)--\(21\) in the handout are outside this record. Let \(F=(f_j)_{j=1}^N\) be a frame spanning \(\C^M\) or \(\R^M\). A unit-norm tight frame (UNTF) satisfies \(\|f_j\|=1\) and \(\sum_j f_jf_j^*=(N/M)I\). An equiangular tight frame (ETF) also has constant \(|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|\) for \(i\ne j\); its squared value is \((N-M)/(M(N-1))\) in the nondegenerate range. A real flat ETF has coordinates \(\pm M^{-1/2}\). Write \(\mathbb T=\{z\in\C:|z|=1\}\), and define the phase-invariant intensity map
\[
 A_F:\C^M/\mathbb T\to\R^N,\qquad
 [x]\mapsto(|\langle x,f_j\rangle|^2)_{j=1}^N.
\]
Its injectivity means phase retrieval for all signals. The source questions are preserved individually:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Zauner's conjecture: do complex ETFs with \(N=M^2\) exist for every positive integer \(M\)?
\item Hadamard conjecture: for every positive integer \(k\), does a real \(4k\)-by-\(4k\) matrix \(H\) with entries \(\pm1\) satisfy \(HH^T=4kI\)?
\item Paulsen problem: quantify distance from a frame approximately tight and approximately equal-norm to a frame with both properties. The corrupted tolerance glyph is interpreted as \(\eps\); the source does not define the approximation norms, frame normalization or distance.
\item Dustin Mixon: does \(N<4M-4\) force noninjectivity of \(A_F\)?
\item Dan Edidin: near \(N=4M-4\), can the injective-frame locus be open while its complement has positive measure?
\item Dan Edidin: what does injectivity of the displayed restricted map \(B:[x]\mapsto(|\langle x,f_k\rangle|^2)_k\) imply for phase retrieval? Its extracted domain \(\mathbb P^{M-1}/\mathbb T\) and missing codomain are unresolved; projective vectors without a norm convention do not define these intensities. The extraction labels this item ``16'' before item \(7\).
\item Bernhard Bodmann: find measurement numbers \(N\) allowing a quantitative stability measure; the metric, signal class and constants are not specified.

\item Ferenc Szollosi: determine the maximum number of equiangular lines in \(\R^{14}\). The source's historical stated possibilities are \(28,29,30\), without a current-status verification.
\item Ferenc Szollosi: give a full algebraic classification of complex ETFs for small \((M,N)\); the parameter range and equivalence are unspecified.

\item Dustin Mixon: for fixed \(N\), does ``worst case coherence'' strictly decrease as \(M\) increases? For unit vectors coherence is \(\max_{i\ne j}|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|\); whether the question optimizes its minimum over frames or means another worst-case quantity is unspecified.

\[

The aid's Hadamard partial result uses an order-\(h\) Hadamard matrix split into \(r\) rows and \(h-r\) complementary rows, with \(1\leq r<h\). After normalizing columns in their respective dimensions, one family is a flat ETF exactly when the other is. If \(\lambda\) is the common absolute raw cross-inner-product of the \(r\)-row columns, the necessary conditions are
\[
 \lambda^2=\frac{r(h-r)}{h-1},\qquad
 \lambda\equiv r\pmod2,\qquad h-1\mid r(r-1).
\]
Thus fixed \(r\geq2\) cannot produce such ETFs for arbitrarily large \(h\); \(r=1\) yields the standard complementary flat simplex family. This is a restriction on that deletion mechanism, not on all real flat ETFs or on the Hadamard conjecture itself.

The aid separately reports that the phase-retrieval-injective frame locus is open, and combines Vinzant's injective \(11\)-frame in \(\C^4\) with Li's June 2026 nonempty open noninjective locus. Under those reported results, \((M,N)=(4,11)\) gives a nonempty open injective locus and a positive-Lebesgue-measure complement, answering item \(5\) affirmatively and refuting the universal formulation in item \(4\). Li's result is explicitly a recent preprint, and no precise citation is supplied. No resolution of the remaining merged questions is asserted.
