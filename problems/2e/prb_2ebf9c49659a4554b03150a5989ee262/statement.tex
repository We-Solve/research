\section{Erdős–Straus conjecture}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For an integer $n>2$, an Egyptian-fraction representation of $4/n$ by three terms means a sum of reciprocals of three positive integers. Repeated denominators are allowed.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every integer $n>2$, do there exist $x,y,z\in\mathbb N$ such that\[ \frac1x+\frac1y+\frac1z=\frac4n? \] \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can four divided by every integer greater than two be written as a sum of three positive unit fractions?
