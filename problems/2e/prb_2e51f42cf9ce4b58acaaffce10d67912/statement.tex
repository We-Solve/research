\textbf{Four invariant-theory questions.} For a linear algebraic group \(G\) acting on an affine variety \(X\), is \(K[X]^G\) finitely generated? If it is, find generators. Determine its structural properties, and determine whether invariant functions separate distinct \(G\)-orbits. This is a list of tasks over a broad class, not an asserted affirmative theorem for every group action.\par Let \(K\) be the intended field, \(A=K[X]\), and suppose the action is algebraic, with coaction \(\rho:A\to K[G]\otimes_KA\). The invariant ring is \(A^G=\{f\in A:\rho(f)=1\otimes f\}\). Over an algebraically closed field this is equivalently the ring of regular functions satisfying \(f(gx)=f(x)\) for all \(g,x\). Finite generation asks for finitely many \(f_1,\ldots,f_r\in A^G\) with \(A^G=K[f_1,\ldots,f_r]\); the computational question asks to determine such a list from a specified presentation of the action when possible.
