\section{Imbalance conjecture}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $G$ be a finite simple undirected graph. For $e=uv\in E(G)$ put $\operatorname{imb}(e)=|\deg_G(u)-\deg_G(v)|$ and let $M_G$ be the multiset of these values, one for each edge. A multiset of nonnegative integers is graphic if it is the multiset of vertex degrees of some finite simple graph.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If $\operatorname{imb}(e)>0$ for every $e\in E(G)$, must $M_G$ be graphic? \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the list of positive edge-degree imbalances always the degree list of another simple graph?
