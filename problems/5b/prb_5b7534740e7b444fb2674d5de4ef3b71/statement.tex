\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite graph \(G=(V,E)\), a \emph{perfect matching} is a subset \(M\subseteq E\) such that every vertex of \(G\) is incident with exactly one edge of \(M\). Define
\[
\operatorname{pm}(G)=\left|\{M\subseteq E : M\text{ is a perfect matching of }G\}\right|.
\]
