\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a pseudo-Riemannian metric $g$ with Levi-Civita connection $\nabla$, a symmetric covariant tensor $K_{a_1\ldots a_m}$ is a Killing tensor of order $m$ if
\[
\nabla_{(a_0}K_{a_1\ldots a_m)}=0,
\]
where parentheses denote symmetrization over all displayed indices. Raising indices gives a homogeneous degree-$m$ polynomial
\[
I_K(x,p)=K^{a_1\ldots a_m}(x)p_{a_1}\cdots p_{a_m}
\]
that Poisson-commutes with the geodesic Hamiltonian $H=\tfrac12g^{ab}p_ap_b$. An Einstein metric satisfies $\operatorname{Ric}(g)=\Lambda g$ for a constant $\Lambda$.

\textbf{Construction problem.} Construct stationary axially symmetric four-dimensional Einstein metrics admitting Killing tensors of higher order, interpreted as some integer $m\geq3$. In the conventional spacetime reading, stationarity is generated by a Killing field timelike in a stationary region, axial symmetry by a Killing field with periodic orbits, and the two symmetry fields commute.
