\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701704}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Use the standard conventions that locally compact groups are Hausdorff, compact group spaces are Hausdorff, and actions are continuous. A lattice in a locally compact group $G$ is a discrete subgroup $\Gamma\leq G$ such that $G/\Gamma$ admits a nonzero finite $G$-invariant Radon measure. No countability assumption is imposed.

For a compact $G$-space $X$, let $\mathcal P(X)$ denote the space of regular Borel probability measures with its weak-* topology, with $G$ acting by pushforward. The action on $X$ is minimal if every orbit is dense, and strongly proximal if, for every $\mu\in\mathcal P(X)$, the closure of $G\mu$ contains a Dirac measure $\delta_x$ for some $x\in X$. The Furstenberg boundary $\partial_{sp}G$ is the universal minimal strongly proximal compact $G$-space: every minimal strongly proximal compact $G$-space is a continuous $G$-equivariant image of $\partial_{sp}G$.

For a subgroup $\Gamma\leq G$, define
\[
\Gamma_x=\{\gamma\in\Gamma:\gamma x=x\},\qquad
F_\Gamma(X)=\{x\in X:\Gamma_x=\{e\}\}.
\]
The restricted action is faithful if $\bigcap_{x\in X}\Gamma_x=\{e\}$, and topologically free, in the precise sense supplied by the source, if $\overline{F_\Gamma(X)}=X$.

\textbf{Question (a).} Does there exist a locally compact group $G$ with two lattices $\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2\leq G$ such that, for $X=\partial_{sp}G$, both restricted actions are faithful and
\[
\overline{F_{\Gamma_1}(X)}=X,
\qquad
\overline{F_{\Gamma_2}(X)}\ne X?
\]
\textbf{Question (b).} If the answer to (a) is affirmative, can such a choice also satisfy $X\cong G/H$ as a $G$-space for some closed subgroup $H\leq G$, with the quotient topology and the left translation action? Here the identification is a $G$-equivariant homeomorphism, and $G/H$ is compact because it represents $X$.

The separation and continuity conventions above, and the standard meaning of lattice, make explicit terminology left implicit in the supplied record; they are not additional source-specific hypotheses.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can two lattices in the same locally compact group act faithfully on its Furstenberg boundary while only the first action has a dense set of points with trivial stabilizer? If so, can this happen when the boundary is a homogeneous space $G/H$?
