\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701723}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a finite group, and let \(\operatorname{Irr}(G)\) denote the set of irreducible complex characters of \(G\). For \(\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)\), define its field of values by
\[
\Q(\chi)=\Q\bigl(\chi(g):g\in G\bigr).
\]
For a positive integer \(k\), call \(G\) \(k\)-rational if
\[
[\Q(\chi):\Q]\mid k
\qquad\text{for every }\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G).
\]
Here the positive-integer domain of \(k\) is the conventional interpretation of the divisibility condition; the supplied statement does not explicitly specify that domain.

A composition series of \(G\) is a chain
\[
1=G_0\triangleleft G_1\triangleleft\cdots\triangleleft G_n=G
\]
whose quotients \(G_i/G_{i-1}\) are nontrivial simple groups. A cyclic composition factor necessarily has prime order; write \(C_p\) for a cyclic group of prime order \(p\).

Does there exist a function
\[
f:\Z_{\geq 1}\longrightarrow\R
\]
such that, for every positive integer \(k\), every finite \(k\)-rational group \(G\), and every prime \(p\),
\[
\bigl(C_p\text{ is isomorphic to a composition factor of }G\bigr)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p\leq f(k)?
\]
Thus the bound must depend only on \(k\), uniformly over all such finite groups and their cyclic composition factors.

The supplied record states that, when \(k=1\), one has \(p\leq 11\), attributing this result to J. G. Thompson. This attribution and special-case assertion are retained as supplied background and have not been independently verified.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there a bound depending only on the positive integer \(k\) for the prime orders of cyclic composition factors of finite groups whose irreducible complex character fields all have degrees dividing \(k\)? The supplied record labels this problem open and attributes the bound \(11\) for \(k=1\) to J. G. Thompson; these are supplied assessments, not independently verified conclusions.
