Can finitely many residue classes $a_i\pmod {d_i}$, with $d_i>1$ and no distinct moduli dividing one another, cover all integers? The known answer is no, by Balister--Bollob\'as--Morris--Sahasrabudhe--Tiba. We do not reproduce their full distortion-sieve proof. We prove the assertion when the moduli together involve at most two primes, and a prime-power exclusion for any putative counterexample.
