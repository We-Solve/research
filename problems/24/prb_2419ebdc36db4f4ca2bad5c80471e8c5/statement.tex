\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic \(p>0\), and let \(G\) be a split reductive algebraic group over \(k\). A simple rational \(G\)-module is a nonzero finite-dimensional \(k\)-vector space \(V\) equipped with an algebraic representation \(G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)\) and having no proper nonzero \(G\)-stable \(k\)-subspace. Choose a split maximal torus \(T\subseteq G\), and write \(X^*(T)=\operatorname{Hom}_{k\text{-groups}}(T,\mathbb G_m)\) for its character lattice. The weight decomposition and formal character of \(V\) are
\[
V=\bigoplus_{\mu\in X^*(T)}V_\mu,
\qquad
\operatorname{ch}_T(V)=\sum_{\mu\in X^*(T)}\dim_k(V_\mu)e^\mu\in\Z[X^*(T)],
\]
where \(V_\mu\) is the subspace on which \(T\) acts through \(\mu\), and \(e^\mu\) denotes the corresponding basis element of the group ring. Only finitely many weight spaces are nonzero, and
\[
\dim_k V=\sum_{\mu\in X^*(T)}\dim_k(V_\mu).
\]
The requested task is to determine \(\dim_k V\) and \(\operatorname{ch}_T(V)\) for every isomorphism class of simple rational \(G\)-modules \(V\), and to give a uniform construction or description of these modules.
