 N=\lfloor t/p\rfloor,\qquad X_i=\mathbf1_A\circ T^i,

\]
For integer gap \(g\ge1\), define the positive short-return excess
\[
 Q_A(g)=\sum_{j=1}^g\max\{\mu(A\cap T^{-j}A)-p^2,0\}.
\]
For the entire far-field sigma algebra
\(\mathcal B_{i,N,g}=\sigma(X_j:1\le j\le N,\ |j-i|>g)\),
use the normalized event-mixing coefficient
\[
 M_A(g)=\sup_{N\ge1}\sup_{1\le i\le N}
 \frac1p\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{i,N,g}}
 |\E[(X_i-p)\mathbf1_B]|.
\]
