\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix a prime $p$. Let $\mathbb Q_p$ be the $p$-adic field and $\mathbb C_p$ the completion of its algebraic closure. Use the branch of the $p$-adic logarithm $\log_p:\mathbb C_p^*\to\mathbb C_p$ with $\log_p(p)=0$, agreeing near $1$ with
\[
\log_p(1+u)=\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}u^n}{n}\qquad(|u|_p<1).
\]
For $\lambda\in\mathbb Q_p^*$ let $T_\lambda:\mathbb Q_p\to\mathbb Q_p$ be $T_\lambda(x)=\lambda x$. This restriction on $\lambda$ is necessary for the displayed map to be a nonzero multiplication self-map of $\mathbb Q_p$.

Can one define a meaningful $p$-adic entropy-like invariant $h_p$, on a suitable class of dynamical systems containing these maps, such that
\[
h_p(T_\lambda)=\log_p\lambda\qquad(\lambda\in\mathbb Q_p^*)?
\]
The source suggests invariance under topological conjugacy as one possible desired property: if a homeomorphism $\psi$ conjugates $S$ to $T$, so $\psi\circ S=T\circ\psi$, then $h_p(S)=h_p(T)$. This is proposed as an example of an invariance requirement, not a complete supplied list of axioms. The target values are $p$-adic, rather than the nonnegative real values of ordinary entropy.
