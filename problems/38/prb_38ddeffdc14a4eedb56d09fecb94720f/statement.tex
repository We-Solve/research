\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((G,u)\) be a compact matrix quantum group, with distinguished finite-dimensional corepresentation \(u\) on a complex vector space \(H\). Write \(C(G)\) for its function algebra and \(1\) for its unit. For each integer \(n\geq 1\), define
\[
V_n=\operatorname{Fix}(u^{\otimes n})=\{\xi\in H^{\otimes n}:u^{\otimes n}(\xi\otimes 1)=\xi\otimes 1\}.
\]
The fusion semiring \(R^+(G)\) is the semiring of equivalence classes of finite-dimensional unitary corepresentations of \(G\), with addition induced by direct sum and multiplication induced by tensor product; its distinguished additive basis consists of irreducible corepresentation classes.

\textbf{Source problem.} Characterize the pairs \((G,u)\) for which the sequence \((V_n)_{n\geq 1}\) admits a \emph{free decomposition}. Determine whether, for every compact matrix quantum group with its distinguished corepresentation \((G,u)\),
\[
(V_n)_{n\geq 1}\text{ admits a free decomposition}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
G\text{ has a free fusion semiring}.
\]
Develop a generalization of free decomposition that permits possibly negative free cumulants.
