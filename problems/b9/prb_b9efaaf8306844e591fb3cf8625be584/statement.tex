\section{Are there infinitely many sexy primes}\label{9400161}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 9400161 · Number Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A sexy-prime pair is $(p,p+6)$ with both entries prime. They need not be consecutive primes.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Are there infinitely many primes $p$ for which $p+6$ is prime? \sref{9400161}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Are there infinitely many pairs of primes differing by six?
