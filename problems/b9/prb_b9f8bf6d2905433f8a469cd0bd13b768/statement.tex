Using the left--left convention, a Yetter--Drinfeld module over \(\C G\) is a complex vector space \(V\) with a linear \(G\)-action and a grading
\[
V=\bigoplus_{h\in G}V_h,
\qquad g\cdot V_h=V_{ghg^{-1}}\quad(g,h\in G).
\]
Its braiding is determined by
\[
c(v\otimes w)=h\cdot w\otimes v
\qquad(v\in V_h,\ w\in V).
\]
Let \(T(V)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}V^{\otimes n}\) be the tensor algebra with its braided Hopf algebra structure in which every element of \(V\) is primitive. The Nichols algebra of \(V\) is
\[
\mathcal B(V)=T(V)/I(V),
\]
where \(I(V)\) is the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in \(\bigoplus_{n\geq2}V^{\otimes n}\).
