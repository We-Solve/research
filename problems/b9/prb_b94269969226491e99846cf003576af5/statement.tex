\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 20000457}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an elliptic curve \(E/\Q\) with \(\operatorname{rank}E(\Q)>0\), define
\[
\mathcal D(E)=\{p:\overline{E(\Q)}^{\,p}=E(\Q_p)\},
\]
where closure is in the \(p\)-adic topology. Takloo-Bighash asks to describe this set, whether it is always nonempty, and whether there is a modular interpretation. The source also suggests data collection to test positive prime density, relates the question to Brauer-type problems for surfaces, and separates surjectivity of reduction from surjectivity in the formal group.

Let \(E_j\) be the standard formal filtration at the identity of a minimal/N\'eron model: for a formal parameter \(t\), \(E_j\) consists of points with \(v_p(t)\geq j\). Put \(m_p=1\) for odd \(p\) and \(m_2=2\). For any subgroup \(\Gamma\subset E(\Q_p)\), the attached aid reports
\[
\overline\Gamma=E(\Q_p)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Gamma\twoheadrightarrow E(\Q_p)/E_{m_p+1}.
\]
For odd \(p\), define \(s_p(\Gamma)\geq1\) by the closure of its formal logarithms,
\[
\overline{\log_E(\Gamma\cap E_1)}=p^{s_p(\Gamma)}\Z_p,
\]
when nonzero, and use \(s_p=\infty\) otherwise. The aid reports the exact index
\[
[E(\Q_p):\overline\Gamma]
=[E(\Q_p)/E_1:\operatorname{im}\Gamma]\,p^{s_p(\Gamma)-1},
\]
with infinite index in the zero-log case. At an odd good-reduction prime, its finite criterion for rational points is
\[
p\in\mathcal D(E)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
E(\Q)\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal E(\Z/p^2\Z),
\]
for the smooth proper good-reduction model \(\mathcal E\). At bad primes the finite quotient must retain the appropriate reduction/component data. This is a congruence/N\'eron-model interpretation, not a claimed classical modular-curve interpretation.

The aid presents the following explicitly low-confidence worked example:
\[
E:\ y^2+xy=x^3-5455771x-5039899603,
\qquad P=\left(\frac{328219}{100},\frac{109777927}{1000}\right).
\]
For the curve labelled \texttt{20622.j1}, it reports \(E(\Q)=\Z P\) and
\[
\mathcal D(E)=\{3,7\}.
\]
The rank/generator verification, local computations and proof excluding every other prime are not supplied in the record, so the equality is retained as an unreviewed candidate claim. The aid reports universal nonemptiness still open and claims no general positive-density or classical modular-curve theorem.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For a positive-rank elliptic curve, determine the primes where rational points are dense in the full \(p\)-adic group. The attached aid reports finite-level criteria and an exact-index formula, plus a low-confidence finite-prime example, while leaving universal nonemptiness and a classical modular interpretation unresolved.
