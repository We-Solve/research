Let $S\subset\R$ be a unital subring and let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over $S$. Its nonzero spectrum means the multiset of nonzero complex eigenvalues, with algebraic multiplicity. A matrix $B$ over $S$ is primitive in the nonnegative-matrix sense if all its entries are nonnegative real numbers and $B^k$ has all entries strictly positive for some integer $k\geq1$.

Square matrices $A_0,A_1$ over $S$, possibly of different sizes, are elementary strong shift equivalent over $S$ if rectangular matrices $R,T$ over $S$ satisfy $A_0=RT$ and $A_1=TR$.
