\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Sigma_g,\Sigma_h\) denote closed connected oriented surfaces of genera \(g,h\). The question is whether there exist such genera and a closed smooth \(4\)-manifold \(X\) with both
\[
 X\cong\mathbb H^4/\Gamma
 \quad\text{for a torsion-free discrete cocompact group of real hyperbolic isometries},
 \qquad
 \Sigma_g\longrightarrow X\xrightarrow{p}\Sigma_h
\]
a smooth locally trivial fiber bundle. Equivalently, \(X\) must admit a complete Riemannian metric of constant sectional curvature \(-1\), while \(p\) has the specified smooth surface fiber. No Riemannian-product structure or totally geodesic fiber is requested. The usual intended surface-bundle case has \(g,h\geq2\), but the excerpt does not explicitly give their range.
