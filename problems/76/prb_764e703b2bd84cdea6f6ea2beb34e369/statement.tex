\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701541}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A group \(G\) is \emph{periodic} if every element has finite order, that is,
\[
\forall g\in G\;\exists n\in\Z_{\geq 1}\quad g^n=1_G.
\]
A group is \emph{simple} if it is nontrivial and has no normal subgroups other than the identity subgroup and itself.

Let \(\mathfrak M\) be a finite set of finite nonabelian simple groups. The supplied problem asks whether every periodic group \(G\) saturated with groups from \(\mathfrak M\), in the sense of Kourovka Notebook Problem 14.101, satisfies
\[
\exists S\in\mathfrak M\quad G\cong S.
\]
The case \(|\mathfrak M|=1\) is singled out as being of special interest.

\textbf{Unresolved definition.} The supplied record refers to Problem 14.101 for the meaning of saturation but does not include that definition. If the intended definition is
\[
\forall H\leq G\quad
\bigl(|H|<\infty\Longrightarrow
\exists K\leq G\;\exists S\in\mathfrak M\;
(H\leq K\ \text{and}\ K\cong S)\bigr),
\]
then the question is whether this condition, together with periodicity of \(G\), always implies \(G\cong S\) for some \(S\in\mathfrak M\). This explicit interpretation is conditional and requires comparison with the cited definition in Problem 14.101. No assumption that \(G\) is locally finite is supplied.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every periodic group saturated with groups from a finite set \(\mathfrak M\) of finite nonabelian simple groups have to be isomorphic to a member of \(\mathfrak M\)? The singleton case is of special interest. The exact saturation definition depends on the unsupplied reference to Problem 14.101. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied source information.
