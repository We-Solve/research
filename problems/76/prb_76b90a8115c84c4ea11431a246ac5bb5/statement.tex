\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Give each set \(X\subseteq\R\) its usual subspace topology. An open \(\omega\)-cover of \(X\) is a family \(\mathcal U\) of open subsets of \(X\) such that \(X\notin\mathcal U\) and every finite subset of \(X\) is contained in some member of \(\mathcal U\). Write \(\Omega(X)\) for the collection of these covers. An open \(\gamma\)-cover is an infinite family of open subsets of \(X\) such that each point of \(X\) belongs to all but finitely many members; write \(\Gamma(X)\) for the collection of these covers. The property \(\mathsf S_1(\Omega,\Gamma)\) means that for every sequence \((\mathcal U_n)_{n\in\N}\) from \(\Omega(X)\), there are choices \(U_n\in\mathcal U_n\) such that \(\{U_n:n\in\N\}\in\Gamma(X)\). In particular, the selected family must be infinite.
