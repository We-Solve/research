\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each integer \(d\geq 1\), let \(K_d\subset\R^d\) be a convex body, meaning a compact convex set with nonempty interior. Let \(X_{d,0},\ldots,X_{d,d}\) be independent random points with distribution \(\operatorname{Vol}_d(K_d)^{-1}\operatorname{Vol}_d\!\restriction_{K_d}\), where \(\operatorname{Vol}_d\) denotes \(d\)-dimensional Lebesgue measure. Define
\[
P_{d+1}^{K_d}=\operatorname{conv}\{X_{d,0},\ldots,X_{d,d}\},\qquad V_d=\operatorname{Vol}_d(P_{d+1}^{K_d}).
\]
The first requested problem is to formulate and prove central limit theorems for \(V_d\) as \(d\to\infty\), separately when \(K_d\) is a Euclidean ball, a regular simplex, a cube, or a cross-polytope. In particular, a central limit theorem for the volume itself would require appropriate deterministic centering constants \(a_d\in\R\) and scaling constants \(b_d>0\) such that
\[
\frac{V_d-a_d}{b_d}\xrightarrow[d\to\infty]{\mathrm{distribution}}\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
The source does not specify these constants or the geometric normalization of the bodies. This displayed limit expresses the requested type of result, not an established conclusion. A central limit theorem for \(\log V_d\) is a different statement and does not by itself supply the requested volume limit theorem.

Separately, for integers \(d\geq4\) and \(n\geq1\), let \(U_1,\ldots,U_n\) be independent random variables uniformly distributed on the vertex set \(\{0,1\}^d\), and define
\[
P_n^d=\operatorname{conv}\{U_1,\ldots,U_n\}.
\]
Thus sampling is with replacement, as required by independence. The second requested problem is to determine the asymptotic behavior of

\mathbb E\bigl[f_\ell(P_n^d)\bigr],\qquad 2\leq\ell\leq d-2.

Consequently, the second request is not a fully specified asymptotic proposition.
