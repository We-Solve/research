\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each prime \(p\equiv 1\pmod 4\), let \(\F_p=\Z/p\Z\) and let \(Q_p=\{a^2:a\in\F_p\setminus\{0\}\}\). The Paley graph \(G_p\) is the simple undirected graph with vertex set \(\F_p\), in which distinct vertices \(x,y\) are adjacent exactly when \(x-y\in Q_p\). Its \emph{1-localization at \(0\)} is the induced graph
\[
G_{p,1}=G_p[Q_p].
\]
For a finite simple graph \(H\), write \(\overline H\) for its complement and \(\omega(H)\) for its clique number. Use the Lov\'asz theta convention
\[
\vartheta(H)=\max\left\{\sum_{u,v\in V(H)}X_{uv}: X\in\R^{V(H)\times V(H)},\ X=X^{\mathsf T},\ X\succeq0,\ \operatorname{tr}(X)=1,\ X_{uv}=0\ \text{whenever }\{u,v\}\in E(H)\right\}.
\]
\textbf{Conjecture 2 (Bandeira et al., as attributed in the supplied record).} As \(p\) tends to infinity through primes congruent to \(1\) modulo \(4\),
\[
\vartheta\!\left(\overline{G_{p,1}}\right)\sim\sqrt{\frac p2},
\qquad\text{that is,}\qquad
\lim_{\substack{p\to\infty\\p\text{ prime},\ p\equiv1\pmod4}}
\frac{\vartheta(\overline{G_{p,1}})}{\sqrt{p/2}}=1.
\]
The supplied background states that localization at any other vertex gives an isomorphic graph, cites this fact to [Kunisky-2023-SpectralPseudorandomnessPaleyClique], and displays

The last theta term in that displayed background concerns the full graph \(G_p\); the conjectured asymptotic concerns the localized graph \(G_{p,1}\). These are retained as distinct expressions. The background attributes empirical evidence for the conjectured asymptotic to [MMP-2019-LPCliquesPaley], says that it would recover the Hanson--Petridis bound, and states that \(G_{p,1}\) is circulant, so the corresponding theta semidefinite program reduces to a linear program. \subsection{Short English statement}
For primes \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), restrict the Paley graph to the neighbors of \(0\). The conjecture predicts that the Lov\'asz theta number of the complement of this induced graph, divided by \(\sqrt{p/2}\), tends to \(1\).
