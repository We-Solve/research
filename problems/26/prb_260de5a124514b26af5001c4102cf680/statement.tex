\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S=S_{g,n}$ be the finite-type hyperbolic surface fixed in the source, and let $d_{\mathrm{Th}}$ be the asymmetric Thurston metric on $\mathcal T(S)$. For a pseudo-Anosov mapping class $f$ with stretch factor $\lambda(f)>1$, distinguish its one-step infimal displacement and stable length:
\[
 \sigma_{\mathrm{Th}}(f)=\inf_Xd_{\mathrm{Th}}(X,fX),\qquad
 \tau_{\mathrm{Th}}(f)=\lim_{k\to\infty}
          \frac{d_{\mathrm{Th}}(X,f^kX)}k.
\]
Problem 2.8, attributed to K. Rafi, asks for the asymptotic number of pseudo-Anosov conjugacy classes with translation length less than $R$. The intended reading in the aid is $N_\sigma(R)=\#\{[f]:\sigma_{\mathrm{Th}}(f)<R\}$; stable length is a distinct possible reading. One must also fix primitive versus all classes, inverse identification, and any effective mapping-class quotient or orbifold weighting.

The aid records $\tau_{\mathrm{Th}}(f)=\log\lambda(f)$ and
\[
 \log\lambda(f)\leq\sigma_{\mathrm{Th}}(f)
                  \leq2\log\lambda(f).
\]
For closed $S$ of genus $g\geq2$, put $h=6g-6$ and $N_L(R)=\#\{[f]:\log\lambda(f)<R\}$ in matching counting conventions. The aid identifies the stable count with the Eskin--Mirzakhani count and reports
\[
 N_L(R)\sim\frac{e^{hR}}{hR}.
\]
Its use for the exact original conjugacy-class count requires checking conventions, particularly finite ineffective kernels and geodesic weights; those can affect leading constants. Under the reported normalization, the unconditional comparison gives
\[
 N_L(R/2)\leq N_\sigma(R)\leq N_L(R),
\]
so the lower and upper exponential rates are bounded by $h/2$ and $h$, respectively. This alone does not prove the sharp asymptotic for infimal displacement. The supplied closed-surface count is not extended here to all punctured types.

A precise conditional reduction uses
\[
 \Delta(f)=\sigma_{\mathrm{Th}}(f)-\log\lambda(f)\geq0.
\]
Suppose there is $\eps(R)\geq0$ tending to zero for which
\[
 E_R=\#\{[f]:\log\lambda(f)<R,\ \Delta(f)>\eps(R)\}
          =o(N_L(R)).
\]
Then
\[
 N_L(R-\eps(R))-E_R\leq N_\sigma(R)\leq N_L(R).
\]
The stated asymptotic for $N_L$ therefore yields $N_\sigma(R)\sim N_L(R)$. This hypothesis permits exceptional classes but is stronger than a bounded defect or equality of exponential growth exponents.

The aid also says that the invariant-foliation scaling clause of an announced Horbez--Tao theorem in a 2020 Oberwolfach report would imply $\Delta(f)=0$ for every pseudo-Anosov, yielding the sharp count in matching conventions. It explicitly labels that route conditional: no public full proof was located during the supplied research run, and the checked sources still listed the work in preparation. The announcement is not treated as a verified published proof here.
