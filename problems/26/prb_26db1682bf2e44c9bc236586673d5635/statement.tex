\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source asks for Lasota--Yorke-type inequalities for compositions of operators, but does not specify the operator class, spaces, norms or desired uniformity. The supplied research aid gives this abstract strong/weak formulation. Let $\mathcal B$ be continuously embedded in a weaker normed space, with strong norm $\|\cdot\|_s$ and weak norm $|\cdot|_w$. Suppose a sequence of linear operators $L_j$ acts on these spaces and, for all $v\in\mathcal B$, satisfies
\[
 \|L_jv\|_s\leq a_j\|v\|_s+b_j|v|_w,
 \qquad |L_jv|_w\leq c_j|v|_w,
 \qquad a_j,b_j,c_j\geq0.
\]
Put $L^{(n)}=L_n\cdots L_1$, so time runs from right to left. The reported chronological composition inequality is
\[
 \|L^{(n)}v\|_s\leq
 \left(\prod_{i=1}^n a_i\right)\|v\|_s
 +\left(\sum_{j=1}^n b_j
      \prod_{i=j+1}^n a_i\prod_{i=1}^{j-1}c_i\right)|v|_w,
\]
with empty products equal to $1$. Thus the defect introduced at step $j$ is multiplied by earlier weak factors and later strong factors; interchanging those chronological roles would give a different assertion.

The aid's uniform sufficient condition is: $c_j\leq1$, $b_j\leq B$, $a_j\leq A$ for fixed nonnegative $A,B$, and there are $r\geq1$ and $0<\rho<1$ such that
\[
 \prod_{i=k+1}^{k+r}a_i\leq\rho\qquad\text{for every }k\geq0.
\]
Then the strong initial coefficient decays exponentially; explicitly, writing $n=qr+s$ with $0\leq s<r$, the reported bound has the form
\[
 \|L^{(n)}v\|_s\leq A^s\rho^q\|v\|_s
 +\frac{B(1+A+\cdots+A^{r-1})}{1-\rho}|v|_w.
\]
The block condition is sliding and uniform in the starting time, and permits individual $a_j$ to exceed one. The summary also reports a scalar counterexample showing that the weaker assertion $a_j<1$ at every step alone need not give decaying products. No specific dynamical transfer operators or application-dependent strong/weak spaces are supplied, so this abstract criterion is retained as partial progress rather than a universal answer for arbitrary operator compositions.
