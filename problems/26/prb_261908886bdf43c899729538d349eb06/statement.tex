\[
A\sum|c_k|^2\leq\int_S\left|\sum_{k\in\Lambda}c_ke^{2\pi ikt}\right|^2dt
\leq B\sum|c_k|^2
\]
for every finitely supported coefficient family. Syndetic means bounded gaps in \(\Z\). The question is to construct a full finite partition of the integer frequency set into such classes, with a specified effective input model for \(S\).

The candidate rational-grid synthesis takes
\[
S_A=\bigcup_{a\in A}[a/N,(a+1)/N),\quad
\varnothing\ne A\subseteq\{0,\ldots,N-1\},\quad s=|A|.
\]

\[
\Lambda_j=B_j+N\Z
\]
gives periodic syndetic Riesz classes. The exact Riesz bounds are the least and greatest squared singular values of \(V_{A,B_j}\), divided by \(N\). The report claims the number \(\lceil N/s\rceil\) is globally optimal, not merely optimal among the displayed periodic constructions.
