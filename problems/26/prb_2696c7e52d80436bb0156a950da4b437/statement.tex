\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 10600020}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Represent a virtual knot \(K\) by an embedded circle \(\kappa:S^1\hookrightarrow S_g\times I\), where \(S_g\) is a closed oriented genus-\(g\) surface and \(I=[0,1]\), modulo the usual stable surface equivalence: isotopy, surface identifications and stabilization/destabilization away from the knot. Its supporting genus \(g(K)\) is the least genus of such a realization. A minimal realization has \(g=g(K)\).

The assertion requested in the source is
\[\begin{split}
\forall K\ne U_{\mathrm{vir}},\ \exists\kappa:S^1\hookrightarrow S_{g(K)}\times I,\
\exists e:S_{g(K)}\times I\hookrightarrow\R^3:\qquad\\
\kappa\text{ represents }K,\quad e\text{ is unknotted},\quad
[e\circ\kappa]\ne[U_{\mathrm{cl}}].
\end{split}\]
Here \(U_{\mathrm{vir}}\) is the virtual unknot, \(U_{\mathrm{cl}}\) the classical unknot, and the final inequality is classical ambient-isotopy inequivalence in \(\R^3\). Under the standard reading, an unknotted embedding of the thickened surface is an embedding as a collar of a standard unknotted genus-\(g\) surface, up to ambient isotopy. The excerpt does not define that embedding class, so it must be confirmed rather than replaced by an arbitrary embedding. The unknotted requirement is on the thickened surface; the resulting knot is required to be nontrivial.

The quantifiers permit choosing both a minimal realization and a suitable unknotted ambient embedding for each \(K\). They do not require every minimal realization or every unknotted embedding to produce a nontrivial classical knot. The source reports a partial solution by Dye, but the relevant citation and scope are missing.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Every nontrivial virtual knot should have a realization of minimal supporting genus whose thickened surface can be embedded unknottedly in three-space so that the resulting classical knot remains nontrivial.
