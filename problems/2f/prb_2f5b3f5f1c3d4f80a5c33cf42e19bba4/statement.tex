\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $V$ be a finite vertex set and $G_0=(V,\mathcal E_0)$ a hypergraph. For a hypergraph $H=(V,\mathcal E_H)$, define its cut degree and rank by
\[
 d_H(X)=\#\{e\in\mathcal E_H:e\cap X\ne\varnothing,
                              \ e\cap(V\setminus X)\ne\varnothing\},
 \qquad \operatorname{rank}(H)=\max_{e\in\mathcal E_H}|e|,
\]
counting multiplicities if parallel hyperedges are permitted. The source does not specify that convention or the rank of an edgeless hypergraph.

The input is a crossing negamodular integer-valued function $R:2^V\to\Z$ satisfying
\[
 R(X)\ne1\qquad\text{for every }X\subseteq V.
\]
With a commonly used supermodular-sign convention, crossing negamodularity means
\[
 R(A)+R(B)\leq R(A\setminus B)+R(B\setminus A)
\]
for crossing pairs $A,B$, customarily requiring all four regions $A\cap B$, $A\setminus B$, $B\setminus A$, and $V\setminus(A\cup B)$ nonempty. The precise sign and crossing-pair convention are not defined in the supplied excerpt and must be checked against the source.

\textbf{Optimization problem.} Find an added hypergraph $G=(V,\mathcal E)$ of minimum total size such that
\[
 d_G(X)\geq R(X)-d_{G_0}(X)\quad\text{for every }X\subseteq V,
 \qquad \operatorname{rank}(G)\leq\operatorname{rank}(G_0).
\]
Thus it is the added hypergraph whose size is minimized, and the augmented cut requirements are $d_G(X)+d_{G_0}(X)\geq R(X)$. The source says ``total size'' without defining whether this means $\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}|e|$, number of hyperedges, or another cost. No one objective is silently selected.
