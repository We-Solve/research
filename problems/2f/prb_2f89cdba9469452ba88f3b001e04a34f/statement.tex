\begin{enumerate}
\item Describe the space of geodesic currents for \(M_{g,m}\).
\item Are weighted sums of Dirac masses at pairs of fixed points of pseudo-Anosov elements dense in that space? For a pseudo-Anosov \(f\), write its endpoints as \((f^-,f^+)\in Y\). The relevant atomic currents must satisfy the chosen invariance and finiteness conditions; a single Dirac mass at one ordered pair is generally not a \(G\)-invariant measure. The excerpt does not specify orbit sums, weights, convergence, ordered versus unordered pairs, or the topology in which density is requested.
\item Does there exist an absolutely continuous current \(\mu\) in the source's precise null-set sense: is there a \(G\)-invariant measure class \([\mu_0]\) on \(X\) such that for every Borel \(A\subseteq X\),
\[
 \mu_0(A)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad

\]
An invariant measure class means that the action preserves its null sets, not necessarily a selected measure. The displayed condition compares the first marginal's null sets with \([\mu_0]\); it is not replaced by absolute continuity with respect to \(\mu_0\times\mu_0\).
\item Is there a distinguished such current whose invariant boundary measure class is determined by Hausdorff measure for one of the source's boundary distance functions \(\delta\)? The choice of \(\delta\), Hausdorff dimension or gauge, and meaning of ``distinguished'' are not specified. No uniqueness or normalization is added.
