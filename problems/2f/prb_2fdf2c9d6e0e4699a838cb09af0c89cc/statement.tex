Fix $k\geq2$. Among all red--blue colourings of $[n]$, minimise the number of monochromatic arithmetic progressions of length $k$, counting each progression with positive common difference once. Determine or sharply estimate the optimal asymptotic coefficient $\delta_k$ in the universal lower bound
\[\#\{\text{monochromatic }k\text{-term progressions}\}\geq (\delta_k+o(1))n^2.\]
An asymptotic description of $\delta_k$ as $k$ grows is requested.
