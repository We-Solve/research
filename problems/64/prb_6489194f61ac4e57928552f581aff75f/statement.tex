\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathbb H_{\mathbb C}^2\) be complex hyperbolic two-space, whose holomorphic isometry group is \(\operatorname{PU}(2,1)\) and whose visual boundary is topologically \(S^3\). For a discrete subgroup \(G\le\operatorname{PU}(2,1)\), its limit set \(\Lambda(G)\subseteq\partial_\infty\mathbb H_{\mathbb C}^2\) is the set of boundary accumulation points of an orbit \(Gx\). Use the usual convex-cocompact condition: the quotient by \(G\) of the closed geodesic convex hull of \(\Lambda(G)\) in \(\mathbb H_{\mathbb C}^2\) is compact.

Let \(\mathcal C\) be the standard Sierpi\'nski carpet, obtained from a closed square by repeatedly deleting the open middle square of the subdivision of each remaining square into nine equal squares. Does there exist a convex-cocompact discrete subgroup \(G\le\operatorname{PU}(2,1)\) such that
\[
 \Lambda(G)\cong\mathcal C?
\]
The equivalence requested is a homeomorphism of topological spaces. No torsion-free condition, prescribed embedding of the carpet in \(S^3\), or metric equivalence is added.
