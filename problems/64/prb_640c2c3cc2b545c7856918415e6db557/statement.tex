\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(p\) be a prime, and define the localization
\[
\Z_{(p)}=\left\{\frac{m}{d}:m,d\in\Z,\ d\ne0,\ p\nmid d\right\}\subseteq\Q.
\]
For \(x\in\Q\), put
\[
\gamma_p(x)=\frac{x^p-x}{p\bigl((x^p-x)^2-1\bigr)},
\qquad
S_p=\gamma_p(\Q)=\{\gamma_p(x):x\in\Q\}.
\]
This rational function is defined at every rational number: a rational solution of \(x^p-x=\pm1\) would be an integer by the rational root theorem, whereas \(x^p-x\equiv0\pmod p\) for every integer \(x\).
Let \(R_p=\Z_{(p)}[S_p]\) denote the smallest subring of \(\Q\) containing \(\Z_{(p)}\) and \(S_p\), and define
\[
\Gamma_p(\Q)=\left\{\frac{a}{1+pb}:a,b\in R_p,\ 1+pb\ne0\right\}.
\]
For a positive integer \(n\), define the \(n\)-fold sumset
\[
\Sigma_n(S_p)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}S_p
=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\gamma_p(x_i):x_1,\ldots,x_n\in\Q\right\}.
\]
Repetitions are allowed; since \(\gamma_p(0)=0\), sums with fewer than \(n\) terms can be padded with zeros.
