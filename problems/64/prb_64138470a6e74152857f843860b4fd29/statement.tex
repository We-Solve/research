\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
In the $\delta$-distance game, Peter chooses a leaving vertex of a simplex in the unit ball, and Roland chooses a replacement vertex that preserves the $\delta$-distance property and crosses the opposite face; the game ends when the simplex contains the origin.
