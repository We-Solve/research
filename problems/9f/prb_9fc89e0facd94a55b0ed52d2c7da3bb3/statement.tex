\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 6200007}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $Z$ be a compact metrizable space and let a group $G$ act on $Z$ by homeomorphisms. Under the usual discrete-group convention, the action is a convergence action if its induced action on
\[
Z^{(3)}=\{(z_1,z_2,z_3)\in Z^3:z_i\ne z_j\text{ for }i\ne j\}
\]
is properly discontinuous: for every compact $K\subset Z^{(3)}$, only finitely many $g\in G$ satisfy $gK\cap K\ne\varnothing$. Assume every $G$-orbit is dense in $Z$. The source calls this topological transitivity; its explicit every-orbit condition is retained, rather than the weaker existence of a dense orbit.

Does there exist a geodesic Gromov-hyperbolic space $X$ with ideal boundary homeomorphic to $Z$ and a uniformly quasi-isometric quasi-action extending the given boundary action? More precisely, seek constants $K\geq1$, $C,A\geq0$, maps $q_g:X\to X$ for $g\in G$, and a homeomorphism $\iota:Z\to\partial X$ such that each $q_g$ is a $(K,C)$ quasi-isometry and
\[
\sup_x d_X(q_e x,x)\leq A,\qquad
\sup_x d_X(q_g(q_hx),q_{gh}x)\leq A\qquad(g,h\in G),
\]
while the induced boundary maps satisfy
\[
\partial q_g\circ\iota=\iota\circ g\qquad(g\in G).
\]
A $(K,C)$ quasi-isometry obeys $K^{-1}d(x,y)-C\leq d(q_gx,q_gy)\leq Kd(x,y)+C$ and has image within distance $C$ of every point of $X$. No properness, cocompactness, finite generation, or isometric action requirement is imposed by the source.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every convergence action on a compact metrizable space with all orbits dense arise as the boundary action of a uniformly controlled quasi-action on a Gromov-hyperbolic space?
