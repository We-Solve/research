\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
In the standard smooth interpretation, an Engel manifold is a pair \((M,\mathcal D)\), where \(M\) is a smooth four-dimensional manifold and \(\mathcal D\subset TM\) is a smooth rank-two distribution satisfying
\[
\mathcal D^2=\mathcal D+[\mathcal D,\mathcal D],\qquad
\mathcal D^3=\mathcal D^2+[\mathcal D,\mathcal D^2],\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal D^2)=3,\quad \mathcal D^3=TM.
\]
Here brackets of distributions denote the pointwise spans of Lie brackets of their local smooth sections. Under this interpretation, define
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(M,\mathcal D)
=\{f\in\operatorname{Diff}^{\infty}(M):
\dd f_x(\mathcal D_x)=\mathcal D_{f(x)}\text{ for every }x\in M\}.
\]
The supplied Question 10, attributed there to a suggestion of Yoshi Mitsumatsu, asks whether a generic Engel manifold satisfies
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(M,\mathcal D)=\{\operatorname{id}_M\}.
\]
