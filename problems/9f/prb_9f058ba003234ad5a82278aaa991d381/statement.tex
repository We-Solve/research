A stable pair requires semi-log-canonical singularities and an ample $\Q$-Cartier log canonical divisor. A chosen divisor marking and its coefficient are extra data beyond an abstract polarization and are not fixed by the original item.

The report describes existing KSBA compactifications in every K3 degree and an explicit degree-four Type II nef-$\lambda$-family/Tyurin sector, while saying a complete marked-hyperplane/Type III classification was not located. It gives no references, definition of the $\lambda$-family, or assessment date. These are supplied scope-limited literature claims, not a complete classification.

Its new special case concerns a reduced simple-normal-crossing factorized quartic
\[
 X_0=V(F_{d_1}\cdots F_{d_s})\subset\PP^3,\qquad
 \sum_i d_i=4,
\]
with smooth components meeting with simple normal crossings, and a general Cartier hyperplane section $H$. The report claims that $(X_0,\epsilon H)$ is Gorenstein KSBA stable and $\Q$-Gorenstein smoothable for every rational $0<\epsilon\leq1$. The four reducible degree partitions are $1+3$, $2+2$, $1+1+2$ and $1+1+1+1$. For a quartic hypersurface $K_{X_0}\sim0$, so the asserted ample log canonical divisor is $\epsilon H$. The full interval and the generality/transversality of $H$ are essential.

The relative log canonical model is reported to contract back to the raw reducible quartic family. The exact blowup center, family hypotheses and contraction construction are not supplied.
