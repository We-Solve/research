\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a finite group and \(k\) a field. A \(kG\)-module \(V\) is faithful if its action homomorphism \(G\to\operatorname{GL}_k(V)\) is injective, and absolutely irreducible if \(\overline{k}\otimes_k V\) is irreducible over \(\overline{k}G\), where \(\overline{k}\) is an algebraic closure of \(k\). Define
\[
Z^1(G,V)=\{f:G\to V: f(gh)=f(g)+g\cdot f(h)\text{ for all }g,h\in G\},
\]
\[
B^1(G,V)=\{f_v:G\to V: f_v(g)=g\cdot v-v\text{ for some }v\in V\},
\qquad H^1(G,V)=Z^1(G,V)/B^1(G,V).
\]
