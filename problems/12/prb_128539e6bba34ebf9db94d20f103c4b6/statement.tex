\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Gamma\) be discrete and let \(L:\Gamma\to[0,\infty)\) be a symmetric subadditive length, with \(L(e)=0\). Write \(\lambda\) for the left regular representation, \(C_r^*(\Gamma)\) for its norm closure and \(\tau(a)=\langle a\delta_e,\delta_e\rangle\). Rapid decay (RD) with respect to \(L\) means that for some \(C\ge1,s\ge0\),
\[
 \|\lambda(f)\|\le C\|f\|_{2,s},\qquad
 \|f\|_{2,s}^2=\sum_g|f(g)|^2(1+L(g))^{2s}
\]
for every finitely supported \(f\). The source contains five questions, not just the first.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Let \(D\delta_g=L(g)\delta_g\) on its natural dense domain, and let \(\delta(a)=[D,a]\) wherever that commutator has a bounded extension. Determine when the Connes state-space distance
\[
 d_D(\varphi,\psi)=\sup\{|\varphi(a)-\psi(a)|:a=a^*,\ \|\delta(a)\|\le1\}
\]
induces the weak-* topology. The source, citing Rieffel [Ri], uses norm precompactness of the trace-zero commutator unit ball as the criterion and reports the result for \(\Z^d\) [Ri] and hyperbolic groups [OR]. It also cites [AC1] for precompactness of higher iterated-commutator unit balls for all sufficiently large orders under RD. Appropriate length and domain hypotheses are essential; the claim is not for every arbitrary length on those groups.
\item Determine when
\[
 C_r^*(\Gamma)=C_u^*(|\Gamma|)\cap\mathcal N(\Gamma).
\]
Here, for a proper length, use the right-invariant metric \(d(g,h)=L(gh^{-1})\), \(C_u^*\) is the norm closure of bounded finite-propagation operators on \(\ell^2\Gamma\), and \(\mathcal N(\Gamma)=\lambda(\Gamma)''\). This convention makes the displayed intersection the right-translation-invariant part of the uniform Roe algebra. The source cites Roe's book [Ro] and reports the equality for RD with a conditionally negative length, meaning the kernel \(L(gh^{-1})\) is conditionally negative definite, equivalently its square-root distance has the Hilbert-space realization described there. The aid warns that this ordinary invariant-translation approximation property is distinct from stronger coefficient or tensor versions.
\item For finitely generated torsion-free \(\Gamma\) with RD, prove the Kaplansky zero-divisor assertion: \(ab\ne0\) for nonzero \(a,b\in\C\Gamma\). The source's motivation is Lafforgue [La2], giving trivial idempotents under RD and additional unspecified geometric hypotheses. Trivial idempotents alone do not prove absence of zero divisors.
\item For finitely generated RD \(\Gamma\) and any finitely supported symmetric probability \(\nu\), ask whether constants \(c>0,d\ge0\) give
\[
 \nu^{*2n}(e)\sim c n^{-d}\rho^{2n},\qquad \rho=\|\lambda(\nu)\|.
\]
No assumption that the support generates all of \(\Gamma\) is added. The even-time convolution notation repairs the supplied extraction.
\item Investigate a relation between RD and entropy of ergodic actions on measure spaces. No entropy notion, action class, measure convention or proposed relation is specified.
\end{enumerate}

The attached aid gives two quantitative partial results. Assume \(L\) is proper and positive away from \(e\), and set
\[
 \mathcal L_k=\{a\in\operatorname{Dom}(\delta^k):\tau(a)=0,\ \|\delta^k(a)\|\le1\},\qquad
 E_R=\operatorname{span}\{\lambda(g):L(g)\le R\}.
\]
For integer \(k>s\) and \(R\ge1\), it reports
\[
 \sup_{a\in\mathcal L_k}\operatorname{dist}(a,E_R)\le C2^sR^{s-k},
\]
which yields norm total boundedness. A higher-order result does not establish the first-commutator case for arbitrary RD exponent.

If \(\operatorname{supp}\nu\) is in the radius-\(R_0\) ball, the aid reports
\[
 C^{-2}(1+nR_0)^{-2s}\rho^{2n}\le\nu^{*2n}(e)\le\rho^{2n}.
\]
The normalized sequence \(r_n=\nu^{*2n}(e)/\rho^{2n}\) is \(r_n=\int_0^1t^n\,d\eta(t)\), where \(\eta\) is the pushforward of the spectral measure of \(\lambda(\nu)/\rho\) at \(\delta_e\) under squaring. The reported pure-power equivalent is equivalent to
\[
 \eta([1-\epsilon,1])\sim\frac{c\epsilon^d}{\Gamma(d+1)}\quad(\epsilon\downarrow0),
\]
with \(\Gamma(d+1)\) the Euler gamma function. The polynomial window alone does not prove this spectral-edge regular variation. The summary mentions 2026 RD-entropy results without stating them or supplying citations; no definite entropy theorem can be recovered from that sentence. The appended ``Which one of these groups have RD?'' is identified by the supplied verification as the next section's heading, not a sixth specified question.
