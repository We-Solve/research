Let $T_t f(x)=f(x-t)$ on $L^2(\R)$, fix an integer $n\geq2$, and let $\varphi_1,\ldots,\varphi_r\in L^2(\R)$ with $r<\infty$. Set
\[
 V=\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{T_k\varphi_j:
                    k\in\Z,\ 1\leq j\leq r\}.
\]
Thus finite generation is by integer translates and closure is in $L^2$, with no frame, Riesz-basis, support or regularity assumption added. The target is necessary-and-sufficient, appropriately checkable conditions on the generators or intrinsic data of $V$ for
