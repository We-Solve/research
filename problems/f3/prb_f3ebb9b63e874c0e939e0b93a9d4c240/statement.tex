Erdős Problem 399 concerns representations $n! = x^k \pm y^k$. This attack targets the
research-solved Cambie variant shipped as `sorry` in formal-conjectures: for coprime
$x, y$ with $xy > 1$,
\[
n! \ne x^4 + y^4 .
\]
Lean statement:
\begin{verbatim}
theorem cambie {n x y : N} (hxy : x.Coprime y) (h1 : 1 < x * y) :
    n ! != x ^ 4 + y ^ 4
\end{verbatim}
