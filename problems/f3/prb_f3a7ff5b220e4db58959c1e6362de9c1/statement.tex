\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For finite $A,B\subseteq\Z$, define the representation count
\[
\nu(n)=\#\{(a,b)\in A\times B:a+b=n\}=(\mathbf1_A*\mathbf1_B)(n).
\]
It has total mass $|A||B|$. Sort its positive values as $r_1\ge r_2\ge\cdots$, extending by zeros. These are the column heights of its Ferrers diagram. The corrected source condition is
\[
r_k^2\le\sum_{j\ge k}r_j\qquad(k\ge1).
\]
V. Lev's question asks for other general constraints on integer-set representation functions, beyond that sorted-spectrum condition. For row lengths $N_t=\#\{n:\nu(n)\ge t\}$, the source also attributes to Pollard
\[
\sum_{i=1}^tN_i\ge t(|A|+|B|-t),\qquad1\le t\le\min(|A|,|B|),
\]
and says it follows as well from the column condition. Missing cardinality signs and the split square in the extraction are corrected explicitly.

The attached aid gives a location-sensitive condition. For each $n$ let $R_n=A\cap(n-B)$ and, for $d\ge1$, let
\[
q_n(d)=\#\{a\in R_n:a+d\in R_n\}.
\]
Its stronger fiberwise bounds and their sum are
\[
\nu(n-d)\ge q_n(d),\qquad\nu(n+d)\ge q_n(d),
\]
\[
\sum_{d\ge1}\min\{\nu(n-d),\nu(n+d)\}\ge\binom{\nu(n)}2.
\]
All sums are finite. Each positive-difference fiber pair produces a distinct crossed representation at both reflected locations.

For every finite $R\subset\Z$, the choice $A=R$, $B=n-R$ realizes equality with the full difference profile: $\nu(n)=|R|$ and $\nu(n-d)=\nu(n+d)=\#\{a\in R:a+d\in R\}$. Thus the condition is sharp for all fiber sizes and realized positive-difference profiles. It contains information lost by sorting. For example the finitely supported proposed function with values $\nu(0)=2$, $\nu(1)=\nu(2)=1$ has sorted profile $(2,1,1)$ satisfying every column inequality, but violates the mirrored inequality at $0$. The aid provides a new necessary condition, not a complete characterization of all realizable representation functions.
