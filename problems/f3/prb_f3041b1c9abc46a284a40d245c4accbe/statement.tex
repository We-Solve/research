\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathcal C\) be a Noetherian stable homotopy category in the precise sense of the source's memoir, with sphere/unit object \(S\), and let \(R=\pi_*S\) be its graded coefficient ring. The conjecture asks for a bijection between the intended thick subcategories and specialization-closed subsets of \(\operatorname{Spec}R\).

A thick subcategory is full, triangulated and closed under retracts. For the standard graded interpretation, \(\operatorname{Spec}^hR\) consists of homogeneous prime ideals, and specialization-closed means \(\mathfrak p\in Z\), \(\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak q\) imply \(\mathfrak q\in Z\). The usual support formulation would use
\[

 \mathcal T\longmapsto\bigcup_{X\in\mathcal T}\operatorname{supp}_R(X),

with inverse \(Z\mapsto\{X:\operatorname{supp}_R(X)\subseteq Z\}\) inside the intended object class.
