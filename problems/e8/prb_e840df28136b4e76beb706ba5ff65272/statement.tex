\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A number field is a finite extension of \(\Q\). Let \(k\) be a number field, fix an algebraic closure \(\overline{k}\), and write \(G_k=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{k}/k)\), with its profinite topology. For a \(k\)-curve \(X\), let \(X(k)\) denote its set of \(k\)-rational points.

The supplied formulation does not explicitly require geometric connectedness. Under the customary interpretation that the curve is geometrically connected, let \(X\) be a smooth, projective, geometrically connected curve over \(k\) with genus \(g(X)\geq 2\). Choose a geometric base point \(\overline{x}\) over \(\overline{k}\), and set \(X_{\overline{k}}=X\times_k\overline{k}\). Interpret the supplied notation \(\pi_1(X/k)\) as the arithmetic étale fundamental group in the exact sequence of profinite groups
\[
1\longrightarrow\pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X_{\overline{k}},\overline{x})
\longrightarrow\pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\overline{x})
\xrightarrow{p}G_k\longrightarrow 1.
\]
A continuous section is a continuous group homomorphism
\[
s:G_k\longrightarrow\pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\overline{x})
\qquad\text{such that}\qquad p\circ s=\operatorname{id}_{G_k}.
\]

\textbf{Weak Section Conjecture over Number Fields (supplied formulation).} For every number field \(k\) and every curve \(X/k\) satisfying the preceding hypotheses, is the equivalence
\[
X(k)\neq\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\,\text{a continuous group homomorphism }s:G_k\to\pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\overline{x})
\text{ with }p\circ s=\operatorname{id}_{G_k}
\]
true?

Geometric connectedness is an explicit interpretive qualification here and requires confirmation against the source. The target concerns existence of a section; it does not assert a bijection between rational points and conjugacy classes of sections.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The supplied weak section conjecture asks whether a smooth projective curve of genus at least \(2\) over a number field has a rational point exactly when its arithmetic étale fundamental-group projection admits a continuous group-theoretic section. The formulation uses the customary geometrically connected interpretation of a curve, which the supplied text leaves implicit.
