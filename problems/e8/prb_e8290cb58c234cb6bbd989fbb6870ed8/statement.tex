\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $f$ and $g$ be degree-$d$ multilinear real polynomials on $\{-1,1\}^n$ such that every variable has relative influence at most $\delta$ in both polynomials. Can $\delta$ be chosen inverse-polynomially in $d$ while still guaranteeing a point $x\in\{-1,1\}^n$ for which $f(x)\neq0$ and $g(x)\neq0$? \sref{30006210}{2}
