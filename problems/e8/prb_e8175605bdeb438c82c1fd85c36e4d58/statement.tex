\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A finite simple graph $G=(V,E)$ is normal if it has a family $\mathcal Q$ of cliques and a family $\mathcal S$ of stable (independent) sets such that
\[
 \bigcup_{Q\in\mathcal Q}Q=V=\bigcup_{S\in\mathcal S}S,
 \qquad Q\cap S\ne\varnothing
       \quad\forall Q\in\mathcal Q,\ S\in\mathcal S.
\]
These are vertex covers by sets, not edge-clique covers or necessarily partitions. A clique and stable set can share at most one vertex, so each required intersection is exactly one point; maximality of the covering sets is not assumed.
