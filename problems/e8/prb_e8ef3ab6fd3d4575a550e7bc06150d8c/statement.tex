\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A convex body in \(\R^n\) means a compact convex set with nonempty interior. Write \(\operatorname{vol}_n\) for Lebesgue measure on \(\R^n\), and \(\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}\) for the Euclidean volume induced on an affine hyperplane. For \(n=1\), interpret \(\operatorname{vol}_0\) as counting measure. An affine hyperplane has the form
\[
H(u,t)=\{x\in\R^n:\langle x,u\rangle=t\},\qquad u\in S^{n-1},\quad t\in\R,
\]
where \(S^{n-1}=\{u\in\R^n:\|u\|_2=1\}\).

\exists c>0\ \forall n\in\Z_{\geq 1}\ \forall K\subset\R^n\text{ a convex body},\qquad
\operatorname{vol}_n(K)=1

\exists u\in S^{n-1}\ \exists t\in\R:\quad
\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}\bigl(K\cap H(u,t)\bigr)\geq c.
