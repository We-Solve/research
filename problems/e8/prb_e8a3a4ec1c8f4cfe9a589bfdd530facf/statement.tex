\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each integer \(n\geq 1\), let \(S_n\) be the group of permutations of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\). A generating set \(X_n\subseteq S_n\) determines the undirected Cayley graph with edges joining \(g\) to \(gx\) for \(x\in X_n\cup X_n^{-1}\). Its graph metric is the word metric
\[
d_n(g,h)=\min\bigl\{k\geq 0:g^{-1}h=x_1\cdots x_k,\quad x_i\in X_n\cup X_n^{-1}\bigr\}.
\]
Here the empty product is the identity.

Under the usual unweighted convention, a wall on \(S_n\) is an unordered partition \(\{A,S_n\setminus A\}\) into two nonempty subsets. A wall structure \(\mathcal W_n\) is a collection of walls such that every pair of points is separated by finitely many walls and every pair of distinct points is separated by at least one wall. Its wall metric is
\[
d_{\mathcal W_n}(g,h)=\#\bigl\{W\in\mathcal W_n: g\text{ and }h\text{ lie in different parts of }W\bigr\}.
\]
The walls are partitions of the vertex set; no compatibility of their parts with edges or group invariance is imposed in this interpretation.

\textbf{Question (supplied as Problem 2(i)).} Do there exist an integer \(D\geq 1\), generating sets \(X_n\subseteq S_n\), wall structures \(\mathcal W_n\), and nondecreasing functions
\[
\rho_-,\rho_+:[0,\infty)\longrightarrow[0,\infty),
\qquad \lim_{t\to\infty}\rho_-(t)=\infty,
\]
such that \(|X_n|\leq D\) for every \(n\geq 1\) and
\[
\rho_-\bigl(d_n(g,h)\bigr)
\leq d_{\mathcal W_n}(g,h)
\leq \rho_+\bigl(d_n(g,h)\bigr)
\qquad
\text{for all }n\geq 1\text{ and }g,h\in S_n?
\]
The same control functions must work for every \(n\); the comparison is through the identity map on each vertex set. This spells out the supplied clean statement's requirement of uniform coarse equivalence.
