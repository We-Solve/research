\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Gamma\leq \operatorname{SL}_2(\C)\) be an arithmetic lattice. Here a lattice is a discrete subgroup for which \(\operatorname{SL}_2(\C)/\Gamma\) has finite invariant volume; arithmeticity is understood in its usual sense for lattices in this Lie group. Let \(p\) be any prime, and let \(\F_p=\Z/p\Z\).

Interpret a normal chain of \(p\)-power-index subgroups as a descending sequence
\[
\Gamma\geq\Gamma_1\geq\Gamma_2\geq\cdots,
\qquad \Gamma_i\trianglelefteq\Gamma,
\qquad [\Gamma:\Gamma_i]=p^{a_i},\quad a_i\in\Z_{\geq0},
\]
with
\[
\bigcap_{i\geq1}\Gamma_i=\{1\}.
\]
Take \(H^1(\Gamma_i,\F_p)\) to mean group cohomology with trivial action on the coefficient module \(\F_p\). Thus
\[
H^1(\Gamma_i,\F_p)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\Gamma_i,(\F_p,+)),
\]
and its dimension is taken over \(\F_p\).

\textbf{Question.} For every arithmetic lattice \(\Gamma\leq\operatorname{SL}_2(\C)\), every prime \(p\), and every chain satisfying these conditions, is it true that
\[
\lim_{i\to\infty}
\frac{\dim_{\F_p}H^1(\Gamma_i,\F_p)}{[\Gamma:\Gamma_i]}=0?
\]
No cocompactness, torsion-freeness, or congruence-subgroup hypothesis is stated in the supplied record. The conventions that every \(\Gamma_i\) is normal in \(\Gamma\) and that coefficients have trivial action make standard implicit terminology explicit; their agreement with the intended source formulation requires review.

The supplied dated curation labels this question open and states that results for selected \(p\)-adic analytic towers give asymptotics without establishing the assertion for arbitrary chains. This is a supplied assessment, not an independently verified mathematical or literature conclusion.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every arithmetic lattice in \(\operatorname{SL}_2(\C)\) and every prime \(p\), must the dimension of first cohomology over \(\F_p\), divided by subgroup index, tend to zero along every descending normal chain of \(p\)-power index with trivial intersection? Normality is interpreted relative to the original lattice, and coefficients are interpreted as trivial. The supplied open-status assessment requires independent review.
