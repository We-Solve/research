\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701419}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a prime \(p\), a finite \(p\)-group is a finite group whose order is a power of \(p\). A subgroup \(H\leq G\) has exponent \(p\) if \(H\neq\{1\}\) and \(h^p=1\) for every \(h\in H\). Write \([G:H]\) for its index. A subgroup \(C\leq G\) is characteristic if \(\alpha(C)=C\) for every \(\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(G)\).

Is there a function
\[
f:\{p\in\N:p\text{ is prime}\}\longrightarrow\N_{>0}
\]
such that, for every prime \(p\) and every finite \(p\)-group \(G\),
\[
\bigl(\exists H\leq G:\ \exp(H)=p\ \text{and}\ [G:H]=p\bigr)
\ \Longrightarrow\
\bigl(\exists C\leq G:\ C\text{ is characteristic in }G,\ \exp(C)=p,\ [G:C]\leq f(p)\bigr)?
\]
The bound must depend only on \(p\), uniformly over all such groups \(G\).
\subsection{Short English statement}
E. I. Khukhro asks whether every finite \(p\)-group with an exponent-\(p\) subgroup of index \(p\) has a characteristic exponent-\(p\) subgroup whose index is bounded solely in terms of \(p\).
