\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S^2\) be the two-dimensional sphere. Under the standard smooth, strongly convex convention, a Finsler metric is a function \(F:TS^2\to[0,\infty)\), positive on nonzero tangent vectors, smooth away from the zero section, positively homogeneous of degree one in each tangent space, and such that the fibrewise Hessian of \(\tfrac12F^2\) is positive definite away from the zero section. The supplied record does not specify regularity conventions or require reversibility, namely \(F(x,v)=F(x,-v)\).

Write \(K_F(x,v)\) for the flag curvature at a nonzero tangent vector \(v\in T_xS^2\). A metric has constant flag curvature \(K\) if \(K_F(x,v)=K\) for every such \((x,v)\). Use the standard convention that \(F\) is Zoll if every unit-speed geodesic is periodic and all have the same least positive period. A Killing vector field is a smooth vector field \(X\) whose local flow \(\phi_t\) preserves \(F\):
\[
F\bigl(\phi_t(x),d\phi_t|_x(v)\bigr)=F(x,v)
\]
whenever the flow is defined. The phrase ``no Killing vector field'' is understood to mean no nonzero Killing vector field.

\textbf{Construction problem, literal final-sentence formulation.} Construct a Finsler metric \(F\) on \(S^2\) and a constant \(K\in\R\) such that
\[
K_F(x,v)=K\quad\text{for every }x\in S^2,\ v\in T_xS^2\setminus\{0\},
\qquad F\text{ is not Zoll},
\]
and every Killing vector field of \(F\) is identically zero.
