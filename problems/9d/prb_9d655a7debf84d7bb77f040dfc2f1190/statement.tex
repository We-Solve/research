\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The supplied question concerns Kropina metrics but does not specify their defining conventions. Under the usual smooth, globally defined convention, let \(M\) be a closed smooth manifold (compact and without boundary), let \(g\) be a smooth Riemannian metric, and let \(\beta\) be a smooth nowhere-vanishing one-form. Set
\[
\mathcal A=\{(x,v)\in TM:\beta_x(v)>0\},\qquad F(x,v)=\frac{g_x(v,v)}{\beta_x(v)}\quad ((x,v)\in\mathcal A).
\]
Thus \(F=\alpha^2/\beta\), where \(\alpha(x,v)=\sqrt{g_x(v,v)}\); this is the Kropina metric on its admissible cone \(\mathcal A\).

An admissible closed geodesic means a nonconstant smooth map \(\gamma:\R/T\Z\to M\), for some \(T>0\), satisfying \(\beta_{\gamma(t)}(\dot\gamma(t))>0\) for every \(t\), and stationary for the energy
\[
E_F(\gamma)=\frac12\int_0^T F(\gamma(t),\dot\gamma(t))^2\,\dd t
\]
under every smooth periodic variation through admissible curves, with \(T\) fixed.

\textbf{Question (Burns--Matveev, Question 7, supplied attribution 2019).} Does every Kropina metric on a closed manifold admit an admissible closed geodesic? Under the convention above, this asks whether, for every such \((M,g,\beta)\), there exist \(T>0\) and \(\gamma\) with the stated properties.
