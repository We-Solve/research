\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X^{(j)}=(X_1^{(j)},\ldots,X_n^{(j)})\) be tuples converging to \(X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\) in the source's intended topology, and let \(\delta\) be its intended free entropy dimension. The exact proposed lower-semicontinuity statement is
\[
\delta(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\leq
\liminf_{j\to\infty}\delta(X_1^{(j)},\ldots,X_n^{(j)}).
\]
It is to hold for all admissible convergent sequences and all allowed tuple sizes, after their common domain and convergence notion have been fixed. The inequality direction is part of the question; it is not upper semicontinuity.

Free entropy dimensions are ordinarily attached to tuples in a tracial noncommutative probability space, commonly self-adjoint elements of a finite von Neumann algebra \((\mathcal M,\tau)\). Several distinct invariants occur in this setting, including the original microstates free entropy dimension, modified microstates dimension \(\delta_0\), and nonmicrostates variants. The source writes only \(\delta\) and reproduces no definition, so none of these alternatives is silently substituted or identified with the others.

The mode of convergence is also absent. Allowing tuples in different algebras, imposing uniform operator-norm bounds, and requiring microstates or embeddability can affect the precise statement and its domain. The draft does not choose the weakest or strongest of these possibilities.
