Conventionally these are oriented \(3\)-dimensional cobordisms with two boundary markings by \(F\), each inducing integral homology isomorphisms, with suitable agreement along \(\partial F\). Let \(\sim_{Y_d}\) denote the source's degree-\(d\) clasper equivalence, and let \(\mathcal F^Y_\star\) denote its finite-type filtration.
