\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
This is the continuation of Problem 23, not Problem 2. Its preceding companion supplies the real-cube definitions. For $a\in S^{n-1}$ and $t\geq0$ set
\[
 V_\infty(a,t)=\operatorname{vol}_n\{x\in[-1,1]^n:|\langle x,a\rangle|\leq t\},\quad
 A_\infty(a,s)=\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}\{x\in[-1,1]^n:\langle x,a\rangle=s\},
\]
and $f_m=(1,\ldots,1,0,\ldots,0)/\sqrt m$ with $m$ nonzero coordinates. The raw coordinate central section has volume $2^{n-1}$, so the source's bounds $1\leq A(a,0)\leq\sqrt2$ must be interpreted with normalized section volume, not literally with this raw $A_\infty$. (a) V. Milman asks whether, for every fixed $t>0$, $\min_{a\in S^{n-1}}V_\infty(a,t)$ is attained at some standard vector $f_m$, up to cube symmetries.

(b) Does the minimum equal $V_\infty(f_1,t)$ for $0<t\leq2\sqrt2-2$? The source attributes the established range $t\leq3/4$ to F. Barthe and A. Koldobsky. It reports the complex analogue for $t\leq4/5$ (H. Koenig) and asks about extending it to a source-labeled threshold $t_0\approx0.867$. Its defining equation is OCR-damaged, including the left-hand string ``$\pi t\ 2$'' and square-root/arcsine grouping; it is not assigned an invented repaired equation here. The complex slab, volume, and parameter conventions need source review.

(c) H. Koenig asks for the optimal lower constant in a negative-moment Khintchine inequality. For integers $m\geq2$, independent $X_j$ uniform on $S^{m-1}\subseteq\R^m$, and $-(m-1)<p<0$, define
\[
 B_{m,p}=\inf_{r\geq1,\ a\in\R^r\setminus\{0\}}
 \frac{(\E\|\sum_{j=1}^r a_jX_j\|_2^p)^{1/p}}{\|a\|_2}.
\]
The target is this best constant, written $c_p^{-1}$ in the source with dimension dependence suppressed. The $p$-moment expression is not an ordinary norm for negative $p$. The aid reports two necessary sharp benchmarks, but their formulas are not supplied.

(d) With $B_1^n=\{x:\sum_i|x_i|\leq1\}$, find integral formulas for all slab volumes
$V_1(a,t)=\operatorname{vol}_n(B_1^n\cap\{|\langle x,a\rangle|\leq t\})$
and noncentral section volumes $A_1(a,s)=\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(B_1^n\cap\{\langle x,a\rangle=s\})$, and determine slab-extremizing directions. Here $a$ is unit, $t\geq0$, and $s\in\R$.

The aid supplies partial formulas and a two-dimensional resolution of the last minimum. For real-cube standard competitors, if
\[
 F_m(u)=\frac1{m!}\sum_{j=0}^m(-1)^j\binom mj(u-j)_+^m,
\]
then $2^{-n}V_\infty(f_m,t)=F_m((m+t\sqrt m)/2)-F_m((m-t\sqrt m)/2)$, the Irwin--Hall expression. This does not prove standard directions exhaust all minimizers.

It also reports signed-simplex divided-difference and Fourier formulas for all $B_1^n$ slabs and noncentral sections, without giving those formulas in this record. In dimension two, its exact raw-volume result is
\[
 \min_{a\in S^1}V_1(a,t)=
 \begin{cases}
 2\sqrt2\,t,&0\leq t\leq2-\sqrt2,\\
 4t-2t^2,&2-\sqrt2\leq t\leq1,\\
 2,&t\geq1.
 \end{cases}
\]
Diagonal normals attain the first strict regime and coordinate normals the second; both attain the transition value. This cross-polytope result is distinct from the cube thresholds in (b). The general cube minimization and optimal negative-moment constants are not claimed solved by these supplied partial results.
