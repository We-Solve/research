\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701542}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\F_5\) be the field with five elements. For a nonzero finite-dimensional \(\F_5\)-vector space \(V\), let \(GL(V)\) denote its group of invertible linear maps. A subgroup \(G\leq GL(V)\) is a \(5'\)-subgroup if \(5\nmid |G|\), and it acts irreducibly if its only invariant linear subspaces are \(0\) and \(V\). For a finite group \(H\), let \(k(H)\) denote the number of its conjugacy classes. Write \(V\rtimes G\) for the semidirect product of the additive group of \(V\) by the given action of \(G\), with multiplication
\[
(v,g)(w,h)=(v+g(w),gh).
\]
Does there exist an integer \(n\geq 1\), an \(n\)-dimensional \(\F_5\)-vector space \(V\), and a subgroup \(G\leq GL(V)\) such that
\[
5\nmid |G|,\qquad G\text{ acts irreducibly on }V,\qquad k(V\rtimes G)=|V|=5^n,
\]
while \(G\) is not cyclic?

The supplied source further reports that the analogous existence question, with \(\F_p\) and a \(p'\)-subgroup in place of \(\F_5\) and a \(5'\)-subgroup, has an affirmative answer for \(p=2,3\) and a negative answer for primes \(p>5\). These are supplied background assertions attributed to the journal reference below; they have not been independently verified here. The local registry labels the question for \(p=5\) as open.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there a noncyclic group of order coprime to \(5\) acting faithfully and irreducibly on a nonzero finite vector space over \(\F_5\), such that the resulting semidirect product has exactly as many conjugacy classes as the vector space has elements?
