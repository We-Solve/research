\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For \(n\ge2\), the braid group \(B_n\) has generators \(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\), relations \(\sigma_i\sigma_j=\sigma_j\sigma_i\) when \(|i-j|\ge2\), and
\[
 \sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}.
\]
Let \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\) be the field of algebraic numbers. Does each \(B_n\) admit a faithful finite-dimensional representation
\[
 \rho_n:B_n\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{d_n}(\overline{\mathbb Q}),
 \qquad\ker\rho_n=\{1\},
\]
for some finite integer \(d_n\ge1\)? The case \(n=1\) is trivial if included. No bound on \(d_n\), nor one dimension for all \(n\), is required.

For fixed \(n\), finitely many generator matrices over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\) have entries in a common number field. Thus the coefficient requirement can equivalently be expressed by some number field \(K_n\) and a faithful map into \(\operatorname{GL}_{d_n}(K_n)\). No single field for all \(n\) is imposed. A representation over a transcendental-parameter field would require a separately proved faithful algebraic specialization to answer this question; merely substituting parameters is not sufficient.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every braid group have an injective representation by finite matrices with algebraic-number entries? Dimension and number field may depend on the braid index; transcendental-parameter representations alone do not meet the stated coefficient requirement.
