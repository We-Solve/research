\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a discrete group $\Gamma$, let $\underline E\Gamma$ be a classifying space for proper actions: stabilizers are finite and $(\underline E\Gamma)^F$ is contractible for every finite subgroup $F$. In the ordinary complex, trivial-coefficient formulation, the Baum--Connes conjecture asserts that
\[
 \mu_i:K_i^\Gamma(\underline E\Gamma)\longrightarrow K_i(C_r^*\Gamma)
\]
is an isomorphism for $i=0,1$. A version with coefficients is stronger and is not specified by the excerpt. The Novikov conjecture asserts oriented homotopy invariance of higher signatures $\langle\mathcal L(M)\smile f^*\alpha,[M]\rangle$ for closed oriented manifolds with classifying maps $f:M\to B\Gamma$ and rational cohomology classes $\alpha$ of complementary degree.

Question 2.1 asks whether mapping class groups and $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ satisfy Baum--Connes, and whether $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ satisfies Novikov. Here $F_n$ is free of rank $n$ and $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n)$. Surface types and rank ranges are not stated.

The source attributes to Rosenthal [75], extending Carlsson--Pedersen [23], the following sufficient criterion for split injectivity of the Baum--Connes map and for Novikov. There is a cocompact proper-action model $E=\underline E\Gamma$ with a compactification $X$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the $\Gamma$ action extends to $X$;
\item $X$ is metrizable;
\item $X^F$ is contractible for every finite $F\leq\Gamma$;
\item $E^F$ is dense in $X^F$ for every finite $F\leq\Gamma$;
\item for every compact $K\subset E$, every $y\in Y=X\setminus E$, and every neighborhood $U$ of $y$ in $X$, there is a neighborhood $V$ of $y$ such that, for all $\gamma\in\Gamma$, $\gamma K\cap V\ne\varnothing$ implies $\gamma K\subset U$.
\end{enumerate}
Split injectivity does not assert surjectivity or the full Baum--Connes conjecture. This is a model for proper actions, not necessarily a free universal space.

The source proposes the spine of Outer space as a candidate $E$ for $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$, an analogous construction for $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$, and a filling-arc-system complex for punctured-surface mapping class groups, related to Outer space by [47], Section 5. These suggestions do not supply the required compactification or all five properties. The registry's a-T-menability and CAT$(0)$ discussion supplies neither a general theorem with full hypotheses nor a decisive status argument.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Ask Baum--Connes for mapping class groups and free-group outer automorphism groups, and Novikov for the latter. Preserve the five compactification conditions of the proposed split-injectivity approach without confusing that conclusion with a full assembly isomorphism.
