\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S_g\) be the oriented surface appearing in the source record, and let \(\gamma\colon S^1\to S_g\) parameterize the given generically immersed closed curve. In the usual smooth interpretation, a generic immersion has only transverse double points as self-intersections. Write \(D^2=\{x\in\R^2:\|x\|\leq 1\}\), and identify \(\partial D^2\) with \(S^1\).

The supplied existence question is whether, for every such pair \((S_g,\gamma)\), there exist a compact \(3\)-manifold \(M\), an identification \(h\colon\partial M\to S_g\), and a proper map \(f\colon D^2\to M\) having only stable singularities, with boundary tracing \(\gamma\). Interpreting the source's boundary condition as requiring the disk interior to lie in the manifold interior gives
\[
 h\circ f|_{\partial D^2}=\gamma,
 \qquad f^{-1}(\partial M)=\partial D^2.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 h\bigl(f(D^2)\cap\partial M\bigr)=\gamma(S^1).
\]
Here topological properness means that inverse images of compact sets are compact; it is automatic for a continuous map from the compact disk into a Hausdorff manifold. The condition \(f^{-1}(\partial M)=\partial D^2\) is an additional boundary condition, whose interpretation requires confirmation.

It also does not explicitly state that \(S_g\) is compact and without boundary, although those properties are necessary for an identification with the entire boundary of a compact ordinary \(3\)-manifold. No orientation of \(M\), nor compatibility with the given orientation of \(S_g\), is explicitly required in the source.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Given a generically immersed closed curve on an oriented surface, the supplied problem asks whether it bounds a disk map with only stable singularities inside some compact \(3\)-manifold whose entire boundary is that surface. The meaning of stable singularities and the precise boundary requirements need clarification. The supplied open status is a dated curation label, not an independently verified conclusion.
