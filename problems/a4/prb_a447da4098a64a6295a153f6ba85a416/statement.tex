\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\in\Z\), \(n\geq3\), and let \((M,g_0)\) be a smooth complete \(n\)-dimensional Riemannian manifold. To describe the curvature condition, for a Riemannian metric \(h\) set \(\widehat h=h+\dd s^2\) on \(M\times\R\). Use the curvature convention in which \(R(u,v,u,v)\) is positive on orthonormal pairs on a round sphere. For every \(\widehat h\)-orthonormal four-frame \((e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4)\), write \(\widehat R_{ijkl}=R_{\widehat h}(e_i,e_j,e_k,e_l)\) and define its isotropic curvature expression by
\[
\mathcal I_{\widehat h}(e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4)
=\widehat R_{1313}+\widehat R_{1414}+\widehat R_{2323}+\widehat R_{2424}-2\widehat R_{1234}.
\]
The weak \(\mathrm{PIC1}\) condition means that this expression is nonnegative at every point of \(M\times\R\) for every such frame. The strict version requires it to be positive. This product formulation applies also when \(n=3\).

\frac{\partial}{\partial t}g(t)=-2\operatorname{Ric}_{g(t)},\qquad g(0)=g_0,
