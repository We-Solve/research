\section{Absolute Continuity and Densities of Bernoulli Convolutions}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $1/2<\lambda<1$, let $\nu_\lambda$ be the distribution of $\sum_{n\geq0}\varepsilon_n\lambda^n$, where the $\varepsilon_n$ are independent fair signs. Determine exactly when $\nu_\lambda$ is absolutely continuous, and classify the possible regularity of its density.
