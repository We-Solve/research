\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(a=a(x)\) be the spatial damping coefficient in the vibrating-string equation
\[
 u_{tt}(x,t)=u_{xx}(x,t)-2a(x)u_t(x,t),\qquad 0<x<1,
\]
with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions
\[
 u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0
\]
for all times for which the evolution is considered. The supplied record does not specify the admissible regularity, range, or sign of \(a\).

 \qquad \phi(0)=\phi(1)=0,\qquad \phi\not\equiv0.
