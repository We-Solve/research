\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A projective directed manifold is a pair \((X,V)\), where \(X\) is a smooth complex projective manifold and \(V\subseteq T_X\) is a directed structure. The supplied record does not specify whether \(V\) must be a holomorphic vector subbundle or may have singularities. In the vector subbundle case, writing \(r=\operatorname{rank}V\), its canonical line bundle is
\[
K_V=\det(V^*)=\bigwedge^r V^*.
\]
For a line bundle \(L\) on an \(n\)-dimensional projective manifold, bigness means
\[
\limsup_{m\to\infty}\frac{\dim_{\C}H^0(X,L^{\otimes m})}{m^n}>0.
\]
An entire curve is a holomorphic map \(f\colon\C\to X\). When \(V\) is a vector subbundle, tangency means
\[
\dd f_z(T_z\C)\subseteq V_{f(z)}\qquad\text{for every }z\in\C.
\]
