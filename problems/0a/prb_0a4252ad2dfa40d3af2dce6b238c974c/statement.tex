\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Problem 42 asks to classify metrics of nonnegative sectional curvature on $S^2\times\R^4$, or more generally $S^n\times\R^k$. The supplied aid interprets the noncompact problem for complete smooth metrics; the original sentence omits completeness and a classification equivalence. The general problem is not restricted to invariant or product metrics.

The aid's soul reduction says that any soul of a complete nonnegatively curved manifold diffeomorphic to $S^n\times\R^k$ is an $n$-dimensional homotopy sphere with normal rank $k$. In the target case $S^2\times\R^4$, the soul is diffeomorphic to $S^2$ and its oriented rank-four normal bundle is topologically trivial. Triviality of the bundle does not force triviality of its metric normal connection or normal holonomy, and does not give an isometric product classification.

The exact classified subclass has $n,k\geq2$ and the orthogonally doubly warped $O(n+1)\times O(k)$-invariant form
\[
 g=dr^2+a(r)^2g_{S^{k-1}}+b(r)^2g_{S^n},\qquad r\in[0,\infty).
\]
The sphere metrics have curvature one. Smoothness at the singular orbit requires $a(0)=0$, a smooth odd extension of $a$ with $a'(0)=1$, and a smooth even positive $b$ with $b'(0)=0$. The functions stay positive for $r>0$, and the radial interval is unbounded for this complete ansatz.

The aid's classification is that nonnegative sectional curvature is equivalent to
\[
 b(r)\equiv b_0>0,\qquad a''(r)\leq0,
\]
together with those positivity and smoothness conditions. The radial curvature $-b''/b$ forces concavity of $b$; starting with $b'(0)=0$, any negative slope would destroy positivity at finite radius, so completeness forces $b$ constant. Concavity and positivity of $a$ on the entire ray similarly imply $0\leq a'\leq1$. The remaining radial and rotational curvature terms are then nonnegative. Thus every metric in this specified subclass splits isometrically as the round sphere $S^n$ of radius $b_0$ times a rotationally symmetric nonnegatively curved $\R^k$. No splitting of arbitrary metrics is inferred.

For fixed $b_0$, the aid's family
\[
 a_c(r)=cr+(1-c)\arctan r,\qquad 0\leq c\leq1,
\]
satisfies the full smoothness, positivity and concavity requirements. Its order-$k$ asymptotic volume coefficient is
\[
 \lim_{R\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(B_R)}{R^k}
   =\operatorname{Vol}(S^n_{b_0})\,\omega_k\,c^{k-1},
\]
where $\omega_k$ is the Euclidean unit-ball volume. This separates all parameters by isometry, including $c=0$ with zero order-$k$ coefficient. The noncompact factor has an asymptotically cylindrical end in the bounded-profile case $c=0$. These examples demonstrate moduli within the symmetric subclass, not a classification of all complete metrics on the underlying product.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify complete nonnegatively curved metrics on $S^2\times\R^4$ and related products. The supplied aid gives soul and normal-bundle restrictions, then solves a specified doubly warped invariant subclass: completeness forces the sphere factor to have constant radius. A family of radial profiles gives pairwise nonisometric examples distinguished by volume growth.
