\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
An \emph{integer homology \(3\)-sphere} is a closed, connected, orientable \(3\)-manifold \(M\) satisfying
\[
H_i(M;\Z)\cong H_i(S^3;\Z)\qquad\text{for every }i\geq 0.
\]
For the Heegaard Floer terminology, take a smooth structure and an orientation on \(M\). With the conventional coefficient choice \(\F_2=\Z/2\Z\), a rational homology \(3\)-sphere \(Y\) is an \emph{L-space} if
\[
\dim_{\F_2}\widehat{HF}(Y;\F_2)=|H_1(Y;\Z)|,
\]
where \(\widehat{HF}\) denotes hat Heegaard Floer homology. A \emph{non-L-space} is a rational homology sphere that does not satisfy this equality. In particular, for an integer homology sphere the L-space condition is \(\dim_{\F_2}\widehat{HF}(M;\F_2)=1\).
