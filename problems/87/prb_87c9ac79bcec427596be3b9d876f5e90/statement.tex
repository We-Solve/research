\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701439}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
All groups considered are finite. A formation \(\mathfrak F\) is a nonempty class of finite groups, closed under isomorphisms, homomorphic images, and subdirect products. Here a subdirect product of \(G_1,\ldots,G_r\) is a subgroup of \(G_1\times\cdots\times G_r\) whose projection onto each factor is surjective. A Fitting formation is a formation that is also closed under taking normal subgroups and under products of normal members: if \(A,B\trianglelefteq X\) and \(A,B\in\mathfrak F\), then \(AB\in\mathfrak F\).

For a finite group \(X\), its \(\mathfrak F\)-residual is
\[
X^{\mathfrak F}=\bigcap\{N\trianglelefteq X:X/N\in\mathfrak F\}.
\]
A subgroup \(Y\leq X\) is subnormal in \(X\) if there is a finite chain
\[
Y=Y_0\trianglelefteq Y_1\trianglelefteq\cdots\trianglelefteq Y_m=X,
\qquad m\geq 0.
\]
For subgroups \(U,V\), write \(\langle U,V\rangle\) for the subgroup they generate.

A formation \(\mathfrak F\) has the \emph{Wielandt property for residuals} if, for every finite group \(G\) and every pair \(U,V\leq G\), setting \(H=\langle U,V\rangle\), subnormality of both \(U\) and \(V\) in \(H\) implies
\[
H^{\mathfrak F}=\langle U^{\mathfrak F},V^{\mathfrak F}\rangle.
\]
\textbf{Question (A. Ballester-Bolinches, Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.10).} Does every Fitting formation of finite groups have the Wielandt property for residuals?

The supplied registry labels this problem open; that status and the supplied edition information have not been independently verified.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every Fitting formation \(\mathfrak F\), must the \(\mathfrak F\)-residual of a group generated by two subgroups, each subnormal in their join, equal the subgroup generated by their individual \(\mathfrak F\)-residuals? All groups are finite.
