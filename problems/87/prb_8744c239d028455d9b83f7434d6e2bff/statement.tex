\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(N\geq 1\) be an integer, let \(G\) be a finitely generated group, and let \(\iota:G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{SL}_N(\Z)\) be an injective group homomorphism. For each integer \(m\geq 1\), define the principal congruence subgroup
\[
\Gamma_N(m)=\ker\!\left(\operatorname{SL}_N(\Z)\longrightarrow\operatorname{SL}_N(\Z/m\Z)\right),
\]
where the map is reduction of matrix entries modulo \(m\), and \(\Gamma_N(1)=\operatorname{SL}_N(\Z)\). Use the standard convention that an ambient congruence subgroup is a subgroup \(C\leq\operatorname{SL}_N(\Z)\) containing \(\Gamma_N(m)\) for some \(m\geq 1\). Transporting intersections back to the abstract group \(G\), define
\[
\mathcal C_{\iota}=\left\{\iota^{-1}(C): C\leq\operatorname{SL}_N(\Z),\ \Gamma_N(m)\leq C\text{ for some }m\geq 1\right\}.
\]
Equivalently, with \(K_{\iota}(m)=\iota^{-1}(\Gamma_N(m))\), this family consists of the subgroups \(H\leq G\) containing \(K_{\iota}(m)\) for at least one \(m\geq 1\).

\textbf{Question.} For every integer \(N\geq 1\), every finitely generated group \(G\), and every pair of embeddings \(\iota_1,\iota_2:G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{SL}_N(\Z)\), must
\[
\mathcal C_{\iota_1}=\mathcal C_{\iota_2}
\]
hold? Equivalently, can the family of congruence subgroups of the abstract group \(G\) depend on its chosen embedding?
