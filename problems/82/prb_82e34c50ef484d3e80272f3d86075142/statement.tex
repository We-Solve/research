\section{Predictive Thermostated Dynamics and Subgrid Reservoir Modeling}\label{30001531}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30001531 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} Thermostated dynamics augments resolved variables with auxiliary feedback or reservoir variables to reproduce a prescribed statistical ensemble. A time correlation of observables $A,B$ has the form $\langle A(X_t)B(X_0)\rangle$ under the chosen ensemble. In a PDE or gridpoint model, subgrid variables are unresolved degrees of freedom and their energetics form an effective reservoir. A perturbed vector field specifies the feedback force on the resolved dynamics.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualifications.} Correct equilibrium averages do not by themselves guarantee correct transient dynamics or correlations. The source asks both dynamical validation and reservoir/vector-field modeling questions; no particular universal thermostat or numerical tolerance is fixed.

\paragraph{Catalogue mathematical target.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Can thermostated dynamics be predictive on short time scales and can its correlations be computed accurately; how should subgrid-scale reservoir energetics and perturbed vector fields be modeled?
