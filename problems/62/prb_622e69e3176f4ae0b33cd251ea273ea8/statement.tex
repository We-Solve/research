\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(F\) be Thompson's group: the group, under composition, of increasing piecewise linear homeomorphisms of \([0,1]\) having finitely many breakpoints, all dyadic rational, and slopes in \(\{2^k:k\in\Z\}\). Let \(M=L(F)\) be its group von Neumann algebra, generated on \(\ell^2(F)\) by the left regular representation \(\lambda(g)\delta_h=\delta_{gh}\), with canonical trace \(\tau(x)=\langle x\delta_e,\delta_e\rangle\).

For a nonprincipal ultrafilter \(\mathcal U\) on \(\N\), define the tracial ultrapower
\[
M^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(\N,M)\big/\{(x_n):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau}=0\},\qquad \|x\|_{2,\tau}=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2}.
\]
Identify \(M\) with its diagonal image in \(M^{\mathcal U}\). Its central sequence algebra is
\[
C_{\mathcal U}(M)=M'\cap M^{\mathcal U}=\{x\in M^{\mathcal U}:xa=ax\text{ for every }a\in M\}.
\]
A \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(M\) is McDuff if \(M\cong M\,\overline\otimes\,\mathcal R\), where \(\mathcal R\) is the hyperfinite \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor. Using the standard convention for separable factors, call \(M\) super McDuff if it is McDuff and \(C_{\mathcal U}(M)\) is a factor for every nonprincipal \(\mathcal U\) on \(\N\); being a factor means that its center consists exactly of scalar multiples of its identity.

\textbf{Question (Elayavalli, Problem 21, 2025).} Is \(L(F)\) super McDuff? In the convention just specified, does
\[
L(F)\cong L(F)\,\overline\otimes\,\mathcal R
\quad\text{and}\quad
Z\!\left(L(F)'\cap L(F)^{\mathcal U}\right)=\C1
\quad\text{for every nonprincipal }\mathcal U\text{ on }\N
\]
hold?
