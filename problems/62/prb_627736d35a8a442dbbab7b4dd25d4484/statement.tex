Can \(A\subseteq\mathbb N\) and \(c>0\) satisfy
\[
S(N):=\sum_{j\leq N}1_A^{*2}(j)=cN+O(1)?
\]
Here the convolution counts ordered representations, with repetitions allowed. The answer is no. We prove the bounded-error assertion itself; stronger quantitative error estimates require further work and are not claimed. Attempt status: solved.
