For every $f:\mathbb N\to\{-1,1\}$, is
\[
 \sup_{d,m\geq1}\left|\sum_{j=1}^m f(jd)\right|=\infty?
\]
The answer is affirmative by Tao's discrepancy theorem. This note uses
its stronger Hilbert-space formulation as an external theorem, gives the
precise scalar and finite deductions, and proves the first nontrivial
finite obstruction directly. It does not claim a new proof of Tao's
analytic theorem.
