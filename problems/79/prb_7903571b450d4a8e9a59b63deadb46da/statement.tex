\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30000037}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Work in the complex Hilbert space \(L^2(\R,\dd x)\), where functions are identified almost everywhere. For \(a,b\in\R\), define translation and modulation by
\[
(T_ag)(x)=g(x-a),\qquad (M_bg)(x)=e^{2\pi i b x}g(x).
\]
The exact source question is whether, for every \(g\in L^2(\R)\) with \(\|g\|_2>0\), the family
\[
\{g,T_1g,T_2g,M_1g\}
\]
is linearly independent over \(\C\). Equivalently, does
\[
c_0g(x)+c_1g(x-1)+c_2g(x-2)+c_3e^{2\pi i x}g(x)=0
\quad\text{for almost every }x\in\R
\]
with \((c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3)\in\C^4\) imply \(c_0=c_1=c_2=c_3=0\), for every such \(g\)? The four time-frequency parameters are exactly \((0,0),(1,0),(2,0),(0,1)\); they are not arbitrary four distinct points.

\subsection{Short English statement}
For every nonzero square-integrable function on \(\R\), ask whether its three integer translates \(g,T_1g,T_2g\), together with its unit modulation \(M_1g\), are linearly independent. The fixed configuration must be distinguished from the general four-point HRT question and from the supplied unverified twelve-shift claim.
