\textbf{Conjecture 0.1, with subgroup notation separated.} Let \(H\) be a reductive subgroup of a classical group \(G\) acting on \(V\). Must either \(H\supseteq[G,G]\), where \([G,G]\) is the derived subgroup, or\[M_{2k}(H,V)>M_{2k}(G,V)\quad\text{for some integer }1\le k\le4?\] The extraction uses the same letter for both groups; \(H\le G\) resolves that collision without changing the alternatives. In the usual complex representation-theoretic convention, \(M_{2k}(H,V)=\dim_{\C}\operatorname{End}_H(V^{\otimes k})=\dim_{\C}(V^{\otimes k}\otimes(V^*)^{\otimes k})^H\). For a compact form this is the normalized Haar integral of \(|\operatorname{tr}_V(h)|^{2k}\). The excerpt does not itself define the moments or all conjectural dimension and field hypotheses, so this standard convention needs checking. Under it, \(\operatorname{End}_G(V^{\otimes k})\subseteq\operatorname{End}_H(V^{\otimes k})\); the question asks whether equality through these low moments forces containment of \([G,G]\).
