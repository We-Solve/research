\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A map $f:(X,d_X)\to(Y,d_Y)$ is a coarse embedding if there exist nondecreasing finite-valued control functions $\rho_-,\rho_+:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ with $\rho_-(t)\to\infty$ as $t\to\infty$ and
\[
 \rho_-(d_X(x,x'))\le d_Y(f(x),f(x'))
           \le\rho_+(d_X(x,x'))\qquad(x,x'\in X).
\]
No continuity or small-scale injectivity is required by this large-scale definition.

The question is to find further numerical assignments $I$ on metric spaces, or on a clearly specified class of them, that are nondecreasing under coarse embeddings:
\[
 X\text{ coarsely embeds into }Y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad I(X)\le I(Y).
\]
The target may be an ordered numerical range, such as $[0,\infty]$, provided its meaning is specified. Such an assignment must in particular take equal values when coarse embeddings exist in both directions.

The word ``more'' refers to an unstated baseline of known examples; the source does not prescribe a list to extend, a completeness criterion, a minimum distinguishing power, or restrictions on the metric spaces. This is a request to develop useful monotone invariants, not a conjecture that a particular proposed formula is monotone or a demand to classify every possible invariant.
