\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\mathbb H^4$ be real hyperbolic four-space, with boundary $S^3$ carrying the standard round metric. For a discrete group $G\leq\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^4)$, its limit set $\Lambda(G)\subset S^3$ is the accumulation set of an orbit $Gx$ in the boundary. The group is convex cocompact if the quotient of the convex hull of $\Lambda(G)$ by $G$ is compact. Consider non-elementary groups that are free of finite rank at least two.

The question asks whether
\[
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists G\text{ free and convex cocompact in }\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^4),
\qquad \dim_H\Lambda(G)>3-\varepsilon.
\]
Hausdorff dimension here is for the round boundary metric; it is at most three. The source also asks the more restrictive Schottky version. If Schottky means a classical round-ball pairing construction, it is a specific subclass and should not be silently identified with all free convex-cocompact groups. The record does not give its Schottky convention.

No fixed rank is specified; in the displayed existence formulation the rank may depend on $\varepsilon$. No uniform geometric constants or single group attaining dimension three are requested. A fixed-rank version would be a separate strengthening requiring source clarification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can free convex-cocompact groups in real hyperbolic four-space have limit sets with Hausdorff dimension arbitrarily close to the ambient boundary dimension three? Ask also for the source's Schottky subclass.
