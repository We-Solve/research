\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\N=\{1,2,\ldots\}\), and give every \(X\subseteq\R\) its usual subspace topology. For this draft, a cover of \(X\) means a family \(\mathcal U\) of subsets of \(X\) with \(\bigcup\mathcal U=X\) and \(X\notin\mathcal U\). Let \(\mathcal B(X)\) be the family of countable Borel covers of \(X\), where Borel means Borel in the subspace \(X\). Let \(\mathcal B_\Gamma(X)\) consist of those \(\mathcal U\in\mathcal B(X)\) that are infinite and satisfy
\[
\forall x\in X,\qquad \bigl|\{U\in\mathcal U:x\notin U\}\bigr|<\aleph_0.
\]
Thus each point belongs to all but finitely many members of a Borel \(\gamma\)-cover. These are explicit cover conventions adopted for this draft; the supplied record does not state the original conventions, so they require source verification.

For families \(\mathscr A\) and \(\mathscr D\) of covers of \(X\), the selection principle \(\mathsf S_1(\mathscr A,\mathscr D)\) means
\[
\forall (\mathcal U_n)_{n\in\N}\in\mathscr A^{\N}\;
\exists (U_n)_{n\in\N}\quad
\left[(\forall n\in\N\;U_n\in\mathcal U_n)
\ \land\ \{U_n:n\in\N\}\in\mathscr D\right].
\]
The selected family is regarded as a set, so repetitions are discarded; it need not be infinite when \(\mathscr D=\mathcal B(X)\).

\textbf{Question (Tsaban's 2003 source list, Problem 4).} Does ZFC prove the existence of a set \(X\subseteq\R\) such that
\[
|X|>\aleph_0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathsf S_1\bigl(\mathcal B_\Gamma(X),\mathcal B(X)\bigr)?
\]
Equivalently, can one establish in ZFC that there is an uncountable \(X\subseteq\R\) such that, for every sequence \((\mathcal U_n)_{n\in\N}\) of countable Borel \(\gamma\)-covers of \(X\), there are choices \(U_n\in\mathcal U_n\) satisfying \(X=\bigcup_{n\in\N}U_n\)? The phrase ``in ZFC'' is interpreted here as asking for provable existence, rather than merely existence in some model of ZFC.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does ZFC prove that there is an uncountable set of reals for which one can select one member from each of any sequence of countable Borel \(\gamma\)-covers so that the selected sets cover the space? Each point belongs to all but finitely many members of each \(\gamma\)-cover.
