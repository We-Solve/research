\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(B_n\) be the Artin braid group on \(n\) strands with generators \(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\), and let \(\iota:B_n\to B_{n+1}\) be the standard inclusion adding a trivial strand. On the disjoint union of these groups, take the equivalence relation generated by
\[
 ab\longleftrightarrow ba\quad(a,b\in B_n),
 \qquad
 b\longleftrightarrow\sigma_n\iota(b)\quad(b\in B_n).
\]
The usual braid relations are already imposed inside each \(B_n\). The second relation includes positive destabilization as its inverse. The negative stabilization/destabilization \(b\leftrightarrow\sigma_n^{-1}\iota(b)\) is omitted.

Question 2 asks to classify the resulting equivalence classes. The source reports [OS03] identifying this problem with transverse-link classification up to transverse isotopy in the intended contact-geometric setting. A transverse link is everywhere transverse to the contact planes, and transverse isotopy stays within that class; the ambient contact manifold, positivity and closure conventions are not stated here and must be recovered.

and states its invariance under the two retained moves. It notes that this separates some ordinary-Markov-equivalent braids. It further reports independent examples of Birman--Menasco [BM] and Etnyre--Honda [EH] that are ordinary-Markov-equivalent and have equal Bennequin number but are inequivalent under the retained moves. Besides full classification, the source asks for a new invariant distinguishing those example pairs, motivated by the complexity of their existing proofs; it does not assert that the Bennequin number is complete.
\subsection{Short English statement}
