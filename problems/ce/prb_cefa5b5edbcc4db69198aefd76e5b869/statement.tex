\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(T^2=\R^2/\Z^2\), and let \(F\) be a Finsler metric on \(T^2\). Write
\[
S_FT^2=\{v\in TT^2:F(v)=1\}
\]
for its unit tangent bundle, denoted by \(ST^2\) in the supplied record. Let \(\varphi_F^t\) be its geodesic flow and let \(\mu_L\) be the associated Liouville measure on \(S_FT^2\). The zero-entropy hypothesis is interpreted as
\[
h_{\mathrm{top}}(\varphi_F^1)=0.
\]
A direction \(\xi\in S^1\subset\R^2\) is rational if
\[
\xi\in\mathcal R:=\left\{\frac{k}{\lVert k\rVert_2}:k\in\Z^2\setminus\{0\}\right\}.
\]
For \(v\in S_FT^2\), let \(\gamma_v\) be the unit-speed geodesic with initial velocity \(v\), and let \(\widetilde\gamma_v\) be a lift to \(\R^2\). A precise forward-time interpretation of its asymptotic direction is
\[
\rho_+(v)=\lim_{t\to+\infty}
\frac{\widetilde\gamma_v(t)-\widetilde\gamma_v(0)}
{\lVert\widetilde\gamma_v(t)-\widetilde\gamma_v(0)\rVert_2},
\]
whenever the quotient is defined for all sufficiently large \(t\) and this limit exists. This definition is independent of the chosen lift. Under this interpretation, put
\[
E_\xi=\{v\in S_FT^2:\rho_+(v)\text{ exists and equals }\xi\}.
\]
Thus \(E_\xi\) is a set of initial unit tangent vectors, which makes the measure expression meaningful, rather than a set of unparametrized geodesics.

The question is whether, for every such zero-topological-entropy Finsler metric,
\[
\mu_L\!\left(\bigcup_{\xi\in\mathcal R}E_\xi\right)
<\mu_L(S_FT^2).
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
For every Finsler metric on the two-torus whose geodesic flow has zero topological entropy, must the initial unit tangent vectors generating geodesics of rational asymptotic direction occupy strictly less than the total Liouville measure?
