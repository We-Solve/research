\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
The circular assume–guarantee rule with premises $\Sigma_{P_1}\parallel\Sigma_{Q_2}\preccurlyeq\Sigma_{Q_1}\parallel\Sigma_{Q_2}$ and $\Sigma_{Q_1}\parallel\Sigma_{P_2}\preccurlyeq\Sigma_{Q_1}\parallel\Sigma_{Q_2}$, and conclusion $\Sigma_{P_1}\parallel\Sigma_{P_2}\preccurlyeq\Sigma_{Q_1}\parallel\Sigma_{Q_2}$, is valid for deterministic systems. Is it valid for general systems? \sref{30001199}{2}
