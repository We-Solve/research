\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be an infinite-dimensional Banach space. The source does not specify whether its scalar field is \(\R\) or \(\C\); all linear spaces and maps below use the scalar field of \(X\).

For a closed linear subspace \(Y\subseteq X\), equip the quotient \(X/Y\) with its Banach-space norm
\[
\|x+Y\|_{X/Y}=\inf_{y\in Y}\|x-y\|_X.
\]
A sequence \((u_n)_{n\geq 1}\) in \(X/Y\) is a Schauder basis if every \(z\in X/Y\) has a unique sequence of scalars \((a_n)_{n\geq 1}\) such that
\[
\lim_{N\to\infty}\left\|z-\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_nu_n\right\|_{X/Y}=0.
\]

\textbf{Question (S. Banach [B]--A. Pe{\l}czy\'{n}ski [Pe0], as attributed in the supplied source).} Does every infinite-dimensional Banach space \(X\) admit a closed linear subspace \(Y\subseteq X\) such that \(X/Y\) is infinite-dimensional and has a Schauder basis?
