\section{Sharp Spectral-Cluster Density Bounds on Manifolds}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
Write $n=\dim M\geq2$ and take $\Delta_g$ nonnegative. The source exponents are $s(p)=\frac{n-1}{2}(\frac12-\frac1p)$ for $2\leq p\leq\frac{2(n+1)}{n-1}$ and $s(p)=n(\frac12-\frac1p)-\frac12$ for $\frac{2(n+1)}{n-1}\leq p\leq\infty$. The second exponent is determined by $2s(p)+(n-1)/\alpha(p)=n-1$, allowing $\alpha(\infty)=\infty$. The space $E_\lambda$ is the displayed frequency cluster. Intermediate rank means $r_\lambda\to\infty$ and $r_\lambda/\dim E_\lambda\to0$. For an orthonormal basis $(u_j)$ of $Q$, its basis-independent density is $\rho_Q(x)=\sum_j|u_j(x)|^2$. The constants in the lower bound are independent of $\lambda$.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Let $M$ be smooth, compact, and boundaryless, let $E_\lambda=\mathbf{1}_{\{x:\lambda^2\leq x<(\lambda+1)^2\}}(\Delta_g)L^2(M)$, and suppose $1\ll r_\lambda\ll\dim E_\lambda$. For $2\leq p\leq\infty$, do there exist subspaces $Q_\lambda\subset E_\lambda$ with $\dim Q_\lambda\sim r_\lambda$ such that $\lVert\rho_{Q_\lambda}\rVert_{L^{p/2}(M)}\geq c\lambda^{2s(p)}r_\lambda^{1/\alpha(p)}$ for some $c>0$ independent of $\lambda$, where $\rho_Q$ is the density of an orthonormal basis of $Q$?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The exponent definitions and the source\textquotesingle s intermediate-rank formulation were recovered from PDF pages 23--24. The report proves the sharpness construction for $M=S^2$ and asks the general compact manifold case; that special case is not substituted for the full target. Source asymptotic comparability conventions for $\sim$ are retained.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can intermediate-rank subspaces attain the predicted sharp spectral-cluster density bounds for every indicated exponent?
