 p_v=\sum_{s(e)=v}t_et_e^*\quad\text{if }0<|s^{-1}(v)|<\infty.
\]

\textbf{Extension problem.} For every \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\) and closed two-sided ideal \(I\subseteq A\), consider the canonical short exact sequence

\[
I\cong C^*(E),\qquad A/I\cong C^*(F),
\]

\exists G\in\mathcal G\quad A\cong C^*(G).
\]
