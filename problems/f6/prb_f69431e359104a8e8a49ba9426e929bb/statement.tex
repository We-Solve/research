\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $G$ be a hyperbolic group and $Z=\partial_\infty G$ its boundary. On the usual interpretation of the source's undefined abbreviation, $\operatorname{ACD}(Z)$ is Ahlfors-regular conformal dimension: the infimum of $\dim_H(Z,\rho)$ over Ahlfors-regular metrics $\rho$ quasisymmetrically equivalent to a reference visual metric. A metric is Ahlfors $Q$-regular if it supports a measure $\nu$ and a constant $C\geq1$ with
\[
C^{-1}r^Q\leq\nu(B_\rho(z,r))\leq Cr^Q
\quad(0<r\leq\operatorname{diam}_\rho Z),
\]
and quasisymmetric equivalence means that relative distance ratios are controlled by a homeomorphism of $[0,\infty)$.

A visual metric on the boundary of a hyperbolic model $X$ has, for some $\varepsilon>0$ and $C\geq1$,
\[
C^{-1}e^{-\varepsilon(\xi\mid\eta)_o}\leq\rho(\xi,\eta)\leq C e^{-\varepsilon(\xi\mid\eta)_o},
\]
where $(\xi\mid\eta)_o$ is the boundary Gromov product at a basepoint. A geometric action is proper and cocompact by isometries.

The conjectured equality is
\[
\operatorname{ACD}(\partial_\infty G)
=\inf_{(X,\rho)}\dim_H(\partial_\infty X,\rho),
\]
where $G$ acts geometrically on $X$ and $\rho$ is an admissible visual metric. The models must admit the indicated ideal boundary and visual metrics; the source says metric spaces without giving further model hypotheses.

The bolder conjecture is naturally read as follows: if Ahlfors-regular conformal dimension is attained by some metric in the conformal gauge, then there is a geometric model $X$ and a visual metric on $\partial_\infty X$ attaining that value. If instead ``the infimum'' means the displayed right-hand infimum, attainment by a visual metric is already built into its indexing and the last sentence would be tautological. The record does not resolve this referent.
