\section{Balanced Signings in the Subtour Polytope}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


For an undirected graph let $\delta(S)$ be the edges with exactly one endpoint in $S$. In the source's subtour cut relaxation, $x_e\geq0$ and $x(\delta(S))\geq2$ for every nonempty proper $S\subset V$. For an even terminal set $T$, a balanced signing is $s:T\to\{-1,1\}$ with $\sum_{v\in T}s(v)=0$. A capacitated transshipment routes these supplies and demands using edge capacities $x_e$; its cut feasibility condition is $|\sum_{v\in S\cap T}s(v)|\leq x(\delta(S))$ for every $S\subseteq V$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every point of the subtour-elimination polytope and every even terminal set, does there exist a balanced signing realizable by a capacitated transshipment? \sref{statement}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
The source passage uses the cut-based relaxation without degree equations. The displayed capacities are the coordinates of the given point; there is no additional scaling of capacities in this question.

Choose equally many source and sink terminals so their unit demands can be routed within the subtour point's edge capacities.
