\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The restored question concerns ancient solutions of
\[
 u_t=u_{xx}+\lambda u+|u|^{p-1}u\quad\text{on }(0,\pi),
\]
with Dirichlet boundary conditions, and asks for simultaneous prescription of alternating extrema $u_1,\ldots,u_m$ and ordered blow-up positions
\[
 0<x_1<\cdots<x_m<\pi,
\]
subject to the compatibility hypotheses of two separate existence theorems. The original request is to unify Theorems 1 and 2, informally to ``marry'' them. Their statements are not supplied.

An ancient solution is defined for all $t<T$ for some terminal time $T$, with the regularity needed to solve the equation. Under the usual homogeneous Dirichlet convention it satisfies $u(t,0)=u(t,\pi)=0$. Finite-time blow-up at $T<\infty$ means unbounded solution amplitude as $t\uparrow T$; a spatial blow-up point can be defined by local unboundedness near that point as $t\uparrow T$. The exact solution class, boundary values, parameter conditions on $p,\lambda$, and backward asymptotics as $t\to-\infty$ are absent. In particular, a usual superlinear hypothesis $p>1$ must be checked against the missing theorems rather than silently assumed.

The intended target is a \emph{single} ancient solution realizing both permitted extremal data and permitted spatial data, rather than one solution for each independent existence theorem. However, the source does not specify at what time or in which asymptotic/profile sense the $u_i$ are extrema, whether they are finite or extended values, the precise alternating-sign convention, or whether the $x_i$ exhaust the blow-up set. Finite numbers $u_i$ cannot simply be identified with divergent blow-up heights. The compatibility conditions may couple values, positions, nodal structure and parameters; without them the statement cannot be strengthened to arbitrary alternating values at arbitrary ordered points.
