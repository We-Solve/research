A left Yetter--Drinfeld module over \(kG\) is a \(G\)-graded vector space \(V=\bigoplus_{g\in G}V_g\) with a left \(G\)-action satisfying
\[
h\cdot V_g\subseteq V_{hgh^{-1}}\qquad(g,h\in G).
\]
Its braiding is
\[
c(v\otimes w)=(g\cdot w)\otimes v\qquad(v\in V_g,\ w\in V).
\]
Define its support by \(\operatorname{supp}(V)=\{g\in G:V_g\ne0\}\). Interpret nonabelian support as the condition
\[
\exists g,h\in\operatorname{supp}(V)\quad gh\ne hg,
\]
equivalently, that the subgroup generated by the support is nonabelian. This interpretation requires confirmation from the source context.

The Nichols algebra of \(V\) is
\[
\mathcal B(V)=T(V)/I(V),
\]
where \(T(V)\) has its braided Hopf algebra structure with \(V\) primitive, and \(I(V)\) is the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in \(\bigoplus_{n\ge2}V^{\otimes n}\). For a unital \(k\)-algebra \(A\), define
\[
\operatorname{GKdim}_k A
=\sup_W\limsup_{n\to\infty}
\frac{\log\bigl(\dim_k W^n\bigr)}{\log n},
\]
where \(W\) ranges over finite-dimensional \(k\)-subspaces of \(A\) containing \(1\), and \(W^n\) is the span of products of \(n\) elements of \(W\).
