\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic zero. Write \(K_0(\operatorname{Var}/k)\) for the Grothendieck ring of varieties over \(k\): its additive generators are isomorphism classes \([V]\) of separated reduced schemes of finite type over \(k\), with relations \([V]=[Z]+[V\setminus Z]\) for closed reduced subschemes \(Z\subseteq V\), and multiplication \([V][W]=[V\times_k W]\). Set \(\mathbb L=[\mathbb A^1_k]\). Two varieties \(X,Y\) are \(L\)-equivalent if there exists an integer \(r\geq 0\) such that
\[
([X]-[Y])\mathbb L^r=0\qquad\text{in }K_0(\operatorname{Var}/k).
\]
In the usual \(k\)-linear interpretation, \(D\)-equivalence means the existence of an exact \(k\)-linear equivalence
\[
D^b(\operatorname{Coh}(X))\simeq D^b(\operatorname{Coh}(Y)),
\]
where these are the bounded derived categories of coherent sheaves.

The supplied question asks: for every field \(k\) of characteristic zero and every pair of smooth projective simply connected \(D\)-equivalent varieties \(X,Y\) over \(k\), must \(X\) and \(Y\) be \(L\)-equivalent? The exponent \(r\) may depend on \(k,X,Y\), and \(r=0\) is allowed.

The source leaves the meaning of ``simply connected'' over an arbitrary characteristic-zero field unspecified. One possible interpretation requires geometric connectedness and trivial geometric \(\acute{e}\)tale fundamental group \(\pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X_{\bar k})\), and similarly for \(Y\). Over \(\C\), topological simple connectedness instead concerns the fundamental group of the associated complex analytic space. These interpretations must not be silently identified. The source also does not explicitly specify \(k\)-linearity of the derived equivalence. Thus the question above preserves the supplied scope, but its precise hypothesis requires clarification of these conventions.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every pair of smooth projective simply connected derived-equivalent varieties over a characteristic-zero field have equal Grothendieck classes after multiplication by some nonnegative power of the affine-line class? The meanings of simple connectedness and linearity of the derived equivalence need clarification.
