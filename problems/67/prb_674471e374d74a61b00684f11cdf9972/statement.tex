\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\textbf{Problem 4 (D. Zaitsev).} Let \(M\) be a smooth CR manifold such that for every \(p,q\in M\) there is a local CR diffeomorphism \((U,p)\to(V,q)\). Must local infinitesimal CR automorphisms span \(T_pM\), for one and hence every point? The source preamble takes manifolds smooth unless stated otherwise, and its remark says the answer is yes in the analytic case. Infinitesimal automorphisms here mean germs of real vector fields whose local flows preserve the CR structure, not global complete vector fields; local germ homogeneity does not assert a global group action.

\textbf{Reported equivalent smooth-section condition.} The aid reports that tangent spanning at \(p\) is equivalent to a jointly smooth family \(h_q(x)\), defined on a common sufficiently small source neighborhood, with
\[
 h_p=\operatorname{id},\qquad h_q(p)=q,
\]
where each \(h_q\) is a local CR diffeomorphism and \(q\) ranges over a neighborhood of \(p\). This is a target-parametrized local section of the germ pseudogroup through the identity. Pointwise existence of separate germs is the source hypothesis; smooth dependence and a common neighborhood are additional conclusions, not automatic choices. Differentiating a smooth section supplies the infinitesimal fields, while compositions of local flows of spanning fields supply a section.

\textbf{Reported stratified finite-jet sufficient condition.} Assume further that \(M\) is connected, of finite type and finitely nondegenerate, in the setting of Kim--Zaitsev finite-jet reconstruction. For a fixed source point \(p\), let \(A_p^r\) be the set of admissible invertible \(r\)-jets of actual CR germs from \(p\) to variable targets, with target projection \(\beta:A_p^r\to M\). Suppose this set admits a countable locally finite smooth stratification and the required smooth reconstruction on the relevant jet neighborhoods, on a common local domain. The precise finite-type/nondegeneracy and reconstruction hypotheses are not reproduced in the supplied summary and must be checked.

The aid reports that germ transitivity then implies infinitesimal transitivity. Target surjectivity, countability and Sard's theorem force a stratum with a full-rank target-projection point. A local smooth section there reconstructs a family of CR germs; composition with one fixed inverse centers it at the identity, giving the preceding section condition. Local homogeneity transports spanning to every point.

Accordingly a counterexample within that reconstruction class cannot have a full-rank smooth admissible-jet branch of the indicated kind; in particular it cannot satisfy the stated countably stratifiable sufficient criterion. This does not prove that admissible smooth jet sets always possess such a stratification, nor exhibit a pathological counterexample.

The aid also gives a transitive finite-dimensional \emph{holonomic} Lie-groupoid realization as a sufficient condition: arrows must represent actual CR germs with compatible smooth reconstruction, not merely formal jets. The source's unrestricted smooth implication remains reported apparently open in the literature checked, without supplied date or full references. Neither sufficient condition is asserted universally.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The issue is whether separate transitive CR germs admit smooth target dependence. The aid equates infinitesimal spanning with such a local section and gives a conditional finite-jet/Sard criterion. It does not prove the needed stratifiability for every smooth CR manifold, and the source's analytic positive remark is retained.
