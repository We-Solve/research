Let \(M\) be a closed smooth \(3\)-manifold, where closed means compact and without boundary, and let \(\varphi:\R\times M\to M\) be a smooth flow. Write \(\varphi_t(p)=\varphi(t,p)\) and let \(X(p)=\left.\frac{\dd}{\dd t}\right|_{t=0}\varphi_t(p)\) be its generating vector field. Interpret a periodic orbit as the image of a nonconstant trajectory through a point \(p\) such that \(\varphi_T(p)=p\) for some \(T>0\). For the supplied finite family \(\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_N\), put
\[
L=\bigcup_{i=1}^{N}\gamma_i.
\]

\[
\partial S=L,\qquad X(q)\notin T_qS\quad\text{for every }q\in S^\circ,

\[
\text{for every }p\in M\setminus L\text{ there exist }t_-<0<t_+\text{ such that }
