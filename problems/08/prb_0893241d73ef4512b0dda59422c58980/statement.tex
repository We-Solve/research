\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Coxeter system is specified by a set \(S\) and a symmetric matrix \(m_{st}\) with \(m_{ss}=1\) and \(m_{st}\in\{2,3,4,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}\) for distinct \(s,t\in S\). Its Coxeter group is
\[
W(S,m)=\left\langle S\ \middle|\ s^2=1\ (s\in S),\ (st)^{m_{st}}=1\ (s\ne t,\ m_{st}<\infty)\right\rangle.
\]
An entry \(m_{st}=\infty\) imposes no relation on \(st\). In the graph notation of the supplied excerpt, a nonedge has \(m_{st}=2\), and an edge whose label is omitted has label \(3\). Write \(W_\Gamma\) for the group specified by such a labelled graph \(\Gamma\).

For a group \(G\), let
\[
\mathcal N_G=\{N\trianglelefteq G:[G:N]<\infty\}.
\]
Its profinite completion is
\[
\widehat G=\varprojlim_{N\in\mathcal N_G}G/N,
\]
where each finite quotient has the discrete topology and the inverse limit has the induced topology from their product. An isomorphism of profinite completions is understood here to be an isomorphism of topological groups.

Let \(\mathcal W\) denote the class described in the supplied statement as all Coxeter groups. A group \(W\in\mathcal W\) is \(\mathcal W\)-profinitely rigid if, for every \(W'\in\mathcal W\), an isomorphism \(\widehat W\cong\widehat{W'}\) of profinite groups implies an isomorphism \(W\cong W'\) of abstract groups.

\textbf{Question.} Is every Coxeter group \(\mathcal W\)-profinitely rigid? Equivalently, is the following implication valid for every pair \(W_\Gamma,W_\Lambda\in\mathcal W\)?
\[
\widehat{W_\Gamma}\cong\widehat{W_\Lambda}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
W_\Gamma\cong W_\Lambda.
\]
The conclusion concerns the groups, without requiring an isomorphism to preserve their specified Coxeter generators or labelled graphs.
