\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.68}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


The exterior class $\Sigma$ consists of holomorphic injective functions on $\{z:|z|>1\}$ with Laurent expansion $f(z)=z+b_0+\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}b_jz^{-j}$. For locally injective holomorphic $f$, put $P_f=f''/f'$ and $S_f=P_f'-\tfrac12P_f^2$, its pre-Schwarzian and Schwarzian derivatives.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $f\in\Sigma$ and sequence $(f_n)\subset\Sigma$, does \[\sup_{|z|>1}(|z|^2-1)^2|S_{f_n}(z)-S_f(z)|\longrightarrow0\] imply \[\sup_{|z|>1}(|z|^2-1)|P_{f_n}(z)-P_f(z)|\longrightarrow0?\] \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does convergence of exterior Schwarzians in their weighted uniform norm imply convergence of pre-Schwarzians in the corresponding weaker norm?
