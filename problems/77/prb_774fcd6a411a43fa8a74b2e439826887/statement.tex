\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A binary ball packing consists of balls \(B(x_a,r_{t(a)})\subset\R^n\), where \(n\geq1\), \(r_1,r_2>0\), and \(t(a)\in\{1,2\}\), satisfying
\[
\lVert x_a-x_b\rVert\geq r_{t(a)}+r_{t(b)}\qquad(a\ne b).
\]
Thus distinct balls have disjoint interiors. The source does not specify the dimension, so \(n\) is an unresolved parameter; this notation does not assert that the intended question quantifies over every dimension.

The following gives an explicit conditional interpretation of the unspecified averaging conventions. Put \(Q_R=[-R,R]^n\) and
\[
N_i(R)=\#\{a:x_a\in Q_R,\ t(a)=i\}.
\]
If prescribed proportion means proportion by number of balls, one may require, for a prescribed \(p\in(0,1)\),
\[
\lim_{R\to\infty}\frac{N_1(R)}{N_1(R)+N_2(R)}=p,
\qquad
\lim_{R\to\infty}\frac{N_2(R)}{N_1(R)+N_2(R)}=1-p.
\]
Here the total count must tend to infinity. The source does not specify whether the proportion is by number or by occupied volume, whether zero proportions are allowed, or which spatial averaging convention is intended. These choices remain unresolved.

For a packing \(\mathcal P\) for which the following limit exists, define its density under this convention by

\delta(\mathcal P)=\lim_{R\to\infty}
\frac{\operatorname{vol}_n\!\left(Q_R\cap\bigcup_a B(x_a,r_{t(a)})\right)}{(2R)^n}.
\]
Two balls touch when the distance between their centers equals the sum of their radii. Define the mean number of type \(j\) balls touching a type \(i\) ball, when the limit exists, by
\[

\sum_{\substack{a:x_a\in Q_R\\t(a)=i}}
\#\{b\ne a:t(b)=j,\ \lVert x_a-x_b\rVert=r_i+r_j\},
