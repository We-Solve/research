\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a smooth Riemannian metric $g$, write $\operatorname{Sc}_g=\operatorname{tr}_g\operatorname{Ric}_g$; nonnegative scalar curvature means $\operatorname{Sc}_g(x)\geq0$ at every point. The orthogonal group $O(n-1)$ consists of real $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ matrices $A$ with $A^TA=I$.
