\section{Weak Cosmic Censorship for Einstein–Vlasov}\label{30001361}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30001361 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} The Einstein--Vlasov system couples $G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi T_{\mu\nu}[f]$ to collisionless transport of a nonnegative phase-space density $f$ along the spacetime geodesic flow on the particle mass shell. The stress-energy tensor is obtained by integrating particle momenta against $f$. Asymptotically flat initial data describe an isolated system; spherical symmetry reduces the metric and matter fields to radial variables. Weak cosmic censorship asks for complete future null infinity for generic admissible data. Strong cosmic censorship concerns inextendibility of the maximal globally hyperbolic development in a specified regularity class.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualifications.} Genericity, data topology, matter regularity, and the precise completeness formulation must be fixed in a proof. The source emphasizes that dust and scalar-field results cannot be transferred automatically to Vlasov matter; this is not a theorem for every initial datum without exceptional sets.

\paragraph{Catalogue mathematical target.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Does weak cosmic censorship hold for the asymptotically flat Einstein–Vlasov system, in particular for generic spherically symmetric initial data?

\paragraph{Saved literature context.} The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The extracted Einstein--Vlasov weak-cosmic-censorship claim remains a conjecture; analytical results establish important collapse and regularity cases, but not the asserted general behavior. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.
