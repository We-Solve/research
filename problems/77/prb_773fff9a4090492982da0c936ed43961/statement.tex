Let \(X=\PP^2(\C)\) be the complex projective plane, with complex structure \(J\). For a real hypersurface \(M\subset X\) of class at least \(C^2\), its complex tangent distribution is
\[
H_pM=T_pM\cap J(T_pM),\qquad p\in M.
\]
This distribution has real rank two. The hypersurface is \emph{Levi-flat} if \(HM\) is integrable; equivalently, its Levi form vanishes identically. Locally, if \(M=\{\rho=0\}\), where \(\rho\) is a real defining function with \(\dd\rho\ne0\) on \(M\), this means
\[
(\partial\bar\partial\rho)_p(v,\bar v)=0
\quad\text{for every }p\in M\text{ and }v\in T_p^{1,0}M.
\]
The resulting local leaves are complex curves.
