\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source asks for an equivariant homotopy refinement of Némethi's combinatorial lattice theory for plumbed three-manifolds. A plumbing presentation $\Gamma$ specifies a four-manifold built from plumbed disk bundles and its oriented boundary $Y_\Gamma$. The precise class of graphs/bundles, definiteness and homological assumptions are not fixed in the question. In the usual stable-homotopy formulation, the target is a combinatorially constructed lattice spectrum $\mathcal X_\Gamma$ with an $S^1$ or $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ action and a comparison
\[
\mathcal X_\Gamma\simeq_G\operatorname{SWF}(Y_\Gamma)
\qquad(G=S^1\text{ or }\operatorname{Pin}(2)),
\]
with the required spin or spin$^c$ data, representation suspensions and grading normalization specified. Here $S^1$ is the unit complex numbers and $\operatorname{Pin}(2)=S^1\cup jS^1$ in the unit quaternions. The right side denotes the corresponding Seiberg--Witten Floer stable homotopy invariant. Pin(2) symmetry requires appropriate spin/self-conjugacy data and is not asserted for every unspecified spin$^c$ structure.

The comparison should refine the chosen lattice homology theory and compute the equivariant Floer type, rather than merely an isomorphism of homology modules. The source does not specify which lattice homology/cohomology version is recovered, the precise equivariant category or grading shifts, allowed plumbing moves or a fully stated functoriality requirement. These must be fixed before the request becomes one definite construction theorem. Its ``or'' permits an $S^1$ refinement or a Pin(2) refinement, rather than silently requiring both at once.

The background credits Némethi [Ném05, Ném08], building on Ozsváth--Szabó [OS03b], with lattice invariants for plumbed manifolds. It describes Zemke [Zem25]'s identification of lattice and Heegaard Floer homology for boundaries of plumbing trees as an announcement, while also supplying a Duke Mathematical Journal 2025 publication citation. Both the wording and published bibliographic item are retained; homology agreement is not a full equivariant homotopy comparison.

The notes cite computational applications [CK14, DM19, AKS20], recovery of algebraic-link Floer complexes from multivariable Alexander polynomials [BLZ24], knot lattice homology [OSS14a], and surgery exact sequences [Ném11, Gre13d]. \subsection{Short English statement}
Build a combinatorial $S^1$- or Pin(2)-equivariant lattice spectrum that recovers the Seiberg--Witten Floer stable homotopy type of the intended plumbed manifolds. The source records homology equivalences and restricted spectral comparisons but leaves a general refinement, its scope and conventions open.
