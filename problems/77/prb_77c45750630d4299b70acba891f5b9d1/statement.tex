\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S\) be the finite-type surface in the source's mapping-class convention. A group is virtually free if it contains a free subgroup of finite index. The first question asks for a finitely generated subgroup \(G\leq\operatorname{MCG}(S)\) such that
\[G\text{ is not virtually free},\qquad
\forall g\in G,\ \operatorname{ord}(g)=\infty\Longrightarrow g\text{ is pseudo-Anosov}.\]
Finite-order elements are permitted; the condition does not require \(G\) torsion free. Nor is merely containing a nonabelian free subgroup enough to satisfy the requested non-virtual-freeness.

The source asks two strengthened existence questions for such \(G\): can it be chosen so that the associated extension \(\Gamma_G\) is word hyperbolic, and can it be chosen convex cocompact? In the customary surface-group setting the extension has form
\[1\longrightarrow\pi_1(S)\longrightarrow\Gamma_G\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow1,\]
with its source-specific mapping-class action; its construction is not reproduced. Word hyperbolicity is a condition on the extension's Cayley graph, not merely on \(G\).

The usual Teichm\"uller-space meaning of convex cocompactness is that an orbit \(Gx\subset\mathcal T(S)\) is quasiconvex: some fixed neighborhood of that orbit contains every Teichm\"uller geodesic between its orbit points. The exact convention must be checked from the preceding text. The three existence requests are retained without asserting an equivalence between a purely pseudo-Anosov condition, extension hyperbolicity and convex cocompactness. The surface range and whether one seeks an example for a particular \(S\) or some allowable \(S\) are not supplied.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find a finitely generated, non-virtually-free mapping-class subgroup whose infinite-order elements are all pseudo-Anosov, preferably with hyperbolic surface-group extension or convex-cocompact Teichm\"uller action.
