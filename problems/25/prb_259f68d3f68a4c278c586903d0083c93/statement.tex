\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $K$ be a finite simplicial complex with vertex set $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$ whose geometric realization embeds in $S^{d-1}$. For the chosen algebraic-shifting conventions, write $\Delta^{\mathrm{sym}}(K)$ and $\Delta^{\mathrm{ext}}(K)$ for symmetric and exterior algebraic shifting, viewed as labeled simplicial complexes on $[n]$.

Let $C_d(n)$ be the cyclic $d$-polytope, conventionally the convex hull of $n$ distinct points $(t,t^2,\ldots,t^d)$ on the moment curve, and let
\[
 \Delta(d,n)=\Delta^{\mathrm{sym}}(\partial C_d(n))
\]
under the usual boundary-complex interpretation. The source says ``shifting of the cyclic polytope'' rather than explicitly its boundary, so this convention and the range such as $n\ge d+1$ must be checked.

The Kalai--Sarkaria conjecture, as supplied, asks for containment
\[

\[
 \Delta^{\mathrm{sym}}(K)\subseteq\Delta(d,n),
