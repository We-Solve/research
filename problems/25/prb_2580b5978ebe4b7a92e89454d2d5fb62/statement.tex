\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source concerns a measure-theoretic limit of finite reversible Markov chains, with transition densities \(p_\infty(x,y,t)\) relative to a stationary probability measure \(\pi\). In the usual semigroup setting these densities are nonnegative, symmetric in \(x,y\), integrate to one, and satisfy Chapman--Kolmogorov identities, initially up to almost-everywhere versions. The source does not reproduce the compactness, normalization or convergence hypotheses giving this limit.

Construct a natural topology on an appropriate realization of its state space that is complete and separable and for which the limit Markov process is Feller. One suggested construction is a transition-density pseudometric of the form
\[
 d_w(x,y)^2=\int_0^\infty w(t)
 \int|p_\infty(x,z,t)-p_\infty(y,z,t)|^2\,\pi(\dd z)\,\dd t,
\]
with an appropriate weight or time range. The excerpt does not prescribe either, and finiteness near \(t=0\) must not be assumed. The expression may identify distinct states, and a measurable density defined only almost everywhere does not automatically specify every state in a metric completion.

A successful realization must address versions, null states, separation or quotienting, completion, the measure and semigroup on added states, and the intended Feller condition. In a locally compact setting the standard condition is a strongly continuous semigroup on \(C_0\); on a general Polish space a bounded-continuous-function formulation and its continuity convention must be specified. The source does not choose among these meanings, so no stronger uniform-continuity requirement or local compactness is inserted.

The summary attributes a measure-theoretic density limit to Towsner and a path-space topology handling instantaneous states to Landim, while saying the requested natural complete separable Feller state-space realization is not established in the reviewed literature. A path-space convergence topology does not itself provide that state-space construction. These are partial-progress attributions without independently verified current status.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Turn the measure-theoretic limit of reversible finite chains into a natural complete separable state space supporting a Feller process. The suggested integrated-density distance needs choices about versions, zero-distance states, completion and Feller continuity that the excerpt does not supply.
