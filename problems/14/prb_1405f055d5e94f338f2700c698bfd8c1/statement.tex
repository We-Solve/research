\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The supplied question asks whether a distal action can have sofic topological entropy equal to \(-\infty\). Its ambient group, space, and quantification over sofic approximations are not specified. The following is a precise formulation under the usual convention of countable discrete sofic groups acting on nonempty compact metrizable spaces; these conventions require confirmation against the source.

Let \(G\) be a countable discrete group. A sofic approximation is a sequence \(\Sigma=(\sigma_i\colon G\to\operatorname{Sym}(d_i))_{i\geq1}\), with \(d_i\to\infty\), such that, for every \(s,t\in G\),
\[
\frac{1}{d_i}\bigl|\{v:\sigma_i(st)v=\sigma_i(s)\sigma_i(t)v\}\bigr|\longrightarrow1,
\]
and, for every distinct \(s,t\in G\),
\[
\frac{1}{d_i}\bigl|\{v:\sigma_i(s)v\ne\sigma_i(t)v\}\bigr|\longrightarrow1.
\]
The existence of such a sequence means that \(G\) is sofic.

Let \(G\curvearrowright X\) be an action by homeomorphisms on a nonempty compact metrizable space, and let \(\rho\) be a compatible metric. The action is distal if
\[
\forall x,y\in X,\quad x\ne y\ \Longrightarrow\ \inf_{g\in G}\rho(gx,gy)>0.
\]
For a nonempty finite set \(F\subseteq G\) and \(\delta>0\), define
\[
\operatorname{Map}(\rho,F,\delta,\sigma_i)
=\left\{\phi\colon\{1,\ldots,d_i\}\to X:
\max_{s\in F}\left(\frac1{d_i}\sum_{v=1}^{d_i}
\rho(s\phi(v),\phi(\sigma_i(s)v))^2\right)^{1/2}<\delta\right\}.
\]
For maps \(\phi,\psi\), put \(\rho_\infty(\phi,\psi)=\max_v\rho(\phi(v),\psi(v))\). Write \(N_\varepsilon(Y,\rho_\infty)\) for the largest cardinality of a subset of \(Y\) whose distinct points have distance at least \(\varepsilon\), with \(N_\varepsilon(\varnothing,\rho_\infty)=0\). In this convention, the sofic topological entropy relative to \(\Sigma\) is
\[
h_\Sigma(G\curvearrowright X)
=\sup_{\varepsilon>0}\ \inf_{\substack{F\subseteq G\text{ finite}\\F\ne\varnothing}}\ \inf_{\delta>0}
\limsup_{i\to\infty}\frac1{d_i}\log N_\varepsilon\bigl(\operatorname{Map}(\rho,F,\delta,\sigma_i),\rho_\infty\bigr),
\qquad \log0=-\infty.
\]
\textbf{Question under these conventions.} Do there exist \(G\), a sofic approximation \(\Sigma\) of \(G\), and a distal action \(G\curvearrowright X\) as above such that
\[
h_\Sigma(G\curvearrowright X)=-\infty?
\]
