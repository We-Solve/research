\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $f:M\to M$ be a $C^r$ diffeomorphism of a compact Riemannian manifold. A finite-alphabet symbolic extension of $(M,f)$ is a subshift $Y\subseteq A^{\Z}$ over a finite alphabet $A$, together with a continuous surjection $\pi:Y\to M$ satisfying $\pi\sigma=f\pi$. Here $Y$ is closed and shift invariant. Under the usual topological convention define
\[
h_{\mathrm{sex}}(f)=\inf\{h_{\mathrm{top}}(\sigma|_Y):(Y,\sigma)\text{ is a finite-alphabet symbolic extension of }(M,f)\},
\]
with $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$. The source asks whether there exist $M$ and $f$ in the intended differentiability class for which $h_{\mathrm{sex}}(f)=+\infty$.

Each individual finite-alphabet subshift has entropy at most $\log|A|$. Thus under this convention infinite symbolic-extension entropy means that no finite-alphabet symbolic extension exists; it does not mean a finite-alphabet extension has infinite entropy. This also differs from the measure-dependent symbolic-extension entropy function.

The source does not specify the range of $r$, whether $r$ is fixed or quantified, the dimension, or whether $r=\infty$ is intended. These distinctions matter to the claimed results. The supplied summary asserts a universal negative answer for all compact-manifold diffeomorphisms, but cites smooth and surface results and gives no theorem supporting that unrestricted conclusion. It also merges $C^r$ and $C^\infty$ claims and entropy-close extensions without their hypotheses. These assertions are retained as supplied, unresolved status evidence; they are not used to declare the unrestricted question solved.
