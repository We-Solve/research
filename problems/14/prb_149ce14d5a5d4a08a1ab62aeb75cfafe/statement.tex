\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The braid group \(B_n\) has generators \(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\), with relations
\[
 \sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1},\qquad
 \sigma_i\sigma_j=\sigma_j\sigma_i\quad(|i-j|\geq2).
\]
A representation \(\rho:B_n\to\operatorname{GL}(V)\) is irreducible if \(V\) has no nonzero proper invariant subspace. The Birman--Murakami--Wenzl (BMW) algebra is a parameter-dependent quotient of the braid group algebra; its modules give braid-group representations through the quotient map.

S. J. Bigelow asks for a larger family of irreducible \(B_n\)-representations containing those arising from BMW algebras. The natural construction target is an explicitly described family with the BMW representations recovered up to isomorphism in the specified parameter regime and with genuinely additional irreducible representations. Merely naming all irreducible representations as a family would not supply the requested construction.

It also does not specify whether inclusion means actual members or specializations of a parameterized family, nor a measure of how much larger the family should be. No faithfulness, unitarity, classification of every irreducible, or compatibility between different strand numbers is added as a requirement.
