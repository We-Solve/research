\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix an algebraic closure \(\overline{\Q}\) of \(\Q\). For a positive integer \(d\), define
\[
\Q^{(d)}=\mathop{\mathrm{compositum}}_{\substack{\Q\subseteq K\subseteq\overline{\Q}\\[K:\Q]\leq d}}K,
\]
the smallest subfield of \(\overline{\Q}\) containing every such number field \(K\).
For \(\alpha\in\overline{\Q}\), let
\[
f_\alpha(T)=a\prod_{i=1}^{n}(T-\alpha_i)\in\Z[T]
\]
be its primitive irreducible polynomial, with positive leading coefficient \(a\), where \(n=[\Q(\alpha):\Q]\) and the \(\alpha_i\) are its complex conjugates. Its absolute multiplicative Weil height is
\[
H(\alpha)=\left(a\prod_{i=1}^{n}\max\{1,|\alpha_i|\}\right)^{1/n}.
\]
A subfield \(L\subseteq\overline{\Q}\) has \emph{Property (N)}, or the Northcott property, if
\[
\forall X\in\R,\quad X\geq1\ \Longrightarrow\ \#\{\alpha\in L:H(\alpha)<X\}<\infty.
\]
\textbf{Question.} For a given positive integer \(d\), does \(\Q^{(d)}\) have Property (N)? Equivalently, is
\[
\forall X\in\R,\quad X\geq1\ \Longrightarrow\ \#\{\alpha\in\Q^{(d)}:H(\alpha)<X\}<\infty
\]
true for every positive integer \(d\)?
