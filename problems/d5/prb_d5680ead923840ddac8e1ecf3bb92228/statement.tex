For a finite planar set $P$, let $c(P)$ count the distinct Euclidean circles containing at least three points of $P$. We study
\[
 f(n)=\min\{c(P):|P|=n,\ P\text{ is neither collinear nor concyclic}\}.
\]
The exclusion of collinear sets is essential: without it the literal displayed question has the trivial answer zero. We use the weaker nondegeneracy convention, not the different problem which forbids every collinear triple.

The current problem page, checked 23 September 2026, says DECIDABLE: the corrected large-$n$ formula is known for $n>393$, with small orders remaining. We do not claim that a finite sample closes that gap. The argument below independently establishes an exact structured-family formula and $f(4)=3$, $f(5)=5$. No novelty over the literature is claimed for these elementary facts.
