Suppose $n_1<n_2<\cdots$ and $n_k>(1+\varepsilon)k\log k$ for some $\varepsilon>0$. Must the number of positive integers $m<n_k$ not covered by the first $k$ chosen residue classes fail to be $o(k)$? No. We verify the simple counterexample attributed to Cambie in the current problem discussion. No novelty is claimed.
