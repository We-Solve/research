\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an integer \(n\geq 1\), let \(B_n\) be the braid group with standard generators \(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\). A positive braid is a braid represented by a word using only these generators with positive exponents. A positive braid knot is a knot in \(S^3\) isotopic to the closure of such a braid, where the closure has one component. Allowing the empty braid includes the unknot; the supplied record states no nontriviality restriction.

Here is a precise smooth, unoriented formulation of the supplied question. Let \(K_0,K_1\subset S^3\) be arbitrary smooth positive braid knots, with no restriction on their braid indices. They are smoothly concordant if there is a smooth proper embedding
\[
F:S^1\times[0,1]\longrightarrow S^3\times[0,1]
\]
with
\[
F(S^1\times\{i\})=K_i\times\{i\}\qquad(i=0,1),
\]
whose interior lies in \(S^3\times(0,1)\). They are ambiently isotopic if there is a smooth ambient isotopy \(H_t:S^3\to S^3\), \(t\in[0,1]\), satisfying \(H_0=\operatorname{id}_{S^3}\) and \(H_1(K_0)=K_1\).

The question is whether, for every such pair,
\[
K_0\text{ and }K_1\text{ are smoothly concordant}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
K_0\text{ and }K_1\text{ are ambiently isotopic}.
