\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $X$ be a complete Riemannian $n$-manifold with scalar curvature bounded below by a positive constant. Does there exist a constant $C_n$, depending only on dimension, such that its filling radius satisfies
\[\operatorname{FillRad}(X)\leq C_n\bigl(\inf_{x\in X}\operatorname{Sc}(x)\bigr)^{-2}?\]
The filling radius measures the neighborhood size needed to fill the fundamental cycle after the metric-space embedding used in its definition.
The catalogue transcription gives exponent $-2$; the cited original is needed to determine whether this exponent is correct.
