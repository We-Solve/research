\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source asks, for a finite-horizon toral billiard with two masses \(m_1,m_2\), how to compute viscosity/momentum transfer, define heat, check an Einstein relation, and decide whether unequal masses acquire equal kinetic energy in a limit. The printed ``Saini billard'' is naturally read as Sinai billiard, but the geometry, particle interactions, scatterers, ensemble, conserved quantities, current, and limiting operation are not specified. Finite horizon conventionally bounds free-flight lengths; it does not by itself specify those missing data.

The aid separates different models. For two two-dimensional particles with \(m_i>0\), write
\[
 K_i=|p_i|^2/(2m_i),\qquad H=K_1+K_2=E>0.
\]
On the full microcanonical Liouville energy shell, with positions independent of momenta in the admissibility constraint and no fixed total-momentum condition, it reports
\[
 K_1/E\sim\operatorname{Uniform}[0,1],\qquad
 \E K_1=\E K_2=E/2.
\]
If the chosen billiard flow is ergodic for that full-shell measure, this gives almost-everywhere Ces\`aro time-average equipartition,
\[
 \lim_{T\to\infty}\frac1T\int_0^TK_i(t)\,\dd t=E/2.
\]
It does not give pointwise convergence of instantaneous energies, nor establish ergodicity for the unspecified source system.

For two elastically colliding hard disks on a bare torus without fixed scatterers, total momentum \(P=p_1+p_2\) is conserved. On the invariant shell \(P=0\), the aid reports
\[
 K_1/K_2=m_2/m_1,
\]
so unequal masses do not equipartition there. Fixed scatterers can destroy this momentum constraint, and the bare-torus model is not automatically a finite-horizon Sinai model. This is a constraint diagnostic, not an unqualified counterexample to every interpretation of the source.

For a specified scalar stationary centered current \(J(t)\) with finite variance and absolutely integrable autocorrelation \(C(s)=\E[J(0)J(s)]\), put \(A(t)=\int_0^tJ(s)\,\dd s\). The aid reports the Green--Kubo/Einstein--Helfand identity
\[
 \lim_{t\to\infty}\frac{\E[A(t)^2]}{2t}
 =\int_0^\infty C(s)\,\dd s.
\]
Physical viscosity or conductivity requires the appropriate current and its temperature/volume normalization. If the intended Einstein relation instead concerns diffusion versus mobility/linear response, that additional response model is not supplied by this identity.

The aid's coboundary obstruction is that raw net inter-label energy transfer satisfies

when particle exchange is the only source of kinetic-energy change. Since \(0\le K_2\le E\), its Einstein--Helfand growth coefficient is zero. Similarly a net momentum increment defined as \(p_2(t)-p_2(0)\) is bounded on a fixed-energy shell, but purely interparticle momentum transfer need not equal that increment in a system with external scatterer impulses. These bounded observables do not imply zero physical spatial viscosity or conductivity. Nonzero transport must be formulated using spatial, periodic-boundary-corrected currents or reservoirs, rather than a bounded label-energy difference. \subsection{Short English statement}
The source asks about transport and equal kinetic energies in a two-mass billiard. The supplied aid shows that the answer depends on the invariant ensemble and observable: full-shell averages equipartition, a fixed-zero-momentum shell generally does not, and bounded label-energy transfer cannot serve as a nonzero spatial transport coefficient.
