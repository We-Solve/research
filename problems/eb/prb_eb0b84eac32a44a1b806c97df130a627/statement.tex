\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source asks which vector bundles over $S^2\times S^2$ or the spaces extracted as $\C P^2\mathbin{\pm}\C P^2$, with structure group not reducible to a torus, admit $\sec\geq0$. The aid interprets the latter bases as $\C P^2\#\overline{\C P}^{,2}$ and $\C P^2\#\C P^2$, and the metric question as existence of a complete nonnegatively sectionally curved metric on the bundle's total space. Rank, real versus complex category and completeness are not explicit in the original sentence. The trailing ``4 COMPILED BY M. KERIN'' is a page/footer, not an additional condition.

The reported partial result concerns oriented real rank-three bundles $E\to B$, with structure group $SO(3)$, over these closed simply connected oriented four-manifolds. Reduction to a maximal torus $SO(2)$ is equivalent to splitting as $\R\oplus\eta$ with $\eta$ an oriented real plane bundle. The aid gives the exact characteristic-class criterion
\[
 \exists e\in H^2(B;\Z):\quad e\bmod2=w_2(E),\qquad
 e\smile e=p_1(E).
\]
Here $e$ is the Euler class of $\eta$. Sufficiency uses the Dold--Whitney classification, whose admissibility condition is $p_1\bmod4=\mathcal P(w_2)$, with $\mathcal P$ the Pontryagin square. An arbitrary class pair not satisfying this condition is not declared a bundle.

The criterion can be written without hiding parity data. Choose generators $x,y$ and the positive generator $u\in H^4(B;\Z)$, write $w_2=\alpha\bar x+\beta\bar y$ with $\alpha,\beta\in\{0,1\}$ and $p_1=nu$, and require integers $a\equiv\alpha$, $b\equiv\beta\pmod2$. For the respective intersection forms it is
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c}
 B&\text{torus-reduction equation}\\\hline
 S^2\times S^2&n=2ab\\
 \C P^2\#\overline{\C P}^{,2}&n=a^2-b^2\\
 \C P^2\#\C P^2&n=a^2+b^2.
 \end{array}
\]
These equations encode the reported exceptional congruences on the first two bases. On the positive-definite base, the aid reports that after Dold--Whitney admissibility and sum-of-two-squares representability, the required parities can be chosen automatically. For $n>0$, ordinary representability means every prime congruent to $3$ modulo $4$ occurs to even exponent; $n=0$ and negative $n$ require their usual separate treatment. No signature orientation is silently changed.

This classifies their failure of torus reduction, not their total-space curvature. The aid explicitly does \emph{not} decide whether those total spaces admit complete $\sec\geq0$ metrics, and does not answer the original problem in other ranks or bundle categories.
