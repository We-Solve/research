\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a compact connected Riemann surface of genus $g\ge2$. Its holomorphic automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(X)$ is finite. Call $X$ nonarithmetic when its uniformizing cocompact Fuchsian lattice in $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\R)$ is nonarithmetic. The arithmetic/nonarithmetic distinction concerns the uniformizing lattice, not simply the abstract finite automorphism group.

\textbf{Problem 2.17 (nonarithmetic extremal surfaces).} Answer the preceding extremal automorphism questions after restricting to nonarithmetic surfaces. In particular, for prescribed finite-group properties $\mathcal P$, including nilpotence, solvability, and being a $p$-group, find bounds on the orders of the full automorphism groups, show that the appropriate bounds are sharp for infinitely many genera, determine the frequency of sharpness genera, and determine the least genus at which each bound is attained. The prime $p$ is a parameter in the $p$-group question; its quantification should be specified.

A useful precise extremal notation, when the class is nonempty, is
\[
 A_{\mathcal P}^{\mathrm{na}}(g)=\sup\{|\operatorname{Aut}(X)|:
 \operatorname{genus}(X)=g,\ X\text{ nonarithmetic},\
 \operatorname{Aut}(X)\text{ has }\mathcal P\}.
\]
The property is imposed on $\operatorname{Aut}(X)$ itself, as in the neighboring source problem, not merely on an arbitrary subgroup acting on $X$. For proposed sharp lower and upper estimates $L_{\mathcal P}(g)\le A_{\mathcal P}^{\mathrm{na}}(g)\le U_{\mathcal P}(g)$, the frequency and least-genus tasks concern the equality sets
\[
 E^-_{\mathcal P}=\{g:A_{\mathcal P}^{\mathrm{na}}(g)=L_{\mathcal P}(g)\},\qquad
 E^+_{\mathcal P}=\{g:A_{\mathcal P}^{\mathrm{na}}(g)=U_{\mathcal P}(g)\}.
\]
Here the lower estimates concern the extremal attainable order, not a claim that every surface has a large automorphism group. Frequency could require asymptotics of $\#\{g\le G:g\in E^\pm_{\mathcal P}\}$ or the Dirichlet-series criterion used in the neighboring low-symmetry question; the excerpt does not choose a single frequency notion.

The supplied summary lists unrestricted/arithmetic-context models: Hurwitz's $84(g-1)$ bound, Larsen's frequency theorem, Accola's $8(g+3)$ lower bound when $3\mid g$, and Zomorrodian's nilpotent upper bound $16(g-1)$. These are context for seeking nonarithmetic analogues, not claimed answers for the restricted class. The source's final sentence points to a following model result that is not reproduced in this record.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find sharp automorphism-order bounds for nonarithmetic Riemann surfaces, including nilpotent, solvable, and $p$-group full automorphism groups. Determine infinitely many sharp genera, their frequency, and the first genus attaining each extremal bound.
