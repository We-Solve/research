\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\), a Cartan subalgebra is a commutative \(C^*\)-subalgebra \(B\subseteq A\) satisfying the following conditions: \(B\) contains an approximate identity for \(A\); \(B\) is maximal abelian in \(A\); the normalizer
\[
N_A(B)=\{a\in A:aBa^*\subseteq B,\ a^*Ba\subseteq B\}
\]
generates \(A\) as a \(C^*\)-algebra; and there is a faithful conditional expectation \(E:A\to B\). Here a conditional expectation is a completely positive contractive projection onto \(B\) satisfying \(E(b_1ab_2)=b_1E(a)b_2\), and faithfulness means that \(E(a^*a)=0\) implies \(a=0\). A maximal abelian subalgebra is also called a MASA. Write \(\widehat B\) for the character space of \(B\), so that \(B\cong C_0(\widehat B)\).

Let \(\mathcal W\) denote the Razak--Jacelon algebra, let \(\mathcal Z\) denote the Jiang--Su algebra, and let
\[
A_{\mathrm{CAR}}=M_{2^\infty}=\bigotimes_{n=1}^{\infty}M_2(\C),
\]
with the unital connecting embeddings \(x\mapsto x\otimes 1_2\). Let \(\mathcal O_2\) be the universal unital \(C^*\)-algebra generated by isometries \(s_1,s_2\) satisfying \(s_1s_1^*+s_2s_2^*=1\).
