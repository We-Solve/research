\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\textbf{Simultaneous-core containment question.} For coprime positive integers \(p,q\), is the Young diagram of every partition that is simultaneously a \(p\)-core and a \(q\)-core contained in that of the distinguished partition \(\kappa_{p,q}\)? A partition \(\lambda=(\lambda_1\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_\ell>0)\) has diagram \([\lambda]=\{(i,j):1\le j\le\lambda_i\}\). The hook length at \((i,j)\) is \(\lambda_i-j+\lambda'_j-i+1\), where \(\lambda'\) is the conjugate partition. A \(p\)-core has no hook length divisible by \(p\). Both core conditions are required at once; the source is not asking about the union of the separate \(p\)-core and \(q\)-core classes. The parameters are integers, not silently restricted to primes.\par With parts padded by zeros, the desired containment is\[\lambda_i\le(\kappa_{p,q})_i\quad\text{for every }i\ge1.\] A bound only on \(|\lambda|\), or maximality under another partition order, does not by itself give this coordinatewise diagram containment. The record does not define \(\kappa_{p,q}\); its assessment refers to the maximal simultaneous core. Under the usual intended convention, that distinguished partition may be specified by its first-column hook lengths: let \(B_{p,q}=\Z_{>0}\setminus\{ap+bq:a,b\in\Z_{\ge0}\}\), list its finitely many elements as \(b_1>\cdots>b_r\), and set \((\kappa_{p,q})_i=b_i-r+i\). This is an explicit conventional interpretation of the missing notation, to be checked against the source, not a proof of the containment claim. When \(p=1\) or \(q=1\), this gap set and the only simultaneous core are empty; a restriction to \(p,q\ge2\) would isolate the nontrivial cases but is not given in the record.\par The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the question open.
