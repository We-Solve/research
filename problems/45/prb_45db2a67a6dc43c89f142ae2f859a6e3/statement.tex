Let \(\mathcal D\) be a triangulated category intended to be associated with a surface, and let \(K_0(\mathcal D)\) be its Grothendieck group. A locally finite Bridgeland stability condition on \(\mathcal D\) consists of a group homomorphism \(Z:K_0(\mathcal D)\to\C\), called the central charge, and a slicing \(\mathcal P(\phi)\subseteq\mathcal D\), indexed by \(\phi\in\R\), satisfying the following conditions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every nonzero \(E\in\mathcal P(\phi)\) satisfies \(Z([E])=m(E)e^{i\pi\phi}\) for some \(m(E)>0\).
\item \(\mathcal P(\phi+1)=\mathcal P(\phi)[1]\) for every \(\phi\in\R\).
\item If \(\phi_1>\phi_2\), then \(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(E_1,E_2)=0\) whenever \(E_j\in\mathcal P(\phi_j)\).
\item Every nonzero object admits a finite Harder--Narasimhan filtration by distinguished triangles, with nonzero factors in \(\mathcal P(\phi_1),\ldots,\mathcal P(\phi_n)\) for strictly decreasing phases \(\phi_1>\cdots>\phi_n\).
\item There exists \(\varepsilon>0\) such that, for every \(\phi\in\R\), the extension-closed category generated by the \(\mathcal P(\psi)\) with \(\phi-\varepsilon<\psi<\phi+\varepsilon\) has finite length in its natural quasi-abelian structure.
\end{enumerate}
