\section{Fundamental Laws of Biology}\label{75}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 75 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and notation.}A mathematical law for a biological model is a relation between specified state variables, parameters and their evolution, accompanied by a domain of validity. Universality would mean applicability across a declared class of biological systems. The challenge asks whether principles with explanatory and predictive roles comparable to physical laws can be identified; the system class and standard of validation remain to be formulated.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Identify governing principles for biological systems, analogous to physical laws.
\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}A programme, not a quantified conjecture: neither the class of biological systems nor a terminal success criterion is fixed. The historical DARPA mirror was unavailable during this check.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find mathematical principles that govern broad classes of biological systems.
