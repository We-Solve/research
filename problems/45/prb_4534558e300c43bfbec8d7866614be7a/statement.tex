\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 700485}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be an arbitrary group, and let \(a,b\in G\), where \(a\) has infinite order, meaning that \(a^m\ne 1_G\) for every positive integer \(m\). For a subset \(S\subseteq G\), write \(\langle S\rangle\) for the smallest subgroup of \(G\) containing \(S\). For every positive integer \(n\), define
\[
H_n=\langle a^n,b\rangle,
\qquad B=\langle b\rangle=\{b^k:k\in\Z\}.
\]
Find a necessary and sufficient condition on \((G,a,b)\) for
\[
\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}H_n=B.
\]
The requested characterization is to apply to every such group and pair of elements. No restriction on the order of \(b\), or further hypothesis on \(G\), is supplied.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize when, for a group containing an element \(a\) of infinite order and an arbitrary element \(b\), the intersection of all subgroups \(\langle a^n,b\rangle\), over positive integers \(n\), is exactly the cyclic subgroup generated by \(b\).
