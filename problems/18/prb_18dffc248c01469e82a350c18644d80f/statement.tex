\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A subgroup $H\leq G$ is separable if, for every $x\in G\setminus H$, there exist a finite group $Q$ and a homomorphism $q:G\to Q$ with $q(x)\notin q(H)$. Equivalently, $H$ is closed in the profinite topology, or is the intersection of the finite-index subgroups of $G$ containing it.
