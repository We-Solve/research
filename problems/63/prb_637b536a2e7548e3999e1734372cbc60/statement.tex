\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix a real magnetic parameter \(\beta\in\R\) and a prescribed volume \(V>0\). For \(x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)\in\R^3\), define
\[
A_\beta(x)=\frac{\beta}{2}(-x_2,x_1,0).
\]
For a bounded open set \(\Omega\subset\R^3\), let \(H_0^1(\Omega;\C)\) denote the closure of \(C_c^\infty(\Omega;\C)\) in the Sobolev \(H^1\) norm. Define the first Dirichlet eigenvalue of the magnetic operator \((i\nabla+A_\beta)^2\) by
\[
E_1(\Omega,\beta)
=
\inf_{\substack{u\in H_0^1(\Omega;\C)\\u\ne0}}
\frac{\displaystyle\int_\Omega
\left|i\nabla u+A_\beta u\right|^2\,\dd x}
{\displaystyle\int_\Omega |u|^2\,\dd x}.
\]
Thus the Dirichlet boundary condition is imposed through the form domain \(H_0^1(\Omega;\C)\), without assuming a smooth boundary.

Let
\[
\mathcal A_V
=\{\Omega\subset\R^3:\ \Omega\text{ is bounded and open},\ |\Omega|=V\},
\qquad
m_\beta(V)=\inf_{\Omega\in\mathcal A_V}E_1(\Omega,\beta),
\]
where \(|\Omega|\) denotes three-dimensional Lebesgue measure.

The problem asks, for each fixed \(\beta\in\R\) and \(V>0\), to find a domain \(\Omega_*\in\mathcal A_V\) satisfying
\[
E_1(\Omega_*,\beta)=m_\beta(V),
\]
or equivalently
\[
E_1(\Omega_*,\beta)\le E_1(\Omega,\beta)
\quad\text{for every }\Omega\in\mathcal A_V.
\]
No connectedness, boundary regularity, or uniqueness condition is supplied. The source wording does not distinguish an existence theorem for such a domain from an explicit identification or characterization of a minimizing shape; that distinction remains unresolved in this draft.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For a fixed real magnetic parameter and a prescribed positive volume, seek a bounded open subset of \(\R^3\) that minimizes the lowest Dirichlet magnetic eigenvalue among all bounded open sets of that volume.
