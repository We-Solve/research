\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F_n$ be free of finite rank $n$, and let $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n)$. Question 4.4 asks for an algorithm that, on finite descriptions of $\alpha,\beta\in\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$, halts and decides whether
\[
 \exists\theta\in\operatorname{Out}(F_n),\qquad
 \beta=\theta\alpha\theta^{-1}.
\]
One concrete input convention specifies representative automorphisms by their values on a fixed free basis. The condition is then that representatives satisfy $\beta=c_u\circ\theta\circ\alpha\circ\theta^{-1}$ for some automorphism $\theta$ and inner automorphism $c_u$. No complexity bound or output conjugator is demanded. The excerpt does not distinguish algorithms for each fixed rank from a uniform algorithm taking the rank as input.

The source records Martin Lustig's detailed web outline [65], but says neither that proof nor another had been accepted for publication at the time. The registry reports a circa-2007 circulated claim still unpublished and not regarded there as a complete generally accepted solution. This is supplied publication/status information, not a proof assessment by this draft.

The registry lists restricted solutions: Los--Lustig for fully irreducible elements; Krsti\'c--Lustig--Vogtmann for linearly growing elements; Feighn--Handel for UPG (unipotent polynomially growing) elements; and the full rank-three problem in a 2025 paper. Fully irreducible means no positive power preserves the conjugacy class of a proper nontrivial free factor. The UPG restriction includes polynomial growth and unipotence of the action on first homology; it is not silently replaced by an unrestricted all-rank result. The summary reports the general arbitrary-rank case still open.

The source also compares Hemion's mapping-class conjugacy algorithm [52], Mosher's effective pseudo-Anosov algorithm [70], the isomorphism problem for surface-by-$\Z$ groups viewed among geometrizable $3$-manifold groups [76], and Grunewald's conjugacy algorithm for $\operatorname{GL}_n(\Z)$ [39]. These comparisons do not by themselves give an algorithm for $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there a terminating algorithm deciding conjugacy of arbitrary free-group outer automorphisms? The supplied record reports several restricted algorithms and a rank-three solution, while treating general rank as unresolved.
