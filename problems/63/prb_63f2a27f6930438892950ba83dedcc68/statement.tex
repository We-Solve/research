\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an undirected graph \(H=(V(H),E(H))\) and a set \(S\subseteq V(H)\), define the edge cut
\[
\delta_H(S)=\{e\in E(H):e\text{ has one endpoint in }S\text{ and the other in }V(H)\setminus S\}.
\]
For a positive integer \(k\), the cut formulation of \(k\)-edge-connectivity is
\[
|\delta_H(S)|\geq k\qquad\text{for every }\varnothing\neq S\subsetneq V(H).
