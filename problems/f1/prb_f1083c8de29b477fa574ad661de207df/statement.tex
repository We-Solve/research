A^{\times}=\{u\in A:\text{there exists }v\in A\text{ with }uv=1\}.

Let \(F\in A[X,Y]\) be a homogeneous polynomial of degree \(d\geq 3\), assumed in the source to have no multiple factors. Put
\[
S_F(A)=\{(x,y)\in A^2:F(x,y)\in A^{\times}\}.
\]
For \((x,y),(x',y')\in S_F(A)\), define
\[
(x,y)\sim(x',y')
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{there exists }u\in A^{\times}\text{ such that }(x',y')=(ux,uy).
\]
This is the equivalence relation induced by simultaneous multiplication by a unit; homogeneity gives \(F(ux,uy)=u^dF(x,y)\).

\[

(x,y)=(ux_i,uy_i).
