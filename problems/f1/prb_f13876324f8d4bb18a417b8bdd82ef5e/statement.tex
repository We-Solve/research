\section{A one-coupling J-Schur certificate for projected spectral enclosures}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and notation.}For compact $K$, its approximation number is $\alpha_N(K)=\inf_{\operatorname{rank}F<N}\|K-F\|$. Write $\operatorname{spr}(A)=\sup\{|z|:z\in\sigma(A)\}$. Resonances are poles of a suitably continued resolvent; an isoresonant family has the same resonance data. The source uses a J-self-adjoint structure and refers to J as a conjugation; its exact convention must be preserved in a chosen approximation scheme.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
The record bundles source items 17.1--19.1.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Devise projected-space methods for rough rigorous and sharp useful enclosures of spectra of J-self-adjoint operators, incorporating the source’s J structure, and assess consequences for their evolution problems.
\item For Schrödinger operators $-\Delta+V$ on $\mathbb R^d$ with bounded compactly supported potentials, find spectral or pseudospectral data that distinguish members of isoresonant complex-potential families in dimensions $d\ge2$, including nontrivial smooth complex potentials with no resonances. Ask the analogous question for isospectral families in settings such as the circle.
\item For a Banach-space operator $A$ and compact perturbation $K$ with $\alpha_N(K)\to0$, estimate discrete eigenvalue counts of $A+K$ outside disks using resolvent growth near $\sigma(A)$. In particular classify operators whose resolvent has polynomial growth toward the spectrum; classify those admitting $M\ge1$ such that $\|(\lambda-A)^{-1}\|\le M/\operatorname{dist}(\lambda,\sigma(A))$ for every resolvent parameter; and classify those admitting $\|(\lambda-A)^{-1}\|\le M/(|\lambda|-\operatorname{spr}(A))$ whenever $|\lambda|>\operatorname{spr}(A)$.
\end{enumerate}
\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The long counting bound in the PDF motivates the question rather than constituting an additional conjecture. All three requested classifications and both isoresonant settings are retained. J is not silently changed from conjugation to a linear indefinite-metric involution. The catalogue title/status was paired with a machine-generated research attempt; that attempt is not peer-reviewed evidence of settlement.
