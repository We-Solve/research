\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Problem \(18\) asks to deduce ``fact \(2\)'' from ``fact \(1\),'' or conversely, or to give a Lie-algebraic proof of fact \(2\). The facts themselves are absent from the canonical excerpt. The aid interprets fact \(1\) as critical conformal covariance of the flat operator \(P=\Delta^{n/2}\) in even dimension, and fact \(2\) as
\[
 \Delta^{n/2}\log\Omega=0
\]
for a positive flat-to-flat conformal scale. This reconstruction and its conventions must be checked against the preceding source text.

For a local Euclidean conformal map \(\phi\), write \(\phi^*g_0=\Omega_\phi^2g_0\) and \(b_\phi=\log\Omega_\phi\). The intended critical covariance is
\[
 P(f\circ\phi)=\Omega_\phi^n(Pf)\circ\phi,
\]
so conformal pullback preserves \(\ker P\). Logarithmic scales satisfy
\[
 b_{\phi\circ\psi}=b_\psi+b_\phi\circ\psi.
\]
For a conformal Killing field \(X\), the infinitesimal scale is \(\sigma_X=(\operatorname{div}X)/n\); in Euclidean dimension at least three it is affine and hence belongs to \(\ker\Delta^{n/2}\).

The aid's abstract integration lemma says: if a connected Lie group acts continuously and smoothly on a function space preserving the closed kernel of a linear differential operator \(P\), and a smooth function-valued group \(1\)-cocycle has every infinitesimal value in that kernel, then all its finite cocycle values lie in the kernel. Applied to logarithmic scales on a compatible conformal transformation domain, it yields the claimed implication from covariance to fact \(2\). Passing from a connected transformation-group argument to all local flat-to-flat changes requires the usual local classification/generator and component checks; these details are not in the summary. The aid also reports an independent Schouten recurrence. In dimension two the separate holomorphic/antiholomorphic description gives harmonic logarithmic scale wherever the derivative is nonzero. No inference is made across singularities of a conformal map.
