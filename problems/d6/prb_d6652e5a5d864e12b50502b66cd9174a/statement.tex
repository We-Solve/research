\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


Spectral independence bounds the operator norm or largest eigenvalue of the source\textquotesingle s conditional influence matrices, uniformly after fixing subsets of coordinates. Mixing is measured by closeness to the stationary law, and $n$ is the number of coordinates.

A Markov chain evolves according to a transition kernel; mixing time measures convergence of its law to the stationary law, usually in total variation. Glauber dynamics updates a single coordinate at a time according to its conditional distribution.
