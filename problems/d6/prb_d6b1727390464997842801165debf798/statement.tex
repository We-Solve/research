\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite non-cyclic group \(G\), its \emph{normal covering number} is
\[
\gamma(G)=\min\left\{r\in\N:\ r\geq1,\ \exists H_1,\ldots,H_r<G,\quad G=\bigcup_{i=1}^{r}\ \bigcup_{g\in G}gH_i g^{-1}\right\},
\]
where \(H_i<G\) means that \(H_i\) is a proper subgroup. Let \(S_n\) and \(A_n\) denote the symmetric and alternating groups on \(n\) letters. All limits below are taken as \(n\to\infty\) through the indicated parity. We may restrict to \(n\geq4\), so both groups are non-cyclic; discarding finitely many indices does not change these limits.

The original questions of D. Bubboloni, C. E. Praeger, and P. Spiga are to determine the following exact values:
\begin{align*}
\text{(ae)}\quad&\liminf_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ even}}}\frac{\gamma(S_n)}{n},\\
\text{(ao)}\quad&\liminf_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ odd}}}\frac{\gamma(S_n)}{n},\\
\text{(b)}\quad&\liminf_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ even}}}\frac{\gamma(A_n)}{n}\quad\text{and}\quad\liminf_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ odd}}}\frac{\gamma(A_n)}{n},\\
\text{(c)}\quad&\limsup_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ even}}}\frac{\gamma(S_n)}{n},\\
\text{(d)}\quad&\limsup_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ odd}}}\frac{\gamma(A_n)}{n}\quad\text{and}\quad\limsup_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ even}}}\frac{\gamma(A_n)}{n}.
\end{align*}
The supplied record labels the overall problem partially solved. Its background reports that parts (ae), (c), and (d) are solved, with respective values
\[
\liminf_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ even}}}\frac{\gamma(S_n)}n=\frac16,
\qquad
\limsup_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ even}}}\frac{\gamma(S_n)}n=\frac14,
\]
\[
\limsup_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ odd}}}\frac{\gamma(A_n)}n=\frac13,
\qquad
\limsup_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ even}}}\frac{\gamma(A_n)}n=\frac14.
\]
These reported resolutions are attributed to S. Eberhard and C. Mellon (2025). The remaining exact-value questions in the supplied record are (ao) and both quantities in (b). Its comments of 2025, attributed to the same reference, report

\frac1{12}\leq\liminf_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ odd}}}\frac{\gamma(S_n)}n\leq\frac16,

\qquad
\frac16\leq\liminf_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ odd}}}\frac{\gamma(A_n)}n\leq\frac4{15}.
\]
The statement also reports as known that

\limsup_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\text{ odd}}}\frac{\gamma(S_n)}n=\frac12.
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine the exact asymptotic lower and upper limits, separately for even and odd degrees, of the normal covering numbers of symmetric and alternating groups divided by their degrees. The supplied record reports solutions to (ae), (c), and (d), leaving the exact lower limits in (ao) and (b) unresolved, with the stated 2025 bounds.
