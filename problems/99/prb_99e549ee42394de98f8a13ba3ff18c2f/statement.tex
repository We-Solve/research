Let \(\zeta\) be an invariant level and put \(Z=\boldsymbol\mu^{-1}(\zeta)\), \(Q=Z/G\). With coefficients in a chosen ring \(R\), the equivariant restriction is
\[
 H_G^*(W;R)\longrightarrow H_G^*(Z;R).

\[
