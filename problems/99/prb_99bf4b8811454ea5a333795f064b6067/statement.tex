\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be a smooth closed \(3\)-manifold, where closed means compact without boundary, and let \(\alpha\) be a contact form, so that \(\alpha\wedge\dd\alpha\) is nowhere zero. Its Reeb vector field \(R_\alpha\) is characterized by
\[
\alpha(R_\alpha)=1,\qquad \dd\alpha(R_\alpha,\cdot)=0.
\]
Write \(\varphi^t\) for its flow and \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(\varphi^1)\) for the topological entropy of its time-one map. An invariant torus means a smoothly embedded torus \(T\subset M\) satisfying \(\varphi^t(T)=T\) for every \(t\in\R\).

Under the usual compact-surface convention, a global surface of section for a nonsingular flow on a \(3\)-manifold \(N\) is a compact embedded surface \(S\subset N\), possibly with boundary, whose boundary consists of periodic trajectories, whose interior is transverse to the flow, and such that every trajectory in \(N\setminus\partial S\) meets \(\operatorname{int}S\) in both positive and negative time.
