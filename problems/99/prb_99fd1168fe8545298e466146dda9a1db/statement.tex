\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathcal D\) be a triangulated category and let \(F\colon\mathcal D\to\mathcal D\) be an exact endofunctor. A Bridgeland stability condition \(\sigma=(Z,\mathcal P)\) consists of a central charge \(Z\colon K_0(\mathcal D)\to\C\) and a slicing satisfying the stability-condition axioms; in particular, every nonzero object has a finite Harder--Narasimhan filtration with semistable factors of strictly decreasing phases. If these factors for \(E\ne0\) are \(A_1,\ldots,A_r\), define its mass by
\[
 m_\sigma(E)=\sum_{j=1}^r |Z(A_j)|,
 \qquad m_\sigma(0)=0.
\]
A standard mass-growth convention at parameter zero is
\[
 h_\sigma(F)=\sup_{E\in\mathcal D,\ E\ne0}
 \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\log m_\sigma(F^nE),
\]
with \(\log 0=-\infty\).

 E_{j-1}\longrightarrow E_j\longrightarrow G[k_j]
