\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S^1=\R/\Z\), and let \(\mathrm{Diff}^{\omega}_+(S^1)\) be the group, under composition, of orientation-preserving real-analytic diffeomorphisms of \(S^1\). For a group \(G\), write \(G'=[G,G]\) for its commutator subgroup and \(G''=[G',G']\). The group \(G\) is metabelian if \(G''=\{1\}\). Let \(F_2\) denote the free group on two generators.

Under the interpretation that the source's phrase ``free subgroup'' means a nonabelian free subgroup, its explicit question is whether

\forall G\leq \mathrm{Diff}^{\omega}_+(S^1),\qquad
G''\neq\{1\}\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists\text{ an injective group homomorphism }F_2\longrightarrow G.
