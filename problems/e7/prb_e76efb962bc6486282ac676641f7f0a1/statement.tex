\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Given a modulus $N$ and prescribed degrees $d_f,d_g$, construct irreducible coprime polynomials
\[
 f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{d_f}a_ix^i,\qquad
 g(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{d_g}b_jx^j\quad\text{in }\Z[x],
 \qquad a_{d_f}b_{d_g}\ne0,
\]
with a common root modulo $N$ and coefficients of minimal possible absolute size. In the usual nontrivial setting, $N$ is an integer greater than one and the degrees are positive integers; the source does not explicitly specify their admissible ranges or impose compositeness of $N$.

Having a common root means
\[
 \exists r\in\Z/N\Z:\qquad f(r)=g(r)=0\quad\text{in }\Z/N\Z.
\]
The source does not prescribe $r$, so it is a variable of the construction rather than fixed input. The standard polynomial-selection interpretation of coprime is $\gcd_{\Q[x]}(f,g)=1$. It cannot mean $(f,g)=\Z[x]$ for $N>1$, since a polynomial identity $Af+Bg=1$ with $A,B\in\Z[x]$ would contradict the common-root condition upon evaluation modulo $N$. The intended irreducibility convention and any primitive or content normalization should be checked against the report.

The optimization objective is unresolved. For example, one possible scalar objective would minimize
\[
 H(f,g)=\max\bigl(\{|a_i|:0\le i\le d_f\}\cup\{|b_j|:0\le j\le d_g\}\bigr)
\]
over all admissible pairs, but the record does not select this objective rather than separate heights, weighted or skewed norms, or another comparison of coefficients. Nor does it supply a conjectural minimum, asymptotic rate in $N$, or computational complexity requirement. Thus the requested construction must retain the prescribed degrees, irreducibility, coprimality, common modular root, and a genuinely optimal coefficient criterion once that criterion is recovered; the example height above is not silently adopted as the source's definition.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The problem asks for optimally small integer polynomials of prescribed degrees that are irreducible, coprime over the rationals, and share a root modulo the input modulus. The exact meaning of optimal coefficient size and the admissible input conventions still need specification.
