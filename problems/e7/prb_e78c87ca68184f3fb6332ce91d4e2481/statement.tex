\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite subset \(X\) of an abelian group and integer \(n\geq1\), write \(nX=\{x_1+\cdots+x_n:x_i\in X\}\). The source, attributed to N. Katz, asks whether there exists finite \(A\subseteq\Z\) such that
\[
 |2A|=k|A|,\qquad
 |nA'|\geq f(n,k)|A'|\quad\text{for every proper }A'\subsetneq A,
\]
where \(f(n,k)\) is defined in the preceding Problem 3.16. That preceding definition is not included in this record. The supplied verification emphasizes that its defining doubling constraint is strict, whereas this question places \(A\) on the equality boundary; the inequality for proper subsets is indeed \(\geq\), not an extraction reversal.

The aid treats a literal reading with fixed integer \(n\geq2\), real \(k>1\), and \(|A|\geq2\). To make one natural extremal reading explicit, define
\[
 f_{\mathrm{nat}}(n,k)=\sup\left\{\frac{|nX|}{|X|}:\varnothing\ne X\subseteq\Z\text{ finite},\ |2X|<k|X|\right\}.
\]
This displayed definition is a conditional reconstruction of the absent previous problem, not a verified quotation of it. Under this reading the reported singleton obstruction is
\[
 f_{\mathrm{nat}}(n,k)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad1<k\leq\tfrac32.
\]
Since every singleton is a proper subset of \(A\) when \(|A|\geq2\), the required inequality forces \(f_{\mathrm{nat}}(n,k)\leq1\). The aid's reported classification for this reading is
\[
 \exists A\subseteq\Z\text{ finite},\ |A|\geq2,\ |2A|=k|A|,
 \ \forall\varnothing\ne A'\subsetneq A:\ |nA'|\geq f_{\mathrm{nat}}(n,k)|A'|
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad k=\tfrac32.
\]
At \(k=3/2\), precisely the two-element sets \(A\) work. Thus, if \(k\) was intended to be an integer at least two, this reading has a uniformly negative answer. This is not a classification for an unspecified different definition of \(f\), for \(n=1\), or for alternative subset conventions.

The aid also gives a cutoff obstruction independent of that missing definition. For every integer \(n\geq1\), every finite \(A\) in any abelian group, and every integer \(1\leq r<|A|\), it reports
\[
 \min_{\substack{A'\subsetneq A\\|A'|\geq r}}
 \frac{|nA'|}{|A'|}\leq\frac1r\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}.
\]
Thus a repair requiring the inequality only for proper subsets of cardinality at least \(r\) still cannot demand a larger universal lower-growth factor than this cap. The case \(r=1\) recovers the singleton obstruction. The source's unqualified reference to every proper subset also includes the empty subset; with the usual \(n\varnothing=\varnothing\) for \(n\geq1\) and a finite factor, that case adds only \(0\geq0\).
\subsection{Short English statement}
The preceding definition of \(f(n,k)\) is missing. For the aid's natural strict-doubling extremal reading, singleton proper subsets collapse the desired lower bound, leaving exactly two-element sets at doubling ratio \(3/2\). A separate cardinality-cutoff bound applies in every abelian group.
