\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Hermitian metric on a complex manifold \(X\) is represented by a smooth positive real \((1,1)\)-form \(\omega\). It is K\"ahler if \(\dd\omega=0\), and non-K\"ahler otherwise. A non-K\"ahler metric need not be on a manifold that admits no K\"ahler metric; the supplied question does not distinguish these settings.

For reference, the classical K\"ahler problem mentioned in the source has the following formulation. Let \(X\) be a compact connected complex manifold of complex dimension \(n\geq 1\), and let \(\omega\) be a K\"ahler form. For every \(F\in C^\infty(X,\R)\) satisfying
\[
\int_X e^F\omega^n=\int_X\omega^n,
\]
Yau's theorem gives a unique \(\varphi\in C^\infty(X,\R)\), normalized by \(\sup_X\varphi=0\), such that
\[
\omega_\varphi:=\omega+\sqrt{-1}\,\partial\bar\partial\varphi>0,
\qquad
\omega_\varphi^n=e^F\omega^n.
\]
The associated Calabi-conjecture statement prescribes the Ricci form within a fixed K\"ahler class: for every smooth real closed \((1,1)\)-form \(\rho\) representing \(2\pi c_1(X)\) in real de Rham cohomology, there is a unique K\"ahler form \(\widetilde\omega\) in \([\omega]\) with \(\operatorname{Ric}(\widetilde\omega)=\rho\). Here \(c_1(X)\) is the first Chern class of the holomorphic tangent bundle, and, locally for a Hermitian metric matrix \((g_{j\bar k})\), the convention is
\[
\operatorname{Ric}(\omega)=-\sqrt{-1}\,\partial\bar\partial\log\det(g_{j\bar k}).
\]
These are reference formulations of the classical results, not additional hypotheses supplied for the requested generalization.
