Let \(V=(V,Y,\mathbf{1},\omega)\) be a vertex operator algebra over \(\C\), with vacuum \(\mathbf{1}\), conformal vector \(\omega\), and mode notation
\[
Y(a,z)=\sum_{m\in\Z}a_m z^{-m-1},\qquad L(0)=\omega_1.
\]
Being of \emph{CFT type} means that
\[
V=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}V_n,\qquad
V_n=\{v\in V:L(0)v=nv\},\qquad
\dim_{\C}V_n<\infty,\qquad V_0=\C\mathbf{1}.
\]
Define
\[
C_2(V)=\operatorname{span}_{\C}\{a_{-2}b:a,b\in V\}.
\]
The algebra is \(C_2\)-cofinite if \(\dim_{\C}(V/C_2(V))<\infty\). Under the convention used here, \(V\) is \emph{rational} if every admissible \(V\)-module is a direct sum of irreducible admissible modules. Here an admissible module is a weak \(V\)-module with a grading \(M=\bigoplus_{r\geq0}M(r)\) satisfying
\[
a_m M(r)\subseteq M(r+n-m-1)
\quad(a\in V_n,\ m\in\Z),
\]
where \(M(s)=0\) for \(s<0\).

The VOA automorphism group is
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(V)=\left\{g\in\operatorname{GL}_{\C}(V):
\begin{array}{l}
g\mathbf{1}=\mathbf{1},\quad g\omega=\omega,\\
gY(a,z)b=Y(ga,z)gb\quad\text{for all }a,b\in V
\end{array}\right\}.
\]
