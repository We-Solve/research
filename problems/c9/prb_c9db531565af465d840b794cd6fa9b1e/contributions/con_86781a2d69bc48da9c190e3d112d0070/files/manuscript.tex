\paragraph{Continuous symmetries already need coherence data.} On \(L^2(\mathbb R)\), define \(U(a,b)\psi(x)=e^{ibx}\psi(x+a)\), for real \(a,b\). These operators are unitary. Continuity on compactly supported smooth functions follows by dominated convergence and uniform continuity; density and the unitary norm bound extend strong continuity to every \(L^2\) vector. Direct multiplication gives
\[
 U(a,b)U(a',b')=e^{iab'}U(a+a',b+b').
\]
Thus the additive group \(\mathbb R^2\) acts projectively, with continuous scalar comparison \(c(g,h)=e^{iab'}\), where \(g=(a,b)\), \(h=(a',b')\).
\paragraph{Associativity becomes a coherence equation.} For \(k=(a'',b'')\),
\[
 c(g,h)c(g+h,k)
 =e^{i(ab'+ab''+a'b'')}
 =c(h,k)c(g,h+k).
\]
This is exactly the consistency of the two triple-product parenthesizations. It permits the honest central extension with multiplication \((g,z)(h,w)=(g+h,zwc(g,h))\). Translation and modulation also have commutator phase \(e^{iab}\), which a discrete list of symmetry labels would fail to record.
