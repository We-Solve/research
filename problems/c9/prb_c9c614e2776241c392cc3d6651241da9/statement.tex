\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\Gamma$ be a finitely generated Gromov-hyperbolic group, equipped with a word metric, and let $\partial_\infty\Gamma$ be its boundary. For a basepoint $o$, a visual metric has the form
\[
 C^{-1}a^{-(\xi|\eta)_o}\leq d_v(\xi,\eta)
       \leq Ca^{-(\xi|\eta)_o}\qquad(\xi\ne\eta)
\]
for suitable $a>1$, $C\geq1$ and the extended Gromov product. Write $\dim$ for topological covering dimension. The record asks three separate questions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is $\dim\partial_\infty\Gamma=\operatorname{mdim}(\partial_\infty\Gamma,d_v)$ for a visual metric?
\item What is the exact value of $\operatorname{hdim}(\mathbb H^2\times\mathbb H^2)$, with the usual product metric on the two hyperbolic planes?
\item For every noncompact symmetric space $X$ without Euclidean factors, is $\operatorname{hdim}(X)\geq\dim X$?
\end{enumerate}
Here $\operatorname{hdim}$ is the report's \emph{hyperbolic dimension}, not Hausdorff dimension, and $\operatorname{mdim}$ is its boundary metric-dimension invariant. The supplied extraction does not define either invariant. In particular, the latter cannot be silently identified with Hausdorff dimension, mean dimension, capacity dimension, or an Assouad-type dimension: their definitions and metric dependence differ. A source-specific cover definition is needed to make the first and the hyperbolic-dimension questions fully precise. The boundary degeneracies for elementary hyperbolic groups and the choice of visual metric parameters are also not discussed.

For comparison, asymptotic dimension $\operatorname{asdim}X\leq n$ means that for every scale $R>0$ there is a uniformly bounded cover with $R$-multiplicity at most $n+1$. The supplied assessment reports the Gromov asymptotic-dimension question from the passage as proved by Buyalo and Lebedeva; the usual associated hyperbolic-group formula is $\operatorname{asdim}\Gamma=\dim\partial_\infty\Gamma+1$ in its non-elementary setting.
