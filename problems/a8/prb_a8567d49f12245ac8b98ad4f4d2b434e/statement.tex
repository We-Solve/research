A \(G\)-flow is a nonempty compact Hausdorff space \(X\) with a continuous action \(G\times X\to X\). It is minimal if \(\overline{G\cdot x}=X\) for every \(x\in X\). A point \(x\in X\) is generic if its orbit \(G\cdot x\) is comeager in \(X\), meaning that \(X\setminus(G\cdot x)\) is a countable union of nowhere dense subsets of \(X\).
