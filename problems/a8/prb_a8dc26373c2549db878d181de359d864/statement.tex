\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 800295}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(D^2=\{x\in\R^2:\|x\|\leq 1\}\) be the closed disk, with its standard orientation, and let \(\partial D^2=\{x\in\R^2:\|x\|=1\}\). Under composition, define
\[
G=\{f:D^2\to D^2\mid f\text{ is an orientation-preserving homeomorphism and }f(x)=x\text{ for every }x\in\partial D^2\}.
\]
A group is \emph{left orderable} if it admits a strict total order \(\prec\) such that
\[
\forall a,b,h\in G,\qquad a\prec b\ \Longrightarrow\ h\circ a\prec h\circ b.
\]
Here a strict total order is an irreflexive, transitive relation for which exactly one of \(a\prec b\) and \(b\prec a\) holds whenever \(a\ne b\).

\textbf{Question (Navas, 2017, Question 9).} Is \(G\) left orderable? The question concerns the group itself under composition; no continuity requirement on the order is specified.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the group of all orientation-preserving homeomorphisms of the closed two-dimensional disk that fix every boundary point admit a total order preserved by left composition?
