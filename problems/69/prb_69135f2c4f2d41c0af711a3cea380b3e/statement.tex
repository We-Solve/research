\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Write \(\R[x]=\R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\), and let \(\Sigma_{\R}[x]\) be the set of finite sums of squares of polynomials in \(\R[x]\). For real polynomials \(g_1,\ldots,g_r\), define
\[
K=\{x\in\R^n:g_i(x)\geq0\text{ for }1\leq i\leq r\},\qquad
Q_{\R}(g)=\left\{\sigma_0+\sum_{i=1}^r\sigma_i g_i:\sigma_i\in\Sigma_{\R}[x]\right\}.
\]
Thus \(p\in1+Q_{\R}(g)\) means that
\[
p-1=\sum_j h_{0j}^2+\sum_{i=1}^r g_i\sum_j h_{ij}^2
\]
for finitely many real polynomials \(h_{ij}\). Such an identity is called a rational sum-of-squares certificate here if every \(h_{ij}\) belongs to \(\Q[x]\). The assertion that every certificate requires irrational coefficients means that a real certificate exists but no certificate of this rational form exists; it does not merely mean that some coefficients of the expanded multipliers are irrational.

For an integer \(d\geq0\), let \(A_d=\{\alpha\in\Z_{\geq0}^n:|\alpha|\leq d\}\). A rational truncated moment vector is \(y=(y_\alpha)_{\alpha\in A_d}\in\Q^{A_d}\), with associated functional
\[
L_y\!\left(\sum_{\alpha\in A_d}p_\alpha x^\alpha\right)=\sum_{\alpha\in A_d}p_\alpha y_\alpha.
\]
A representing measure on \(K\) is a finite positive Borel measure supported on \(K\), with the indicated monomials integrable, such that \(y_\alpha=\int_K x^\alpha\,\dd\mu(x)\) for every \(\alpha\in A_d\). No normalization \(y_0=1\) is supplied.

Under the usual strict interpretation of a separating polynomial, the extracted question asks whether there exist data as above and \(p\in\Q[x]\) of degree at most \(d\) such that \(y\) has no representing measure on \(K\),

\]
but there are no finite families \(q_{ij}\in\Q[x]\) satisfying
\[
p-1=\sum_j q_{0j}^2+\sum_{i=1}^r g_i\sum_j q_{ij}^2.
\]
Here membership in \(1+Q_{\R}(g)\) gives \(p\geq1\) on \(K\), so the displayed negative evaluation expresses separation from moment vectors of positive measures.
