\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
This record merges three questions. First, for free group factors $A,B$ with a common diffuse von Neumann subalgebra $D$, what hypotheses ensure that $A*_D B$ is a free group factor? The damaged ``$A?DB$'' is interpreted as the tracial amalgamated free product, requiring compatible traces and trace-preserving conditional expectations. Second, does a regular weakly-rigid subalgebra, in Popa's sense, force free entropy dimension at most one? Regularity means that its unitary normalizer generates the ambient algebra; the precise weak-rigidity convention and the generating tuple/invariant are not defined in the source. Third, if a finitely generated group $\Gamma=\langle\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_n\rangle$ has large first $L^2$-Betti number, must the microstates free entropy dimension of its group generators be large? The source attributes the nonmicrostates counterpart to Mineyev--Shlyakhtenko, but does not quantify ``large.''

The aid's explicit sufficient condition for the first question is the asymmetric free-factor identity. If $H\hookrightarrow G$ is injective, with canonical group traces and inclusions, then
\[
L(H*K)*_{L(H)}L(G)\cong L(K*G).
\]
For finite integers $p,q\geq2$, take $H=\Z$, $K=F_{p-1}$ and $G=F_q$. The cyclic subgroup is a free factor on the $F_p$ side, but its injection into $F_q$ can be arbitrary. The result is
\[
L(F_p)*_{L(\Z)}L(F_q)\cong L(F_{p+q-1}).
\]
This is a sufficient diffuse-amalgam criterion, not a classification of all diffuse embeddings or all interpolated-factor conventions.

Put $r=p+q-1$ and let $X$ be the natural selfadjoint group tuple formed from the real and imaginary parts of free generator unitaries. The aid asserts
\[
\delta_0(X)=\delta^*(X)=\beta_1^{(2)}(F_r)-\beta_0^{(2)}(F_r)+1=r,
\qquad\beta_1^{(2)}(F_r)=r-1,\quad\beta_0^{(2)}(F_r)=0.
\]
Here $\delta_0$ is the microstates invariant in the specified tuple convention, while $\delta^*$ denotes the supplied nonmicrostates version; exact variant choices must be matched to the reference. Equality for these free generators does not establish a universal microstates lower bound for arbitrary groups.

The aid also reports an entropy-regularity obstruction: a diffuse regular subalgebra of finite $1$-bounded entropy forces every finite ambient generating tuple to have $\delta_0\leq1$, under the applicable entropy and embeddability conventions. It says the diffuse hyperfinite cyclic amalgam in the displayed free-group example cannot generate the ambient factor through any ordinal spectral-normalizer chain. The precise spectral-normalizer operation, successor and limit rules, and entropy hypotheses are not defined in the record, so this is a reported entropy-preservation obstruction requiring those definitions, rather than a theorem about an arbitrarily chosen normalizer chain.

Accordingly, a proposed universal lower bound $\delta_0(X)\geq\beta_1^{(2)}(\Gamma)-\beta_0^{(2)}(\Gamma)+1$ would be incompatible with the stated regular finite-entropy condition for infinite groups with $\beta_1^{(2)}(\Gamma)>0$. This conditional incompatibility does not disprove the broad third question, assert that such a group/subalgebra combination exists, or show that all Popa weakly-rigid regular inclusions satisfy the aid's finite-entropy hypothesis. Those bridges and the general three questions remain outside the supplied special-case synthesis.
