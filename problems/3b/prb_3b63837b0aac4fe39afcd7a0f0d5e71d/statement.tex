\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $k,n$ be integers with $n\geq k+1\geq4$, and let $H=(V,E)$ be a $k$-uniform hypergraph with $|V|=n$ and $E\subseteq\binom Vk$. For $S\in\binom V{k-1}$ define its codegree by \[d_H(S)=|\{v\in V\setminus S:S\cup\{v\}\in E\}|,\qquad\delta_{k-1}(H)=\min_{S\in\binom V{k-1}}d_H(S).\] A tight Hamiltonian cycle is a cyclic ordering $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ of all vertices such that $\{v_i,v_{i+1},\ldots,v_{i+k-1}\}\in E$ for every $i$, with indices taken modulo $n$. The conjecture asks whether \[\delta_{k-1}(H)\geq\left\lfloor\frac{n-k+3}{2}\right\rfloor\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H\text{ contains a tight Hamiltonian cycle}.\] The source quantifies over the full stated range, without a sufficiently-large-$n$ qualification, an asymptotic error, or a divisibility condition on $n$. The supplied 2026-08-21 literature triage labels the record partially solved, reports a proved $3$-uniform case and important further ranges, and says general $k$-uniform tight Hamilton-cycle thresholds remain open. It does not state the precise size hypotheses of those partial results, so they are not silently promoted to the full parameter range above.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does minimum codegree at least $\lfloor(n-k+3)/2\rfloor$ force a cyclic ordering in which every $k$ consecutive vertices form an edge, for every $n\geq k+1\geq4$? The supplied assessment reports partial results without a general resolution.
