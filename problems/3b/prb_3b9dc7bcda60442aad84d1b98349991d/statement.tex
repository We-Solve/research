\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 11100052}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathcal M\) be a suitably specified symmetric monoidal model category and \(P\) an operad, not assumed cofibrant. Write \(\operatorname{Alg}_P(\mathcal M)\) for its algebra category. Find hypotheses generalizing the Schwede--Shipley monoid axiom that give the intended model structure, naturally the transferred one whose weak equivalences and fibrations are detected on underlying objects. The monoid axiom requires pushouts and transfinite composites generated by maps \(j\otimes X\), for trivial cofibrations \(j\), to be weak equivalences. The desired framework should cover suitable all-objects-fibrant cases as examples; fibrancy alone is not asserted sufficient.

Also find additional hypotheses under which an aritywise weak equivalence of operads \(\phi:P\to Q\) induces a Quillen equivalence
\[
 \phi_!: \operatorname{Alg}_P(\mathcal M)
 \ \rightleftarrows\ \operatorname{Alg}_Q(\mathcal M):\phi^*.
\]
Here restriction \(\phi^*\) has extension-of-algebras left adjoint when the required constructions exist, and Quillen equivalence means the derived adjunction is an equivalence of homotopy categories. Noncofibrancy as an operad and failure of underlying \(\Sigma\)-cofibrancy are different conditions; the needed convention is not specified.

The intended application compares an \(E_\infty\) operad with the commutative operad. The source says \(E_\infty\) should remain usable where strict commutativity fails and should not alter the homotopy theory when strict commutativity works under the added comparison hypotheses. Neither an unconditional transferred-model theorem nor rectification solely from commutative admissibility is asserted. The ambient cofibrant-generation/smallness, unit, tensor, symmetry, flatness and operad hypotheses are precisely what the problem asks to determine.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find admissibility and rectification conditions for algebras over noncofibrant operads, extending the monoid axiom and covering suitable fibrant settings, especially the \(E_\infty\)-to-commutative comparison.
