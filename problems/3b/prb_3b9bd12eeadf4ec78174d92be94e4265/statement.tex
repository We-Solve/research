\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A compact quantum group is a pair \(\mathbb G=(A,\Delta)\), where \(A\) is a nonzero unital \(C^*\)-algebra and \(\Delta:A\to A\otimes_{\min}A\) is a unital \(*\)-homomorphism satisfying
\[
(\Delta\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\Delta)\Delta,
\]
with both linear spans \(\Delta(A)(1\otimes A)\) and \(\Delta(A)(A\otimes1)\) dense in \(A\otimes_{\min}A\). Let \(h\) be its Haar state, characterized by
\[
(h\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta(a)=(\mathrm{id}\otimes h)\Delta(a)=h(a)1\qquad(a\in A).
\]
If \(\pi_h\) denotes the Gelfand--Naimark--Segal representation associated with \(h\), the reduced \(C^*\)-algebra is
\[
C_r(\mathbb G)=\overline{\pi_h(A)}^{\|\cdot\|}.
\]
A nonzero \(C^*\)-algebra \(B\) is simple if every norm-closed two-sided ideal \(I\subseteq B\) satisfies \(I=\{0\}\) or \(I=B\). For unital \(B\), write \(K_0(B)\) for the Grothendieck group of stable Murray--von Neumann equivalence classes of projections in matrix algebras over \(B\), and write
\[
K_1(B)=\varinjlim_n\pi_0\bigl(U(M_n(B))\bigr),
\]
where \(\pi_0\) uses norm-continuous paths and stabilization is \(u\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(u,1)\).
