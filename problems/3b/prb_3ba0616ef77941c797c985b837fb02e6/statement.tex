\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For \(n\geq2\), let \(B_n\) be the Artin braid group generated by \(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\), with
\[\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1},\qquad
\sigma_i\sigma_j=\sigma_j\sigma_i\quad(|i-j|\geq2).\]
A representation over a field \(k\) is a homomorphism \(\rho:B_n\to\operatorname{GL}_k(V)\) for a nonzero \(k\)-vector space \(V\). It is irreducible when its only invariant linear subspaces are \(0\) and \(V\). Two representations are equivalent when a linear isomorphism intertwines their actions. The source asks to classify all irreducible representations of \(B_n\): describe the equivalence classes, with an exhaustive construction and a criterion for equivalence.
