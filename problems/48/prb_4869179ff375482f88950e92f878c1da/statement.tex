\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $f$ have a Taylor expansion $f(z)=\sum_{k\ge0}a_kz^k$ at zero. The source prints the matrix definition
\[
H_p^{(n)}=(a_{ij})_{1\le i,j\le p+1},\qquad
a_{ij}=a_{n+1+j-2}=a_{n+j-1}.
\]
Literally, the entries do not depend on $i$, so all rows coincide and the matrices have rank at most one. This cannot be silently identified with the usual Hankel construction. A natural possible correction is $a_{ij}=a_{n+i+j-2}$, whose entries are constant along anti-diagonals, but the intended formula must be checked in the source.

\varepsilon_1^{(n)}\ge\varepsilon_2^{(n)}\ge\cdots\ge\varepsilon_p^{(n)}\ge0
