\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $p_n$ be the increasing sequence of primes and $g_n=p_{n+1}-p_n$. The historical source attributes to D. R. Heath--Brown (1982), under RH and Montgomery's pair-correlation conjecture, the conclusion
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{g_n}{\log p_n}=0.
\]
T. H. Chan asks for a converse from small normalized prime gaps to pair correlation; the source notes that the small-gap assertion was unconditional post-GPY. J. Pintz expresses serious doubt. The same source asks, through J. B. Conrey, whether random matrix theory can improve Heath--Brown's conditional maximum-gap bound. K. Soundararajan expresses strong doubt that it should help.

The extracted gap inequality has damaged typography. The attached aid specifically repairs it as
\[
g_n\ll\sqrt{p_n\log p_n},
\]
with the entire product under the radical, under the same RH and pair-correlation hypotheses. This is an attributed repair, rather than a silent choice between $\sqrt{p_n}\log p_n$ and $\sqrt{p_n\log p_n}$. The exact normalization and uniformity of Montgomery's $F(\alpha)$ are not supplied, so the converse is a research question rather than a fully specified equivalence here.

The aid supplies an obstruction to one natural $L^2$/positivity argument. Set $\theta(x)=\sum_{p\le x}\log p$ and
\[
J_\theta(Y,h)=\int_Y^{2Y}|\theta(x+h)-\theta(x)-h|^2\,\dd x.
\]
If a prime-free gap of length $G$ lies so that all starting points of its internal length-$h$ intervals belong to $[Y,2Y]$, then its contribution is at least $(G-h)h^2$. At $h=2G/3$ this is $4G^3/27$. The aid uses this optimized single-gap contribution to expose a square-root barrier in that particular method, not a universal obstruction to all random-matrix arguments.

Separately, it reports a generalized-prime point-set countermodel. Start with a recursion $a_{m+1}=a_m+\log a_m$ for $a_1>1$, and add suitably lacunary consecutive pairs $q_j,q_j+1$. The resulting increasing point set $B=\{b_n\}$ is claimed to satisfy
\[
\#\{b_n\le x\}\sim\frac{x}{\log x},\qquad
\liminf_n\frac{b_{n+1}-b_n}{\log b_n}=0,
\]
while its reported dyadic variance obeys, for $h=(\log X)^2$,
\[
J_B^*(X,h)=O(X\log^2X)
=o\left(X\log^2X\log\frac{X}{\log^2X}\right).
\]
The exact definition and weighting of $J_B^*$ and the lacunarity schedule are omitted, so this variance claim is retained in the aid's notation pending verification. The model shows, as reported, that PNT density and the scalar liminf statement alone do not imply a model-independent pair-correlation-scale variance law. It does not refute a converse special to the actual primes or settle Conrey's question.
