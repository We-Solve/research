\section{Adiabatic Expansions for Gapless Quantum Systems}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


For a slowly varied Hamiltonian, an adiabatic expansion approximates the evolved state or observables in powers of the driving rate. A spectral gap separates a ground-state sector from the rest of the spectrum. Gapless systems lack a uniform positive separation. Imaginary-time correlations are correlations under exponential rather than unitary evolution.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Can the adiabatic-expansion framework, whose convergence follows from imaginary-time correlation estimates for gapped systems, be extended to gapless quantum systems even without interactions?

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}An extension must state its observables, time scaling and error estimates; the question does not assume an adiabatic theorem with the same hypotheses as in the gapped case.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine whether the source adiabatic-expansion method extends to gapless quantum systems, including noninteracting ones.
