\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Heegaard splitting of a compact orientable three-manifold is $M=C_1\cup_\Sigma C_2$, where $C_1,C_2$ are compression bodies with common positive boundary $\Sigma$. It is weakly reducible if there are compressing disks $D_i\subset C_i$ whose boundary curves on $\Sigma$ are disjoint. It is stabilized if there are compressing disks on opposite sides whose boundary curves meet transversely in exactly one point. Boundary stabilization is the usual amalgamation with a standard splitting in a collar of a boundary component; it is a distinct operation from ordinary stabilization.
