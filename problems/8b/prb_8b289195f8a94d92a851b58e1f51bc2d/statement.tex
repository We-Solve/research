\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a Riemannian \(n\)-manifold, \(n\geq3\), let
\[
P_{ij}=\frac1{n-2}\left(\operatorname{Ric}_{ij}-\frac R{2(n-1)}g_{ij}\right),\qquad
J=\operatorname{tr}_gP.
\]
For even \(n\geq4\), the source asks whether there is a nonzero natural scalar invariant \(I_g\), a universal finite linear combination of complete metric contractions of tensor products of \(\nabla^\ell P\), \(\ell\geq0\), satisfying
\[
I_{e^{2\omega}g}=e^{-n\omega}I_g\qquad(\omega\in C^\infty(M)).
\]
Nonzero means nonzero for at least one metric; the assertion is pointwise, not merely an integral identity or equality modulo divergences. In the standard metric-contraction class a monomial with \(k\) Schouten factors and derivative orders \(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_k\) must satisfy \(2k+\sum_i\ell_i=n\). Additional orientation-tensor or nonpolynomial conventions are not specified by the source.

The supplied aid reports that the derivative-free sector vanishes in every dimension \(n\geq3\), for any fixed conformal weight and with no constant term. At critical weight, its candidate space has basis
\[
\prod_{j\geq1}\bigl(\operatorname{tr}((P^\sharp)^j)\bigr)^{m_j},
\qquad \sum_{j\geq1}j m_j=n/2,
\]
indexed by the partitions of \(n/2\). On the test metrics \(e^{2u_A}\delta\), \(u_A(x)=-\tfrac12A_{ij}x^ix^j\), the Schouten tensor at the origin is the arbitrary symmetric matrix \(A\); conformal covariance from the flat metric forces every such polynomial coefficient to vanish.

The aid further reports a triangular jet realization: at a point, finitely many completely symmetric tensors
\[
\nabla_{(i_1}\cdots\nabla_{i_k}P_{i_{k+1}i_{k+2})}
\]
can be prescribed using locally conformally flat metrics. This leaves Cotton-type and curvature-commutator compatibility components as the essential possible sector for a higher-derivative counterexample. It does not assert that all unsymmetrized covariant jets are freely prescribable or that the general question is solved.

For \(n=4\), the aid reports that weight counting and identities reduce the standard candidates to \(a\Delta J+bJ^2+c|P|^2\), and independent fourth-jet and Hessian tests force \(a=b=c=0\), recovering the source's stated negative answer in that dimension.

The source explains the intended uniqueness consequence. If two critical \(Q\)-curvatures have the same transformation law
\[
e^{n\omega}Q_{e^{2\omega}g}=Q_g+\mathcal P_{n,g}\omega
\]
for the same GJMS operator \(\mathcal P_n\), and both are pure-Schouten-jet formulas, then their difference is an invariant of the class in question. A negative answer forces equality. The source also emphasizes that more negative weights do admit such expressions, naming squared Bach norm in dimension four and squared ambient-obstruction-tensor norm in higher even dimensions. The critical-weight restriction cannot be dropped.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a pointwise scalar conformal invariant of critical weight be built only from the Schouten tensor and its derivatives? The supplied aid eliminates derivative-free formulas and recovers the known four-dimensional negative answer, while leaving higher-derivative compatibility components unresolved. A negative answer would give the stated restricted \(Q\)-curvature uniqueness.
