\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For every integer \(k\geq3\), let \(C_{2k+1}\) be the undirected simple cycle with vertex set \(\Z/(2k+1)\Z\) and edges between consecutive residues. For a finite graph \(G\), let \(\alpha(G)\) be its maximum independent-set size. In the strong power \(G^{\boxtimes m}\), two distinct words are adjacent exactly when in every coordinate their entries are equal or adjacent in \(G\). Define the nonlogarithmic Shannon capacity by
\[
 \Theta(G)=\sup_{m\geq1}\alpha(G^{\boxtimes m})^{1/m}
 =\lim_{m\to\infty}\alpha(G^{\boxtimes m})^{1/m}.
\]
The limit exists by supermultiplicativity of the independence numbers. A logarithmic information-rate convention would instead report \(\log_2\Theta(G)\).

The stated problem is to determine the exact values
\[
 \Theta(C_{2k+1})\qquad\text{for every }k\geq3.
\]
This asks for all odd lengths at least seven, rather than only a large-length asymptotic or a capacity of directed graphs or permutations.
