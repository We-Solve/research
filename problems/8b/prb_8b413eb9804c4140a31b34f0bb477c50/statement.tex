\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 11000281}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$, let $S_g$ be a closed oriented genus-$g$ surface in the usual convention, and let $\operatorname{Mod}^{\pm}(S_g)$ be its extended mapping class group, allowing orientation-reversing homeomorphisms. Question 3.1 asks whether
\[
 \forall n\geq4\quad\forall g\geq1\quad
 \forall\varphi:\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\to\operatorname{Mod}^{\pm}(S_g),
 \qquad |\varphi(\operatorname{Aut}(F_n))|<\infty.
\]
No injectivity or finite-index-image hypothesis is imposed.

The source reports, citing [21], that infinite image is impossible unless the target contains the symmetric group $\Sigma_{n+1}$. This is only a necessary condition. It says sufficiently large genus realizes every symmetric group and then gives the numerical requirement
\[
 84g-6\geq(n+1)!.
\]
That printed bound is retained as a source assertion requiring review: it is not the usual $84(g-1)$ bound for orientation-preserving automorphisms in genus at least two, and the extended group and genus-one case require different conventions. It is not silently corrected or adopted as a proved obstruction.

The source also asserts that no injective homomorphisms from the free-group automorphism groups under discussion to mapping class groups exist, citing an earlier quoted Brendle--Hamidi-Tehrani result whose rank hypotheses are absent. This is not extrapolated to all ranks. Finally it reports infinite-image homomorphisms from $\operatorname{Aut}(F_3)$ for some genera and asks implicitly for the smallest such genus. That rank-three discussion does not contradict the explicit $n\geq4$ question, and no minimum is supplied.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For rank $n\geq4$, must every homomorphism from $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$ to any extended closed-surface mapping class group have finite image? The source's symmetric-group obstruction and printed numerical bound need precise verification.
