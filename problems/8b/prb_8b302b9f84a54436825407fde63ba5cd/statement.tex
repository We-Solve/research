\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Use the conventional normalized univalent class $S$ of injective holomorphic functions on $\mathbb D$ with $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=1$. For a subset $\mathcal C$ of the real vector space of holomorphic functions, $f\in\mathcal C$ is an extreme point if $f=tg+(1-t)h$, with $g,h\in\mathcal C$ and $0<t<1$, implies $g=h=f$.
