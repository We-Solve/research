\textbf{Attached unreviewed status and algorithm claim.} The aid reports the broad polynomial-time Weaver/KS construction as open, while saying BSS-inspired determinant barriers cover an equal-norm dense regime $m\ge49d^2$. The full hypotheses, output guarantee and reference for that regime are not supplied.

Its separate rounded-state dynamic program has the following specified input: rational real symmetric positive-semidefinite $d$-by-$d$ matrices $A_1,\ldots,A_m$ with $\sum_iA_i=I_d$. Define
\[\operatorname{OPT}=\min_{\sigma\in\{\pm1\}^m}\left\|\sum_i\sigma_iA_i\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}.\]
For a positive rational tolerance, explicitly restricting to $0<\tau\le1$ for the nontrivial approximation range, the aid claims a deterministic output signing with
\[\left\|\sum_i\sigma_iA_i\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\operatorname{OPT}+\tau\]
and at most
\[\left(\frac{6dm}{\tau}\right)^{d(d+1)/2}\]
rounded states per layer. The tolerance restriction avoids reading a state bound below one for arbitrarily large $\tau$; larger tolerances are trivial since every signing has discrepancy at most one. The real-symmetric state dimension is essential and is not silently replaced by complex Hermitian dimension.

The aid says an explicit rational bit-complexity analysis accompanies the algorithm, but that analysis is not in the summary. The stated count is exponential in dimension and depends polynomially on $1/\tau$ only when $d$ is fixed; it is not a dimension-uniform polynomial-time KS solution. An existential bound on $\operatorname{OPT}$, including an applicable MSS bound for rank-one inputs, would translate to that bound plus $\tau$ in fixed dimension, subject to the claimed implementation details.

The aid also reports a one-dimensional counterexample to ``support-only deweighting'' of a BSS certificate: dropping positive sparsification weights and retaining just their support need not yield the desired unweighted partition. The actual counterexample is not supplied. It separately claims that for commuting rank-one summands with $\sum_iA_i=I$ and $\|A_i\|\le\alpha$, greedy signing achieves operator discrepancy at most $\alpha$ in the sign convention above.
