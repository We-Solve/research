\]
where \(W_1(\mu,\nu)=\inf_{\gamma\in\Pi(\mu,\nu)}\int d(u,v)\,\gamma(\dd u,\dd v)\), with finite first moments. For an invariant probability measure \(\pi\), define relative entropy by
