\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
An integral polytope is a nonempty polytope \(P=\operatorname{conv}(v_1,\ldots,v_m)\subseteq\R^n\), where \(v_j\in\Z^n\). Write \(d=\dim P\); no full-dimensionality assumption is imposed. Its Ehrhart polynomial is the polynomial \(L_P(t)\) characterized by
\[
L_P(t)=\#(tP\cap\Z^n)\qquad(t\in\Z_{\geq 0}),
\qquad tP=\{tx:x\in P\}.
\]
Write
\[
L_P(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{d}a_i(P)t^i.
\]
For this draft, interpret \emph{Ehrhart positive} in the standard strict sense
\[
a_i(P)>0\qquad\text{for every }i\in\{0,\ldots,d\}.
\]
Thus the condition concerns the coefficients through the actual degree \(d\), including the constant coefficient; coefficients beyond degree \(d\) are not tested. The supplied record does not explicitly define its positivity convention, so this interpretation requires source confirmation.
