\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For irrational $\alpha\in\R$, $0\le a\le b\le1$, and $N\ge1$, use the source's ordered-pair statistic
\[
 R_2([a,b],N,\alpha)=\frac1N\#\left\{(i,j):1\le i,j\le N,\ i\ne j,
 \ \alpha(i^2-j^2)\in[a/N,b/N]+\Z\right\}.
\]
Conjecture 20, attributed to Z. Rudnick and P. Sarnak, asks whether suitable bad rational approximation of $\alpha$ implies
\[
 \lim_{N\to\infty}R_2([a,b],N,\alpha)=b-a
 \quad\text{for each fixed }[a,b]\subset[0,1].
\]
The usual meaning of badly approximable is the existence of $c>0$ such that $|\alpha-p/q|\ge c/q^2$ for every $p\in\Z$, $q\ge1$. The source instead refers to [165] for ``exact conditions'', which are not included; the intended Diophantine class therefore requires source review rather than silently identifying all possible meanings.

The source reports the limit for a full-measure set of parameters [164] and for a residual set [165], says that no explicit parameter was then known, expects it for algebraic integers, and notes failures for some well-approximable irrationals. These are historical source statements, not a current literature assessment; within the initial irrational setup, the algebraic-integer expectation concerns irrational algebraic integers. The supplied aid reports a parity-sensitive exact reduction. Write $\ell=b-a$, $I_N=[a/N,b/N]\pmod1$, and $-I_N$ for its reflected circle arc. For $1\le h<N$, define
\[
 C_{N,h}(J)=\#\{s\in\Z:h+2\le s\le2N-h,\ s\equiv h\pmod2,
                    \ \alpha h s\pmod1\in J\}.
\]
Then
\[
 R_2=\frac1N\sum_{h=1}^{N-1}\bigl(C_{N,h}(I_N)+C_{N,h}(-I_N)\bigr).
\]
With
\[
 E_{N,h}=C_{N,h}(I_N)+C_{N,h}(-I_N)-\frac{2(N-h)\ell}{N},
\]
the exact discrepancy is
\[
 R_2-\ell=\frac1N\sum_{h=1}^{N-1}E_{N,h}-\frac{\ell}{N}.
\]
Thus the finite-$N$ diagonal correction is $-\ell/N$, and the reflected arc cannot be omitted for ordered pairs. For the nonempty canonical blocks
\[
 B_{N,j}=\{h\in\Z:2^j\le h<2^{j+1},\ h<N\},\qquad j\ge0,
\]
the reported sufficient cancellation target is
\[
 \max_{j:B_{N,j}\ne\varnothing}
 \frac{\left|\sum_{h\in B_{N,j}}E_{N,h}\right|}{|B_{N,j}|}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
This is a sufficient condition, not a proof that the conjectured Diophantine parameters satisfy it.

The aid also reports that an approximation $|\alpha-p/q|\le1/(10q^3)$, $q\ge2$, forces $R_2([1/5,4/5],q,\alpha)=0$. Hence infinitely many such approximations obstruct the asserted limit for that interval, despite ordinary Weyl equidistribution of $\alpha n^2\pmod1$ for irrational $\alpha$. These are unreviewed reported reduction and obstruction results, not a resolution of Conjecture 20.
