\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathcal M\) be a graphical statistical model with a specified collection of observed random variables and a specified collection of hidden random variables. Its observed model \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{obs}}\) consists of the distributions of the observed variables obtained by marginalizing the hidden variables in distributions belonging to \(\mathcal M\). If a chosen algebraic realization places this observed model in an affine space \(k^N\), define its model variety by
\[
V_{\mathcal M}=\overline{\mathcal M_{\mathrm{obs}}}^{\mathrm{Zar}}\subseteq k^N,
\]
and its coordinate ring by \(k[V_{\mathcal M}]=k[x_1,\ldots,x_N]/I(V_{\mathcal M})\). The field, ambient space, and algebraic realization are additional choices; the supplied record does not specify them or guarantee such a realization for every interpretation of a graphical model.

For terminology, if \(W\subseteq\PP^n_k\) is a projective variety over an algebraically closed field and \(r\geq1\) is an integer, its \(r\)-th secant variety is
\[
\sigma_r(W)=\overline{\bigcup_{w_1,\ldots,w_r\in W}\langle w_1,\ldots,w_r\rangle}^{\mathrm{Zar}}.
\]
This definition does not select the secant varieties intended by the source.
