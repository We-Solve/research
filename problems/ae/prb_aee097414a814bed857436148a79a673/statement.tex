Let \(I=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\). A sequence \(a=(a_n)_{n\in I}\) of nonnegative real numbers is log-concave if
\[
 a_n^2\geq a_{n-1}a_{n+1}\qquad(n\geq1).
\]
Some conventions additionally require no internal zeros: whenever \(i<j<k\) and \(a_i a_k>0\), one requires \(a_j>0\). The supplied record does not specify whether this additional condition is intended.

For formal power series
\[
 A(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nt^n,\qquad B(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}b_nt^n,

\[
 (A\odot B)(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nb_nt^n.

No analytic convergence is needed for this definition. Polynomials can be treated as such series by extending their coefficient sequences by zeros.
