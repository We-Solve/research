\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


Embed $\mathbb P^{k-1}\times\mathbb P^{k-1}\times\mathbb P^{k-1}$ by the Segre map, identifying its points with projective rank-one tensors. Its $k$th secant variety is the Zariski closure of projective linear spans of at most $k$ such points. The ideal is the set of homogeneous polynomials vanishing on this variety.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Problem 29. Compute the ideal of the secant varieties Sec k(Pk−1 × Pk−1 × Pk−1) for k ≥ 4. \sref{20001205}{2}
