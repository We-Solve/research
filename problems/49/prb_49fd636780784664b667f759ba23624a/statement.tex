J^2=-I,\qquad J^*=-J,\qquad J^*J=I.
\]
Define the symplectic form \(\omega(u,v)=\langle Ju,v\rangle\). For a unit vector \(e\in H\), let \(P_e\) be the orthogonal projection onto the two-dimensional symplectic plane \(\operatorname{span}_{\R}\{e,Je\}\). For \(r,R>0\), define the open ball and open symplectic cylinder by
\[
B_H(r)=\{x\in H:\|x\|<r\},\qquad
Z_e(R)=\{x\in H:\|P_ex\|<R\}.
\]

\[
\omega\bigl(D\Phi(x)u,D\Phi(x)v\bigr)=\omega(u,v)
\quad\text{for every }x\in U\text{ and }u,v\in H.
