\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(B_3=\langle \sigma_1,\sigma_2\mid \sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_1=\sigma_2\sigma_1\sigma_2\rangle\) be the braid group on three strands, with \(\sigma_i\) the positive Artin crossing of strands \(i\) and \(i+1\). A positive three-braid is a word in these generators using only positive powers. Write \(\widehat{\beta}\subset S^3\) for the standard closure of a braid \(\beta\), equipped with its braid orientation. Define the knots
\[
K=\widehat{\sigma_1^3\sigma_2^3\sigma_1^6\sigma_2^6},
\qquad
J=\widehat{\sigma_1^3\sigma_2^5\sigma_1^3\sigma_2^7}.
\]
Here smooth concordance means that there exists a smooth proper embedding
\[
F:S^1\times[0,1]\longrightarrow S^3\times[0,1]
\]
whose image meets the boundary of \(S^3\times[0,1]\) exactly in \(K\times\{0\}\) and \(J\times\{1\}\), with the restrictions to the two end circles parametrizing the prescribed knot orientations.

\textbf{Question.} Are \(K\) and \(J\) smoothly concordant?
