\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix a Lorentzian conformal manifold \((M^n,[g])\), \(n\geq2\), together with orientation, time orientation and a chosen spin structure. Let \(S_g\) be its complex spinor bundle, \(D_g\) its Dirac operator, and \(P_g\) its twistor operator. For Clifford convention \(X\cdot Y+Y\cdot X=-2g(X,Y)\), write
\[
(P_g\psi)(X)=\nabla_X^S\psi+\tfrac1nX\cdot D_g\psi,
\qquad
h(g)=\dim_\C\ker D_g,\quad t(g)=\dim_\C\ker P_g.
\]
Kernels here consist of unrestricted global smooth spinors and may be infinite dimensional. The definitions and conformal covariance are supplied by companion record 20002032. Baum's Problem 13 asks to classify structures with \(t(g)>0\) or \(h(g)>0\). The original \((M,[g])\) notation omits the necessary spin-structure choice and any analytic support/integrability condition.

The supplied aid reports Cauchy saturation of the harmonic branch. On every nonempty globally hyperbolic Lorentzian spin manifold without boundary, of dimension at least two, and for a smooth spacelike Cauchy hypersurface \(\Sigma\), restriction gives
\[
\{\psi\in\Gamma_{\mathrm{sc}}(S_g):D_g\psi=0\}
\cong\Gamma_c(S_g|_\Sigma).
\]
Here spatially compact solutions have support contained in the causal hull of some compact set; the right side is compactly supported smooth data in the restricted spacetime spinor bundle. Thus this kernel, and hence the unrestricted smooth kernel, is infinite dimensional. This is an initial-value statement, not an elliptic finite-dimensional-kernel theorem or an assertion about an \(L^2\) kernel.

To show spin dependence, the aid gives the compact flat Lorentzian torus
\[
M=\R^n/\Z^n,\qquad g=-\dd t^2+\dd x_1^2+\cdots+\dd x_{n-1}^2,\qquad n\geq3.
\]
For the trivial spin structure it reports \((h,t)=(\infty,\operatorname{rank}_\C S)\), where \(\operatorname{rank}_\C S=2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\). Integer null Fourier frequencies give infinitely many harmonic modes, while periodic twistor spinors are parallel. For the spin structure antiperiodic only in time, temporal frequencies are half-integers and spatial frequencies integers; there is no null frequency, and the reported result is \((h,t)=(0,0)\). This torus is a separate example, not globally hyperbolic.

The same Lorentzian conformal metric therefore has different answers for different spin structures. The aid supplies a broad harmonic-kernel result and an explicit obstruction to the unadorned classification notation, but not a classification of Lorentzian structures admitting twistor spinors or of other support/regularity versions of harmonic spinors.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify Lorentzian conformal spin structures admitting harmonic or twistor spinors. The supplied aid shows that unrestricted harmonic solutions are abundant on globally hyperbolic manifolds and that both kernels can change with spin structure on one flat torus. The spin structure and analytic kernel convention are essential.
