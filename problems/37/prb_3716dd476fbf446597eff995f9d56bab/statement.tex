\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 2307082}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $E\subset\C$ and $\mathbb D=\{\lambda\in\C:|\lambda|<1\}$. The record gives $F:E\times\mathbb D\to\C$ and two unambiguous conditions:
\[
 F(\cdot,\lambda):E\to\C\text{ is injective for every }\lambda\in\mathbb D,
\]
\[
 F(w,\cdot):\mathbb D\to\C\text{ is holomorphic for every }w\in E.
\]
Its third condition is printed as ``$F(z)=z$, when $w=0$,'' which omits an argument and conflicts with the variable roles in the first two conditions. In particular, the record does not assume $0\in E$.

\textbf{Recorded extension question.} Does there exist $G:\C\times\mathbb D\to\C$ satisfying the same three conditions and
\[
 G(w,\lambda)=F(w,\lambda)\quad(w\in E,\ \lambda\in\mathbb D)?
\]
The exact meaning of the third condition must first be recovered.

A standard holomorphic-motion version, explicitly conditional on the intended normalization being
\[
 F(w,0)=w\qquad(w\in E), \tag{1}
\]
is this: does every normalized holomorphic motion of an arbitrary subset $E$ over the whole parameter disc extend to a normalized holomorphic motion of all of $\C$ over the same disc? The full extension would satisfy
\[
 G(\cdot,\lambda)\text{ injective},\qquad
 G(w,\cdot)\text{ holomorphic},\qquad G(w,0)=w,
\]
in addition to agreement on $E\times\mathbb D$. No analyticity in the spatial variable $w$ is required, and no reduction of the parameter disc is requested. This states the familiar type of problem without authenticating (1) as a correction of the supplied third clause.

The problem is attributed to D. Sullivan, W. Thurston, and H. Royden, communicated by D. Hamilton. The supplied summary labels it OPEN-TRIAGE, says it was open in the 2018 edition, and reports no definitive resolution located. That status is recorded as supplied evidence and has not been independently assessed.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Ask whether a family of injections of an arbitrary planar subset, holomorphic in a disc parameter, extends to such a family on the entire plane over the same disc. The printed basepoint condition has swapped or omitted variables; the standard holomorphic-motion normalization $F(w,0)=w$ is presented only conditionally.
