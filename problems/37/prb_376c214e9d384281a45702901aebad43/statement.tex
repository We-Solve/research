\section{Computational Recognition of Polytope Face Vectors and Cones}\label{30004226}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 30004226 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


Let $\mathcal P^d$ denote convex $d$-dimensional polytopes and $f(P)=(f_0(P),\ldots,f_{d-1}(P))$, where $f_j$ counts $j$-dimensional faces. Let $\sigma^d$ be a $d$-simplex and $a=f(\sigma^d)$. The cone with apex $a$ generated by face vectors is $a+\operatorname{cone}\{f(P)-a:P\in\mathcal P^d\}$. A hyperplane through $a$ supports this cone when the cone lies in one of its closed halfspaces; an interior ray is a ray from $a$ lying in its relative interior.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For fixed $d$, can membership in $f(\mathcal P^d)$ be decided in time polynomial in the binary encoding length of the input vector? Is it decidable whether a hyperplane through $f(\sigma^d)$ supports $\operatorname{Cone}_{f(\sigma^d)}(f(\mathcal P^d))$ or contains an interior ray? \sref{30004226}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
The input consists of an integer vector or finitely encoded hyperplane coefficients. The supplied question does not select a computational model for arbitrary real hyperplanes; decidability requires an effective input representation.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Decide which integer vectors are polytope face counts and recognize supporting hyperplanes of the cone they generate.
