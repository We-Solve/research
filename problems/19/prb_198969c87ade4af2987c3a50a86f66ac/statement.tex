Let \(\mathbb K\) denote the scalar field. The supplied source does not specify whether \(\mathbb K=\R\) or \(\mathbb K=\C\); this choice remains unresolved, and all spaces and linear maps below are understood over the same field. For isomorphic Banach spaces \(X\) and \(Y\), not necessarily infinite-dimensional, define their Banach--Mazur distance by
\[
 d_{BM}(X,Y)=\inf\bigl\{\|T\|\,\|T^{-1}\|:T:X\to Y\text{ is a bounded linear bijection with bounded inverse}\bigr\},
\]
where the norms of the maps are operator norms. For each positive integer \(n\), let \(\ell_\infty^n=\mathbb K^n\) with norm
\[
 \|(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\|_\infty=\max_{1\leq j\leq n}|x_j|,
\]
and, retaining the maximum in the supplied formulation, set
\[
 R_\infty^n=\max\bigl\{d_{BM}(X,\ell_\infty^n):X\text{ is a Banach space over }\mathbb K,\ \dim_{\mathbb K}X=n\bigr\}.
\]
\textbf{Problem (Distance from the cube; attributed to A. Pe{\l}czy\'{n}ski, [Pe2]).} Determine the asymptotic behavior of \(R_\infty^n\) as \(n\to\infty\).
