\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30002634}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be a compact smooth manifold of dimension \(n\geq 3\), and let \(g\) be a smooth Riemannian metric on \(M\). Define its conformal class by
\[
[g]=\{e^{2u}g:u\in C^\infty(M,\R)\}.
\]
For a Riemannian metric \(h\) on \(M\), define
\[
\operatorname{Isom}(M,h)=\{\phi\in\operatorname{Diff}(M):\phi^*h=h\},
\]
and define
\[
\operatorname{Conf}(M,g)=\{\phi\in\operatorname{Diff}(M):\phi^*g=e^{2v}g\text{ for some }v\in C^\infty(M,\R)\}.
\]
Let \(g_{\mathrm{rd}}\) denote the standard round metric on \(S^n\). The hypothesis that \((M,g)\) is not conformal to the round sphere means that there are no diffeomorphism \(F:M\to S^n\) and function \(w\in C^\infty(M,\R)\) with \(g=e^{2w}F^*g_{\mathrm{rd}}\).

\textbf{Lichnerowicz conjecture, as supplied.} For every such \((M,g)\) not conformal to the round sphere, do there exist \(u\in C^\infty(M,\R)\) and \(c\in\R\) such that, with \(\widetilde g=e^{2u}g\),
\[
\operatorname{Scal}_{\widetilde g}(x)=c\quad\text{for every }x\in M,
\qquad
\operatorname{Isom}(M,\widetilde g)=\operatorname{Conf}(M,g)
\]
as subgroups of \(\operatorname{Diff}(M)\)?

\subsection{Short English statement}
The question asks whether every compact Riemannian manifold of dimension at least three that is not conformal to the round sphere admits a conformal metric of constant scalar curvature whose full isometry group equals the original conformal transformation group. Connectedness and boundary conventions remain unspecified in the supplied record.
