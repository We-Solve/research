\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\N=\{1,2,\ldots\}\). For a positive integer \(m\), define the von Mangoldt function by
\[
\Lambda(m)=\begin{cases}\log p,&m=p^k\text{ for a prime }p\text{ and }k\geq1,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
\]
The supplied target is incomplete as written. An explicitly qualified candidate formulation is the following: for every fixed polynomial \(f\in\Z[t]\), irreducible over \(\Q\), of degree \(d\geq2\), with positive leading coefficient and with no fixed prime divisor, establish the weighted Bateman--Horn asymptotic below. Here, having no fixed prime divisor means
\[
\text{for every prime }p\text{ there exists }a\in\Z\text{ such that }p\nmid f(a).
\]
For each prime \(p\), put
\[
\rho_f(p)=\#\{a\in\Z/p\Z:f(a)\equiv0\pmod p\},\qquad
\mathfrak S_f=\lim_{y\to\infty}\prod_{p\leq y}\frac{1-\rho_f(p)/p}{1-1/p},
\]
with the product taken over primes in increasing order. Choose \(N_f\in\N\) such that \(f(n)\geq2\) for every integer \(n\geq N_f\). The intended conjectural assertion is
\[
\sum_{\substack{n\in\N\\N_f\leq n\leq x}}\Lambda(f(n))\sim\mathfrak S_f x
\qquad(x\to\infty),\qquad 0<\mathfrak S_f<\infty.
\]
The polynomial \(f(t)=t^2+1\) is an explicitly requested fixed-polynomial instance.
