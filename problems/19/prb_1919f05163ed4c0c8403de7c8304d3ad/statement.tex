\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(E/\Q\) be an elliptic curve satisfying \(\operatorname{End}_{\Q}(E)\cong\Z\), where \(\operatorname{End}_{\Q}(E)\) is the ring of endomorphisms defined over \(\Q\). For a rational prime \(p\) of good reduction, let \(E_p/\F_p\) denote its reduction and put \(N_p=\#E_p(\F_p)\). A positive integer \(n\) is squarefree if \(\ell^2\nmid n\) for every prime \(\ell\). Write \(\pi(x)=\#\{p\le x:p\text{ is prime}\}\) and
\[
S_E(x)=\#\{p\le x:p\text{ is a prime of good reduction for }E,\ N_p\text{ is squarefree}\}.
\]
The supplied question asks whether, for every such \(E\), there exists an explicit constant \(\delta(E)\), depending on \(E\), such that
\[
S_E(x)\sim\delta(E)\pi(x)\qquad(x\to\infty).
\]
Here \(f(x)\sim g(x)\) means that \(f(x)/g(x)\to1\). Restriction to primes of good reduction makes the reduction notation well defined; the source does not explicitly state this restriction.

Two qualifications remain unresolved. First, the supplied literature assessment calls the stated hypothesis the non-CM case, whereas the condition \(\operatorname{End}_{\Q}(E)\cong\Z\) does not itself impose the geometric non-CM condition \(\operatorname{End}_{\overline{\Q}}(E)\cong\Z\). The displayed hypothesis is retained literally. Second, the source gives neither a positivity hypothesis nor a convention for \(\delta(E)=0\). With the usual meaning of \(\sim\), the displayed assertion requires \(\delta(E)>0\); a possibly zero density statement \(S_E(x)=\delta(E)\pi(x)+o(\pi(x))\) would be a different formulation. No choice between these intended meanings, or definition of what counts as an explicit constant, is supplied.
