For $n$ distinct points in the real plane, list in nondecreasing order the numbers of points on all lines containing at least two of them, including repetitions. Let $f(n)$ count the possible lists. We prove
\[
 \exp(c\sqrt n)\leq f(n)\leq\exp(C\sqrt n)
\]
for large $n$. In particular this proves the requested upper bound. The additional question in the historical discussion about a limiting leading constant is not asserted here.
