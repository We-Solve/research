\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#13; partial reconstruction and structural bounds, 2026-09-23.}
Let $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ satisfy property P: there are no $a,b,c\in A$ such that $a<\min\{b,c\}$ and $a\mid b+c$. Must $|A|\le N/3+O(1)$? The elements $b$ and $c$ are permitted to be equal; neither the statement nor the primary source requires them to be distinct.
