\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A rational homology three-sphere is a closed connected three-manifold $M$ with $H_*(M;\mathbb Q)\cong H_*(S^3;\mathbb Q)$. Choose the orientations needed for its quantum and LMO invariants. Let $Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M)$ be the full LMO invariant in its completed diagram target, and let $\tau_r^G(M)$ and $\tau_r^{PG}(M)$ be the source's quantum $G$ and projective $PG$ invariants at an admissible level $r$.

Find nonhomeomorphic rational homology three-spheres $M,M'$ such that
\[
Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M)=Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M')
\]
but, for some simply connected compact simple Lie group $G$ and some admissible level $r$, at least one of
\[
\tau_r^G(M)\ne\tau_r^G(M'),\qquad
\tau_r^{PG}(M)\ne\tau_r^{PG}(M')
\]
holds. The same $G$, level, and normalization must be used in each comparison. The two quantum alternatives are joined by ``or,'' not both required.

LMO equality means equality in every degree, not agreement through a finite truncation. The exact LMO normalization, level/root conventions, projective variant, and orientation category for nonhomeomorphism should be fixed from the source; distinguishing a manifold from its reverse orientation is not silently treated as nonhomeomorphism of the underlying unoriented manifolds.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find two nonhomeomorphic rational homology three-spheres with identical full LMO invariants but different quantum $G$ or projective $PG$ invariants at some admissible level.
