Let $m_1$ be the supremum of the upper planar densities of measurable
sets containing no two points at distance one. Is $m_1\leq1/4$?
The question is known to have an affirmative answer. This attempt
proves the elementary bounds
\[
 \frac{\pi}{8\sqrt3}\leq m_1\leq\frac27.
\]
The sharper step needed to reach $1/4$ is unresolved in this attempt.
