\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite presentation \(P=\langle S\mid R\rangle\), let \(\operatorname{Area}_P(w)\) be the least number of relator \(2\)-cells in a van Kampen diagram for a null word \(w\). Its Dehn function is
\[
 \delta_P(L)=\max\{\operatorname{Area}_P(w):|w|\leq L,\ w=1\text{ in the presented group}\}.
\]
The group invariant is its growth class under the usual coarse comparison: \(f\preceq g\) if some integer \(C\geq1\) gives \(f(L)\leq Cg(CL+C)+CL+C\) for all integer \(L\geq0\), and \(f\simeq g\) if both comparisons hold. Thus the requested group Dehn functions do not depend on a chosen finite presentation up to \(\simeq\).
