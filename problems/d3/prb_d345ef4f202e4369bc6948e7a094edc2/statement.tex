b_k^{(2)}(X)=\dim_{\mathcal N(\pi_1X)}\overline H_k^{(2)}(\widetilde X),
\]

For a finite complex with multiple connected components, the usual componentwise convention sums these numbers over its components. For a finite aspherical complex with nonzero \(k\)th \(L^2\)-Betti number, the question asks whether every manifold thickening has dimension at least \(2k\).
