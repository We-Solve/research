\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 801053}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Equip \(\R^2\) with the standard symplectic form \(\omega=\dd x\wedge\dd y\). An entire symplectomorphism, in the sense explicitly supplied by the record, is a real analytic diffeomorphism \(f:\R^2\to\R^2\) satisfying \(f^*\omega=\omega\) for which there exists a holomorphic map \(F:\C^2\to\C^2\) with \(F|_{\R^2}=f\). The record does not require \(F\) to be a holomorphic automorphism.

The source asks whether such an \(f\) can have a nontrivial annular rotation domain, declaring a domain trivial when the whole dynamics is conjugate to a rotation. It does not specify the definition of an annular rotation domain, the regularity of either conjugacy, or any irrationality requirement.

A conditional formulation makes these missing choices explicit. Put \(\mathbb T=\R/\Z\), and define
\[
T_\theta(t,s)=(t+\theta,s)\quad\text{on }\mathbb T\times(0,1),
\qquad
R_\varphi(x,y)=\bigl(x\cos(2\pi\varphi)-y\sin(2\pi\varphi),\ x\sin(2\pi\varphi)+y\cos(2\pi\varphi)\bigr).
\]
Fix classes \(\mathscr C_{\mathrm{loc}}\) and \(\mathscr C_{\mathrm{glob}}\) of admissible conjugacies, and a set \(\Theta\subseteq\mathbb T\) of admissible local rotation angles. Under the interpretation that an annular rotation domain is an invariant open annulus conjugate to \(T_\theta\), and that whole dynamics means dynamics on \(\R^2\), the question is whether there exist an entire symplectomorphism \(f\), an open set \(U\subseteq\R^2\), an angle \(\theta\in\Theta\), and a conjugacy \(h:U\to\mathbb T\times(0,1)\) such that
\[
f(U)=U,\qquad h\in\mathscr C_{\mathrm{loc}},\qquad h\circ f|_U=T_\theta\circ h,
\]
while
\[
\nexists\,\varphi\in\mathbb T\ \nexists\,H:\R^2\to\R^2\text{ in }\mathscr C_{\mathrm{glob}}
\quad H\circ f=R_\varphi\circ H.
\]
This is a conditional schema, not a uniquely determined formalization: the supplied record does not determine these conjugacy classes, the angle restriction, whether the rotation domain must be maximal, or whether whole dynamics instead refers to the complex extension.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does a real analytic symplectomorphism of \(\R^2\), extending holomorphically to \(\C^2\), possess an annular region with rotation dynamics although its whole dynamics is not conjugate to a rotation? The source leaves the rotation-domain and conjugacy conventions unresolved.
