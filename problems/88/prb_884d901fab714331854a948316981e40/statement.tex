\textbf{Reported space--time flux-register identity.} Let a closed sphere have a nonoverlapping finite-volume partition into cells \(K\) of areas \(|K|\). Let \(h_K^n\) be the cell-average water depth and
\[
 M^n=\sum_K|K|h_K^n.
\]
Assume no water-mass sources, no external boundary, and updates over a common synchronization interval of the form
\[
 |K|(h_K^{n+1}-h_K^n)=-\sum_{f\subseteq\partial K}\mathcal F_{K,f}^n,
\]
where \(\mathcal F_{K,f}^n\) is the outward time-integrated mass-flux register, including face measure, all Runge--Kutta weights and all local substeps. Decompose nonconforming coarse/fine faces into common interface pieces and sum their registers over the same interval. For every paired interface \(f=K|L\), put
\[
 \delta_f^n=\mathcal F_{K,f}^n+\mathcal F_{L,f}^n.
\]
The aid's exact algebraic identity is
\[
 M^{n+1}-M^n=-\sum_f\delta_f^n,
 \qquad
 M^N-M^0=-\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\sum_f\delta_f^n.
\]
Matched interfaces have zero defect; the sum can therefore be restricted to global/local or coarse/fine interfaces at which registers are mismatched. In particular,
\[
 |M^N-M^0|\leq\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\sum_f|\delta_f^n|.
\]
The triangle bound is sharp without cancellation information. Refluxing, meaning replacement/correction of a coarse register by the negative sum of the matching fine-side outward registers, makes each paired defect zero. It therefore gives exact mass conservation in algebraic arithmetic even under subcycling and Runge--Kutta stages, provided those same corrected integrated registers are used in the actual updates. Roundoff or other changes to the update are not silently excluded from a real implementation's measured error.

\textbf{Reported hydrostatic interface calibration.} At a face with bed values \(b_L,b_R\), use the shared value \(b_*=\max\{b_L,b_R\}\). For a possibly dry lake at rest with level \(H\), define
\[
 h_i=(H-b_i)_+,\quad q_i=0,\quad \eta_i=h_i+b_i,\quad
 h_i^*=(\eta_i-b_*)_+\qquad(i=L,R).
\]
Then the two nonnegative reconstructed depths agree:
\[
 h_L^*=h_R^*=(H-b_*)_+.
\]
For a consistent numerical mass flux evaluated on these identical zero-velocity states, the face mass flux is zero, including wet/dry interfaces. Thus a stage at this reconstructed lake-at-rest state contributes zero mass register and zero interface defect. This statement alone is not a full momentum/source well-balancing proof: preservation of the complete lake state through every stage requires the rest of the scheme to preserve it as well.

This is a conservation and drift certificate for a specified class of updates. It does not certify accuracy, positivity of all evolved cell depths, momentum conservation, energy behavior or general long-time stability, and no numerical mesh or simulation is supplied.
