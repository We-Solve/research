\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $R$ be the usual hyperfinite type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with separable predual, let $\mathbb T=\{z\in\C:|z|=1\}$, and let $\operatorname{Out}(R)=\operatorname{Aut}(R)/\operatorname{Inn}(R)$. The source asks whether for every locally compact group $G$ and every class
\[
 [c]\in H^3_{\mathrm{Borel}}(G,\mathbb T)
\]
there exists an automorphic representation, or kernel, $\rho:G\to\operatorname{Out}(R)$ whose Connes--Jones invariant is $[c]$. The coefficient action on scalar $\mathbb T$ is trivial. The required continuity/measurability conventions and any countability assumption on $G$ are not specified.

In a lift convention making the obstruction explicit, choose measurable automorphisms $\alpha_g$ representing $\rho(g)$ and measurable unitaries $u(g,h)\in R$ with
\[
 \alpha_g\alpha_h=\operatorname{Ad}(u(g,h))\alpha_{gh}.
\]
Their associativity defect is the scalar $c(g,h,k)\in\mathbb T$ determined by
\[
 \alpha_g(u(h,k))u(g,hk)
 =c(g,h,k)u(g,h)u(gh,k).
\]
It is a $3$-cocycle; in the normalized convention its value is one if any argument is the identity, and it satisfies
\[
 c(h,k,l)c(g,hk,l)c(g,h,k)
 =c(gh,k,l)c(g,h,kl).
\]
Changing lifts changes the cocycle by a coboundary, leaving the obstruction class. Some pairing/order conventions replace this class by its inverse; the convention must match the cited invariant when comparing an individual class. Realizing a cohomology class does not require equality to a preselected cocycle at every triple.

The target is an outer automorphic representation with this possibly nonzero defect, not a genuine $G$-action lifted homomorphically to $\operatorname{Aut}(R)$, which has zero obstruction. No faithfulness condition is added. For general locally compact groups, pointwise Borel versus almost-everywhere measurable cohomology, measurable lifts and the topology on automorphisms must be fixed before the assertion is fully precise.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every Borel third-cohomology class of a locally compact group occur as the scalar associativity obstruction of an outer representation on the hyperfinite $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor? The supplied assessment reports the countable discrete case, while the general group and measurability conventions remain unresolved here.
