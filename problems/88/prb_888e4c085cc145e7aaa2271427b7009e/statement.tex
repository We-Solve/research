\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathfrak c=|\R|=2^{\aleph_0}\). For a Banach space \(X\), its norm density is
\[
\operatorname{dens}(X)=\min\{\,|D|:D\subseteq X\text{ and }\overline D^{\|\cdot\|}=X\,\}.
\]
Fix a scalar field \(\mathbb K\in\{\R,\C\}\). An infinite basic sequence in a Banach space \(X\) over \(\mathbb K\) is a sequence \((x_n)_{n\geq1}\) of nonzero vectors that is a Schauder basis for its closed linear span: every vector in that span has a unique norm-convergent expansion \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_nx_n\), with \(a_n\in\mathbb K\). Such a sequence is unconditional if there is a constant \(C\geq1\) such that, for every \(m\geq1\), all \(a_1,\ldots,a_m\in\mathbb K\), and all \(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_m\in\mathbb K\) with \(|\theta_n|\leq1\),
\[
\left\|\sum_{n=1}^{m}\theta_na_nx_n\right\|
\leq C\left\|\sum_{n=1}^{m}a_nx_n\right\|.
\]
The constant may depend on the sequence.

\textbf{Problem 19.} Does every Banach space \(X\) satisfying
\[
\operatorname{dens}(X)>\mathfrak c
\]
contain an infinite unconditional basic sequence?

The source also asks whether additional properties, such as reflexivity, affect the answer. In particular, does the same existence assertion hold for every reflexive Banach space \(X\) with \(\operatorname{dens}(X)>\mathfrak c\)? Here reflexivity means that the canonical embedding \(X\to X^{**}\) is surjective.

\textit{Unresolved qualifications.} The source does not specify the scalar field; the formulation above can be read separately over \(\R\) and over \(\C\), without asserting equivalence of those versions. Reflexivity is the only additional property named, so the broader request about additional properties has no further specified hypotheses or precise target.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every Banach space whose norm density is strictly greater than the cardinality of the continuum contain an infinite unconditional basic sequence? The source also asks whether assumptions such as reflexivity change the answer. The scalar field and any further additional properties are unspecified.
