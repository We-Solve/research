\section{10 Lectures and 42 Open Problems — Sharp tightness of the Angular Synchronization SDP}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


Let $z\in\mathbb{C}^n$ satisfy $|z_i|=1$ and let $C=zz^*+\sigma W$, where $\sigma\ge0$, $W=W^*$ has independent centered complex Gaussian entries above the diagonal, and the lower triangle is determined by conjugation. The diagonal convention changes the objective only by a constant. The Gaussian variance convention is held fixed. Angular synchronization estimates $z$ from $C$, up to multiplication by a common complex phase. Its maximum-likelihood optimization and semidefinite relaxation are
\[
 \max_{|x_i|=1} x^*Cx,\qquad
 \max\{\operatorname{Tr}(CX):X=X^*,\ X\succeq0,\ X_{ii}=1\}.
\]
The relaxation is tight when it has a rank-one optimum $X=xx^*$, which yields a global optimum of the first problem. Correlation with ground truth means the normalized overlap $|z^*x|/n$; multiplying $x$ by a common phase does not change it. ``With high probability'' refers to the Gaussian draw and means probability tending to one as $n$ grows along a stated noise scaling.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is the SDP for angular synchronization tight (with high probability) for noise levels $\sigma$ essentially until the solution of angular synchronization no longer correlates with the ground truth? \sref{statement}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
This is the author's historical sharp-threshold question, not a claim that tightness fails in every nonzero-noise regime. Zhong and Boumal prove tightness for $\sigma=O(\sqrt{n/\log n})$; Zhang's later high-noise analysis quantifies a small discrepancy between the relaxation and maximum likelihood rather than establishing exact equality all the way to loss of correlation. The original word ``essentially'' specifies neither threshold constants nor allowed logarithmic losses. These limitations are retained instead of selecting a new sharp conjecture. The primary papers were inspected on 2026-10-11.
