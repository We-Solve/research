\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Sigma_g\) be a closed connected oriented surface of genus \(g\) with an area form \(\omega_g\). Write \(\operatorname{Ham}(\Sigma_g,\omega_g)\) for the group of time-one maps of smooth time-dependent Hamiltonian isotopies; with one standard sign convention, \(\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega_g=-\dd H_t\). The source suppresses the area form, and this closed-surface convention should be matched to the report.

For a pair of genera \(g>h\), the first question asks whether there is a finitely generated abstract group \(\Gamma\) such that
\[
\Gamma\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Ham}(\Sigma_g)
\quad\text{but}\quad
\Gamma\not\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Ham}(\Sigma_h),
\]
where \(\hookrightarrow\) means an injective group homomorphism. The second asks whether
\[
\forall\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Ham}(\Sigma_h)\text{ finitely generated},\qquad
\Gamma\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Ham}(\Sigma_g).
\]
The target embedding need not extend to a homomorphism of the full Hamiltonian groups or arise from an embedding of the underlying surfaces. No metric or topological embedding requirement on these abstract finitely generated groups is added.

Equivalently, let \(\mathscr F_g\) be the isomorphism classes of finitely generated groups admitting an embedding in \(\operatorname{Ham}(\Sigma_g)\). The questions are whether \(\mathscr F_g\setminus\mathscr F_h\ne\varnothing\) and whether \(\mathscr F_h\subseteq\mathscr F_g\). They are separate questions; positive answers to both would give strict inclusion. The word ``conversely'' does not make either assertion a logical consequence of the other.

The source does not specify whether these questions concern every fixed pair \(g>h\), the existence of some such pair, or a restricted genus range. In particular sphere and torus cases are not silently excluded. The supplied literature assessment checked 2026-08-21 reports the genus-sensitive finitely-generated-subgroup problem open in the stated generality, without a resolving theorem or precise partial cases.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Compare the finitely generated groups that embed in Hamiltonian diffeomorphism groups of two genera: can higher genus realize a group unavailable at lower genus, and does it realize every lower-genus example? The intended quantifier over genus pairs is unspecified.
