Let $f(n)$ be the number of sum-free subsets of $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$, where sum-free excludes $x+y=z$ even when $x=y$. We prove
\[
 f(n)=2^{n/2+o(n)}.
\]
The classical problem is resolved, with stronger constant-factor bounds known. Our reconstruction proves exactly the requested exponential asymptotic using the general hypergraph container theorem as an explicit external input, and proves the required arithmetic supersaturation estimate directly.
