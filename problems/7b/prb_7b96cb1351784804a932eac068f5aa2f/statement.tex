\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a compact complex manifold of complex dimension \(n\), equipped with a smooth positive definite real \((1,1)\)-form \(\omega\); thus \((X,\omega)\) is a compact Hermitian manifold. No assumption that \(d\omega=0\) is imposed. Use the convention \(d^c=\frac{i}{2}(\bar\partial-\partial)\), so that \(dd^c=i\partial\bar\partial\). The source does not specify this normalization; any positive constant rescaling gives the same family of forms below after rescaling the potentials.

For smooth real-valued functions, define
\[
C^\infty(X,\R)\cap\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)
=\{\varphi\in C^\infty(X,\R):\omega+dd^c\varphi\geq 0\},
\]
where the inequality means that the real \((1,1)\)-form is positive semidefinite at every point. Define
\[
T(X,\omega)=\inf_{\substack{\varphi\in C^\infty(X,\R)\\\omega+dd^c\varphi\geq0}}
\int_X(\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n.
\]
The admissible set contains \(\varphi=0\), and all these integrals are nonnegative.

\textbf{Question (Tosatti, as attributed in the supplied source record).} Does there exist a compact Hermitian manifold \((X,\omega)\) such that \(T(X,\omega)=0\)? Equivalently, does there exist such a pair for which, for every \(\varepsilon>0\), there is a function \(\varphi\in C^\infty(X,\R)\) satisfying
\[
\omega+dd^c\varphi\geq0,
\qquad
\int_X(\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n<\varepsilon?
\]
The question concerns the infimum and does not require a single smooth potential to attain zero total mass. The dimension \(n=\dim_{\C}X\) is not fixed in the supplied question.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a compact Hermitian manifold admit smooth \(\omega\)-plurisubharmonic potentials whose associated semipositive forms have arbitrarily small total top-degree mass? The supplied registry labels this question open; that label is not an independently verified assessment.
