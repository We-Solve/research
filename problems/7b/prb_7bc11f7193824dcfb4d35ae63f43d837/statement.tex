\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701389}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a finitely generated group and let \(S=S^{-1}\) be a finite generating set. Write \(\lvert g\rvert_S\) for the least number of elements of \(S\) whose product is \(g\), and define the word growth function by
\[
\gamma_{G,S}(n)=\#\{g\in G:\lvert g\rvert_S\le n\},\qquad n\in\Z_{\ge0}.
\]
The group has intermediate growth if its growth is subexponential but is not bounded above by any polynomial. Explicitly, these conditions are
\[
\begin{gathered}
\forall a>1\ \exists C_a>0\ \forall n\in\Z_{\ge0}:\quad \gamma_{G,S}(n)\le C_a a^n,\\
\forall d\in\Z_{\ge0}\ \forall C>0\ \exists n\in\Z_{\ge1}:\quad \gamma_{G,S}(n)>Cn^d.
\end{gathered}
\]
These conditions do not depend on the choice of finite generating set. The Schur multiplier of \(G\) is the abelian group \(M(G)=H_2(G;\Z)\), where the coefficients have trivial \(G\)-action.

\textbf{Problem.} Construct a finitely generated group \(G\) of intermediate growth for which \(M(G)\) is finitely generated as an abelian group; equivalently, require
\[
H_2(G;\Z)\cong\Z^r\oplus A
\]
for some integer \(r\ge0\) and some finite abelian group \(A\). Here intermediate growth is interpreted in its standard sense for finitely generated groups. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that label is not an independently verified assessment.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct a finitely generated group whose word growth is subexponential and exceeds every polynomial upper bound, and whose Schur multiplier \(H_2(G;\Z)\) is finitely generated as an abelian group.
