\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a finite group and \(H\leq G\). For \(g\in G\), put \(H^g=g^{-1}Hg\), and define \(N_G(H)=\{g\in G:H^g=H\}\). Use the conventional intersection condition for a tightly embedded subgroup: \(|H|\) is even and
\[
|H\cap H^g|\text{ is odd for every }g\in G\setminus N_G(H).
\]
Let \(Q_8\) denote the quaternion group of order \(8\), and let \(C_2\) denote the cyclic group of order \(2\). A nontrivial elementary abelian \(2\)-group is a group isomorphic to \((C_2)^r\) for an integer \(r\geq 1\). A Sylow \(2\)-subgroup of a finite group \(H\) is a subgroup whose order is the largest power of \(2\) dividing \(|H|\).

The classification problem is to describe, up to isomorphism, all finite groups \(G\) containing a tightly embedded subgroup \(H\) with a Sylow \(2\)-subgroup \(P\) satisfying
\[
P\cong Q_8\times (C_2)^r
\qquad\text{for some integer }r\geq 1.
