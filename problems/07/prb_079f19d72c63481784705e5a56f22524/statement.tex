\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Identify $\C^3$ with $\R^6$ using coordinates $z_j=x_j+\sqrt{-1}y_j$ and its standard symplectic form
\[
 \omega_0=\sum_{j=1}^3\dd x_j\wedge\dd y_j.
\]
For a closed smooth real three-manifold $M$, a Lagrangian embedding is a smooth embedding $F:M\hookrightarrow\C^3$ such that $F^*\omega_0=0$. Closed means compact without boundary. The literal verified question asks
\[
 \forall\text{ closed }3\text{-manifolds }M\ \exists F:M\hookrightarrow\C^3,
 \qquad F^*\omega_0=0.
\]
No exactness, monotonicity or orientation condition is explicitly imposed. An immersion with self-intersections does not meet the embedding target.

The universal wording needs reconciliation with the exact-Lagrangian obstruction. Let $\lambda_0=\tfrac12\sum_j(x_j\dd y_j-y_j\dd x_j)$, so $\dd\lambda_0=\omega_0$. Any Lagrangian embedding makes $F^*\lambda_0$ closed; if $H^1(M;\R)=0$, this form is exact. The standard nonexistence of closed exact Lagrangians in standard $\C^3$ therefore obstructs the literal universal assertion, for example for $S^3$. No exactness hypothesis has been added to the source question: it follows in these cases from the cohomology. The intended unresolved classification must consequently be distinguished from the unqualified ``every'' formulation.

The literature assessment checked on 21 August 2026 reports embeddings after connected summing with $S^1\times S^2$ and restrictions for prime manifolds, without a general classification. Such connected-sum results change the manifold and do not imply an embedding of the original $M$; immersion constructions likewise do not answer the stated embedding question. The supplied open label and broad classification assessment cannot be treated as a verified claim that the literal universal assertion survives the obstruction above.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The literal question asks for a Lagrangian embedding of every closed three-manifold into standard $\C^3$. Its universal wording needs correction or qualification: manifolds with vanishing real first cohomology would force a forbidden closed exact Lagrangian. Connected-sum and immersion results concern different targets.
