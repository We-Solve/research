\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701299}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an integer \(n\geq 5\), let \(B_n\) be the Artin braid group
\[
B_n=\left\langle \sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\ \middle|\ 
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_i\sigma_j&=\sigma_j\sigma_i &&(1\leq i,j\leq n-1,\ |i-j|\geq 2),\\
\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i&=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1} &&(1\leq i\leq n-2)
\end{aligned}
\right\rangle.
\]
A factor group means a quotient \(B_n/N\), where \(N\trianglelefteq B_n\). A finitely generated group is Gromov hyperbolic if, for some finite generating set, its Cayley graph with the edge-length-one path metric has \(\delta\)-thin geodesic triangles for some \(\delta\geq 0\): each side lies in the closed \(\delta\)-neighborhood of the other two sides. A hyperbolic group is non-elementary if it is not virtually cyclic, where virtually cyclic means having a cyclic subgroup of finite index.

The question is whether
\[
\forall n\in\Z,\quad n\geq 5\ \Longrightarrow\ 
\exists N\trianglelefteq B_n\quad
B_n/N\text{ is non-elementary Gromov hyperbolic}.
\]
The supplied registry labels this problem open; this is a supplied status label, not an independently verified mathematical or literature assessment.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every braid group \(B_n\) with \(n>4\) admit a quotient that is Gromov hyperbolic and not virtually cyclic?
