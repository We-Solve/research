There is a two-colouring of \(\omega_1^3\) with no monochromatic product of three countably infinite sets. Transporting this colouring by bijections gives the negative answer for any three sets of cardinality \(\aleph_1\).
The result is due to Prikry--Mills (1978), as recorded at \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/1128}.
We reconstruct the colouring and its verification from the coherent-rank method in the proof source
\url{https://github.com/plby/lean-proofs/blob/main/src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos1128.lean}.
The rank-existence lemma stated next is an explicit external input. No local Lean compilation is claimed.
