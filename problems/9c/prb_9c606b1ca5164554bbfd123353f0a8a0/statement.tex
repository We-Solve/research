\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A semigroup is a nonempty set \(S\) equipped with an associative binary operation \(S\times S\to S\). A variety of semigroups is a class of semigroups defined by identities in the language of one binary operation. An endomorphism of a semigroup \(S\) is a map \(h:S\to S\) satisfying \(h(xy)=h(x)h(y)\) for all \(x,y\in S\).
