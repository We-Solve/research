\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((K,v)\) be a valued field, with valuation ring \(\mathcal O_v\), maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m_v\), residue field \(k_v=\mathcal O_v/\mathfrak m_v\), and ordered value group \(\Gamma_v=v(K^\times)\). Equicharacteristic zero means
\[
\operatorname{char}(K)=\operatorname{char}(k_v)=0.
\]
The valuation is henselian if every simple root in \(k_v\) of the reduction of a monic polynomial \(f\in\mathcal O_v[X]\) lifts to a root of \(f\) in \(\mathcal O_v\). The supplied record does not require the valuation to be nontrivial.
