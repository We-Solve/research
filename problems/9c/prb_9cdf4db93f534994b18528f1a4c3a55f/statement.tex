Let $G\subset\C$ be a nonempty bounded planar region (in the usual convention, open and connected). Its Bergman space is
\[
 L_a^2(G)=\{f\in\mathcal O(G):\int_G|f(z)|^2\,dA(z)<\infty\},
\]

The source asks whether there exists such a region for which $M_z$ has a \emph{continuous interpolating measure supported on $\partial G$}. Under the usual positive finite-measure convention, continuous means atomless:
\[
 \mu\ne0,\qquad \mu(\{\zeta\})=0\quad\forall\zeta\in\C,
 \qquad \operatorname{supp}\mu\subseteq\partial G.
\]
