\section{Strength of the Krein–Šmulian Theorem}\label{7500205}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 7500205 · Logic}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Work over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, the subsystem of second-order arithmetic with $\Delta^0_1$ comprehension and $\Sigma^0_1$ induction; all structures and orders are countable and coded by sets of integers. $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ adds comprehension for every arithmetical formula (set parameters allowed), and $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ adds arithmetical transfinite recursion along every coded well-order. Reverse-mathematical equivalence means provability of both implications over this base. Let $X$ be a separable real Banach space and $X^*$ its continuous dual. The weak-star topology $\sigma(X^*,X)$ is pointwise convergence on $X$. A set $C\subseteq X^*$ is bounded-weak-star closed if for every $r>0$, $C\cap\{f:\|f\|\le r\}$ is weak-star closed. The Krein--\v Smulian theorem asserts that a convex $C$ is weak-star closed exactly when it is bounded-weak-star closed.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine the exact reverse-mathematical strength of this separable Krein--\v Smulian theorem over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$. \sref{7500205}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which axioms exactly suffice to infer weak-star closedness of a convex dual set from closedness on every norm ball?
