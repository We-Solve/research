\section{Boundary integrability and the explicit confocal-caustic dictionary}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let a smooth closed convex curve $\Gamma\subseteq\mathbb R^2$ bound a billiard table. If a neighborhood of the boundary is foliated by caustics, must $\Gamma$ be an ellipse? A caustic is a curve with the property that a billiard trajectory tangent to it remains tangent after every reflection. The question also asks whether these caustics must be analytic.
