\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a proper CAT(0) geodesic space $X$, its visual boundary $\partial_\infty X$ is the space of asymptotic geodesic rays with the cone topology. If a group acts geometrically on $X$, it acts on this boundary by homeomorphisms.

Construct useful analytic structures on CAT(0) boundaries and determine what geometric or analytic information they carry beyond topology. The source does not prescribe a measure, a compatible metric, a function space, or a differential/energy operator, nor specify what invariance or naturality a construction must satisfy. It is therefore a research program rather than a claim of one named structure with fixed axioms.

The absence of a uniquely preferred metric for the proposed analysis should not be read as absence of every geometric boundary structure. For example, the angular distance is $\angle(\xi,\eta)=\sup_{x\in X}\angle_x(\xi,\eta)$, and its induced path-length metric is the Tits metric, which may take value $+\infty$ and need not induce the visual topology. The supplied summary mentions Tits geometry as existing structure without claiming that it resolves the desired analytic problem.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Develop useful analysis on CAT(0) boundaries and identify structures beyond their topology. Specify the measure, geometry, or operators and the sense in which they are natural; existing Tits geometry alone is not a supplied complete answer.
