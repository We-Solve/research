\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
An affine building of type \(\widetilde A_2\) is a Euclidean building whose apartments have the triangular Coxeter tessellation of type \(\widetilde A_2\). A Bruhat--Tits building means an affine building arising from the Bruhat--Tits construction for an algebraic group over a nonarchimedean valued field; the condition below concerns isomorphism as buildings.

A group \(H\) is simple if \(H\ne\{1\}\) and its only normal subgroups are \(\{1\}\) and \(H\). A group \(\Gamma\) is virtually simple if it contains a simple subgroup of finite index, that is,
\[
\exists H\leq\Gamma\quad [\Gamma:H]<\infty,\qquad H\ne\{1\},\qquad
\forall N\trianglelefteq H\quad (N=\{1\}\ \lor\ N=H).
\]
The subgroup \(H\) is not required to be normal in \(\Gamma\).
