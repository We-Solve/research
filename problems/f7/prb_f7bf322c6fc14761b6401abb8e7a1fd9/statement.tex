\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(s\in\Z\) with \(s\geq3\), and let \(N\geq2\) be an integer. For a set \(P\subset[0,1)^s\) of \(N\) distinct points, define its normalized anchored discrepancy function by
\[
\Delta_P(x)=\frac{1}{N}\#\bigl(P\cap[0,x)\bigr)-\prod_{j=1}^s x_j,
\qquad x\in[0,1]^s,
\]
where \([0,x)=\prod_{j=1}^s[0,x_j)\). Define the star discrepancy and its optimal value by
\[
D_N^*(P)=\sup_{x\in[0,1]^s}|\Delta_P(x)|,
\qquad d_s^*(N)=\inf_{\substack{P\subset[0,1)^s\\ \#P=N}}D_N^*(P).
\]
The infimum expresses the source's optimization over point sets without assuming attainment. Here \(\log\) denotes the natural logarithm.

\textbf{Question under the anchored interpretation.} For every fixed integer \(s\geq3\), do there exist constants \(c_s,C_s>0\) and an integer \(N_s\geq2\) such that, for every integer \(N\geq N_s\),
\[
c_s\frac{(\log N)^{s-1}}{N}
\leq d_s^*(N)
\leq C_s\frac{(\log N)^{s-1}}{N}?
\]
In addition, do the Chen--Skriganov and digital-net constructions identified by the supplied curation attain the corresponding upper order, with constants independent of \(N\) for fixed \(s\)?
