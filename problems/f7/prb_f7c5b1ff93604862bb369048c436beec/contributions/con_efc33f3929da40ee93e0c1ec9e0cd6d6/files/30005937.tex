\subsection{The ideal decoder is a projection, not automatically a network}
The primary source explicitly considers an ideal measurable-function setting before discussing network approximation. Fix a measurable encoder $Z=f_{\rm enc}(X)$ for a random vector $X\in\mathbb R^D$ with $\mathbb E\|X\|^2<\infty$. Allow ambient-valued measurable decoders and let $m(Z)=\mathbb E[X\mid Z]$.
\begin{proposition}
For every decoder $g$ with $\mathbb E\|g(Z)\|^2<\infty$,
\[
\mathbb E\|X-g(Z)\|^2
=\mathbb E\|X-m(Z)\|^2+\mathbb E\|m(Z)-g(Z)\|^2.
\]
Hence the ideal decoder is $m$ up to equality almost surely. A restricted decoder class incurs its best approximation error to $m$ in addition to the irreducible conditional variance.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Conditional Jensen gives square integrability of $m$. Expand $X-g=(X-m)+(m-g)$. The cross term has zero expectation because $m-g$ is measurable with respect to $Z$ and $\mathbb E[X-m\mid Z]=0$. For unbounded $m-g$, first truncate it, use the conditional-expectation identity for bounded functions, then pass to the limit by Cauchy--Schwarz and square integrability. The identity follows. Its last term is nonnegative and vanishes exactly when $g(Z)=m(Z)$ almost surely.
\end{proof}
To test the inference from this identity to an architectural equality, let $U$ be uniform on $[-1,1]$, $X=(U,U^2)$, and encode $Z=U$. The ideal decoder $(z,z^2)$ has zero risk. Among affine decoders, the first coordinate can be reconstructed exactly, while the least-squares approximation to $U^2$ is the constant $1/3$: symmetry gives $\mathbb EU^3=\mathbb EU=0$, and minimizing over the slope and intercept therefore gives slope zero and intercept $\mathbb EU^2=1/3$. Its residual is
\[
\mathbb E(U^2-1/3)^2=1/5-1/9=4/45.
\]
Thus optimizing the ideal expression and declaring a restricted decoder able to attain it loses a strictly positive architecture error.
The codomain also matters. For a constant encoder and $X$ uniform on the unit circle, the ambient conditional mean is zero and has risk one. If a decoder is required to output on the circle, every constant output $u$ has risk $\mathbb E\|X-u\|^2=2$ instead. The measurable projection formula presupposes that the conditional mean is an allowed output.
