\section*{Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups — Problem 36}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $B^n=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:|x|<1\}$, with $n\geq2$, and let $f:B^n\to B^n$ be a quasiconformal homeomorphism. One analytic definition is that $f\in W^{1,n}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ and there is a finite constant $K_f$ such that
\[
 \lVert Df(x)\rVert^n\leq K_f\,|\det Df(x)|
 \quad\text{for almost every }x\in B^n.
\]
Here the norm is the operator norm, and the Sobolev condition means that the coordinate functions and their first weak derivatives are locally $n$-integrable.

Can $f$ be approximated, uniformly on compact subsets of $B^n$, by smooth diffeomorphisms $f_j:B^n\to B^n$ which are globally quasiconformal? Globally means each $f_j$ has one finite distortion bound valid throughout the ball, rather than bounds which may diverge near its boundary. The stronger question asks whether one can choose a single finite $K$, independent of $j$, such that
\[
 \lVert Df_j(x)\rVert^n\leq K\,|\det Df_j(x)|
 \quad\text{for every }j\text{ and almost every }x.
\]
The bound may depend on $f$ and dimension; it is not stipulated to equal the optimal distortion of $f$. No equality of boundary values is imposed. Smoothing that changes the image domain, loses injectivity or yields only local distortion bounds does not satisfy the requested approximation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can quasiconformal maps of a ball be approximated by globally quasiconformal diffeomorphisms, with uniformly bounded distortion?
