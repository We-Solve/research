\section{\texorpdfstring{$\beta$}{beta}-ensembles}\label{8000006}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 8000006 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $B$ be standard Brownian motion on $[0,\infty)$ and $\beta>0$. Define the Dirichlet stochastic Airy operator $A_\beta=-d^2/dx^2+x+(2/\sqrt\beta)B^{\prime}$ through the closure of the quadratic form \[q_\beta[f]=\int_0^\infty(|f^{\prime}|^2+x|f|^2)\,dx+\frac2{\sqrt\beta}\int_0^\infty|f|^2\,dB\] initially on smooth compactly supported $f$ with $f(0)=0$; the last term can equivalently be read by integration by parts against the continuous Brownian path. Use the source's sign convention $H_\beta=-A_\beta$, whose eigenvalues decrease as $\lambda_1>\lambda_2>\cdots$. For $s\in\mathbb R$, the Airy kernel on $L^2(s,\infty)$ is $K_{\rm Ai}(x,y)=[\operatorname{Ai}(x)\operatorname{Ai}^{\prime}(y)-\operatorname{Ai}^{\prime}(x)\operatorname{Ai}(y)]/(x-y)$, continuously extended at $x=y$; $\operatorname{Ai}$ is the standard decaying Airy solution.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Use $H_\beta$ to obtain distributional information on its eigenvalues for general $\beta>0$, and in particular derive directly from $H_2$ the Tracy--Widom identity \[\mathbb P\{\lambda_1(B,2)\le s\}=\det(I-K_{\rm Ai})_{L^2(s,\infty)}.\] Here the determinant is the Fredholm determinant of the Airy integral operator. \sref{8000006}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Develop eigenvalue statistics for general beta through the stochastic Airy operator and obtain the beta-two Tracy--Widom formula directly from that operator.
