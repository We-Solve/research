\section{Long-Time Landau–Pekar Dynamics of Strongly Coupled Polarons}\label{30004240}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30004240 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
A polaron is a quantum particle coupled to a phonon field, with coupling parameter $\alpha$. The Landau--Pekar ansatz approximates the joint state by a particle wave function coupled to a coherent field whose amplitudes evolve by effective equations. Strong coupling means $\alpha\to\infty$. A time window longer than order $\alpha^2$ requires a comparison bound on intervals whose size exceeds that scaling in the source\textquotesingle s time convention, with the error tending to zero in its specified norm.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For a strongly coupled polaron as $\alpha\to\infty$, can approximation of the quantum dynamics by the Landau–Pekar ansatz be proved on time scales longer than $\lvert t\rvert\lesssim\alpha^2$?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The initial states, norm and time normalization must remain those of the report. Extending a formal ansatz or proving a bound on the same order-$\alpha^2$ interval does not satisfy the requested longer-time control.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Extend the strong-coupling Landau--Pekar approximation beyond the currently indicated quadratic time window.
