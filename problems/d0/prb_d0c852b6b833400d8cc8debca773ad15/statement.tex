\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Identify Euclidean space $\mathbb{E}^4$ with $\R^4$ equipped with its standard inner product. Under the usual smooth embedded interpretation of a closed surface, let $M$ be a nonempty compact smooth two-dimensional manifold without boundary and let $f\colon M\hookrightarrow\R^4$ be a smooth embedding. Define the induced Riemannian metric by
\[
 g_p(v,w)=\langle \dd f_p(v),\dd f_p(w)\rangle,
 \qquad p\in M,\quad v,w\in T_pM,
\]
and write $K_g\colon M\to\R$ for its Gaussian curvature.

\textbf{General existence question.} Does there exist such a pair $(M,f)$ satisfying
\[
 \forall p\in M,\qquad K_g(p)<0?
\]
The source does not explicitly impose orientability on $M$ in this general question.

\textbf{Genus-two question.} In particular, does there exist such a pair $(M,f)$ with $M$ connected, orientable, and of genus $2$, satisfying the same strict curvature inequality at every point?
