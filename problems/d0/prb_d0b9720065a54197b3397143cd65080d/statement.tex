\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Write \(\mathbb T^2=(\R/2\pi\Z)^2\). Let \(\mathcal A\) denote the ring of real-valued trigonometric polynomials on \(\mathbb T^2\): finite sums of the form
\[
p(\theta)=\sum_{k\in F}\bigl(a_k\cos(k\cdot\theta)+b_k\sin(k\cdot\theta)\bigr),\qquad F\subset\Z^2\text{ finite},\quad a_k,b_k\in\R.
\]
For \(M\in\mathcal A^{m\times m}\), symmetry means \(M(\theta)^{\mathsf T}=M(\theta)\) for every \(\theta\in\mathbb T^2\), and positive semidefiniteness means
\[
v^{\mathsf T}M(\theta)v\geq0\qquad\text{for every }\theta\in\mathbb T^2\text{ and every }v\in\R^m.
\]
Zeros and singular values of \(M(\theta)\) are permitted; strict positivity is not assumed.

The supplied question asks, for every integer \(m\geq2\), whether every positive-semidefinite symmetric trigonometric matrix polynomial \(M\) on \(\mathbb T^2\) admits an identity
\[
M(\theta)=S(\theta)^{\mathsf T}S(\theta)\qquad\text{for every }\theta\in\mathbb T^2,
\]
with \(S\) a trigonometric matrix polynomial.

The coefficient domain and the permitted dimensions of \(S\) are unresolved in the supplied wording. Under the real-valued interpretation defined above, allowing an arbitrary finite number of rows would make the question precisely
\[
\forall m\in\Z,\ m\geq2,\quad
\forall M\in\mathcal A^{m\times m},\quad
\left[\begin{gathered}M^{\mathsf T}=M,\\
v^{\mathsf T}M(\theta)v\geq0\quad(\theta\in\mathbb T^2,\ v\in\R^m)
\end{gathered}\right]
\Longrightarrow
\left[\exists r\in\Z_{\geq1}\ \exists S\in\mathcal A^{r\times m}:\ M=S^{\mathsf T}S\right].
\]
Requiring a square factor instead replaces the conclusion by \(\exists S\in\mathcal A^{m\times m}:M=S^{\mathsf T}S\). These are distinct formulations, and the record does not select between them. The real-valued interpretation is suggested by symmetry and the ordinary transpose, but is not explicitly specified. A complex-valued formulation would require clarification of the positivity convention and whether the intended operation is transpose or conjugate transpose. No such replacement is made here.

The record labels the case \(d=2\), \(m\geq2\) open. This is a supplied status assessment, not an independently established mathematical or literature conclusion.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The question asks whether every positive-semidefinite symmetric trigonometric matrix polynomial on the two-dimensional torus has a trigonometric factorization \(M=S^{\mathsf T}S\), for every matrix size \(m\geq2\). The coefficient domain and whether \(S\) may be rectangular need clarification. The supplied open status requires independent review.
