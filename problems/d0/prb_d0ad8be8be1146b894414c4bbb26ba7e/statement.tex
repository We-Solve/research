\textbf{Source question.} Under what conditions on $K$ can a set $A$ of density $1/2$ be almost invariant under every map $\phi_{a,b}$ with $|a|,|b|\leq K$? The universal quantifier over the stated affine maps is retained, but ``almost invariant'' and the regime of $K$ are not defined in the supplied statement.

For clarity about the missing definition, the symmetric difference of sets $B,C$ is $B\triangle C=(B\setminus C)\cup(C\setminus B)$. Two possible quantities that one could consider are
\[
 \frac{|A\triangle\phi_{a,b}(A)|}{p}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \frac{|A\triangle\phi_{a,b}^{-1}(A)|}{p}.
\]
Here $\phi^{-1}(A)$ denotes a preimage, not necessarily application of an inverse map. The source selects neither of these errors, nor a normalization by $|A|$, nor an allowable error threshold. More generally, bijectivity requires $a$ to be invertible modulo $p$.
