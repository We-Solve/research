\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a Stein complex analytic space and let \(U\subseteq X\) be open. Here Stein has its usual complex analytic meaning: holomorphic functions separate points, global holomorphic functions induce local holomorphic embeddings, and the space is holomorphically convex. Explicitly, for every compact subset \(K\) of a Stein space \(Y\), its holomorphic hull
\[
\widehat K_Y=\{y\in Y: |f(y)|\leq \sup_{z\in K}|f(z)|\text{ for every }f\in\mathcal O(Y)\}
\]
is compact, where \(\mathcal O(Y)\) denotes the algebra of holomorphic functions on \(Y\).

The supplied source does not define ``locally Stein.'' Under the standard interpretation relative to the ambient space \(X\), this means that for every \(p\in\partial_X U\) there exists an open neighborhood \(V_p\subseteq X\) of \(p\) such that \(U\cap V_p\) is Stein. With this interpretation, the question is whether, for every Stein space \(X\) and every open subset \(U\subseteq X\),
\[
\left[\forall p\in\partial_X U\ \exists V_p\subseteq X\text{ open},\quad p\in V_p\ \text{and}\ U\cap V_p\text{ is Stein}\right]
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad U\text{ is Stein}.
\]
