\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) and let \(S_n\) be the symmetric group on \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\). For \(g\in S_n\), let \(c(g)\) denote the number of cycles in its disjoint cycle decomposition, including fixed points. Define its reflection length by
\[
\ell(g)=\min\{r\geq 0:g=t_1\cdots t_r,\ \text{each }t_i\text{ a transposition}\}.
\]
Then \(\ell(g)=n-c(g)\).

Under the ordinary complex character interpretation, a character is a function \(\chi_V(g)=\operatorname{tr}(\rho_V(g))\) associated with a finite-dimensional complex representation \(\rho_V:S_n\to\operatorname{GL}(V)\). The requested problem is to give, for arbitrary \(n\), a simple description of the collection
\[
\mathcal C_n=\left\{\chi_V:\ \forall g,h\in S_n,\quad c(g)=c(h)\Longrightarrow\chi_V(g)=\chi_V(h)\right\}.
\]
Equivalently, these are the characters for which there exists a function \(F:\{0,\ldots,n-1\}\to\C\) such that
\[
\chi_V(g)=F(\ell(g))\qquad\text{for every }g\in S_n.
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
Describe the characters of \(S_n\) whose value on a permutation depends only on its total number of cycles, counting fixed points, or equivalently on its reflection length. The precise class of characters intended and the meaning of a ``simple description'' require source review.
