\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(F=F(X)\) be the free group on a finite basis \(X\), and let \(S\) be a finite set. Let \(\operatorname{Sym}(S)\) be the group of permutations of \(S\), and define the permutational wreath product by
\[
F\wr_S\operatorname{Sym}(S)=F^S\rtimes\operatorname{Sym}(S),
\qquad (\sigma\cdot h)_t=h_{\sigma^{-1}(t)}.
\]
Interpret the supplied wreath recursion as a group homomorphism
\[
\phi:F\longrightarrow F^S\rtimes\operatorname{Sym}(S),
\qquad \phi(g)=((g_t)_{t\in S},\sigma_g).
\]
Using the displayed coordinate convention, define actions \(\rho_n:F\to\operatorname{Sym}(S^n)\) recursively by
\[
\rho_0(g)(\varepsilon)=\varepsilon,
\qquad
\rho_{n+1}(g)(sw)=\sigma_g(s)\,\rho_n(g_{\sigma_g(s)})(w)
\quad(s\in S,\ w\in S^n).
\]
Here juxtaposition on the right denotes concatenation of a letter and a word. Define
\[
N=\bigcap_{n\geq0}\ker\rho_n
