In the usual locally compact group setting, a crossed-product functor assigns a completion \(A\rtimes_E G\) of the convolution \(*\)-algebra \(C_c(G,A)\), functorially in \(A\). Exactness means that every \(G\)-equivariant short exact sequence of coefficient algebras induces a short exact sequence

0\longrightarrow I\rtimes_E G\longrightarrow A\rtimes_E G\longrightarrow (A/I)\rtimes_E G\longrightarrow 0.
