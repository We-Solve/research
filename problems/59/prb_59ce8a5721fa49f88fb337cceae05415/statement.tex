\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S^3\) be the oriented \(3\)-sphere, and let \(H=\mathrm{Homeo}_0(S^3)\) denote the identity component of its homeomorphism group, with the compact-open topology. For a connected based \(4\)-manifold \(M\) and a representation \(\rho:\pi_1(M)\to H\), the associated flat oriented sphere bundle is
\[
E_\rho=(\widetilde M\times S^3)/\pi_1(M)\longrightarrow M,
\qquad
\gamma\cdot(x,s)=(\gamma x,\rho(\gamma)(s)).
\]
Its Euler class is \(e(E_\rho)\in H^4(M;\Z)\), the primary obstruction to a section. If \(M\) is closed and oriented, its Euler number is \(\langle e(E_\rho),[M]\rangle\); these additional hypotheses are not specified in the supplied question.

The supplied Question 24 asks whether there is a geometric proof of the preceding theorem, specifically through a family of representations
\[
\rho_i:\pi_1(M_i)\longrightarrow \mathrm{Homeo}_0(S^3),\qquad i\in I,
\]
with \(M_i\) \(4\)-manifolds, exhibiting the unboundedness of the Euler class.
