\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\textbf{Conjecture 3 in its supplied Deser--Schwimmer interpretation.} Let $n$ be even and let $p(g)$ be a universal scalar polynomial Riemannian invariant, formed from complete contractions of covariant derivatives of curvature, of weight $-n$: $p(c^2g)=c^{-n}p(g)$. Assume that for every closed $n$-dimensional Riemannian manifold and every smooth $\omega$,
\[
 \int_Mp(e^{2\omega}g)\,\dd V_{e^{2\omega}g}
   =\int_Mp(g)\,\dd V_g.
\]
The conjecture asks for a universal constant $c$, a local conformal invariant $\ell$ of weight $-n$, and a natural vector field $T$ such that
\[
 p(g)\,\dd V_g=c\,\operatorname{Pff}(g)
                    +\ell(g)\,\dd V_g
                    +(\operatorname{div}_gT(g))\,\dd V_g.
\]
Normalize the Euler form by $\int_M\operatorname{Pff}=\chi(M)$ on oriented closed manifolds; other normalizations change $c$. Pointwise conformal invariance means $\ell(e^{2\omega}g)=e^{-n\omega}\ell(g)$. The last term is an exact divergence and integrates to zero. The original phrase ``$S$ as above'' has its antecedent outside this extract; the universal complete-contraction and closed even-dimensional scope comes from the research aid. That aid attributes a proof in this scope to Alexakis. No extension to arbitrary nonpolynomial invariants, boundary terms or other signatures is inferred.

\textbf{Source filtrations and Exercise 6.} For curvature monomials of weight $-n$, the source counts $k_\nabla+2k_R=n$ and filters invariant densities by at most $p$ derivatives, denoted $\mathcal P^p$. Thus $\mathcal P^0\subset\mathcal P^2\subset\cdots\subset\mathcal P^{n-2}$ in the intended class, with the Pfaffian in $\mathcal P^0$. The aid interprets ``$\mathcal P^{\mathrm{odd}}=0$'' as vanishing of odd \emph{homogeneous derivative-degree pieces}; it cannot mean that odd-index filtered spaces are zero. The source also says exact divergences inject into $\mathcal P^2/\mathcal P^0$; the relevant divergence subspace/quotient conventions are not specified enough here to adopt this as a general assertion for all derivative orders.

For the following curvature formulas let $n\ge4$, $J=R/(2(n-1))$ and $P_{ij}=(\operatorname{Ric}_{ij}-Jg_{ij})/(n-2)$. Write $\Delta_0=\operatorname{div}\operatorname{grad}$ and $D=-\Delta_0$, so the sign is explicit. Exercise 6 asks for nontriviality of the top derivative class of $Q_n\,\dd V_g$ in $\mathcal P^{n-2}/\mathcal P^{n-4}$ and agreement with $D^{(n-2)/2}J\,\dd V_g$ in the compatible positive-Laplacian convention. The second filtration, $\mathcal L^p$, counts polynomial degree in the conformal factor derivatives in the transformation of a density: local conformal invariant densities lie in $\mathcal L^0$ and $Q$-curvature density in $\mathcal L^1$. The density formulation is essential to the stated linear law.

\textbf{Supplied four-dimensional normalization dictionary.} Put $E=\operatorname{Ric}-(R/4)g$ and let $W$ be the Weyl tensor, with full tensor contraction norms. The aid gives
\[
 \mathcal E_4=|\operatorname{Riem}|^2-4|\operatorname{Ric}|^2+R^2
             =|W|^2-2|E|^2+\frac{R^2}{6},
\]
\[
 Q_4=-\frac16\Delta_0R-\frac12|E|^2+\frac{R^2}{24},\qquad
 \operatorname{Pff}_4=\frac{\mathcal E_4}{32\pi^2}\,\dd V_g,
 \qquad \mathcal E_{\mathrm{AIM}}=4\mathcal E_4.
\]
In particular
\[
 \mathcal E_4=|W|^2+4Q_4+\frac23\Delta_0R,
\]
\[
 Q_4\,\dd V_g=8\pi^2\operatorname{Pff}_4
       -\frac14|W|^2\,\dd V_g-\frac16\Delta_0R\,\dd V_g,
\]
\[
 \operatorname{Pff}_4=\frac1{8\pi^2}Q_4\,\dd V_g
       +\frac1{32\pi^2}|W|^2\,\dd V_g
       +\frac1{48\pi^2}\Delta_0R\,\dd V_g.
\]
These identities convert in both directions between a $Q_4$ decomposition and a normalized Pfaffian decomposition, with the displayed Weyl density locally conformally invariant and $\Delta_0R$ an exact divergence. They do not remove the universal hypotheses of Conjecture 3. The aid's informal phrase ``positive Laplacian'' is replaced by the explicit $\Delta_0,D$ definitions rather than mixed sign conventions.

\textbf{Tractor construction and additional formula question in the extract.} The standard conformal tractor bundle has a metric-dependent splitting
\[
 \mathcal T\cong\mathcal E[1]\oplus(T^*M\otimes\mathcal E[1])
                         \oplus\mathcal E[-1],
\]
with normal tractor connection, in suppressed density notation,
\[
 \nabla_a^{\mathcal T}\begin{pmatrix}\sigma\\\mu_b\\\tau\end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}
 \nabla_a\sigma-\mu_a\\
 \nabla_a\mu_b+g_{ab}\tau+P_{ab}\sigma\\
 \nabla_a\tau-P_a{}^b\mu_b

\]
The source uses $I_g=(2-n,0,J)$ as a section of $\mathcal T[-1]$, and operators $\mathcal B_{n-2}$, written with a section-sign glyph in the extraction, of leading form $D^{n/2-1}$ plus lower-order terms. Its identities are
\[
 \mathcal B_{n-2}I_g=(0,0,Q_n),\qquad
 I_{e^{2\omega}g}=I_g+\mathcal D\omega,\qquad

\[
