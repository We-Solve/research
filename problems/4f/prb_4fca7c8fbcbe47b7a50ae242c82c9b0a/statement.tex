\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be a smooth real manifold of dimension \(2n\) with an almost-complex structure \(J\), meaning a smooth bundle endomorphism \(J:TM\to TM\) satisfying \(J^2=-\mathrm{id}_{TM}\). Write \(\Omega^{p,q}(M,J)\) for the smooth complex-valued forms of type \((p,q)\), where \(0\leq p,q\leq n\). The exterior derivative decomposes according to type as
\[
d=\mu+\partial+\bar\partial+\bar\mu,
\]
with respective bidegrees \((2,-1)\), \((1,0)\), \((0,1)\), and \((-1,2)\). Without integrability, \(\partial^2\) and \(\bar\partial^2\) need not vanish, so the classical formulas below cannot simply be assumed to define cohomology groups.

For comparison, when \(J\) is integrable, one has \(d=\partial+\bar\partial\), \(\partial^2=\bar\partial^2=0\), and \(\partial\bar\partial+\bar\partial\partial=0\). The classical groups are
\[
H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(M)=\frac{\ker(\bar\partial:\Omega^{p,q}\to\Omega^{p,q+1})}{\bar\partial\Omega^{p,q-1}},
\]
\[
H_{BC}^{p,q}(M)=\frac{\{\alpha\in\Omega^{p,q}:\partial\alpha=0,\ \bar\partial\alpha=0\}}{\partial\bar\partial\Omega^{p-1,q-1}},
\qquad
H_A^{p,q}(M)=\frac{\ker(\partial\bar\partial:\Omega^{p,q}\to\Omega^{p+1,q+1})}{\partial\Omega^{p-1,q}+\bar\partial\Omega^{p,q-1}}.
\]
Here the form spaces in these formulas are on \(M\), and form spaces with indices outside \(0,\ldots,n\) are zero. Complex de Rham cohomology is
\[
H_{\mathrm{dR}}^k(M;\C)=\frac{\ker(d:\Omega^k(M;\C)\to\Omega^{k+1}(M;\C))}{d\Omega^{k-1}(M;\C)}.
\]
In the integrable case, representatives induce natural maps
\[
H_{BC}^{p,q}(M)\longrightarrow H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(M)\longrightarrow H_A^{p,q}(M),
\]
and a \(d\)-closed representative gives a map \(H_{BC}^{p,q}(M)\to H_{\mathrm{dR}}^{p+q}(M;\C)\). Complex conjugation exchanges bidegrees \((p,q)\) and \((q,p)\) on Bott--Chern and Aeppli cohomology and squares to the identity. Wedge product makes Bott--Chern cohomology a bigraded algebra and Aeppli cohomology a bigraded module over it.
