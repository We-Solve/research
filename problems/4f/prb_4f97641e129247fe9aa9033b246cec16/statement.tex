A type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor is an infinite-dimensional finite von Neumann algebra \(M\) with center \(\C 1\), equipped with its faithful normal normalized trace \(\tau\). \[
M^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(\N,M)\big/\{(x_n):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau}=0\},
\qquad
\tau_{\mathcal U}([(x_n)])=\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\tau(x_n).
