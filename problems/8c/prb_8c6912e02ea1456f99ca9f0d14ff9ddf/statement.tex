Let \(M\) be a polyhedral manifold with its polyhedral metric \(g\). Write \(\Sigma_g\) for the metric singular locus, namely the points having no neighborhood isometric to a Euclidean open set, and put \(M^\circ=M\setminus\Sigma_g\). The regular locus carries a flat Riemannian metric and its Levi--Civita holonomy representation. The supplied record does not specify whether irreducibility concerns full or restricted holonomy, or the real or complex representation. These choices are left unresolved here. Write \(b_2(M)\) for the second Betti number; the coefficient convention is unspecified in the record (the usual rational convention is \(\dim_{\Q}H_2(M;\Q)\)).

\textbf{First conjectural assertion.} Suppose that \(M\) is nonnegatively curved and nonflat, that its holonomy is irreducible, and that \(b_2(M)>0\). Does there exist an integer \(n\geq 1\) and a natural complex manifold structure \(J\) on \(M\) such that
\[
\dim_{\R}M=2n,\qquad (M,J)\cong_{\mathrm{bihol}}\mathbb{CP}^{n},
\]
and such that \(g\) is a singular K\"ahler metric for \(J\)? Here the dimension equality records a necessary part of the proposed conclusion; even dimensionality is not an explicit hypothesis in the supplied first assertion. The source does not define either the naturality of \(J\) or the required behavior of a singular K\"ahler metric along \(\Sigma_g\). Compatibility on \(M^\circ\) would require \(g\) to be Hermitian for \(J\) and the associated form \(\omega(u,v)=g(Ju,v)\) to be closed, but these regular-locus conditions alone do not specify the intended extension across \(\Sigma_g\).

\textbf{Further conjectural assertion.} For every polyhedral K\"ahler manifold \(M^{2n}\), does there exist a natural complex structure in the following source-stated sense: a complex analytic space \(X\) of complex dimension \(n\) and a PL-diffeomorphism
\[
f:M\longrightarrow X
\]
such that
\[
\operatorname{codim}_{\C}\operatorname{Sing}(X)\geq 3,
\]
and \(f\) is biholomorphic outside the metric singularities? An empty analytic singular locus is permitted by the codimension condition. To make the last clause precise, one would need a specified complex structure \(J^\circ\) on \(M^\circ\) for which
\[
f(M^\circ)\subseteq X_{\mathrm{reg}},\qquad
f|_{M^\circ}:(M^\circ,J^\circ)\longrightarrow X\setminus f(\Sigma_g)

is a biholomorphism. This displays a natural interpretation of that clause, rather than a source-verified definition. On the regular locus, polyhedral K\"ahler terminology involves a parallel orthogonal complex structure; the record does not specify the additional local conditions at metric singularities, or whether this complex structure is given as part of the data.

\subsection{Short English statement}
The supplied conjecture predicts that a nonflat, nonnegatively curved polyhedral manifold with irreducible holonomy and positive second Betti number naturally becomes complex projective space with a singular K\"ahler metric. A further assertion predicts a compatible complex analytic model for every polyhedral K\"ahler manifold, with analytic singularities only in complex codimension at least \(3\). Several geometric conventions and the meaning of naturality remain unspecified.
