\textbf{Conjecture (B. Sh. in the source).} For every real polynomial $p\in\mathbb R[x]$ of degree $k$ with simple real zeros, is
\[
\#_r\big((k-1)(p')^2-kpp''\big)\leq\#_{nr}(p)?
\]
For a nondegenerate root-counting formulation take $k\geq2$; the source does not state a lower bound, and for $k=1$ the polynomial on the left is identically zero, for which a finite zero count is not defined. The same degeneracy occurs for constant input. Thus these cases require a convention or exclusion.

The source calls this the Hawaiian conjecture for $G_1$; the displayed polynomial, rather than an unspecified definition of $G_1$, is the target here. The supplied research summary reports the conjecture false and attributes counterexamples to Katkova, Tyaglov, and Vishnyakova. It provides neither a counterexample nor an identifiable publication, so the negative resolution is recorded as a literature attribution rather than independently established here.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Compare the number of real roots of $(k-1)(p')^2-kpp''$ with the number of nonreal roots of $p$. The supplied summary reports counterexamples, while the degree and root-counting conventions need clarification.
