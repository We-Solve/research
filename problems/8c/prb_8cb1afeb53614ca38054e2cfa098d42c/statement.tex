\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a closed oriented smooth \(3\)-manifold \(M\), let \(\operatorname{Spin}(M)\) be the set of isomorphism classes of spin structures, namely lifts of its oriented frame bundle through \(\operatorname{Spin}(3)\to\operatorname{SO}(3)\). This set is a torsor for \(H^1(M;\Z/2\Z)\): it has a free transitive action of that group but no specified distinguished element.

M. Polyak asks for an invariant \(\lambda(M,\sigma)\) of a closed \(3\)-manifold together with a spin structure such that, for every manifold in the intended class,
\[
 \lambda_{\mathrm{CWL}}(M)
   =\sum_{\sigma\in\operatorname{Spin}(M)}\lambda(M,\sigma),
\]
where \(\lambda_{\mathrm{CWL}}\) is the Casson--Walker--Lescop invariant in the source's normalization. The desired invariant should be invariant under the appropriate isomorphisms of spin manifolds. The target additive group, orientation conventions and any further requirements distinguishing a substantive spin refinement from an arbitrary decomposition of the sum are not supplied.

The source additionally says that \(\operatorname{Spin}^{c}(M)\) is a torsor over \(H^1(M;\Z)\). This conflicts with the standard torsor group \(H^2(M;\Z)\) for spin-\(c\) structures. The printed assertion is recorded as a source issue, rather than silently used as a definition or substituted into the target equation. Orientation and smoothness above make the standard spin terminology explicit; the original excerpt says only closed \(3\)-manifold and does not spell out those conventions.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct a spin-structure refinement of the Casson--Walker--Lescop invariant whose sum over all spin structures recovers the original invariant for each closed \(3\)-manifold.
