\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 11300002}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(F\to E\to B\) be a fibration of simply connected spaces, interpreted in the usual CW-type rational-homotopy setting. The fiber \(F\) is rationally elliptic if
\[
 \dim_\Q H^*(F;\Q)<\infty,
 \qquad\dim_\Q\bigl(\pi_*(F)\otimes\Q\bigr)<\infty.
\]
Its Euler characteristic is \(\chi(F)=\sum_j(-1)^j\dim_\Q H^j(F;\Q)\).

\emph{Halperin conjecture (Stephen Halperin).} For every such fibration with \(F\) rationally elliptic and \(\chi(F)\neq0\), the rational cohomological Serre spectral sequence collapses at \(E_2\). Since \(B\) is simply connected, its \(E_2\)-page is
\[
 E_2^{p,q}=H^p(B;\Q)\otimes H^q(F;\Q),
\]
and collapse means \(d_r=0\) for every \(r\geq2\). This concerns the spectral sequence; it does not by itself specify a canonical tensor-product ring isomorphism for \(H^*(E;\Q)\) or resolve multiplicative extensions. Finite-type and convergence conventions for the general space class should be checked, rather than adding a finite-dimensional base assumption.

The supplied summary gives an equivalent algebraic formulation for the intended elliptic fibers: the rational cohomology algebra has no nonzero degree-lowering derivations. In explicit sign notation, for each integer \(r>0\), every linear map \(D:H^*(F;\Q)\to H^{*-r}(F;\Q)\) satisfying
\[
 D(ab)=D(a)b+(-1)^{r|a|}aD(b)
\]
is zero. This retains the source's degree-lowering convention despite its notation \(\operatorname{Der}_{>0}\).

The summary reports many confirmed cases: truncated-polynomial cohomology examples such as even spheres and complex projective spaces, flag spaces \(G/T\), equal-rank homogeneous spaces, few-generator cohomology algebras, and closure under suitable products/fibrations. These are supplied progress claims with incomplete hypotheses and references, not an unrestricted claim about all homogeneous spaces or all fibrations.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Every fibration of simply connected spaces with rationally elliptic fiber of nonzero Euler characteristic should have a rational Serre spectral sequence with no differentials after \(E_2\). The claimed equivalent formulation rules out all negative-degree derivations of the fiber's rational cohomology algebra.
