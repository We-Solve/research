Every triangle-free graph on \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\), for \(n\geq18\),
has three distinct independent vertices \(a,b,a+b\).
The threshold 18 is sharp. This is a computer-assisted
reconstruction of the finite result attributed to Ben Barber
at \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/895}, checked
24 September 2026. The encoding and the logical certificate
check are described below. No extension to the open general
Hindman-set question is claimed.
