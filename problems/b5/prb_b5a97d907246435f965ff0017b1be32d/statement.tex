In a common integral convention, a polymatroid on a finite ground set \(E\) has a rank function \(r:2^E\to\Z_{\ge0}\) with \(r(\varnothing)=0\), \(r(A)\le r(B)\) for \(A\subseteq B\), and
\[
r(A)+r(B)\ge r(A\cup B)+r(A\cap B).
\]
For \(e\in E\), the usual rank-function deletion and contraction on \(E\setminus\{e\}\) are
\[
(r\setminus e)(A)=r(A),\qquad
(r/e)(A)=r(A\cup\{e\})-r(\{e\}).
\]
