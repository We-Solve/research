\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X_\Sigma\) be a smooth projective toric variety over the ground field \(\mathbf k\) intended in the cited report, and let \(L\) be an ample line bundle on \(X_\Sigma\). The supplied extraction does not specify the field or whether a split torus is understood. Put \(V=H^0(X_\Sigma,L)\), and consider the embedding defined by the complete linear series,
\[
\varphi_{|L|}:X_\Sigma\hookrightarrow\PP(V^\vee).
\]
The question is whether this embedding is projectively normal for every such \(X_\Sigma\) and \(L\). Projective normality means that the homogeneous coordinate ring of the embedded variety is integrally closed in its fraction field. The equivalent formulation supplied in the source asks whether, for every integer \(m\geq1\), multiplication of global sections gives a surjective map
\[
\mu_m:\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbf k}^{m}H^0(X_\Sigma,L)
\longrightarrow H^0(X_\Sigma,L^{\otimes m}),
\qquad
s_1\cdots s_m\longmapsto s_1\otimes\cdots\otimes s_m.
\]
Equivalently in this normal-variety setting, the section ring \(\bigoplus_{m\geq0}H^0(X_\Sigma,L^{\otimes m})\) should be generated by its degree-one part. The question concerns all positive tensor powers, with no restriction on dimension. The fact that the ample bundle supplies an embedding is used in the smooth projective toric context, as in the source, rather than assumed for ample bundles on arbitrary varieties.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every ample line bundle on a smooth projective toric variety give a projectively normal embedding, so that every section of every positive tensor power is a sum of products of sections of the original bundle?
