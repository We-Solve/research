\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a topological space \(X\), let \(H_3(X;\R)\) denote ordinary singular homology with real coefficients. For a finite singular chain \(c=\sum_i a_i\sigma_i\), written with distinct singular simplices, set \(\lVert c\rVert_1=\sum_i|a_i|\). The \(\ell^1\)-seminorm of \(\alpha\in H_3(X;\R)\) is
\[
\lVert\alpha\rVert_1=\inf\{\lVert c\rVert_1:c\text{ is a singular }3\text{-cycle representing }\alpha\}.
\]
For a closed, connected, oriented \(3\)-manifold \(M\), write \([M]_{\R}\) for its real fundamental class and define its simplicial volume by \(\lVert M\rVert=\lVert[M]_{\R}\rVert_1\). Here closed means compact and without boundary.
