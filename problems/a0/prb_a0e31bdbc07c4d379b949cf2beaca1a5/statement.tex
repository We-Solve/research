\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A \emph{finite-rank Coxeter group} is a group admitting a presentation
\[
W=\left\langle S\ \middle|\ s^2=1\ (s\in S),\ (st)^{m_{st}}=1\ (s\ne t,\ m_{st}<\infty)\right\rangle,
\]
where \(S\) is finite and \(m_{st}=m_{ts}\in\{2,3,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}\). When \(m_{st}=\infty\), no relation on \(st\) is imposed. Finite rank refers to the existence of such a finite generating set; it does not require \(W\) to be finite.

For a group \(G\), its \emph{profinite completion} is the topological group
\[
\widehat G=\varprojlim_{\substack{N\trianglelefteq G\\ [G:N]<\infty}}G/N,
\]
with its inverse-limit topology, each finite quotient being discrete. Isomorphisms of profinite completions below are isomorphisms of topological groups.

Let \(\mathcal C\) denote the class of finite-rank Coxeter groups. For an invariant \(I\) defined on \(\mathcal C\), being \emph{determined by the profinite completion within \(\mathcal C\)} means that
\[
\forall W_1,W_2\in\mathcal C,\qquad
\widehat{W_1}\cong\widehat{W_2}
\ \Longrightarrow\ I(W_1)\simeq I(W_2),
\]
where \(\simeq\) denotes equality or the appropriate isomorphism relation for the values of \(I\). For a property \(P\), the corresponding condition is
\[
\forall W_1,W_2\in\mathcal C,\qquad
\widehat{W_1}\cong\widehat{W_2}
\ \Longrightarrow\bigl(P(W_1)\Longleftrightarrow P(W_2)\bigr).
\]
No equality of the ranks of \(W_1\) and \(W_2\) is assumed here.
