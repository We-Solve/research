\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mu:\N\to\{-1,0,1\}\) be the Möbius function: \(\mu(1)=1\), \(\mu(n)=0\) if a prime square divides \(n\), and \(\mu(n)=(-1)^k\) if \(n\) is a product of \(k\) distinct primes. For an integer modulus \(r\geq2\), let \(\sum_{\chi\bmod r}\) denote the sum over all Dirichlet characters modulo \(r\), including the principal character. All sums over \(n\leq x\) are over positive integers. Write \(n^{it}=\exp(it\log n)\), with natural logarithms.

The supplied conjecture asks that, for every fixed \(A>0\) and every \(q\in[0,1]\),

\frac{1}{r-1}\sum_{\chi\bmod r}
\left|\sum_{n\leq x}\mu(n)\chi(n)\right|^{2q}

\left(\frac{x}{1+(1-q)\sqrt{\log\log x}}\right)^q,
