For a strictly increasing positive integer sequence $A=(n_k)$, let $\phi_A(k)$ count those $1\le m\le n_k$ for which
$n_k/\gcd(m,n_k)$ is unequal to every earlier $n_j$.
We construct $A$ with
\[
 \frac1N\sum_{k\le N}\frac{\phi_A(k)}{n_k}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Haight's thesis is credited with the known resolution. The explicit prime-times-divisors construction below is reconstructed from the public proof linked in the problem discussion, \emph{Erdos1000.lean} in \texttt{plby/lean-proofs}; that source credits Aristotle for formalization. We prove the mathematical construction here and do not claim to have run Lean or to have a new result.
