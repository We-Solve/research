\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a positive integer \(n\), let \(S_n\) be the group of permutations of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\), and let \(F_2=\langle x,y\rangle\) be the free group on two generators. For \(w\in F_2\), let \(|w|\) denote the number of letters in its freely reduced expression over \(\{x,x^{-1},y,y^{-1}\}\). Thus powers are counted by their expanded length. This standard word-length convention makes explicit a convention not specified in the supplied statement.

For \(\sigma,\tau\in S_n\), write \(w(\sigma,\tau)\) for the image of \(w\) under the homomorphism \(F_2\to S_n\) sending \(x\) to \(\sigma\) and \(y\) to \(\tau\). A nontrivial two-variable law for \(S_n\) is a word \(w\ne 1\) in \(F_2\) such that
\[
\forall\sigma\in S_n\;\forall\tau\in S_n,\qquad w(\sigma,\tau)=\operatorname{id}.
\]
Here ``nontrivial'' refers to the element of the free group. Membership in \(F_2\) does not require both generators to occur; the supplied statement imposes no such additional condition.

Define
\[
L(n)=\min\bigl\{|w|:w\in F_2\setminus\{1\},\ \forall\sigma,\tau\in S_n,\ w(\sigma,\tau)=\operatorname{id}\bigr\}.
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
Find the shortest nonidentity reduced word in two free generators that becomes the identity under every substitution of two permutations from \(S_n\). The main target suggested by the supplied background is the growth of this minimum length as \(n\) tends to infinity.
