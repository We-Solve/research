\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Why is semidefinite programming computationally slow? For
$$X(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=\{(x_1,x_2,t_1,t_2,z_1,z_2)\in\mathbb R^4\times\{0,1\}^2:t_i\geq x_i^2,\ 0\leq\alpha x_1+\beta x_2\leq z_1,\ 0\leq\gamma x_2\leq z_2\},$$
find a scalable compact description of $\operatorname{conv}X(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$. What is the minimum number of auxiliary variables needed to represent a nonsemialgebraic convex hull semialgebraically? Develop randomized copositivity and complete-positivity tests, and recognize named structures in flat mathematical programs. Finally, if $m\geq12$ points in the shell between the unit sphere and its circumscribed dodecahedron are mutually at least unit distance apart, find concise proofs for $m>16$ of the conjectured Euclidean shell inequality and formulate its $\ell_1$ analogue. \sref{statement}{2}
