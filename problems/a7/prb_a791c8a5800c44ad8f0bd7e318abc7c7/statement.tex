\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be a fixed integer, and let \(B_n\) denote the braid group on \(n\) strands. The supplied statement leaves the range and quantification of \(n\) implicit; the formulation below uses the usual interpretation that the question is asked separately for each positive integer \(n\). For \(\beta\in B_n\), let \(\widehat\beta\subset S^3\) be its oriented braid closure.

A quasimorphism on a group \(G\) is a function \(f:G\to\R\) with finite defect
\[
D_f:=\sup_{a,b\in G}|f(ab)-f(a)-f(b)|<\infty.
\]
It is homogeneous if \(f(g^k)=k f(g)\) for every \(g\in G\) and every \(k\in\Z\). The supplied transcription truncates the homogeneity definition; this is its standard interpretation.

A link is a nonempty oriented smooth closed one-dimensional submanifold \(L\subset S^3\). Define
\[
\chi_4(L):=\max\bigl\{\chi(F): F\subset B^4\text{ is a compact smooth properly embedded oriented surface,}
\ \partial F=L\subset\partial B^4=S^3,\text{ and }F\text{ has no closed components}\bigr\}.
\]
The surfaces may be disconnected, and their boundary orientations must agree with the orientation of \(L\). For a knot \(K\), its smooth slice genus \(g_4(K)\) is the minimum genus of a compact connected smooth properly embedded oriented surface in \(B^4\) with oriented boundary \(K\); thus \(2g_4(K)=1-\chi_4(K)\).

\textbf{Question.} Is the following assertion true for each fixed positive integer \(n\)?
\[
\forall f:B_n\to\R\text{ homogeneous quasimorphism},\quad
\exists A,C\in\R\quad\forall\beta\in B_n,\qquad
|f(\beta)|\leq A\chi_4(\widehat\beta)+C.
\]
The constants may depend on \(f\) and the fixed \(n\), but not on \(\beta\). No sign restriction on \(A\) or \(C\) is stated in the source.

\subsection{Short English statement}
For each fixed number of braid strands, ask whether every homogeneous quasimorphism is bounded in absolute value by an affine function of the maximal Euler characteristic of a smooth oriented surface in the four-ball bounded by the braid closure. The constants may depend on the quasimorphism and strand number. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved, citing a bound for the fractional Dehn twist coefficient while describing the universal question as unresolved; this assessment has not been independently verified.
