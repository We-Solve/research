\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#1171; independent attempt, 2026-09-23.}
Let $\Omega=\omega_1\cdot\omega_1$ and $\beta=\omega_1\cdot\omega$, where the products are ordinal products. For every finite $k$, must every map
\[
c:[\Omega]^2\longrightarrow\{0,1,\ldots,k\}
\]
have either a colour-$0$ homogeneous subset of order type $\beta$, or three vertices whose three pairs all have one common colour $i\in\{1,\ldots,k\}$? This is
\[
\Omega\longrightarrow(\beta,\underbrace{3,\ldots,3}_{k})^2_{k+1}.
\]
For $k=0$ the assertion is immediate. The issue is an unconditional ZFC statement for every finite $k$, rather than merely its consistency.
