\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\in\Z\) with \(n\geq3\), and let \((S^n,g)\) be the round sphere, with Riemannian volume measure \(\dd v_g\). Write \(\Delta_g=\operatorname{div}_g\nabla_g\). To fix an explicit standard convention for the conformal Laplacian, set
\[
Y=-\Delta_g+\frac{n-2}{4(n-1)}\operatorname{Scal}_g.
\]
The supplied record does not specify the radius of the round sphere or the constant normalization of the conformal Laplacian; these conventions must be used consistently throughout the comparison.

For every \(W\in C^\infty(S^n;\R)\) satisfying
\[
W(x)>0\quad\text{for every }x\in S^n,
\qquad
\int_{S^n}W^{n/2}\,\dd v_g=\operatorname{Vol}_g(S^n),
\]
define
\[
Y_W=W^{-1/2}YW^{-1/2},
\]
where \(W^{-1/2}\) acts by multiplication. Interpret \(Y\) and \(Y_W\) as their positive self-adjoint elliptic realizations on \(L^2(S^n,\dd v_g)\), with domain \(H^2(S^n)\). The sphere is closed, so no boundary condition is imposed. For either operator \(A\), write its eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity, as \(\lambda_j(A)\), and define its heat trace for \(t>0\) by

\[
\max_{t>0}\left[t^{n/2}\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tY_W})\right]
