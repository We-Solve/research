\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(d\geq 2\) and \(n\geq 1\) be integers, and write \([n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}\). Under the standard array convention, a \(d\)-dimensional permutation of order \(n\) is a function
\[
A:[n]^{d+1}\longrightarrow\{0,1\}
\]
such that every coordinate line contains exactly one entry equal to \(1\): for every \(j\in\{1,\ldots,d+1\}\) and every fixed choice of the other coordinates,
\[
\sum_{t=1}^{n}A(x_1,\ldots,x_{j-1},t,x_{j+1},\ldots,x_{d+1})=1.
\]
For \(T\subseteq[n]^{d+1}\), put
\[
A(T)=\sum_{x\in T}A(x),\qquad \operatorname{vol}(T)=|T|.
\]
