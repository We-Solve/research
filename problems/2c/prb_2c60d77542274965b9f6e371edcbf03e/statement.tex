\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(P\) be a finite partially ordered set. An order ideal is a subset \(A\subseteq P\) such that \(x\in A\) and \(y\leq x\) imply \(y\in A\). Define
\[
\max(A)=\{x\in A:\text{there is no }y\in A\text{ with }x<y\}.
\]
Here \(|A|\) denotes cardinality. The expressions below require \(A\neq\varnothing\), since the supplied record gives no convention for a maximum over the empty set.
