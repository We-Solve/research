\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Omega\subseteq\R^d\) be a domain, let \(H_0^1(\Omega)\) be the closure of \(C_c^\infty(\Omega)\) in the Sobolev \(H^1\) norm, and let \(H^{-1}(\Omega)=(H_0^1(\Omega))'\). Given a nonlinear map
\[
F:H_0^1(\Omega)\longrightarrow H^{-1}(\Omega),
\]
the source asks about the homogeneous Dirichlet problem
\[
-\Delta u=F(u)\quad\text{in }\Omega,\qquad u=0\quad\text{on }\partial\Omega.
\]
Its weak formulation is to find \(u\in H_0^1(\Omega)\) such that
\[
\int_{\Omega}\nabla u\cdot\nabla v\,\dd x
=\langle F(u),v\rangle_{H^{-1},H_0^1}
\quad\text{for every }v\in H_0^1(\Omega).
\]
Can semiorthogonal spline wavelets be used to construct an adaptive scheme that both evaluates the nonlinear terms and solves this problem with asymptotically optimal complexity?
