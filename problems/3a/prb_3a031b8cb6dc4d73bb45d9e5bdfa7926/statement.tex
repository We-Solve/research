\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a smooth complex projective variety. Write \(\Omega_X^1\) for its holomorphic cotangent bundle and \(\kappa(X)\) for its Kodaira dimension, defined as the maximum dimension of the images of its pluricanonical rational maps, with value \(-\infty\) if no positive tensor power of the canonical bundle has a nonzero global section. A holomorphic one-form \(\omega\in H^0(X,\Omega_X^1)\) is nowhere vanishing if \(\omega_x\ne 0\) in \(T_x^*X\) for every \(x\in X\). A birational model of \(X\) is a variety birational to \(X\). A complex abelian variety is a connected projective complex algebraic group.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Compact K\"ahler threefold extension question.} A compact K\"ahler threefold is a compact complex manifold of complex dimension three admitting a K\"ahler metric. Its underlying differentiable manifold fibers over the circle if it admits a smooth locally trivial fiber bundle map to \(S^1=\{z\in\C:|z|=1\}\). Does the classification of smooth projective threefolds whose underlying differentiable manifolds fiber over \(S^1\) extend to compact K\"ahler threefolds with this property?

The supplied record does not state that projective classification or the conclusion intended by its extension. Consequently, this part identifies the intended question but does not determine a precise classification conjecture. No equivalence between fibering over the circle and possessing a nowhere-vanishing holomorphic one-form is asserted here.

\item \textbf{Birational splitting question.} For every smooth complex projective variety \(X\) satisfying
\[
\kappa(X)\ge 0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\exists\,\omega\in H^0(X,\Omega_X^1)\ \forall x\in X,\ \omega_x\ne 0,
\]
does there exist a birational model \(Y\) of \(X\) admitting a finite \(\acute{e}\)tale cover with a positive-dimensional abelian variety as a factor?

Interpreting ``as a factor'' as a global product decomposition, the requested data have the form
\[
Y\dashrightarrow X\ \text{birational},\qquad
p:\widetilde Y\longrightarrow Y\ \text{finite, surjective, and \(\acute{e}\)tale},
\qquad
\widetilde Y\cong A\times Z,
\qquad \dim_{\C}A>0,
\]
where \(A\) is a complex abelian variety and \(Z\) is the remaining factor. Here an \(\acute{e}\)tale morphism is understood in the usual algebraic sense; over \(\C\), its analytification is locally a biholomorphism. The supplied record does not specify whether \(Y\) must be smooth or projective, or the required regularity and projectivity of \(Z\). The displayed formulation therefore records the intended product-splitting target while leaving those qualifications unresolved.
\end{enumerate}
\subsection{Short English statement}
The record asks whether a projective threefold classification involving smooth fibrations over the circle extends to compact K\"ahler threefolds. It also asks whether every smooth complex projective variety of nonnegative Kodaira dimension carrying a nowhere-vanishing holomorphic one-form has a birational model with a finite \(\acute{e}\)tale cover splitting off a positive-dimensional abelian variety. The classification to be extended and some conditions on the birational model and product factor are absent from the supplied record.
