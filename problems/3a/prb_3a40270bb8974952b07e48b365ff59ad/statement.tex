\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be an arbitrary compact Lie group and let \(r=\operatorname{rank}(G)\), the dimension of a maximal torus. Use \(\operatorname{Sp}^G_{\Q}\) for a model category of rational genuine \(G\)-equivariant spectra, with rational equivalences defined on equivariant stable homotopy groups. This is not silently replaced by naive spectra carrying a \(G\)-action.

The source's Conjecture 2 asks for an abelian category \(\mathcal A(G)\), with enough injectives and injective dimension exactly \(r\), and an algebraic model with a Quillen equivalence
\[
\operatorname{Sp}^G_{\Q}\simeq_Q\operatorname{dg}\mathcal A(G).
\]
Here \(\operatorname{dg}\mathcal A(G)\) denotes differential graded objects, conventionally chain complexes with a suitable model structure whose weak equivalences are quasi-isomorphisms. Injective dimension \(r\) means that every object admits an injective resolution of length at most \(r\), with \(r\) the optimal bound. A Quillen equivalence is requested, not merely a bare equivalence of triangulated homotopy categories; the chosen models and whether a zigzag of equivalences is allowed require source specification. No symmetric monoidal equivalence is separately imposed.
