\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite simplicial complex $K$ on $n$ vertices, let $f_d(K)$ count faces of cardinality $d+1$ and let $\dim K=\max_{\sigma\in K}(|\sigma|-1)$. Betti numbers are understood relative to one fixed coefficient convention, for example $b_i(K)=\dim_{\mathbb F}H_i(K;\mathbb F)$ over a fixed field $\mathbb F$.

The verified source formulation fixes a strongly hereditary class $\mathcal K$, a constant $A>0$ and a dimension parameter $d$, and assumes that every $n$-vertex $K\in\mathcal K$ satisfies
\[
 \sum_i b_i(K)<An^d.
\]
It asks whether this implies the following fractional Helly assertion, in the source's order-$d$ terminology:
\[
 \forall t>0\ \exists s(t)>0\ \forall K\in\mathcal K,
 \qquad f_d(K)\ge t\binom n{d+1}
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \dim K\ge s(t)n,
 \quad n=|V(K)|.
\]
The constant $s(t)$ may depend on the fixed class, $A$ and $d$, but must be independent of $n$ and the particular $K$. Thus a positive fraction of possible $d$-faces should force a face whose dimension is a positive linear fraction of the vertex count.
