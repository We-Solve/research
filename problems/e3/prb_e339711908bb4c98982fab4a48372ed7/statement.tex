\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A closed smooth manifold is a compact smooth manifold without boundary. An almost-complex structure on such a manifold \(M\) is a smooth real vector-bundle endomorphism \(J:TM\to TM\) satisfying
\[
J\circ J=-\operatorname{id}_{TM}.
\]
Its existence forces every component of \(M\) to have even real dimension. Write
\[
H^*(M;\Q)=\bigoplus_{k\geq 0}H^k(M;\Q)
\]
for singular cohomology with rational coefficients, equipped with its cohomological grading, unit, and cup product.

Characterize the finite-dimensional, nonnegatively graded, graded-commutative unital \(\Q\)-algebras \(A\) for which there exist a closed smooth manifold \(M\), an almost-complex structure \(J\) on \(M\), and an isomorphism
\[
A\cong H^*(M;\Q)
\]
of graded unital \(\Q\)-algebras. Equivalently, give necessary and sufficient conditions for such a realization.
