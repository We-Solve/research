Entries are arbitrary real edge weights; zero entries represent absent edges. Under the convention allowing diagonal target weights and assigning equal weight to each target vertex, define
\[
 t(H,W)=\frac{1}{n^{|V(H)|}}\sum_{\phi:V(H)\to[n]}\prod_{\{u,v\}\in E(H)}w_{\phi(u),\phi(v)}.
