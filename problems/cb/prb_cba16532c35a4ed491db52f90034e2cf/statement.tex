\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be an oriented, closed, connected manifold, and put \(n=\dim M\). Here closed means compact with empty boundary. The manifold is aspherical if its universal covering space is contractible. Its fundamental group \(\Gamma=\pi_1(M)\) is residually finite if, for every \(g\in\Gamma\setminus\{1\}\), there is a homomorphism from \(\Gamma\) to a finite group whose value on \(g\) is nonidentity.

For \(A\in\{\Z,\R\}\), let \([M]_A\in H_n(M;A)\) be the fundamental class determined by the orientation. For a finite singular chain written using distinct singular simplices as \(c=\sum_j a_j\sigma_j\), set \(\lVert c\rVert_1=\sum_j|a_j|\). Define
\[
\lVert M\rVert_A
=\inf\bigl\{\lVert c\rVert_1 : c\in C_n(M;A),\ \partial c=0,\ [c]=[M]_A\bigr\}.
\]
The real simplicial volume is \(\lVert M\rVert=\lVert M\rVert_{\R}\), and the integral simplicial volume is \(\lVert M\rVert_{\Z}\). Define the stable integral simplicial volume by
\[
\lVert M\rVert_{\Z}^{\infty}
=\inf_{p:N\to M}\frac{\lVert N\rVert_{\Z}}{\deg p},
\]
where the infimum ranges over all connected finite-sheeted covering maps, \(N\) carries the orientation induced from \(M\), and \(\deg p\) is the positive number of sheets.

\textbf{Question retained from the source record.} Is it true that every oriented, closed, connected, aspherical manifold \(M\) with residually finite fundamental group and \(\lVert M\rVert=0\) satisfies
\[
\lVert M\rVert_{\Z}^{\infty}=\lVert M\rVert=0?
\]
Equivalently, under these hypotheses, does every \(\varepsilon>0\) admit a connected finite-sheeted covering \(p:N\to M\) such that
\[
\frac{\lVert N\rVert_{\Z}}{\deg p}<\varepsilon?
\]
No dimension restriction is supplied.
