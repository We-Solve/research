\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(H\) be the Hilbert space intended in the report and let \(B(H)\) be its bounded operators. A MASA is a maximal abelian self-adjoint subalgebra. Put
\[
M=B(H)\otimes_{\max}B(H),\qquad
D^c=\{x\in M:xd=dx\text{ for all }d\in D\}
\]
for the relative commutant of \(D\subseteq M\). Tensor subalgebras below are understood as the norm closures of their algebraic tensors in this ambient maximal \(C^*\)-tensor product, not as weak-operator closures.

The source poses these questions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every MASA \(C\subseteq B(H)\), is
\[
(C\otimes1)^c=C\otimes B(H)\quad\text{inside }M?
\]
\item For arbitrary MASAs \(C_1,C_2\subseteq B(H)\), is \(C_1\otimes C_2\) maximal abelian in \(M\)?
\item In the special case \(C\cong\ell^\infty(\N)\), does \(C\) have the extension property, and is \(C\otimes C\) a MASA in \(M\)?
\end{enumerate}
In the usual unital-inclusion convention, the extension property means that every pure state of \(C\) has a unique pure-state extension to \(B(H)\) (equivalently, a unique state extension). The source does not define this property explicitly. It also does not specify separability of \(H\) or the concrete realization of the inclusion \(C\cong\ell^\infty(\N)\); the standard diagonal inclusion in \(B(\ell^2(\N))\) should not be silently identified with every unspecified representation.

The supplied assessment reports the Kadison--Singer extension component solved by Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava, while describing the arbitrary-MASA maximal-tensor commutant and MASA questions as unresolved in its cited literature. The three questions are therefore not assigned a single uniform open status, and no inference from the reported extension result to the remaining tensor questions is added without its hypotheses.
