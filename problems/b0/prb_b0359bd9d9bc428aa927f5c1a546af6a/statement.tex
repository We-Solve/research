\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The title places the question in the setting of type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factors. A type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor is a von Neumann algebra with center \(\C 1\), no nonzero minimal projections, and a faithful normal tracial state \(\tau\) normalized by \(\tau(1)=1\). Here separability is understood as separability of the predual. Write \(\|x\|_{2,\tau}=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2}\).

Under the finite-set convention for Property Gamma, a tracial factor \((M,\tau)\) has Property Gamma if, for every finite subset \(F\subseteq M\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\), there is a unitary \(u\in M\) such that
\[
\tau(u)=0,\qquad \|ux-xu\|_{2,\tau}<\varepsilon\quad\text{for every }x\in F.
\]
