\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 800897}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a compact complex manifold of complex dimension \(n\geq 3\), and let \(\omega\) be a smooth, strictly positive, real \((1,1)\)-form on \(X\), defining a Hermitian metric. No assumption that \(d\omega=0\) is imposed. Fix the convention \(dd^c=i\partial\bar\partial\); the supplied source does not specify a normalization.

A function \(\varphi:X\to\R\) is bounded and \(\omega\)-plurisubharmonic if it is bounded, is locally the sum of a plurisubharmonic function and a smooth real-valued function, and satisfies
\[
\omega+dd^c\varphi\geq 0
\]
as a current. For such functions, interpret \((\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n\) as the complex Monge--Amp\`ere measure defined using the Bedford--Taylor products for locally bounded potentials and the smooth form \(\omega\).

For each compact complex manifold \(X\) of complex dimension \(n\geq 3\), characterize the Hermitian forms \(\omega\) for which
\[
\forall\,\varphi\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)\cap L^\infty(X),\qquad
\int_X(\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n=\int_X\omega^n.
\]
Here \(\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)\cap L^\infty(X)\) denotes the bounded \(\omega\)-plurisubharmonic functions just defined. The requested characterization is a necessary and sufficient condition on \(\omega\). The normalization of \(dd^c\) and the interpretation of the measure are explicit conventions for this draft; they are not specified in the supplied statement.
\subsection{Short English statement}
On a compact Hermitian manifold of complex dimension at least three, characterize the Hermitian forms whose total Monge--Amp\`ere mass is unchanged by every bounded \(\omega\)-plurisubharmonic perturbation. The local registry labels this question open; that label is supplied source data and has not been independently verified.
