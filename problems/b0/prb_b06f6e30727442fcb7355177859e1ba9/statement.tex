\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 700213}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A group \(G\) is finitely generated if \(G=\langle S\rangle\) for some finite subset \(S\subseteq G\). It has finite exponent \(n\) if \(n\) is the least positive integer such that \(g^n=1_G\) for every \(g\in G\).

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find an infinite finitely generated group \(G\) whose exponent \(n\) satisfies \(1\leq n<100\).
\item[(b)] Does there exist an infinite finitely generated group \(G\) of exponent exactly \(5\)?
\end{enumerate}
The supplied registry labels this problem open; this status is source metadata and has not been independently verified.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find an infinite group generated by finitely many elements with finite exponent less than \(100\). In particular, determine whether such a group can have exponent \(5\).
