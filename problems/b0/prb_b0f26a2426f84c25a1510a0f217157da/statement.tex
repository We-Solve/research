\[
\delta^+(S)=\{(u,v)\in A:u\in S,\ v\notin S\}.
\]
The nontrivial directed-cut version of the decision question is whether
\[
\exists S\quad \varnothing\subsetneq S\subsetneq V,\qquad
|\delta^+(S)\cap A_R|\le r,\qquad
|\delta^+(S)\cap A_B|\le b.
