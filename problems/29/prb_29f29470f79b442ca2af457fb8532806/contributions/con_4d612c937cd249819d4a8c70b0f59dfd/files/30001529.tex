\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} Oceanic stratification is the vertical variation of density, often represented by $N^2=-(g/\rho_{\rm ref})\partial_z\rho$ in a stably stratified Boussinesq convention. Overturning circulation is the large-scale transport in vertical and meridional directions, measured by a corresponding streamfunction. The source's mid-depth region lies approximately 1000--3000 m below the surface, below the main thermocline and above the abyss. Its model has a semi-enclosed basin and a southern reentrant channel representing the Antarctic Circumpolar Current.

\paragraph{An exact vertical balance and its ambiguity.} On $0\leq z\leq H$, prescribe constant upward velocity $w$, diffusivity $\kappa>0$, buoyancy endpoints $b(0)=b_0$, $b(H)=b_1$, and steady balance $wb'=\kappa b''$. For $w\ne0$ integration gives
\[
 b(z)=b_0+(b_1-b_0)\frac{e^{wz/\kappa}-1}{e^{wH/\kappa}-1}.
\]
For $w=0$ its continuous limit is the linear profile. Differentiation verifies the equation and endpoints. The stratification $N^2=b'$ is positive when $b_1>b_0$, regardless of the sign of $w$, because numerator and denominator signs cancel.

\paragraph{A concrete identification obstruction.} The profile depends on $w$ and $\kappa$ only through $w/\kappa$. Multiplying both by any $c>0$ leaves $b$ and $N^2$ unchanged while multiplying vertical transport by $c$. Thus stratification data alone cannot identify overturning in this model. Even full knowledge of the profile and boundary values does not resolve the ambiguity without an independent transport or mixing measurement.

\paragraph{Source scope.} The actual source has a basin and a reentrant southern channel, with competing mean and eddy overturning. Its mid-depth stratification cannot be identified with this one-dimensional imposed-upwelling balance without justification. The calculation exposes why an independent closure for eddy compensation, boundary forcing and mixing is needed. Deriving that closure and its basin/channel matching is the first gap; neither a universal ocean formula nor a unique inference of overturning from stratification has been proved.
