Let $D(n)$ count the antichains in $\mathcal P([n])$, including the empty family. An antichain has no two \emph{distinct} comparable members. Put $M=\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$. We prove the known asymptotic
\[D(n)=2^{(1+o(1))M}.\tag{1}\]
This is an asymptotic determination, not an exact formula for every Dedekind number. The lower bound $D(n)\ge2^M$ is immediate: every subfamily of the middle level is an antichain. The work is the matching exponent from above.
