\section{Problem 1.1}\label{8700001}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 8700001 · Graph Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $0\ne f\in\mathbb Z[X,Y]$, let $d=\deg f$, and let $H=\max|c_{ij}|$ be its coefficient height. Assume its zero set in $\mathbb Z^2$ is finite.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find an upper bound $B(d,H)$ for $\max\{|x|,|y|\}$ for every integer solution of $f(x,y)=0$, depending only on $d,H$. The question asks for a determinable bound, not merely the existence of a maximum for each fixed $f$. \sref{8700001}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Give a degree-and-coefficient bound for every integer solution of a plane equation known to have only finitely many integer solutions.
