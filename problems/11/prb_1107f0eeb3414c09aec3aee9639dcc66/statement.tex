\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a Banach space. Its density character is
\[
\operatorname{dens}(X)=\min\{\lvert D\rvert:D\subseteq X\text{ is norm-dense in }X\}.
\]
Write \(\mathfrak c=2^{\aleph_0}\) for the cardinality of the continuum, and let \(\aleph_1\) be the first uncountable cardinal. The space \(X\) is reflexive if its canonical embedding into \(X^{**}\) is surjective.

An infinite basic sequence \((x_n)_{n\geq1}\) in \(X\) is a Schauder basis for its closed linear span. It is unconditional if every convergent series in this basis converges to the same sum after every permutation of its terms. It is subsymmetric if it is unconditional and there is a constant \(C\geq1\) such that, for every strictly increasing sequence \(k_1<k_2<\cdots\) of positive integers, every \(m\geq1\), and every choice of scalars \(a_1,\ldots,a_m\),
\[
 C^{-1}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^m a_i x_i\right\|
 \leq \left\|\sum_{i=1}^m a_i x_{k_i}\right\|
 \leq C\left\|\sum_{i=1}^m a_i x_i\right\|.
\]
The scalars here belong to the scalar field of \(X\).

The two questions in Problem 20 of Dodos and Lopez-Abad are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Does every Banach space \(X\) satisfying \(\operatorname{dens}(X)>\mathfrak c\) contain an infinite subsymmetric basic sequence?
\item Does every reflexive Banach space \(X\) satisfying \(\operatorname{dens}(X)>\aleph_1\) contain an infinite subsymmetric basic sequence?
\end{enumerate}
