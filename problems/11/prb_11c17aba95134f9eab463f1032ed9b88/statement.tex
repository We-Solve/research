\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Question \(1.12\) proposes holomorphic-curve theory suitable for an extended field-theory picture, allowing target boundaries or open targets with asymptotic conditions. It suggests replacing Lagrangian boundary behavior by asymptotics in the unit cotangent bundle, and allowing Lagrangian cylinders, corners and two types of asymptotics. This is a research program: it does not specify the symplectic targets, almost complex structures, energies, asymptotic operators, compactification, transversality or gluing axioms. The aid reports substantial but distinct boundary, cylindrical, switching, sectorial and quilted theories, not a single automatic equivalence between all these settings.

Its first local statement is an exact energy budget. Let \((X,\omega=\dd\lambda)\) be exact symplectic and let cyclically ordered exact Lagrangians \(L_j\) satisfy \(\lambda|_{L_j}=\dd f_j\). Consider a finite-energy compatible-\(J\) holomorphic polygon with corner limits \(p_j\in L_{j-1}\cap L_j\), its boundary side on \(L_j\) oriented from \(p_j\) to \(p_{j+1}\), and no extra interior/asymptotic ends. Under the regularity and corner convergence allowing Stokes' theorem,
\[
 E(u)=\int u^*\omega
     =\sum_j\bigl(f_{j-1}(p_j)-f_j(p_j)\bigr).
\]
The sign convention follows the stated boundary ordering. Extra punctures would require additional action terms; the formula does not invent them for the general program.

For the second statement fix the standard complex local model \(\C^m\) and the ordered transverse planes
\[
 \Lambda_0=\R^m,\qquad
 \Lambda_1=\bigoplus_{j=1}^m e^{i\theta_j}\R,
 \qquad0<\theta_j<\pi.
\]
Let \(u\) be holomorphic on a punctured upper half-disk, with positive-real boundary in \(\Lambda_0\), negative-real boundary in \(\Lambda_1\), finite energy and limit \(0\) at the corner. The reflected expansion in this exact linear model has components
\[
 u_j(z)=\sum_{k\geq0}c_{jk}z^{\theta_j/\pi+k},\qquad c_{jk}\in\R,
\]
locally near the corner. In coordinates \(z=e^{-\pi s+i\pi t}\), \(s\to+\infty\), \(0\leq t\leq1\), this becomes
\[
 u_j(s,t)=\sum_{k\geq0}c_{jk}
       e^{-(\theta_j+k\pi)s}e^{i(\theta_j+k\pi)t}.
\]
The coordinate orientation is fixed by the upper-half-disk convention. The smallest positive exponent \(\min_j\theta_j\) is the spectral gap for this ordered end model; exchanging boundary/end conventions changes the corresponding angle convention. Uniform exponential estimates require a positive uniform gap, as well as the relevant size/regularity control.

The explicit one-dimensional family
\[
 u_\theta(z)=z^{\theta/\pi},\qquad0<\theta<\pi,
 \qquad u_\theta(s,t)=e^{-\theta s}e^{i\theta t}
\]
shows that no positive uniform decay exponent persists as \(\theta\downarrow0\). Exact primitive jumps and an angle gap are independent pieces of control: the former gives an energy budget and the latter an asymptotic rate. These local results do not supply global moduli compactness, transversality, corner gluing or the requested extended field theory.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The source proposes a broad holomorphic-curve theory with boundary, corners and asymptotic ends. The aid treats an exact linear corner: primitive jumps determine energy and Lagrangian angles determine exponential decay. A degenerating angle destroys any positive uniform rate, so the local calculation does not supply the missing global field-theory framework.
