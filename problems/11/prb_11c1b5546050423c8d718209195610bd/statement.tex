Finally, for \(T\in\R^n\otimes\R^n\otimes\R^n\), define its real tensor rank by

 \operatorname{rank}_{\R}(T)=\min\left\{r:T=\sum_{i=1}^r a_i\otimes b_i\otimes c_i,
