\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Borel probability measure \(\mu\) on \(\R\) is symmetric about zero if \(\mu(B)=\mu(-B)\) for every Borel set \(B\subseteq\R\), where \(-B=\{-x:x\in B\}\).

To explain the free-probability terminology, let \((\mathcal A,\varphi)\) be a unital \(*\)-probability space: \(\varphi\) is a positive linear functional with \(\varphi(1)=1\). Unital \(*\)-subalgebras \(\mathcal A_1,\mathcal A_2\) are free if
\[
\varphi(x_1\cdots x_n)=0
\]
for every \(n\geq1\) and every choice \(x_j\in\mathcal A_{\epsilon_j}\) with \(\varphi(x_j)=0\), \(\epsilon_j\in\{1,2\}\), and \(\epsilon_j\neq\epsilon_{j+1}\).
