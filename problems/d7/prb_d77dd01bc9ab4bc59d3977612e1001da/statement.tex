\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(N\geq3\), put \(2^*=2N/(N-2)\), and define \(D^{1,2}(\R^N)\) as the completion of \(C_c^\infty(\R^N)\) in the norm \(\|\nabla u\|_{L^2}\), identified with its Sobolev realization in \(L^{2^*}(\R^N)\). Consider the critical equation
\[
 -\Delta u=|u|^{2^*-2}u\quad\text{on }\R^N.
\]
For finite-energy solutions, a standard weak interpretation is
\(\int\nabla u\cdot\nabla\psi=\int |u|^{2^*-2}u\psi\) for all \(\psi\in C_c^\infty(\R^N)\). Say \(u\) is sign-changing when both its positive and negative parts are nonzero. Let \(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{sc}}\) denote the sign-changing solutions in \(D^{1,2}(\R^N)\).

In the conventional variational normalization, set
\[
 J(u)=\frac12\int_{\R^N}|\nabla u|^2-
 \frac1{2^*}\int_{\R^N}|u|^{2^*},\qquad
 S=\inf_{0\ne v\in D^{1,2}}\frac{\int|\nabla v|^2}{(\int|v|^{2^*})^{2/2^*}},
 \qquad e_*=S^{N/2}/N.
\]
These definitions provide a standard energy scale; the source does not print its inequality defining the symbol \(\varepsilon_0\). Its four questions are retained separately.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Determine the optimal energy-gap constant \(\varepsilon_0\). If the intended gap is additive above twice the positive ground-state scale, the corresponding quantity in the normalization above is \(\inf_{u\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{sc}}}J(u)-2e_*\). A relative gap, or a gap in \(\int|\nabla u|^2\), has different numerical units. The missing source inequality is needed to identify its \(\varepsilon_0\); positivity or a value is not asserted here.
\item With \(m_{\mathrm{sc}}=\inf_{u\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{sc}}}J(u)\), does some \(u_*\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{sc}}\) satisfy \(J(u_*)=m_{\mathrm{sc}}\)? The competitors are solutions, not all sign-changing test functions or all elements of a nodal constraint set.
\item Do there exist sign-changing finite-energy solutions which have no \(O(2)\) symmetry modulo conformal transformations? Equivalently, is it false that every such solution can be made \(O(2)\)-invariant by an allowed conformal transformation? For the usual conformal action the transformed function is \(|\det DT|^{(N-2)/(2N)}u\circ T\), where defined. The group action of \(O(2)\), its embedding into the spatial or conformal symmetry group, and the admissible conformal transformations are not specified; they cannot be replaced by the trivial action.
\item Is every bounded sign-changing entire solution of the equation in \(D^{1,2}(\R^N)\)? This question begins with bounded solutions without assuming finite energy, unlike the preceding questions. No decay condition is inserted. The source should be checked for any intended additional restrictions, which would materially change this universal assertion.
\end{enumerate}

The four requests concern the optimal nodal energy gap, attainment of the least nodal solution energy, existence of solutions lacking \(O(2)\) symmetry after conformal changes, and finite-energy membership of bounded entire nodal solutions.
