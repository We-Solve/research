\paragraph{Exact consequence of a defect intertwiner.} Suppose \(H_L,H_R\) are finite-dimensional self-adjoint Hamiltonians and \(D\) is a map with \(DH_L=H_RD\). Induction gives \(DH_L^n=H_R^nD\); the norm-convergent exponential series then gives
\[
 De^{-itH_L}=e^{-itH_R}D.
\]
If \(D^*D=P\) is a projection and \(P\psi=\psi\), then \(\|D\psi\|=\|\psi\|\). Taking the adjoint intertwining relation shows \(P\) commutes with \(H_L\), so this norm-preserving subspace is dynamically invariant. Neither statement requires \(D\) to be an invertible symmetry on the full Hilbert space.
\paragraph{Composition and the first gap.} Two compatible intertwiners compose to an intertwiner by associativity of matrix multiplication. This gives an ordinary category of Hamiltonians and defect maps, but does not automatically identify the physical defect junctions as two-morphisms, define their horizontal composition, or verify interchange and associator coherence. Moreover global intertwining alone is insufficient for local perfect transmission without spatial locality information. The first missing work is extracting those junction/locality data from the exact source two-dimensional model and proving their two-category axioms. The report's categorical question therefore remains unresolved.
