\section{Pre-Asymptotic Sampling Numbers of Barron Spaces}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A sampling number is the smallest worst-case reconstruction error achievable from $m$ point evaluations, with the admissible linear or nonlinear reconstruction convention fixed. A Barron space controls a neural-integral representation norm; Fourier-analytic spaces instead constrain weighted Fourier content.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine the asymptotic nonlinear sampling numbers for variational Barron spaces. For the Fourier-analytic spaces $U_d^\alpha$, determine the pre-asymptotic dependence of $\sigma_m(U_d^\alpha)_{L^p}$ on $d$ and $m$, and determine the optimal reconstruction error when the sample points are uniform random points. \sref{statement}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
The norms defining the variational Barron space and $U_d^\alpha$, the sampling domain, and the error/probability convention are source-specific; pre-asymptotic dimension dependence is part of the target.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine optimal errors from finitely many samples in the stated Barron and Fourier-analytic function spaces, including random samples.
