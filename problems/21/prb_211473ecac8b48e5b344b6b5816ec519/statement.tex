Suppose an element $\alpha(v_0)$ has finite positive topological entropy $H$. For rational $c=p/q>0$, $c\ne1$, define $\alpha_c(v)=\alpha(cv)$. Then
\[
h_{\mathrm{top}}(\alpha_c(v_0))=cH\ne H,
\]
since $\alpha(cv_0)^q=\alpha(v_0)^p$ and entropy scales under positive integral iterates. Therefore no homeomorphism can conjugate the two actions preserving every parameter $v$.
