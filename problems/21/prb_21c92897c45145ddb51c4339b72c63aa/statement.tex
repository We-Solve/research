For an infinite set $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ with $A(N)=o(N)$,
must
\[
 \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac{(A+A)(N)}{A(N)}\geq3?
\]
Here $S(N)=|S\cap[1,N]|$. Freiman proved the affirmative answer.
This attempt establishes the sharpness of the constant by a
fully specified example with limsup equal to three. The universal
lower bound is unresolved in this attempt.
