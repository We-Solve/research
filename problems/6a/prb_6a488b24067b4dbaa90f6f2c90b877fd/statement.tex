\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The leading conjecture concerns the round sphere with a small-dimensional closed set removed: for every closed $\Sigma_0\subset S^n$ with topological dimension at most one, the complement $S^n\setminus\Sigma_0$ is area extremal in the source's ``subcomplete'' sense. The record does not define area extremality, subcompleteness, the comparison metrics/maps, or the dimension range. Thus these terms cannot be replaced by ordinary complete-manifold area rigidity.

The appended programme studies geometric sizes of incomplete $n$-manifolds with $\operatorname{Sc}\geq\sigma$, including compact manifolds with boundary, without assuming they embed in complete manifolds having that scalar lower bound. Its example, for $n\geq2$, is
\[
X_n=\widetilde{S^2\setminus\{N,S\}}\times\R^{n-2},
\]
with the lifted unit-round product metric and antipodal punctures $N,S$. The source denotes this by $\widetilde\Sigma_\pi^n$ and reports
\[
\operatorname{Sc}(X_n)=2,\qquad
\operatorname{waist}_1[X_n]=\pi,\qquad \operatorname{width}_1(X_n)\leq\pi,
\]
\[
\operatorname{waist}_m[X_n]=\operatorname{width}_{n-m}(X_n)=\infty
\quad(m=2,\ldots,n),\qquad K\text{-area}[X_n]=\infty.
\]
The definitions and normalizations of these waist, width and $K$-area quantities are not supplied, so the displayed equalities are source claims rather than verified computations with a substituted standard width.

For $r>0$, the source's $r$-interior is
\[
X^{-r}=\{x\in X:\ \overline B_X(x,r')\text{ is compact in }X
\text{ for every }0<r'<r\}.
\]
It reports that all the relevant waists and $K$-area of $X_n^{-r}$ are small, in fact zero in this example. It also describes completeness as nonemptiness of the $r=\infty$ interior. In the connected Riemannian setting, the latter means that some point has compact closed balls of every finite radius, with the usual completeness/properness relationship; it is not asserted for arbitrary metric spaces without hypotheses. The metric and invariant conventions on the interior subsets, the range of $r$, and any empty-interior conventions need checking.

The leading sphere-complement conjecture and this incomplete-manifold discussion are distinct parts of the retained extract. No unprovided general waist/$K$-area estimate for all $r$-interiors is inferred from the example.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is a round sphere minus any closed set of topological dimension at most one area extremal in the source's subcomplete sense? The extract also develops incomplete-manifold and $r$-interior examples, whose invariant conventions are missing.
