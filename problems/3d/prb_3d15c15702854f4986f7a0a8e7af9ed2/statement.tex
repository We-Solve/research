\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S^3\) denote the smooth three-sphere. Under the usual contact-geometric interpretation of the supplied question, let \(\alpha\) be a smooth contact form on \(S^3\), so that \(\alpha\wedge\dd\alpha\) is nowhere zero, and suppose that its contact structure \(\xi=\ker\alpha\) is tight, meaning that it contains no overtwisted disk. Its Reeb vector field \(R_\alpha\) and flow \(\phi_t\) are defined by
\[
\alpha(R_\alpha)=1,\qquad \dd\alpha(R_\alpha,v)=0\quad\text{for every tangent vector }v,\qquad
\frac{\dd}{\dd t}\phi_t(x)=R_\alpha(\phi_t(x)).
\]
The flow is defined for all real times because \(S^3\) is compact.

A closed Reeb orbit is the image \(\gamma\) of a periodic trajectory: there are \(p\in S^3\) and \(\tau>0\) such that \(\phi_\tau(p)=p\) and \(\gamma=\{\phi_t(p):t\in\R\}\). Here \(\gamma\) is treated as a geometric orbit; the supplied record does not specify how multiple covers are to be treated.

For this draft, a disk-like global surface of section bounded by \(\gamma\) means the image \(D=u(\overline{\mathbb D})\) of a smooth embedding of the closed disk
\[
\overline{\mathbb D}=\{(a,b)\in\R^2:a^2+b^2\leq 1\}
\]
into \(S^3\), with \(u(\partial\overline{\mathbb D})=\gamma\). Write \(D^\circ=u(\mathbb D)\). Require
\[
R_\alpha(q)\notin T_qD^\circ\qquad(q\in D^\circ)
\]
and
\[
\begin{gathered}
\forall x\in S^3\setminus\gamma\quad\forall T>0\quad
\exists t_+,t_-\in\R:\\
t_+>T,\qquad t_-<-T,\qquad
\phi_{t_+}(x)\in D^\circ,\qquad \phi_{t_-}(x)\in D^\circ.
\end{gathered}
\]
Thus every trajectory other than the boundary orbit meets the disk interior transversely at arbitrarily large positive and negative times. The boundary orbit itself is tangent to the Reeb flow and is excluded from this intersection requirement.
