\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a group equipped with a twin \(BN\)-pair \((B^+,B^-,N)\), where \(B^+\) and \(B^-\) denote its distinguished positive and negative Borel subgroups. Let \(\theta\in\operatorname{Aut}(G)\) satisfy \(\theta^2=\operatorname{id}_G\), and write
\[
K=G^\theta=\{g\in G:\theta(g)=g\}.
\]
The hypothesis to be dropped is
\[
\theta(B^+)=B^-.
\]
No replacement condition on the images of the Borel subgroups is specified in the supplied record.

\textbf{Problem (theory without Borel exchange).} Develop an appropriate theory of Iwasawa decomposition for such groups and involutions without assuming \(\theta(B^+)=B^-\). Under the usual fixed-subgroup interpretation of Iwasawa decomposition, a concrete factorization question is to determine for which such data the multiplication map
\[
K\times B^+\longrightarrow G,\qquad (k,b)\longmapsto kb,
\]
is surjective, that is, when
\[
G=KB^+
\]
holds, and to describe the appropriate replacement for the decomposition when it does not hold.
