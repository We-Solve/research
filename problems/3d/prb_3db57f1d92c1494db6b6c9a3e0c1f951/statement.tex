Must every graph with $n$ vertices and more than $n^2/4$ edges have at least $2n^2/9$ edges lying in a $5$-cycle? No. We use a rational choice of parameters in the F\"uredi--Maleki construction recorded as Construction 2 by Grzesik--Hu--Volec. This avoids rounding issues and supplies an explicit $168$-vertex counterexample. It is a reconstruction, not a new extremal result.
