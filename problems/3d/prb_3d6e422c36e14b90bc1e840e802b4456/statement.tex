\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be an integer. A domain \(\Omega\subset\C^n\) is hyperconvex if it admits a continuous negative plurisubharmonic exhaustion \(\rho\), meaning that \(\{z\in\Omega:\rho(z)<c\}\) is relatively compact in \(\Omega\) for every \(c<0\). Write \(\operatorname{PSH}(\Omega)\) for the plurisubharmonic functions on \(\Omega\), namely upper semicontinuous functions whose restrictions to complex lines are subharmonic, allowing identically \(-\infty\) restrictions.

Let \(\Omega\subset\C^n\) be a bounded hyperconvex domain, and let \(f\in C^\infty(\Omega;\R)\) satisfy
\[
\exists\,\varepsilon>0\quad\forall z\in\Omega,\qquad f(z)\geq\varepsilon.
\]
Assume, as stipulated in the source, that there is a unique function \(u\in\operatorname{PSH}(\Omega)\cap C(\Omega)\) satisfying
\[
(dd^c u)^n=f\,dV\quad\text{in }\Omega,
\qquad
\lim_{\Omega\ni z\to\zeta}u(z)=0\quad(\zeta\in\partial\Omega).
\]
Here \(dV\) denotes Euclidean volume, and the equation is an equality of measures using the complex Monge--Amp\`ere measure for locally bounded plurisubharmonic functions. One may fix the convention \(dd^c=i\partial\bar\partial\); the source does not specify a normalization, and a different positive normalization only rescales the density.

\textbf{Question (attributed in the source to Blocki).} With smoothness interpreted as interior smoothness, must every such stipulated solution satisfy

u\in C^\infty(\Omega)?
\]
The source asks whether \(u\) is automatically smooth without explicitly specifying the smoothness locus. It does not state that \(f\) extends smoothly to \(\overline\Omega\), impose an upper bound or integrability condition on \(f\), or impose boundary regularity on \(\Omega\). The boundary condition above is interpreted as continuous boundary limits. Existence and uniqueness are retained as part of the supplied setup, rather than asserted here as a theorem for all such densities. Smoothness up to the boundary is not specified by the supplied formulation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For a bounded hyperconvex domain in \(\C^n\), consider the stipulated unique continuous plurisubharmonic solution of the complex Monge--Amp\`ere equation with zero boundary values and a smooth density bounded below by a positive constant. Must the solution be smooth in the interior? The source does not explicitly specify whether its word ``smooth'' also concerns the boundary.
