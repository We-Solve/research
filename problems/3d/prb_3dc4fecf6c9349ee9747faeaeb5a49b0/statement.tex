\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix a real constant \(\gamma\geq1\), independent of \(n\). An \(n\times n\) doubly stochastic matrix \(A=(a_{ij})\) has real entries \(a_{ij}\geq0\) with every row sum and column sum equal to one. Its permanent is
\[
\operatorname{per}(A)=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\prod_{i=1}^n a_{i,\sigma(i)}.
\]
Assume the squared Frobenius-norm bound
\[
\sum_{i,j=1}^n a_{ij}^2\leq\gamma.
\]
The main question is whether
\[
\operatorname{per}(A)=n^{O(1)}e^{-n}
\]
uniformly over these matrices as \(n\to\infty\), with the polynomial exponent and constants allowed to depend on the fixed \(\gamma\). The requested polynomial-factor upper estimate can be stated as
\[
\exists C_\gamma>0\ \exists k_\gamma\geq0\ \exists n_\gamma\quad
\forall n\geq n_\gamma\ \forall A\text{ satisfying the hypotheses},\qquad
\operatorname{per}(A)\leq C_\gamma n^{k_\gamma}e^{-n}.
\]
If the equality notation is intended to require two-sided polynomial comparability, one must additionally require a lower bound of the form \(C_\gamma^{-1}n^{-k_\gamma}e^{-n}\). The extraction does not expand its asymptotic notation. In particular, \(\gamma\) is fixed before the matrix dimension; no dimension-independent constants uniform over all \(\gamma\) are asserted.

\subsection{Short English statement}
For fixed \(\gamma\), does a doubly stochastic matrix with squared Frobenius norm at most \(\gamma\) have permanent at most a polynomial in its dimension times \(e^{-n}\)? The raw source also contains a separately catalogued planar tree-versus-triangulation question.
