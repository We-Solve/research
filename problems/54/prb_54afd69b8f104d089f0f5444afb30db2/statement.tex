\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(K\) be a field, let \(\overline K\) be an algebraic closure, and let \(G\) be an absolutely almost simple simply connected algebraic group over \(K\). A \(K\)-form of \(G\) is an algebraic group \(G'\) over \(K\) such that \(G'_{\overline K}\simeq G_{\overline K}\). A maximal \(K\)-torus of an algebraic group \(H\) is a torus defined over \(K\) whose base change to \(\overline K\) is a maximal torus of \(H_{\overline K}\). Write
\[
\mathcal T_K(H)=\{[T]_K:T\text{ is a maximal }K\text{-torus of }H\},
\]
where \([T]_K\) denotes the \(K\)-isomorphism class of the torus \(T\). Interpreting ``the same maximal \(K\)-tori'' as equality of these sets, define
\[
\operatorname{gen}_K(G)=\{[G']_K:G'\text{ is a }K\text{-form of }G,\ \mathcal T_K(G')=\mathcal T_K(G)\}.
\]
Thus each maximal \(K\)-torus of either group must be \(K\)-isomorphic to a maximal \(K\)-torus of the other.

\textbf{Question.} Under what hypotheses on \(K\) and \(G\) is
\[
\#\operatorname{gen}_K(G)<\infty?
\]
In particular, does this finiteness hold for every such \(G\) over every field \(K\) finitely generated over its prime field and having ``good characteristic''?
