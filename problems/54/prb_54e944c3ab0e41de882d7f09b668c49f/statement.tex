\section{Uniqueness of Stationary Black-Hole Exteriors}\label{30001360}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30001360 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} An asymptotically flat vacuum initial data set $(\Sigma,\bar g,K)$ satisfies the Einstein constraints and has maximal globally hyperbolic development $(M,g)$ with $\operatorname{Ric}(g)=0$. The Kerr family describes rotating vacuum black holes by mass $m>0$ and specific angular momentum $a$; its subextremal black-hole range is $|a|<m$. Future null infinity $\mathcal I^+$ is the ideal endpoint boundary of outgoing null rays, and $J^-(\mathcal I^+)$ is its causal past. Completeness of $\mathcal I^+$ is the future-completeness condition in the report's asymptotically flat formulation. A stationary exterior admits a time-translation Killing field. A bifurcate event horizon has future and past null branches meeting in a bifurcation surface. Nondegeneracy excludes zero surface gravity. The source's uniqueness question concerns sufficiently regular asymptotically flat vacuum stationary exteriors with the stated simple-connectivity and horizon assumptions.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualifications.} The catalogue literature assessment calls this a dynamical settling expectation, but the clean statement and primary uniqueness contribution concern stationary uniqueness. The report explains real-analytic uniqueness and seeks weaker regularity. Dynamical Kerr stability is a different target, related to ID 30001359.

\paragraph{Catalogue mathematical target.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Are the Kerr solutions the only sufficiently regular stationary black-hole exteriors with a simply connected, non-degenerate bifurcate event horizon?

\paragraph{Saved literature context.} The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The extracted statement is an asymptotic-settling expectation for general dynamical black-hole exteriors rather than a theorem; existing uniqueness results are stationary or perturbative and do not settle this general dynamical claim. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine whether the regular stationary vacuum black-hole exteriors under the report's horizon assumptions are all Kerr.
