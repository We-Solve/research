\section{Computational Complexity of Bruhat-Interval Invariants}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


A Coxeter system is a group generated by involutions with pairwise product orders specified by its Coxeter matrix. $\ell(w)$ is the shortest generator-word length. The Bruhat interval $[u,v]$ consists of elements between $u$ and $v$ in Bruhat order.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Given a Coxeter system $(W,S)$ and elements $u\leq v$ with Coxeter lengths $\ell(u)$ and $\ell(v)$, what is the computational complexity of computing the Bruhat-interval quantity $P_{u,v}$, equivalently the coefficients $a_i(u,v)$, or all cd-coefficients $[w]_{u,v}$, as a function of the Coxeter matrix and the input lengths? \sref{statement}{2}
