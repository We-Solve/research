\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701492}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(A\) be an arbitrary abelian group, written additively, and let \(\operatorname{Aut}(A)\) denote its group of automorphisms. An automorphism \(\gamma\) is fixed-point-free if \(\{a\in A:\gamma(a)=a\}=\{0\}\). A group is periodic if each of its elements has finite order; no common bound on these orders is assumed.

Suppose that \(\alpha,\beta\in\operatorname{Aut}(A)\) each have order exactly \(5\) and are fixed-point-free, and put \(G=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle\leq\operatorname{Aut}(A)\). Is the following implication true for every such \(A,\alpha,\beta\)?
\[
\bigl(\forall g\in G\;\exists n\in\Z_{\geq 1}:g^n=1_G\bigr)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |G|<\infty.
\]
No finiteness or finite generation assumption on \(A\) is supplied.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must a periodic group generated by two fixed-point-free automorphisms of order exactly \(5\) of the same abelian group be finite? The problem is attributed to V. D. Mazurov.
