 S\cap\{v:v\mid p\}=\varnothing.

\[
 \forall n\geq1\ \forall\text{ continuous homomorphisms }
 \rho:G_{K,S}\to\operatorname{GL}_n(\Z_p):\qquad
 |\rho(G_{K,S})|<\infty.

\subsection{Short English statement}
If ramification is allowed at only finitely many places away from $p$, must every continuous integral $p$-adic representation of the specified Galois group have finite image? Equivalently, must it lack infinite compact $p$-adic analytic quotients? The source's full-versus-pro-$p$ group convention needs confirmation.
