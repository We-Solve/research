\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a positive integer \(m\), let \(\mathcal F\) be a family of \(m\) distinct sets. A subfamily \(\mathcal U\subseteq\mathcal F\) is \emph{union-free} if there are no pairwise distinct \(X,Y,Z\in\mathcal U\) such that \(X\cup Y=Z\). Define
\[
f(m)=\max\bigl\{k\in\{0,\ldots,m\}:\text{every family of }m\text{ sets contains a union-free subfamily of size at least }k\bigr\}.
\]
