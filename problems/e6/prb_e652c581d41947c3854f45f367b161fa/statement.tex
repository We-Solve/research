\mathbb H=\{z:\operatorname{Im}z>0\},\qquad
B^2=\{z:\operatorname{Im}z>0,\ |z|<1\}.
\]

Let $\mathcal F$ be the source's class of analytic conformal maps $f:\mathbb H\to B^2$; whether conformal explicitly requires global injectivity should be confirmed. An angular limit $\alpha$ at $0$ means that $f(z)\to\alpha$ as $z\to0$ in each nontangential region $|\operatorname{Re}z|\le C\operatorname{Im}z$, $C>0$.

\liminf_{r\downarrow0}\frac{m(A\cap(0,r))}{r}>0,
\qquad A=\{|z|:z\in E\},
