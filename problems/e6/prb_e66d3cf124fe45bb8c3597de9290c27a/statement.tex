Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a regular local ring of characteristic $p>0$ and let $P\subset R$ be a height-two prime. If $\operatorname{Spec}(R/P)$ has log canonical singularities in the discrepancy sense, must
\[
 P^{(3)}\subset P^2?
\]

The supplied report first claims the unconditional regular-local Frobenius containment: for every height-$h$ prime, every $e\geq1$, and $q=p^e$,

 P^{(h(q-1)+1)}\subset P^{[q]}\subset P^q,
