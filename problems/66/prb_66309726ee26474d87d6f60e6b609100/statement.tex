\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\Lambda\subset\partial_\infty\mathbb H^3$ and let $C(\Lambda)$ denote its hyperbolic convex hull. For a point $p$ in that hull, write
\[
d(p)=\operatorname{dist}_{\mathbb H^3}\bigl(p,\mathbb H^3\setminus C(\Lambda)\bigr).
\]
This measures depth inside the hull; for a point outside the hull the distance to the exterior is zero. A quasicircle is the image of a round circle under a quasiconformal self-homeomorphism of the boundary sphere.
