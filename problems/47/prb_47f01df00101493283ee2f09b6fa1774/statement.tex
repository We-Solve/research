\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a countable discrete group. Its real group algebra is
\[
\R G=\left\{\sum_{g\in G}a_g g : a_g\in\R,\ a_g=0\text{ for all but finitely many }g\right\},
\]
with multiplication induced by the group law and involution \((\sum_g a_g g)^*=\sum_g a_g g^{-1}\). Let \(\lambda_g\) act on \(\ell^2(G;\R)\) by \((\lambda_g\xi)(h)=\xi(g^{-1}h)\). The reduced real group \(C^*\)-algebra \(C_r^*(G;\R)\) is the operator-norm closure of the image of \(\R G\) under this representation, with the operator-adjoint involution. Denote the resulting inclusion by \(\iota_G:\R G\to C_r^*(G;\R)\). For an abelian group \(M\), put \(M[1/2]=M\otimes_{\Z}\Z[1/2]\).

\textbf{Question.} Is the completion-induced homomorphism
\[
L_n(\iota_G)[1/2]:L_n(\R G)[1/2]\longrightarrow L_n(C_r^*(G;\R))[1/2]
\]
an isomorphism for every countable discrete group \(G\) and every degree \(n\in\Z\)?

For every countable discrete group, does completing its real group algebra to its reduced real group \(C^*\)-algebra induce an isomorphism on \(L\)-theory in every degree after inverting \(2\)? The intended \(L\)-theory convention remains unspecified in the supplied record.
