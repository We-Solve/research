\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $d\geq3$, let $R$ be a reduced commutative ring containing $\Q$, and put $Y=(y_1,\ldots,y_{d-1})$, $A=R[t][x,Y]$, $A_0=A/(t^2)$. Reduced means that $R$ has no nonzero nilpotent elements. The exact source asks for
\[
 \bar\gamma\in\operatorname{Aut}_x(A_0)
                 \cap\operatorname{Aut}_{R[t]}(A),
 \qquad \bar\gamma\notin\operatorname{Aut}_x(A).
\]
This expression is retained, but it compares automorphisms of \emph{different rings} without specifying an identification, reduction preimage or image convention. The subscript $x$ is also not defined.

There is a canonical reduction homomorphism
\[
 \rho:\operatorname{Aut}_{R[t]}(A)
       \longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{R[t]/(t^2)}(A_0),
\]
since coefficient-fixing automorphisms preserve $(t^2)$. Under the common convention that $\operatorname{Aut}_x$ means fixing the coordinate $x$, two possible questions are quite different:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Does some lift $\gamma$ have $\rho(\gamma)\in\operatorname{Aut}_x(A_0)$ but $\gamma\notin\operatorname{Aut}_x(A)$?
\item Does some $\bar\gamma\in\operatorname{Aut}_x(A_0)\cap\operatorname{im}\rho$ admit no lift fixing $x$, that is $\bar\gamma\notin\rho(\operatorname{Aut}_x(A))$?
\end{enumerate}
The first, if read literally with no further condition, is trivial: $\gamma(x)=x+t^2$, $\gamma(Y)=Y$ is an $R[t]$-automorphism reducing to the identity but not fixing $x$. It does \emph{not} witness the second question, because the identity also has an $x$-fixing lift. Thus an individual non-fixing lift and an obstruction to \emph{every} fixing lift must not be confused. Another meaning of $\operatorname{Aut}_x$ could change both formulations.
