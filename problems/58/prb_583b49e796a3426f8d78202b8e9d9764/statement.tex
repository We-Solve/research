For $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ and a fixed positive integer $r$, write $rA$ for sums of exactly $r$ members, allowing repetitions, and $r^{\wedge}A$ for sums of exactly $r$ pairwise distinct members. If $A$ is a basis of order $r$, must $r^{\wedge}A$ have positive lower density? The companion question asks whether positive upper density of $rA$ implies positive upper density of $r^{\wedge}A$.
