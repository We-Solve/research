\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(K\) be a fixed field. For a \(K\)-vector space \(V\), a Hamel basis is a subset \(B\subseteq V\) such that every \(v\in V\) has a unique representation
\[
 v=\sum_{b\in\operatorname{supp}(a)}a(b)b,
 \qquad a:B\longrightarrow K,
 \qquad \operatorname{supp}(a)=\{b\in B:a(b)\ne0\}\text{ finite}.
\]
The empty sum is zero; in particular, the zero vector space has the empty basis. Write
\[
\mathsf{B}(K):\quad
\text{Every vector space over the fixed field }K\text{ has a Hamel basis}.
\]
