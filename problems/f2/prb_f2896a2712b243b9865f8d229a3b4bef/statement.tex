\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
In \(K=\C(Z)\), let \(v=\operatorname{ord}_{Z=0}\), with \(v(0)=\infty\), and
\[
 \mathcal O_0=\{x\in K:v(x)\ge0\}=\C[Z]_{(Z)}.
\]
Pheidas's Question 25 asks whether \(\mathcal O_0\) is definable, particularly existentially definable, in the ring language with a named constant for \(Z\). The exact allowance of any further parameters is not specified; the aid's undecidability conclusion concerns the stated \((\C(Z);Z)\) language, without silently adding arbitrary complex constants.

The aid studies the one-equation existential set
\[
 S=\{x\in K:\exists y\in K,\ y^2=1+Zx^2\}.
\]
It reports the exact projected parametrization
\[
 S=\left\{\frac{2r}{1-Zr^2}:r\in K\right\},
\]
with corresponding \(y=(1+Zr^2)/(1-Zr^2)\). The denominator never vanishes for rational \(r\), since \(1/Z\) is not a square in \(K\). Its conclusions are
\[
 S\subsetneq\mathcal O_0,\qquad 1\notin S,
 \qquad
 S\longrightarrow\mathcal O_0/Z^N\mathcal O_0\text{ is onto for every }N\ge1.
\]
It also contains an element of every nonnegative valuation; the parametrization with \(r=Z^m\) gives valuation \(m\) for \(m\ge0\). Thus \(S\) is \(Z\)-adically dense in \(\mathcal O_0\), even though it fails to contain the regular rational function one.

The source of the distinction is the ambient field. In \(\C((Z))\) the same existential predicate defines exactly \(\C[[Z]]\), by the valuation parity obstruction for negative valuation and the Hensel square root of a unit congruent to one for nonnegative valuation. In \(\C(Z)\), a formal square root need not be globally rational. Finite jets do not distinguish the dense set \(S\) from \(\mathcal O_0\); this fact is not a nondefinability theorem for every possible existential formula.

If \(\mathcal O_0\) had an existential definition in the stated language, its complement would also be existential, using
\[
 x\notin\mathcal O_0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists u\in\mathcal O_0:\ Zxu=1.
\]
The aid further cites Daans's Proposition 5.1 for the consequence that such a definition would imply undecidability of the existential theory of \((\C(Z);Z)\). The proposition's exact hypotheses and full citation are not supplied. No definition or unconditional undecidability result is established by this conic example.
