\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((W,S)\) be a Coxeter system, let \(\ell\colon W\to\Z_{\geq 0}\) be its length function, and let \(\leq\) denote Bruhat order: \(x\leq y\) when a reduced expression for \(x\) occurs as a subword of a reduced expression for \(y\).

Write \(P^W_{x,y}(q)\) for the Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomial in the following normalization. The Hecke algebra has standard basis \(\{T_x:x\in W\}\), the usual braid relations, and quadratic relations \((T_s-q)(T_s+1)=0\). Its bar involution satisfies \(\overline{q^{1/2}}=q^{-1/2}\) and \(\overline{T_x}=T_{x^{-1}}^{-1}\). For each \(y\in W\), the element
\[
C'_y=q^{-\ell(y)/2}\sum_{x\leq y}P^W_{x,y}(q)T_x
\]
is bar invariant, where \(P^W_{y,y}(q)=1\), \(P^W_{x,y}(q)\in\Z[q]\), and
\[
\deg P^W_{x,y}\leq \frac{\ell(y)-\ell(x)-1}{2}\qquad(x<y).
\]
These conditions characterize the polynomials.

The question attributed in the supplied source to Bj\"{o}rner is: for every Coxeter system \((W,S)\) and every pair \(u,v\in W\) satisfying \(u\leq v\), do there exist a Coxeter system \((W',S')\) and an element \(w\in W'\) such that
\[
P^W_{u,v}(q)=P^{W'}_{e',w}(q),
\]
where \(e'\) is the identity of \(W'\)? The system \((W',S')\) and the element \(w\) may depend on \((W,S,u,v)\). The supplied formulation imposes no finiteness or crystallographic hypothesis on either Coxeter system.

As background, the supplied source states, citing [Polo] and also [CasRep], that for every \(P(q)\in\Z_{\geq 0}[q]\) with \(P(0)=1\), there exist a positive integer \(n\) and elements \(a,b\in S_n\) with \(a\leq b\) such that \(P(q)=P^{S_n}_{a,b}(q)\), with \(S_n\) carrying its standard Coxeter generators. This background assertion does not require \(a\) to be the identity. Its cited bibliography entries are not supplied, so it is retained as an attributed source assertion.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomial attached to a comparable pair in a Coxeter group also occur for a pair consisting of the identity and an element of some possibly different Coxeter group? The source attributes the question to Bj\"{o}rner; the registry labels it open, without independent status verification here.
