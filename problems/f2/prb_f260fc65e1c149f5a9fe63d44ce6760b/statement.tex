For a smooth projective curve $C$ and line bundle $L$, set $V=H^0(C,L)$. Koszul cohomology $K_{p,q}(C,L)$ is the middle homology of
\[
\bigwedge^{p+1}V\otimes H^0(C,L^{q-1})\longrightarrow
\bigwedge^pV\otimes H^0(C,L^q)\longrightarrow
\bigwedge^{p-1}V\otimes H^0(C,L^{q+1}),
\]
with differentials induced by multiplication of sections. Canonical syzygies take $L=K_C$ when the canonical map is an embedding. A precise proposed degeneration should identify its family, corresponding complexes and the specialization or lifting step yielding information on these groups.
