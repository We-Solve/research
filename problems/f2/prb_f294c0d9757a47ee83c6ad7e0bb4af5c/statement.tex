\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be a closed smooth three-dimensional manifold, and let \(\alpha\) be a contact form on \(M\), meaning that \(\alpha\wedge\dd\alpha\) is nowhere zero. Its Reeb vector field \(R_\alpha\) is determined by
\[
\alpha(R_\alpha)=1,\qquad \dd\alpha(R_\alpha,\cdot)=0.
\]
Define the Reeb action spectrum by
\[
\mathcal A(\alpha)=\left\{\int_0^T\alpha_{\gamma(t)}(\dot\gamma(t))\,\dd t:\ T>0,\ \gamma:\R/T\Z\longrightarrow M,\ \dot\gamma=R_\alpha\circ\gamma\right\}.
\]
Each such action equals the period \(T\). This definition includes multiple covers. Interpret the additive rank of the spectrum as the rank of the additive subgroup it generates:
\[
G_\alpha=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^k n_j a_j:\ k\geq 0,\ n_j\in\Z,\ a_j\in\mathcal A(\alpha)\right\}\subseteq\R,
\qquad
r(\alpha)=\dim_{\Q}\bigl(G_\alpha\otimes_{\Z}\Q\bigr)
=\dim_{\Q}\operatorname{span}_{\Q}\mathcal A(\alpha).
\]
The dimension is allowed to be infinite. Two periodic Reeb orbits are geometrically distinct when their images in \(M\) differ; multiple covers of one orbit therefore count only once.

\textbf{Questions.} Which values of \(r(\alpha)\) occur for contact forms on closed three-manifolds? Does every such pair \((M,\alpha)\) satisfy
\[
r(\alpha)=2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\#\{\operatorname{im}(\gamma):\gamma\text{ is a periodic Reeb orbit of }R_\alpha\}=2?
\]
