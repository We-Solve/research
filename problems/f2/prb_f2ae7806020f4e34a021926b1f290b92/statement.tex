\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix the source's class \(\mathcal L\) of links and a coefficient field \(k\). Let \(V\) be the unital commutative \(k\)-algebra of finite-type invariants on \(\mathcal L\), with pointwise multiplication. Finite type of order at most \(d\) means that the invariant, extended to singular links by the crossing-difference rule, vanishes on links with more than \(d\) transverse double points. The source does not fix \(k\), orientation/framing, or component-number conventions.

For any ideal \(I\lhd V\), define
\[
Z(I)=\{L\in\mathcal L:\forall v\in I,\ v(L)=0\},
\qquad
J(I)=\{v\in V:\forall L\in Z(I),\ v(L)=0\},
\]
\[
\sqrt I=\{v\in V:\exists m\ge1,\ v^m\in I\}.
\]
Evaluation \(\operatorname{ev}_L:V\to k\) is an algebra homomorphism. Because \(k\) is a field, each evaluation kernel is prime, and
\[
J(I)=\bigcap_{L\in Z(I)}\ker\operatorname{ev}_L
\supseteq\sqrt I.
\]
If \(Z(I)=\varnothing\), the intersection convention gives \(J(I)=V\).

Bar-Natan asks whether equality always holds:
\[
\forall I\lhd V,\qquad J(I)=\sqrt I.
\]
Equivalently, whenever \(v\notin\sqrt I\), must there be an actual link \(L\) with all invariants in \(I\) zero and \(v(L)\ne0\)? The ideal is not assumed finitely generated. This evaluates on links, not automatically on every algebra homomorphism \(V\to k\) or a completed/formal link space.
