\paragraph{An elementary multiple-cover inversion.}Fix one effective degree variable \(Q\) and postulate a genus-zero multiple-cover series
\[F(Q)=\sum_{d\ge1}n_d\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{Q^{kd}}{k^3}=\sum_{m\ge1}A_mQ^m.
\]
This is a formal identity: each coefficient has finitely many divisor contributions, so no analytic convergence is needed. Coefficient comparison gives \(m^3A_m=\sum_{d\mid m}d^3n_d\). Let \(\mu(k)\) be the arithmetic Mobius function, characterized by \(\sum_{k\mid n}\mu(k)=1\) for \(n=1\) and zero otherwise. Summing the last identity with these coefficients yields
\[n_m=\sum_{k\mid m}\frac{\mu(k)}{k^3}A_{m/k}.
\]
The proof exchanges finite divisor sums; the inner Mobius sum cancels every proper divisor contribution. For instance \(n_1=A_1\) and \(n_2=A_2-A_1/8\).
\paragraph{A concrete integrality obstruction.}Integer coefficients \(A_m\) alone do not imply integer \(n_m\). Taking \(A_1=1\) and \(A_2=0\) forces \(n_2=-1/8\). Conversely integer BPS inputs naturally produce rational cover coefficients. Thus integrality is extra enumerative structure, not a formal consequence of changing generating-function variables.
\paragraph{Full scope remaining.}No actual Calabi--Yau invariants are computed here and the postulated cover convention is explicitly limited to genus zero and one degree variable. Holomorphic anomaly equations, higher-genus cover factors, elliptic and K3 fiber integration, geometric integrality and boundary ambiguities all remain unproved. The first gap is relating a rigorously defined geometric amplitude to the cover expansion and to a specified pushforward. This known formal inversion is a consistency tool, not a solution of the fibered anomaly/BPS programme.
