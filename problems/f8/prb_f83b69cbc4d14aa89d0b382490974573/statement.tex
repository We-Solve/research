\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


For an integer matrix $A$ and statistic vector $b$, the nonnegative integer fiber is $\mathcal F_b=\{x\in\mathbb N^d:Ax=b\}$. A Markov basis is a finite set of integer moves in $\ker A$ connecting every such fiber by moves that remain nonnegative. Its binomial generators form the toric ideal $I_A=\langle x^u-x^v:Au=Av\rangle$. An ideal of points consists of polynomials vanishing on a finite point set. A Gröbner basis depends on a coefficient field and monomial order. The R environment can call external algebra packages through software interfaces.
