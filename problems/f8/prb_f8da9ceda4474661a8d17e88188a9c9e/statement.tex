Starting from $K_n$, repeatedly select uniformly among the triangles currently present and remove the selected triangle's three edges. Stop when no triangle remains, and denote the final number of edges by $f(n)$. Determine the typical structure and parameters of the terminal graph. In particular, are
\[
\mathbb E f(n)\asymp n^{3/2}\qquad\text{and}\qquad f(n)\ll n^{3/2}
\]
valid, with the latter holding asymptotically almost surely?
