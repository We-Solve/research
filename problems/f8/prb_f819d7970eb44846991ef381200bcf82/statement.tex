\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source discusses PFA (the Proper Forcing Axiom), a universal Aronszajn line $\eta_C$, and the question whether universality or well-quasi-ordering by embeddability forces
\[
 |\R|=2^{\aleph_0}\le\aleph_2.
\]
A standard Aronszajn line has cardinality $\aleph_1$, no suborder of type $\omega_1$ or its reverse, and no uncountable separable suborder. An order embedding preserves and reflects the linear order. Universality of $\eta_C$ means that every Aronszajn line embeds into it. Well-quasi-ordering by embeddability means that every sequence $(L_i)_{i\in\N}$ of Aronszajn lines has $i<j$ with $L_i$ embedding into $L_j$.
