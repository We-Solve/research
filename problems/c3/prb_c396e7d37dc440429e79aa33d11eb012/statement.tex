The radius-two disk assertion is true, with centre equal to the arithmetic mean of the roots. The least possible diameter of a connected closed unit lemniscate is also two. The conjectured greatest width two is false. Thus the page's negative overall status must not be read as a counterexample to its opening disk-containment question.

We reconstruct the conformal-map proof of containment and give an explicit width counterexample. The historical source is Pommerenke, \emph{On some problems by Erd\H{o}s, Herzog and Piranian}, Michigan Math. J. 6 (1959), 221--225, as cited at \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/1046}. Our analytic inputs are the Riemann mapping theorem, continuous boundary extension for locally connected boundaries, and elementary complex analysis. The coefficient estimate needed below is proved rather than imported.
