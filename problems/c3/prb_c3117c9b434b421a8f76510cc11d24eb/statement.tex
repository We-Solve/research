\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an orthogonal projection $P=P^*=P^2\in M_n(\C)$ and a coordinate set $A$, let $Q_A$ be its diagonal coordinate projection. To fix the aid's convention, define for $1\le r\le n$
\[\pi_r(P)=\min_{\substack{\{1,\ldots,n\}=A_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup A_r\\A_j\ne\varnothing}}\max_j\|Q_{A_j}PQ_{A_j}\|.\]
Direct-compression $(r,c)$-pavability means $\pi_r(P)\le c$, while strict $r$-pavability means $\pi_r(P)<1$. This is not automatically the convention of paving the zero-diagonal matrix $P-\operatorname{diag}P$. The original does not specify a target constant or whether empty blocks are permitted.

\textbf{Original request.} Fix $r$ and find conditions on a projection's diagonal that guarantee $r$-paving, independently of the dimension, with interest in the geometry of the problem. A criterion for one specified projection, existence of some projection with a diagonal, and a guarantee for every projection with that diagonal are distinct possible targets; the source does not choose among them.

\textbf{Attached unreviewed fixed-fiber theorem.} In the complex rank-two fiber
\[P\in M_4(\C),\qquad\operatorname{diag}P=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2),\]
the aid asserts
\[\pi_2(P)=\frac12+\min\{|p_{12}|,|p_{13}|,|p_{14}|\}.\]
Over this fiber it reports the exact range
\[\left[\frac12,\frac12+\frac1{2\sqrt3}\right],\]
with an explicit projection path realizing every value, although that path is not in the supplied summary. Every real projection in the same fiber is reported to have $\pi_2(P)=1/2$.

Consequently the existential optimal constant for this diagonal is $1/2$, whereas the universal worst-fiber constant is $1/2+1/(2\sqrt3)$. The aid says the diagonal therefore cannot decide whether an individual complex projection is $(2,3/4)$-pavable: the same diagonal admits both outcomes. This does not separate mere strict two-pavability, since every reported value is below one. The result is restricted to nonempty direct-compression blocks, $r=2$, $n=4$ and rank two; it is not a general dimension-independent classification.

The aid separately reports an exact rank-one characterization. For $P=vv^*$ with $\|v\|=1$ and diagonal entries $a_i=|v_i|^2$, compression on $A$ has norm $\sum_{i\in A}a_i$. Thus $(r,c)$-pavability is exactly the existence of $r$ nonempty bins partitioning the indices with each sum at most $c$, in the stated convention. This bin-packing condition is not claimed to reduce to a single largest-diagonal inequality or to give an efficient general algorithm. All assertions require independent review.
