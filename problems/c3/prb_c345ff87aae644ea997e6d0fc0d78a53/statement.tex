\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be a closed smooth manifold, and let \(\operatorname{Met}^{\infty}(M)\) denote its space of smooth Riemannian metrics. For \(g\in\operatorname{Met}^{\infty}(M)\), write \(S_gM\) for the unit tangent bundle and \(\varphi_g^t\) for the geodesic flow. This flow is \emph{Anosov} if there is a continuous invariant splitting
\[
T(S_gM)=E^s\oplus\R X_g\oplus E^u,
\]
where \(X_g\) generates the flow, and constants \(C,\lambda>0\) such that, for every \(t\geq0\),
\[
\|D\varphi_g^t(v)\|\leq Ce^{-\lambda t}\|v\|\quad(v\in E^s),\qquad
\|D\varphi_g^{-t}(w)\|\leq Ce^{-\lambda t}\|w\|\quad(w\in E^u).
\]
Here the norms may be taken with respect to a fixed auxiliary Riemannian metric on \(S_gM\). Define
\[
\operatorname{Met}_{\mathrm{Anosov}}(M)
=\{g\in\operatorname{Met}^{\infty}(M):\varphi_g^t\text{ is Anosov}\}.
\]
