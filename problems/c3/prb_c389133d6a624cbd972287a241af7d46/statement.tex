\section{Axis and low-dimensional cases of Levitin's disk-intersection conjecture}\label{20002500}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 20002500 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and notation.}An indefinite matrix pencil $A-\lambda B$ has eigenvalues where its determinant vanishes. A Nevanlinna function maps the upper half-plane to itself and the lower half-plane to itself under conjugate symmetry; meromorphic examples have real poles. A Schauder basis permits a unique norm-convergent ordered expansion. Counts in the source are zeros and poles, with a multiplicity convention requiring specification for a proof.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
The record bundles primary items 11.1--13.1.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Let $n\ge1$, $0<c<2$, and let $A$ be the $2n\times2n$ tridiagonal matrix with diagonal entries $c$ and adjacent off-diagonal entries $1$. Let $B=\operatorname{diag}(I_n,-I_n)$. Prove that every non-real $\lambda\in\sigma(BA)$ satisfies both $|\lambda-c|\le2$ and $|\lambda+c|\le2$.
\item Extend the spectral results for $(-d^2/dx^2+c/(1+|x|))\psi=\lambda\operatorname{sgn}(x)\psi$ to more general potential functions, specifying the admissible class and conclusions.
\item Let $m_j$ be meromorphic Nevanlinna functions and $I\subset\mathbb R$ a nonempty interval such that the number of poles in every nonempty subinterval $J\subset I$ tends to infinity with $j$. If $g$ is analytic on a complex neighborhood of $I$, show for every open complex neighborhood $U$ of $J$ that the number of zeros of $m_j-g$ in $U$ tends to infinity; also show that the difference between the numbers of such zeros and poles of $m_j$ in $U$ is bounded by a constant independent of $j$. The source records the case where $\lim_jj^{-1}\#\{\text{poles in }J\}$ exists.
\item For $V(x)=\sum_{k=-m}^m v_ke^{2ikx}$, $v_k\in\mathbb C$, classify coefficient sets for which eigenfunctions of $-d^2/dx^2+V$ on $(0,\pi)$ with periodic boundary conditions form a Schauder basis of $L^2(0,\pi)$. The source records the case $V=e^{-2ix}+be^{2ix}$ as affirmative exactly when $|b|=1$.
\end{enumerate}
