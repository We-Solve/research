\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a group \(G\), define its lower central series by \(\gamma_1(G)=G\) and \(\gamma_{i+1}(G)=[\gamma_i(G),G]\), where \([H,G]\) is generated by the commutators \(h^{-1}g^{-1}hg\) with \(h\in H\) and \(g\in G\). The group \(G\) has nilpotence class exactly \(p\) if \(\gamma_{p+1}(G)=\{1\}\) and \(\gamma_p(G)\ne\{1\}\).

Let \(\operatorname{Norm}(G)\) be the lattice of normal subgroups of \(G\), with meet \(N\cap M\) and join \(NM\). For an abelian group \(A\), let \(\operatorname{Sub}(A)\) be its subgroup lattice, with meet \(B\cap C\) and join \(BC\). Here a lattice embedding means an injective map preserving binary meets and joins. The supplied source does not explicitly specify whether preservation of the least and greatest elements is also required; no such additional requirement is imposed in this formulation.

\textbf{Problem.} For every prime \(p\), find a finite group \(G\) of order \(p^n\) for some integer \(n\geq 1\), of nilpotence class exactly \(p\), such that, for every abelian group \(A\), there is no injective map
\[
\phi:\operatorname{Norm}(G)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sub}(A)
\]
satisfying, for all \(N,M\trianglelefteq G\),
\[
\phi(N\cap M)=\phi(N)\cap\phi(M),\qquad
\phi(NM)=\phi(N)\phi(M).
\]
The abelian group \(A\) is not assumed finite.

The supplied statement says that the problem is solved for \(p=2\) and \(p=3\). This is a source-reported qualification, not an independently verified result; the registry labels the overall problem as open.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For each prime \(p\), find a finite \(p\)-group of nilpotence class exactly \(p\) whose normal-subgroup lattice cannot embed, preserving meets and joins, into the subgroup lattice of any abelian group, including an infinite one. The supplied source reports solutions for \(p=2,3\) and labels the overall problem open.
