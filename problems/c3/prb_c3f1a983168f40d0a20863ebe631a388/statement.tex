A connected manifold \(M\) is \emph{aspherical} if its universal covering space \(\widetilde M\) is contractible. For a closed, connected, oriented \(n\)-manifold, let \([M]_{\R}\in H_n(M;\R)\) be its real fundamental class. Define its simplicial volume by
\[
\lVert M\rVert=\inf\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{k}|a_j|:\ z=\sum_{j=1}^{k}a_j\sigma_j\in C_n(M;\R),\ \partial z=0,\ [z]=[M]_{\R}\right\},

\[
\chi(M)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}(-1)^i\dim_{\R}H_i(M;\R).
