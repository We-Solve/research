\section{Base-encoded monomial-curve designs}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


For a finite design $D$, evaluation sends a polynomial $f$ to $(f(v))_{v\in D}$. Monomials in a model set $L$ are linearly independent modulo $I(D)$ precisely when their evaluation vectors are independent. Thus an evaluation matrix with columns indexed by $L$ has full column rank. Statistical design properties impose additional conditions beyond that rank test.
