\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathcal S\) be the set of closed spin \(3\)-manifolds in the source's category, taken up to isomorphism of spin manifolds. A spin structure is a lift of the oriented frame bundle through \(\operatorname{Spin}(3)\to\operatorname{SO}(3)\). Let \(\sim_{Y_d^s}\) denote the source's spin \(Y_d\)-equivalence relation. G. Massuyeau asks for an explicit description of
\[
 \mathcal S/\!\sim_{Y_d^s},
\]
particularly for \(d=2\) and \(d=3\).

A classification would, for example, provide specified data \(\mathcal I_d\) and a realizable complete invariant \(I_d:\mathcal S\to\mathcal I_d\) such that
\[
 I_d(M,\sigma)=I_d(M',\sigma')
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 (M,\sigma)\sim_{Y_d^s}(M',\sigma'),
\]
thereby identifying the quotient with its explicitly described invariant values. This expresses the requested description without proposing particular invariants. The record does not define the \(Y_d^s\) moves or their degree and spin-transport conventions; although the notation points to spin clasper-surgery equivalence, the exact relation must be recovered from the source. It does not specify connectedness, orientation/equivalence conventions beyond spin, or the full allowed range of \(d\).
