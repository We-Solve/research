\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(p\) be a prime and \(G\) a finite group. For a positive integer \(n\), let \(n_p=p^{v_p(n)}\) denote the largest power of \(p\) dividing \(n\). Let \(O_p(G)\) denote the largest normal \(p\)-subgroup of \(G\), and let \(\operatorname{IBr}_p(G)\) denote the set of irreducible \(p\)-Brauer characters of \(G\), associated with simple representations over an algebraically closed field of characteristic \(p\). For \(\beta\in\operatorname{IBr}_p(G)\), its degree is \(\beta(1)\). Let \(A_7\) denote the alternating group on seven letters. Using the standard section interpretation, \(A_7\) is \emph{involved in} \(G\) if there exist \(H\leq G\) and \(K\trianglelefteq H\) such that \(H/K\cong A_7\).

Assume that
\[
O_p(G)=1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\beta(1)_p\leq p\quad\text{for every }\beta\in\operatorname{IBr}_p(G).
\]
The problem asks whether, for every such prime \(p\) and finite group \(G\), the following bounds hold:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{(i)} & |G|_2\leq 2^7,\quad\text{if }p=2;\\[2pt]
\text{(ii)} & |G|_p\leq p^2,\quad\text{if }p\geq5,\text{ or if }p=3\text{ and }A_7\text{ is not involved in }G;\\[2pt]
\text{(iii)} & |G|_3\leq 3^3,\quad\text{if }p=3\text{ and }A_7\text{ is involved in }G.
\end{array}
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
For a finite group with trivial normal \(p\)-subgroup, suppose every irreducible \(p\)-Brauer character has degree divisible by at most one factor of \(p\). Must the \(p\)-part of the group order satisfy the stated bounds, with the bound for \(p=3\) depending on whether \(A_7\) occurs as a section? The supplied record labels the problem open and describes the bounds as conjecturally sharp; these labels require independent review.
