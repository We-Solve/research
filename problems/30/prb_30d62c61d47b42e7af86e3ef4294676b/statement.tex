\section{Homeomorphism type of exponential Knaster continua}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For a real parameter $\lambda>1/e$, let $E_\lambda(z)=\lambda e^z$ on $\mathbb C$, and let $E_\lambda^n$ denote the $n$th iterate, with $E_\lambda^0$ the identity. Define $C_\lambda=\{z\in\mathbb C:0\leq\operatorname{Im}(E_\lambda^n(z))\leq\pi\text{ for every }n\geq0\}$. Give $C_\lambda$ its subspace topology from the plane. A homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse. The source describes these explicitly defined sets as Knaster-like continua.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every pair of real parameters $\lambda,\mu>1/e$, are $C_\lambda$ and $C_\mu$ homeomorphic? \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do the explicitly defined invariant strip sets have the same topology for all real exponential parameters greater than $1/e$?
