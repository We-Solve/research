\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For each \(c\in\Q\), let \(f_c\colon\Q\to\Q\) be the map \(f_c(x)=x^2+c\). Define its iterates by \(f_c^{\circ 0}(x)=x\) and \(f_c^{\circ(n+1)}=f_c\circ f_c^{\circ n}\) for integers \(n\geq 0\). A point \(x\in\Q\) is periodic if \(f_c^{\circ n}(x)=x\) for some integer \(n\geq 1\). Its exact period is the least such positive integer. Write
\[
\operatorname{Per}(f_c,\Q)=\{x\in\Q: f_c^{\circ n}(x)=x\text{ for some integer }n\geq 1\}.
\]
\textbf{Conjecture (Poonen, 1998; supplied source attribution).} For every \(c\in\Q\) and every \(x\in\operatorname{Per}(f_c,\Q)\), the exact period of \(x\) is at most \(3\).

Here period means least positive period, rather than an arbitrary return time. The supplied record gives an unresolved bibliography key for the Poonen reference; its full bibliographic details are not supplied.
