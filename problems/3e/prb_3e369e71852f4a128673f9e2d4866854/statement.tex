\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((X,J)\) be a compact complex manifold. A Hermitian--symplectic structure is understood here to mean a smooth real differential two-form \(\Omega\) such that
\[
\dd\Omega=0,
\qquad
\Omega_x(v,J_xv)>0
\quad\text{for every }x\in X\text{ and }0\ne v\in T_xX.
\]
The positivity condition implies that \(\Omega\) is nondegenerate; thus \(\Omega\) is a symplectic form taming the complex structure \(J\). It is not required to have type \((1,1)\). This is the standard taming interpretation of the terminology; the supplied excerpt does not explicitly define it.

The complex manifold \((X,J)\) is Kähler if there exists a smooth real differential form \(\omega\) of type \((1,1)\) satisfying
\[
\dd\omega=0,
\qquad
\omega_x(v,J_xv)>0
\quad\text{for every }x\in X\text{ and }0\ne v\in T_xX.
