\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\in\Z\) with \(n\geq4\), and let \((S^n,g_{\mathrm{rd}})\) be the unit round sphere, a compact manifold without boundary. Write \(\dd v_{\mathrm{rd}}\) for its volume measure and \(\operatorname{vol}_{\mathrm{rd}}(S^n)\) for its volume. Use the convention \(-\Delta_{g_{\mathrm{rd}}}\geq0\) and the following normalization of the round conformal Laplacian:
\[
Y=-\Delta_{g_{\mathrm{rd}}}+\frac{n-2}{4(n-1)}\operatorname{Scal}_{g_{\mathrm{rd}}}
 =-\Delta_{g_{\mathrm{rd}}}+\frac{n(n-2)}4.
\]

W(x)>0\quad(x\in S^n),\qquad
\int_{S^n}W^{n/2}\,\dd v_{\mathrm{rd}}
 =\operatorname{vol}_{\mathrm{rd}}(S^n),
\]

Y_W=W^{-1/2}YW^{-1/2},
\]

Regard \(Y_W\) as the positive self-adjoint realization on \(L^2(S^n,\dd v_{\mathrm{rd}})\) of this elliptic operator initially defined on smooth functions. No boundary conditions are needed. If \(\lambda_j(W)\), \(j\geq1\), are its eigenvalues, repeated according to multiplicity, set

f_W(t)=t^{n/2}\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tY_W})

\qquad t>0.
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
For each integer \(n\geq4\), consider every smooth positive weight on the round sphere whose \(n/2\)-power has the same integral as the constant weight \(1\). Must the heat trace of the corresponding weighted conformal Laplacian, multiplied by \(t^{n/2}\), be nonincreasing for every positive time? The supplied word ``decreasing'' leaves strictness unspecified. The open label and reported special cases are supplied dated curation and require independent review.
