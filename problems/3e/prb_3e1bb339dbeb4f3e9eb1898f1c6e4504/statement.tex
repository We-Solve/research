\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 1100121}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A closed hyperbolic \(3\)-manifold is a connected compact smooth \(3\)-manifold without boundary equipped with a complete Riemannian metric of constant sectional curvature \(-1\). A finite cover means a connected covering map of finite positive degree. To fiber over the circle means to admit a locally trivial bundle map to \(S^1\) with connected closed surface fiber. Thurston's question is the assertion
\[
\forall M\text{ closed hyperbolic }3\text{-manifold},\quad
\exists\,p:\widetilde M\longrightarrow M\text{ a finite cover},\quad
\exists\,f:\widetilde M\longrightarrow S^1\text{ a surface bundle}.
\]
No orientability hypothesis is stated in the source; finite covers are unrestricted. The supplied research summary records an affirmative resolution via Agol's virtual-specialness theorem together with the cubulation and virtual-fibering results it describes. This literature assessment is supplied metadata: the extraction notes independently retain an open-list presentation and a pending current-status release review.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every closed hyperbolic \(3\)-manifold have a finite-sheeted cover that is a surface bundle over the circle? The supplied registry labels the question solved affirmatively, while retaining source-verification review notes.
