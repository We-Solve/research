Suppose the deterministic system $\dot x=f(x,y)$, $\dot y=g(x,y)$ has a stable fixed point, translated to $0$, and its real Jacobian $A\in\R^{2\times2}$ has eigenvalues $-\lambda\pm i\omega$ with $\lambda>0$ and $\omega\ne0$. The source's noisy linearization is

 dU_t=AU_t\,dt+\begin{pmatrix}\sigma_1&0\\0&\sigma_2\end{pmatrix}dW_t,
 \qquad U_t=\begin{pmatrix}x_t\\y_t\end{pmatrix},
\]
with the usual interpretation of $W$ as a pair of independent standard Brownian motions. It states that this process can be approximated by a constant times a rotation times a pair of independent standard Ornstein--Uhlenbeck processes, and asks for an analogue for a discrete-time reaction--diffusion equation.
