\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S^1=\R/\Z\) with its standard orientation and real analytic structure. Let \(G=\mathrm{Diff}_+^\omega(S^1)\) be the group of orientation-preserving real analytic diffeomorphisms of \(S^1\), and let \(G^\delta\) denote this group equipped with the discrete topology. Write \(BG^\delta\) for its classifying space. The associated universal oriented circle bundle
\[
EG^\delta\times_{G^\delta}S^1\longrightarrow BG^\delta
\]
has an Euler class \(\chi\in H^2(BG^\delta;\Z)\). Let \(\chi_{\Q}\in H^2(BG^\delta;\Q)\) be its image under the coefficient homomorphism \(\Z\to\Q\), and let \(\chi_{\Q}^k\) denote its \(k\)-fold cup power.

Is it true that, for every integer \(k\geq 2\),
\[
\chi_{\Q}^k\neq 0\quad\text{in}\quad H^{2k}\!\left(B\mathrm{Diff}_+^\omega(S^1)^\delta;\Q\right)?
\]
Here the interpretation of the source's undefined notation \(\chi_{\Q}\) as the rationalized universal Euler class requires confirmation against the full source. The supplied excerpt describes the powers of the Euler class for real analytic flat circle bundles as unknown; this is a supplied source assessment, not an independently verified literature claim.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every cup power of degree at least two of the rational Euler class remain nonzero for the discrete group of orientation-preserving real analytic circle diffeomorphisms? The supplied registry labels this question open.
