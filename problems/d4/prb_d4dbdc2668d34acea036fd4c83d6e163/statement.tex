\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(d\in\Z\) satisfy \(d>2\). Equip \(\R^d\) with its Euclidean inner product \(\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle\) and norm \(\|\cdot\|_2\), and define
\[
S^{d-1}=\{n\in\R^d:\|n\|_2=1\},\qquad
\overline B_d=\{x\in\R^d:\|x\|_2\leq1\}.
\]
For \(n\in S^{d-1}\), \(\varepsilon>0\), and \(a\in\R\), define the closed slab of width \(\varepsilon\), normal direction \(n\), and offset \(a\) by
\[
P(n,\varepsilon,a)=\{x\in\R^d:a\leq\langle x,n\rangle\leq a+\varepsilon\}.
\]
Changing \(a\) allows an arbitrary translate of the slab while preserving its normal direction and width.

The finite covering question described in the supplied record is whether, for every integer \(d>2\), there exists a finite constant \(C(d)>0\) such that, for every positive integer \(m\), every choice of directions \(n_1,\ldots,n_m\in S^{d-1}\), and every choice of widths \(\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_m>0\),
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{m}\varepsilon_i>C(d)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists(a_1,\ldots,a_m)\in\R^m\quad
\overline B_d\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}P(n_i,\varepsilon_i,a_i).
\]
Thus \(C(d)\) must depend only on the dimension; the offsets may depend on the entire given family. The displayed slab inequalities and the strict inequality on the total width are retained exactly.

\subsection{Short English statement}
For each integer dimension \(d>2\), is there a threshold depending only on \(d\) such that every finite family of slabs with prescribed normal directions and positive widths whose sum exceeds that threshold can be translated to cover the unit ball? The supplied curation labels this question open. The source does not specify whether the ball includes its boundary; the displayed formulation uses the closed ball.
