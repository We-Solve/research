\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Using the conventional real-affine interpretation, let $H_i\subset\R^n$ be proper hyperplanes
\[
H_i=\{x\in\R^n:a_i\cdot x=b_i\},\qquad a_i\ne0,\quad 1\leq i\leq m.
\]
They form an essential cover of $\{0,1\}^n$ with all variables represented when the following conditions hold:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every cube vertex lies in some $H_i$.
\item For each $i$, there is a vertex $x_i\in\{0,1\}^n$ lying in $H_i$ and in no other $H_j$; thus removing $H_i$ destroys the cover.
\item For every coordinate $j\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, some normal $a_i$ has $(a_i)_j\ne0$.
\end{enumerate}
The last condition does not require every coefficient of every hyperplane to be nonzero. Define $f(n)$ as the minimum possible $m$ for such a cover.
