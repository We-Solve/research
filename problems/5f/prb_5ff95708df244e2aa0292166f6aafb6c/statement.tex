Let \(P=(P_1,P_2):\R^2\to\R^2\) be a polynomial mapping, with \(P_1,P_2\in\R[x,y]\). Use the total-degree convention
\[
\deg P=\max\{\deg P_1,\deg P_2\},\qquad
J_P(x,y)=\det\begin{pmatrix}
\partial P_1/\partial x & \partial P_1/\partial y\\
\partial P_2/\partial x & \partial P_2/\partial y
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
