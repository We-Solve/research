In its defining braided setting, a Nichols algebra is the quotient of the tensor algebra \(T(V)\) by the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal having zero intersection with degrees zero and one. If \(k\) is its ground field, define its Hilbert series by
\[
H_{\mathfrak B}(t)=\sum_{d\geq 0}\dim_k(\mathfrak B_d)t^d\in\mathbb Z[t].

(r)_u=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}u^j.
