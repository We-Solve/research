\section{Sofic Groups}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is every group sofic? Here soficity means that every finite subset admits arbitrarily accurate approximations by permutations of a finite set: multiplication is respected on all but a prescribed small proportion of points, and distinct group elements are represented by permutations differing on nearly all points.
