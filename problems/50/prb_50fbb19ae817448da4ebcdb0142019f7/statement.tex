\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 10400213}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For every compact Lie group \(G\), admissible level \(k\), and knotted trivalent graph (KTG) \(K:\Gamma\to\R^3\), the conjecture attributed to D. Bar-Natan and D. Thurston asks for a coherent family of measures \(\mu_K\) on the space \(\mathcal A_G(\Gamma)/\mathcal G_G(\Gamma)\) of gauge-equivalence classes of \(G\)-connections on \(\Gamma\), satisfying four requirements:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Compatibility with the source's KTG operations.
\item Localization near connections extending to \(S^3\setminus K\).
\item A half-twist framing change acts by \(\exp(iH\hbar/2)\), where \(H\) is the stated Schr\"odinger operator on \(G\).
\item Recovery of the quantum invariant by
\[I_R(K)=\int h_R(A)\,\dd\mu_K(A).\]
\end{enumerate}
Here \(R\) assigns representations of \(G\) to edges and appropriate intertwiners to vertices, and \(h_R\) is the corresponding gauge-invariant holonomy expression, with edge holonomies contracted using those intertwiners. The formula is required for the admissible colorings \(R\), not just a single representation.

For a finite graph, a customary holonomy model chooses an orientation of each edge and writes the gauge space as \(G^{E(\Gamma)}/G^{V(\Gamma)}\), with \((u_v)\) acting on edge transports by \(g_e\mapsto u_{t(e)}g_eu_{s(e)}^{-1}\). This explains a possible gauge-space realization, but does not specify the source's bundle and connection conventions. The compatibility equations for KTG operations, topology/scale and exact meaning of localization, and the extension/restriction map connecting graph connections to complement connections are all absent. No flatness requirement is added: the excerpt does not state one. The level range, relation of \(\hbar\) to \(k\), definition/domain of \(H\), and how its exponential acts on graph measures or observables also remain unspecified. ``Measures'' is not silently strengthened to positive probability measures or replaced by distributions; the intended analytic category and existence of the integrals require clarification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct gauge-space measures for every colored KTG that respect graph operations, localize near extendable connections, have the specified half-twist action, and integrate holonomy functions to the quantum invariants. Several analytic and compatibility conditions are only qualitative in the source.
