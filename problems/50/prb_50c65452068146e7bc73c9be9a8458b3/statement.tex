\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 20002028}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Problem \(9\) asks, for a compact manifold of even dimension greater than two, for smooth metrics \(g_j\) with total \(Q\)-curvature \(\int_M Q_{g_j}\,\dd V_{g_j}\to+\infty\). In the usual closed-manifold interpretation, dimension four is included. The aid reports that this inclusion is genuinely in the source and refutes the universal positive-infinity assertion in that dimension.

For a closed smooth four-manifold, use the reported AIM normalization
\[
 Q_4=\frac{R^2}{6}-\frac{|\operatorname{Ric}|^2}{2}
               -\frac{\Delta R}{6}.
\]
The Laplacian sign affects its pointwise divergence term but not its integral. With \(|W|^2\) the full tensor squared norm in the corresponding Chern--Gauss--Bonnet convention,
\[
 \mathcal Q(g):=\int_MQ_4(g)\,\dd V_g
       =8\pi^2\chi(M)-\frac14\int_M|W_g|^2\,\dd V_g
       \leq8\pi^2\chi(M).
\]
Thus no sequence on such a manifold has \(\mathcal Q(g_j)\to+\infty\). Changing the Weyl norm to a curvature-operator convention would change the displayed coefficient, so its normalization is material.

The aid also supplies a stronger range statement. Define
\[
 \mathcal W_{\inf}(M)=\inf_g\int_M|W_g|^2\,\dd V_g,\qquad
 S_Q(M)=8\pi^2\chi(M)-\frac14\mathcal W_{\inf}(M),
\]
where the infimum ranges over all smooth Riemannian metrics. It reports that for every background metric \(g_0\) and prescribed coordinate ball \(U\subset M\), there are metrics \(g_j\) equal to \(g_0\) outside \(U\) with
\[
 \mathcal Q(g_j)\longrightarrow-\infty.
\]
The mechanism is compactly supported scale-invariant Weyl-energy bubbles. Together with path-connectedness of the smooth metric space and continuity of \(\mathcal Q\), this gives the exact value set
\[
 \{\mathcal Q(g):g\text{ smooth Riemannian}\}
 =(-\infty,S_Q(M))\quad\text{or}\quad(-\infty,S_Q(M)].
\]
The upper endpoint is present exactly when the infimum Weyl energy is attained. Neither its numerical value nor attainment is asserted for every manifold. This four-dimensional correction refutes the source's unrestricted claim but gives no conclusion here for all higher even dimensions or for boundary problems with additional terms.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The printed positive-infinity claim fails on closed four-manifolds: Gauss--Bonnet bounds total \(Q_4\) above by topology minus Weyl energy. The aid reports local metric modifications making it tend to negative infinity and describes its exact interval of values, with the upper endpoint depending on Weyl-energy attainment.
