\section*{Kirby Problem 2.12}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S_g$ be a closed connected orientable surface of genus $g\geq2$. For a
hyperbolic metric $\rho$ on $S_g$, a systole is an unoriented closed geodesic
of least positive length. Count distinct geometric geodesics, without choices
of starting point or orientation. Define
\[
N(g)=\max_{\rho}\#\{\text{systoles of }(S_g,\rho)\}.
\]
Determine $N(g)$, in particular its optimal asymptotic order as $g$ grows.

The associated topological question asks for
\[
C(g)=\max\{|\mathcal A|: \mathcal A\text{ consists of distinct essential
simple curve classes on }S_g,\ i(a,b)\leq1\}.
\]
Here essential means not null-homotopic; $i(a,b)$ is the smallest number of
transverse intersections among representatives of the two isotopy classes.
Require the inequality for every two different members of $\mathcal A$.
Determine $C(g)$ exactly or asymptotically, with matching upper bounds and
constructions. The curve-set question also makes sense in genus one.
Systoles are simple and any two intersect at most once on a closed hyperbolic
surface, so the first problem is a geometrically restricted case of the second.
No metric is prescribed in maximizing $N(g)$.
\subsection{Short English statement}
What are the largest possible collections of systoles, and of simple curves meeting pairwise at most once, on a genus-$g$ surface?
