\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A subset \(K\) of a real vector space \(V\) is convex if \((1-t)x+ty\in K\) for all \(x,y\in K\) and \(t\in[0,1]\). A real-valued function \(f:D\to\R\) on a convex domain \(D\subseteq V\) is convex if
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)\leq (1-t)f(x)+tf(y)
\qquad(x,y\in D,\ t\in[0,1]).
\]
