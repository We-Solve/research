\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 11000245}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix the ambient genus \(g\) and the source's mapping class group \(\Gamma_g\). For a fixed closed-surface genus \(h\geq2\), let
\[
 \mathscr S_{g,h}=\{H\leq\Gamma_g:
 H\cong\pi_1(\Sigma_h),\quad
 \text{every }1\ne f\in H\text{ is pseudo-Anosov}\}.
\]
The question is whether \(\mathscr S_{g,h}/\Gamma_g\) is finite, where \(\Gamma_g\) acts by conjugation \(H\mapsto\gamma H\gamma^{-1}\). These are conjugacy classes of subgroup images, not of chosen parametrizing embeddings. No uniform bound over varying \(h\), or extra convex-cocompactness condition, is requested.

The source notes that existence from its Question 1.1 is open, so the finite number could be zero. The statement does not presume existence of any purely pseudo-Anosov surface subgroup. Exact ambient surface conventions and whether closed surface groups are the intended surface-subgroup class must be checked against the missing earlier definitions.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For each fixed subgroup genus, are there finitely many ambient conjugacy classes of surface subgroups whose nonidentity elements are all pseudo-Anosov, allowing that there may be none?
