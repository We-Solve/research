\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\textbf{Curated question.} Let \(M\) be a closed smooth manifold with \(\chi(M)=0\), and let \(\alpha\in\Omega^1(M;\R)\) satisfy \(d\alpha\ne0\). Does there exist \(f\in C^\infty(M,\R)\) such that\[\alpha_x+(df)_x\ne0\quad\text{in }T_x^*M\quad\text{for every }x\in M?\] Here closed conventionally means compact without boundary, \(\chi\) is Euler characteristic, and \(d\) is exterior differentiation. The perturbation is by an exact one-form; in particular \(d(\alpha+df)=d\alpha\). The requested conclusion is pointwise nonvanishing of the covector, not closedness of the perturbed form.\par The usual meaning of \(d\alpha\ne0\) is that \(d\alpha\) is not identically zero, so \(\alpha\) is not closed. The stronger requirement \((d\alpha)_x\ne0\) at every point is not stated and must not silently replace it. Connectedness is also omitted: if disconnected manifolds are allowed, \(\chi(M)=0\) only gives the sum of component Euler characteristics, whereas a nowhere-zero one-form requires vanishing Euler characteristic on each closed component.
