\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The complex full flag manifold in $\C^3$ is
\[
F_{12}=\{(L,P):L\subset P\subset\C^3,\ \dim_\C L=1,\ \dim_\C P=2\}.
\]
It has complex dimension three, with its natural complex structure $J$. For a smooth real three-manifold $M$, an immersion $f:M\to F_{12}$ is totally real if
\[
D_xf(T_xM)\cap J D_xf(T_xM)=\{0\}\qquad(x\in M).
\]
Since the real dimension of $M$ equals the complex dimension of the target, these are maximally totally real tangent spaces.

The source asks for the homotopy classification of such immersions. For a fixed $M$, one natural formulation is to determine the path components of the space of totally real immersions: $f_0$ and $f_1$ are equivalent if there is a family $f_t$ through totally real immersions for $0\leq t\leq1$. This is a constrained regular-homotopy question, distinct from asking only which ordinary homotopy classes of maps contain a totally real immersion. The source does not choose between these readings or specify whether reparametrizations of $M$ are identified.

No assumptions on compactness, boundary, orientation, or topology of $M$ are supplied. A complete classification must say whether $M$ is fixed and include any relative boundary conditions. No CR, Lagrangian, or embedding condition is imposed by total reality, and none is added from the related-programme summary.
