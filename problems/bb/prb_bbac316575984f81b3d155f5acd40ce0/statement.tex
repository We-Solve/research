\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A median graph is a connected graph \(X\) such that, for every three vertices \(x,y,z\), the intersection \(I(x,y)\cap I(y,z)\cap I(z,x)\) consists of exactly one vertex, where \(I(u,v)=\{w:d_X(u,v)=d_X(u,w)+d_X(w,v)\}\) and \(d_X\) is the edge-path metric. Actions on graphs are understood to be by graph automorphisms. Following the source's convention, an action \(G\curvearrowright X\) is \emph{proper} if every vertex stabilizer is finite. It is \emph{metrically proper} if, for a base vertex \(o\),
\[
\forall R\geq 0,\qquad \#\{g\in G:d_X(o,go)\leq R\}<\infty.
\]
This condition is independent of the base vertex. No local finiteness assumption on \(X\) is supplied.

Let \(N\trianglelefteq G\), put \(Q=G/N\), and give \(Q\) the left translation action of \(G\). Define
\[
A_Q=\bigoplus_{q\in Q}\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z
=\{f:Q\to\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z:\operatorname{supp}(f)\text{ is finite}\},
\qquad (g\cdot f)(q)=f(g^{-1}q).
\]
The permutational wreath product in question is
\[
W(G,N)=(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)\wr_QG=A_Q\rtimes G,
\qquad (f,g)(h,k)=(f+g\cdot h,gk).
\]
For any group \(H\), write out the following property without assuming any additional restrictions on the median graph:
\[
\text{there exist a median graph }Y\text{ and a metrically proper action }H\curvearrowright Y.
\]
