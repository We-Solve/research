\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $T=\{t_1,t_2,t_3\}\subset\R^2$ be a nondegenerate triangle, meaning its vertices are noncollinear. It is non-equilateral if the three pairwise distances are not all equal. Graham's assertion is
\[
\forall T\text{ non-equilateral}\quad
\exists\chi_T:\R^2\to\{1,2,3\}\quad
\forall\text{ Euclidean isometries }g:\R^2\to\R^2,
\]
\[
\bigl|\{\chi_T(g(t_1)),\chi_T(g(t_2)),\chi_T(g(t_3))\}\bigr|\ge2.
\]
Thus no translated, rotated, or reflected congruent copy of that fixed triangle is monochromatic. Arbitrary rescalings are not congruent copies.
