\textbf{Question 1.2.} Which Legendrian submanifolds $L$ admit generating functions with prescribed behavior at infinity, typically quadratic at infinity, and what changes for other asymptotic conditions? The source also mentions a Giroux criterion without infinity restrictions, explained by Yasha Eliashberg, and a formal infinite-dimensional action-functional construction. These are distinct categories and are not identified with global quadratic-at-infinity existence.

For a precise standard model, assume an ambient jet space $J^1B=T^*B\times\R$ with contact form $\dd z-p\,\dd q$. A finite-dimensional generating family $F:B\times\R^N\to\R$ has $\partial_\eta F$ transverse to zero and generates
\[
 \Sigma_F=\{\partial_\eta F=0\},\qquad
 (q,\eta)\longmapsto(q,\partial_qF(q,\eta),F(q,\eta)).
\]
For an embedded generated $L$, require this map to identify $\Sigma_F$ with $L$. A generating-family germ is defined near its fiber-critical locus. It is different from a globally defined family satisfying an asymptotic model. For compact $B$, one common quadratic-at-infinity convention is $F(q,\eta)=Q(\eta)$ outside a compact subset, with $Q$ a fixed nondegenerate quadratic form; a linear model uses a nonzero linear form. Noncompact bases, linear-quadratic models, equivalences by fiber diffeomorphism and stabilization, and fiberwise tameness require separate conventions. The source does not fix them or its ambient jet-space hypotheses.

\textbf{Supplied obstruction-level example.} In standard contact $\R^3=J^1\R$, let $U$ be the maximal Legendrian unknot with Thurston--Bennequin number $\operatorname{tb}(U)=-1$ and rotation number $\operatorname{rot}(U)=0$. Let $S_+$ and $S_-$ be positive and negative stabilizations. The aid asserts
\[
 \operatorname{tb}(S_\pm U)=-2,\quad
 \operatorname{rot}(S_\pm U)=\pm1,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{tb}(S_+S_-U)=-3,\quad
 \operatorname{rot}(S_+S_-U)=0.
\]
Under the stated Giroux--Latour semi-local stable-Lagrangian-Gauss-map criterion, the single stabilizations have no generating-family germ, while the opposite double stabilization has such a germ. Nevertheless the latter has no global linear-at-infinity generating family in the aid's convention, because its unknot Thurston--Bennequin number is not maximal. Thus vanishing of the germ obstruction does not imply global asymptotically controlled existence. This does not identify linear-at-infinity with quadratic-at-infinity, nor make rotation the only obstruction in every ambient space or dimension.
