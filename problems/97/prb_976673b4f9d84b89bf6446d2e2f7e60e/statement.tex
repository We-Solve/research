\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source asks whether function spaces can provide martingale decompositions and concatenations for a class of hyperbolic maps. It leaves the class, measures, function spaces and meaning of concatenation unspecified. The attached aid treats a finite-state, time-inhomogeneous Markov coding as a restricted interpretation.

Let \(X_n\) be a Markov chain on a finite set \(E\), with transition kernels \(P_n\), laws \(\mu_{n+1}=\mu_nP_n\), and observables \(f_n:E\to\R\) satisfying \(\mu_nf_n=0\) and \(\sup_n\|f_n\|_\infty<\infty\). Suppose the Dobrushin coefficients obey
\[
 \sup_n\frac12\max_{x,x'\in E}\sum_{y\in E}|P_n(x,y)-P_n(x',y)|\le\rho<1.
\]
On functions modulo constants this contracts the oscillation seminorm. The aid reports a uniformly bounded future Poisson corrector
\[
 h_n=\sum_{j=n}^{\infty}P_n\cdots P_{j-1}f_j,
 \qquad h_n=f_n+P_nh_{n+1},
\]
where the empty product is the identity. With \(\mathcal F_n=\sigma(X_0,\ldots,X_n)\), the increments
\[
 D_{n+1}=h_{n+1}(X_{n+1})-(P_nh_{n+1})(X_n)
\]
have conditional mean zero and give the exact forward decomposition
\[
 \sum_{n=0}^{N-1}f_n(X_n)
 =\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}D_{n+1}+h_0(X_0)-h_N(X_N).
\]
The endpoint is uniformly bounded, and truncating the future series gives an exponentially small lookahead error under the uniform contraction assumption.

For the explicit binary model \(E=\{-1,1\}\), take
\[
 P_n(x,y)=\frac{1+a_nxy}{2},\qquad |a_n|\le\rho<1,\qquad f_n(x)=b_nx,\quad |b_n|\le B,
\]
with \(X_0\) uniform, so all marginal laws remain uniform. Then the reported formulas specialize to
\[
 H_n=\sum_{j=n}^{\infty}b_j\prod_{k=n}^{j-1}a_k,\quad
 h_n(x)=H_nx,\quad
 D_{n+1}=H_{n+1}(X_{n+1}-a_nX_n).
\]
In particular \(|H_n|\le B/(1-\rho)\), and the truncation at \(j=n+K\) has error at most \(B\rho^{K+1}/(1-\rho)\). The predictable variance is deterministic:
\[
 V_N=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\E(D_{n+1}^2\mid\mathcal F_n)
     =\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}H_{n+1}^2(1-a_n^2).
\]
The aid reports that \(V_N\to\infty\) gives a standard Gaussian limit for \(V_N^{-1/2}\sum_{n<N}b_nX_n\), using the bounded martingale increments and bounded endpoint. Loss of contraction at the boundary \(a_n=1\) can invalidate the bounded-corrector argument; no conclusion for that boundary is inferred from the contracting case.

The aid also reports a moving-conjugate two-leg horseshoe realization as a sequential uniformly hyperbolic example. The supplied summary gives neither the conjugacies nor the invariant sets or coding measure. Consequently the symbolic formulas above are precise conditional statements; they are not a construction of that realization or a theorem for arbitrary concatenated hyperbolic maps or their physical measures.
