In the usual unit-disc setting, let \(\mathbb D=\{z\in\C:|z|<1\}\), let \(\mathcal X\) be the intended space of analytic functions, and let \(E\subset\mathbb D\). A uniqueness set for \(\mathcal X\) means that \(f\in\mathcal X\) and \(f|_E=0\) imply \(f\equiv0\). Given a radial minorant function \(M\), the quantitative property suggested by the cleaned statement is
\[

 \bigl[\log|f(z)|\leq-M(|z|)\text{ for every }z\in E\bigr]
