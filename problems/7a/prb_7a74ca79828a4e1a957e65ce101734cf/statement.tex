\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S\) be a topological space, and let \(\mathcal B(S)\) denote the sigma-algebra generated by its open sets. Let \(C(S,\R)\) be the real vector space of continuous real-valued functions on \(S\). Fix a finite-dimensional real vector subspace \(V\subseteq C(S,\R)\) and a real-linear functional \(L\in V^*=\operatorname{Hom}_{\R}(V,\R)\).

The problem asks for necessary and sufficient conditions on \((S,V,L)\) for the existence of a representing Borel measure. Under the usual convention that a measure is nonnegative and countably additive, this means finding conditions equivalent to the existence of a measure
\[
\mu:\mathcal B(S)\longrightarrow[0,\infty]

\[
\forall f\in V,\qquad \int_S |f|\,\mathrm d\mu<\infty
\quad\text{and}\quad
L(f)=\int_S f\,\mathrm d\mu.

The integrability requirement makes explicit that each displayed integral represents the finite real number \(L(f)\).
