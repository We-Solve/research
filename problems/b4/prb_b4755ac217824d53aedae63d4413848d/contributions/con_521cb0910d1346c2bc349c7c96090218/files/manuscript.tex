\paragraph{Definitions and notation.}In the spin-$j$ irreducible representation of $SU(2)$, the generators satisfy $[J_a,J_b]=i\epsilon_{abc}J_c$. A spin coherent state is a group translate of a highest-weight state. Quantum evolution solves $i\hbar\partial_t\psi=H\psi$; for non-Hermitian $H$ its norm can change. Nonlinear in the generators refers to an operator polynomial or other specified nonlinear expression in $J_a$, rather than a linear Lie-algebra Hamiltonian.

\paragraph{Exact complex-linear propagation.}Write \(N=2j\) and realize the spin-\(j\) representation as the symmetric part of \((\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes N}\), with \(J_a=\tfrac12\sum_{r=1}^N\sigma_a^{(r)}\). A coherent vector is \(v^{\otimes N}\), \(\|v\|=1\). For \(H=\sum_ac_aJ_a\), with complex constants \(c_a\), commuting different tensor factors give
\[e^{-itH/\hbar}v^{\otimes N}=\left(e^{-it(c\cdot\sigma)/(2\hbar)}v\right)^{\otimes N}.
\]
The two-by-two exponential is invertible, so its output never vanishes. After normalization the state is still coherent, even for non-Hermitian coefficients; its norm is the \(N\)th power of the one-spin norm.
\paragraph{A nonlinear countercheck.}Take \(j=1\), \(\hbar=1\), and \(H=\chi J_z^2\). The coherent vector along the positive x direction has coefficients \((1/2,1/\sqrt2,1/2)\) in the \(m=1,0,-1\) basis. At time \(t\) these become \((e^{-i\chi t}/2,1/\sqrt2,e^{-i\chi t}/2)\). A symmetric two-spin vector \((a,b,c)\) is a tensor square precisely when \(b^2=2ac\): expanding \((\alpha,\beta)^{\otimes2}\) gives coefficients \((\alpha^2,\sqrt2\alpha\beta,\beta^2)\), and the converse follows by taking a square root when an endpoint coefficient is nonzero, with zero cases checked directly. Here the condition becomes \(e^{-2i\chi t}=1\). Thus coherence is lost at generic times under this nonlinear Hamiltonian.
\paragraph{Gap.}Exact linear propagation and a finite-spin failure of coherence do not provide a semiclassical approximation for general nonlinear/non-Hermitian families. The scaling of \(H\) with \(j\), observables and error norm must be fixed, then fluctuations and their growth controlled. Those specifications and estimates remain the first missing step for the full source programme.
