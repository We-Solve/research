\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathcal A_n(\varnothing;\Q)\) be the rational space of closed vertex-oriented trivalent Jacobi diagrams with \(2n\) vertices modulo antisymmetry and IHX, with multiplication \(\sqcup\) given by disjoint union. Write
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal A}(\varnothing;\Q)
   =\prod_{n\geq0}\mathcal A_n(\varnothing;\Q),\qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal A}(\varnothing;\Q)_{\mathrm{conn}}
   =\prod_{n\geq1}\mathcal A_n(\varnothing;\Q)_{\mathrm{conn}},
\]
where the connected subspace is spanned by diagrams with connected graph. For a group-like LMO invariant normalized to have degree-zero term \(1\), its logarithm is the formal degree-completed series
\[
 \log Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M)
 =\sum_{r\geq1}\frac{(-1)^{r+1}}r
       \bigl(Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M)-1\bigr)^{\sqcup r},
\]
which lies in the completed connected subspace.

An integral, respectively rational, homology \(3\)-sphere has the homology of \(S^3\) over \(\Z\), respectively \(\Q\). The problem is to characterize the two images
\[
 \left\{\log Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M):
          M\text{ an integral homology }3\text{-sphere}\right\},\qquad
 \left\{\log Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M):
          M\text{ a rational homology }3\text{-sphere}\right\}
\]
inside \(\widehat{\mathcal A}(\varnothing)_{\mathrm{conn}}\): give necessary and sufficient conditions for a connected diagram series to occur in each case. The excerpt does not explicitly fix coefficients, orientation/equivalence conventions, or the normalization of \(Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}\); the rational, unit-normalized interpretation above specifies the usual formal logarithm and must be checked against the source convention, especially for rational homology spheres.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine exactly which completed series of connected Jacobi diagrams occur as logarithms of LMO invariants of integral homology spheres, and which occur for rational homology spheres.
