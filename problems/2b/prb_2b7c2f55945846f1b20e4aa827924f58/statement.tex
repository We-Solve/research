Let $N=\prod_{j\leq k}p_j$, and list its $\phi(N)$ reduced residues
in $[1,N)$ in increasing order. The question asks for a continuous
limiting distribution of successive gaps divided by $N/\phi(N)$.
Hooley proved that the limit is $1-e^{-c}$ at $c\geq0$.
Here we derive exact finite formulas, tightness, and disappearance
of bounded unscaled gaps. The exponential limit is unresolved
in this attempt.
