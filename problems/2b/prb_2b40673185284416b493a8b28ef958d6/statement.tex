c_0(\mathbb K)=\left\{(a_n)_{n\geq1}\in\mathbb K^{\N}:\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n=0\right\},\qquad

A Banach space \(Y\) is Lipschitz isomorphic to a subset of \(X\) if there exist a map \(f_Y:Y\to X\) and constants \(0<a_Y\leq b_Y<\infty\) such that
\[
a_Y\|u-v\|_Y\leq\|f_Y(u)-f_Y(v)\|_X\leq b_Y\|u-v\|_Y
\qquad(u,v\in Y).
\]
These inequalities make \(f_Y\) a bijection onto its image with Lipschitz inverse. The map and constants may depend on \(Y\).

\qquad(z\in c_0(\mathbb K))?
\]
