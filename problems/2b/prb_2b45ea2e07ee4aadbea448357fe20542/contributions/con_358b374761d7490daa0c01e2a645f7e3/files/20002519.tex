\paragraph{An invariant transverse-Jacobi criterion.}Along a chosen null geodesic with affine parameter \(s\), suppose the screen Jacobi equation is \(A''(s)+\mathcal R(s)A(s)=0\), where \(A\) is a real \(2\times2\) matrix, \(A(0)=0\), \(A'(0)=I\), and \(\mathcal R\) is a continuous symmetric optical tidal matrix in a parallel orthonormal screen frame. A nonzero initial screen vector \(v\) produces Jacobi field \(A(s)v\); consequently a conjugate point at \(s>0\) is exactly \(\det A(s)=0\). Orthogonal changes of endpoint screen frames multiply \(A\) on the left and right by invertible matrices, preserving the zero set of the determinant.
\paragraph{Exact curvature tests.}For \(\mathcal R=0\), \(A=sI\), with no positive conjugate point. For \(\mathcal R=\kappa I\), \(\kappa>0\), \(A=\sin(\sqrt\kappa s)I/\sqrt\kappa\), and the first is \(\pi/\sqrt\kappa\). For negative \(\kappa\), the hyperbolic-sine solution has none. Wherever \(A\) is invertible, \(B=A'A^{-1}\) satisfies \(B'+B^2+\mathcal R=0\), by direct differentiation. Its trace is the logarithmic derivative \((\det A)'/\det A\), linking area expansion to the caustic criterion.
