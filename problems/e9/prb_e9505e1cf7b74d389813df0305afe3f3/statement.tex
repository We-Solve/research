\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $n\ge4$ be even, let $[g]$ be a smooth Riemannian conformal structure, and write
\[
 J=\frac{R}{2(n-1)},\qquad
 P_{ij}=\frac{\operatorname{Ric}_{ij}-Jg_{ij}}{n-2}.
\]
Here $P$ is the Schouten tensor, distinct from the GJMS operator $P_n$. Let $\mathcal E[w]$ denote conformal densities of weight $w$, and use the nonnegative Laplacian $\Delta=-\operatorname{div}\operatorname{grad}$. A linear natural operator $A:\mathcal E[0]\to\mathcal E[-n]$ is conformally invariant when
\[
 A_{e^{2\omega}g}f=e^{-n\omega}A_gf.
\]
Its normalized principal symbol is that of $\Delta^{n/2}$.

\textbf{Problem 11.} Is the critical GJMS operator $P_n$ uniquely characterized by that principal part, conformal invariance, and coefficients expressible purely using the Schouten jets $\nabla^\ell P$, $\ell\ge0$, together with metric contractions? \textbf{Supplied dimension-four result.} In the narrower class of parity-even polynomial homogeneous natural operators built by complete contractions of Schouten jets, the aid asserts uniqueness in dimension four without a self-adjointness assumption. With $\delta=d^*$, the resulting operator is
\[
 P_4=\Delta^2+\delta\bigl((2Jg-4P)\,\dd\bigr)

\]
This asserted coefficient audit is a supplied partial result, not a proof of uniqueness in all even dimensions or in every possible nonpolynomial/orientation-sensitive naturality class.

\textbf{Two-obstruction reduction in general even dimension.} For a competitor $A$, put $D=A-P_n$. Cancellation of principal symbols makes $D$ lower order. Since $P_n(1)=0$, define
\[
 s_g=D_g(1)=A_g(1),\qquad D_0f=Df-s_gf.
\]
The scalar $s$ is a natural conformal invariant of weight $-n$, and $D_0$ is a conformally invariant lower-order operator annihilating constants. Locally it factors through exact one-form jets:
\[
 Df=s_gf+B_g(\dd f),
\]
where $B$ is defined on jets of exact one-forms. The decomposition into $s$ and $D_0$ is canonical for a fixed $D$; extending $B$ to an operator on all one-forms need not be canonical. Thus absence of Schouten-jet scalar conformal invariants removes only the multiplication anomaly $s$. A residual operator on exact one-form jets must also be ruled out to establish uniqueness. Any Schouten-only claim for this residual must additionally check the allowed coefficient identities for the critical GJMS operator itself.
