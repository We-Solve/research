\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an odd prime power \(p\equiv 1\pmod 4\), the Paley graph \(G_p\) has vertex set \(\F_p\); distinct vertices \(u,v\) are adjacent when \(u-v\) is a nonzero square in \(\F_p\). Its clique number \(\omega(G_p)\) is the maximum cardinality of a set of pairwise adjacent vertices. The supplied record does not specify whether its parameter \(p\) ranges over primes or over prime powers, or whether further restrictions apply.

One standard interpretation of the degree-\(4\) Sum-of-Squares upper bound for the clique number of a finite graph \(G=(V,E)\) is the following. Introduce real variables \((x_v)_{v\in V}\), and let \(\R[x]_{\leq d}\) denote the real polynomials of total degree at most \(d\). Define
\[
S_4(G)=\sup_L\sum_{v\in V}L(x_v),
\]
where \(L:\R[x]_{\leq4}\to\R\) is linear and satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
L(1)&=1,\\
L(f^2)&\geq0 &&\text{for every }f\in\R[x]_{\leq2},\\
L\bigl((x_v^2-x_v)r\bigr)&=0
&&\text{for every }v\in V,\ r\in\R[x]_{\leq2},\\
L(x_ux_vr)&=0
&&\text{for every distinct }u,v\text{ with }\{u,v\}\notin E,
\ r\in\R[x]_{\leq2}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is an explicit candidate convention for the relaxation. The supplied record refers instead to the precise construction in reference \texttt{KY-2022-PaleyDegree4SOS}; its identification with the convention above has not been established from the supplied evidence.

Let \(\mathcal P\) denote the intended unbounded set of admissible Paley orders. Subject to resolving both the domain \(\mathcal P\) and the intended relaxation, the conjecture attributed to Bandeira et al., Conjecture 4, is
\[
\exists\varepsilon>0\ \exists C>0\ \exists p_0>0\ 
\forall p\in\mathcal P:\qquad
p\geq p_0\ \Longrightarrow\ S_4(G_p)\leq C p^{1/2-\varepsilon}.

The supplied background describes numerical evidence suggesting a polynomial improvement over the \(\sqrt p\) scale and reports that the authors of \texttt{KY-2022-PaleyDegree4SOS} prove that the relaxation cannot improve beyond an \(\Omega(p^{1/3})\) scale. That reported lower bound, its parameter domain, and its precise hypotheses have not been independently verified. The registry labels the conjecture open; this is a supplied status label.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The conjecture asks whether the degree-\(4\) Sum-of-Squares upper bound for the clique number of the Paley graph is at most \(C p^{1/2-\varepsilon}\), for some fixed \(\varepsilon>0\) and all sufficiently large admissible \(p\). The intended range of \(p\) and the precise relaxation require confirmation. The supplied record reports numerical support and a lower-bound obstruction at the \(p^{1/3}\) scale.
