\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a group with identity \(e\), equipped with a topology \(\tau\) such that multiplication \(G\times G\to G\), with the product topology on \(G\times G\), and inversion \(G\to G\) are continuous. A subgroup \(H\leq G\) is closed if \(G\setminus H\in\tau\), and proper if \(H\ne G\).

Describe the topological groups \((G,\tau)\) satisfying
\[
\forall H\leq G,\qquad H\text{ is closed in }G\ \Longrightarrow\ H=G.
\]
The quantifier includes the identity subgroup \(\{e\}\); the source does not restrict the condition to nontrivial subgroups or exclude the trivial group.

The continuity conditions above state the common underlying structure but do not resolve that convention. The intended separation hypotheses must therefore be checked before this classification request is regarded as fully specified.
