\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Give every subset of \(\R\) its subspace topology. A countable open cover of \(X\) is a countable family of subsets open in \(X\) whose union is \(X\). A \(\gamma\)-cover of \(X\) is an infinite open cover such that each point of \(X\) belongs to all but finitely many members. Write \(\mathcal O\) for the family of countable open covers of \(X\), and \(\Gamma\) for the family of countable \(\gamma\)-covers of \(X\). The selection property \(\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Gamma)\), also called the Hurewicz property, means that for every sequence \((\mathcal U_n)_{n\in\N}\) of members of \(\mathcal O\), none of which has a finite subcover, there are finite subfamilies \(\mathcal V_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n\) such that
\[
\left\{\bigcup\mathcal V_n:n\in\N\right\}\in\Gamma.
\]
A set \(F\subseteq\R\) is \(\sigma\)-compact if \(F=\bigcup_{n\in\N}K_n\) for compact sets \(K_n\subseteq\R\). A set \(B\subseteq\R\) is Borel if it belongs to the \(\sigma\)-algebra generated by the open subsets of \(\R\). It is \(F_{\sigma\delta}\) if
\[
B=\bigcap_{m\in\N}\bigcup_{n\in\N}C_{m,n}
\]
for closed sets \(C_{m,n}\subseteq\R\).

Under the non-strict inclusion reading of the source notation, the first question is whether
\[
\begin{gathered}
\forall B\subseteq\R\ \forall X\subseteq B,\\
\bigl[B\text{ is Borel and }X\text{ satisfies }\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Gamma)\bigr]\\
\Longrightarrow\ \exists F\subseteq\R\,
\bigl[F\text{ is }\sigma\text{-compact and }X\subseteq F\subseteq B\bigr].
\end{gathered}
\]
The second question asks whether the same assertion holds when the hypothesis that \(B\) is Borel is replaced by the stronger hypothesis that \(B\) is \(F_{\sigma\delta}\).

The source writes \(X\subset F\subset B\) in its conclusion without specifying whether either containment must be proper; the displayed formulation uses non-strict containment and leaves that notational qualification for review. \subsection{Short English statement}
Must every Hurewicz subset \(X\) of a Borel set \(B\subseteq\R\) be contained in a \(\sigma\)-compact set lying inside \(B\)? The source asks the same question when \(B\) is \(F_{\sigma\delta}\). Properness of the intermediate containments is not specified.
