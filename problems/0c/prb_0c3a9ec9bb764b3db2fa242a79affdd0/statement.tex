\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A shrinking Ricci soliton, in the vector-field convention, is a Riemannian manifold \((M,g)\) equipped with a vector field \(V\) and a constant \(\lambda>0\) satisfying
\[
\operatorname{Ric}_g+\tfrac12\mathcal L_Vg=\lambda g,
\]
where \(\operatorname{Ric}_g\) is the Ricci tensor and \(\mathcal L_Vg\) is the Lie derivative of the metric. In the gradient case, \(V=\nabla_g f\) for a smooth function \(f\), and the equation becomes \(\operatorname{Ric}_g+\nabla_g^2f=\lambda g\).
