\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A spherical cone metric on the topological sphere \(S^2\) is a metric of Gaussian curvature \(+1\) away from finitely many marked points, with a conical singularity of total angle \(\alpha_i>0\) at the \(i\)-th marked point. Let \(k\) be the fixed number of such points, and set
\[
A=2\pi(2-k)+\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_i.
\]
In this curvature normalization, \(A\) is the area given by the cone version of Gauss--Bonnet.
