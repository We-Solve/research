In the usual terminology, a \emph{Countryman line} is a linear order \(C\) of cardinality \(\aleph_1\) whose square \(C\times C\), ordered coordinatewise, is a union of countably many chains. Thus there are chains \(K_n\subseteq C\times C\), for \(n\in\N\), with

 C\times C=\bigcup_{n\in\N}K_n,
 \qquad
 (c_0,c_1)\leq(d_0,d_1)
