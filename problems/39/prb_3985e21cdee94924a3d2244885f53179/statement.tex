\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an integer \(r\geq 0\), let
\[
\pi\colon X_r=\operatorname{Bl}_{p_1,\ldots,p_r}(\PP^2)\longrightarrow\PP^2

The supplied \emph{\((-1)\)-curve conjecture} asks whether, for every integer \(r\geq0\), every such configuration of points, and every irreducible reduced curve \(C\subset X_r\),

\subsection{Short English statement}
The conjecture asks whether every irreducible reduced curve of negative self-intersection on a plane blown up at \(r\geq0\) general points must be a smooth rational \((-1)\)-curve. The supplied assessment instead uses very general points; that distinction and the unspecified ground field remain unresolved.
