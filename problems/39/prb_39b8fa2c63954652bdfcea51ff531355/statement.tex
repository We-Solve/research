\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

Set \(P=\prod_{j=1}^{n}X_j\), with all products taken over \(k\). For each \(i\), let

\iota_i:\prod_{j\ne i}X_j\longrightarrow P

be the morphism inserting \(x_i\) in the \(i\)-th coordinate. An empty product here means \(\operatorname{Spec}(k)\). For a \(k\)-scheme \(T\), write \(1_T:T\to U\) for the composite of its structure morphism with the identity section of \(U\).

The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every such collection of data,

 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad

The face restrictions and conclusion are equalities of morphisms of \(k\)-schemes, not merely equalities on \(k\)-rational points. This is a provisional formulation: the missing definition of \(\delta(D)\) prevents a fully specified mathematical assertion.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The conjecture says that a morphism from a product of pointed open subsets of \(\mathbb P^1_k\) to a wound unipotent group must be constantly the identity if it is the identity on every chosen coordinate face and the number of factors exceeds the degree of primitivity of the boundary. The supplied record leaves that boundary invariant undefined, so the numerical hypothesis requires clarification.
