\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30003019}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(C/\Q\) be a smooth, proper, geometrically connected curve. Write \(C(\Q)\) for its set of \(\Q\)-rational points and define its genus by
\[
g(C)=\dim_{\Q}H^0(C,\Omega^1_{C/\Q}),
\]
where \(\Omega^1_{C/\Q}\) is the sheaf of regular differential forms.

The Uniform Mordell Conjecture in the supplied record asks whether
\[
\forall g\in\Z_{\geq 2}\;\exists N(g)\in\Z_{\geq 0}\;\forall C/\Q,\qquad
\bigl(C\text{ is smooth, proper, geometrically connected and }g(C)=g\bigr)
\Longrightarrow \#C(\Q)\leq N(g).
\]
The bound must depend only on \(g\), uniformly over all such curves over \(\Q\); no bound on the Mordell--Weil rank of their Jacobians is assumed. Taking the bound to be a nonnegative integer is equivalent to asking for a finite numerical bound.

The supplied curation labels the problem partially solved while describing this precise genus-only assertion as unresolved. It attributes finiteness to Faltings and strong uniform bounds involving genus, field degree, and Mordell--Weil rank to Vojta, Dimitrov--Gao--Habegger, and K\"uhne, and mentions explicit 2026 constants. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified conclusions of this draft. Their precise theorem hypotheses and parameter dependence are not given in the record.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For each integer genus \(g\geq 2\), is there a finite bound depending only on \(g\) for the number of rational points on every smooth, proper, geometrically connected curve of genus \(g\) over \(\Q\)? The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and the stated genus-only bound unresolved; these labels require independent review.
