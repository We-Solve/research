\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A correlated equilibrium uses a joint recommendation distribution for which following recommendations is optimal against every allowed unilateral deviation. Quantum equilibria use shared quantum states and local operations. A semidefinite hierarchy imposes progressively longer operator-moment positivity constraints.

A semidefinite program optimizes a linear functional of a symmetric matrix subject to affine constraints and positive semidefiniteness. The condition $X\succeq0$ means $v^TXv\geq0$ for every real vector $v$.

An $n$-qubit pure state is a unit vector in $(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes n}$, considered up to global phase. A quantum algorithm applies permitted quantum operations and measurements; complexity depends on the gate and input-access model.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Construct a convergent semidefinite-programming hierarchy for maximizing social welfare over quantum correlated equilibria by replacing the explicit tensor-product constraints with suitable commutation constraints. \sref{30006571}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
The equilibrium definition and finite-dimensional versus commuting-operator model are source-dependent. Replacing tensor-product structure by commutation is the requested step; equivalence of those models is not assumed.
