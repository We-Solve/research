\textbf{Interface-limit question.} Let \(\Omega\subset\R^N\), \(N\ge3\), be a domain with one hole. For sufficiently large \(p\), does \(-\Delta u=u^p\) admit solutions that converge away from a suitable \((N-1)\)-dimensional manifold \(M\) to a harmonic function and tend to \(1\) on \(M\)? Since convergence concerns varying exponents, the intended unknown is a family \(u_p\), not a single solution at one fixed \(p\).
