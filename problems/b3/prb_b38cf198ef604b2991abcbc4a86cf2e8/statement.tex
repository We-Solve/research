\section{Reliable Model Reduction for Nonlinear Parameterized Systems}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


A state-space system evolves as $\dot x=f(x,u)$ with output $y=h(x,u)$. Model reduction seeks a lower-dimensional system preserving specified input-output properties. Observability distinguishes initial states from their output trajectories, while controllability concerns reachable states under allowed inputs.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Develop mathematically justified model-reduction methods for nonlinear, parameterized, coupled, and large-scale systems, including error bounds and reliable rules for choosing Krylov/Laguerre design parameters so that the reduced model gives a good approximation to the original system's response for arbitrary input signals while preserving relevant structure such as stability or passivity. \sref{statement}{2}
