For terminology only, let \(F\) be a nonarchimedean local field of residue characteristic \(p\), and let \(G\) be a connected reductive algebraic group over \(F\). Under the usual complex-coefficient convention, a representation \((\pi,V)\) of \(G(F)\) is smooth if every vector has an open stabilizer. An irreducible smooth representation \(\pi\) is supercuspidal if it is not a subquotient of any normalized parabolically induced representation \(\operatorname{Ind}_{P(F)}^{G(F)}\sigma\), where \(P\) is a proper \(F\)-parabolic subgroup with Levi factor \(M\), and \(\sigma\) is an irreducible smooth complex representation of \(M(F)\).
