For a transcendental entire function $f(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_nz^n$, put
\[
 \mu(r)=\max_{n\ge0}|a_n|r^n,\qquad
 M(r)=\max_{|z|=r}|f(z)|.
\]
The question asks whether existence of $\lim_{r\to\infty}\mu(r)/M(r)$ forces that limit to be zero. The current problem page records a negative answer by Clunie and Hayman, with every limit in $[0,1/2]$ achievable. This attempt establishes necessary restrictions and the zero-limit example, without reconstructing their positive-limit construction.
