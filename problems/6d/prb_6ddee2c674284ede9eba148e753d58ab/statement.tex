The phrase ``3-critical 3-uniform hypergraph'' has two relevant meanings. Under transversal criticality, $\tau(H)=3$ and $\tau(H-e)=2$ for every edge; under chromatic criticality, $\chi(H)=3$ and deleting any edge or vertex permits a proper two-colouring. The minimum-degree requirement is $\delta(H)\ge7$. Li's resolution gives a negative answer to the first interpretation and a positive answer to the second.
