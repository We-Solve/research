\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A four-dimensional Ricci flow is a family of Riemannian metrics \(g(t)\) on a smooth manifold \(M\) with \(\dim M=4\), satisfying
\[
\frac{\partial g}{\partial t}=-2\operatorname{Ric}_{g(t)}.
\]
An ancient flow is one defined for all sufficiently negative times. Parabolic scaling by \(c>0\) replaces \(g(t)\) by \(c\,g(t/c)\), on the correspondingly rescaled time interval. Write \(S^n\) for the \(n\)-sphere, \(\mathbb{CP}^2\) for the complex projective plane, \(O(3)\) for the orthogonal group of \(\R^3\), and \(\Z_2=\Z/2\Z\).
