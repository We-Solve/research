Let \(G\) be a finite cyclic group, written additively, and put \(n=|G|\). Let \(\C^G\) be the space of complex-valued functions on \(G\), and let \(\widehat G\) be its character group. For a function \(f\) on a finite set \(S\), define
\[
\operatorname{supp}(f)=\{s\in S:f(s)\ne0\},\qquad \|f\|_0=|\operatorname{supp}(f)|.
\]
Using standard unnormalized conventions, define the Fourier transform and the short-time Fourier transform with window \(\varphi\in\C^G\) by
\[
\widehat x(\chi)=\sum_{t\in G}x(t)\overline{\chi(t)},\qquad \chi\in\widehat G,

\[
V_\varphi x(a,\chi)=\sum_{t\in G}x(t)\overline{\varphi(t-a)}\,\overline{\chi(t)},\qquad (a,\chi)\in G\times\widehat G.
\]
Thus \(\|V_\varphi x\|_0\) counts nonzero coefficients among the \(n^2\) time-frequency positions. These conventions supply definitions omitted from the record; nonzero normalization factors do not change support sizes.
