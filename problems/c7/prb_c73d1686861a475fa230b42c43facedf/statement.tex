\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a full-rank lattice \(L\subset\R^n\) of covolume one, use the Epstein-zeta convention supplied in companion record 20001945:
\[
E(L,s)=\sum_{0\ne x\in L}|x|^{-2s}\qquad(\operatorname{Re}s>n/2),
\]
with its meromorphic continuation elsewhere. There is a pole at \(s=n/2\), not a zero. The genuine first item of this concatenated record asks
\[
\forall n\geq1\ \exists L\subset\R^n\text{ of covolume one}\quad
E(L,s)\ne0\quad\forall s\in(0,\infty)\setminus\{n/2\}.
\]
The source notes that if a fixed lattice \(L'\) locally minimizes \(E(L,s)\), among covolume-one lattices, for every positive regular parameter \(s\), then it has no odd-order positive-real zero. This does not exclude even-order zeros or prove the stated existence question.

Let \(L^*=\{y:x\cdot y\in\Z\text{ for all }x\in L\}\), \(\theta_K(t)=\sum_{x\in K}e^{-\pi t|x|^2}\), and \(\lambda_1(K)=\min_{x\ne0}|x|\). The supplied aid reports a sufficient certificate: for both \(K=L,L^*\),
\[
\theta_K(t)-1\leq t^{-n/2}\qquad(t>1),
\]
with strictness on a positive-measure set for at least one side. It yields negative \(E(L,s)\) for \(0<s<n/2\); the convergent series is positive for \(s>n/2\), giving zero-freeness. A reported sufficient one-temperature test, imposed for both \(K=L,L^*\), is
\[
\lambda_1(K)^2\geq\frac n{2\pi},\qquad\theta_K(1)<2.
\]
The aid reports a rigorous modular-form tail estimate verifying the certificate for the Leech lattice, and exact factorizations giving examples in dimensions 1, 2, 4 and 8. The estimates and factorizations are not displayed in this record. The every-dimension existence question remains unresolved in the aid.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every dimension contain a lattice whose meromorphically continued Epstein zeta function has no positive-real zeros? The supplied aid gives a dual theta-tail certificate and examples in selected dimensions. The record also concatenates several distinct vertex-algebra and lattice questions, whose missing definitions remain explicit.
