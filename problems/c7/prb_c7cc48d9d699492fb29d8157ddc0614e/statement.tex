\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 800714}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\) be any finite field, and let \(X\) be a geometrically connected, smooth, projective surface over \(k\). Here geometrically connected means that \(X\times_k\overline{k}\) is connected, where \(\overline{k}\) is an algebraic closure of \(k\). Write \(\operatorname{Br}(X)=H^2_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\mathbb G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}}\) for its cohomological Brauer group, with \(\mathbb G_m\) the multiplicative group scheme. Interpret the source notation as the \(2\)-primary torsion subgroup
\[
B=\operatorname{Br}(X)(2)=\{\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}(X):2^n\alpha=0\text{ for some integer }n\geq 0\}.
\]
The question is whether, for every such \(k\) and \(X\), there exists a biadditive pairing
\[
b:B\times B\longrightarrow\Q/\Z
\]
that is alternating and non-degenerate. Explicitly, biadditivity requires
\[
\begin{aligned}
b(\alpha+\alpha',\beta)&=b(\alpha,\beta)+b(\alpha',\beta),\\
b(\alpha,\beta+\beta')&=b(\alpha,\beta)+b(\alpha,\beta')
\end{aligned}
\qquad(\alpha,\alpha',\beta,\beta'\in B),
\]
alternation requires \(b(\alpha,\alpha)=0\) for every \(\alpha\in B\), and non-degeneracy is interpreted here as
\[
\begin{aligned}
\{\alpha\in B:b(\alpha,\beta)=0\text{ for every }\beta\in B\}&=\{0\},\\
\{\beta\in B:b(\alpha,\beta)=0\text{ for every }\alpha\in B\}&=\{0\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
No restriction on the characteristic of \(k\) and no finiteness assumption on \(B\) appear in the supplied statement. The source record does not specify whether non-degeneracy is intended to require the stronger condition that the induced homomorphism \(B\to\operatorname{Hom}(B,\Q/\Z)\) be an isomorphism; this distinction remains unresolved if \(B\) is infinite.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the \(2\)-primary torsion in the Brauer group of every geometrically connected, smooth, projective surface over a finite field admit an alternating biadditive pairing into \(\Q/\Z\) with zero left and right radicals? The supplied record labels this question open; that status is not independently verified.
