\paragraph{Definitions and notation.}Let $a$ be the analytic symbol on a two-dimensional phase space specified in the source, $h>0$ the semiclassical parameter and $a^w(x,hD)$ its Weyl quantization. For a self-adjoint one-degree-of-freedom Hamiltonian, the classical action around an energy curve underlies Bohr--Sommerfeld quantization, with a Maslov correction. For a complex symbol the corresponding cycles and actions may be complex.
