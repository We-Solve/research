\section{Strong Continuity Without Ultraviolet Regularization}\label{30006190}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30006190 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


Ultraviolet regularization smears the continuum fermionic interaction by Gaussian functions of width $\sigma$. The CAR algebra $\mathcal A$ is generated by the canonical anticommutation-relation observables. In this source, strong continuity of a $C^*$-dynamical system means pointwise operator-norm continuity: $\lim_{t\to0}\|\tau_t(A)-A\|=0$ for every $A\in\mathcal A$. This is stronger than continuity in the strong operator topology of a Hilbert-space representation. Removing the cutoff takes $\sigma\to0$.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Does strong continuity of the many-body dynamics persist when the ultraviolet regularization is removed, that is, in the limit $\sigma\to 0$?

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}Strong continuity at fixed cutoff does not automatically pass to a cutoff limit; the source representation and limiting dynamics must be identified.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine whether the source many-body time evolution remains strongly continuous after its ultraviolet cutoff is removed.
