\section{Strong Continuity Without Ultraviolet Regularization}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


Ultraviolet regularization smears the continuum fermionic interaction by Gaussian functions of width $\sigma$. The CAR algebra $\mathcal A$ is generated by the canonical anticommutation-relation observables. In this source, strong continuity of a $C^*$-dynamical system means pointwise operator-norm continuity: $\lim_{t\to0}\|\tau_t(A)-A\|=0$ for every $A\in\mathcal A$. This is stronger than continuity in the strong operator topology of a Hilbert-space representation. Removing the cutoff takes $\sigma\to0$.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Does strong continuity of the many-body dynamics persist when the ultraviolet regularization is removed, that is, in the limit $\sigma\to 0$?

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}Strong continuity at fixed cutoff does not automatically pass to a cutoff limit; the source representation and limiting dynamics must be identified.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine whether the source many-body time evolution remains strongly continuous after its ultraviolet cutoff is removed.

\paragraph{Unbounded free energy is not itself an obstruction.} Let \(U_t\) be a strongly continuous unitary group on a one-particle Hilbert space, possibly generated by an unbounded Hamiltonian. On the CAR algebra, the free automorphism is \(\tau_t(a(f))=a(U_tf)\). The CAR norm identity is \(\|a(g)\|=\|g\|\): the upper bound follows from \(a(g)^*a(g)+a(g)a(g)^*=\|g\|^2I\), and equality follows by acting on the normalized one-particle vector in direction \(g\). Consequently
\[
 \|\tau_t(a(f))-a(f)\|=\|(U_t-I)f\|\longrightarrow0.
\]
Telescoping products proves continuity on polynomials in the generators. Approximate any CAR element by such a polynomial and use isometry of automorphisms to extend norm continuity to the norm closure.
\paragraph{A sufficient cutoff passage criterion.} For interacting cutoff automorphisms \(\tau_t^\sigma\), suppose on a norm-dense subalgebra and some fixed \(T>0\), \(\sup_{|t|\leq T}\|\tau_t^\sigma(A)-\tau_t(A)\|\to0\). Then
\[
 \|\tau_t(A)-A\|\leq
 2\sup_{|s|\leq T}\|\tau_s^\sigma(A)-\tau_s(A)\|
 +\|\tau_t^\sigma(A)-A\|,
\]
where the factor two is a harmless upper bound. Fix a small cutoff error, then let \(t\to0\); density extends the result if the limit is an isometric automorphism group.
\paragraph{First interaction gap.} The source asks whether such a limiting many-body dynamics exists with sufficient norm control as Gaussian smearing is removed. Fixed-cutoff continuity and strong operator convergence in a representation do not supply the displayed uniform norm approximation. Proving that estimate, or a suitable equicontinuity substitute with algebra invariance, is the first missing step. The free calculation is a boundary check, not an answer for unregularized interactions.
