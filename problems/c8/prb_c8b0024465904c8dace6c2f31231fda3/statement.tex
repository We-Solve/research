\section{Kirby Problem 3.51}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $Y$ be a closed oriented rational homology $3$-sphere, so $H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)$ is finite. The group $\operatorname{SU}(2)$ consists of complex $2\times2$ unitary matrices of determinant one. A representation is abelian if its image is an abelian subgroup. Write $I^\#(Y;\mathbb C)$ for framed instanton Floer homology over $\mathbb C$, the version defined via the standard admissible bundle on $Y\#T^3$; the framing includes reducible representations that ordinary instanton homology does not include. An instanton L-space has $\dim_{\mathbb C}I^\#(Y;\mathbb C)=|H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)|$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every rational homology $3$-sphere $Y$, does
\[
\bigl(\forall\rho:\pi_1(Y)\to\operatorname{SU}(2),\ \rho(\pi_1(Y))\text{ abelian}\bigr)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\dim_{\mathbb C}I^\#(Y;\mathbb C)=|H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)|?
\]
No cyclic-finiteness or Morse--Bott nondegeneracy assumption is added. \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must a rational homology sphere with no nonabelian SU(2) representation have the minimum possible framed instanton Floer dimension?
