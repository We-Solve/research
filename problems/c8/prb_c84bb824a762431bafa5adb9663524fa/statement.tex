\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If a closed $n$-manifold $M$ admits a metric of positive scalar curvature, must the macroscopic dimension of its universal cover satisfy $\dim_{\mathrm{mc}}(\widetilde M)\leq n-2$?
