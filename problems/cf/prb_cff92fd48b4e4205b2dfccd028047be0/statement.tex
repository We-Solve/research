\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a group \(G\), define \([a,b]=a^{-1}b^{-1}ab\), \(G'=[G,G]\), and \(G''=[G',G']\). The characteristic subgroup \(G''\) determines the second derived quotient \(G/G''\). Define
\[
\mathrm{IMA}(G)=\ker\!\left(\mathrm{Aut}(G)\longrightarrow\mathrm{Aut}(G/G'')\right)
=\{\alpha\in\mathrm{Aut}(G):\alpha(g)G''=gG''\text{ for every }g\in G\}.
\]
For each integer \(n\geq 3\), let \(F_n=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle\) be the free group of rank \(n\).

\textbf{Problem (V. G. Bardakov, 1999).} Find generators and defining relations for \(\mathrm{IMA}(F_n)\), for every integer \(n\geq 3\). More precisely, describe a generating family \(S_n\) of automorphisms in \(\mathrm{IMA}(F_n)\) and a family of relations \(R_n\) such that the homomorphism determined by these automorphisms gives an isomorphism
\[
\langle S_n\mid R_n\rangle\cong\mathrm{IMA}(F_n).
\]
The supplied statement does not require either family to be finite.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For each free group of finite rank \(n\geq 3\), describe generators and all defining relations for the group of automorphisms inducing the identity on \(F_n/F_n''\). The supplied registry labels this problem open; that status has not been independently verified.
