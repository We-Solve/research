Let $\operatorname{SF}(N)$ be the largest size of $A\subseteq[1,N]$ for which no nonempty subset has a square sum. Nguyen--Vu proved $\operatorname{SF}(N)\leq N^{1/3}(\log N)^C$ for an absolute $C$, matching Erd\H{o}s's construction up to logarithmic factors. Here the lower construction is proved with explicit parameters, along with a general upper bound from two-element subsets. The deep upper bound is acknowledged but not reproduced.
