\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30000758}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\) be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and \(\mathfrak q\) a finite-dimensional algebraic Lie algebra over \(k\). Identify \(S(\mathfrak q)=k[\mathfrak q^*]\), with its Lie--Poisson bracket determined on linear functions by \(\{x,y\}(\lambda)=\lambda([x,y])\). Define
\[
 \mathfrak q_\lambda=\{x\in\mathfrak q:\lambda([x,y])=0\ \forall y\in\mathfrak q\},\qquad
 \ell=\operatorname{ind}\mathfrak q=\min_{\lambda\in\mathfrak q^*}\dim\mathfrak q_\lambda,
 \qquad b(\mathfrak q)=\frac{\dim\mathfrak q+\ell}{2}.
\]
A unital \(k\)-subalgebra \(A\subseteq S(\mathfrak q)\) is Poisson-commutative when \(\{A,A\}=0\), and maximal means maximal under inclusion among such subalgebras. The first question is
\[
 \forall A\subseteq S(\mathfrak q)\text{ maximal Poisson-commutative},\qquad
 \dim_{\mathrm{Krull}}A=b(\mathfrak q)\ ?
\]
Maximal under inclusion is the hypothesis; maximal possible dimension is the proposed conclusion. Ordinary associative commutativity would be automatic in \(S(\mathfrak q)\) and is not the intended condition.

For the second question set
\[
 \mathfrak q^*_{\mathrm{reg}}=\{\lambda:\dim\mathfrak q_\lambda=\ell\},\qquad
 Z(\mathfrak q)=\{f\in S(\mathfrak q):\{f,g\}=0\ \forall g\in S(\mathfrak q)\}.
\]
Assume \(\operatorname{codim}_{\mathfrak q^*}(\mathfrak q^*\setminus\mathfrak q^*_{\mathrm{reg}})\geq2\), and that the Poisson center \(Z(\mathfrak q)\) contains algebraically independent homogeneous elements \(f_1,\ldots,f_\ell\) satisfying \(\sum_i\deg f_i=b(\mathfrak q)\). For every regular \(\xi\in\mathfrak q^*_{\mathrm{reg}}\), write
\[
 f(\lambda+t\xi)=\sum_{j=0}^{\deg f}f_{\xi,j}(\lambda)t^j,
 \qquad
 B_\xi=F_\xi(Z(\mathfrak q))
   =k[f_{\xi,j}:f\in Z(\mathfrak q),\ 0\leq j\leq\deg f].
\]
Is the morphism induced by the inclusion \(B_\xi\hookrightarrow S(\mathfrak q)\),
\[
 \pi_\xi:\mathfrak q^*=\operatorname{Spec}S(\mathfrak q)
       \longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}B_\xi,
\]
flat for every such \(\mathfrak q\) and \(\xi\)? Equivalently, is \(S(\mathfrak q)\) a flat \(B_\xi\)-module? The second question has the additional hypotheses displayed above; they are not imposed on the first question.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The first question asks whether every inclusion-maximal Poisson-commutative subalgebra reaches the stated dimension bound. The second asks whether the map collecting all central argument-shift coefficients is flat under the codimension, degree-sum, and regularity hypotheses.
