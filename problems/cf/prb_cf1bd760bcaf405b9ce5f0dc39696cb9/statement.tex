\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


Degrees of freedom are the independent coordinates needed to describe a model state. An asymptotic analysis studies a sequence of models indexed by a size parameter $n$ as $n\to\infty$. For quantities $a_n,b_n>0$, $a_n=O(b_n)$ means $|a_n|\leq Cb_n$ eventually for a constant $C$, and $a_n=o(b_n)$ means $a_n/b_n\to0$. A useful large-system approximation must specify the system family, observable, limit and error measure; no one such family is selected by this record.
