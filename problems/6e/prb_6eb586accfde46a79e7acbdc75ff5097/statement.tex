\textbf{Problem.} Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $(Y,h,\varphi,\sigma)$ for the existence of a complete smooth Riemannian $n$-manifold $(X,g)$ and a diffeomorphism $\iota:Y\longrightarrow\partial X$ such that
\[
R_g(x)\geq\sigma\quad\text{for every }x\in X,
\qquad
\iota^*(g^\top)=h,
\qquad
H_g\circ\iota=\varphi.
\]
The pair $(g^\top,H_g)$ is the Bartnik boundary data. The identification requires $Y$ to represent the entire boundary. No compactness assumption on $X$ is supplied. For a manifold with boundary, completeness can be interpreted as completeness of the intrinsic Riemannian length metric on $X$, including its boundary; the source does not explicitly confirm this interpretation. The dimension range, meaning of $\sigma$, completeness interpretation, and mean-curvature convention remain unresolved qualifications of this draft.
