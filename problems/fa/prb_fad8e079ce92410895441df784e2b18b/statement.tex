\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a torus cusp of a hyperbolic \(3\)-manifold, a \emph{slope} is an unoriented isotopy class of essential simple closed curves on a torus cross-section. Dehn filling along that slope attaches a solid torus to the corresponding boundary component of a compact core so that its meridian represents the chosen slope. Filling several torus cusps requires a choice of slope at each cusp being filled.
