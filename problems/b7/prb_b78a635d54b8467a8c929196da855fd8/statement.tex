\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a groupoid \(G\), write \(G^{(0)}\) for its unit space and \(r,s:G\to G^{(0)}\) for the range and source maps. The groupoid is étale when \(s\), equivalently \(r\), is a local homeomorphism. A twist \(\Sigma\) over \(G\) is a central circle extension \(G^{(0)}\times\mathbb T\hookrightarrow\Sigma\twoheadrightarrow G\), where \(\mathbb T=\{z\in\C:|z|=1\}\). For a subgroupoid \(S\subseteq G\), the restricted twist \(\Sigma|_S\) is its inverse image in \(\Sigma\). The subgroupoid is clopen if it is both open and closed in \(G\).

Write \(C_r^*(G;\Sigma)\) for the reduced twisted groupoid \(C^*\)-algebra, formed by completing the compactly supported sections of the associated complex line bundle in the reduced norm, whenever the intended topological and analytic hypotheses make this construction available. Put
\[
 A=C_r^*(G;\Sigma),\qquad B=C_r^*(S;\Sigma|_S).
\]
The intended inclusion \(B\subseteq A\) is the canonical one induced by extension by zero of compactly supported sections on the open subgroupoid \(S\).

A \(C^*\)-subalgebra \(B\subseteq A\) is a Cartan subalgebra if all of the following hold:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(B\) is maximal abelian in \(A\), meaning that \(B\) is abelian and \(B'\cap A=B\).
\item \(B\) contains an approximate identity for \(A\).
\item \(B\) is regular: with
\[
 N_A(B)=\{a\in A:aBa^*\subseteq B\text{ and }a^*Ba\subseteq B\},
\]
one has \(\overline{\operatorname{span}}N_A(B)=A\).
\item There exists a faithful conditional expectation \(E:A\to B\). Here a conditional expectation is a completely positive contractive projection onto \(B\) satisfying \(E(b_1ab_2)=b_1E(a)b_2\) for \(a\in A\) and \(b_1,b_2\in B\); faithfulness means \(E(a^*a)=0\) implies \(a=0\).
\end{enumerate}
