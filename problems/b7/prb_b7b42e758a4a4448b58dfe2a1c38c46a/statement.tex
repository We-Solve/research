\section{Persistence and Permanence of Weakly Reversible Reaction Networks}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a weakly reversible mass-action reaction network, must every positive trajectory be persistent, staying away from the boundary in the long-time limit? Must it also be permanent, eventually remaining between uniform positive lower bounds and finite upper bounds within the relevant stoichiometric class? Weak reversibility means that every reaction edge belongs to a directed cycle.
