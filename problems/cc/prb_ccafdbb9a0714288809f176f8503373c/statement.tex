\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be a compact connected Riemannian manifold without boundary and \(f\) a smooth vector field with flow \(\phi^t\). Connectedness and absence of boundary state the standard framework of the aid; the original says only compact manifold. Set
\[
 L_f(x,v)=\tfrac12\|v-f(x)\|_x^2.
\]
\textbf{Source questions (Albert Fathi and the appended Hamilton--Jacobi item).} Characterize the zero-cohomology Aubry set dynamically, and in particular determine whether it contains the flow's chain recurrent set. Also determine when zero is the unique viscosity solution of the displayed stationary Hamilton--Jacobi equation.

Two source defects require explicit separation. The invariant zero-action graph in \(TM\) is \(\{(x,f(x)):x\in M\}\), not generally the tangent zero section. The Legendre dual of \(L_f\) is
\[
 H_f^+(x,p)=\tfrac12\|p\|_x^2+p(f(x)),
\]
whereas the source prints \(H_f^-(x,p)=\tfrac12\|p\|_x^2-p(f(x))\). The latter is dual to \(L_{-f}\). The cotangent zero section is invariant for these Hamiltonians. Furthermore every constant solves either equation at level zero, so uniqueness must mean modulo constants, or after normalization such as \(u(x_*)=0\).

To distinguish projected and lifted sets, define the time-\(t\) action
\[
 A_t(x,y)=\inf_{\gamma(0)=x,\,\gamma(t)=y}
 \int_0^t L_f(\gamma(s),\dot\gamma(s))\,\mathrm ds,
\]
with absolutely continuous curves. At the zero critical value, let
\[
 h_f(x,y)=\liminf_{t\to\infty}A_t(x,y),\qquad
 \mathcal A_f=\{x\in M:h_f(x,x)=0\}.
\]
The lifted Aubry set is on the zero-action graph; the dynamical comparison is between its projection \(\mathcal A_f\subseteq M\) and recurrent subsets of \(M\).

For \(T>0\), a returning chain at \(x\) consists of \(x_0=x,x_1,\ldots,x_m=x\), \(m\geq1\), and times \(t_i\geq T\), \(0\leq i<m\). Its jumps are
\[
 e_i=d(\phi^{t_i}(x_i),x_{i+1}).
\]
Define \(\mathcal R_2(f)\) by requiring, for every \(T>0\) and \(\varepsilon>0\), such a chain with \(\sum_i e_i^2<\varepsilon\). Define strong chain recurrence \(\operatorname{SCR}(f)\) using \(\sum_i e_i<\varepsilon\), and ordinary chain recurrence \(\operatorname{CR}(f)\) using \(\max_i e_i<\varepsilon\), with the same quantifiers and the fixed Riemannian distance.
