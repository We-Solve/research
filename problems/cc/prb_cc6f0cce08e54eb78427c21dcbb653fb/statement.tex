\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a finite nonabelian simple group. A left Yetter--Drinfeld module over \(\C G\) is a complex vector space \(V\) with a left \(G\)-action and a grading
\[
V=\bigoplus_{g\in G}V_g,
\qquad h\cdot V_g\subseteq V_{hgh^{-1}}\quad(g,h\in G).
\]
Its support is \(\operatorname{supp}(V)=\{g\in G:V_g\ne0\}\). The associated braiding is
\[
c(v\otimes w)=g\cdot w\otimes v
\qquad(v\in V_g,\ w\in V).
\]
Write \({}_{\C G}^{\C G}\mathcal{YD}\) for this category. The Nichols algebra of \(V\) is
\[
\mathcal B(V)=T(V)/I(V),\qquad T(V)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}V^{\otimes n},
\]
where \(T(V)\) has its braided Hopf algebra structure with every element of \(V\) primitive, and \(I(V)\) is the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in \(\bigoplus_{n\ge2}V^{\otimes n}\).

\text{For every finite nonabelian simple group }G
\text{ and every }0\ne V\in{}_{\C G}^{\C G}\mathcal{YD},
\qquad \dim_{\C}\mathcal B(V)=\infty.
\]
