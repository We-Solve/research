\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Equip $\C^6$ with the Hermitian inner product $\langle u,v\rangle=\sum_{r=1}^6\overline{u_r}v_r$. Two orthonormal bases $B_a=(u_{a,1},\ldots,u_{a,6})$ and $B_b=(u_{b,1},\ldots,u_{b,6})$ are mutually unbiased if
\[
|\langle u_{a,j},u_{b,k}\rangle|^2=\frac16
\qquad(1\le j,k\le6).
\]
A family of seven mutually unbiased bases consists of bases $B_1,\ldots,B_7$ satisfying this condition for every $1\le a<b\le7$.
