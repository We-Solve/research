\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 11100056}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A monoidal model category $\mathcal C$ has a monoidal product compatible with its model structure through the pushout-product and unit axioms. A simplicial monoidal model category additionally has compatible simplicial enrichment, tensors, and cotensors, satisfying the simplicial model-category axioms and the required compatibility with the monoidal product.

\textbf{Model categories problem 10 (Hovey).} Is every monoidal model category Quillen equivalent to a simplicial monoidal model category? The source does not restrict to cofibrantly generated, combinatorial, presentable, or already enriched categories. A precise version must say whether one direct adjunction or a zigzag is allowed and what monoidal compatibility the Quillen equivalence must preserve. An equivalence of underlying model categories alone need not transport the original monoidal structure in the way needed for the stated motivation.

The motivation is to make $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal C)$ a central algebra over $\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{sSet})$. In action notation, this asks for an action $K\odot X$ with coherent comparison isomorphisms compatible with derived tensor product, schematically
\[
 (K\odot X)\otimes^{\mathbb L}Y
 \cong K\odot(X\otimes^{\mathbb L}Y)
 \cong X\otimes^{\mathbb L}(K\odot Y),
\]
with the appropriate associativity, unit, and centrality coherence. The source says centrality is the remaining issue in Hovey's book and that the simplicial monoidal case can be handled. This is not merely a request for an unenriched equivalence of ordinary homotopy categories.

The registry summary calls the problem solved through Muro--Raptis and Lurie, but gives vague title fragments, an approximate date, and no exact theorem. A presentation result for suitable presentable monoidal infinity-categories would still need its hypotheses and monoidal comparison spelled out before it answers the unqualified all-model-categories question. No such general implication is verified in this draft.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every monoidal model category be replaced, with the needed monoidal compatibility, by a simplicial monoidal model category? The intended application is a coherent central action of the homotopy category of spaces on its homotopy category.
