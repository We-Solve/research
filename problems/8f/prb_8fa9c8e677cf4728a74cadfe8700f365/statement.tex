\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30000751}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a number field \(K\), that is, a finite extension of \(\Q\), let \(\mathcal O_K\) be its ring of integers and let \(\mathcal O_K^\times\) be its group of units. Determine all such fields \(K\) for which
\[
\forall\alpha\in\mathcal O_K\quad\exists m\in\Z_{\geq0}\quad\exists u_1,\ldots,u_m\in\mathcal O_K^\times
\quad \alpha=\sum_{i=1}^m u_i.
\]
The number of summands may depend on \(\alpha\), and units may be repeated. The empty sum represents zero; allowing only positive lengths gives the same property because \(0=1+(-1)\). Equivalently, the additive group of \(\mathcal O_K\) is generated by its units, since the negative of a unit is a unit. The request is a classification of number fields, naturally up to field isomorphism, with this property. It does not require a uniform bound on the number of summands.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify the number fields whose every algebraic integer is a finite sum of units of their ring of integers. The length of the sum may vary with the integer.
