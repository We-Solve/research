\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(F_n\) be the free group of rank \(n\), and let \(\operatorname{GL}(m,\Z)\) be the group of invertible integral \(m\times m\) matrices. The source asks whether
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 H\leq\operatorname{Aut}(F_n),\quad
 [\operatorname{Aut}(F_n):H]<\infty,\\
 K\leq\operatorname{GL}(m,\Z),\quad
 [\operatorname{GL}(m,\Z):K]<\infty,\quad
 \exists\text{ surjection }H\twoheadrightarrow K
 \end{gathered}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad m\geq n-1.
\]
No injectivity of the homomorphism is requested. The precise ranges of \(n,m\) are not supplied. In particular \(m=1\) gives the finite group \(\operatorname{GL}(1,\Z)=\{\pm1\}\), with the trivial subgroup already of finite index; this makes an exclusion or infinite-target convention necessary when \(n\geq3\). Such a restriction is not silently added. The closing phrase ``Indeed one might ask'' refers to an omitted further question.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must a finite-index quotient of a finite-index subgroup of \(\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\) inside \(\operatorname{GL}(m,\Z)\) satisfy \(m\geq n-1\), with the missing low-rank exclusions clarified?
