\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


$I$ is the identity matrix. $W^{1,\infty}$ consists of essentially bounded functions with essentially bounded weak derivative. The funnel restricts the output norm by the prescribed time-dependent bound; $\xi$ is the feedback controller\textquotesingle s internal state.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Consider the single-input, single-output system $\dot{x}(t)=Ax(t)+bu(t)$, $y(t)=cx(t)$, where $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ and $b,c^\top,x^0\in\mathbb{R}^n$ are unknown. Assume that, for some known $r\in\mathbb{N}$, there is a $\rho\in\{1,\ldots,r\}$ such that $cA^ib=0$ for $i=1,\ldots,\rho-2$ and $cA^{\rho-1}b>0$, and that $$\det\begin{bmatrix}sI-A&b\\c&0\end{bmatrix}\ne0\qquad(\operatorname{Re}s\geq0).$$ For an arbitrary performance funnel $$\mathcal{F}_\varphi=\{(t,e)\in\mathbb{R}_{\geq0}\times\mathbb{R}^m:\varphi(t)\lVert e\rVert<1\},$$ where $\varphi\in W^{1,\infty}(\mathbb{R}_{\geq0},\mathbb{R})$, $\varphi(0)=0$, $\varphi(s)>0$ for $s>0$, and $\liminf_{s\to\infty}\varphi(s)>0$, construct a feedback law $$u(t)=-\gamma_r(k(t),y(t),\xi(t)),\qquad k(t)=\bigl[1-(\varphi(t)\lVert y(t)\rVert)^2\bigr]^{-1},\qquad \dot\xi(t)=f(y(t),u(t),\xi(t)),$$ with polynomial $\gamma_r$ and $f$, such that $y$ remains in $\mathcal{F}_\varphi$. \sref{30000197}{2}
