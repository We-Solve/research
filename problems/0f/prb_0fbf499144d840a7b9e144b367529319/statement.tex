\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Consider positional games on $E(K_n)$. In the usual $(1:b)$ Maker--Breaker convention, Maker claims one previously unclaimed edge and Breaker claims $b$ per turn; Maker seeks a spanning subgraph with a specified increasing property, and Breaker seeks to prevent this. Turn order, exact versus at-most quotas, and the treatment of an incomplete final turn are not stated in the record and must be fixed.

For any fixed such convention, write

b_{\mathrm{MB}}(n,\mathcal P)=\max\{b\geq1:\text{Maker has a winning strategy for }\mathcal P\},

The source asks to close the remaining gap for the Hamilton-cycle bias $b_{\mathrm{MB}}(n,\mathrm{Ham})$, to determine the perfect-matching bias $b_{\mathrm{MB}}(n,\mathrm{PM})$, and to determine the bias $b_{\mathrm{MB}}(n,\mathrm{Conn}_k)$ for $k$-vertex-connectivity. Here a Hamilton cycle visits every vertex, a perfect matching covers every vertex and requires even $n$, and a graph is $k$-vertex-connected if it has more than $k$ vertices and remains connected after deletion of any fewer than $k$ vertices.
