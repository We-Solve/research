\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\Sigma$ be an exotic smooth $n$-torus, meaning a smooth manifold homeomorphic to $\mathbb T^n=(S^1)^n$ but not diffeomorphic to it. The question concerns dimensions and smooth structures for which such $\Sigma$ exists. Equip $T^*\Sigma$ with its canonical symplectic structure. The source asks whether there exists a completely integrable convex Hamiltonian $H:T^*\Sigma\to\R$ for a given such $\Sigma$. Under the usual smooth Liouville interpretation, complete integrability means functions $F_1=H,F_2,\ldots,F_n$ with \[\{F_i,F_j\}=0\quad(1\leq i,j\leq n),\qquad dF_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dF_n\ne0\] on an appropriate open dense regular set.
