\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Interpret a knot as a smooth oriented embedding of $S^1$ in $S^3$, considered up to orientation-preserving ambient isotopy. This ambient manifold and orientation convention are standard interpretations that are not explicitly specified in the supplied record. Two knots $K_0$ and $K_1$ are smoothly concordant, written $K_0\sim K_1$, if they cobound a smoothly and properly embedded oriented annulus in $S^3\times[0,1]$, with boundary $(-K_0)\times\{0\}\sqcup K_1\times\{1\}$. Write $\mathcal C$ for the set of these concordance classes; this interprets the undefined symbol ``C'' in the original excerpt.

A knot $K$ is fibered if its exterior $S^3\setminus\operatorname{int}(\nu K)$, where $\nu K$ is a tubular neighborhood, fibers smoothly over $S^1$ with fiber a compact connected Seifert surface for $K$. Here a Seifert surface is a compact connected oriented embedded surface in $S^3$ whose oriented boundary is $K$, and its intersection with the exterior is understood when describing the fiber.

Let $B_n$ be the braid group on $n$ strands, with positive Artin generators $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}$. A knot is strongly quasipositive if it is the oriented closure of a braid that is a product of positive embedded band generators
\[
 a_{i,j}=(\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\cdots\sigma_{j-2})\,\sigma_{j-1}\,(\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\cdots\sigma_{j-2})^{-1},\qquad 1\leq i<j\leq n,
\]
for some $n\geq1$, with empty products allowed. In particular, $a_{i,i+1}=\sigma_i$.
