\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source asks for useful classes of spaces on which an infinitesimal metric condition for quasiconformality implies a local condition, and notes Loewner spaces as a known setting. The two conditions themselves are not reproduced in the excerpt.

For orientation, for a homeomorphism \(f:(X,d)\to(Y,\rho)\), a common infinitesimal metric quantity is
\[
 H_f(x)=\limsup_{r\downarrow0}\frac{L_f(x,r)}{l_f(x,r)},
\]
where
\[
 L_f(x,r)=\sup_{d(x,a)\le r}\rho(f(x),f(a)),\qquad
 l_f(x,r)=\inf_{d(x,b)\ge r}\rho(f(x),f(b)),
\]
for radii and spaces where these quantities are meaningful. An infinitesimal bounded-distortion condition may require \(H_f(x)\le H\) uniformly; a possible local comparison condition is local weak quasisymmetry, controlling
\[
 d(x,a)\le d(x,b)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \rho(f(x),f(a))\le C\rho(f(x),f(b))
\]
for triples in suitable neighborhoods. Full local quasisymmetry gives a control function for arbitrary ratios instead. These are illustrative conventional notions, not an identification of the missing source definitions. Whether the infinitesimal requirement is everywhere or almost everywhere, and how the local constants are uniform, also matters.

With the intended predicates fixed, the task is to identify classes of domain/codomain spaces for which every eligible homeomorphism satisfying the infinitesimal predicate satisfies the local predicate, with the intended quantitative dependence. The source gives no exact maximality criterion for ``good classes''.

The usual Loewner framework involves a metric measure structure and quantitative modulus bounds for curve families joining continua; dimension and regularity assumptions are material to the relevant equivalence theorems. The supplied summary attributes a developed theory to Heinonen--Koskela and calls the record survey material, but gives no exact theorem hypotheses for domain, target, or measure. The registry nevertheless labels it open.
