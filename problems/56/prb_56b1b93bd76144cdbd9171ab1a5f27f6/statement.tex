\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(R\) denote the hyperfinite \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor with its normalized faithful normal trace \(\tau_R\). Under the usual ultrapower convention, fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter \(\mathcal U\) on \(\N\) and define
\[
R^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(\N,R)\big/\mathcal I_{\mathcal U},\qquad
\mathcal I_{\mathcal U}=\left\{(x_n):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau_R}=0\right\},\qquad
\|x\|_{2,\tau_R}=\tau_R(x^*x)^{1/2}.
\]
A \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor is a finite von Neumann algebra with center \(\C1\), no nonzero minimal projections, and a normalized faithful normal trace. Interpret separability as separability of the predual. An embedding here means a unital, normal, injective, trace-preserving \(*\)-homomorphism. A factor is Connes embeddable if it admits such an embedding into a tracial ultrapower of \(R\).

The supplied question asks whether the following implication holds for every Connes embeddable separable \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(N\):
\[
\left[
\begin{gathered}
\text{for every pair of embeddings }\pi_1,\pi_2:N\longrightarrow R^{\mathcal U},\\
\text{there exists }\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(R^{\mathcal U})
\text{ such that }\pi_2=\alpha\circ\pi_1
\end{gathered}
\right]
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad N\cong R.
\]
Here \(\operatorname{Aut}(R^{\mathcal U})\) consists of normal unital \(*\)-automorphisms, and \(\cong\) means isomorphism of von Neumann algebras. The automorphism may depend on the pair of embeddings.

The source record does not specify the index set, the ultrafilter, or whether its assertion is intended for one fixed ultrafilter or uniformly for all eligible ultrafilters. The display uses a fixed nonprincipal ultrafilter on \(\N\) as the conventional interpretation; that convention and its intended quantification require confirmation from the source.
\subsection{Short English statement}
If every two embeddings of a Connes embeddable separable \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(N\) into \(R^{\mathcal U}\) differ by an automorphism of the target, must \(N\) be isomorphic to the hyperfinite factor \(R\)? The supplied record labels this question open; the ultrafilter convention remains to be confirmed.
