The Azumaya Brauer group \(\operatorname{Br}_{\mathrm{Az}}(\mathcal X)\) is the group of Morita-equivalence classes of Azumaya \(\mathcal O_{\mathcal X}\)-algebras, with operation induced by tensor product.
