\section{Loop Operators and Microscopic Nonabelian Anyons}\label{30004242}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30004242 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
In two spatial dimensions, anyons are excitations whose exchanges act through braid-group representations. Abelian statistics are scalar phases, while nonabelian statistics act nontrivially on a multidimensional fusion space. A microscopic loop operator is an observable or unitary associated with transporting an excitation around a closed path, constructed from a local many-body Hamiltonian. It must reproduce the relevant ground-state action, charge transport and braiding relations.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Can loop operators and the corresponding anyon description be constructed for microscopic models with nonabelian anyons, extending the known abelian construction?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The admissible Hamiltonians, locality and gap assumptions must follow the source abelian construction. A formal topological field-theory braid representation alone does not produce operators for the requested microscopic models.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct microscopic loop operators realizing nonabelian anyon transport and statistics.
