\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\textbf{Question 1.38.} What can be said about pseudoholomorphic curves for almost complex structures compatible with locally conformal symplectic geometry, and can they be useful tools for studying it? The source records Eliashberg's skepticism, not a mathematical impossibility assertion. Its ``theis'' is reported as a source typo for ``this'', and ``c.s.s.'' is a surrounding historical abbreviation for conformal/locally conformal symplectic structures, now usually l.c.s.

\textbf{Convention-explicit exact setting from the aid.} Let $M^{2n}$ carry a nondegenerate two-form $\Omega$ and a closed one-form $\eta$. To match the aid's positive exponential, use
\[
 \dd\Omega+\eta\wedge\Omega=0,\qquad
 d_\eta=\dd+\eta\wedge,\qquad
 \Omega=d_\eta\lambda=\dd\lambda+\eta\wedge\lambda.
\]
This is Lichnerowicz exactness in the stated sign convention; conventions with $\dd\Omega=\eta\wedge\Omega$ instead use $d_\eta=\dd-\eta\wedge$ and a negative exponential. Let $J$ be any $\Omega$-compatible almost complex structure, with associated positive metric $g(v,w)=\Omega(v,Jw)$.

For a $J$-holomorphic map $u:(\Sigma,j)\to M$ from a compact Riemann surface, possibly with boundary, its Lee charge is $[u^*\eta]\in H^1(\Sigma;\R)$, equivalently its periods. If this charge vanishes, choose $h$ with $\dd h=u^*\eta$. The aid's weighted Stokes identity is
\[
 e^h u^*\Omega=\dd(e^h u^*\lambda),\qquad
 \int_\Sigma e^h u^*\Omega
       =\int_{\partial\Sigma}e^h u^*\lambda.
\]
For closed connected $\Sigma$, compatibility and this identity force $u$ to be constant. In particular every compatible holomorphic sphere is constant, since its first cohomology vanishes. The statement applies to arbitrary compatible $J$ in this exact setting, not only a specially adapted contact-type structure.

A conformal gauge change consistent with the chosen sign is
\[
 \Omega'=e^f\Omega,\quad\eta'=\eta-\dd f,\quad
 \lambda'=e^f\lambda,\quad h'=h-f\circ u.
\]
Then $e^{h'}u^*\Omega'=e^hu^*\Omega$ and $e^{h'}u^*\lambda'=e^hu^*\lambda$; the charge class is unchanged. Additive constants in $h$ multiply both sides by the same positive constant.

\textbf{Compactness scope.} The absence of nonconstant sphere bubbles supplies smooth subsequential compactness for a sequence of maps from a fixed closed Riemann surface into a compact target, with fixed smooth compatible $J$ and a uniform Dirichlet-energy bound for $g$. A common compact image set can replace compactness of the target. No compactness claim is made with escaping images, varying degenerating domains, unbounded energy, or unrestricted boundary conditions. The general l.c.s. form is not closed, so ordinary symplectic homological-area bounds are not assumed.

The aid reports later partial affirmative developments involving charged elliptic curves, contact instantons and Reeb dynamics, alongside unbounded-energy and holomorphic-sky-catastrophe obstructions to an unrestricted theory. \subsection{Short English statement}
The question asks whether holomorphic curves can study locally conformal symplectic structures. In the supplied exact setting, weighted Stokes shows that every closed zero-Lee-charge curve is constant, so compatible spheres cannot bubble. Smooth compactness still needs a compact image, fixed domain and energy bounds; the broader nonexact theory and its reported later applications remain separate.
