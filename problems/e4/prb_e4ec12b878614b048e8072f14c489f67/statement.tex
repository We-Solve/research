\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Lambda\) be the isolated-singularity \(R\)-order in the source, and let \(\operatorname{CM}\Lambda\) denote its category of finitely generated modules that are maximal Cohen--Macaulay over \(R\). In a standard local convention, \(R\) is a commutative Noetherian Cohen--Macaulay local ring, an \(R\)-order is a finite \(R\)-algebra maximal Cohen--Macaulay as an \(R\)-module, and isolated singularity requires nonsingularity away from the maximal ideal. A customary nonsingular-order condition there is \(\operatorname{gl.dim}\Lambda_{\mathfrak p}=\dim R_{\mathfrak p}\). The report's exact base-ring, completeness, dimension, and order conventions are not supplied and must be checked; these conventions do not assert the conjecture for every ring fitting this broad template.

For \(M\in\operatorname{CM}\Lambda\), let \(\operatorname{add}M\) be the full subcategory of direct summands of finite direct sums of \(M\). Under the usual maximal orthogonality convention, \(\mathcal C=\operatorname{add}M\) is functorially finite and
\[
\mathcal C=\{X\in\operatorname{CM}\Lambda:\operatorname{Ext}^i_\Lambda(X,C)=0
\text{ for every }C\in\mathcal C,\ 1\leq i\leq n-1\}
\]
and
\[
\mathcal C=\{X\in\operatorname{CM}\Lambda:\operatorname{Ext}^i_\Lambda(C,X)=0
\text{ for every }C\in\mathcal C,\ 1\leq i\leq n-1\}.
\]
Functorial finiteness means that every object has both a right and a left \(\mathcal C\)-approximation. Maximal does not merely mean inclusion-maximal among pairwise Ext-orthogonal subcategories.

The source conjecture, attributed to Iyama by the supplied literature field, asks whether for any two such generators \(M,N\) for the same \(\Lambda\),
\[
D^b(\operatorname{mod}\operatorname{End}_\Lambda(M))
\simeq
D^b(\operatorname{mod}\operatorname{End}_\Lambda(N))
\]
as triangulated categories. Here \(\operatorname{mod}\) denotes finitely generated modules with a consistent side convention. These are the ordinary endomorphism rings, not the stable endomorphism quotients. The permitted \(n\) and its relation to \(\dim R\), and any intended \(R\)-linearity of the equivalence, are not specified in the extracted record.

The supplied assessment checked 2026-08-21 reports results for maximal \(1\)-orthogonal subcategories and for several low-dimensional or additional-structure cases, but says the general statement is not established. The exact hypotheses of those cases are absent; the secondary reference is labeled as concerning stable endomorphism rings and must not automatically be applied to the ordinary rings above.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For one isolated-singularity order, are the endomorphism rings of any two additive generators of maximal \((n-1)\)-orthogonal Cohen--Macaulay subcategories derived equivalent? The base-ring, dimension, and orthogonality conventions need source verification.
