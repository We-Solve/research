\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $K$ be a number field, let $\mathcal O_K$ be its ring of integers, and let $f\in K(x)$ have degree at least $2$. Regard $f$ as a map $\PP^1(K)\to\PP^1(K)$, and write $f^0=\operatorname{id}$ and $f^{n+1}=f\circ f^n$. A point $\alpha\in K$ is wandering if its forward orbit $\{f^n(\alpha):n\in\Z_{\ge0}\}$ is infinite.

\textbf{Supplied Conjecture 50, with provisional conventions.} For every number field $K$, every $f\in K(x)$ of degree at least $2$, every wandering $\alpha\in K$, every $\beta\in K$, and every real constant $c$, the set
