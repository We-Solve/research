\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(C_{12}=\Z/12\Z\) denote the cyclic group of order \(12\), and let \(A_4\) denote the group of even permutations of \(\{1,2,3,4\}\). For a subgroup \(H\leq G\), write \([G:H]\) for its index, and write \(H\trianglelefteq G\) to mean that \(H\) is normal in \(G\).

Does there exist a finite group \(G\) with normal subgroups \(K,L\trianglelefteq G\) such that
\[
[G:K]=[G:L]=12,\qquad K\cong L,\qquad G/K\cong C_{12},\qquad G/L\cong A_4?
\]
Here \(K\cong L\) means isomorphism as abstract groups.

\subsection{Short English statement}
