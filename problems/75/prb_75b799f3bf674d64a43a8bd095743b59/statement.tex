Let $f_{n+1}$ be Semaev's $(n+1)$-st summation polynomial. Given a prime $p$ and $x\in\mathbb{F}_p$, is there an effective general algorithm that finds integers $x_1,\ldots,x_n$, each of size at most $p^{1/n+\delta}$, such that $f_{n+1}(x_1,\ldots,x_n,x)\equiv0\pmod p$ whenever such a tuple is required by the index-calculus construction? \sref{statement}{2}
