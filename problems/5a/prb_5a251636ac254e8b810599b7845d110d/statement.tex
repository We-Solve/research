\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $G$ be a connected semisimple Lie group, $\Gamma<G$ an irreducible lattice, and $A$ a closed subgroup of a split Cartan subgroup $D<G$ with $\dim A>1$. Put $M=G/\Gamma$ and
\[
 Z_G(A)=\{g\in G:ga=ag\text{ for every }a\in A\},\qquad
 \alpha_0(a)(g\Gamma)=ag\Gamma.
\]
The source's Conjecture 1 asserts local rigidity in the following sense: every sufficiently $C^1$-small perturbation $\alpha$ of $\alpha_0$ through smooth $A$-actions admits a smooth diffeomorphism $h:M\to M$ and a continuous homomorphism $\beta:A\to Z_G(A)$ such that
\[
 h\bigl(\alpha(a)(g\Gamma)\bigr)=\beta(a)h(g\Gamma)
 \quad(a\in A,\ g\Gamma\in M).
\]
Thus the homogeneous model acts by left multiplication through $\beta$. The statement allows noncocompact lattices and disconnected closed $A$ as written. The precise topology on actions, the meaning of uniform smallness on a noncompact quotient, the regularity of the perturbed actions, and whether the model homomorphism must be close to the inclusion are not specified by the extract; they require clarification for a fully quantified neighborhood formulation.

The attached aid treats an algebraic local model under the additional assumption $A=\exp(\mathfrak a)$ connected inside a split Cartan. With $\mathfrak d=\operatorname{Lie}(D)$ and restricted root spaces $\mathfrak g_\lambda$ for $\lambda\in\Sigma(\mathfrak g,\mathfrak d)$, it reports
\[
 \mathfrak z_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak a)=
 \mathfrak z_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak d)\oplus
 \bigoplus_{\lambda|_{\mathfrak a}=0}\mathfrak g_\lambda.
\]
A model homomorphism can be written $\beta(a)=a\,c(a)$ with $c:A\to Z_G(A)$ a homomorphism. Writing $\iota:\mathfrak a\hookrightarrow\mathfrak g$ for inclusion, its differential has the form $\dd\beta=\iota+R$, where $R:\mathfrak a\to\mathfrak z_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak a)$ is linear and
\[
 [R(X),R(Y)]=0\quad(X,Y\in\mathfrak a).
\]
This is the quadratic commuting cone for the connected model, not a local-rigidity theorem for arbitrary perturbations. If $\lambda|_{\mathfrak a}=0$, $N\in\mathfrak g_\lambda$ is a root vector, and $\ell\in\mathfrak a^*$, the reported wall models are
\[
 \beta_\varepsilon(\exp X)=\exp X\,\exp\bigl(\varepsilon\ell(X)N\bigr),
 \qquad X\in\mathfrak a.
\]
They approach the original homogeneous action as $\varepsilon\to0$ and illustrate unipotent centralizer drift invisible in $D$ alone.

The aid reports strong $C^1$ local-rigidity results for full Cartan and generic restrictions, and a 2025 KAM result for broad nongeneric wall cases with high-$C^\ell$ smallness and additional cocompactness and structural hypotheses. It does not supply their full statements or references and does not claim those results prove the literal conjecture in its full stated scope. \subsection{Short English statement}
Does every sufficiently $C^1$-small perturbation of a higher-dimensional split-Cartan subgroup action on $G/\Gamma$ become a homogeneous action through the full centralizer after a smooth change of coordinates? The supplied algebraic wall models identify necessary target freedom but do not establish the conjecture for all stated lattices and restrictions.
