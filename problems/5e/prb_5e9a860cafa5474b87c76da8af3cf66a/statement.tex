\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A lattice tetrahedron is a full-dimensional polytope
\[
T=\operatorname{conv}\{v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3\}\subset\R^3,
\qquad v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3\in\Z^3.
\]
Using the standard interpretation of \emph{clean}, require
\[
\partial T\cap\Z^3=\{v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3\}.
\]
Write \(I(T)=\operatorname{int}(T)\cap\Z^3\), and let \(\mathcal C_k\) be the class of clean lattice tetrahedra with \(\#I(T)=k\), for each integer \(k\geq1\). The interior lattice points are \emph{collinear} if there is an affine line \(L\subset\R^3\) with \(I(T)\subset L\). In particular, sets of one or two interior points are collinear. Let \(V(T)\) denote ordinary Euclidean volume; normalized lattice volume is \(6V(T)\).
