\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((M,\omega,T)\) be a symplectic toric manifold as in the supplied record: \(T\) is a torus acting effectively and Hamiltonianly on the symplectic manifold \((M,\omega)\), with \(\dim M=2\dim T\). Write \(\mathfrak t\) for the Lie algebra of \(T\), let \(\Phi:M\to\mathfrak t^*\) be a moment map, and let \(\Delta=\Phi(M)\) be the moment polytope specified in the record. For every \(u\in\operatorname{int}\Delta\), put
\[
L_u=\Phi^{-1}(u),
\]
the Lagrangian toric fiber over \(u\). Here the interior is taken in \(\mathfrak t^*\).

A fiber \(L_u\) is \emph{Hamiltonian displaceable} if there exists a Hamiltonian symplectomorphism \(\varphi\in\operatorname{Ham}(M,\omega)\) such that
\[
\varphi(L_u)\cap L_u=\varnothing.
\]
A Hamiltonian symplectomorphism is a time-one map of a smooth time-dependent Hamiltonian flow defined throughout the time interval \([0,1]\). If noncompact manifolds are included, the required support convention for the Hamiltonians must additionally be specified.
