\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be an integer, let \(p\) be a prime, and let \(k\) be a field of characteristic \(p\). Write \(S_n\) for the group of permutations of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\). A partition \(\lambda\vdash n\) is a finite nonincreasing sequence of positive integers with sum \(n\). A partition is \(p\)-regular if no positive integer occurs as a part at least \(p\) times. Let \(\mathcal P(n)\) denote the partitions of \(n\), and let \(\mathcal P_p(n)\) denote the \(p\)-regular partitions of \(n\).

For \(\lambda\in\mathcal P(n)\), let \(S_k^\lambda\) be the Specht module over the group algebra \(kS_n\). For \(\mu\in\mathcal P_p(n)\), let \(D_k^\mu\) be the simple \(kS_n\)-module indexed by \(\mu\) in the standard Specht-module parametrization. Define the decomposition number
\[
 d_{\lambda\mu}(k)=[S_k^\lambda:D_k^\mu]\in\Z_{\geq 0}
\]
to be the number of composition factors isomorphic to \(D_k^\mu\) in a composition series of \(S_k^\lambda\); this multiplicity is independent of the chosen composition series.

\textbf{Problem.} Determine every entry of the decomposition matrix

 \bigl(d_{\lambda\mu}(k)\bigr)_{\lambda\in\mathcal P(n),\ \mu\in\mathcal P_p(n)}

for symmetric groups over fields of prime characteristic, uniformly for all positive integers \(n\), all primes \(p\), and all fields \(k\) of characteristic \(p\).
