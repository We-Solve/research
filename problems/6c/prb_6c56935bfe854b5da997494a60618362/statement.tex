Almost surely, only $O((\log n)^2)$ different sites have ever been favourites by time $n$. We prove the maximum-local-time upper bound directly, and give the deterministic record-level argument converting a bound on simultaneous favourites to a bound on all historical favourites.

The one deep input is Hao--Li--Okada--Zheng, \emph{Favorite Sites for Simple Random Walk in Two and More Dimensions}, Theorem 1.1, \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00995}: for planar simple random walk, almost surely $\limsup_n|F(n)|=3$, hence $|F(n)|\le3$ eventually. Their theorem concerns dimension two. T\'oth's earlier three-favourite theorem concerned dimension one, so we do not attribute the planar input to that earlier paper.
