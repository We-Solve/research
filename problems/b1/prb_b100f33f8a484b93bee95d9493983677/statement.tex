The supplied question concerns Hénon maps but does not specify the ambient map, its parameter restrictions, or whether saddle points must be fixed points or may be periodic points. The following notation uses the conventional complex setting, conditionally on that interpretation.

Let \(f\colon\C^2\to\C^2\) be a complex Hénon polynomial automorphism in the intended, unspecified class. Its forward filled set is
\[
K^+(f)=\left\{z\in\C^2:\sup_{n\geq 0}\|f^n(z)\|<\infty\right\}.
\]
A periodic point \(p\) of least period \(m_p\geq1\) is a saddle if the eigenvalues of \(D(f^{m_p})_p\) can be labeled \(\lambda_s,\lambda_u\) with
\[
0<|\lambda_s|<1<|\lambda_u|.
\]
Its unstable manifold, with its intrinsic complex manifold topology, is
\[
W^u(p)=\left\{z\in\C^2:f^{-km_p}(z)\longrightarrow p\text{ as }k\longrightarrow\infty\right\}.
