Let \(\Z\mathcal M\) be the free abelian group on the source's intended 3-manifold classes, and let \(F_d^{\mathrm{as}}\) and \(F_d^{\mathrm b}\) be its two specified descending finite-type filtrations. Conjecture 10.7 asserts, for every degree \(d\), that both
\[
 F_d^{\mathrm{as}}(\Z\mathcal M)/F_{d+1}^{\mathrm{as}}(\Z\mathcal M)
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 F_d^{\mathrm b}(\Z\mathcal M)/F_{d+1}^{\mathrm b}(\Z\mathcal M)
\]
are torsion free.

Precisely, for either filtration \(F^\star\), if \(x\in F_d^\star\) and \(m\) is a nonzero integer with \(mx\in F_{d+1}^\star\), then \(x\in F_{d+1}^\star\). This is the saturation of \(F_{d+1}^\star\) inside \(F_d^\star\). No assertion of finite generation or freeness is added.
