\section{Conjecture 4.20}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb R_{>0}$ for the multiplicative group of positive real numbers. Fix positive integers $m,n,k$ and integers $a_{ij\kappa}$, where $1\le i\le n$, $1\le j\le m$, $1\le\kappa\le k$. For $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\in(\mathbb R_{>0})^k$ define
\[
\Gamma(x)=\left\{\left(\prod_{j=1}^m\prod_{\kappa=1}^k x_\kappa^{a_{ij\kappa}s_j}\right)_{i=1}^n:(s_1,\ldots,s_m)\in\mathbb Z^m\right\}.
\]
Here the product uses the $\kappa$th coordinate $x_\kappa$, in agreement with the parametrized-torus construction surrounding the displayed formula. Density uses the usual real topology. Positive numbers $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_k$ are multiplicatively independent if $\prod_\kappa\gamma_\kappa^{b_\kappa}=1$ for integers $b_\kappa$ only when all $b_\kappa=0$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If there exists $x\in(\mathbb R_{>0})^k$ for which $\Gamma(x)$ is dense in $(\mathbb R_{>0})^n$, is $\Gamma(\gamma)$ dense in $(\mathbb R_{>0})^n$ for every multiplicatively independent tuple of positive algebraic numbers $\gamma=(\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_k)$? \sref{statement}{2}

\subsection{Short English statement}
If this integer-exponent construction yields a dense subgroup for some positive real parameters, must it also be dense for every multiplicatively independent positive algebraic parameter tuple?
