\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a discrete group and let \(\underline EG\) be a classifying \(G\)-CW complex for proper actions: its \(H\)-fixed point space is contractible for every finite subgroup \(H\leq G\) and empty for every infinite subgroup. Assume that \(G\) admits such a model with compact quotient \(G\backslash\underline EG\). This is a proper-action model, not a free \(EG\) unless its stabilizers are trivial. Let \(BG=EG/G\) for a contractible free \(G\)-space \(EG\).

Use \(\pi^0(BG)\) for unreduced degree-zero stable cohomotopy, conventionally
\[
\pi^0(BG)=[\Sigma^\infty BG_+,\mathbb S],
\]
where \(\mathbb S\) is the sphere spectrum. The usual homotopy-theoretic Burnside ring convention is \(A_{\mathrm{ho}}(G)=\pi_G^0(\underline EG)\). Its augmentation \(\epsilon:A_{\mathrm{ho}}(G)\to\Z\) is induced by forgetting to the trivial subgroup, with \(\underline EG\) nonequivariantly contractible, and \(I=\ker\epsilon\). The source does not reproduce these definitions or its equivariant stable-homotopy model, so they require source matching.

The proposed Segal-type completion assertion is
\[
\pi^0(BG)\cong\widehat{A_{\mathrm{ho}}(G)}_{I}
=\varprojlim_{r\geq1}A_{\mathrm{ho}}(G)/I^r
\]
for every group with the stated cocompact proper model. The completion is \(I\)-adic, not a single quotient or a completion at a separately chosen prime. The intended isomorphism is ordinarily the natural completion comparison and ring structure, but the extract gives no construction of that map; an abstract group isomorphism is a weaker claim and must not silently replace the intended completion theorem. The full ring \(\pi^0(BG)\), including its unit, is used rather than reduced stable cohomotopy.

\subsection{Short English statement}
For a discrete group with a cocompact classifying space for proper actions, ask whether stable cohomotopy of \(BG\) is the augmentation-ideal completion of its homotopy-theoretic Burnside ring. Proper and free classifying spaces are distinguished.
