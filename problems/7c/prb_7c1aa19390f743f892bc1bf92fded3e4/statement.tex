\section{Rigorous Construction of Conformal Field Theories}\label{30005927}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30005927 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A conformal field theory has correlation functions transforming covariantly under conformal maps. A rigorous path integral must define those correlations or measures rather than only formal integral symbols. Bootstrap consistency constrains spectra and operator-product coefficients through compatibility, including crossing relations.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
How can rigorous examples of conformal field theories be constructed, including rigorous path integrals and justification of the bootstrap hypothesis?

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}This programme contains several construction and justification questions. The source does not specify one target dimension, spectrum or universal existence claim.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct rigorous conformal field theories and justify their path-integral and bootstrap descriptions.
