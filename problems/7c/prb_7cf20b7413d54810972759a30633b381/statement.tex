\section{Optimal Bounds for Pairs of Commuting Integer Matrices}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


$M_n(\mathbb Z)$ is the set of $n\times n$ integer matrices, and $XY=YX$ means the two products agree. The notation $\ll_\varepsilon$ bounds the left side by a constant times the right side, with allowed dependence stated by the subscript.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $X\in M_n(\mathbb{Z})$, let $|X|$ be the maximum absolute value of its entries, and define $N(T)=\#\{(X,Y)\in M_n(\mathbb{Z})^2:|X|\leq T,\ |Y|\leq T,\ XY=YX\}$. Prove that, for every $\varepsilon>0$, $N(T)\ll_\varepsilon T^{n^2+1+\varepsilon}$ for arbitrary $n$. \sref{statement}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
The source fixes or varies $n$ according to its counting convention; dependence of the implicit constant on $n$ is not explicit in the supplied statement.
