\section{Research attempt: Primitive-direction counting in dimension two}

Put $B=\lfloor T\rfloor\geq1$. For every $n$, taking $X=aI_n$ and arbitrary $Y$ gives exactly $(2B+1)^{n^2+1}$ pairs in the box. Hence the conjectured exponent cannot be reduced.

For $n=2$, write
\[
X=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Y=\begin{pmatrix}e&f\\g&h\end{pmatrix},\qquad
v=(a-d,b,c),\quad w=(e-h,f,g).
\]
Direct multiplication shows that $XY=YX$ if and only if
\[
bg-cf=0,\quad (a-d)f-b(e-h)=0,\quad c(e-h)-(a-d)g=0.
\]
These are exactly the three two-coordinate minors of $v,w$, up to sign. If $v=0$, $X$ is scalar and this contributes $(2B+1)^5$ pairs.

If $v\ne0$, choose the unique primitive integer vector $u$ on its rational line whose first nonzero coordinate is positive. Then $v=ku$, $k\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$. Vanishing minors give $w=\ell u$ with rational $\ell$. Primitivity supplies integers $z_i$ with $\sum z_i u_i=1$, so $\ell=\sum z_iw_i$ is an integer. Let $H=\|u\|_\infty$. Every coordinate of $v,w$ has absolute value at most $2B$, so
\[
1\leq H\leq2B,\qquad 0<|k|\leq2B/H,\qquad |\ell|\leq2B/H.
\]
The number of possible $u$ of height $H$ is at most the number of all integer vectors on that cube shell,
\[
(2H+1)^3-(2H-1)^3=24H^2+2.
\]
There are at most $(4B/H)(4B/H+1)\leq24B^2/H^2$ pairs $(k,\ell)$, since $H\leq2B$. Once $v,w$ are fixed, choosing $a,e$ determines all other entries, so there are at most $(2B+1)^2$ matrices realizing them. Ignoring incompatible entry bounds only increases this upper count. Consequently
\[
N_2(T)\leq (2B+1)^5+(2B+1)^2
\sum_{H=1}^{2B}(24H^2+2)\frac{24B^2}{H^2}=O(B^5).
\]
Together with the scalar lower bound, this proves $N_2(T)=\Theta(T^5)$ for $T\geq1$.

\paragraph{Where the attempted extension stops.}
The essential reduction above was that the nonscalar centralizer modulo scalar matrices has just one rational direction. For a diagonal matrix with $n$ distinct diagonal entries, commutation forces an arbitrary diagonal matrix, whose quotient by scalars has dimension $n-1$. For $n\geq3$ that quotient has more than one direction. A naive replacement by $n-1$ independent height-bounded parameters does not retain the shell cancellation just proved; rational lattice covolumes and degenerations must be controlled. This precise missing estimate is not supplied by dimension counting.
