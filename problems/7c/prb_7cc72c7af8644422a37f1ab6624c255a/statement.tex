 1<m_2(F_1)\le m_2(F_2).

 m_2(F)=\max_{\substack{H\subseteq F\\v(H)\ge3}}

Subgraphs are not required to be induced. The denominator is positive for every term under the hypothesis.

Let $G_{n,p}$ have vertex set $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and independent edge probability $p$. Write $G\to(F_1,F_2)$ when every red/blue edge coloring of $G$ contains a red copy of $F_1$ or a blue copy of $F_2$. The source asks whether there is a constant $b>0$, depending on the fixed pair but not $n$, such that this Ramsey property has threshold
\[
 p=b\,n^{-1/m_2(F_1,F_2)}.
\]
