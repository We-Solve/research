\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
In the usual ADM setting, let \(n\geq 3\) and let \((M^n,g)\) be a smooth complete Riemannian manifold with scalar curvature \(\operatorname{Scal}_g\geq 0\). The supplied record does not specify the quantitative decay, differentiability, or integrability conditions intended by asymptotic flatness. These conditions must be fixed so that the ADM mass exists and is independent of the admissible asymptotic coordinates.

Writing \(S_r=\{x:|x|=r\}\), with Euclidean outward unit normal \(\nu\), Euclidean area element \(\dd A_\delta\), and \(\omega_{n-1}\) the area of the Euclidean unit sphere, the usual normalization is
\[
 m_{\mathrm{ADM}}(E)=\frac{1}{2(n-1)\omega_{n-1}}\lim_{r\to\infty}\int_{S_r}\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}\bigl(\partial_jg_{ij}-\partial_i g_{jj}\bigr)\nu^i\,\dd A_\delta.

\textbf{Question.} Does there exist such a complete manifold and an asymptotically flat end \(E\) with well-defined ADM mass satisfying
\[

\]
