\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a group \(G\), its profinite completion is the topological group
\[
\widehat G=\varprojlim_{\substack{N\trianglelefteq G\\[G:N]<\infty}}G/N.
\]
Here an isomorphism between profinite completions is understood to be an isomorphism of topological groups.

Let \(H\) be an arbitrary finitely generated free group. Consider either of the following constructions:
\begin{itemize}
\item Free groups \(F_1,F_2\) and injective homomorphisms \(\iota_j:H\hookrightarrow F_j\), giving the amalgamated product
\[
\Lambda=F_1*_{\iota_1(H)=\iota_2(H)}F_2.
\]
\item A free group \(F_1\) and injective homomorphisms \(\alpha,\beta:H\hookrightarrow F_1\), giving the HNN extension
\[
\Lambda=\left\langle F_1,t\ \middle|\ t^{-1}\alpha(h)t=\beta(h)\text{ for every }h\in H\right\rangle.
\]
\end{itemize}
Let \(\Gamma\) be a free group or a surface group. The supplied question asks whether, for every such choice,
\[
\widehat\Lambda\cong\widehat\Gamma
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Lambda\cong\Gamma.
\]
The conclusion concerns an isomorphism of abstract groups.
