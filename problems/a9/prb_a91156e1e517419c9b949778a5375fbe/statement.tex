\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 700289}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A group is finitely generated if it is generated by a finite subset. A group is polycyclic if it admits a finite subnormal series whose factors are cyclic, with finite cyclic factors allowed. Here, an abelian-by-polycyclic-by-finite group means a group \(G\) having normal subgroups \(A\trianglelefteq G\) and \(P\trianglelefteq G\) such that
\[
A\leq P,\qquad A\text{ is abelian},\qquad P/A\text{ is polycyclic},\qquad [G:P]<\infty.
\]
For a group \(M\), let \(M'=[M,M]\) be its commutator subgroup. The group \(M\) is metabelian if \(M'\) is abelian, equivalently \(M''=[M',M']=\{1\}\). Let \(\operatorname{Aut}(M)\) denote the group of all automorphisms of \(M\), under composition.

\textbf{Problem (B. A. F. Wehrfritz, Kourovka Notebook 5.5).} For every finitely generated abelian-by-polycyclic-by-finite group \(G\), does there exist a finitely generated metabelian group \(M\) and an injective group homomorphism
\[
\iota:G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(M)?
\]
The group \(M\) may depend on \(G\). The source mentions making properties such as residual finiteness transparent as motivation; this is not an additional conclusion requested in the embedding question. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that status has not been independently verified.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every finitely generated abelian-by-polycyclic-by-finite group be realized as a subgroup of the automorphism group of some finitely generated metabelian group? This is the embedding question attributed to B. A. F. Wehrfritz in Kourovka Notebook Problem 5.5.
