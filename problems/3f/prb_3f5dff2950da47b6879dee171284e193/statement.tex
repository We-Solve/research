\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701728}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k,n\in\Z_{>0}\). A group \(G\) satisfies \(C_{k,n}\) if
\[
\forall g_1,\ldots,g_k\in G,\qquad
\bigl|\langle g_1,\ldots,g_k\rangle\bigr|\le n.
\]
Thus every subgroup generated by at most \(k\) elements must be finite of order at most \(n\). Here the source's phrase ``\(k\)-generated'' is interpreted in its usual sense, allowing redundant generators. A group is \emph{locally finite} if every subgroup generated by finitely many elements is finite.

The following questions are attributed to A. Yu. Olshanskii, Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.70 (2022).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Does there exist an integer \(k\ge2\) such that, for every positive integer \(n\) and every group \(G\), condition \(C_{k,n}\) implies that \(G\) is locally finite?
\item[(b)] Is it true that, for every positive integer \(n\) and every group \(G\), condition \(C_{2,n}\) implies that \(G\) is locally finite?
\item[(c)] Find further pairs \((k,n)\in\Z_{>0}^{\,2}\) for which every group satisfying \(C_{k,n}\) is locally finite.
\end{enumerate}
The source presents these conditions as a strengthening of the Burnside restriction and supplies \((k,n)=(2,20)\) as an example: it asserts that every group satisfying \(C_{2,20}\) is metabelian and, under this condition, locally finite. Here \emph{metabelian} means that the commutator subgroup \(G'\) is abelian. This example is retained as a supplied assertion, without independent verification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
If all subgroups generated by at most \(k\) elements have order bounded by \(n\), must every finitely generated subgroup be finite? The problem asks whether some fixed \(k\ge2\) works for every \(n\), whether \(k=2\) works, and which further pairs \((k,n)\) guarantee this conclusion.
