\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a locally compact topological space, let \(V\subseteq C(X,\R)\) be a finite-dimensional real vector space with basis \(a_1,\ldots,a_m\), and let \(s=(s_1,\ldots,s_m)\in\R^m\). Here \(C(X,\R)\) denotes the space of continuous real-valued functions on \(X\). Define the linear functional \(L_s:V\to\R\) by
\[
L_s\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^m c_j a_j\right)=\sum_{j=1}^m c_j s_j.
\]
The requested characterization is a necessary and sufficient condition on \((X,V,s)\) for the existence of a positive Radon measure \(\mu\) on \(X\) such that
\[
a_j\in L^1(X,\mu),\qquad
s_j=\int_X a_j(x)\,\mathrm d\mu(x)
\quad\text{for every }j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}.
\]
Equivalently, every \(f\in V\) must be integrable and satisfy \(L_s(f)=\int_X f\,\mathrm d\mu\). Integrability is made explicit because the prescribed moments are finite real numbers; differences of two infinite integrals are not allowed. No assumption of finite total mass, compact support, polynomial functions, or membership of the constant function \(1\) in \(V\) is supplied.
