\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For $k\geq2$, let $K_n^{(k)}$ be the complete $k$-uniform hypergraph and let $r_k(n,n)$ be the least $N$ such that every red/blue coloring of $K_N^{(k)}$ contains a monochromatic $K_n^{(k)}$. Write $H\to(K_n^{(k)})_2$ for this arrowing property. Its size Ramsey number is
\[
 \widehat r_k(n,n)=\min\{|E(H)|:H\text{ is }k\text{-uniform and }H\to(K_n^{(k)})_2\}.
\]
The host may have any finite vertex set.

\textbf{Reported critical-edge structure.} Let $n\geq3$ and let $H$ be edge-minimal with $H\to(K_n^{(3)})_2$, meaning deletion of any edge destroys arrowing. For each $e\in E(H)$, fix one avoiding coloring of $H-e$. Coloring $e$ red and then blue gives witness vertex sets $R_e,B_e$, respectively, with
\[
 |R_e|=|B_e|=n,\qquad R_e\cap B_e=e.
\]
All triples on $R_e$ other than $e$ are red in the same fixed avoiding coloring, and all triples on $B_e$ other than $e$ are blue. This same-coloring requirement is part of the assertion.

The aid derives, for every supported vertex and every pair of positive codegree,
