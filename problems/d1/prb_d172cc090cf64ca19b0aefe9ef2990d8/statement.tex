\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(F_n\) be a free group of finite rank \(n\), and let \(\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\). Its free-by-cyclic mapping-torus group is
\[
 G_\varphi=F_n\rtimes_\varphi\Z
 =\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n,t\mid
 tx_it^{-1}=\varphi(x_i),\ 1\leq i\leq n\rangle.
\]
A group is automatic if it admits a finite generating set and a regular language of representatives with the appropriate synchronous fellow-traveler property for generator multiplication. This does not require biautomaticity or a unique normal form.
