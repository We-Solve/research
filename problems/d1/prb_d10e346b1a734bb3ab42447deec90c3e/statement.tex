\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 11000273}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$. For a finitely generated group $H$, its first Betti number is
\[
 b_1(H)=\dim_{\Q}H_1(H;\Q)=\dim_{\Q}(H/[H,H])\otimes_{\Z}\Q.
\]
Positive first Betti number is equivalent here to existence of an epimorphism $H\twoheadrightarrow\Z$; a nontrivial finite abelianization does not suffice.

\textbf{Question 2.5.} For each integer $n>3$, does there exist $H\le\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$ such that
\[
 [\operatorname{Aut}(F_n):H]<\infty,\qquad b_1(H)>0?
\]
The source range is exactly $n\ge4$. Its following rank-three example is motivation, not an answer in that range.

The source attributes the example to Alex Lubotzky, explained to the authors by Andrew Casson. View $F_3=\langle a,b,c\rangle$ as a three-petal rose fundamental group and take its two-sheeted cover with subgroup
\[
 K=\langle a,b,c^2,cac^{-1},cbc^{-1}\rangle.
\]
Let $G\le\operatorname{Aut}(F_3)$ stabilize $K$. Its action on $H_1(K;\Q)$ preserves the deck-involution eigenspaces. The negative eigenspace has basis
\[
 [a]-[cac^{-1}],\qquad[b]-[cbc^{-1}].
\]
The source reports that the induced integral action gives an epimorphism $G\twoheadrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_2(\Z)$. Pulling back a finite-index nonabelian free subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\Z)$ gives a finite-index subgroup of $\operatorname{Aut}(F_3)$ surjecting onto a nonabelian free group, hence onto $\Z$.

The source says analogous cover constructions for $F_n$ give finite-index subgroups mapping onto $\mathrm{GL}_m(\Z)$, but in these constructions $m\ge n-1$. For $n\ge4$ this does not supply the rank-two target used in the preceding free-group argument. No positive-Betti conclusion for the requested range follows from that example alone.

The registry summary claims an affirmative solved result for both automorphism and outer-automorphism groups but supplies no primary citation or exact higher-rank construction. This conflicts with the open/OPEN-TRIAGE metadata and is not adopted as a verified answer. The outer-automorphism assertion is also broader than the question posed here.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For every free-group rank at least four, ask whether an automorphism group has a finite-index subgroup surjecting onto $\Z$. The source gives a rank-three construction, while its unsupported research summary claims a broader affirmative answer.
