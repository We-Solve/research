\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 20001109}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For finite $A\subset\Z$, $|A|=n$, define its seven-corner completion set
\[
 S(A)=\{x+a+b+c:x,x+a,x+b,x+c,x+a+b,x+b+c,x+a+c\in A\},
\]
with integer $x,a,b,c$ and no original nonzero-step restriction.

\textbf{Problem 2.9 (T. Gowers).} If $|S(A)|<cn$, what structure of $A$ follows? The constant range and proposed structural conclusion are unspecified. B. Green comments that the answer is ``no'' as written, that $S$ could be dissociated, and that he, Tim and Terry had not found a satisfactory reformulation.

\textbf{Reported obstruction.} Taking all steps zero shows $A\subseteq S(A)$; thus the hypothesis is vacuous for nonempty $A$ if $c\leq1$. A Sidon set has no nontrivial additive quadruple, equivalently its unordered pair-sums are all distinct. The aid proves every Sidon $A$ satisfies $S(A)=A$. In particular
\[
 A_n=\{1,3,\ldots,3^{n-1}\},\qquad |S(A_n)|=n,\qquad
 |A_n+A_n|=n(n+1)/2.
\]
These sets are dissociated (no nonzero relation with coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$). For every $c>1$ they satisfy the printed small-completion condition while having maximal pair-sum growth. This refutes a general small-doubling/standard additive-structure inference, rather than every logically possible statement called ``structure.''

\textbf{Reported multiplicity repair.} Define $C^{\times}(A)$ to count all ordered quadruples $(x,a,b,c)$ satisfying the seven membership conditions with $abc\ne0$. Let $m_z(A)$ count those with missing vertex $z=x+a+b+c$, so $C^{\times}(A)=\sum_{z\in S(A)}m_z(A)$. These definitions disambiguate the summary's rendered $C_x$ notation. Put
\[
 E(A)=\#\{(u,v,w,t)\in A^4:u+v=w+t\}.
\]
The aid's corrected-energy bound is
\[
 C^{\times}(A)\leq n\big[E(A)-(2n^2-n)\big].
\]
The subtracted term counts trivial additive quadruples. The nonzero-step restriction is material; it is not part of the original definition of $S$.

If, for a fixed $\kappa>0$,
\[
 \frac1{|S(A)|}\sum_{z\in S(A)}m_z(A)\geq\kappa n^3,
\]
then $|S(A)|\geq n$ and the bound forces additive energy at least $\kappa n^3$. The aid invokes Balog--Szemer\'edi--Gowers to obtain $A'\subset A$ of size at least $c_\kappa n$ with $|A'-A'|\leq C_\kappa|A'|$. This conclusion needs large nonzero-step multiplicity, not merely few distinct missing vertices; exact constants are absent.

The report also computes, for integer $N\geq0$,
\[
 S(\{0,1,\ldots,N\})
 =[-\lfloor N/2\rfloor,\,\lfloor3N/2\rfloor]\cap\Z.
\]
The counterexample and corrected criterion do not supply the universally preferred reformulation sought in Green's comment.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Few distinct seven-corner completions do not force small doubling: Sidon sets already have completion set equal to themselves. The aid gives an explicit powers-of-three example, then shows that a stronger hypothesis on nonzero-step completion multiplicities forces high additive energy and a large small-difference subset. It also computes the exact interval case.
