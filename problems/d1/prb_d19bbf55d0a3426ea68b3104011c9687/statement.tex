\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\mathcal V$ be the symmetric monoidal model category intended by the preceding, omitted step, with its intended model structure on operads. For an operad $O$, let $\operatorname{Alg}_O(\mathcal V)$ be its algebra category and $U_O$ the forgetful functor to $\mathcal V$.

Hovey's Model categories item 5 asks for three statements:
\begin{enumerate}
\item For a cofibrant operad $O$, construct a model structure on $\operatorname{Alg}_O(\mathcal V)$ in which a map is a fibration or weak equivalence exactly when its underlying map under $U_O$ is one.
\item For a weak equivalence $\varphi:O\to P$ of cofibrant operads, show that extension and restriction of scalars
\[
 \varphi_!:\operatorname{Alg}_O(\mathcal V)
 \rightleftarrows\operatorname{Alg}_P(\mathcal V):\varphi^*
\]
form a Quillen equivalence, with extension the left adjoint.
\item Relate an $E_\infty$ operad to a cofibrant approximation $Q\operatorname{Comm}\to\operatorname{Comm}$ of the commutative-ring operad. Such an approximation has cofibrant source and is a weak equivalence in the specified operad model structure.
\end{enumerate}
The source considers the last assertion probably already known. It supplies no ambient category, transfer/admissibility, smallness, monoidal-unit, arity or operadic weak-equivalence hypotheses, so these are not unconditional theorems for arbitrary monoidal model categories.

Definitions of $E_\infty$ may impose aritywise contractibility and symmetric-group cofibrancy without full cofibrancy as an operad. Thus a cofibrant resolution can provide an $E_\infty$ model under suitable conventions, but not every object called $E_\infty$ is automatically cofibrant in every operad model structure. Nor does the second statement alone rectify $E_\infty$ algebras to strict commutative algebras when $\operatorname{Comm}$ is not cofibrant. The registry's solved label must be read with these absent hypotheses resolved.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Transfer model structures to algebras over cofibrant operads, compare them under weakly equivalent cofibrant operads, and interpret $E_\infty$ structures through cofibrant commutative-operad resolutions. The ambient hypotheses are essential.
