\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30000921}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $H(\C)$ be the space of entire functions with the topology of uniform convergence on compact sets, and let $D:H(\C)\to H(\C)$ be differentiation, $Df=f'$. Define
\[
 HC(D)=\{f\in H(\C):\overline{\{D^nf:n\in\N_0\}}=H(\C)\}.
\]
The question is whether
\[
 \forall f\in HC(D)\ \forall p\in\C[z]\text{ nonconstant}:\qquad
                   pf\in HC(D),
\]
where $(pf)(z)=p(z)f(z)$ is pointwise multiplication.

Equivalently, for every such $f,p$, every entire target $g$, every $R>0$, and every $\varepsilon>0$, must there exist $n\in\N_0$ such that
\[
 \sup_{|z|\le R}|(pf)^{(n)}(z)-g(z)|<\varepsilon\ ?
\]
The iterate may depend on all these data. The operator applied is differentiation of the product, not the polynomial differential operator $p(D)$ applied to $f$. No uniform iterate, growth bound, or genericity condition on $f$ is added to the universal question.

The supplied assessment reports a residual subclass of functions with the desired property. A residual set contains a countable intersection of open dense sets in the intended topology; that statement does not by itself settle all $f\in HC(D)$. The record does not say whether the reported subclass works for one fixed polynomial or simultaneously for all nonconstant polynomials, nor whether residuality is asserted in $H(\C)$ or in $HC(D)$. Those are partial-result qualifications requiring source review, not replacements for the full quantifiers above.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must multiplication by every nonconstant polynomial preserve every entire function with a dense differentiation orbit? The question concerns derivatives of the product and all hypercyclic functions, beyond a reported residual subclass.
