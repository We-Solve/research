The supplied question concerns a geometric construction in rational Witt bordism. Its precise category and orientation conventions are not stated. The following gives a conditional formulation under the usual convention of compact, oriented PL stratified pseudomanifolds without codimension-one strata; these conventions require confirmation from the source.

For such an \(n\)-dimensional stratified pseudomanifold \(X\), the rational Witt condition requires that, for every singular stratum of odd codimension \(2k+1\), with link \(L\) of dimension \(2k\),
\[
I^{\bar m}H_k(L;\Q)=0,
\qquad \bar m(c)=\left\lfloor\frac{c-2}{2}\right\rfloor.
\]
Here \(I^{\bar m}H_k\) denotes intersection homology with lower middle perversity. In the absence of codimension-one strata, the relevant integers satisfy \(k\geq 1\). An orientation means an orientation of the regular stratum.

Two closed \(n\)-dimensional rational Witt spaces \(X\) and \(Y\) are rational Witt bordant if there is a compact, oriented \((n+1)\)-dimensional stratified pseudomanifold \(W\) with collared boundary and an orientation-preserving identification
\[
\partial W\cong X\sqcup(-Y),
\]
where the interior satisfies the rational Witt condition and the stratification near the boundary is the product of the boundary stratification with a collar. The notation \(-Y\) means \(Y\) with reversed orientation. A space has at worst isolated singularities when its singular locus consists of isolated points; an empty singular locus is allowed.
