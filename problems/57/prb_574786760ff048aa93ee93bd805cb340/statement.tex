\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Work in the intended ZFC setting. For an infinite cardinal \(\kappa\), let
\[
 H(\kappa)=\{x:|\operatorname{tc}(\{x\})|<\kappa\},
\]
where \(\operatorname{tc}\) is transitive closure, and consider the structure \(\langle H(\omega_2),\in\rangle\). A parameter-free definable well-order here means a first-order formula \(\phi(x,y)\) in the membership language, with no parameters, whose interpretation in this structure is a well-order of its whole domain. A version with one real parameter permits a formula \(\psi(x,y,z)\) and a real \(z\subseteq\omega\); it does not permit arbitrary ordinal or other parameters from \(H(\omega_2)\).

Martin's Maximum (MM) is the forcing axiom asserting that, for every stationary-set-preserving partial order and every family of at most \(\omega_1\) dense subsets, there is a filter meeting them all. Stationary-set-preserving means that forcing with the order preserves every stationary subset of \(\omega_1\). The two questions are distinct.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Starting with a supercompact cardinal, can one find a forcing extension satisfying both full MM and the existence of a parameter-free definable well-order of \(\langle H(\omega_2),\in\rangle\)? Alternatively, does some other explicitly stated reasonable large-cardinal hypothesis suffice? The word ``reasonable'' gives no specific replacement hypothesis. In the requested extension \(W\), both \(\omega_2\) and \(H(\omega_2)\) are those of \(W\), and definability is over \(\langle H(\omega_2)^W,\in\rangle\), not merely over the ground model or all of \(W\).
\item Does MM alone imply existence of a well-order of \(H(\omega_2)\) definable over that same structure with at most one real parameter? In modelwise form, in every universe satisfying \(\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{MM}\), must there be a real \(z\) and a formula \(\psi\) such that
\[
 x<_{\psi,z}y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \langle H(\omega_2),\in\rangle\models\psi(x,y,z)
\]
is a well-order of \(H(\omega_2)\)? A formula ignoring \(z\) includes the parameter-free case. No additional large-cardinal assumption is appended to this implication question.
\end{enumerate}
Well-order means a genuine well-order in the ambient universe of the statement, not just a relation that some smaller structure fails to see as ill-founded. The source does not require a particular logical complexity or explicitly state whether one uniform defining formula must work in every MM model. That metamathematical uniformity issue is left open rather than silently strengthened.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can forcing produce full Martin's Maximum together with a parameter-free well-order definable inside \(H(\omega_2)\), starting from suitable large cardinals? Separately, does MM itself guarantee such an internal definable well-order using at most one real parameter? The forcing-extension and implication quantifiers, and the restriction on parameters, are kept distinct.
