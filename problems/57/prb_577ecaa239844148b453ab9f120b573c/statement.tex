\section{Conjecture 2.7 — Hall}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For positive integers $x,y$, compare the square $y^2$ and the cube $x^3$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is there an absolute constant $C>0$ such that every $x,y\ge1$ with $y^2\ne x^3$ satisfies \[|y^2-x^3|\ge C\max\{y^2,x^3\}^{1/6}?\] \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is every nonzero gap between a positive square and a positive cube at least a fixed constant times the sixth root of their larger value?
