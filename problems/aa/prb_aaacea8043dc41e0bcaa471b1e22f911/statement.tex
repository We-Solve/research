\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For positive integers \(m,n\), let \(A=(a_{ij})\in\R^{m\times n}\) have columns \(v_1,\ldots,v_n\in\R^m\) satisfying
\[
\|v_j\|_2=\left(\sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}^2\right)^{1/2}\leq 1\qquad(1\leq j\leq n).
\]
Define its signed coordinate discrepancy by
\[
\operatorname{disc}(A):=\min_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n}\|A\varepsilon\|_\infty
=\min_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n}\max_{1\leq i\leq m}\left|\sum_{j=1}^n\varepsilon_j a_{ij}\right|.
\]
The broader target in the supplied curated statement is the Koml\'os conjecture, in its standard real finite-dimensional formulation:
\[
\exists C\in[0,\infty)\ \ \forall m,n\geq1\ \ \forall A\in\R^{m\times n},\qquad
\left[\forall j,\ \sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}^2\leq1\right]
\Longrightarrow \operatorname{disc}(A)\leq C.
\]
Thus \(C\) must be independent of both dimensions and of the vectors. The real ambient field is an explicit conventional interpretation; the supplied excerpt does not specify it.
