\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Work under the usual tracial von Neumann algebra interpretation. A \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(N\) is a von Neumann algebra with center \(\C 1\), no nonzero minimal projections, and a faithful normal tracial state \(\tau\) normalized by \(\tau(1)=1\). Write \(\|x\|_{2,\tau}=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2}\). Haagerup's property and Property \((T)\) are understood as properties of finite von Neumann algebras; the supplied record does not specify their definitions or conventions.

Let \(\mathcal U\) be the ultrafilter on \(\N\) implicit in the source notation. Define the tracial ultrapower by
\[
N^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(\N,N)/\mathcal I_{\mathcal U},\qquad
\mathcal I_{\mathcal U}=\left\{(x_n)\in\ell^\infty(\N,N):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau}=0\right\}.
\]
For finite-dimensional unital von Neumann subalgebras \(M_n\subseteq N\), their tracial ultraproduct, equipped with the restricted traces, has the natural realization
\[
\prod_{n\to\mathcal U}M_n
=\left\{[(x_n)]_{\mathcal U}\in N^{\mathcal U}:x_n\in M_n\text{ for every }n,\ \sup_n\|x_n\|<\infty\right\}.
\]

The supplied question, under this unital interpretation, is: given a \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(N\) with Haagerup's property and a unital von Neumann subalgebra \(B\subseteq N^{\mathcal U}\) with Property \((T)\), must there exist a sequence \((M_n)_{n\in\N}\) of finite-dimensional unital von Neumann subalgebras of \(N\) such that
\[
B\subseteq\prod_{n\to\mathcal U}M_n\subseteq N^{\mathcal U}?
\]
Here the first inclusion concerns the given copy of \(B\) in \(N^{\mathcal U}\). Equivalently, one sequence \((M_n)\) must allow every \(b\in B\) to have a uniformly operator-norm-bounded representative \((x_n)\) with \(x_n\in M_n\) for every \(n\).

The source does not explicitly say that \(B\) is a von Neumann subalgebra, that the subalgebras share the ambient unit, or that \(\mathcal U\) is nonprincipal. These are unresolved conventions: the displayed formulation uses the usual unital von Neumann algebra reading, without adding separability assumptions or a nonprincipality hypothesis.
