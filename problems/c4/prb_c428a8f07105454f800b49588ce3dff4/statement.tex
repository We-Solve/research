\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be the class of measurable real-valued functions $f:\R\to\R$, with the precise measurable convention to be fixed. Define the shears
\[
 H_f(x,y)=(x,y+f(x)),\qquad V_f(x,y)=(x+f(y),y).
\]
They are bijections with $H_f^{-1}=H_{-f}$ and $V_f^{-1}=V_{-f}$. Put
\[
 S=\{H_f:f\in M\}\cup\{V_f:f\in M\},\qquad
 \mathcal G=\langle S\rangle.
\]
Here $\langle S\rangle$ means the subgroup consisting of finite compositions of the displayed maps; no topological closure is taken. Under the usual Lebesgue-measurable interpretation these are invertible Lebesgue-measure-preserving transformations of $\R^2$. Whether transformations are compared pointwise or modulo null sets is not specified in the source.

\textbf{Questions.} (1) Give an intrinsic description of the group $\mathcal G$ generated by the horizontal and vertical measurable shears. (2) Is this generation bounded: does there exist an integer $N\geq0$ such that
\[
 \forall T\in\mathcal G\quad
 \exists m\in\{0,\ldots,N\}\ \exists S_1,\ldots,S_m\in S:
 T=S_1\circ\cdots\circ S_m?
\]
The empty composition is the identity. Equivalently, if
\[
 \ell_S(T)=\min\{m\geq0:T=S_1\circ\cdots\circ S_m,\ S_i\in S\},
\]
then the second question asks whether $\sup_{T\in\mathcal G}\ell_S(T)<\infty$. The bound must be uniform over all generated transformations, not merely finite for each transformation separately.
