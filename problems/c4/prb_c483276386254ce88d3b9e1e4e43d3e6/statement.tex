\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Banach space is called superreflexive if it admits an equivalent uniformly convex norm. A norm \(\lVert\cdot\rVert_*\) is uniformly convex if, for every \(\varepsilon\in(0,2]\), there exists \(\delta>0\) such that
\[
\lVert x\rVert_*=\lVert y\rVert_*=1,\quad \lVert x-y\rVert_*\geq\varepsilon
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\left\lVert\frac{x+y}{2}\right\rVert_*\leq1-\delta.
\]

For an explicit conventional interpretation of an expander sequence, let \(G_n=(V_n,E_n)\), \(n\in\N\), be finite connected undirected simple graphs, with their shortest-path metrics \(d_n\). Require \(|V_n|\to\infty\), and require constants \(D<\infty\) and \(h>0\), independent of \(n\), such that every vertex has degree at most \(D\) and
\[
|\partial_{E_n}A|\geq h|A|
\qquad\text{whenever }A\subseteq V_n,\quad 0<|A|\leq |V_n|/2,
\]
where \(\partial_{E_n}A\) consists of edges with exactly one endpoint in \(A\). The supplied record does not specify its graph conventions or whether regularity is intended; this convention requires confirmation against the cited report.

The sequence \((G_n)\) equi-coarsely embeds into a Banach space \(Y\) if there exist maps \(f_n:V_n\to Y\) and nondecreasing functions \(\rho_-,\rho_+:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)\), independent of \(n\), with \(\lim_{t\to\infty}\rho_-(t)=\infty\), such that
\[
\rho_-(d_n(u,v))\leq\lVert f_n(u)-f_n(v)\rVert_Y
\leq\rho_+(d_n(u,v))
\qquad(n\in\N,\ u,v\in V_n).
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}

The second asks whether every separable superreflexive Banach sphere is uniformly homeomorphic to the sphere of \(\ell_2\). Its intended dimensional restriction is unresolved.
