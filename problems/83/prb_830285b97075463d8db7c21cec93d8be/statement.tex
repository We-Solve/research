\section{Threshold-Avoiding Control for Bank Capital Ratios}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


For a ratio process $R_t$, the first threshold-crossing time is $\tau=\inf\{t\geq0:R_t<0.08\}$. A control law chooses allowed actions using the information available up to the current time.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does there exist a control law for the stochastic model that prevents a bank's capital adequacy ratio from dropping below $0.08$? If not, determine a control law that postpones the first threshold crossing for as long as possible. \sref{statement}{2}
