\section{Seymour's r-graph conjecture}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $r\ge1$. A finite loopless multigraph $G$ is an $r$-graph if it is $r$-regular and $|\delta_G(X)|\ge r$ for every odd-cardinality vertex subset $X$, where $\delta_G(X)$ consists of edges with one endpoint in $X$. The chromatic index $\chi'(G)$ is the minimum number of edge colors with distinct colors on edges having a common endpoint.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does every $r$-graph satisfy $\chi'(G)\le r+1$? \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every regular multigraph satisfying the odd-cut condition be edge-colored with one extra color?
