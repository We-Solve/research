\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\in\Z\) satisfy \(n\geq 3\). For a nonnegative integer \(g\), let
\[
W_{g,1}=D^{2n}\mathbin{\#}_g(S^n\times S^n)
\]
denote the smooth manifold obtained by taking the connected sum of \(g\) copies of \(S^n\times S^n\) and removing the interior of a smooth \(2n\)-disk; conventionally \(W_{0,1}=D^{2n}\). Its boundary is diffeomorphic to \(S^{2n-1}\).

Use the customary convention that \(\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_{g,1})\) is the full group of smooth diffeomorphisms of \(W_{g,1}\) that agree with the identity on a neighborhood of the boundary, equipped with the \(C^\infty\) topology. Here \(BG\) denotes the classifying space of a topological group \(G\), and
\[
H^*(B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_{g,1});\Q)
=\bigoplus_{k\geq 0}H^k(B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_{g,1});\Q)
\]
is singular cohomology with its grading and cup product.

\textbf{Problem.} Determine this graded rational cohomology ring at small genus, particularly determine
\[
H^*\!\left(B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}\bigl((S^n\times S^n)\setminus\operatorname{int}(D^{2n})\bigr);\Q\right)
\qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq 3.
\]
The requested computation concerns all cohomological degrees. The source gives no numerical meaning to ``small genus,'' so the genus-one case is an explicit target while the extent of the remaining small-genus request is unresolved. The collar convention for boundary fixing and the \(C^\infty\) topology above are customary interpretations of notation that the supplied record does not explicitly define.
\subsection{Short English statement}
