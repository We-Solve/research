For an integer matrix $A$ and statistic vector $b$, the nonnegative integer fiber is $\mathcal F_b=\{x\in\mathbb N^d:Ax=b\}$. A Markov basis is a finite set of integer moves in $\ker A$ connecting every such fiber by moves that remain nonnegative. Its binomial generators form the toric ideal $I_A=\langle x^u-x^v:Au=Av\rangle$. For a two-way table, row sums, column sums, and a weighted sum of cells are linear statistics of its vectorized entries. A polyhedral cone is an intersection of finitely many homogeneous linear halfspaces. A statistical p-value is a tail probability under a specified null distribution.
