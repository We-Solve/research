\section{Global Stability and Asymptotics of Cosmological Spacetimes}\label{30006048}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30006048 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


The de Sitter and Kerr--de Sitter families are backgrounds with positive physical cosmological constant. Nonlinear stability asks whether all constraint-satisfying data sufficiently close in a specified norm have global developments remaining controlled and approaching a described background or asymptotic regime. The sign of the written Einstein equation depends on the curvature convention.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For $\Lambda>0$, do sufficiently small perturbations of de Sitter or Kerr–de Sitter initial data yield global solutions of $\operatorname{Ric}(g)+\Lambda g=0$, and can those solutions be described asymptotically?

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The report already describes established de Sitter and slowly rotating Kerr--de Sitter stability. The broad catalogue wording must not be read as declaring those known cases open; the unresolved parameter and asymptotic extensions need the source hypotheses.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine global stability and asymptotics of the stated cosmological backgrounds under sufficiently small admissible perturbations.
