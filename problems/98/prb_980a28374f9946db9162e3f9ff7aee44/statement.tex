\textbf{Question (Bownik and Rzeszotnik, Problem 10, 2019).}
For every admissible pair \((A,\Gamma)\) in the intended source setting, does every orthonormal wavelet \(\psi\) associated with \((A,\Gamma)\) satisfy Calder\'on's formula
\[
\sum_{j\in\Z}
\bigl|\widehat\psi((A^T)^j\xi)\bigr|^2=1
\qquad\text{for Lebesgue-almost every }\xi\in\R^n?
\]

\[
\lim_{N\to\infty}\sum_{j=-N}^{N}
\bigl|\widehat\psi((A^T)^j\xi)\bigr|^2.
