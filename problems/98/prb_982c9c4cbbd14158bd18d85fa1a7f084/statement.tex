\section{Open Problems in Geometric Nonlinear Quantum Dynamics}\label{30002310}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30002310 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
A Hilbert manifold is a differentiable manifold modeled on a Hilbert space. A densely defined vector field is defined on a dense domain and a flow solves its evolution equation. Stone\textquotesingle s theorem identifies strongly continuous unitary groups with self-adjoint generators in the linear case. For a composite quantum system, no signaling means that local operations on one subsystem do not alter another subsystem\textquotesingle s observable statistics. Dissipative hydrodynamics permits irreversible evolution, rather than only Hamiltonian transport.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Develop a theory of densely defined vector fields on Hilbert manifolds that generate flows, generalizing Stone’s theorem. What are the best special-relativistic and general-relativistic analogues of the diffeomorphism group’s role in quantum mechanics? Develop nonlinear gauge transformations that may leave Hilbert space, extend nonlinear quantum time evolutions to composite systems with or without the no-signaling property, and apply differential geometry to generalized, possibly dissipative, quantum hydrodynamics.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The target consists of all five linked construction questions in the supplied statement. It is a programme, not one uniquely specified conjecture; no particular nonlinear evolution or signaling axiom is imposed beyond what the question asks to investigate. The primary numbered list also begins with a request for a general theory of quasi-invariant measures on spaces of embeddings and immersions, omitted from the abbreviated catalogue paragraph; this is identified as an additional source research direction rather than silently discarded. A quasi-invariant measure and its transforms have the same null sets under the relevant diffeomorphism action.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Build geometric foundations for nonlinear quantum evolution, its relativistic symmetries, gauge changes, composites and dissipative hydrodynamics.
