\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\N\) denote the natural numbers and let \([\N]^{\aleph_0}\) denote their infinite subsets. For \(A,B\subseteq\N\), write \(A\subseteq^* B\) if \(A\setminus B\) is finite. A family \(\mathcal F\subseteq[\N]^{\aleph_0}\) is centered if every nonempty finite subfamily has infinite intersection. An infinite set \(P\subseteq\N\) is a pseudointersection of \(\mathcal F\) if \(P\subseteq^* A\) for every \(A\in\mathcal F\). Define
\[
\mathfrak p=\min\bigl\{|\mathcal F|:\mathcal F\subseteq[\N]^{\aleph_0}\text{ is centered and has no infinite pseudointersection}\bigr\}.
\]
A family \(\mathcal F\subseteq[\N]^{\aleph_0}\) is linearly refinable if there exists a simultaneous choice \((\widehat A)_{A\in\mathcal F}\) such that each \(\widehat A\) is an infinite subset of \(A\), and
\[
\forall A,B\in\mathcal F,\qquad
\widehat A\subseteq^*\widehat B\ \text{or}\ \widehat B\subseteq^*\widehat A.
\]
Define
\[
\mathfrak p^*=\min\bigl\{|\mathcal F|:\mathcal F\subseteq[\N]^{\aleph_0}\text{ is centered and is not linearly refinable}\bigr\}.
\]
