Let \(k\geq1\), let \(z_1,\ldots,z_k\in X\), and let \(\tau_1,\ldots,\tau_k>0\) satisfy

The question is whether, for every such collection, there exists an \(\omega\)-plurisubharmonic function \(\phi\) with

\qquad\text{whenever }0<\lVert w_j(x)\rVert<r_j.
\]
Here the norm is the Euclidean norm in the coordinate space; changing centered holomorphic coordinates changes the logarithm by a bounded term.
