\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $n\geq1$ and $1\leq k\leq n$ be integers, and use the centered unit cube $I^n=[-1/2,1/2]^n\subset\R^n$; this is a translation of $[0,1]^n$. Write $\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)$ for the $k$-dimensional linear subspaces, $P_L$ for orthogonal projection onto $L$, and $\operatorname{vol}_k$ for Euclidean $k$-volume. The corrected source question asks to determine all four extremal quantities
\[
 \max_{L\in\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)}\operatorname{vol}_k(I^n\cap L),\qquad
 \min_{L\in\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)}\operatorname{vol}_k(I^n\cap L),
\]
\[
 \max_{L\in\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)}\operatorname{vol}_k(P_LI^n),\qquad
 \min_{L\in\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)}\operatorname{vol}_k(P_LI^n).
\]
The first two are \emph{central} sections, not arbitrary affine sections. The original extraction explicitly states projection extrema, while the corrected\_verified statement also includes central sections; both levels of source wording are noted rather than silently discarding the corrected part.

The additional simplex quantity is
\[
 \gamma(n,k)=\max_{v_0,\ldots,v_k\in I^n}
 \operatorname{vol}_k\operatorname{conv}\{v_0,\ldots,v_k\}.
\]
For affinely independent vertices this volume is
\[
 \frac1{k!}\sqrt{\det\bigl(\langle v_i-v_0,v_j-v_0\rangle\bigr)_{1\leq i,j\leq k}}.
\]
Degenerate choices have zero $k$-volume. Here inscribed means that all vertices lie in the cube, hence the simplex is contained in it; no regular-simplex restriction is made. The source asks for the values for general $n,k$, without specifying whether classification of all equality configurations is also required.

The original text additionally mentions Minkowski's, Furtw\"angler's and Keller's conjectures in a cube-tiling context, but gives no formulas for the first two or a full statement of the third. It records the historical Keller status as true for $n\leq6$ and false for $n\geq8$, leaving $n=7$ unspecified there. The supplied assessment checked 21 August 2026 reports Keller's conjecture resolved in all dimensions, while saying the broader projection and inscribed-simplex extremal questions remain only partially settled. This later status report is not a solution of the extremal questions and does not supply a complete list of resolved $(n,k)$ cases or separately assess every central-section case.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine maximum and minimum volumes of central sections and orthogonal projections of a unit cube, and the largest volume of a $k$-simplex contained in it.
