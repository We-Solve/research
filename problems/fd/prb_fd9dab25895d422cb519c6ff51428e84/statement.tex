\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a positive integer \(n\), identify Euclidean space \(\mathbb E^n\) with \(\R^n\), equipped with the distance \(d(x,y)=\|x-y\|_2\). A red--blue coloring is an arbitrary function \(c:\R^n\to\{\mathrm{red},\mathrm{blue}\}\); no measurability or other regularity is required. For a subset \(K\subseteq\R^n\), a blue isometric copy of \(K\) means the image of a map \(f:K\to\R^n\) satisfying
\[
\|f(u)-f(v)\|_2=\|u-v\|_2\quad\text{for all }u,v\in K,
\qquad c(f(u))=\mathrm{blue}\quad\text{for all }u\in K.
\]
Such a map is injective. Determine all pairs \((n,K)\), with \(n\geq 1\) and \(K\subseteq\R^n\), for which
\[
\begin{aligned}
\forall c:\R^n\to\{\mathrm{red},\mathrm{blue}\},\qquad
&\bigl[\exists x,y\in\R^n:\ \|x-y\|_2=1
\ \land\ c(x)=c(y)=\mathrm{red}\bigr]\\
&\quad\lor\ \bigl[\exists f:K\to\R^n:\
(\forall u,v\in K,\ \|f(u)-f(v)\|_2=\|u-v\|_2)\\
&\hspace{110pt}\land\ (\forall u\in K,\ c(f(u))=\mathrm{blue})\bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
Equivalently, for each fixed ambient dimension, classify the subsets with this property.
