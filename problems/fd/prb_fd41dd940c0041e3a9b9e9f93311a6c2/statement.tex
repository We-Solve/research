For each integer \(n\geq 1\), let \(B_n=\{x\in\R^n:\|x\|\leq 1\}\), where \(\|\cdot\|\) is the Euclidean norm, and let \(v_n=\operatorname{vol}(B_n)\). A packing of unit balls has a center set \(C\subset\R^n\) satisfying \(\|c-c'\|\geq 2\) for distinct \(c,c'\in C\). Define its upper density by
\[
\overline\delta(C)=\limsup_{R\to\infty}\frac{v_n\,\#(C\cap[-R,R]^n)}{(2R)^n},
\qquad
\Delta_n=\sup_C\overline\delta(C).
\]
Here the supremum ranges over all such center sets.

To make the first question precise in a standard Cohn--Elkies formulation, use the Fourier transform convention
\[
\widehat f(y)=\int_{\R^n}f(x)e^{-2\pi i\langle x,y\rangle}\,\dd x.
\]
Call a real, even Schwartz function \(f:\R^n\to\R\) admissible if
\[
\widehat f(0)>0,\qquad \widehat f(y)\geq 0\quad(y\in\R^n),\qquad f(x)\leq 0\quad(\|x\|\geq 2).
\]
Such a function gives the Cohn--Elkies upper bound \(\Delta_n\leq v_n f(0)/\widehat f(0)\).
