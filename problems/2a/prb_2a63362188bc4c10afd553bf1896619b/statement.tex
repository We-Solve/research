\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathbb C_{\exp}=(\C;+ ,\cdot,0,1,\exp)\), where \(\exp(z)=e^z\) is the usual complex exponential. Let \(\mathbb B_{\exp}=(B;+ ,\cdot,0,1,\exp_B)\) denote the intended Zilber pseudo-exponential field. The requested assertion is
\[
\mathbb C_{\exp}\cong\mathbb B_{\exp}.
\]
Explicitly, this asks for a field isomorphism \(f:\C\to B\) such that
\[
\forall z\in\C,\qquad f(e^z)=\exp_B(f(z)).
\]
For this assertion, the intended model \(B\) must have cardinality \(|\C|=2^{\aleph_0}\). The supplied excerpt does not explicitly specify the cardinality of \(\mathbb B_{\exp}\); the continuum-sized model is the intended interpretation requiring confirmation.

\begin{enumerate}
\item The underlying field \(F\) is algebraically closed of characteristic zero, and \(\exp_F:(F,+)\to(F^\times,\cdot)\) is a surjective group homomorphism with
\[
\ker(\exp_F)=\tau\Z
\]
for some \(\tau\in F\) transcendental over \(\Q\). For the usual complex exponential, \(\tau=2\pi i\).
\item Schanuel's conjecture for \(\mathbb C_{\exp}\) is the assertion that, for every integer \(n\geq1\) and every tuple \(\bar z=(z_1,\ldots,z_n)\in\C^n\),
\[
\operatorname{trdeg}_{\Q}\Q(z_1,\ldots,z_n,e^{z_1},\ldots,e^{z_n})
\geq
\dim_{\Q}\operatorname{span}_{\Q}\{z_1,\ldots,z_n\}.
\]
\item To describe strong exponential-algebraic closedness, put
\[
G_n=\mathbb G_a^n\times\mathbb G_m^n,
\qquad G_n(\C)=\C^n\times(\C^\times)^n.
\]
Write coordinates as \((\bar x,\bar y)\). For an integer matrix \(M=(m_{ij})\in\operatorname{Mat}_{r\times n}(\Z)\), define
\[
[M](\bar x,\bar y)=\left(M\bar x,\left(\prod_{j=1}^n y_j^{m_{ij}}\right)_{i=1}^r\right).
\]
An irreducible algebraic subvariety \(V\subseteq G_n\) is \emph{rotund} if, for every \(r\geq1\) and every such \(M\),
\[
\dim\overline{[M](V)}^{\mathrm{Zar}}\geq\operatorname{rank}_{\Q}M.
\]
It is \emph{additively free} if no nonzero \(\bar m\in\Z^n\) makes \(\sum_jm_jx_j\) constant on \(V\), and \emph{multiplicatively free} if no nonzero \(\bar m\in\Z^n\) makes \(\prod_jy_j^{m_j}\) constant on \(V\). Here ``free'' is interpreted as satisfying both conditions.

With these conventions, the supplied SEAC clause asks that, for every \(n\geq1\), every subfield \(A\subseteq\C\) finitely generated over \(\Q\), and every free, rotund irreducible algebraic subvariety \(V\subseteq G_n\) defined over \(A\), there exist \(\bar z\in\C^n\) such that
\[
(\bar z,e^{\bar z})\in V,
\qquad
\operatorname{trdeg}_{A}A(\bar z,e^{\bar z})=\dim V,
\]
where \(e^{\bar z}=(e^{z_1},\ldots,e^{z_n})\). The transcendence-degree equality expresses genericity in \(V\) over \(A\). Dropping this equality gives the source's exponential-algebraic closedness condition, EAC.
