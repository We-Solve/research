\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 700533}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(p\) be a prime. A finite \(p\)-group is a finite group \(G\) with \(|G|=p^n\) for some integer \(n\geq 0\). For \(x\in G\), let \(|x|\) denote its order. Define the Hughes subgroup by
\[
H_p(G)=\bigl\langle x\in G : |x|\neq p\bigr\rangle,
\]
where \(\langle S\rangle\) is the subgroup generated by a subset \(S\subseteq G\).
The requested construction is a finite \(p\)-group \(G\) such that
\[
H_p(G)\neq\{1_G\}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
[G:H_p(G)]=p^3,
\]
where \([G:H_p(G)]\) is the number of left cosets of \(H_p(G)\) in \(G\).
The source does not specify whether such a construction is requested for at least one prime \(p\) or for every prescribed prime \(p\); the outer quantifier on \(p\) remains unresolved.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Construct a finite group of prime power order whose Hughes subgroup, generated by all elements whose order differs from \(p\), is nontrivial and has index exactly \(p^3\). The intended range of primes is unspecified.
