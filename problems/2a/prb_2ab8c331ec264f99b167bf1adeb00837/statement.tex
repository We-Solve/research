\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a finite set of real linear vector fields. In the usual homogeneous convention a field on $\R^r$ has the form $x\mapsto Ax$ for a real matrix $A$, and its full trajectory through $v$ is the image of $t\mapsto e^{tA}v$, $t\in\R$. Let $G$ be a family of trajectories of members of $M$. Write $\R_G$ for the real field expanded by these trajectory sets, subject to the source's convention about how families are named. Two such expansions are interdefinable over the real field if each set or function added in either expansion is definable in the other, with the same specified parameter convention.
