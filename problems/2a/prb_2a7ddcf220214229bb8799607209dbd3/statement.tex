This item is the truncated middle of a proof that Seymour--Sullivan rainbow-reachability Conjecture 6.1 implies Sullivan's neighborhood Conjecture 6.4, not an independent conjecture. Let $G$ be a finite loopless simple digraph on $n$ vertices, each arc carrying a nonempty subset of labels $[r]$. A rainbow path or cycle admits a distinct chosen label on every arc. Let $R_G^+(u)$ and $R_G^-(u)$ be endpoints reachable from $u$, and starts reaching $u$, by positive-length rainbow paths; exclude $u$ itself. Put $d_i^+(u)=\#\{v:i\text{ labels }u\to v\}$. The conditional reachability principle used in the aid is: if there is no rainbow directed cycle, some vertex satisfies $|R_G^+(u)|\ge\sum_i d_i^+(u)$. This is the Conjecture 6.1 hypothesis, not a proved assertion here.

For the safe completion, add a fresh label $r+1$ on every ordered pair
\[
(u,v),\qquad u\ne v,\quad v\notin R_G^-(u).
\]
Enlarge the host arc set when the arc was absent, and add the fresh label to an existing arc when necessary. The resulting $G'$ still has no rainbow directed cycle: such a cycle could use the fresh label at most once, leaving an old-label rainbow return path forbidden by the construction. The conditional principle applied to $G'$ therefore yields
\[
n-1\ge|R_{G'}^+(u)|\ge\sum_{i=1}^r d_i^+(u)+(n-1-|R_G^-(u)|),
\]
and hence the conditional dual inequality $|R_G^-(u)|\ge\sum_i d_i^+(u)$ for some $u$.

For the fragment's application, let $H$ be oriented, with no loops or digons, and place both labels $1,2$ on every arc. There is no rainbow cycle. Its old rainbow in-reachability consists exactly of first and second in-neighborhoods, where $N_2^-(u)$ means distance exactly two and is disjoint from $N^-(u)$. The corrected augmented degree identity is
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3d_i^+(u)=2|N^+(u)|+(n-1-|N^-(u)|-|N_2^-(u)|).
\]
The dual inequality gives a vertex with $|N^-(u)|+|N_2^-(u)|\ge2|N^+(u)|$. Applying this to the reversed digraph yields the Sullivan-2 form
\[
|N^+(u)|+|N_2^+(u)|\ge2|N^-(u)|

for some vertex, conditional on the reachability conjecture. The repair proves this implication within the stated conventions, not either conjecture unconditionally.
