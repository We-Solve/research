\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 700058}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a finitely presented group, and let
\[
d(G)=\min\{\lvert S\rvert:S\subseteq G,\ \langle S\rangle=G\}
\]
be its minimal number of generators. Let \(F\) be a free group of rank \(d(G)\), fix a surjective homomorphism \(\varphi:F\twoheadrightarrow G\), and put \(N=\ker\varphi\). Define
\[
[F,N]=\langle fnf^{-1}n^{-1}:f\in F,\ n\in N\rangle,
\qquad Q_{\varphi}=N/[F,N].
\]
Since \(N\) is normal in \(F\), the subgroup \([F,N]\) is normal in \(N\); moreover, \([N,N]\subseteq[F,N]\), so \(Q_{\varphi}\) is abelian.

\textbf{Problem (L. Ya. Kulikov, 1965; Kourovka Notebook 1.67).} For every such \(G\), \(F\), and fixed \(\varphi\), find a complete system of invariants of the group \(Q_{\varphi}\). Completeness means that, for any two instances satisfying these hypotheses, their invariants agree if and only if their quotient groups \(Q_{\varphi}\) are isomorphic as abstract groups.

The supplied statement does not specify the required form of these invariants or whether an effective procedure for obtaining them from presentation data is required. The fixed epimorphism is retained as part of the input; no independence of its choice is assumed.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Given a finitely presented group \(G\), map a free group with the smallest possible number of free generators onto \(G\), and let \(N\) be the kernel. Find invariants that completely determine the abstract isomorphism type of \(N/[F,N]\). The source leaves the required form and computational nature of the invariants unspecified.
