\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\mathbb F_2\) be the free group on two generators, and let \(A=L(\mathbb F_2)\) be its group von Neumann algebra with its canonical normalized trace \(\tau_A\). For tracial von Neumann algebras \(B\) and \(C\), the supplied record calls an embedding \(\iota:B\hookrightarrow C\) existential if there is an embedding \(j:C\hookrightarrow B^{\mathcal U}\) such that
\[
j\circ\iota=\Delta_B,
\]
where \(\Delta_B\) is the diagonal embedding into the tracial ultrapower. Under the usual tracial convention, these embeddings are unital, normal, injective, and trace preserving.

Here is a precise conditional formulation using standard conventions for the surrounding \(\mathrm{II}_1\)-factor setting. Fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter \(\mathcal U\) on \(\mathbb N\), and write
\[
A^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(\mathbb N,A)\big/\bigl\{(a_n):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|a_n\|_{2,\tau_A}=0\bigr\},
\qquad \Delta_A(a)=[(a,a,\ldots)].
\]
Does there exist a \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(N\) with separable predual and trace-preserving embeddings
\[
\iota:A\hookrightarrow N,
\qquad j:N\hookrightarrow A^{\mathcal U},
\qquad j\circ\iota=\Delta_A,
\]
such that there are no finite von Neumann algebras \((P,\tau_P)\) and \((Q,\tau_Q)\), equipped with faithful normal tracial states and satisfying \(P\ne\mathbb C1\) and \(Q\ne\mathbb C1\), for which
\[
(N,\tau_N)\cong(P,\tau_P)*(Q,\tau_Q)?
\]
In this conditional formulation, \(*\) denotes the reduced tracial free product over the scalars, and the isomorphism preserves the trace.

The conditional conventions above require confirmation: the supplied question says only that \(N\) is separable and is not isomorphic to a nontrivial free product. It does not explicitly require \(N\) to be a \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor, define separability, specify the embedding conventions, specify or quantify the ultrafilter, or identify the permitted free-product components. Thus this displayed formulation records a conventional interpretation rather than a uniquely determined reading of the source. The record also remarks that an analogous existential-embedding notion can be defined for group inclusions; the question displayed in the record concerns \(L(\mathbb F_2)\).
\subsection{Short English statement}
The supplied question asks whether \(L(\mathbb F_2)\) can embed existentially into a separable algebra \(N\) that admits no nontrivial free-product decomposition. Existentiality here means that \(N\) embeds back into an ultrapower of \(L(\mathbb F_2)\), with the composite equal to the diagonal embedding. The precise ambient category and meaning of a nontrivial free product require confirmation.
