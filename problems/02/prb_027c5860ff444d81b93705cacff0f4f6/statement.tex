\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Using the conventional complex-matrix setting, let $A,B\in M_n(\C)$, where $n\geq1$ and $A^*$ denotes the conjugate transpose of $A$. A matrix $A$ is normal if $AA^*=A^*A$. The literal source request is: characterize $\det(A+B)$ when both $A$ and $B$ are normal. It specifies neither prescribed input data for the characterization nor an inequality, inclusion, or other proposed conclusion. Consequently this request alone is not a yes/no conjecture.

\textbf{Separate candidate identified by the supplied review.} The dated triage says that the likely intended target is the Marcus--de Oliveira convex-hull conjecture. To state that candidate explicitly, list the eigenvalues of $A$ and $B$, with algebraic multiplicities, as $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n$ and $\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_n$. Let $S_n$ be the group of permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and set
\[
 z_\sigma=\prod_{i=1}^{n}(\alpha_i+\beta_{\sigma(i)})\qquad(\sigma\in S_n).
\]
For a finite set of complex numbers its convex hull is taken in the real plane $\C\cong\R^2$. The candidate assertion is that, for every $n\geq1$ and every pair of normal complex $n\times n$ matrices,
\[
 \det(A+B)\in\operatorname{conv}\{z_\sigma:\sigma\in S_n\}.
\]
Equivalently, there would exist real coefficients $t_\sigma\geq0$, depending on $A,B$, with $\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}t_\sigma=1$ and
\[
 \det(A+B)=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}t_\sigma
 \prod_{i=1}^{n}(\alpha_i+\beta_{\sigma(i)}).
\]
This candidate is supplied for source review, not silently substituted for the imported request.

For a prescribed-spectra characterization one would instead have to specify the desired information about the set
\[
 \left\{\det\!\left(\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n)
 +U\operatorname{diag}(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_n)U^*\right):
 U\in M_n(\C),\ U^*U=UU^*=I\right\}.
\]
The original request does not say whether it seeks this entire set, bounds on it, or the convex-hull inclusion above. In particular the candidate inclusion is not an assertion that the entire convex hull is attained.

The literature triage dated 2026-08-17 reports partial answers by Bebiano--Queiró for prescribed spectra, a further sufficient condition by Shitov in 2025, and a July 2026 variational treatment by Mulero-Martínez that describes the general normal case as open. Exact hypotheses of those partial results are not reproduced. The review therefore distinguishes the ill-specified literal record from the likely conjectural reconstruction. Its \emph{partially solved} classification and the registry's \emph{open} label are retained as supplied assessments rather than an independent status judgment.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The import asks only to characterize the determinant of a sum of normal matrices. Its review suggests the Marcus--de Oliveira conjecture: the determinant should lie in the convex hull of products formed by pairing the two eigenvalue lists. That precise candidate is stated separately because prescribed spectra, the convex hull, and the required conclusion are missing from the original wording.
