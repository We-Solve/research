\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 20001922}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a smooth Riemannian metric \(g\) on a closed manifold, let \(\ell_{\min}(g)\) be the least length of a nonconstant closed geodesic. \(\ell_{\min}\) is not restricted to noncontractible loops. Alvarez Paiva's Question \(8.8.2\) asks whether volume-one metrics on \(S^3\), or on \(S^2\times S^1\), can have all closed geodesics arbitrarily long; specifically, does every such metric have a closed geodesic of length at most \(10^{24}\)? The aid reports that the numerical superscript was flattened in extraction. A more general finite-universal-bound formulation is
\[
 \ell_{\min}(g)\leq C\operatorname{Vol}(g)^{1/3}
\]
for all metrics on the specified manifold, but existence of some \(C\) and the displayed particular value are distinct formulations.

The supplied special-family theorem is for \(S^1\times S^2\). Fix \(L>0\), a smooth positive \(L\)-periodic function \(f\), and any fixed smooth Riemannian metric \(h\) on \(S^2\). For
\[
 g=\dd t^2+f(t)^2h\quad\text{on }(\R/L\Z)\times S^2,
 \qquad V=\operatorname{Vol}(g),
\]
the aid states
\[
 \ell_{\min}(g)\leq\min\{L,\sqrt{32V/L}\}
             \leq32^{1/3}V^{1/3}.
\]
One candidate geodesic is the base circle of length \(L\); at a minimum of \(f\), the fiber is totally geodesic and supplies the other estimate using the indicated two-sphere systolic bound. This concerns a single fixed fiber metric scaled by \(f\), not an arbitrary metric or arbitrary varying fiber family.

If \(h\) is the unit round sphere metric, the sharper statement is
\[
 \ell_{\min}(g)\leq\min\{L,\sqrt{\pi V/L}\}
             \leq(\pi V)^{1/3}.
\]
The final constant is sharp in this round-fiber family. The aid's equality characterization is exactly the balanced product:
\[
 f(t)\equiv r>0,\qquad L=2\pi r.
\]
These inequalities give explicit positive answers inside the stated warped class but neither handle arbitrary \(S^2\times S^1\) metrics nor answer the \(S^3\) case. The general source question is reported open.

The appended Section \(8.9\) introduction is neighboring context about metrics all of whose geodesics are closed. It names Zoll's early examples and Besse's book, especially Chapter \(0\), Section D, says many listed problems were still open in that source, and stops before its next proposed question. Those historical remarks do not supply an additional proposition to formalize here.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The source seeks a universal volume-normalized short closed geodesic on the three-sphere or \(S^2\times S^1\). The aid proves explicit bounds for circle-warped \(S^2\times S^1\) metrics, with a sharp \(\pi^{1/3}\) constant and balanced-product equality for round fibers. Arbitrary metrics remain outside this partial result.
