\section{Uniqueness of Spherical Schwarzschild–Anti-de Sitter Spacetimes}\label{30006045}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30006045 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A static spacetime has a hypersurface-orthogonal timelike Killing field. Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter is the spherically symmetric black-hole family with negative cosmological constant. A uniqueness theorem compares static solutions subject to an asymptotic conformal boundary, horizon conditions and regularity assumptions.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Prove a uniqueness theorem for spherical Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter spacetimes among static Einstein spacetimes with negative cosmological constant.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The abbreviated source target does not specify all boundary and horizon hypotheses. They must be stated before claiming that all static Einstein metrics with negative cosmological constant are Schwarzschild--AdS.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Establish the appropriate uniqueness theorem for spherical Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black holes among static negatively curved Einstein spacetimes.
