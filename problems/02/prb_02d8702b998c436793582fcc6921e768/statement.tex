\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be an integer and let \(G\) be an amenable group, regarded as discrete. Here amenability means the existence of a left-invariant mean on the bounded real-valued functions on \(G\). Consider an action
\[
\rho\colon G\longrightarrow \operatorname{Diff}^{1}(\R^{2n}),
\]
where every group element acts by a \(C^1\) diffeomorphism; actions of higher regularity are also included.

One construction of its Euler class uses the associated bundle
\[
\pi\colon E_\rho=EG\times_G\R^{2n}\longrightarrow BG,
\]
where \(EG\) is a contractible free \(G\)-space and \(BG=EG/G\). The contractibility of the fibre permits a section \(s\colon BG\to E_\rho\). Pulling back the vertical tangent bundle gives a real vector bundle \(V_\rho=s^*T_\pi E_\rho\) of rank \(2n\); its isomorphism class is independent of the section. Let \(w_\rho\colon G\to\{\pm1\}\) record whether each diffeomorphism preserves or reverses orientation. For \(A=\Z\) or \(A=\R\), let \(A_{w_\rho}\) denote the local coefficient system on \(BG\) in which \(g\) acts by multiplication by \(w_\rho(g)\). The corresponding Euler class is
\[
e_A(\rho)=e_A(V_\rho)\in H^{2n}(BG;A_{w_\rho}).
\]
For an orientation-preserving action these coefficients are untwisted, so the class lies in \(H^{2n}(BG;A)\).
