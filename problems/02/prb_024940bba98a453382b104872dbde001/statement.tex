\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $(M^{2n},\omega)$ be a smooth symplectic manifold and let $H$ be a smooth real-valued Hamiltonian defined near a nonempty compact regular energy hypersurface $S\subset H^{-1}(e)$, so $\dd H\ne0$ along $S$. Here $S$ is understood as a hypersurface without boundary. Use the standard local meaning of contact type: there is a vector field $Y$ on a neighborhood of $S$ with $\mathcal L_Y\omega=\omega$ and $Y$ everywhere transverse to $S$. Then
\[
 \alpha=(\iota_Y\omega)|_S,\qquad \dd\alpha=\omega|_{TS},\qquad
 \alpha\wedge(\dd\alpha)^{n-1}\ne0.
\]
Assume, exactly as in the source,
\[
 H^1(S;\R)=0.
\]
The conjecture asks whether every such hypersurface carries a nonconstant periodic Hamiltonian trajectory. With the convention $\iota_{X_H}\omega=-\dd H$, the required conclusion is
\[
 \exists T>0\ \exists x:\R/T\Z\longrightarrow S:
 \qquad \dot x(t)=X_H(x(t)),\qquad x\text{ nonconstant}.
\]
Equivalently in the contact-type setting, the characteristic line field $\ker(\omega|_{TS})$ should have a closed orbit. Its parametrization can be expressed using the Reeb field of $\alpha$, defined by $\alpha(R_\alpha)=1$ and $\iota_{R_\alpha}\dd\alpha=0$; the Hamiltonian and Reeb parametrizations need not have the same period.

The source's dated assessment explicitly intends an arbitrary symplectic ambient manifold. No assumption that $M=\R^{2n}$, no global primitive of $\omega$, no displaceability condition, and no restriction to dimension three is inserted. The local contact-type convention and the usual regularity/no-boundary conventions should be checked against the report if a more restrictive meaning was intended.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The question asks whether every compact regular contact-type energy surface with vanishing first real cohomology has a nonconstant closed trajectory, in an arbitrary symplectic manifold. The supplied assessment distinguishes this general claim from the Euclidean case.
