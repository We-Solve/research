\section{Hadamard's maximal determinant problem}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For every positive integer $n$, let
\[D(n)=\max\{|\det A|:A\in\{1,-1\}^{n\times n}\}.
\]
The maximum exists because the set of admissible matrices is finite.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine $D(n)$ exactly for every $n\geq1$. \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find the largest absolute determinant among all square sign matrices of every order.
