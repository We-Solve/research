\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 2306064}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\alpha\in\R$ and let
\[
 f(z)=z+\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}a_nz^n
\]
be analytic on $\mathbb D$. Assume $f(z)f'(z)/z\ne0$, with its removable value $1$ at $z=0$. Define $M_\alpha$, the class of $\alpha$-convex functions, by
\[
 \operatorname{Re}\left[(1-\alpha)\frac{zf'(z)}{f(z)}
       +\alpha\left(1+\frac{zf''(z)}{f'(z)}\right)\right]>0
 \quad(z\in\mathbb D),
\]
where the expression has value $1$ at $0$. The source identifies $M_0=S^*$, the normalized starlike class, and $M_1=K$, the normalized convex class, and reports $M_\alpha\subset S^*$ for every real $\alpha$.

The coefficient criteria attributed to Clunie and Keogh are
\[
 \sum_{n=2}^{\infty}n|a_n|<1\ \Longrightarrow\ f\in M_0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{n=2}^{\infty}n^2|a_n|<1\ \Longrightarrow\ f\in M_1.
\]
\textbf{Question (S. Miller).} Find generalizations of these coefficient conditions sufficient for $f\in M_\alpha$, with the dependence on the real parameter $\alpha$ stated explicitly. The parameter is not restricted to $[0,1]$ or to nonnegative values in the supplied definition.

A natural coefficient-criterion format is to find explicit weights $w_n(\alpha)$ such that
\[
 \sum_{n=2}^{\infty}w_n(\alpha)|a_n|<1\ \Longrightarrow\ f\in M_\alpha,
\]
recovering weights $w_n(0)=n$ and $w_n(1)=n^2$ at the stated endpoints. This format explains the desired interpolation/generalization but is not an asserted formula or an additional requirement that every possible answer be a weighted-sum criterion. The source asks for sufficient conditions, without requiring an optimal necessary-and-sufficient characterization.

The supplied research summary records OPEN-TRIAGE, says the question was open in the 2018 edition, and reports no definitive resolution located. These are supplied status claims rather than independent verification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find coefficient-size conditions implying membership in the real-parameter class of $\alpha$-convex functions. They should generalize the known strict weighted-sum criteria with weights $n$ for starlikeness and $n^2$ for convexity, without silently restricting the parameter range.
