\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine which countable ordinals $\beta$ have the property that, if $\alpha=\omega^\beta$, then in any red/blue colouring of the edges of $K_\alpha$ there is either a red $K_\alpha$ or a blue $K_3$. \sref{statement}{1}

\paragraph{Scope and interpretation.}

The duplicated superscript marker in the source’s omega-power notation has been normalized to ordinal exponentiation. \sref{statement}{1}

\paragraph{Residual task reported by the archive.}

The embedded review (2026-08-17) records: Resolve the case where gamma is a sum of three indecomposable ordinals. \sref{statement}{1}

\subsection{Short English statement}

For which countable ordinal exponents does every two-colouring of pairs on the corresponding ordinal power yield a full-order-type red set or a blue triangle?
