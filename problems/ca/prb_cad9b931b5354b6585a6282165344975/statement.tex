A finite simple graph $G$ is diameter-two edge-critical if it has diameter exactly two and deleting any edge makes some pair have distance greater than two, including infinite distance. Must
\[
 e(G)\leq\lfloor |V(G)|^2/4\rfloor
\]
always hold? The live problem page was checked on 23 September 2026. It lists the problem as DECIDABLE and attributes the sufficiently-large-order result to F\"uredi. We address the remaining finite/general structural question. We do not have an effective threshold from that theorem and do not infer a full solution from small computations.
