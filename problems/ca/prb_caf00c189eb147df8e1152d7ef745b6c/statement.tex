For a nonzero meromorphic function $f$ on $\mathbb C$, the order $\operatorname{ord}_a(f)$ is the integer $m$ in a local expression $f(z)=(z-a)^m u(z)$ with $u$ holomorphic and $u(a)\ne0$; positive $m$ indicates a zero and negative $m$ a pole. The two legible requirements in the source are

\operatorname{ord}_a(f)>0\ \Longrightarrow\ \operatorname{ord}_a(f)\in2\mathbb Z_{>0},

\operatorname{ord}_a(f)<0\ \Longrightarrow\ -\operatorname{ord}_a(f)\in\{1,3,5,\ldots\}
\qquad(a\in\mathbb C).
\]
