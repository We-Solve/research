\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(R\) denote the separable hyperfinite type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor with normalized trace \(\tau_R\). Under the usual interpretation, \(\mathcal U\) is a nonprincipal ultrafilter on \(\N\), and the tracial ultrapower is
\[
R^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(\N,R)\big/\{(x_n):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau_R}=0\},\qquad
\|x\|_{2,\tau_R}=\tau_R(x^*x)^{1/2}.
\]
Here separability of a von Neumann algebra means separability of its predual. A type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor is a von Neumann algebra with center \(\C1\), a faithful normal normalized trace, and no nonzero minimal projections. A separable type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor is Connes embeddable if it admits a unital trace-preserving embedding into a tracial ultrapower of \(R\). Embeddings here are injective unital trace-preserving \(*\)-homomorphisms.
