\section*{Mechanistic model selection}
\label{prob:045}
\label{rank:218}
\par\smallskip

\noindent\textbf{Domain.} Mechanistic model discrimination. \quad \textbf{Retained status/source context.} Open intervention-based biological discrimination programme with standard solved statistical components. The supplied primary study demonstrates model-specific selection; indistinguishable models cannot be uniquely recovered simply by collecting more repetitions of the same outputs.
\paragraph{Definitions and assumptions.} Let $M_1,\ldots,M_K$ be specified cell-invasion or biochemical mechanisms, each with bounded parameter set $\Theta_k$ and observation law $P_{k,\theta}^D$ under experiment $D$. Designs include feasible perturbations and time/space readouts with known measurement kernels. For each admissible design $D$, all compared laws are probability measures on a common measurable observation space $(\mathcal Y_D,\mathcal F_D)$. On the tagged parameter space $\mathcal X=\bigsqcup_{k=1}^K(\{k\}\times\Theta_k)$, define $(k,\theta)\sim(l,\eta)$ if $P_{k,\theta}^D=P_{l,\eta}^D$ under every admissible design $D$. The same tagged parameter values are used throughout this comparison across designs. The requested equivalence classes are those of $\mathcal X/\sim$, consistently with the binding identification convention below; overlap of model families is not an equivalence relation on model labels. Mechanistic selection uses independently collected validation data, while parameter fitting uses training data. Complexity penalties and the treatment of model discrepancy are fixed before comparison.
\paragraph{Problem statement.} Characterize equivalence classes and find designs that separate distinguishable mechanisms:
\[
\Delta_{kl}(D)=\inf_{\theta\in\Theta_k,\eta\in\Theta_l}
\operatorname{TV}(P_{k,\theta}^D,P_{l,\eta}^D),
\qquad\inf_{k\ne l}\Delta_{kl}(D)>\delta.
\]
Total variation $\operatorname{TV}$ compares entire observable laws, and overlapping model classes may force $\Delta=0$. Here $\delta>0$ is a prescribed margin fixed before comparison. A nonvacuous comparison has finite $K\ge2$, nonempty parameter sets and a nonempty admissible design menu. Under the total-variation convention below, the strict displayed inequality is infeasible if $\delta\ge1$. The display requires one common design satisfying the margin for every parameter pair across every distinct pair of model labels; it is stronger than the existence of a separating design for each inequivalent fixed pair. Characterization of $\mathcal X/\sim$ and this uniform separation requirement are distinct deliverables. The practical question is what experiments can discriminate specific competing mechanisms.
\paragraph{Additional binding assumptions and resolution criterion.} A positive uniform separation is not implied merely by distinct mechanisms. Nested or intersecting observable-law families make the displayed infimum zero; even disjoint families can have touching closures. Specify margin-separated compact subfamilies for a uniform test, or formulate pointwise discrimination and sample-size-dependent power. Model rejection remains a possible outcome when every candidate is misspecified, not a guaranteed detectable consequence of misspecification. Any claimed rejection guarantee requires a declared goodness-of-fit rule, departures and error control. Neither boundedness nor compactness alone establishes positive separation, and positive pairwise total-variation separation alone is not a uniform-testing theorem for arbitrary composite families. Any use of topology, continuity or additional testing assumptions must be stated and justified for the proposed result. The choice permitted here between the displayed uniform target and a pointwise formulation remains to be declared for a concrete instance; neither may silently replace the other. The instance must also declare whether a design is one experiment, a collection or an adaptive policy, what information may be used to choose it, and whether $P_{k,\theta}^D$ describes one replicate or the full dataset, including its dependence. For statistical selection or rejection claims, declare sample size, data roles, the decision rule, pointwise or worst-case error requirements, and conditioning on training and design selection. Data used for selection are not automatically untouched final evaluation data. Held-out predictive scoring, likelihood-ratio testing and recovery of a biological mechanism have distinct inferential purposes and do not automatically certify one another.

\paragraph{Alternative formulations.} (i) For nested models with regular identifiable parameters, use held-out likelihood or established likelihood-ratio procedures, acknowledging boundary cases. (ii) For nonnested mechanisms sharing baseline outputs, select perturbations maximizing a declared divergence or prediction difference. Good baseline fit cannot establish equivalence under interventions.
\paragraph{Obstructions and partial progress.} The cell-invasion study relates identifiability to model complexity and selection in specific PDE families. More flexible models may fit better while rendering parameters weakly identified. Uniform separation is impossible when model classes intersect or measurement coarsening erases their differences; restrict the parameter region or report shared explanations. This entry concerns distinguishing mechanism classes, unlike rate recovery within one model in \href{https://we-solve.github.io/problems/structural-parameter-identifiability-657575ff/}{Problem~43}.

\subsection*{Binding conventions for a concrete instance}
\paragraph{Objects, quantifiers and non-vacuity.}
An instance declares measurable state, input, output and parameter spaces; a fixed candidate family; admissible initial laws, interventions and environments; a finite horizon or specified asymptotic regime; observation and sampling laws; and performance metrics, positive normalization scales and positive tolerances. These are chosen before fitting or testing, not adjusted after seeing failures. Shared parameters must genuinely be shared across the declared validation domain. Finite dimension alone does not restrict arbitrary real-number encodings: specify a finite library, bounded description length, or regularity/complexity bounds. Exclude constant or no-signal solutions through a fixed informative-output or baseline-improvement requirement where appropriate. A universally quantified result cannot be established by choosing one trivial favorable instance.

\paragraph{Probability laws and data.}
Every expectation or probability refers to the specified stochastic law, including initial uncertainty, process noise, observation noise and sampling as appropriate. A deterministic equation is not a probability model without a declared random input or initial law. ``Observed'' targets in population-level formulas mean population observables $g_j^*$, not noisy estimates asserted to be exact. Let $\widetilde g_j$ be independent validation estimates and suppose
\[
\mathbb{P}\{\lvert\widetilde g_j-g_j^*\rvert\le r_j\text{ for every }j\}\ge1-\alpha.
\]
Then an uncertainty-aware sufficient certification rule for positive target tolerances $\varepsilon_j$ is
\[
\max_j\frac{|\widehat g_j-\widetilde g_j|+r_j}{\varepsilon_j}\le1.
\]
On that simultaneous confidence event it implies every population error is at most its tolerance, by the triangle inequality. Vector/norm versions use corresponding valid simultaneous bounds. Training uncertainty must also be included when the claim averages over repeated training data. Evaluation on finitely many conditions does not establish a continuous-domain supremum without covering and regularity bounds.

\paragraph{Dynamics and well-posedness.}
For ODEs and SDEs give coefficient domains, initial laws and existence/uniqueness conditions in the intended solution sense, or keep the entire admitted solution set. For stochastic jump models specify nonnegative off-diagonal rates, diagonal normalization, rate orientation and non-explosion. For PDEs specify spatial domains, boundary/initial conditions, conservation or source terms, and a weak or classical solution class. Moving-domain concentration equations include dilution/transport terms. A stationary law is used only for a time-homogeneous augmented model under existence assumptions; a stationary approximation to a driven system must be justified. Absorbing extinction states are retained. Survival-conditioned, quasi-stationary and unconditional laws are distinct and must not be substituted for one another.

\paragraph{Identification and causal meaning.}
Two parameter values are observationally equivalent when they induce the same full distribution under all allowed observation/intervention designs, not merely the same likelihood value for one sample. A target is identified only if constant on the equivalence class. Gauge symmetries, label switching and reference conventions are quotiented or fixed. Finite-data compatible sets use a declared confidence, likelihood or discrepancy rule; an empty set signals model rejection. Bayesian credible sets are not automatically confidence sets. A prior or regularizer does not create data identification. Bounds intended to resolve a question must be informative at a predeclared width or decision threshold.

A causal contrast uses well-defined intervention versions and a stated causal model. Random assignment, or explicit consistency, overlap and exchangeability assumptions, is required for causal interpretation from observations. Interference and time-varying confounding are represented when present. Fixing a mediator by intervention differs from conditioning on its observed value. Marginal potential-outcome laws do not determine individual cross-world effects or an arbitrary joint coupling. Ethically admissible designs, rather than unconstrained interventions, define the experimental menu.

\paragraph{Metrics, units, optimization and limits.}
Specify the sampling or reference measure for densities and integrals, the log base, and dimensionless arguments of logarithms. Total variation is $\sup_A|P(A)-Q(A)|$; Wasserstein distances require the declared ground metric and appropriate moment conditions; KL divergence uses its stated absolute-continuity convention. All normed readouts are scaled consistently, and error probabilities use $0<\alpha<1$. A ratio has a positive denominator or an explicit undefined/zero convention. Thermodynamic entropy production is nonnegative in expectation under the relevant hypotheses; individual stochastic realizations need not be. An infimum is not an attained minimum without compactness/coercivity and continuity or another existence argument. Fix the order of large-population, long-time and vanishing-mutation limits. Stability claims declare perturbation space and symmetries.
