\section{A normal-symbol criterion for lower-dimensional modular reduction}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and notation.}A modular or automorphic form transforms according to a specified group and weight on a parameter domain. Moduli of Calabi--Yau threefolds parametrize their geometric structures. Reduction to lower-dimensional forms may mean restriction, factorization or descent under a map of moduli spaces, and these are different operations.
