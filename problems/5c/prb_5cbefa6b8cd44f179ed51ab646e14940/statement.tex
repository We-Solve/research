\section{Mass-stationarity of diffuse random measures via allocations}\label{9900008}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 9900008 · Probability}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $G$ be a locally compact second countable Abelian group, written additively, with Haar measure $\lambda$. Let $X$ be a random element of a measurable space with a jointly measurable $G$-action $\theta_s$, and let $\xi$ be a random locally finite Borel measure on $G$, with $0$ in its support almost surely. Define $\theta_s\xi(A)=\xi(A+s)$ and $\theta_s(X,\xi)=(\theta_sX,\theta_s\xi)$. For each relatively compact Borel $C$ with $0<\lambda(C)<\infty$, let $U_C$ be Haar-uniform on $C$, independent of $(X,\xi)$, and conditionally sample $V_C$ from $\xi$ normalized on $C-U_C$:
\[\mathbb P(V_C\in A\mid X,\xi,U_C)=\frac{\xi(A\cap(C-U_C))}{\xi(C-U_C)}.\]
Mass-stationarity means that these conditional laws are well-defined and
\[(\theta_{V_C}(X,\xi),U_C+V_C)\stackrel d=((X,\xi),U_C)\]
for all such $C$. Assume $\xi$ is diffuse: $\xi(\{s\})=0$ for every $s\in G$ almost surely. For a measurable map $\pi$ from pairs $(X,\xi)$ to $G$, its equivariant allocation is
\[\tau_{(X,\xi)}(s)=s+\pi(\theta_s(X,\xi)).\]
It preserves $\xi$ if $\xi(\tau^{-1}(A))=\xi(A)$ for every Borel $A$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is mass-stationarity of $(X,\xi)$ equivalent to
\[\theta_{\pi(X,\xi)}(X,\xi)\stackrel d=(X,\xi)\]
for every measurable $\pi$ inducing a $\xi$-preserving allocation? Here allocations depend only on $(X,\xi)$; no independent stationary random field is adjoined. \sref{9900008}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
For diffuse mass on a general locally compact Abelian group, do all preserving allocation shifts characterize a typical location in the mass?
