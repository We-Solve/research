\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(N\) be a positive integer and let
\[
S^2=\{x\in\R^3:\lVert x\rVert_2=1\},\qquad
\mathcal C_N=\{(x_1,\ldots,x_N)\in(S^2)^N:x_i\ne x_j\text{ whenever }i\ne j\}.
\]
For \(X=(x_1,\ldots,x_N)\in\mathcal C_N\), define the Thomson energy by
\[
E_{\mathrm T}(X)=\sum_{1\le i<j\le N}\frac{1}{\lVert x_i-x_j\rVert_2}.
\]
Distances are Euclidean chordal distances on the unit sphere. For \(N=1\), the sum is empty and equals zero.

\textbf{Problem.} Develop rigorous certificates establishing global optimality of candidate Thomson configurations. More precisely, given \(N\) and a candidate \(X^*\in\mathcal C_N\), seek a certificate proving
\[
\forall X\in\mathcal C_N,\qquad E_{\mathrm T}(X)\ge E_{\mathrm T}(X^*).
\]
The requested certificate must establish the inequality over the entire configuration space, rather than merely local minimality or numerical evidence. The question does not assert that every candidate is optimal, and the supplied record specifies neither a certificate format nor a terminating certification algorithm.

The source also requests corresponding certificates for Riesz-energy variants. If an inverse-power variant with specified exponent \(s\) is intended, its unnormalized energy has the form
\[
E_s(X)=\sum_{1\le i<j\le N}\lVert x_i-x_j\rVert_2^{-s},
\]
and the corresponding target is
\[
\forall X\in\mathcal C_N,\qquad E_s(X)\ge E_s(X^*).
\]
However, the supplied record does not specify the permitted exponents, normalization, treatment of logarithmic limiting energies, or whether any sphere other than \(S^2\) is intended. Thus this inverse-power formula describes a conditional interpretation; the full scope of the Riesz extension remains unresolved.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For a proposed configuration of \(N\) distinct points on the unit sphere, develop a rigorous certificate that its total reciprocal-distance energy is no larger than that of any competing configuration. The source also asks for Riesz-energy analogues, but leaves their precise parameter range and conventions unspecified.
