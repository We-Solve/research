\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\) be a field, let \(X\) be a smooth projective \(k\)-variety of dimension \(n\), and fix a very ample line bundle \(L\) on \(X\). These parameters make explicit data that the supplied statement leaves unspecified. For a vector bundle \(E\), write \(E(-j)=E\otimes L^{\otimes(-j)}\). A vector bundle \(E\) of positive rank is called Ulrich with respect to \(L\) if
\[
H^i\bigl(X,E(-j)\bigr)=0
\qquad\text{for every }0\leq i\leq n\text{ and every }1\leq j\leq n.
\]
The precise existence question for a fixed pair \((X,L)\) is whether
\[
\exists\,E\text{ a vector bundle on }X,\quad \operatorname{rank}(E)>0,
\quad H^i\bigl(X,E\otimes L^{\otimes(-j)}\bigr)=0
\quad(0\leq i\leq n,\ 1\leq j\leq n).
\]
The supplied record asks for existence on every smooth projective variety. It does not specify the base field, restrictions on its characteristic, or whether the polarization is prescribed, universally quantified, or allowed to depend on \(X\). Consequently, the displayed question is a relative formulation; the scope of the intended universal conjecture remains unresolved.

For a coherent sheaf \(\mathcal F\) on \(X\), its cohomology table relative to \(L\) has entries
\[
\gamma_L(\mathcal F)_{i,t}
=\dim_k H^i\bigl(X,\mathcal F\otimes L^{\otimes t}\bigr),
\qquad 0\leq i\leq n,\quad t\in\Z.
\]
The source additionally describes the existence question as equivalent to equality of the cone of cohomology tables of \(X\) with that of \(\PP^n\). With the projective-space polarization interpreted as \(\mathcal O_{\PP^n_k}(1)\), its asserted equality would read
\[
C(X,L)=C\bigl(\PP^n_k,\mathcal O_{\PP^n_k}(1)\bigr).
\]
However, the record does not specify which sheaves generate these cones, their coefficient field, or whether a closure is taken. This equality and its claimed equivalence are retained as source assertions with unresolved definitions, rather than asserted here as a verified equivalence.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The question asks whether every smooth projective variety carries a positive-rank vector bundle whose cohomology vanishes in all degrees for twists \(-1,\ldots,-n\) relative to a chosen very ample line bundle. The base field and the quantification over that line bundle are unspecified. The source also claims an equivalent equality of cohomology-table cones, but does not define those cones precisely.
