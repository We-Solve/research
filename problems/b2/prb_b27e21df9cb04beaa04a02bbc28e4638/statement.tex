\textbf{Reported constant-coefficient periodic result.} On \(\mathbb T=\R/2\pi\Z\), consider
\[
 u_t+A u_x+D u=\epsilon B u_{xx},\qquad u(0)=u_0\in H^2(\mathbb T;\R^m).
\]
Let \(A,D,B\) be constant real matrices, and suppose a symmetric positive-definite \(S\) and \(\gamma>0\) satisfy
\[
 SA=A^TS,\qquad SD+D^TS\geq2\gamma S,\qquad
 SB+B^TS\geq0.
\]
Use \(\|u\|_{L^2_S}^2=\int_{\mathbb T}u^TSu\,\mathrm dx\),
\( |u_0|_{H^2_S}=\|\partial_x^2u_0\|_{L^2_S}\), and
\[
 K_S(C)=\|S^{1/2}CS^{-1/2}\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
The aid reports the uniform vanishing-viscosity estimate against the solution \(u^0\) with \(\epsilon=0\) and the same datum:
\[
 \|u^{\epsilon,B}(t)-u^0(t)\|_{L^2_S}
 \leq\epsilon K_S(B)t e^{-\gamma t}|u_0|_{H^2_S},
 \qquad t\geq0,
\]
therefore its supremum over all time is at most
\(\epsilon K_S(B)|u_0|_{H^2_S}/(e\gamma)\).

More generally, if both \(B_1,B_2\) satisfy the same entropy-positivity condition, then the reported comparison is
\[
 \|u^{\epsilon,B_1}(t)-u^{\epsilon,B_2}(t)\|_{L^2_S}
 \leq\epsilon K_S(B_1-B_2)t e^{-\gamma t}|u_0|_{H^2_S},
\]
with the corresponding uniform bound obtained by replacing \(te^{-\gamma t}\) by \(1/(e\gamma)\). The viscosity matrices may be singular and need not commute with \(A\); the common symmetrizer and full source damping remain hypotheses. This is a linear periodic estimate, not a nonlinear small-total-variation theorem for arbitrary physical viscosities.

\textbf{Reported diagnostic limitations.} The aid includes anti-diffusion and uncoupled-mode examples. These show why entropy positivity and a source of damping cannot simply be dropped from this estimate. Kawashima-type coupling may provide a different damping mechanism, but no general coupled replacement theorem or its hypotheses is supplied.
