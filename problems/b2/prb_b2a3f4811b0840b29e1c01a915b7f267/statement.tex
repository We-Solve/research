\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(N\) is a von Neumann algebra with center \(\C 1\), no nonzero minimal projections, and a faithful normal tracial state \(\tau\) normalized by \(\tau(1)=1\). Interpret separability as separability of the predual \(N_*\), and nonamenability as nonamenability of \(N\) as a von Neumann algebra.

For the conventional interpretation of the ultrapower notation, fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter \(\mathcal U\) on \(\N\). Write \(\|x\|_{2,\tau}=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2}\), and define the tracial ultrapower by
\[
N^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(\N,N)\big/\mathcal I_{\mathcal U},\qquad
\mathcal I_{\mathcal U}=\left\{(x_n)\in\ell^\infty(\N,N):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau}=0\right\}.
\]
Here \(\ell^\infty(\N,N)\) consists of operator-norm-bounded sequences, and the ultrapower trace is \(\tau_{\mathcal U}([(x_n)])=\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\tau(x_n)\). Interpret an embedding \(\pi:N\to N^{\mathcal U}\) as a unital, normal, injective, trace-preserving \(*\)-homomorphism. Let \(\mathcal U(N^{\mathcal U})\) denote the unitary group of \(N^{\mathcal U}\).

Under these conventions, the question is whether there exists a nonamenable type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor \(N\) with separable predual such that
\[
\forall\,\pi,\rho:N\hookrightarrow N^{\mathcal U}\quad
\exists\,u\in\mathcal U(N^{\mathcal U})\quad
\forall\,x\in N,\qquad \rho(x)=u\pi(x)u^*.
\]
The same unitary must implement conjugacy on all of \(N\), and conjugacy is exact in the ultrapower. \subsection{Short English statement}
Does there exist a nonamenable separable type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor whose embeddings into its own tracial ultrapower are all unitarily conjugate?
