\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a compact complex manifold with complex structure \(J\) and complex dimension \(n\geq 3\). A K\"ahler form on \(X\) is a smooth real differential form \(\omega\) of type \((1,1)\) such that
\[
\mathrm d\omega=0,\qquad \omega_x(v,Jv)>0\quad\text{for every }x\in X\text{ and }0\ne v\in T_xX.
\]
The associated K\"ahler metric is \(g(u,v)=\omega(u,Jv)\).

\textbf{Research problem.} For compact complex manifolds \(X\) of complex dimension greater than two, develop an analogue of the results of Lamari and Buchdahl concerning the existence of K\"ahler metrics, in the context of the spectral theory of nonintegrable complex vector bundles.
