\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A surface bundle with fiber \(F\) and base \(B\) is a locally trivial bundle \(p\colon E\to B\) whose fibers are homeomorphic to \(F\). The supplied record takes \(F\) and \(B\) to be fixed closed surfaces, but does not specify connectedness, orientability, genus restrictions, or the bundle category.

For a closed, connected, oriented \(n\)-manifold \(M\), its simplicial volume is
\[
\|M\|=\inf\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{r}|a_j|:\ c=\sum_{j=1}^{r}a_j\sigma_j\in C_n(M;\R),\ \partial c=0,\ [c]=[M]_{\R}\right\},
\]
where \(c\) ranges over finite real singular cycles and \([M]_{\R}\) is the real fundamental class. Write \(\chi(X)\) for the Euler characteristic of \(X\).

Define
\[
\mathcal V_{\mathrm{or}}(F,B)=\{\|E\|:p\colon E\to B\text{ belongs to }\mathcal B_{\mathrm{or}}(F,B)\}.
