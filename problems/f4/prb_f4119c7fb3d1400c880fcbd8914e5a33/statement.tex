\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be a connected closed hyperbolic \(n\)-manifold, and let \(G=\pi_1(M)\). Interpret the source's \(\Z_2\) as the cyclic group \(\Z/2\Z=\F_2\), with trivial group action on the coefficients. The cohomology groups are ordinary group cohomology. For inclusion \(i:G'\hookrightarrow G\), restriction is the induced map
\[
\operatorname{res}^{G}_{G'}=i^*:H^3(G;\F_2)\longrightarrow H^3(G';\F_2).
\]
Kapovich's question is
\[
\forall M\text{ as above}\ \exists G'\le G,\quad
[G:G']<\infty\quad\text{and}\quad
\forall\alpha\in H^3(G;\F_2),\ 
\operatorname{res}^{G}_{G'}(\alpha)=0.
\]
One subgroup must kill the entire degree-three restriction map, not just one designated class; no normality requirement is imposed.

Since the hyperbolic manifold is aspherical, the equivalent covering-space formulation asks for a connected finite-sheeted cover \(p:M'\to M\) such that
\[
p^*:H^3(M;\F_2)\longrightarrow H^3(M';\F_2)
\]
is the zero homomorphism. No orientability hypothesis is needed for this formulation, and degree three is retained regardless of the ambient dimension \(n\). The source does not restrict the question to \(n=3\).

The supplied research summary reports no complete resolution located. Its suggested relation to residual finiteness and unspecified fundamental-group/\(\ell^2\) phenomena does not supply a theorem establishing the displayed vanishing.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every closed hyperbolic manifold have a finite cover for which pullback on all third cohomology classes with \(\F_2\) coefficients is zero? The supplied source labels this an unresolved question.
