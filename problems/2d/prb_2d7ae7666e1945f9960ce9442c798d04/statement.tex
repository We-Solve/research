\paragraph{Definitions and notation.}Kerr space-time models a rotating vacuum black hole; Schwarzschild is its nonrotating counterpart. Lensing maps initial null-ray directions to source locations. Critical curves are loci where the lens-map derivative is singular; caustics are their images. The strong-deflection limit concerns rays passing near the trapped photon region and requires a specified expansion parameter. Higher-order images correspond to rays making additional turns.
