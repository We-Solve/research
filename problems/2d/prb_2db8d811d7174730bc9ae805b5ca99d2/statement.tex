\section{No conjugate points: flat-growth rigidity and the three-dimensional residue}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
If a Riemannian manifold has no conjugate points, must its underlying smooth manifold admit some Riemannian metric with nonpositive sectional curvature? The new metric need not be the original one.
