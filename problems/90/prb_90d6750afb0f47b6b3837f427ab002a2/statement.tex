\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $H^\infty=H^\infty(\mathbb D)$ with norm $\|F\|_\infty=\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}|F(z)|$, and let $f\in H^\infty$. Read Blaschke sequence in its usual sense $z_n\in\mathbb D$, $\sum_n(1-|z_n|)<\infty$; the extraction instead prints an incomplete term $(\-|z_n|)$, so this conventional repair is explicit. Put
\[
d(z,w)=\left|\frac{z-w}{1-\overline w z}\right|.
\]
The source calls the sequence uniformly separated if $\inf_n\prod_{m\ne n}d(z_m,z_n)>0$, and weakly separated if $\inf_{m\ne n}d(z_m,z_n)>0$.

A nonzero Blaschke product has unit $H^\infty$ norm. Therefore the source's product of norm not necessarily one is naturally interpreted as $cB_0$, with $B_0$ a Blaschke product and $c\in\mathbb C$; $c=0$ covers identically zero interpolation data. An inner function is a bounded holomorphic function whose boundary values have modulus one almost everywhere; its scalar multiple is distinguished from an inner function itself.

\textbf{Questions (J. P. Earl and A. Stray).}
\begin{itemize}
\item[(a)] Must there exist a scaled Blaschke product $B=cB_0$ with $B(z_n)=f(z_n)$ for every $n\geq1$? The source reports this for uniformly separated Blaschke sequences, citing Earl.
\item[(b)] Let
\[
m=\inf\{\|F\|_\infty:F\in H^\infty,\ F(z_n)=f(z_n)\text{ for all }n\}.
\]
Is the minimal-norm interpolant described by the source as unique a constant multiple of a Blaschke product? The source reports yes for finitely many nodes. What if one additionally assumes $f(z_n)\to0$? In that additional setting it reports yes when $z_n\to1$ nontangentially, meaning $|1-z_n|\leq C(1-|z_n|)$ eventually for some $C<\infty$.
\item[(c)] Ask both (a) and (b) for Blaschke sequences satisfying the stated weaker pairwise-separation condition.
\item[(d)] Ask the analogues of (a) and (c) with inner functions in place of Blaschke products. For general unnormalized data the scalar-multiple convention must also be specified in these analogues.
\end{itemize}
Only interpolation of values is required, not derivative data. The supplied summary reports the overall problem open as of the 2018 edition, with no definitive resolution located.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Ask when bounded holomorphic interpolation data admit scaled Blaschke or inner interpolants, and whether norm-minimizing interpolants have that form, including the stated separation and vanishing-data variants.
