\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The source concerns finite-volume locally symmetric spaces and asks whether their \(L^2\)-Betti numbers and \(L^2\)-torsion come entirely from the ``complete interior.'' It also requests an extension of existing calculations first to rank-one and then to higher-rank locally symmetric spaces. The latter extension request is restored in the cleaned statement and retained separately from the interior-contribution question.

In a conventional manifold setup one writes \(M=\Gamma\backslash G/K\), with \(G/K\) a symmetric space and \(\Gamma\) a torsion-free lattice, and uses its locally symmetric metric of finite volume. The record does not itself specify the groups, metric normalization, torsion/orbifold convention or compact and noncompact cases covered. The ranks in the extension request also need definition: real rank of the symmetric space, commonly the dimension of a maximal flat, is not interchangeable with an arithmetic \(\Q\)-rank controlling cusps.

There are distinct \(L^2\)-cohomology conventions to resolve. On \(M\), reduced \(L^2\) de Rham cohomology is a Hilbert-space cohomology whose dimension can be compared with the kernel of a suitably realized Hodge Laplacian. For a regular cover \(\widetilde M\to M\) with deck group \(\Gamma\), the group \(L^2\)-Betti numbers instead use \(\dim_\Gamma\ker\Delta_i\) in the corresponding setup. The supplied record does not say which version and cover are intended. \(L^2\)-torsion further requires its analytic or combinatorial definition, trace/normalization, and the relevant determinant, spectral or acyclicity hypotheses (or a prescribed modification). These are not supplied and cannot be presumed in every finite-volume example.

Most importantly, ``complete interior'' is not defined as a comparison space, region or spectral object. A complete noncompact manifold, a compact truncated core with boundary conditions, and a completed interior of a compactification are not automatically the same comparison problem. To state that the invariants come entirely from it, one needs the proposed equality or decomposition formula and a precise meaning of contributions from the ends. No boundary condition, vanishing of an end invariant, or equality with invariants of a compact core is guessed.
