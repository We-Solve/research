\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 701140}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a group \(H\), let \(\operatorname{Aut}(H)\) denote the group of all automorphisms of \(H\), with composition as its operation, and let \(|H|\) denote its order. For each finite group \(G\), define its iterated automorphism groups by
\[
G_0=G,\qquad G_{i+1}=\operatorname{Aut}(G_i)\quad(i\in\Z_{\geq 0}).
\]
Each \(G_i\) is finite. Does there exist a function
\[
f:\Z_{\geq 1}\longrightarrow\Z_{\geq 1}
\]
such that, for every finite group \(G\) and every integer \(i\geq 0\),
\[
|G_i|\leq f(|G|)?
\]
The quantifier over \(G\) includes groups with nontrivial centre and the trivial group. The same function \(f\) must work for every such group and every iteration index.

As background, the supplied source attributes to Wielandt the theorem that this sequence stabilizes for every finite group with trivial centre, where stabilization is understood up to group isomorphism. The question above imposes no trivial-centre hypothesis. This background attribution and the source's open-status label are supplied information, not independently verified assessments.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there a bound depending only on the order of a finite group that bounds the orders of all its iterated automorphism groups? This is the well-known problem recorded as Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.52, proposed by V. D. Mazurov in 2002.
