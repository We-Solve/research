\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(h_*\) and \(h'_*\) be generalized reduced homology theories on based spaces, and let \(G\) be a compact Lie group. A finite based \(G\)-CW complex means a \(G\)-CW complex with a distinguished \(G\)-fixed basepoint and finitely many equivariant cells outside that basepoint; cells have the form \(G/H\times D^k\), with \(H\leq G\) closed. Its fixed-point space is the based space
\[
X^G=\{x\in X:gx=x\text{ for every }g\in G\}.
\]
Here the homology theories are interpreted as nonequivariant theories evaluated on the underlying based spaces; the source does not explicitly specify an equivariant interpretation.

Determine precisely for which triples \((h_*,h'_*,G)\) there exists a finite based \(G\)-CW complex \(X\) such that
\[
\bigl(\forall n\in\Z,\ h_n(X)=0\bigr)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\bigl(\exists m\in\Z,\ h'_m(X^G)\neq 0\bigr).
\]
Thus the original existence question is asked for each given pair of theories and each given compact Lie group; no universal existence assertion is supplied. No restrictions such as a choice of prime, localization, connectivity, or a particular family of homology theories are specified.

The supplied curation labels the problem partially solved and reports results for abelian compact Lie groups and several other cases. It does not state the precise hypotheses or scope of those results, so that assessment does not supply a classification theorem for the unrestricted formulation above.
