\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((M,\omega)\) be a toric symplectic manifold of real dimension \(2n\), equipped with an effective Hamiltonian action of \(\mathbb T^n=\R^n/\Z^n\) and moment map \(\mu:M\to(\R^n)^*\). Write \(\Delta=\mu(M)\), and restrict attention to the values
\[
U=\{u\in\Delta:\mu^{-1}(u)\text{ is a Lagrangian torus of dimension }n\}.
\]
Here a Lagrangian torus is an embedded submanifold diffeomorphic to \(\mathbb T^n\) on which \(\omega\) restricts to zero. For \(u\in U\), set \(T_u=\mu^{-1}(u)\).

A Hamiltonian isotopy is a smooth family of diffeomorphisms \(\phi_H^t\), \(0\leq t\leq1\), starting at the identity and generated by a smooth time-dependent Hamiltonian \(H_t\), with \(\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=-\dd H_t\). Its flow must exist on all of \(M\) throughout this interval. Write \(\operatorname{Ham}(M,\omega)\) for the group of time-one maps of the admissible Hamiltonian isotopies. The source does not specify whether \(M\) is compact or which support restrictions apply to Hamiltonians when \(M\) is noncompact; the admissible class, and thus this group in the noncompact case, remains a qualification requiring clarification.
