\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Heath-Brown's main question is whether there is a sequence of smooth projective varieties $X_i\subset\PP^{N_i}_{\Q}$ with
\[
 X_i(\Q_v)\ne\varnothing\text{ for every place }v\text{, including infinity},\qquad
 X_i(\Q)=\varnothing,\qquad
 \frac{\dim X_i}{\deg X_i}\longrightarrow\infty
\]
(or at least with the ratios unbounded). Degree is relative to the specified embedding. The supplied verification repairs missing fraction bars and reads the recorded Browning--Heath-Brown benchmark as ratio tending to $1/3$, with dimension tending to infinity.

The source discussion contains several further questions and qualifications. Wooley suggests dropping smoothness and using norm equations $N_{K/\Q}(\sum_{j=1}^d x_j\alpha_j)=ct^d$, with $d=[K:\Q]$ and a basis $\alpha_j$. Heath-Brown separately asks for smooth hypersurface Hasse failures of dimension at least three. Colliot-Th\'el\`ene attributes many general-type examples conditionally to Sarnak--Wang assuming Bombieri--Lang. Wooley proposes starting with a locally soluble smooth hypersurface of degree $d\gg N$ in $\PP^N$, then combining determinant-method bounds and linear sections; Colliot-Th\'el\`ene questions whether this merely forces large coefficients. These are suggestions and conditional claims, not supplied unconditional constructions.

Harari asks whether ratios tending to infinity force geometric rational connectedness, and remarks on difficulty of cohomological Hasse obstructions for geometrically rationally connected complete intersections. Browning states conditionally on a Hartshorne conjecture that a smooth nondegenerate $Y\subset\PP^N$ with $\dim Y\geq2\deg Y+1$ is a complete intersection and hence rationally connected, suggesting the range $1/3<\dim X_i/\deg X_i\leq2$. Finally Tschinkel asks whether Fano varieties over $\C$ with nonzero $\operatorname{Br}(X)=H^3(X,\Z)_{\mathrm{tors}}$ can occur in every dimension at least four; smoothness and the Brauer convention for that identification need clarification.

The supplied report improves only the bounded ratio benchmark. For every positive integer $m\equiv1\pmod3$, set $r=m^2$. It uses an order-three torsor self-cover of the Selmer cubic of degree $m^2$, followed by pushforward of a degree-$2m^2+1$ line bundle to a stable rank-$r$ bundle. The intended elliptic-scroll construction is its projectivization with tautological embedding. The report claims resulting smooth projective geometrically integral $X_m$ satisfy
\[
 X_m\subset\PP^{2r}_{\Q},\qquad \dim X_m=r,\qquad\deg X_m=2r+1,
\]
\[
 X_m(\Q_v)\ne\varnothing\ (\text{all }v),\qquad X_m(\Q)=\varnothing,\qquad
 \frac{\dim X_m}{\deg X_m}\longrightarrow\frac12.
\]
It expressly says these examples are not geometrically rationally connected and do not solve unboundedness. The cubic equation, self-cover, line-bundle data and embedding proof are not supplied, so the construction and its claimed sharp dimensions require independent verification.

The report gives elliptic-scroll examples with exact ratio $m^2/(2m^2+1)$ tending to $1/2$, improving the historical $1/3$ benchmark but remaining bounded. The source's separate hypersurface, rational-connectedness and Fano-Brauer questions remain distinct.
