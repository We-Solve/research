\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(g\geq 3\) be an integer, and let \(\kappa=(k_1,\ldots,k_n)\) be a partition of \(2g-2\) into positive integers. In the standard unmarked, unnormalised convention, the stratum \(\mathcal H(\kappa)\) parametrises isomorphism classes of pairs \((X,\omega)\), where \(X\) is a compact connected Riemann surface of genus \(g\) and \(\omega\neq 0\) is a holomorphic one-form whose zeros have multiplicities \(k_1,\ldots,k_n\). No restriction on the positive area \(\frac{i}{2}\int_X\omega\wedge\overline\omega\) is imposed in this convention.

If \(\Sigma\) is the zero set of \(\omega\), integration over a local basis of relative homology \(H_1(X,\Sigma;\Z)\) gives period coordinates \(z\in\C^{2g+n-1}\). The group \(\mathrm{GL}^{+}(2,\R)\) acts by applying its matrices to the real and imaginary parts of the translation coordinates obtained by integrating \(\omega\).

Under the usual full-stratum convention, an affine invariant submanifold is a connected, closed, immersed complex suborbifold \(\mathcal M\) of a stratum, invariant under this action, whose local branches in period coordinates are defined by homogeneous linear equations with real coefficients:
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{2g+n-1}a_{\ell j}z_j=0,
\qquad a_{\ell j}\in\R.
\]
Here immersed submanifolds may have distinct local branches at the same ambient point.

\textbf{Classification problem.} For every integer \(g\geq 3\), every partition \(\kappa\) of \(2g-2\), and every connected component of \(\mathcal H(\kappa)\), classify all affine invariant submanifolds contained in that component.
