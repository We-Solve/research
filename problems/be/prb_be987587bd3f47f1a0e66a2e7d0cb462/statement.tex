\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be a fixed integer, and let \((M_i^n,g_i,p_i)\) be a sequence of pointed Riemannian \(n\)-manifolds with a pointed metric limit \((X,d,p)\). The supplied record does not specify the convergence topology; the formulation below uses the conventional interpretation of pointed Gromov--Hausdorff convergence:
\[
(M_i^n,d_{g_i},p_i)\xrightarrow{\mathrm{pGH}}(X,d,p).
\]
For fixed constants \(\lambda\in\R\) and \(v>0\), suppose that, for every \(i\),
\[
\operatorname{Ric}_{g_i}(u,u)\geq-\lambda g_i(u,u)
\quad\text{for every tangent vector }u,
\qquad
\operatorname{Vol}_{g_i}\bigl(B_1^{g_i}(p_i)\bigr)>v,
\]
where \(d_{g_i}\) is the Riemannian distance and \(B_1^{g_i}(p_i)=\{q\in M_i:d_{g_i}(q,p_i)<1\}\).

\textbf{Conjectural conclusion (Naber, Conjecture 1, 2020).} The supplied assertion is that \(X\) is homeomorphic to a manifold away from a set of codimension three. If this means Hausdorff codimension at least three and an \(n\)-dimensional topological manifold without boundary, its precise formulation is
\[
\exists S\subseteq X:\qquad
\dim_{\mathrm H}^{d}S\leq n-3,
\qquad
X\setminus S\text{ is a topological }n\text{-manifold}.
\]
Here \(\dim_{\mathrm H}^{d}\) denotes Hausdorff dimension in the metric \(d\), and a topological \(n\)-manifold means a Hausdorff, second-countable space locally homeomorphic to \(\R^n\), with the subspace topology on \(X\setminus S\). For this interpretation in dimensions \(n<3\), use \(\dim_{\mathrm H}\varnothing=-\infty\), so the displayed bound forces \(S=\varnothing\).

\subsection{Short English statement}
Naber's supplied conjecture says that a limit of Riemannian \(n\)-manifolds with a uniform Ricci lower bound and a uniform positive lower bound for the volumes of unit balls at the basepoints is a topological manifold outside an exceptional set of codimension three.
