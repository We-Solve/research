\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The supplied record combines two questions, which are stated separately below. The first concerns periodic Reeb orbits; the second requests a classification without specifying a particular manifold or a class of contact structures.

Let \(n\geq 1\). A contact form on a smooth manifold \(M^{2n-1}\) is a smooth one-form \(\alpha\) satisfying
\[
\alpha\wedge(\mathrm d\alpha)^{n-1}\neq 0
\]
at every point. Its kernel \(\xi=\ker\alpha\) is a coorientable contact structure. The Reeb vector field \(R_\alpha\) is uniquely determined by
\[
\alpha(R_\alpha)=1,\qquad \mathrm d\alpha(R_\alpha,\cdot)=0.
\]
Here a closed manifold means a compact manifold without boundary. A periodic Reeb orbit is a smooth map \(\gamma:\R\to M\) for which some \(T>0\) satisfies
\[
\dot\gamma(t)=R_\alpha(\gamma(t)),\qquad
\gamma(t+T)=\gamma(t)\quad\text{for every }t\in\R.
\]
Such an orbit is necessarily nonconstant.

\textbf{Periodic-orbit question (identified in the supplied background with the Weinstein conjecture).} Under the unrestricted dimension reading of the source, is it true that for every positive integer \(n\), every nonempty closed smooth manifold \(M^{2n-1}\), and every contact form \(\alpha\) on \(M\), the vector field \(R_\alpha\) has a periodic orbit? The source does not state a dimension restriction or impose connectedness.

For the hypersurface formulation, a symplectic manifold \((W^{2n},\omega)\) has a closed, nondegenerate two-form \(\omega\). A smooth embedded hypersurface \(\Sigma\subset W\) is of contact type, in the usual globally defined sense, if there is a vector field \(Y\), defined on a neighborhood of \(\Sigma\), transverse to \(\Sigma\), and satisfying
\[
\mathcal L_Y\omega=\omega.
\]
Then \(\alpha=(\iota_Y\omega)|_{T\Sigma}\) is a contact form, with \(\mathrm d\alpha=\omega|_{T\Sigma}\). The characteristic line at \(x\in\Sigma\) is
\[
\ker(\omega|_{T_x\Sigma})
=\{v\in T_x\Sigma:\omega(v,w)=0\text{ for every }w\in T_x\Sigma\}.
\]
The corresponding question asks whether every nonempty closed contact-type hypersurface in every symplectic manifold has a closed characteristic, meaning a nonconstant immersed closed curve tangent to these lines. Equivalently, after parametrization by \(R_\alpha\), it asks for a periodic Reeb orbit. The supplied record presents this as equivalent to the preceding universal Reeb formulation. No ambient compactness, bounding-domain condition, or outward-pointing condition is supplied. The definition above uses global contact type; the record does not explicitly distinguish it from local contact type.
