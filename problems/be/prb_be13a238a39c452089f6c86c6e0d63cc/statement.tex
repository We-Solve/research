\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an \(\R^d\)-valued random variable \(Z\) on a probability space \((\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)\), define
\[
\|Z\|_{L^2(\Omega;\R^d)}=\bigl(\E[\|Z\|_{\R^d}^2]\bigr)^{1/2},
\]
whenever this quantity is finite. Strong \(L^2\) approximation compares an exact solution and its numerical approximation on a common probability space using this norm.
