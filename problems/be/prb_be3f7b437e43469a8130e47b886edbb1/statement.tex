\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A nonzero unital \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\) is simple if its only norm-closed two-sided ideals are \(\{0\}\) and \(A\). It is stably finite if, for every integer \(n\geq 1\) and every \(v\in M_n(A)\),
\[
v^*v=1_{M_n(A)}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad vv^*=1_{M_n(A)}.
\]
An action of a group \(G\) on \(A\) is a homomorphism \(\alpha:G\to\operatorname{Aut}(A)\), where \(\operatorname{Aut}(A)\) denotes the group of \(*\)-automorphisms of \(A\).

The supplied question asks whether there exist a nonamenable group \(G\), a nonzero unital simple stably finite \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\), and an amenable action \(\alpha:G\to\operatorname{Aut}(A)\). This is an existential question about at least one nonamenable group; it does not assert existence for every nonamenable group.
