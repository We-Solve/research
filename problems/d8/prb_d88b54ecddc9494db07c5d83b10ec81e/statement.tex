Let \(M\) be a closed connected orientable smooth surface of genus \(h\geq 2\). Here closed means compact without boundary. Let \(\operatorname{Met}^\infty(M)\) denote the space of smooth Riemannian metrics on the fixed surface \(M\), with the \(C^\infty\) topology. For \(g\in\operatorname{Met}^\infty(M)\), set
\[
S_gM=\{(x,v)\in TM:g_x(v,v)=1\},
\]
and let \(\varphi_g^t:S_gM\to S_gM\) be its geodesic flow, with generating vector field \(X_g\). This flow is Anosov if there is a continuous \(D\varphi_g^t\)-invariant splitting
\[
T(S_gM)=E_g^s\oplus\R X_g\oplus E_g^u
\]
and constants \(C\geq1\), \(\lambda>0\) such that, for every \(t\geq0\),
\[
\|D\varphi_g^t(v)\|\leq Ce^{-\lambda t}\|v\|\quad(v\in E_g^s),
\qquad
\|D\varphi_g^{-t}(w)\|\leq Ce^{-\lambda t}\|w\|\quad(w\in E_g^u),
\]
where the norms come from an auxiliary Riemannian metric on \(S_gM\).

Define the subspaces
\[
\mathcal A(M)=\{g\in\operatorname{Met}^\infty(M):\varphi_g^t\text{ is Anosov}\},
\qquad
\mathcal N(M)=\{g\in\operatorname{Met}^\infty(M):K_g(x)<0\text{ for every }x\in M\},
\]
with their inherited topologies, where \(K_g\) is Gaussian curvature. Negative curvature gives the inclusion \(i:\mathcal N(M)\hookrightarrow\mathcal A(M)\).

\textbf{Questions.} For every such surface \(M\):
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is \(\mathcal A(M)\) contractible? Explicitly, do there exist \(g_*\in\mathcal A(M)\) and a continuous map
\[
H:\mathcal A(M)\times[0,1]\longrightarrow\mathcal A(M)
\]
satisfying \(H(g,0)=g\) and \(H(g,1)=g_*\) for every \(g\in\mathcal A(M)\)?
\item Is \(i\) a weak homotopy equivalence? Explicitly, does it induce a bijection on path components and, for every \(g\in\mathcal N(M)\) and every integer \(n\geq1\), an isomorphism
\[
i_*:\pi_n(\mathcal N(M),g)\longrightarrow\pi_n(\mathcal A(M),g)?
\]
\end{enumerate}
