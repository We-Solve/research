\paragraph{Testing whether the driver alone controls the gap.} On an \(N\)-dimensional search space, \(N>1\), let \(|s\rangle=N^{-1/2}\sum_x|x\rangle\) and a marked basis state be \(|w\rangle\). Consider the interpolation
\[
 H(t)=-(1-t)|s\rangle\langle s|-t|w\rangle\langle w|,\qquad0\le t\le1.
\]
The driver is independent of \(w\), but is dense in the problem basis. On the span of \(|s\rangle,|w\rangle\), the trace is \(-1\) and determinant is \(t(1-t)(1-1/N)\). Hence the two eigenvalues have separation
\[
 \Delta(t)=\sqrt{1-4t(1-t)(1-1/N)},\qquad \min_t\Delta(t)=N^{-1/2}.
\]
The remaining eigenvalues are zero, so this two-level calculation also identifies the low spectral avoided crossing for \(N>1\).
