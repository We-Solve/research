\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) and let \(GL_n(\R)\) denote the invertible real \(n\times n\) matrices. Under the usual full-rank convention, a lattice in \(\R^n\) is a subgroup \(\Gamma=B\Z^n\), where \(B\in GL_n(\R)\). For \(A\in GL_n(\R)\), \(\gamma\in\Gamma\), and \(f\in L^2(\R^n;\C)\), define translation and normalized dilation by
\[
(T_\gamma f)(x)=f(x-\gamma),\qquad (D_Af)(x)=|\det A|^{1/2}f(Ax).
\]
For a single generator \(\psi\in L^2(\R^n;\C)\), its affine system is
\[
\mathcal W(\psi;A,\Gamma)
=\left\{D_A^jT_\gamma\psi:j\in\Z,\ \gamma\in\Gamma\right\}
=\left\{|\det A|^{j/2}\psi(A^j\,\cdot-\gamma):j\in\Z,\ \gamma\in\Gamma\right\}.
\]
It is an orthonormal wavelet system if its indexed elements are pairwise orthogonal, each has norm one, and their closed linear span is all of \(L^2(\R^n;\C)\).
