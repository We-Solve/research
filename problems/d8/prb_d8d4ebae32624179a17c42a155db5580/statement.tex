\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\textbf{Uniform ramification question.} For a \(p\)-perfect finite group \(G\) and its \(p\)-Frattini quotients \(G_k\), does there exist \(r_0\) such that every \(G_k\) satisfies the regular inverse Galois problem using covers with at most \(r_0\) ramification classes? Here \(p\)-perfect conventionally means that \(G\) has no nontrivial quotient of order \(p\), equivalently \(H^1(G,\F_p)=0\). No hypothesis \(p\mid|G|\), or full group-theoretic perfectness, is silently substituted.\par A \(p\)-Frattini cover is a surjection with pro-\(p\) kernel that is Frattini: no proper closed subgroup of the covering group surjects onto the target. A standard tower uses characteristic finite quotients of the universal \(p\)-Frattini cover of \(G\), often obtained by successive Frattini subgroups of its kernel. The source does not supply the chosen universal cover, filtration, or exact indexing, so \(G_k\) is retained as its intended tower rather than identified with an arbitrary sequence of finite \(p\)-extensions.\par Over a specified constant field \(F\), a regular realization means a finite Galois extension \(E_k/F(t)\) with Galois group \(G_k\) and \(E_k\cap\overline F=F\); equivalently it gives the corresponding geometrically connected Galois cover of the projective line. The source does not name \(F\), for example \(\Q\), or its characteristic. The uniform quantifier is \(\exists r_0\,\forall k\): the bound may depend on the fixed \(G,p,F\) and stated restrictions but not on the level. The individual realizations may vary with \(k\). Requiring them to form a compatible tower of covers would be an additional condition not stated here.\par The phrase \emph{ramification classes} is ambiguous. A bound on branch points, on a branch-cycle conjugacy-class tuple counted with repetitions, or only on the number of distinct inertia classes imposes different restrictions. The precise class rationality, allowed inertia orders, any prime-to-\(p\) restriction, and whether classes must lift coherently through levels are missing. \subsection{Short English statement}
Ask whether every level of a fixed \(p\)-Frattini tower has a regular Galois realization with one bound on its allowed ramification complexity. The bound must be independent of the level. The constant field, class-counting convention, and inertia restrictions are unspecified.
