\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


A cell representative is a chosen member of a cell orbit under $\Gamma$. Completeness means that every $d$-cell orbit has a representative in the proposed collection. A Voronoi cell complex decomposes the relevant space using the source\textquotesingle s Voronoi construction.
