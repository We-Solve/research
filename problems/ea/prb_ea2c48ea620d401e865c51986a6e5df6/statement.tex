Under the generalized continuum hypothesis, decide each ordinal partition relation
\[
\omega_3\longrightarrow(\omega_2,\omega_1+2)^2,
\qquad
\omega_3\longrightarrow(\omega_2+\omega_1,\omega_2+\omega)^2,
\]
\[
\omega_2\longrightarrow(\omega_1^{\omega+2}+2,\omega_1+2)^2.
\]
Separately, determine whether GCH is consistent with
\[
\omega_2\longrightarrow(\omega_1+\omega)^2_2,
\]
and whether it is consistent with the stronger simultaneous assertion $\omega_2\longrightarrow(\xi)^2_2$ for every $\xi<\omega_2$.
