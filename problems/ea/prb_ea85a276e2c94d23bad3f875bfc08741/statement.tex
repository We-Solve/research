\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A metric space \((X,d)\) is \emph{injective} if, whenever \(A\) is a metric subspace of a metric space \(B\), every \(1\)-Lipschitz map \(A\to X\) extends to a \(1\)-Lipschitz map \(B\to X\). Write \(E(X)\) for its injective hull; for injective \(X\), identify \(E(X)\) isometrically with \(X\).

A \emph{geodesic bicombing} on \(X\) is a map \(\sigma:X\times X\times[0,1]\to X\), with \(\sigma_{xy}(t)=\sigma(x,y,t)\), such that
\[
\sigma_{xy}(0)=x,\qquad \sigma_{xy}(1)=y,\qquad d(\sigma_{xy}(s),\sigma_{xy}(t))=|s-t|d(x,y).
\]
It is \emph{convex} if, for every \(x,y,x',y'\in X\), the function
\[
t\longmapsto d(\sigma_{xy}(t),\sigma_{x'y'}(t))
\]
is convex on \([0,1]\). It is \emph{consistent} if, for every \(x,y\in X\), \(0\leq a\leq b\leq1\), and \(u\in[0,1]\),
\[
\sigma_{\sigma_{xy}(a),\sigma_{xy}(b)}(u)=\sigma_{xy}((1-u)a+ub).
\]
Reversibility, meaning \(\sigma_{yx}(t)=\sigma_{xy}(1-t)\), is an additional condition and is not imposed here; the supplied record does not specify whether its bicombing convention includes it.

For an explicit conventional reading of these terms, a geometric action of a discrete group is a proper, cocompact action by isometries, where proper means that \(\{g\in G:gC\cap C\ne\varnothing\}\) is finite for every compact subset \(C\). A Helly graph is a connected graph whose combinatorial closed balls have the finite Helly property: every finite pairwise intersecting family of such balls has nonempty total intersection. A Helly group admits a geometric action by graph automorphisms on a Helly graph. A CAT(0) group admits a geometric action on a CAT(0) geodesic metric space, whose geodesic triangles satisfy the Euclidean comparison inequality. A group is biautomatic if it has a finite symmetric generating set and a regular language of representative words satisfying uniform synchronous fellow-traveller bounds for multiplication by generators on both the left and the right.
