\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(K\) be a number field, and fix an algebraic closure \(\bar K\). Let \(X\) and \(S\) be varieties over \(K\), let \(\pi:X\to S\) be a morphism over \(K\), and let \(f:X\to X\) be an \(S\)-morphism, meaning that \(\pi\circ f=\pi\). For a finite extension \(L/K\) contained in \(\bar K\) and a point \(s\in S(L)\), write
\[
X_s=X\times_{S,s}\operatorname{Spec}L
\]
for the fiber over \(L\), and let \(f_s:X_s\to X_s\) be the induced morphism. Its base change to \(\bar K\) is the geometric fiber map appearing in the source.

Set \(f_s^0=\operatorname{id}_{X_s}\). The set of \(L\)-rational preperiodic points is
\[
\operatorname{PrePer}(f_s,X_s(L))
=\bigl\{x\in X_s(L):\text{ there exist integers }n\geq 0,\ r\geq 1
\text{ with }f_s^{n+r}(x)=f_s^n(x)\bigr\}.
\]
Equivalently, these are the points whose forward orbit under \(f_s\) is finite.

\textbf{Supplied conjectural assertion (Poonen, 2012, Question 3.2; source survey, Conjecture 6).} For every choice of \(K,X,S,\pi,f\) as above and every integer \(D\geq 1\), there exists an integer
\[
B=B(K,X,S,\pi,f,D)\geq 0
\]
such that, for every finite extension \(L/K\) with \([L:K]=D\) and every \(s\in S(L)\),
\[
\#\operatorname{PrePer}(f_s,X_s(L))\leq B.
\]
Thus the bound may depend on the fixed family and on \(D\), but is independent of \(L\) and \(s\).
