\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The supplied question concerns Connes embeddable super McDuff factors of type \(\mathrm{II}_1\). A \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor is a diffuse finite von Neumann algebra with center \(\C 1\), equipped with its normalized faithful normal trace.

To give an explicit conventional formulation, suppose that all factors considered have separable predual, fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter \(\omega\) on \(\N\), and let \(R\) denote the separable hyperfinite \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor. For a tracial von Neumann algebra \((P,\tau_P)\), its tracial ultrapower is
\[
P^\omega=\ell^\infty(\N,P)\big/\{(x_n):\lim_{n\to\omega}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau_P}=0\},
\qquad \|x\|_{2,\tau_P}=\tau_P(x^*x)^{1/2}.

Call \(P\) Connes embeddable if it admits a unital trace-preserving embedding into \(R^\omega\). Call \(P\) super McDuff if \(P'\cap P^\omega\), with \(P\) identified with its diagonal image, is a \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor. Two factors \(P,Q\) are elementarily equivalent, written \(P\equiv Q\), if every sentence in the continuous first-order language of tracial von Neumann algebras has the same value in \(P\) and \(Q\).
