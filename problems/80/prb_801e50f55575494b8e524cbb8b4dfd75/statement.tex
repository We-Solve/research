\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an integer \(n\geq 1\), a Reinhardt domain is a nonempty connected open set \(D\subseteq\C^n\) invariant under coordinatewise rotations: for every \(z\in D\) and \((\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n)\in\R^n\),
\[
(e^{i\theta_1}z_1,\ldots,e^{i\theta_n}z_n)\in D.
\]
For a complex manifold \(U\) and a biholomorphic map \(f:U\to U\), a diagonal rotational linear model consists of such a domain \(D\), a biholomorphism \(h:U\to D\), and a map
\[
L(z_1,\ldots,z_n)=(\lambda_1z_1,\ldots,\lambda_nz_n),\qquad |\lambda_j|=1,
\]
satisfying \(h\circ f\circ h^{-1}=L|_D\). One possible rank convention assigns rank two when the identity component of
