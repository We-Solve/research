\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S^3\) be the three-dimensional sphere, and let \(K\subset S^3\) be an embedded figure-eight knot. A closed hyperbolic \(3\)-manifold is a compact \(3\)-manifold without boundary equipped with a complete Riemannian metric of constant sectional curvature \(-1\). A finite covering \(q:\widetilde M\to M\) means an unbranched covering with finitely many sheets.

For a branched covering \(p:\widetilde M\to S^3\) with branch locus exactly \(K\), the restriction
\[
p:\widetilde M\setminus p^{-1}(K)\longrightarrow S^3\setminus K
\]
is an ordinary finite-sheeted covering. Along each component \(C\) of \(p^{-1}(K)\), its ramification index \(e_C\in\Z_{\geq 1}\) is the transverse local degree: in suitable local coordinates the map has the form
\[
(z,t)\longmapsto (z^{e_C},t).
\]
The components of the full inverse image \(p^{-1}(K)\) include any components with index \(1\); the condition below applies to all of them.

\textbf{Question.} Is it true that for every closed hyperbolic \(3\)-manifold \(M\) and every real number \(L>0\), there exist a finite covering \(q:\widetilde M\to M\) and a branched covering \(p:\widetilde M\to S^3\) with branch locus exactly \(K\), such that
\[
e_C>L\qquad\text{for every connected component }C\text{ of }p^{-1}(K)?
\]
Both covering maps may depend on \(M\) and \(L\). \subsection{Short English statement}
Does every closed hyperbolic \(3\)-manifold admit, for every prescribed positive bound, a finite cover that maps to \(S^3\) branched exactly over the figure-eight knot, with ramification index strictly greater than that bound on every component above the knot?
