\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\Lambda\subset\R^2$ be a planar lattice, conventionally $\Lambda=B\Z^2$ for an invertible real $2\times2$ matrix $B$. Let $S\subset\R^2$ be a closed bounded convex region containing $0$. Using the usual positive-scale convention, put
\[
S(r,P)=P+rS=\{P+rs:s\in S\},\qquad r>0,\quad P\in\R^2,
\]
and $J(r,P)=\Lambda\cap S(r,P)$. A finite configuration $J\subset\Lambda$ is an $S$-oval in the sense of the supplied question if
\[
\exists r>0\ \exists P\in\R^2:\qquad J=\Lambda\cap(P+rS).
\]
Equivalently, all points of $J$ must lie in $P+rS$ and every point of $\Lambda\setminus J$ must lie outside it. Because $S$ is closed, lattice points on its translated scaled boundary are included. Finding a region merely containing $J$, while allowing additional lattice points, is not the stated recognition problem. Only scaling and translation are included; rotations or arbitrary affine changes of $S$ are not introduced.
