\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a Lie algebra \(L\) and \(x\in L\), define the centralizer of \(x\) in \(L\) by
\[
C_L(x):=\{y\in L:[y,x]=0\}.
\]
An \(L^*\)-algebra is understood broadly as a Hilbert Lie algebra equipped with an involution compatible with its Hilbert inner product and Lie bracket. The supplied record does not specify the scalar field, the precise involution convention, whether simplicity excludes only closed ideals or all ideals, or the definition of a Cartan subalgebra. These conventions must be recovered from the cited report before the statement can be made fully unambiguous.

With \(L^*\)-algebra, simplicity, and Cartan subalgebra interpreted according to that report, the question is: for every simple \(L^*\)-algebra \(L\) and every Cartan subalgebra \(\mathfrak h\subseteq L\), does there exist \(x\in\mathfrak h\) such that
\[
\mathfrak h=C_L(x)?
\]
Equivalently, must there exist \(x\in\mathfrak h\) for which
\[
\forall y\in L,\qquad [y,x]=0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ y\in\mathfrak h?
\]
The centralizer here is taken in the whole algebra \(L\). No separability or finite-dimensionality hypothesis is supplied; none is added to this draft.
