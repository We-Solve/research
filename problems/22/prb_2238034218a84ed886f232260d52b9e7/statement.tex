\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Gamma\) be a discrete group, with no countability or finite generation assumption. Let \(\ell^2(\Gamma)\) be the complex Hilbert space with orthonormal basis \((\delta_x)_{x\in\Gamma}\). For \(s\in\Gamma\), define the left and right regular representations by
\[
\lambda_s\delta_x=\delta_{sx},\qquad \rho_s\delta_x=\delta_{xs^{-1}}.
\]
The reduced group \(C^*\)-algebra is
\[
C_r^*(\Gamma)=\overline{\operatorname{span}\{\lambda_s:s\in\Gamma\}}^{\|\cdot\|}\subseteq\mathcal B(\ell^2(\Gamma)).
\]
To make the uniform Roe algebra and the fixed-point action explicit, use the following paired conventions. A bounded operator \(T\) has finite propagation if there is a finite subset \(F\subseteq\Gamma\) such that
\[
\langle T\delta_y,\delta_x\rangle=0\quad\text{whenever }xy^{-1}\notin F.
\]
Define
\[
UC_r^*(\Gamma)=\overline{\{T\in\mathcal B(\ell^2(\Gamma)):T\text{ has finite propagation}\}}^{\|\cdot\|},
\]
and let \(\Gamma\) act on this algebra by \(\alpha_s(T)=\rho_sT\rho_s^*\). Its fixed-point algebra is
\[
UC_r^*(\Gamma)^\Gamma=\{T\in UC_r^*(\Gamma):\rho_sT\rho_s^*=T\text{ for every }s\in\Gamma\}.
\]
The group \(\Gamma\) has the invariant translation approximation property (ITAP) if these concrete operator algebras satisfy
\[
C_r^*(\Gamma)=UC_r^*(\Gamma)^\Gamma.
\]
The question is whether every discrete group has ITAP:
\[
\text{For every discrete group }\Gamma,\qquad C_r^*(\Gamma)=UC_r^*(\Gamma)^\Gamma\;?
\]

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every discrete group have ITAP: must every operator in its uniform Roe algebra that is invariant under conjugation by all right translations belong to its reduced group \(C^*\)-algebra?
