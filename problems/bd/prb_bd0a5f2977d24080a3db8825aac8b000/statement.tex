\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(M\) be a smooth manifold and let \(g(t)\), \(t\in[0,\infty)\), be a smooth family of Riemannian metrics satisfying the Ricci flow equation
\[
\frac{\partial g(t)}{\partial t}=-2\operatorname{Ric}(g(t)).
\]
The flow is called immortal because it exists for every nonnegative time. Homogeneity means that, for each \(t\), the isometry group of \((M,g(t))\) acts transitively on \(M\).

\textbf{Problem.} For every such immortal homogeneous Ricci flow, determine the asymptotic behavior of \(g(t)\) as \(t\to\infty\).
