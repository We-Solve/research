\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 30002529}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A \emph{K\"ahler group} is a group isomorphic to the fundamental group \(\pi_1(X)\) of a connected compact K\"ahler manifold \(X\). For a group \(G\), let \(H^2(G,\R)\) denote its second group cohomology with coefficients in the trivial \(G\)-module \(\R\); equivalently, this is \(H^2(K(G,1);\R)\), where \(K(G,1)\) is a classifying space for \(G\). A subgroup \(G'\leq G\) has finite index if the set of its cosets in \(G\) is finite.

\textbf{Question (Carlson--Toledo, as identified in the supplied curation).} For every infinite K\"ahler group \(\Gamma\), does there exist a subgroup \(\Gamma'\leq\Gamma\) such that
\[
[\Gamma:\Gamma']<\infty
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
H^2(\Gamma',\R)\neq 0?
\]
The cohomology in this question is group cohomology; it is not, in general, the second cohomology of a compact K\"ahler manifold realizing \(\Gamma'\) as its fundamental group. The supplied record labels the question open; that status and its literature assessment have not been independently verified here.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every infinite group arising as the fundamental group of a connected compact K\"ahler manifold have a finite-index subgroup with nonzero second real group cohomology? The supplied curation identifies this as the Carlson--Toledo question.
