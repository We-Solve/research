\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A domain \(D\subset\C^2\) is a nonempty connected open set. A biholomorphic conjugacy between a self-map \(f|_\Omega\) and a linear map \(L\) on an invariant domain \(D\) means a biholomorphism \(h:\Omega\to D\) such that
\[
L(D)=D,\qquad h\circ(f|_\Omega)=L\circ h.
\]
In particular, such a conjugacy requires \(f|_\Omega\) to be a biholomorphic self-map of \(\Omega\).
