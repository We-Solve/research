\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an integer $n\geq1$, a full-rank lattice in $\R^n$ is $\Lambda=A\Z^n$ with $A\in\operatorname{GL}_n(\R)$. Its covolume is $\det\Lambda=|\det A|$, and its shortest-vector length is
\[
\lambda_1(\Lambda)=\min\{\|v\|_2:v\in\Lambda\setminus\{0\}\}.
\]
Under the conventional squared-length normalization, the Hermite constant is
\[
\gamma_n=\sup_{\Lambda\subset\R^n\text{ full-rank lattice}}
\frac{\lambda_1(\Lambda)^2}{(\det\Lambda)^{2/n}}.
\]
It determines the densest lattice packing density of congruent Euclidean balls by $\Delta_n^{\mathrm{lat}}=\operatorname{vol}_n(B(0,1))\gamma_n^{n/2}/2^n$; the density and the Hermite constant are different quantities.

The literal source asks whether $\gamma_n\in\Q$ for every $n$, and asks for the arithmetic nature of these constants in dimensions whose exact values are unknown. The supplied summary instead says that Voronoi perfection theory gives $\gamma_n^n\in\Q$ for all $n$. Under that supplied assertion, each $\gamma_n$ is algebraic, but rationality of its $n$th power does not imply rationality of $\gamma_n$ itself. The source does not specify whether its rationality question concerns the conventional $\gamma_n$, a power of it, or a differently normalized packing constant. In the conventional normalization some known Hermite constants are irrational, so a universal rationality assertion for $\gamma_n$ cannot be treated as the remaining open problem. A dimension-by-dimension arithmetic classification is a separate target and is not equivalent to determining every exact value.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine the arithmetic nature of Hermite constants, distinguishing the constants themselves from their rational powers and from lattice packing densities. The source's universal rationality question depends critically on normalization.
