We construct, for every sufficiently large $n$, $n$ distinct points on the unit sphere in $\mathbb R^3$ with $\gg n^{4/3}$ pairs at distance $\sqrt2$. This supplies the requested $f(n)\to\infty$, with $f(n)$ a positive constant times $n^{1/3}$. The argument reconstructs the point--line incidence mechanism behind the known Erd\H{o}s--Hickerson--Pach result.
