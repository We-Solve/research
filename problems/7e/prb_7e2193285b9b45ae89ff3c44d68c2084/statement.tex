\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Equip \(\C^2\), with coordinates \(z_j=x_j+iy_j\), with its Euclidean metric, symplectic form \(\omega=\sum_{j=1}^2\dd x_j\wedge\dd y_j\), holomorphic volume form \(\Omega=\dd z_1\wedge\dd z_2\), and Liouville form \(\lambda=\tfrac12\sum_{j=1}^2(x_j\dd y_j-y_j\dd x_j)\).

A smooth Lagrangian mean curvature flow is a family of immersions \(F_t:M^2\to\C^2\), with images \(L_t\), satisfying \(F_t^*\omega=0\) and \((\partial_tF_t)^\perp=H_t\), where \(H_t\) is the mean curvature vector. It is ancient if it is defined arbitrarily far backward in time, conventionally on an interval \(( -\infty,T)\). This notation does not impose maximality or prescribe behavior at \(T\). A slice is exact when \(F_t^*\lambda=\dd f_t\) for a globally defined real function \(f_t\) on \(M\); an exact flow has this property at every time.

For an oriented Lagrangian slice, its phase is defined by \(F_t^*\Omega=e^{i\theta_t}\dd\mu_t\), where \(\dd\mu_t\) is its induced volume form and initially \(e^{i\theta_t}\) is circle-valued. The usual quantitative meaning of almost calibrated is that the phase stays in a fixed open semicircle with a positive margin: for a reference angle \(\alpha\) and \(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
\operatorname{Re}\bigl(e^{-i\alpha}e^{i\theta_t(p)}\bigr)\geq\varepsilon.
\]
For a uniform interpretation on the entire ancient flow, the quantifiers are \(\exists\alpha\in\R\;\exists\varepsilon>0\;\forall t<T\;\forall p\in M\). The supplied record does not specify whether this uniform interpretation, or a slice-dependent bound, is intended.

A backward parabolic blow-down is obtained by taking scales \(r_k\to\infty\) and considering, for a fixed spacetime center \((x_0,t_0)\),
\[
L_s^{(k)}=r_k^{-1}\bigl(L_{t_0+r_k^2s}-x_0\bigr),\qquad s<0,
\]
whenever these slices are defined. The stated limiting configuration is a pair of real two-dimensional Lagrangian planes \(P_1,P_2\) whose intersection is a real line, so that \(\dim_{\R}(P_1\cap P_2)=1\). The record does not specify the convergence notion, multiplicities, choice or independence of center, whether convergence is required for the rescaled flow or only selected slices, or whether one such subsequential limit or every blow-down must have this form. Thus \(P_1\cup P_2\) describes the supplied geometric support, without supplying a multiplicity convention.
