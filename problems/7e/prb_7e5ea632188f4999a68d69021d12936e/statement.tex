\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Under the standard commutative local interpretation, a hypersurface ring is a ring \(R\cong S/(f)\), where \(S\) is a commutative Noetherian regular local ring and \(f\) is a nonzero nonunit nonzerodivisor. The word ``singularity'' ordinarily restricts attention to nonregular \(R\). A finitely generated nonzero \(R\)-module \(X\) is maximal Cohen--Macaulay if
\[
\operatorname{depth}_R X=\dim R.
\]
The ring \(R\) has finite Cohen--Macaulay type if there are only finitely many isomorphism classes of indecomposable maximal Cohen--Macaulay \(R\)-modules. Choose one representative \(M_i\) of each such class and put \(M=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{t}M_i\). With the endomorphism-ring convention, its stable Auslander algebra is
\[
A=\underline{\operatorname{End}}_R(M)
 =\operatorname{End}_R(M)/\mathcal P_R(M,M),
\]
where \(\mathcal P_R(M,M)\) is the ideal of endomorphisms factoring through finitely generated projective \(R\)-modules.

If \(A\) is a finite-dimensional algebra over a field \(k\), the usual meaning of bimodule periodicity is the existence of an integer \(p\geq 1\) such that
\[
\Omega^p_{A^{\mathrm e}}(A)\cong A,
\qquad A^{\mathrm e}=A\otimes_k A^{\mathrm{op}},
\]
as \(A\)-bimodules, where \(\Omega^p\) denotes the \(p\)-th syzygy in a minimal projective \(A^{\mathrm e}\)-resolution of \(A\).

\textbf{Question.} Is the stable Auslander algebra of every hypersurface singularity of finite Cohen--Macaulay type periodic? Under the bimodule interpretation above, this asks whether, for every admissible \(R\), there exists \(p=p(R)\geq 1\) with \(\Omega^p_{A^{\mathrm e}}(A)\cong A\); no common period for all \(R\) is requested.
