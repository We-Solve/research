\section{Explicit Deterministic Restricted Isometry Matrices}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.


A matrix $\Phi$ has restricted-isometry constant at most $\delta_s$ if $(1-\delta_s)\lVert x\rVert_2^2\leq\lVert\Phi x\rVert_2^2\leq(1+\delta_s)\lVert x\rVert_2^2$ for every vector with at most $s$ nonzero coordinates. Compressed sensing recovers structured vectors from measurements $\Phi x$.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Can one construct deterministic explicit measurement matrices with small restricted isometry constants when the number of measurements is small, or equivalently when the sparsity level is large? \sref{statement}{2}
