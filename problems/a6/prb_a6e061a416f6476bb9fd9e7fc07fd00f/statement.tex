A set \(A\subseteq V\) is independent if no two distinct vertices of \(A\) are adjacent. P_k(G)\;\Longleftrightarrow\;
\forall A,B\subseteq V,\quad
\left[
\begin{gathered}
A\text{ is independent},\qquad A\cap B=\varnothing,\\
|A|+|B|\leq k
\end{gathered}
\right]
\Longrightarrow
\left[
\exists x\in V\quad
(\forall a\in A,\ x\sim_G a)
