For distinct interpolation nodes $x_1,\ldots,x_n\in[-1,1]$, let $l_j$ be their Lagrange basis polynomials and $\lambda(x)=\sum_j|l_j(x)|$. The question asks whether every fixed nondegenerate interval $I\subset[-1,1]$ satisfies
\[
 \max_{x\in I}\lambda(x)\ge(2/\pi-o(1))\log n.
\]
Tao has proved the stronger lower bound $(2/\pi)\log n-O_I(1)$ for arbitrary nodes; see \emph{Local Bernstein theory, and lower bounds for Lebesgue constants}, \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21453}, and the earlier proof note \url{https://terrytao.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/erdos-1153-1.pdf}. The present attempt does not reconstruct the general theorem. It proves the sharp local lower bound for Chebyshev nodes and a necessary potential-flatness condition for any hypothetical small-Lebesgue-function configuration. The arbitrary-node problem remains unresolved within this attempt.
