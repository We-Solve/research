\section{Feasible High-Performance Plasma Flows for Stellarators}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A stellarator confines plasma using a three-dimensional magnetic geometry. A candidate flow must satisfy the relevant plasma equilibrium and dynamical laws as well as engineering constraints. Stability, efficiency and geometric realizability are different requirements; excluding self-intersections constrains the configuration geometry.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Does there exist a stellarator plasma-flow configuration that is simultaneously highly efficient, dynamically stable, and physically buildable? Develop methods for exploring the configuration space while excluding self-intersections and other nonphysical deformations.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The report poses a design programme. Performance objectives, stability norm and engineering admissibility are needed to define a particular optimization problem.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find a stable, efficient and buildable stellarator plasma-flow configuration, together with methods for exploring physically admissible designs.
