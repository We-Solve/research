\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \([n]=\{1,2,\ldots,n\}\) for a positive integer \(n\). Under the usual interpretation of the integer grid \([n]^d\) as a subset of \(\R^d\), for integers \(d\geq 1\) and \(0\leq k\leq d\), define
\[
\alpha_{d,k}(n)=\max\left\{|A|: A\subseteq[n]^d,\quad |A\cap L|\leq k+1\text{ for every affine subspace }L\subseteq\R^d\text{ with }\dim L=k\right\}.
\]
Thus the forbidden configuration consists of \(k+2\) distinct points contained in a common \(k\)-dimensional affine subspace. Configurations whose affine span has dimension less than \(k\) are also forbidden, since they are contained in a \(k\)-dimensional affine subspace.

\textbf{Problem.} For each fixed pair \((d,k)\) in the intended parameter range, determine the asymptotic order of \(\alpha_{d,k}(n)\) as \(n\to\infty\). In particular, determine the asymptotic order of
\[
\alpha_{4,2}(n)=\max\left\{|A|:A\subseteq[n]^4,\quad |A\cap L|\leq 3\text{ for every affine plane }L\subseteq\R^4\right\}.
\]
Determining the asymptotic order means finding a positive function \(f_{d,k}(n)\) and constants \(c_{d,k},C_{d,k}>0\) such that, for all sufficiently large positive integers \(n\),
\[
c_{d,k}f_{d,k}(n)\leq\alpha_{d,k}(n)\leq C_{d,k}f_{d,k}(n).
\]
The parameters \(d,k\) are held fixed; the constants and the threshold for \(n\) may depend on them.

\subsection{Short English statement}
For a fixed dimension \(d\) and parameter \(k\), find how the largest subset of the integer grid \(\{1,\ldots,n\}^d\) with no \(k+2\) distinct points in a common affine \(k\)-dimensional subspace grows as \(n\to\infty\). The highlighted case asks for the largest subset of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}^4\) with no four points in an affine plane.
