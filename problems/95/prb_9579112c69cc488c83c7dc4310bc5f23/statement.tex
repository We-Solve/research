\section{Phase Retrieval from Gabor and Wavelet Magnitudes}\label{30004928}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 30004928 · Computer Science}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A frame $(\phi_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ in a Hilbert space $H$ satisfies $A\|f\|^2\leq\sum_\lambda|\langle f,\phi_\lambda\rangle|^2\leq B\|f\|^2$ for constants $0<A\leq B<\infty$. Magnitudes cannot distinguish $f$ from $cf$ for $|c|=1$, so uniqueness is understood modulo this global phase. Stability bounds reconstruction error in this quotient by measurement error. Gabor systems use translations and modulations of a window; wavelet systems use translations and dilations.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
What uniqueness and stability guarantees hold for recovering $f\in H$ from magnitude measurements $(|\langle f,\phi_\lambda\rangle|)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$, particularly for Gabor or wavelet frames? \sref{30004928}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
The frame, signal class, measurement norm, and local or uniform form of stability are parameters of the question; no universal stable inverse for every frame is asserted.
