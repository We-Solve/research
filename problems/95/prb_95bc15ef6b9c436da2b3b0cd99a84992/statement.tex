For a finite set $A$ of integers, suppose $|A+A|\le K|A|$, with $K$ fixed. Prove that $|AA|\gg_K |A|^2/\log(2|A|)$. This is the affirmative resolution of Solymosi. The proof below reconstructs his multiplicative-energy argument, with deliberately unoptimized constants and with the reduction from arbitrary signs included. No sum--product theorem is assumed as an external input.
