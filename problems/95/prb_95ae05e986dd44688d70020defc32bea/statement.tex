\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $n\geq2$, let $\Delta\subset\mathbb C^n$ be a ball containing the origin, and let $(F,g)$ be a complete Hermitian manifold. Completeness means completeness of the distance defined by the underlying Riemannian metric of $g$. The source asks whether every holomorphic map
\[
f:\Delta\setminus\{0\}\longrightarrow F
\]
has a holomorphic extension $\widetilde f:\Delta\to F$ satisfying $\widetilde f|_{\Delta\setminus\{0\}}=f$.

This is the unqualified statement supplied in the record: it assumes completeness but imposes no curvature bound on $F$. The accompanying research summary instead describes an affirmative extension theorem with the additional hypothesis of nonpositive holomorphic sectional curvature, attributed to Siu and Shiffman, and also alludes to compact analytic exceptional sets without specifying their conditions. That stronger target hypothesis is not inserted into the original question. A theorem requiring it does not by itself establish the record's affirmative claim for arbitrary complete Hermitian targets.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must a holomorphic map from a punctured complex ball of dimension at least two into any complete Hermitian manifold extend across the puncture? The supplied resolution summary adds a curvature assumption absent from the actual question.
