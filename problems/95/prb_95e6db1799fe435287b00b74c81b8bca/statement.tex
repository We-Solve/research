\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $c(K)$ denote the crossing number of a knot or link $K$, namely the minimum number of crossings among its diagrams. Let $v_8$ denote the hyperbolic volume of a regular ideal octahedron in hyperbolic three-space of sectional curvature $-1$. Write $\log$ for the natural logarithm.

Under the usual interpretation of knots and links in $S^3$, the determinant $\det(K)$ can be defined as the order of $H_1(\Sigma_2(K);\Z)$ when this group is finite, and as zero otherwise, where $\Sigma_2(K)$ is the double cover of $S^3$ branched over $K$. Thus the real expression $\log\det(K)$ requires $\det(K)>0$. When $S^3\setminus K$ admits a complete finite-volume hyperbolic metric, $\operatorname{vol}(K)$ denotes its hyperbolic volume. \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{vol}(K_n)}{c(K_n)}=v_8,
