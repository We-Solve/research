Erdős Problem 494 (with Selfridge and Straus) asks whether a finite set of complex numbers
is determined by its multiset of $k$-subset sums. This attack targets the research-solved
PRODUCT variant shipped as `sorry` in formal-conjectures: the product version is FALSE
(Steinerberger) — there exist $A \ne B$ with equal cardinality and equal multisets of
3-subset products. Lean statement (prodMultiset verbatim from the corpus):
\begin{verbatim}
theorem product :
    exists (A B : Finset C), A.card = B.card /\
      prodMultiset A 3 = prodMultiset B 3 /\ A != B
\end{verbatim}
