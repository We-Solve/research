\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For $n\geq1$, let the $n$th Weyl algebra over $\C$ be
\[
 W_n=\C\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n,y_1,\ldots,y_n\rangle/
 ([x_i,x_j],\,[y_i,y_j],\,[x_i,y_j]-\delta_{ij}1),
\]
where $[a,b]=ab-ba$. Write $\operatorname{Aut}_{\C}(W_n)$ for unital $\C$-algebra automorphisms.

On $\C^{2n}$ with coordinates $(x_1,\ldots,x_n,y_1,\ldots,y_n)$ fix the standard symplectic form $\omega=\sum_i dx_i\wedge dy_i$. Let
\[
 \operatorname{Symp}_{\mathrm{poly}}(\C^{2n},\omega)
 =\{F:\C^{2n}\to\C^{2n}: F,F^{-1}\text{ polynomial},\ F^*\omega=\omega\}.
\]
Equivalently one can use polynomial-algebra automorphisms preserving the standard Poisson bracket, with the usual inverse-pullback convention if identifying their group with geometric composition.

The source asks whether, for each $n$,
\[
 \operatorname{Aut}_{\C}(W_n)
       \cong\operatorname{Symp}_{\mathrm{poly}}(\C^{2n},\omega).
\]

Taking principal symbols is not automatically a construction on every automorphism unless the needed filtration behavior is established. \subsection{Short English statement}
For every $n$, are the complex algebra automorphisms of the Weyl algebra isomorphic as a group to polynomial automorphisms preserving the standard symplectic form on $\C^{2n}$? The source does not specify a stronger natural, filtered or ind-group equivalence.
