\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S\subset\R^n$ be the intended hypersurface of centers, with $n\geq2$. In the unnormalized spherical-integral convention, define
\[
(R_Sf)(x,r)=\int_{|y-x|=r}f(y)\,\dd\sigma(y),
\qquad x\in S,\quad r>0.
\]
Here $\sigma$ is Euclidean surface measure. The spherical-mean convention differs by the known factor $|S^{n-1}|r^{n-1}$; a convention must be fixed for inversion and range formulas. Smooth compactly supported functions $f\in C_c^\infty(\R^n)$ provide a concrete test class, while the source's full compact-support class is not specified.

For the intended hypersurfaces and function spaces, determine:
\begin{enumerate}
\item injectivity, in particular whether $R_Sf=0$ implies $f=0$ for every compactly supported admissible $f$;
\item inversion formulas recovering $f$ from $R_Sf$ when recovery is possible, and stability in specified input and data topologies;
\item how these properties change when only data on a proper subset $D\subset S\times(0,\infty)$ are available;
\item the range, namely necessary and sufficient conditions on data $g(x,r)$ for the existence of an admissible $f$ with $g=R_Sf$.
\end{enumerate}
