\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(k\) denote the common ground field, which is not specified in the supplied record. A Hopf algebra \(u\) over \(k\) is \emph{pointed} if every simple subcoalgebra of \(u\) is one-dimensional, equivalently if its coradical is spanned by its group-like elements. Here a group-like element is an element \(x\) satisfying \(\Delta(x)=x\otimes x\) and \(\varepsilon(x)=1\). The finite-dimensionality requirement is \(\dim_k u<\infty\).

For a Lie algebra \(\mathfrak g''\) over \(k\), its universal enveloping algebra is
\[
U(\mathfrak g'')=T_k(\mathfrak g'')/\langle x\otimes y-y\otimes x-[x,y]:x,y\in\mathfrak g''\rangle,
\]
with its usual Hopf structure, in which the elements of \(\mathfrak g''\) are primitive.

\textbf{Classification problem.} Classify all extensions of the form
\[
u\longrightarrow H\longrightarrow U(\mathfrak g'')

where \(u\) is a finite-dimensional pointed Hopf algebra, and determine the corresponding conformal field theories.
