\section{Local Weak Cosmic Censorship in Charged Spherical Collapse}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


In spherical symmetry the spacetime is represented by a two-dimensional quotient with symmetry spheres. The center is the boundary where their area radius vanishes; $\widehat\Gamma$ denotes its terminal endpoint in the report. A locally naked singularity has future-directed causal curves entering a regular region from the singular endpoint.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Local Weak Cosmic Censorship Conjecture: for generic spacetimes arising from charged spherical collapse, the center endpoint $\widehat{\Gamma}$ is not a locally naked singularity.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The charged collapse model and meaning of genericity are those of the source; neither an uncharged variant nor a universal assertion for every initial datum is substituted.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine whether the final center point of generic charged spherical collapse is locally hidden.
