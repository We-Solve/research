\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S$ be the normalized schlicht class: the injective analytic functions on $\mathbb D$ with $f(0)=0$ and $f'(0)=1$. Since $f(z)/z$ is analytic and zero-free with value one at zero, its analytic logarithm normalized to vanish at zero defines
\[
 \log\frac{f(z)}z=2\sum_{k=1}^\infty\gamma_k z^k.
\]
Real coefficients means that every coefficient of $f(z)=z+\sum_{j\geq2}a_jz^j$ is real. A standard close-to-convex convention is existence of a convex univalent function $g$ and $\alpha\in\R$ such that $\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\alpha}f'/g')>0$ in $\mathbb D$.

The displayed Milin conjecture in the source is
\[
 \forall N\geq1,\qquad
 \sum_{n=1}^{N}\sum_{k=1}^{n} k|\gamma_k|^2
 \leq\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac1k.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 \sum_{k=1}^{N}(N+1-k)\left(k|\gamma_k|^2-\frac1k\right)\leq0.
\]
P. L. Duren's question asks whether the source inequality labeled C6.4 holds for all $N$ if $f$ has real coefficients, or if $f$ is close-to-convex. The excerpt does not attach that label explicitly to a displayed formula; identifying it with the double-sum inequality above is strongly suggested by context but remains a source-label dependency.

The source also reports that starlike functions satisfy $|\gamma_k|\leq1/k$, whereas this fails for general $S$ even in order of magnitude. It attributes to Milin the distinct single-sum estimate
\[
 \sum_{k=1}^{n}k|\gamma_k|^2\leq\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac1k+\delta,
 \qquad\delta<0.312,
\]
and says its correction cannot be replaced by zero. This is not the weighted double-sum statement and must not be conflated with it. The excerpt reports C6.4 for $N=1,2,3$ and says the double-sum conjecture would imply Robertson's conjecture, referring to Problem 6.39.
