\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $f:\C\to\C$ be nonconstant and entire, let $k\geq2$ be an integer, and let $a,b\in\C$ satisfy $b\ne0$ and $b\ne a$. Sharing $a$ ignoring multiplicity (IM) means equality of the level sets:
\[
\forall z\in\C,\qquad f(z)=a\iff f'(z)=a,
\]
without requiring equal zero multiplicities of $f-a$ and $f'-a$. Sharing counting multiplicity (CM) additionally requires those multiplicities to agree. The separate hypothesis on $b$ is only the implication
\[
\forall z\in\C,\qquad f'(z)=b\Longrightarrow f^{(k)}(z)=b.
\]
The requested conclusion is retained exactly:
\[
\exists c,d\in\C^\times,\quad c^{k-1}=1,\qquad
f(z)=d e^{cz}+c^{-1}a\quad\text{for every }z\in\C.
\]

But the proposed conclusion forces $c=1$ and $e^z=d e^z+1$, which cannot hold identically. Thus the printed CM theorem and proposed IM conclusion cannot have exactly these hypotheses and this formula simultaneously.
