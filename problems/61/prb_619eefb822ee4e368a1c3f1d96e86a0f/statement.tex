\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(\Gamma\) be a finite group of Lie type, let \(k\) be a coefficient field, and let \(M\) be a simple \(k\Gamma\)-module. Its group cohomology is
\[
H^i(\Gamma,M)=\operatorname{Ext}^i_{k\Gamma}(k,M),
\]
where \(k\) denotes the trivial \(k\Gamma\)-module.

For a specified root datum with chosen simple coroots \(\alpha_1^\vee,\ldots,\alpha_\ell^\vee\), the fundamental weights \(\omega_j\) satisfy
\[
\langle\omega_j,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=\delta_{ij}
\qquad(1\leq i,j\leq\ell).
\]
Here a fundamental dominant highest weight means one of these \(\omega_j\), provided it belongs to the character lattice of the chosen algebraic group. A highest-weight description of a finite-group module requires an appropriate algebraic-group realization and a specified relationship between its highest-weight modules and the finite-group modules.

for finite groups of Lie type \(\Gamma\) and simple coefficient modules \(M\) whose highest weight, in the intended highest-weight realization, is fundamental dominant, throughout the family intended by the source.
