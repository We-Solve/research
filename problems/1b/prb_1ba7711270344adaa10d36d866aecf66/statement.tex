Determine the asymptotic value of \(\operatorname{ex}(n,C_4)\), the maximum edge count in a simple \(n\)-vertex graph containing no four-cycle. We reconstruct the classical answer
\[
\operatorname{ex}(n,C_4)=\left(\frac12+o(1)\right)n^{3/2}.
\]
