All polynomial identities below are identities in \(A\), so sums of squares are taken modulo the homogeneous vanishing ideal \(I(X)\). For an integer \(d\geq0\), set
\[
\Sigma_{X,2d}=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{r}h_i^2:r\in\Z_{\geq0},\ h_i\in A_d\right\}.

Define \(P_{X,2d}\subseteq A_{2d}\) to consist of those forms whose values are nonnegative at every nonzero real homogeneous representative of a point of \(X(\R)\). This condition is independent of the representative because the degree is even.

pq\in\Sigma_{X,2(s+k)}.

For an irreducible nondegenerate real projective curve with Zariski-dense real points, seek an explicit degree bound, uniform over all nonnegative forms of a fixed even degree, for a sum-of-squares multiplier making their product a sum of squares on the curve.
