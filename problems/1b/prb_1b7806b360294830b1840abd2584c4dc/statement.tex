\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in\C:|z|<1\}$ and $m$ be planar Lebesgue measure. For $K\geq1$, use the standard orientation-preserving convention: a $K$-quasiconformal map $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D$ is a homeomorphism onto the disc, belongs to $W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}$, and satisfies
\[
\|Df(z)\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2\leq KJ_f(z)
\quad\text{for almost every }z\in\mathbb D,
\]
where $J_f=\det Df$.

F. W. Gehring's requested estimate is
\[
\forall K\geq1\ \exists b(K)<\infty\ \forall f\ \forall E\subseteq\mathbb D
\text{ measurable}:\quad
m(f(E))\leq b(K)m(E)^{1/K},
\]
with $f$ ranging over the intended normalized class of such maps and the bound independent of both $f$ and $E$. The supplied statement, however, gives no normalization at all. That omission is material: already at $K=1$ disk automorphisms can have arbitrarily large local area distortion as their normalization points approach the boundary. Thus the full unnormalized class cannot carry a single bound of the displayed form. A condition such as $f(0)=0$ is a plausible missing normalization, but is not inserted as a verified source assumption.

The source says an estimate of this type was known with exponent $a(K)$ in place of $1/K$, but its citation and accompanying normalization are absent. Its intended target is the precise exponent $1/K$, not merely another positive exponent. The supplied summary labels the problem open as of the 2018 edition and reports no definitive resolution located; those status claims do not resolve the formulation's normalization defect and are not independently verified here.
