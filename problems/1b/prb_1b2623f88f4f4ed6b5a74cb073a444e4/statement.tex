\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(A:\R\to\R\) satisfy \(|A(x_1)-A(x_2)|\le M|x_1-x_2|\) for a finite Lipschitz constant \(M\), and let
\[
\Gamma=\{x+iA(x):x\in\R\},\qquad E\subseteq\Gamma\text{ compact},\qquad
\Omega=\widehat\C\setminus E.
\]
The source denotes \(\widehat\C=\C\cup\{\infty\}\) by \(\C^*\); this is the Riemann sphere, not the punctured plane. Let \(H^\infty(\Omega)\) be the bounded holomorphic functions on \(\Omega\), with holomorphy at \(\infty\) interpreted in the coordinate \(1/z\).

Garnett asks to prove the corona theorem for this domain. In its finite-generator form, the target is: for every integer \(m\ge1\) and \(f_1,\ldots,f_m\in H^\infty(\Omega)\) satisfying
\[
\inf_{z\in\Omega}\sum_{j=1}^m|f_j(z)|>0,
\]
there exist \(g_1,\ldots,g_m\in H^\infty(\Omega)\) such that
\[
\sum_{j=1}^m f_j(z)g_j(z)=1\qquad(z\in\Omega).
\]
No quantitative uniform bounds on the solution norms beyond boundedness are requested in the excerpt.

The source additionally inserts the hypothesis \(\Delta_1(t)>0\), but does not define \(\Delta_1\), identify its dependence on \(E\) or \(\Gamma\), or state a range or quantifier for \(t\). That clause is material and cannot simply be discarded; the corona target above is conditional on the intended full hypotheses, rather than a strengthened claim for every compact subset without that clause.
