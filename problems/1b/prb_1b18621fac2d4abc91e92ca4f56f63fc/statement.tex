\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a diffeomorphism \(f:M\to M\) of a compact Riemannian manifold, let \(\mathcal M_f(M)\) be the compact convex space of \(f\)-invariant Borel probability measures. Its entropy function is \(\mu\mapsto h_\mu(f)\), the measure-theoretic entropy. The entropy structure is finer data: in the standard theory it is an equivalence class of specified sequences of entropy approximations \((h_k)\) on \(\mathcal M_f(M)\), converging to this entropy function, with equivalence given by uniform domination in both directions. Namely, for two admissible sequences on the same base, domination of \((v_k)\) by \((u_j)\) means
\[
 \forall k\ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists j\ \forall\mu:
 \quad v_k(\mu)\le u_j(\mu)+\varepsilon.
\]
Only the approximation sequences allowed by entropy-structure theory are intended; an arbitrary pointwise approximation to entropy is not a definition of an entropy structure. The supplied excerpt does not give their construction or axioms.
