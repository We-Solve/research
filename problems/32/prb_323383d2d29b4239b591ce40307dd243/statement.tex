\textbf{First question: symplectic structures and compactification.}
Given an abelian fibration \(f\colon X\to B\), determine conditions under which its total space admits a symplectic structure making \(f\) a Lagrangian fibration and permitting a compactification to a possibly singular irreducible symplectic variety.

\[
\dim_{\C}F=n,\qquad \sigma|_F=0.
\]
