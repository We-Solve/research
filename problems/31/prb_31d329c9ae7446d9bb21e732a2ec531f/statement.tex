The proposed half-of-the-vertices bound is false, even for $K_4$-free graphs. We reconstruct Ma and Tang's explicit construction from \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/1034}: for every sufficiently large $n$, it gives
\[
 e(G)>n^2/4,\qquad G\text{ has no }K_4,\qquad
 |Y(T)|\le(2\sqrt3-3)n+O(1)
\tag{1}
\]
for every triangle $T$, where $Y(T)$ consists of vertices outside $T$ adjacent to at least two of its vertices. Since $2\sqrt3-3<1/2$, this settles the original negative answer. The better unrestricted constant recorded on the problem page is not needed.
