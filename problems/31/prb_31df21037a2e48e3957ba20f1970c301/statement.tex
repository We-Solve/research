\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be an Enriques surface over a number field \(k\), in the usual characteristic-zero smooth projective geometrically integral sense. Skorobogatov's four AIM questions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Does \(X(k)\ne\varnothing\) imply that \(X(k)\) is Zariski dense?
\item Can \(X(\mathbb A_k)\ne\varnothing\) but \(X(k)=\varnothing\)?
\item Can \(X(\mathbb A_k)\ne\varnothing\) but \(X(\mathbb A_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}=\varnothing\)?
\item Can \(X(k)=\varnothing\) but \(X(\mathbb A_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}\ne\varnothing\)?
\end{enumerate}
Here \(X(\mathbb A_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}\) is the adelic set orthogonal to all Brauer classes under the sum of local invariant pairings. The report says parts (b), (c), and (d) have affirmative examples over \(\Q\), while the original-field density implication (a) remains open. No equations or complete references for these examples are supplied; potential density after extension is not substituted for part (a).

The source's Harari remark asks about the canonical \(\Z/2\Z\)-cover \(Y\to X\) by a K3 surface: can a torus torsor \(W\to Y\) have composite \(W\to X\) a torsor under a nonabelian group with compatible action? It asks an analogous question for bielliptic surfaces. The torus, nonabelian group, and required descent compatibility are not specified.

The report proposes a finite audit for canonical-cover descent. Choose the canonical cover \(\pi:Y\to X\) as a \(\mu_2\)-torsor and let \(\pi_\alpha:Y_\alpha\to X\) be its twists. Choose a sufficiently large finite set \(S\) of places, containing archimedean places, those above \(2\), and the relevant bad places so that the stated integral-model conditions hold. In the report's canonical-cover \'etale--Brauer/descent formula it asserts that the union of images
\[
\bigcup_{\alpha\in H^1(k,\mu_2)}
\pi_\alpha\bigl(Y_\alpha(\mathbb A_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}\bigr)
\]
can be restricted to the \(S\)-unramified classes \(H^1(\mathcal O_{k,S},\mu_2)\). The precise good-model hypotheses and whether the named descent set means this cover contribution or the full \'etale--Brauer set are not spelled out in the summary.

Let \(r_1,r_2\) be the numbers of real and complex places of \(k\), let \(S_f\) be the finite places in \(S\), and let \(\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_{k,S})[2]\) be the two-torsion in the \(S\)-ideal class group. The candidate explicit count is
\[
\left|H^1(\mathcal O_{k,S},\mu_2)\right|
=2^{r_1+r_2+|S_f|}\left|\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_{k,S})[2]\right|.
\]
It counts twist classes to audit, not automatically nonempty adelic images or rational points. The report also claims a conditional density case from a relative-Jacobian translation criterion, but gives no criterion hypotheses or formula. None of these reductions resolves unconditional part (a) or Harari's torsor question.
