\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \((M^n,g)\) be a smooth complete Riemannian manifold with nonnegative Ricci curvature, meaning \(\operatorname{Ric}_g(v,v)\geq 0\) for every tangent vector \(v\). Fix a basepoint \(p\in M\), and write \(\pi_1(M,p)\) for the fundamental group of based loops modulo based homotopy. This group is finitely generated if some finite subset generates it. The questions below use the usual convention of connected manifolds without boundary; these conventions are implicit rather than explicitly stated in the supplied source. If disconnected manifolds are allowed, formulate each question for the component containing \(p\).

Let \(\pi:(\widetilde M,\widetilde g)\to(M,g)\) be the universal covering with the lifted metric \(\widetilde g=\pi^*g\). Here Euclidean volume growth means that, for some \(\widetilde p\in\widetilde M\),
\[
\liminf_{r\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{Vol}_{\widetilde g}(B_{\widetilde g}(\widetilde p,r))}{r^n}>0.
\]
Ricci-flat means \(\operatorname{Ric}_g\equiv0\). A Kähler structure means an integrable complex structure \(J\) compatible with \(g\), whose associated two-form \(\omega(X,Y)=g(JX,Y)\) is closed; in this case \(n=2m\) for a positive integer \(m\).

The supplied record asks the following four separate finite-generation questions, attributed to the discussion of Milnor's conjecture:
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every \(n\in\{4,5,6\}\) and every smooth complete \((M^n,g)\) with \(\operatorname{Ric}_g\geq0\), is \(\pi_1(M,p)\) finitely generated?
\item For every positive integer \(n\) and every smooth complete Ricci-flat \((M^n,g)\), is \(\pi_1(M,p)\) finitely generated?
\item For every positive integer \(m\) and every complete Kähler manifold \((M^{2m},g,J)\) with \(\operatorname{Ric}_g\geq0\), is \(\pi_1(M,p)\) finitely generated?
\item For every positive integer \(n\) and every smooth complete \((M^n,g)\) with \(\operatorname{Ric}_g\geq0\) whose universal cover has Euclidean volume growth, is \(\pi_1(M,p)\) finitely generated?
\end{enumerate}
The additional hypotheses in the last three questions are imposed separately. No compactness assumption or additional tangent-cone hypothesis is imposed. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently establish the status of any of these questions.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must the fundamental group of a complete manifold with nonnegative Ricci curvature be finitely generated in dimensions \(4,5,6\)? The record also asks this in arbitrary dimension under each separate additional assumption: Ricci-flatness, a Kähler structure, or Euclidean volume growth of the universal cover.
