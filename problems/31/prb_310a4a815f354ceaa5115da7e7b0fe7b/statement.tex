\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#518; the sufficiently-large-order bound and its sharpness, 2026-09-24.}
In every red-blue coloring of $K_n$, can all vertices be covered by at most $\sqrt n$ monochromatic paths of one common color? Paths may overlap, and singleton vertices count as paths. Let $f(n)$ be the largest, over such colorings, of the minimum number of paths required.
