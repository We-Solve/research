Write \(\operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{top}}(V,h^V)\) for the differential Becker--Gottlieb transfer index, and write \(\operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{an}}(V,h^V)\) for the analytic index corrected by the Bismut--Lott torsion form. Define their difference, in the common differential index group of the referenced construction, by
\[
\delta(V,h^V;\pi,g)
:=\operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{top}}(V,h^V)

\[
\delta(V,h^V;\pi,g)=0,
