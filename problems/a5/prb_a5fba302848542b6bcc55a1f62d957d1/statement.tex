Let \(N\geq 1\) be an integer and let \(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_N\subset\R^3\) be affine lines. Set
\[
A=\bigcup_{i=1}^{N}(\ell_i\cap[0,1]^3),\qquad L=H^1(A),
\]
where \(H^1\) denotes length and \(H^3\) denotes three-dimensional volume, using the usual normalization of Hausdorff measure. The measure is that of the union as a set, without multiplicity.

The proposed question is whether there exists an absolute constant \(c>0\), independent of \(N\) and of the lines, such that for every such union with positive length,
\[
D_N(A)\geq c\,\frac{\log L}{L},

\subsection{Short English statement}
The question asks whether the discrepancy of normalized length on every finite union of lines clipped to the unit cube has a universal lower bound of order \(\log L/L\), where \(L\) is the total length of the union. The test-set family is unspecified, and zero-length unions make the formulas undefined, so these qualifications require clarification.
