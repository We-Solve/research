Let $n_0<n_1<\cdots$ be integers such that
$\mu_k=2^{\aleph_{n_k}}$ is strictly increasing and $\mu_0>\aleph_\omega$. With cardinal sum
\[
 \lambda=\sum_{k<\omega}\mu_k,
\]
must $\lambda\to(\aleph_\omega)^2_2$ hold? The notation means that every two-colouring of the unordered pairs from a set of size $\lambda$ has a monochromatic subset of size $\aleph_\omega$. Shelah proved the affirmative answer.

We prove the cardinal-arithmetic reductions, obtain homogeneous sets of every smaller prescribed finite-index aleph from a precise classical partition theorem, and exhibit why this does not furnish a homogeneous $\aleph_\omega$-set. Shelah's singular-limit coherence argument is not reconstructed, so the requested proof remains unresolved in this attempt.
