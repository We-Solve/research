Let \(A\subseteq\mathbb N\) be infinite, with
\(A(N)=|A\cap[1,N]|=o(N)\), and put
\(D(N)=|(A-A)\cap[1,N]|\).
The general theorem, due to Ruzsa, asserts
\(\limsup D(N)/A(N)=\infty\).
The general proof remains unresolved in this write-up.
We prove the assertion for sets of positive upper
Banach density and for every lacunary sequence.
The first case includes very sparse unions of long,
densely occupied intervals; the second includes sets
with large multiplicative gaps.
