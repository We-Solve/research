\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a rational self-map \(R:\widehat\C\to\widehat\C\), let \(\operatorname{Crit}(R)\) be its critical set and put
\[
 P_R=\overline{\bigcup_{n\geq1}R^n(\operatorname{Crit}(R))}
 \subset\widehat\C.
\]
Since a quasicircle is closed, using the unclosed postcritical union gives the same containment requirement. A spherical quasicircle is the image of \(S^1\) under a quasiconformal homeomorphism of \(\widehat\C\); the source additionally requires \(C\subset\C\), so its curve avoids infinity.

For a given rational map in the intended but unspecified class, the question is whether there exists such a planar quasicircle with
\[
 P_R\subseteq C,
 \qquad C\subseteq R^{-1}(C).
\]
The second condition is precisely \(R(C)\subseteq C\), a forward-invariance containment; it is not the reverse containment or complete invariance. The usual nontrivial dynamical setting takes \(\deg R\geq2\), but the source does not state a degree range.
