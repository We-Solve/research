\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $n\geq2$ and let $S\subset\R^n$ be compact with $0<|S|<\infty$, where $|\cdot|$ is $n$-dimensional Lebesgue measure. Assume its volume centroid is the origin: $|S|^{-1}\int_S x\,dx=0$. Let $\mathcal K$ consist of all compact convex sets with nonempty interior and also $\varnothing$, and let $S\mathbin{\triangle}C=(S\setminus C)\cup(C\setminus S)$. The source chooses
\[
 C_*\in\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{C\in\mathcal K}|S\mathbin{\triangle}C|
\]
and calls $S$ $\eta$-convex if $|S\mathbin{\triangle}C_*|\leq\eta|S|$. Equivalently one may describe the optimal normalized discrepancy $\inf_{C\in\mathcal K}|S\mathbin{\triangle}C|/|S|$; replacing attainment by an infimum alone would need care at an exact endpoint.

Choose $X,Y$ independently and uniformly with respect to volume on $S$. Almost surely they span a two-dimensional linear subspace $H(X,Y)$; put $P(X,Y)=S\cap H(X,Y)$. Denote by $\mu_S$ the resulting distribution on central two-planes, or equivalently the induced distribution of these sections. Santosh Vempala's proposed implication is that there is an absolute constant $A>0$ such that, for all such $n,S$ and tolerances $0\leq\varepsilon\leq1$,
\[
 \Pr\bigl(P(X,Y)\text{ is convex}\bigr)\geq1-\varepsilon
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 |S\mathbin{\triangle}C_*|\leq A n\varepsilon |S|.
\]
This makes the source's $O(n\varepsilon)$ conclusion explicit with a constant independent of $n$, $S$, and $\varepsilon$. The positive-volume, dimension, tolerance-range, and independence conventions are needed to define the sampling statement; they are not spelled out in the damaged excerpt. Convexity in the premise concerns the actual section as a set, whereas the conclusion measures discrepancy only up to null sets.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Sample two volume-uniform points and examine the central plane they span. The conjecture says that if almost all these sections are convex, then the set differs from a convex body by only an $O(n\varepsilon)$ fraction of its volume. The sampling favors planes according to the set itself. The supplied partial results describe this weighting and robust nonconvexity, with a separate regularity assumption for exact setwise convexity.
