Let \((M,\omega)\) be a closed symplectic manifold, where closed means compact and without boundary. The supplied record does not define the displacement-energy and Poisson-bracket conventions. For a smooth Hamiltonian \(H:[0,1]\times M\to\R\), define its Hamiltonian vector field by \(\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=-\dd H_t\), and denote its flow by \(\phi_H^t\). Put
\[
\ell(H)=\int_0^1\left(\max_{x\in M}H_t(x)-\min_{x\in M}H_t(x)\right)\dd t.
\]
Under the working convention that displacement means \(\phi_H^1(U)\cap U=\varnothing\), an open set \(U\subset M\) is displaceable if such an \(H\) exists, and its displacement energy is
\[
e(U)=\inf\{\ell(H):\phi_H^1(U)\cap U=\varnothing\}.
\]
The record leaves unresolved whether displacement is instead required for the closure of \(U\).

Let \(\mathcal U=\{U_i\}_{i\in I}\) be a finite open cover of \(M\). A smooth partition of unity subordinate to \(\mathcal U\) is a family \(f=(f_i)_{i\in I}\) with \(f_i\in C^\infty(M,[0,1])\), \(\operatorname{supp}(f_i)\subset U_i\), and \(\sum_{i\in I}f_i=1\). For smooth real-valued functions \(F,G\), use \(\{F,G\}=\omega(X_F,X_G)\) and \(\|F\|_\infty=\max_{x\in M}|F(x)|\). Define
\[
\operatorname{pb}(f)=\sup_{a,b\in[-1,1]^I}
\left\|\left\{\sum_{i\in I}a_i f_i,\sum_{j\in I}b_j f_j\right\}\right\|_\infty,
\qquad
\operatorname{pb}(\mathcal U)=\inf_{f\text{ subordinate to }\mathcal U}\operatorname{pb}(f),
\]
and, when all cover elements are displaceable, put
\[
e(\mathcal U)=\max_{i\in I}e(U_i).
\]
