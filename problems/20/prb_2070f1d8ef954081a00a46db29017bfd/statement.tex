\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Under the usual interpretation of the selection-principle notation, let \(K\) be a topological group with identity \(e_K\). Write \(\mathcal O(K)\) for the family of open covers of \(K\), and put
\[
\mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}}(K)=\left\{\{kU:k\in K\}: U\subseteq K\text{ is open and }e_K\in U\right\}.
\]
The property \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}},\mathcal O)\) means that for every sequence \((\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq 1}\) from \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}}(K)\), there are finite subfamilies \(\mathcal V_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n\) such that \(\bigcup_{n\geq 1}\mathcal V_n\) covers \(K\). Equivalently, for every sequence \((U_n)_{n\geq 1}\) of open identity neighborhoods, there are finite sets \(F_n\subseteq K\) such that
\[
K=\bigcup_{n\geq 1}F_nU_n,
\qquad F_nU_n=\{fu:f\in F_n,\ u\in U_n\}.
\]
\textbf{Problem (Tka\v{c}enko; Tsaban's Problem 40).} Does ZFC prove the existence of topological groups \(G\) and \(H\), each satisfying \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}},\mathcal O)\), whose product \(G\times H\), equipped with the product topology and coordinatewise group operation, fails this property? Explicitly, the required failure means that there is a sequence \((W_n)_{n\geq 1}\) of open identity neighborhoods in \(G\times H\) such that
\[
\text{for every sequence of finite sets }E_n\subseteq G\times H,
\qquad G\times H\ne\bigcup_{n\geq 1}E_nW_n.
\]
The supplied record does not define \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}}\) or specify separation axioms or further restrictions on the topological groups. The definitions above state the conventional interpretation explicitly; the intended group category requires source review.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can ZFC establish the existence of two topological groups for which every sequence of identity neighborhoods admits finitely many translates at each stage covering the group altogether, while their product fails this covering property? The supplied record labels this problem open; that status is not independently verified.
