The supplied problem asks: determine the optimal regularity of \(p\)-harmonic functions in dimensions \(d\geq 3\). The record does not specify the range of \(p\), the solution concept, the regularity scale, or whether interior or boundary regularity is intended.

For an explicitly conditional formulation in the usual scalar weak setting, let \(d\geq 3\) be an integer, let \(1<p<\infty\), and let \(\Omega\subseteq\R^d\) be open. A real-valued function \(u\in W^{1,p}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)\) is weakly \(p\)-harmonic if
\[
\int_\Omega |\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u\cdot\nabla\varphi\,\mathrm{d}x=0
\qquad\text{for every }\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega),
\]
where the flux \(|\xi|^{p-2}\xi\) is assigned the value zero at \(\xi=0\).

Under this interpretation, the task is to determine the sharp local regularity shared by every such solution, for each \(d\) and \(p\). One precise component, if gradient H\"older regularity is intended, is to determine
\[
\alpha_*(d,p)=\sup\Bigl(\{0\}\cup\bigl\{\alpha\in(0,1]:
\text{ every weakly }p\text{-harmonic }u\text{ on every open }\Omega\subseteq\R^d
\text{ has a representative in }C^{1,\alpha}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)
\bigr\}\Bigr).
\]
Here \(C^{1,\alpha}_{\mathrm{loc}}\) means continuously differentiable with gradient locally H\"older continuous of exponent \(\alpha\); exponent \(1\) means locally Lipschitz continuous gradient. A sharp determination must also settle endpoint attainment whenever \(\alpha_*(d,p)>0\).
