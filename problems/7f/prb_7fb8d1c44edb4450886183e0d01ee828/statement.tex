\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(X\) be a hyperk\"ahler manifold, as in the supplied source, with holomorphic symplectic form \(\sigma\), and let \(T\subset X\) be an embedded Lagrangian submanifold that is a complex torus. Write \(\dim_{\C}X=2r\). Here a complex torus of dimension \(r\) is a quotient \(\C^r/\Lambda\), where \(\Lambda\) is a lattice of rank \(2r\), and the Lagrangian condition means
\[
\dim_{\C}T=r,\qquad \sigma|_T=0.
\]
A holomorphic Lagrangian fibration means a proper surjective holomorphic map with connected fibres whose smooth fibres are Lagrangian submanifolds.

\textbf{Question (Beauville, Question 8, 2010).} Must there exist a holomorphic Lagrangian fibration
\[
f:X\longrightarrow \PP^r
\]
and a point \(b\in\PP^r\) such that \(T=f^{-1}(b)\)?

The supplied record does not specify the convention for ``hyperk\"ahler manifold,'' including whether compactness, simple connectedness, and the condition \(H^0(X,\Omega_X^2)=\C\sigma\) are required. These hypotheses remain subject to source verification. The source also does not specify whether ``fibre'' requires equality as a reduced analytic space or merely equality of the underlying sets. The question concerns a holomorphic fibration on \(X\) itself; a meromorphic map or a fibration on a birational model is not the stated target.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Beauville asks whether every embedded Lagrangian complex torus in a hyperk\"ahler manifold occurs as a fibre of a holomorphic Lagrangian fibration from that manifold to projective space of half its complex dimension. The precise hyperk\"ahler convention and the meaning of fibre equality require review.
