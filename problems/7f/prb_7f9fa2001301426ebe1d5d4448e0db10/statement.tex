\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be a finite integer and let \(O=(0,\ldots,0)\in\C^n\). A holomorphic local dynamical system at \(O\) is understood here as a germ \(f:(\C^n,O)\to(\C^n,O)\) of a holomorphic map fixing \(O\); thus \(f\in\C\{z_1,\ldots,z_n\}^n\) and \(f(0)=0\). Two such germs \(f\) and \(g\) are formally conjugate if there exists a formal change of coordinates
\[
H\in\C[[z_1,\ldots,z_n]]^n,\qquad H(0)=0,\qquad \det DH(0)\ne0,
\]
such that
\[
H\circ f=g\circ H
\]
as an identity of formal power series. The condition on \(DH(0)\) ensures that \(H\) has a formal compositional inverse; convergence of \(H\) is not required.

\textbf{Problem Q5 (Abate, 2011).} Find normal forms and invariants for holomorphic local dynamical systems at \(O\in\C^n\) under formal conjugacy. Here a normal form means a representative obtained by an invertible formal change of coordinates, and an invariant means data unchanged by formal conjugacy. The classification sought concerns the resulting formal conjugacy classes.
