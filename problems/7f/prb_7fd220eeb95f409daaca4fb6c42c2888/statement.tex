\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(p\) be a prime, let \(k\) be an algebraically closed field of characteristic \(p\), and let \(G\) be a finite group. Write \(k[[t]]\) and \(k[[z]]\) for formal power series rings. Suppose that a local \(G\)-Galois extension
\[
\iota:k[[t]]\hookrightarrow k[[z]]
\]
is given. Here the local \(G\)-Galois terminology is interpreted as a finite inclusion equipped with a faithful continuous action of \(G\) by \(k\)-algebra automorphisms of \(k[[z]]\), with
\[
k[[z]]^G=\iota(k[[t]]).
\]
Ramification is allowed.

Does every such extension admit the following lift? There should exist a complete discrete valuation ring \(R\) of characteristic zero, a uniformizer \(\pi\), an identification \(R/(\pi)\cong k\), and a finite injective \(R\)-algebra homomorphism
\[
\Phi:R[[T]]\hookrightarrow R[[Z]],
\]
together with a faithful continuous action of \(G\) by \(R\)-algebra automorphisms of \(R[[Z]]\), such that
\[
R[[Z]]^G=\Phi(R[[T]])?
\]
The required reduction condition is that there exist \(k\)-algebra isomorphisms
\[
\alpha:R[[T]]/\pi R[[T]]\xrightarrow{\sim}k[[t]],
\qquad
\beta:R[[Z]]/\pi R[[Z]]\xrightarrow{\sim}k[[z]],
\]
where \(\beta\) is \(G\)-equivariant and, writing \(\overline{\Phi}\) for the map induced by \(\Phi\),
\[
\beta\circ\overline{\Phi}=\iota\circ\alpha.
\]
The ring \(R\) and all lifting data may depend on the given extension.

The supplied formulation places no restriction on the finite group \(G\). The positive-characteristic interpretation and the local, possibly ramified meaning of \(G\)-Galois are made explicit here. The source's universal formulation and its supplied open-status assessment require mathematical review: the supplied statement about cyclic groups alone does not establish the status of the question for arbitrary finite groups.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Given a finite local Galois extension of power series rings over an algebraically closed field of positive characteristic, ask whether it lifts to power series rings over some complete characteristic-zero discrete valuation ring, preserving the specified group action after reduction. No restriction on the finite group is supplied; the universal scope and supplied open label require review.
