\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} For oscillator coordinates $q_x$ and momenta $p_x$, the Hamiltonian dynamics has Liouville generator $A$. Define $D_x=p_{x+1}\partial_{p_x}-p_x\partial_{p_{x+1}}$; the source's asymmetric momentum-noise generator is $S=\sum_x(D_x^2+p_xp_{x+1}D_x)$. The combined dynamics adds this stochastic generator to the harmonic Hamiltonian generator. Thermal conductivity is the linear-response coefficient relating energy current to a small temperature gradient, or its source Green--Kubo thermodynamic-limit formulation.

\paragraph{Exact generator checks.} Use a periodic chain with at least three sites, write $D_x=p_{x+1}\partial_{p_x}-p_x\partial_{p_{x+1}}$, and $K=\tfrac12\sum_xp_x^2$. Since $D_xK=0$, the stated noise satisfies $SK=0$. For total momentum $P=\sum_xp_x$, however, $D_xP=p_{x+1}-p_x$ and $D_x^2P=-(p_x+p_{x+1})$, so no corresponding conservation follows; for uniform nonzero momenta the drift contribution vanishes and $SP=-2P\ne0$.

\paragraph{Stationarity and a nonclosing hierarchy.} Under the centered product Gaussian measure, $D_x$ is skew-adjoint: its vector field is divergence-free and tangent to the quadratic Gaussian exponent. The adjoint of $a_xD_x$, $a_x=p_xp_{x+1}$, sends $1$ to $-D_xa_x=-(p_{x+1}^2-p_x^2)$. Summing around the ring telescopes to zero. Thus $S^*1=0$, confirming Gaussian invariance in this finite periodic setting. Yet
\[
 S(p_x^2)=2(p_{x-1}^2+p_{x+1}^2-2p_x^2)
 +2p_x^2(p_{x+1}^2-p_{x-1}^2).
\]
The cubic drift creates quartic observables from a quadratic one, so the usual closed quadratic covariance method for a linear harmonic-noise model does not apply directly.
