\operatorname{Fol}_{G,S}(n)=\min\{|F|:\varnothing\ne F\subset G\text{ finite},\ |\partial_SF|\leq |F|/n\},

f(g)=\frac1{|S|}\sum_{s\in S}f(gs)\quad(g\in G).
