\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Paul Balmer asks whether tensor products, exterior powers and cones reconstruct the Koszul complex $K(P,s)$ from the two-term complex $P\xrightarrow{s}A$, and whether this construction extends to tensor triangulated categories. For a finite projective module $P$ of constant rank $n$, the Koszul complex has terms $\bigwedge^iP$ and differential
\[
d_s(p_1\wedge\cdots\wedge p_i)=\sum_{j=1}^i(-1)^{j-1}s(p_j)p_1\wedge\cdots\widehat{p_j}\cdots\wedge p_i.
\]
The source's remark replaces $P\to A$ by $X\to\mathbf1$ in a tensor triangulated category and seeks a class $c(X)\in GW(\mathcal K)$ obstructing a summand $\mathbf1$. Defining such a Grothendieck--Witt group needs duality and coherence data beyond an unspecified triangulated category. Madhav Nori's original remark asserts that such a construction exists, without giving it.

The attached unreviewed aid sets $S=\operatorname{Sym}_A(P)$ and lets $A_s,A_0$ be $S$-algebras via $s$ and zero. It reports
\[
A_s\otimes_S^{\mathbf L}A_0\simeq K(P,s).
\]
It proposes the analogous free-commutative-algebra/bar construction in a presentable symmetric monoidal stable infinity-category, subject to the needed colimit compatibility. This is additional enhancement data; bare tensor triangulated axioms do not provide a canonical functorial bar construction. The spectral free commutative algebra must also be distinguished from the ordinary algebraic symmetric algebra when comparing to the finite exterior-power complex.

The aid reports determinant-twisted, $n$-shifted symmetric self-duality, attributed to Balmer--Gille, and a supported Grothendieck--Witt/Witt class on the zero locus of $s$. It claims that $K(P,s)$ is zero in the derived category exactly when the chosen map $s:P\to A$ splits, equivalently is surjective. This detects the chosen map, not whether some other map splits $P$. Under regular noetherian and $1/2$ hypotheses it further reports that forgetting support yields an $\A^1$-invariant class independent of $s$, whose nonvanishing obstructs any free rank-one summand of $P$. For $A=\R[x]$, $P=A$, $s=x$, it reports a nonzero supported Witt class with zero absolute Witt image. Thus object vanishing, supported quadratic obstruction and absolute section-independent obstruction are distinct levels of information. Exact duality signs, twists, support and group conventions are not supplied.
