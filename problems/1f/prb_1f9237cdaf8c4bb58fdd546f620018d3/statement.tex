\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Paternain's Question 10.1.2 asks whether there exists a smooth Riemannian metric on a closed surface of genus at least two whose geodesic flow has zero Liouville entropy. For a closed connected oriented surface \(M\), write \(A=\operatorname{Area}(M,g)\), let \(g_t\) be the unit-speed geodesic flow on \(SM\), and normalize Liouville measure to a probability \(m\). The quantity at issue is
\[
 h_L(g)=h_m(g_1),
\]
not topological entropy. The literal existence question ranges over higher-genus surfaces; a separate assertion for every prescribed genus is not explicitly quantified in the source.

The aid supplies an obstruction under the stronger geometric assumption of no focal points. Suppose \(\chi(M)<0\), the metric has no focal points, and
\[
 K\geq-\kappa^2\qquad\text{for some }\kappa>0.
\]
Let \(u:SM\to\R\) be the unstable Green--Riccati function, with conventions
\[
 Xu+u^2+K\circ\pi=0,
\]
where \(X\) is the geodesic-flow generator. The aid states that \(0\leq u\leq\kappa\) and
\[
 h_L(g)=\int_{SM}u\,\dd m,\qquad
 \int_{SM}u^2\,\dd m=\frac{2\pi|\chi(M)|}{A}.
\]
The second identity uses invariance to integrate the Riccati equation and Gauss--Bonnet with normalized base area. In particular,
\[
 h_L(g)-\frac{2\pi|\chi(M)|}{\kappa A}
 =\frac1\kappa\int_{SM}u(\kappa-u)\,\dd m\geq0,
\]
so
\[
 h_L(g)\geq\frac{2\pi|\chi(M)|}{\kappa A}>0.
\]
The lower bound is sharp at constant curvature \(K=-\kappa^2\), where \(u=\kappa\). No further equality classification is asserted in the supplied record.

Every smooth closed metric has a finite lower curvature bound, so the obstruction applies to every no-focal-points metric in this topological class. Any affirmative zero-entropy Riemannian example must therefore have focal points. It must also have sign-changing Gaussian curvature: negative curvature occurs somewhere by Gauss--Bonnet, and a metric with \(K\leq0\) everywhere has no focal points and is excluded. Having focal points or sign-changing curvature is only a necessary condition, not a construction or sufficient criterion for zero entropy. No replacement of no focal points by the weaker no-conjugate-points assumption is claimed.

The original background reports that Bangert and Paternain outlined a nonconstructive Finsler proof of zero-Liouville-entropy examples; it does not supply a published construction, reversibility convention or complete proof. The aid reports the unrestricted Riemannian existence question as open through its 8 August 2026 literature check. The final announcement of a special Finsler subquestion is cut off and contains no additional mathematical statement.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The source asks for a higher-genus Riemannian metric with zero Liouville entropy. The aid proves a positive lower bound under no focal points, so any possible example must have focal points and both positive and negative curvature. It supplies no such metric and reports the general question open as of its August 2026 check.
