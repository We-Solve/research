\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A Coxeter system \((W,S)\) consists of a group with presentation
\[
W=\langle S\mid (st)^{m(s,t)}=e\text{ whenever }m(s,t)<\infty\rangle,
\]
where \(m(s,s)=1\) and \(m(s,t)=m(t,s)\in\{2,3,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}\) for distinct generators. Write \(\ell(x)\) for the minimum length of an expression for \(x\) in the generators. The Bruhat order is defined by \(x\leq y\) if some reduced expression for \(y\) contains a reduced expression for \(x\) as a subsequence. For \(u\leq v\), define the interval
\[
[u,v]=\{x\in W:u\leq x\leq v\},
\]
with its induced order.

Interpret \(P_{x,y}(q)\) as the ordinary equal-parameter Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomial, with the following normalization. Let \(t\) be an indeterminate and let \(H(W)\) be the Hecke algebra over \(\Z[t,t^{-1}]\) with basis \(\{T_x:x\in W\}\), identity \(T_e\), and multiplication determined by
\[
T_sT_x=\begin{cases}
T_{sx},&\ell(sx)=\ell(x)+1,\\
t^2T_{sx}+(t^2-1)T_x,&\ell(sx)=\ell(x)-1.
\end{cases}
\]
Its bar involution satisfies \(\overline{t}=t^{-1}\) and \(\overline{T_x}=T_{x^{-1}}^{-1}\). The polynomials \(P_{x,y}(q)\in\Z[q]\) are characterized by bar invariance of
\[
C'_y=t^{-\ell(y)}\sum_{x\leq y}P_{x,y}(t^2)T_x,
\]
together with \(P_{y,y}(q)=1\) and
\[
\deg P_{x,y}\leq\frac{\ell(y)-\ell(x)-1}{2}\qquad(x<y).
\]
The supplied record does not explicitly define \(P\); this standard interpretation requires confirmation against the cited source.

The supplied conjecture asserts that, for every two Coxeter systems \((W_1,S_1)\) and \((W_2,S_2)\), and every \(u,v\in W_1\) and \(w,z\in W_2\) with \(u\leq v\) and \(w\leq z\),
\[
[u,v]\simeq[w,z]\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
P^{W_1}_{u,v}(q)=P^{W_2}_{w,z}(q).
\]
Here an interval isomorphism is a bijection preserving and reflecting order; no compatibility with generator labels or ambient groups is required. The comparability assumptions make explicit the customary nonempty-interval interpretation, which is implicit in the supplied statement. No finiteness assumption on either Coxeter group is imposed.
\subsection{Short English statement}
The conjecture attributed to Brenti says that the Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomial of a Bruhat interval depends only on its abstract partially ordered set, even when the intervals belong to different Coxeter systems. This draft uses the ordinary equal-parameter interpretation of the undefined notation \(P_{u,v}\).
