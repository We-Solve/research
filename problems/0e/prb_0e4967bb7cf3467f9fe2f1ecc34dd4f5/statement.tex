\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
In the usual stratum notation, $\mathcal H_1(\alpha)$ is the moduli space of area-one abelian differentials with prescribed zero orders $\alpha$, and $\mathcal Q_1(\beta)$ is the corresponding space of area-one quadratic differentials with prescribed singularity orders $\beta$. For a holomorphic one-form $\omega$, area is $\tfrac i2\int\omega\wedge\overline\omega$; for a quadratic differential $q$, area is $\int|q|$. Flat-coordinate changes give the usual $\operatorname{SL}_2(\R)$ action, preserving area.

A minimal set for this action means a nonempty closed invariant subset $Z$ containing no proper nonempty closed invariant subset. Equivalently every orbit in $Z$ is dense in $Z$. Compactness is not added: in these noncompact strata it would be a further restriction.

Problem 11 asks to describe the minimal sets of the $\operatorname{SL}_2(\R)$ actions on both $\mathcal H_1(\alpha)$ and $\mathcal Q_1(\beta)$. The source says Veech surfaces give minimal sets, so this generalizes their characterization; it does not assert that all minimal sets arise from Veech surfaces. This concerns full group-action minimal sets, not minimality of a directional flow on one flat surface.
