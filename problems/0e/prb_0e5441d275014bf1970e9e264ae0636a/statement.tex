\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(S\) be a closed surface of genus \(h\ge 2\), as in the supplied source; closed means compact without boundary. The source does not explicitly specify orientability, so its genus convention requires confirmation.

For \(r\in\{3,4,5,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}\), consider the following existence statement: there are a \(C^r\) Riemannian metric \(g\) on \(S\) and a complete unit-speed geodesic \(\gamma:\R\to S\) such that
\[
\nabla^{g}_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma=0,\qquad
 g(\dot\gamma(t),\dot\gamma(t))=1,\qquad
 K_g(\gamma(t))=0\quad\text{for every }t\in\R,
\]
and
\[
\nexists T>0\quad\forall t\in\R:\quad\gamma(t+T)=\gamma(t).
\]
Here \(K_g:S\to\R\) is the Gaussian curvature and \(\nabla^g\) is the Levi--Civita connection. The last condition expresses the usual interpretation of a non-closed geodesic as a nonperiodic geodesic; the source does not separately define that phrase.
