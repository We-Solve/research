\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(F\) be a field satisfying \(\operatorname{char}(F)\ne 7\) and containing no primitive seventh root of unity. Write \(\operatorname{Br}(F)\) for the Brauer group of finite-dimensional central simple \(F\)-algebras, with addition induced by tensor product, and set
\[
\operatorname{Br}(F)[7]=\{\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}(F):7\alpha=0\}.
\]
For a cyclic Galois extension \(L/F\) of degree \(7\), a generator \(\sigma\) of \(\operatorname{Gal}(L/F)\), and \(a\in F^\times\), the cyclic algebra \((L/F,\sigma,a)\) is
\[
\bigoplus_{i=0}^{6}Lu^i,\qquad u^7=a,\qquad u\ell=\sigma(\ell)u\quad(\ell\in L).
\]
It is a central simple \(F\)-algebra of degree \(7\). Define
\[
C_7(F)=\left\langle[(L/F,\sigma,a)]:L/F\text{ cyclic Galois of degree }7,\ \langle\sigma\rangle=\operatorname{Gal}(L/F),\ a\in F^\times\right\rangle
\subseteq\operatorname{Br}(F).
