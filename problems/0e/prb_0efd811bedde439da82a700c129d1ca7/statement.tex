\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $S$ be the compact surface in the source, equipped with a smooth structure for positive regularity. For $0\leq t\leq\infty$, let $\operatorname{Diff}^t(S)$ denote the group of $C^t$ self-diffeomorphisms, with $\operatorname{Diff}^0(S)$ interpreted as the homeomorphism group, and let $\operatorname{Diff}^t_0(S)$ be its identity component in the intended $C^t$ topology.

For nonintegral finite $t=k+\varepsilon$, with $k\in\Z_{\geq0}$ and $0<\varepsilon<1$, the source uses $C^k$ maps whose $k$th derivatives are $\varepsilon$-Hölder in charts; the corresponding inverse must have the same regularity to obtain a group. At integer $t=k$ use ordinary $C^k$ regularity, and $t=\infty$ means smooth. Boundary behavior and precise Hölder topology are not fixed in the supplied statement.

\textbf{Imported pair-of-regularities question.} For given $0\leq r<s\leq\infty$, does there exist a nontrivial finitely generated group $G_r\leq\operatorname{Diff}^r_0(S)$ such that
\[
 \forall\rho:G_r\longrightarrow\operatorname{Diff}^{s}(S)
 \text{ a group homomorphism}:\qquad \rho(G_r)=\{\operatorname{id}_S\}?
\]
Thus the inclusion supplies a faithful $C^r$ action isotopic to the identity, but every $C^s$ action of the abstract group must have trivial image. The target is the full diffeomorphism group, not only its identity component. No continuity of a homomorphism from this abstract finitely generated group is additionally imposed. For the given-pair quantifier order, the group may depend on $S,r,s$, despite the notation $G_r$.

The notation may also suggest the stronger simultaneous critical-regularity version: for each $r$, choose one $G_r$ working for every $s>r$. The short wording does not settle that dependence explicitly, so these quantifier orders are not silently equated.

\textbf{Torsion-free strengthening.} Can the groups in the intended version be chosen torsion-free, meaning that $g^m=1$ with $m\geq1$ implies $g=1$? This is an additional requirement on the same nontrivial finitely generated subgroup.

The source notes weaker alternatives: every smoother action could instead be required to be nonfaithful or to have abelian image. Nonfaithful means a nontrivial kernel; abelian image means the commutator subgroup is killed. Neither conclusion by itself says that the entire image is trivial.

The background and literature triage dated 2026-08-17 report extensive one-dimensional results: Kim--Koberda's prescribed critical-regularity groups include finitely generated groups with infinite simple commutator subgroups and abelian smoother images; Plante--Thurston's $C^2$ nilpotent interval groups are abelian, whereas Farb--Franks provide nonabelian $C^1$ examples; Castro--Jorquera--Navas give sharp nilpotent regularity gaps, and Mann--Wolff provide further one-manifold examples. These are contextual one-dimensional or weaker-image results, not a proof of the exact trivial-image surface statement.

For $r=0$ and $s\geq1$, the source reports particular homeomorphism-group obstructions to faithful diffeomorphism actions using Thurston stability, with Calegari as an example reference. The review emphasizes that nonfaithfulness does not resolve triviality. It says no higher-dimensional surface examples satisfying the full requirement are recorded by K3 and leaves the exact construction and torsion-free strengthening open. \subsection{Short English statement}
Find a nontrivial finitely generated group acting faithfully on a compact surface with regularity $C^r$, isotopic to the identity, but admitting only trivial actions of higher regularity $C^s$. Ask also for torsion-free examples. The supplied review distinguishes this from known one-dimensional, nonfaithful-action and abelian-image results, and leaves the exact surface problem open.
