A Schauder basis \((x_n)_{n\geq 1}\) of a Banach space \(X\) is a sequence such that every \(x\in X\) has a unique expansion \(x=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_nx_n\) converging in norm, with \(a_n\in\mathbb K\). Two basic sequences \((u_n)\) and \((v_n)\) are equivalent if there exist constants \(0<A\leq B<\infty\) such that, for every \(m\geq 1\) and every \(a_1,\ldots,a_m\in\mathbb K\),
\[
 A\left\|\sum_{n=1}^{m}a_nu_n\right\|
 \leq \left\|\sum_{n=1}^{m}a_nv_n\right\|
 \leq B\left\|\sum_{n=1}^{m}a_nu_n\right\|.
\]
A normalized block sequence of \((x_n)\) is a sequence \((y_k)_{k\geq 1}\) of the form
\[
 y_k=\sum_{n=r_k}^{s_k}b_n^{(k)}x_n,
 \qquad 1\leq r_k\leq s_k<r_{k+1},
 \qquad \|y_k\|=1,
\]
where all coefficients belong to \(\mathbb K\). Use the standard block-sequence interpretation of a \emph{Rosenthal basis}: every normalized block sequence \((y_k)\) has a subsequence \((y_{k_j})_{j\geq 1}\) equivalent to \((x_j)_{j\geq 1}\). The supplied record uses the term without defining it, so this interpretation requires confirmation against the cited source.

\textbf{Rosenthal basis problem (H. P. Rosenthal; supplied reference label [FPR]).} Is it true that, for every Banach space \(X\) over the chosen field \(\mathbb K\) and every Rosenthal basis \((x_n)\) of \(X\), either \((x_n)\) is equivalent to the standard unit vector basis of \(c_0(\mathbb K)\), or there exists a real number \(p\) with \(1\leq p<\infty\) such that \((x_n)\) is equivalent to the standard unit vector basis of \(\ell_p(\mathbb K)\)? The choice of alternative and, when applicable, \(p\), may depend on the basis.
