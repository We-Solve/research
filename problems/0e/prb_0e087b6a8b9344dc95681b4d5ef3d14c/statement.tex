\section*{UnsolvedMath ID: 800219}
\textbf{Source problem code:} catalogue entry
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(n\geq 1\) be an integer, and let \(\Omega\Subset\C^n\) be a pseudoconvex domain with smooth boundary. Here a domain is a connected open set, \(\Omega\Subset\C^n\) means that its closure is compact, and smooth boundary is understood to mean \(C^\infty\) boundary. Write \(H(\Omega)\) for the space of holomorphic functions on \(\Omega\). For a positive integer \(k\), let \(C^k(\overline\Omega)\) consist of functions whose real partial derivatives of orders at most \(k\) on \(\Omega\) extend continuously to \(\overline\Omega\), with norm
\[
\|f\|_{C^k(\overline\Omega)}=\max_{|\alpha|\leq k}\sup_{z\in\overline\Omega}|D^\alpha f(z)|,
\qquad \alpha\in\N_0^{2n}.
\]
Set
\[
C^\infty(\overline\Omega)=\bigcap_{m\geq 0}C^m(\overline\Omega),
\qquad A^k(\overline\Omega)=H(\Omega)\cap C^k(\overline\Omega),
\quad k\in\{1,2,\ldots,\infty\}.
\]
Equip \(A^k(\overline\Omega)\) with the induced \(C^k\) topology; for \(k=\infty\), use the topology defined by all finite-order \(C^m\) seminorms.

Is it true that, for every such \(n\) and \(\Omega\), and every integer \(k\geq 1\),
\[
\forall f\in A^k(\overline\Omega)\ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists g\in A^\infty(\overline\Omega):\quad
\|f-g\|_{C^k(\overline\Omega)}<\varepsilon?
\]
The source also includes \(k=\infty\); in that case the requested density is the automatic density of \(A^\infty(\overline\Omega)\) in itself. No additional boundary hypothesis beyond smoothness and pseudoconvexity is supplied.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every holomorphic function on a smoothly bounded, relatively compact pseudoconvex domain in \(\C^n\), with derivatives continuous through order \(k\) on the closure, be approximated uniformly together with those derivatives by holomorphic functions smooth to every order on the closure? The substantive question concerns finite integers \(k\geq 1\).
