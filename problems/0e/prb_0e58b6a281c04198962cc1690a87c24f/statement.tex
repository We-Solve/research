A thick tensor ideal $\mathcal I$ is a full triangulated subcategory closed under direct summands and under $x\otimes y$ for $x\in\mathcal I$ and $y\in\mathcal T$. A prime thick tensor ideal is a proper such ideal $\mathcal P$ satisfying
\[
 x\otimes y\in\mathcal P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x\in\mathcal P\ \text{or}\ y\in\mathcal P.
