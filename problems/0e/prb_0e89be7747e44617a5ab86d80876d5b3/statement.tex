\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $B^n=\{x\in\R^n:|x|<1\}$ and let $f:B^n\to\R^n$ be continuous. Its branch set in the source's topological sense is
\[
B_f=\{x\in B^n:f\text{ is not a local homeomorphism at }x\}.
\]
A local homeomorphism means that some neighborhood of the point is mapped homeomorphically onto a neighborhood in the target. Compactness of $B_f$ means compactness as a subset of $B^n$, not just boundedness in the ambient Euclidean space.

The printed definition of properness says that $f^{-1}(K)$ is compact in $B^n$ whenever $K$ is a \emph{proper subset} of $f(B^n)$. This is not the standard compact-preimage condition and quantifies over noncompact and nonclosed subsets as well. A natural possible correction is to require compact preimages for every \emph{compact subset} $K\subset f(B^n)$, which defines properness relative to the image; properness into all of $\R^n$ is another target convention. No replacement is silently made.

Vuorinen's proposed implication, with the source terms retained, is
\[
\forall n\ge3\quad
\bigl[f:B^n\to\R^n\text{ is continuous, proper, and quasi-analytic, and }B_f\text{ is compact}\bigr]
\Longrightarrow f\text{ is injective}.
\]
The extract does not define ``quasi-analytic'', so the admissible class cannot be recovered precisely. In particular it is not silently replaced by quasiregular, quasiconformal, real-analytic, or a particular smooth quasianalytic class. The implication is therefore a literal conditional schema pending the class and properness definitions.

The source says the planar map $z\mapsto z^2$ on $B^2=\mathbb D$ rules out the conclusion in dimension two, and reports the conjecture true when $f(B^n)=B^n$ and $n\ge3$.
