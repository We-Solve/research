\section{Bisognano–Wichmann Covariance for Standard-Subspace Nets}\label{30002835}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30002835 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
For a standard real subspace $H$, the closed antilinear operator $S(h+ik)=h-ik$ has polar decomposition $S=J\Delta^{1/2}$; $\Delta^{it}$ is its modular group. Covariance of a net means $U(g)H(O)=H(gO)$. Positive energy means the translation spectrum lies in the closed forward light cone. The Bisognano--Wichmann property identifies the modular group of a wedge subspace with the corresponding Lorentz boosts, with the standard $2\pi$ normalization and a convention-dependent sign.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Given a local net of standard subspaces $H(O)$ on double cones $O\subset\mathbb R^4$, covariant under a positive-energy unitary Poincaré representation $U$, does another positive-energy representation $U'$ on the same Hilbert space make $H$ covariant and satisfy the Bisognano–Wichmann property?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The new representation must act on the same Hilbert space and preserve covariance of the same net. Wedge subspaces are obtained from the double-cone net by the source\textquotesingle s closure convention; replacing the net is not an answer.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can a local standard-subspace net be given another positive-energy Poincaré action whose boosts agree with its wedge modular groups?
