Let $\mathcal F\subseteq\binom{[4n]}{2n}$ satisfy
$|A\cap B|\geq2$ for all $A,B\in\mathcal F$. Prove
\[
 |\mathcal F|\leq\frac12\left(\binom{4n}{2n}-\binom{2n}{n}^2\right).
\]
This is a known special case of the complete intersection theorem
of Ahlswede and Khachatrian. We state the external extremal theorem
precisely and supply the parameter calculation, exact count and
sharpness verification. Its general extremal proof is not reproduced.
