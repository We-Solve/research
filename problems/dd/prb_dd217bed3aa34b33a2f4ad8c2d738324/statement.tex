\section{Global Attractors for Expanding Torus Symmetric Spacetimes}\label{30003921}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 30003921 · Mathematical Physics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
A $T^2$-symmetric spacetime admits two commuting spacelike circle symmetries. The source coordinates use a time variable $\tau$ and metric fields $V$ and $Q$, so $V_\tau$ and $Q_\tau$ are their time derivatives. The class $B_0$ is defined in the report by a conserved quantity $B=0$ for non-Gowdy, non-pseudo-homogeneous solutions. An attractor is local when it controls nearby data and global when it controls all admissible data in the indicated symmetry class.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For expanding $T^2$-symmetric spacetimes, is the local attractor global for $B_0$ solutions? For non-$B_0$ solutions, does there exist a local attractor satisfying $V_\tau\to1/2$ and $Q_\tau=O(e^{-3\tau/2})$, and is it global within the $T^2$-symmetric class?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The metric ansatz, conserved quantity $B$ and admissible data are source-defined. All three tasks are retained: globality in $B_0$, existence of the non-$B_0$ attractor with both stated rates, and its globality in the full symmetry class.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Are the proposed late-time attractors global for both the special zero-conserved-quantity class and the remaining torus-symmetric solutions?
