\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

$\sigma_k(x)_2=\inf\{\lVert x-z\rVert_2:z\text{ has at most }k\text{ nonzero coordinates}\}$ is the best $k$-term approximation error. $\Phi$ maps $N$-dimensional signals to $n$ measurements and $\Delta$ is a reconstruction map.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
In compressed sensing, suppose the $\ell^2$ instance-optimal recovery estimate $\lVert x-\Delta(\Phi x)\rVert_2\leq C_0\sigma_k(x)_2$ holds for $k\leq cn\log(N/n)$ with probability at least $1-e^{-bn}$. Can one construct a decoder $\Delta$ with the same guarantee and low algorithmic complexity? \sref{30000802}{2}
