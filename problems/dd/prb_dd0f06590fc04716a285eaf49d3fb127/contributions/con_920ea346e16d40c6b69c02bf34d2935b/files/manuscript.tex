\section{Canonical Equivalence of Complex and Lagrangian Quantizations}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
A quantizable compact symplectic manifold $(X,\omega)$ has a prequantum Hermitian line bundle with compatible connection. A compatible complex polarization gives a Hilbert space of appropriate holomorphic sections. A real polarization from a Lagrangian fibration gives states associated with its Bohr--Sommerfeld fibers, on which the restricted connection has nonzero parallel sections. An isomorphism is canonical only if it is naturally determined by the specified geometric data, rather than chosen from an arbitrary basis.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Given a compact symplectic manifold equipped both with a compatible-complex-structure quantization and with a Lagrangian-fibration quantization, construct a canonical isomorphism between the resulting quantum Hilbert spaces.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The prequantum data, singular-fiber treatment and any half-form corrections must match. Equality of dimensions or existence of some vector-space isomorphism is weaker than the canonical comparison requested.

\paragraph{A symmetry-compatible phase ambiguity.} Suppose two proposed quantization Hilbert spaces each carry a circle action with the same distinct integer weights \(0,\ldots,k\). Choose normalized weight vectors \(e_j\) and \(f_j\). Any circle-equivariant linear map \(T\) must have \(Te_j=c_jf_j\): comparing coefficients in \(T(e^{ij\theta}e_j)=e^{i\theta A}Te_j\) forces every component of different weight to vanish. Unitarity imposes only \(|c_j|=1\). Hence
\[
 T_{\boldsymbol\gamma}e_j=e^{i\gamma_j}f_j,\qquad
 (\gamma_0,\ldots,\gamma_k)\in\mathbb R^{k+1},
\]
is an entire family of equally equivariant unitaries. Even for \(k=0\), an overall phase remains.
\paragraph{Application to an attempted canonical comparison.} Matching dimensions or toric weight labels between holomorphic sections and Bohr--Sommerfeld states cannot choose these phases. Normalizing each state removes its norm freedom but leaves exactly the ambiguity above. A pairing constructed from the common prequantum connection and half-forms could potentially fix relative phases or provide a projective map; that is additional geometric construction, not a consequence of representation multiplicities.
