\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an integer \(n\geq4\), let \(\mathbb H^n\) be real hyperbolic \(n\)-space and \(\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)\) its full isometry group. A lattice \(\Gamma\) is a discrete subgroup of finite Haar covolume. Uniform means cocompact; nonuniform means finite covolume with noncompact quotient. The question imposes neither choice and does not assume arithmeticity, torsion-freeness or orientation preservation.

A group \(\Gamma\) is coherent if every finitely generated subgroup \(H\leq\Gamma\) is finitely presented. It is noncoherent if some subgroup \(H\leq\Gamma\) is generated by finitely many elements but admits no presentation with finitely many generators and relations. Thus the conjectural assertion is
\[
 \forall n\in\Z_{\geq4}\ \forall\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)
 \text{ a lattice}\ \exists H\leq\Gamma,
 \quad H\text{ finitely generated but not finitely presented}.
\]
The subgroup \(H\) may depend on the lattice and dimension. No bound on its number of generators and no universal isomorphism type for the obstruction subgroup is requested. Noncoherence is not the assertion that \(\Gamma\) itself fails to be finitely presented, nor is the existence of a merely infinitely generated subgroup sufficient.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every finite-covolume real-hyperbolic lattice in dimension at least four contain a finitely generated subgroup that is not finitely presented? The question includes both compact and noncompact quotients and arbitrary lattices. The source's reported arithmetic families remain partial progress rather than added hypotheses.
