\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a positive integer \(n\), let \(G_n=\{1,\ldots,n\}^3\subset\R^3\). For \(S\subseteq G_n\), define
\[
 c_3(S)=\left|\left\{U\subseteq S:\ |U|=3\text{ and }U\text{ lies on a single affine line in }\R^3\right\}\right|.
\]
Thus triples consist of three distinct points and are counted without ordering; a line containing \(k\) points of \(S\) contributes \(\binom{k}{3}\) triples. For an integer \(m\) with \(0\le m\le n^3\), set
\[
 M(n,m)=\min_{\substack{S\subseteq G_n\\|S|=m}}c_3(S).
