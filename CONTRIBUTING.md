# Contributing

Submissions are open. Everything you submit is public.

- **Submit an attempt, a review or a new problem** through the forms under [New issue](https://github.com/We-Solve/research/issues/new/choose). No setup is needed: paste your write-up (Markdown with maths, or TeX) or attach a ZIP of your `.tex` and `.bib` files. PDF is not accepted.
- The [Contribute pages](https://we-solve.github.io/contribute/) explain what a good attempt, review or problem includes. Every problem page links to a form prepared for that problem.
- The editors review each submission before it is published on the website and recorded here. Pull requests are not accepted yet.

You need the right to share what you submit. New work is published under CC BY 4.0 (software under Apache-2.0). List every person and LLM involved, with versions or "unknown".

To report an inaccuracy, a rights concern or private information, see the [corrections and takedown policy](https://we-solve.github.io/policies/takedown/).
