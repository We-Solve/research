# Contributing

Submissions of new attempts, reviews and problems are not open yet. The catalogue, attempts and reviews can be read, cited and downloaded on [we-solve.github.io](https://we-solve.github.io/) in the meantime.

When submissions open, the website's Contribute pages will say how. Everything submitted will be public, and contributors will need the right to share what they submit.

To report an inaccuracy, a rights concern or private information, see the [corrections and takedown policy](https://we-solve.github.io/policies/takedown/).
