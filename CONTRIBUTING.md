# Contributing

Submissions are open. Everything you submit is public.

The archive includes some of the hardest research problems. Submit only significant contributions to challenging problems. Rigorous partial progress is welcome; routine restatements and unsupported claims are not sufficient.

Acceptance of LLM-assisted work requires models with demonstrated mathematical capability through the following routes. Examples with demonstrated mathematical success are Astra Pro, Sol 6.1 at High or above, Astra 6 at High or above, Fable, Opus 5.5, Argon, and equivalent future models. Other models qualify through a previously demonstrated multi-agent architecture or a correctness check by a demonstrated model; document the architecture or reviewer and its supporting evidence. People-only contributions are welcome.

Submit mathematical exposition in Markdown or TeX, with .bib files and figures as needed. No PDFs or direct code, including Lean or Python, in issue text, attachments or repository submissions. Host your own code on GitHub or Hugging Face and link to a pinned version.

Select every model first, then complete the separate required thinking level / time dropdown and per-model details. Record actual versions, thinking settings, time budgets and roles; use unknown when unavailable. Never infer or invent a thinking setting from a model name.

- **Submit an attempt, a review or a new problem** through the forms under [New issue](https://github.com/We-Solve/research/issues/new/choose). No setup is needed: paste your write-up (Markdown with maths, or TeX) or attach a ZIP of your `.tex` and `.bib` files. PDFs and direct code are not accepted; link to a pinned version of externally hosted code instead.
- The [Contribute pages](https://we-solve.github.io/contribute/) explain what a good attempt, review or problem includes. Every problem page links to a form prepared for that problem.
- The editors review each submission before it is published on the website and recorded here. Pull requests are not accepted yet.

You need the right to share what you submit. New work is published under CC BY 4.0 (software under Apache-2.0). List every person and LLM involved, with versions, actual thinking settings and time budgets or "unknown".

To report an inaccuracy, a rights concern or private information, see the [corrections and takedown policy](https://we-solve.github.io/policies/takedown/).
